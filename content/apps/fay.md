### What it does in plain English
Fay helps you create interactive digital characters that can talk, listen, and respond to users using artificial intelligence. It connects various AI "brains" with visual avatars so they can hold natural conversations. You can use it to build virtual assistants, news presenters, or customer service bots that work on websites, apps, or even small hardware devices.

### Who it's for
*   **Developers:** People who want to build custom AI chatbots or virtual assistants without starting from scratch.
*   **Business Owners:** Teams looking to add a virtual host or guide to their websites, apps, or physical displays.
*   **Educators:** Teachers or creators who want to build interactive learning tools with a friendly digital face.

### Key features
*   **Flexible AI Connections:** Works with many different AI language models and voice tools, allowing you to swap them out easily.
*   **Offline Capability:** Can run entirely on your own computer without needing an internet connection, ensuring privacy and reliability.
*   **Multi-Platform Support:** Designed to work on Windows, Mac, and Linux, as well as on mobile apps and websites.
*   **Customizable Behavior:** Lets you define how the character speaks, its personality, and what it knows, using simple configuration files.
*   **Interactive Modes:** Supports voice commands, text input, and automatic broadcasting for scenarios like news reading or teaching.
