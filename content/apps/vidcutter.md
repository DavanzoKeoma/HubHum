### What it does in plain English
VidCutter helps you quickly trim unwanted parts from your videos or join multiple clips together into one file. It focuses on speed and simplicity, allowing you to make edits without needing to understand complex video settings. You simply open your video, select the parts you want to keep, and save your new clip.

### Who it's for
* Home users who want to quickly remove commercials or unwanted segments from recorded videos.
* Content creators who need a lightweight tool to join several short clips into a single video file.

### Key features
* **Simple Trimming:** Easily select start and end points to cut out specific sections of a video.
* **Join Clips:** Combine multiple video files into one seamless movie.
* **Fast Processing:** Designed to perform edits quickly without re-encoding the entire video.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** A clean, modern layout that makes video editing accessible to everyone.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works consistently across different computer operating systems.
