### What it does in plain English
OpenChatCut is a local-first video editing application that combines a professional multi-track timeline with conversational artificial intelligence. Instead of manually clicking through menus, you can simply type or speak your editing commands to transform clips, add transitions, and mix audio. Every instruction modifies the actual project tracks directly, keeping your work fully inspectable, undoable, and ready for traditional fine-tuning.

### Who it's for
* **Content creators and YouTubers** looking to streamline talking-head and interview edits through text-based transcripts and automated chapter cuts.
* **Independent filmmakers and video hobbyists** who want AI assistance on a professional multi-track timeline without losing manual frame-by-frame control.
* **Developers and power users** who want to connect external coding assistants to inspect and modify video projects via standard protocol integrations.

### Key features
* **Professional multi-track timeline:** precise control over multiple video and audio tracks, keyframes, transitions, and effects.
* **Conversational AI assistance:** built-in intelligence that reads your project context and executes complex timeline edits from plain English prompts.
* **Transcript-driven editing:** word-level transcription and text-based cuts that let you remove pauses and mistakes by editing the script directly.
* **Local-first privacy:** your projects, media files, and sensitive data stay stored securely on your own computer by default.
* **Flexible production exports:** render high-definition videos with hardware acceleration or export project packages for external software.
