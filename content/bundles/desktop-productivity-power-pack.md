### Why this curated guide
Small, lightweight desktop helpers save hours every week without cluttering your system or prompting for upgrades.

### Featured highlights
* **[AltTab](/HubHum/apps/alt-tab-macos/)** (*Desktop App*): AltTab brings the powerful window-switching behavior of Windows to your Mac, letting you preview and jump between open application windows instantly.
* **[flameshot](/HubHum/apps/flameshot/)** (*Desktop App*): Capture your screen instantly, highlight key details with built-in drawing tools, and share your images effortlessly.
* **[UniGetUI](/HubHum/apps/unigetui/)** (*Desktop App*): UniGetUI brings all your system tools and software updates together into one clean, friendly control panel.
* **[DBeaver](/HubHum/apps/dbeaver/)** (*Desktop App*): Universal database tool for viewing, editing, and managing hundreds of different database systems effortlessly.
* **[Bruno](/HubHum/apps/bruno/)** (*Desktop App*): Bruno is a lightweight, offline-first application that helps developers explore, test, and organize web requests directly on their device.
* **[CC Switch](/HubHum/apps/cc-switch/)** (*Desktop App*): Switch between different AI service providers with a single click and organize your prompts, plugins, and helper tools in one unified dashboard.
