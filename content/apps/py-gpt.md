### What it does in plain English
Py-gpt is a versatile desktop application that brings together major artificial intelligence models in one easy-to-use interface. Users can chat with various smart engines, run specialized automated agents, search through local documents, and generate images or audio without needing technical expertise. It functions locally on your computer while letting you bring your own keys or run completely private models offline.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers looking to summarize long documents and search through local notes effortlessly.
* Writers, creators, and professionals wanting a unified workspace to generate text, images, and voice recordings.
* Everyday computer users who want a private, customizable alternative to online artificial intelligence websites.

### Key features
* Connects with popular cloud models as well as completely offline local models.
* Includes specialized multi-agent workflows for handling complex multi-step tasks.
* Built-in file indexing and document search capabilities for quick reference.
* Supports voice recognition, speech synthesis, and image generation tools.
* Extensible plugin system to add web searches, system commands, and custom tools.
