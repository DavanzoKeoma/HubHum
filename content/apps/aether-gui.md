### What it does in plain English
Aether-GUI acts as an easy-to-use remote control for a powerful anti-censorship tunneling engine running quietly in the background. Instead of typing complex commands into a terminal window, you simply open the app and press a single connect button. It automatically handles the tricky route discovery, identity setup, and background connection maintenance so you can browse freely.

### Who it's for
* Everyday internet users living under heavy network filtering who want a frustration-free connection tool.
* Individuals looking for privacy options without needing to learn technical command-line instructions.
* People who prefer a graphical window with live progress bars and tray status indicators over text terminals.

### Key features
* **One-click connect:** Starts immediately using your best previous settings with zero configuration required.
* **Advanced controls:** Fine-tune protocols, scanning modes, IP versions, and obfuscation methods if you want manual precision.
* **Background operation:** Minimizes neatly to your system tray with clear color-coded status dots.
* **Automatic recovery:** Detects dropped connections and safely attempts reconnecting without closing or throwing errors.
* **Live progress view:** Shows elapsed time and precise percentage bars while searching for working paths.
