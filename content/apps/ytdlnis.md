### What it does in plain English
YTDLnis is a versatile mobile application that lets you download video and audio content directly to your Android device from thousands of popular websites. It organizes your downloads efficiently, letting you trim clips, embed subtitles, and schedule batches without complicated setup steps. You can even share links straight from your browser to start downloading instantly in the background.

### Who it's for
* Media enthusiasts who want to save videos and music for offline viewing and listening on their phones.
* Content creators and students who need to extract audio or trim clips from online lectures and tutorials.
* Power users looking for an advanced mobile downloading utility with scheduling and automation options.

### Key features
* Download video, audio, or custom commands from over a thousand supported websites.
* Integrated editing tools to trim videos, split chapters, burn subtitles, and embed lyrics.
* Playlist management and automated background syncing for channels and feeds.
* Convenient sharing integration to start downloads straight from your web browser.
* Built-in tools for managing cookies, generating login tokens, and handling multiple downloads simultaneously.
