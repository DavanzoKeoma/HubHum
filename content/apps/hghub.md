### What it does in plain English
HGhub acts as a central hub for your digital media by connecting to different content sources through simple plugins. It allows you to search, browse, and watch videos from multiple places without switching between different apps. You can also manage your personal library, track your watch history, and keep up with your favorite shows in one place.

### Who it's for
*   People who want a single, clean interface to manage video content from various online sources.
*   Users who enjoy customizing their viewing experience and want to keep their watch history and collections organized.

### Key features
*   **Unified Library:** Keep all your favorite shows, watch history, and progress in one organized location.
*   **Flexible Sources:** Use plugins to connect to different content providers, including live streams and video feeds.
*   **Customizable Viewing:** Easily switch between different quality settings, video sources, and playback options.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Designed to work smoothly across desktop and mobile devices with intuitive controls.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** No account registration is required to use the app, and you maintain control over your own data.
