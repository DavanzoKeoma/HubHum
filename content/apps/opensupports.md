### What it does in plain English
OpenSupports is a streamlined helpdesk platform designed to collect and organize customer inquiries in one central place. Customers can easily submit help requests through a clean web portal, while your team can efficiently track, manage, and respond to every ticket. Because it is completely open-source and self-hosted, you maintain total ownership over your data and support communications.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners looking to provide professional customer service without expensive subscriptions.
* IT support teams needing a centralized inbox to organize technical issues and user requests.
* Community managers who want a dedicated space to handle member questions and troubleshooting.

### Key features
* Simple and intuitive interface for both customers submitting tickets and staff members managing them.
* Completely free and self-hosted on your own infrastructure or servers.
* Structured ticket tracking to keep conversations organized and never lose track of a client issue.
* Modern web-based dashboard with clear status indicators for open, pending, and resolved queries.
* Active open-source project welcoming community feedback and continuous improvements.
