### What it does in plain English
MonitorControl lets you adjust the brightness, contrast, and volume of external screens connected to your Mac as if they were built-in Apple displays. It pops up familiar on-screen volume and brightness indicators right on your desktop when you press your keyboard's media buttons. You can also fine-tune your setup using simple sliders located directly in your computer's top menu bar.

### Who it's for
* Mac owners who use external monitors, TVs, or portable secondary screens for everyday work and entertainment.
* Remote workers who want a seamless way to adjust screen brightness without reaching for awkward physical buttons on the back of their monitor.

### Key features
* Adjusts external display brightness, contrast, and audio volume using standard Mac keyboard shortcuts.
* Displays native macOS volume and brightness indicators on screen just like official Apple hardware.
* Combines hardware and software dimming to make screens darker than standard minimum limits.
* Synchronizes brightness levels across multiple connected screens using a single slider or shortcut.
* Lightweight menubar interface that blends seamlessly into the standard macOS visual style.

### How to install (Download)
1. Go to the official releases page and [Download for Mac (.dmg)](https://github.com/MonitorControl/MonitorControl/releases/download/v4.4.0/MonitorControl.4.4.0.dmg).
2. Open the downloaded file and drag the app icon into your Mac Applications folder.
3. Launch the app and grant Accessibility permissions in your system settings if you want to use native keyboard keys.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, supports standard keyboard media keys, clean native interface.
* **Cons:** Some older TVs and specialized monitor docks only support software dimming instead of hardware control.
