### What it does in plain English
Hail is a handy mobile utility that lets you freeze, hide, or temporarily disable Android apps when you are not using them. By stopping background activity on demand, it prevents power-hungry software from draining your battery or slowing down your phone. You can easily unfreeze your apps whenever you need them again without losing any personal data or settings.

### Who it's for
* Android users wanting to stop rarely used apps from draining battery life in the background.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to hide specific applications from their device launcher.
* Phone owners seeking a free tool to improve overall device performance and save memory.

### Key features
* **Freeze Apps:** Instantly block and stop applications from running when you do not need them.
* **Hide and Suspend:** Keep specific software hidden from your launcher or place them in a suspended state.
* **Flexible Working Modes:** Compatible with multiple permission methods including device owner setups and privileged system access.
* **Task Automation:** Trigger freezes and launches quickly using custom shortcuts or automation tools.
* **Free and Open Source:** Built entirely as freedom software with no hidden subscriptions or paywalls.
