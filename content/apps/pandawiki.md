### What it does in plain English
PandaWiki is an intelligent knowledge base system that transforms how teams manage documentation, FAQs, and blogs. By connecting advanced artificial intelligence models directly to your content, it allows readers to chat with your documents and find instant answers. You can also import existing content from websites, files, or news feeds seamlessly.

### Who it's for
* Product managers and technical writers wanting to publish searchable user manuals.
* Customer support teams looking to deflect common questions with an automated assistant.
* Engineering squads organizing internal team notes and developer documentation.

### Key features
* Artificial intelligence assistants that generate content, answer reader questions, and power smart search.
* Flexible editor compatible with standard document formats and text styles.
* Content importing tools that pull information from web pages, site maps, and offline files.
* Integration options to embed widgets on external websites or connect with team messaging bots.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Powerful artificial intelligence query features, flexible content import options, open-source core.
* **Cons:** Requires a dedicated Linux server with container support for self-hosting, initial model configuration needed.
