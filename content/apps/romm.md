### What it does in plain English
RomM is a complete digital shelf and player for classic video games that you run on your own home server. It automatically scans your game files, matches them with official artwork and descriptions from online databases, and lets you jump straight into gameplay without installing extra software. Your saved game progress is safely synchronized across multiple devices so you never lose your place.

### Who it's for
* Retro gaming enthusiasts with large collections of classic game files who want a neat, modern library interface.
* People who want to play their favorite childhood games directly inside a web browser on laptops, tablets, or phones.
* Gamers who want a unified hub to manage game saves and track achievements across different devices and handheld consoles.

### Key features
* Play classic titles straight in your web browser with zero complicated setup required.
* Automatically fetch colorful box art, game descriptions, and developer details from multiple online databases.
* Synchronize your saved game files securely across all your devices with built-in conflict resolution.
* Share your private game library with friends and family using granular user permissions and single sign-on.
* Apply fan translations and modifications on the fly using the built-in patching tool.
