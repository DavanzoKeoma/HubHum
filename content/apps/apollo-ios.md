### What it does in plain English
It lets your iPhone, iPad or Mac app ask a GraphQL server for exactly the data it needs and gives you ready‑made Swift objects instead of raw JSON. It automatically remembers previous answers so the app stays fast and up‑to‑date. It checks your queries while you write code, catching mistakes early.

### Who it's for
iOS developers who want to fetch data from GraphQL services without hand‑coding parsing, and small teams that value type‑safe, reliable code.

### Key features
- Write GraphQL queries and get matching Swift structs automatically.  
- No need to parse JSON by hand.  
- Built‑in cache keeps data fresh with minimal network calls.  
- Mistakes appear while you code, not at runtime.  
- Works on iOS, macOS, tvOS and watchOS.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free and open source, type‑safe Swift objects, automatic caching, works across Apple platforms.  
* **Cons:** requires familiarity with Swift and Xcode, initial setup takes a few minutes.
