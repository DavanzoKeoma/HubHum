### What it does in plain English
sherpa-onnx lets you convert spoken words into text, generate lifelike speech from written documents, and process audio files directly on your local device. Because it operates entirely offline, your private recordings and personal voice data never leave your computer or phone. It supports a wide array of everyday audio tasks, from writing notes using your voice to cleaning up background noise or separating mixed audio tracks.

### Who it's for
* Students and professionals who want to transcribe lectures or interviews privately without uploading audio to external cloud servers.
* Developers and hobbyists building custom voice-controlled applications, smart assistants, or offline reading tools.
* Accessibility users looking for reliable, free text-to-speech and voice navigation tools that respect personal data privacy.

### Key features
* **100% Offline Processing:** Runs completely on your device hardware without requiring an active internet connection.
* **Comprehensive Speech Tools:** Handles speech-to-speech, text-to-speech, speaker identification, noise reduction, and voice activity detection.
* **Multi-Platform Support:** Works seamlessly across Windows, mobile devices, single-board computers, and various hardware accelerators.
* **Multi-Language Compatibility:** Supports numerous languages and regional dialects out of the box with pre-trained models.
* **Lightweight and Efficient:** Designed to perform well on everyday hardware as well as embedded and mobile systems.
