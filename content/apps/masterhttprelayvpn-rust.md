### What it does in plain English
This lightweight program creates a secure tunnel from your device through a personal script hosted on your own Google account. Your internet service provider only sees standard traffic heading to Google's trusted network, making it impossible for them to monitor which blocked websites you are visiting. It operates efficiently in the background without needing complex software installations or background services.

### Who it's for
* Everyday internet users living in regions with restricted web access who want a reliable, free method to visit their favorite sites.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to mask their browsing destinations from local network monitors.
* People seeking a simple, standalone tool that works across computers and mobile devices without complicated configurations.

### Key features
* Bypasses strict network firewalls by leveraging Google's edge network as a discreet relay.
* Completely free to operate utilizing the generous standard allowances of your personal Google account.
* Distributed as a single compact file with zero complex runtime requirements or hidden dependencies.
* Compatible with standard web browsers and system-wide proxy configurations on multiple desktop and mobile platforms.
* Includes straightforward diagnostic tools and clear connection status indicators to troubleshoot connectivity quickly.
