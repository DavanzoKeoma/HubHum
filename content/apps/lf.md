### What it does in plain English
lf is a lightweight tool designed to help you browse, copy, move, and organize files on your computer without needing a mouse. It runs inside your terminal window, making it incredibly fast and efficient for handling large folders or complex file structures. Because it is built to be minimal, it uses very little memory and starts up instantly whenever you need it.

### Who it's for
* Power users and developers who prefer keyboard-based navigation over clicking through folders.
* People working on older or low-power computers who want to save system resources.

### Key features
* **Keyboard-driven navigation:** Move through your folders using familiar shortcuts similar to text editors.
* **Extremely lightweight:** Starts instantly and uses minimal memory, even when managing thousands of files.
* **Highly customizable:** Tailor the controls and appearance to match your personal workflow perfectly.
* **Cross-platform:** Works consistently across Linux, macOS, Windows, and BSD systems.
* **Non-intrusive:** It focuses on file management and lets you use your favorite external tools for editing or viewing files.
