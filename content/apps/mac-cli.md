### What it does in plain English
Mac-CLI is a collection of shortcuts that lets you control your computer's settings and maintenance tasks through text commands. Instead of digging through menus, you can quickly perform actions like checking your internet speed, clearing your trash, or managing network settings. It is designed to save you time by automating repetitive tasks that you would otherwise do manually.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want to speed up their daily computer management.
* People who enjoy using text-based tools to perform system maintenance quickly.

### Key features
* **System Control:** Easily manage power settings like sleep, restart, or shutdown.
* **Network Tools:** Quickly check your connection speed, local IP, or manage Wi-Fi and DNS settings.
* **File Management:** Efficiently find large files, manage hidden files, or compress and extract archives.
* **Maintenance:** Keep your system running smoothly by monitoring memory usage and emptying the trash with one command.
* **Git Integration:** Simplify your project management with quick commands to handle branches and updates.

### How to install (Download)
To install Mac-CLI, open your terminal and run one of the following commands:

**Using curl:**
`sh -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guarinogabriel/mac-cli/master/mac-cli/tools/install)"`

**Using wget:**
`sh -c "$(wget https://raw.githubusercontent.com/guarinogabriel/mac-cli/master/mac-cli/tools/install -O -)"`

After running the command, restart your terminal to begin using the tool.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, open-source, and significantly speeds up routine system tasks.
* **Cons:** Requires using the terminal interface, which may have a learning curve for those unfamiliar with text-based commands.
