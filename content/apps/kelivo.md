### What it does in plain English
Kelivo brings all your favorite artificial intelligence models together into a single, clean application for your phone or computer. You can connect your accounts or keys to switch effortlessly between different services without opening multiple tabs or apps. It also gives your conversations an active workspace where models can read files, search the web, and help you get real tasks done.

### Who it's for
* Students and writers looking to compare answers from multiple artificial intelligence models in one place.
* Developers and power users who want an organized workspace with file editing and local sandbox terminal support.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer keeping their conversation history stored locally on their own hardware.

### Key features
* Access multiple major chat models and assistant providers in a unified application interface.
* Bind chats to custom workspaces so models can read, write, and interact with local project files.
* Built-in support for web searching, text-to-speech, and secure offline data backups.
* Keep your conversation history completely private with local device storage and optional WebDAV or S3 backups.
* Cross-platform design that runs smoothly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
