### What it does in plain English
AutoSubs automatically listens to your video or audio files and creates accurate, timestamped subtitles for you. Because it runs entirely on your own machine, your data stays private and never leaves your computer. It even connects directly to popular video editing software to make your workflow faster and easier.

### Who it's for
*   **Video Creators:** Editors who want to add captions to their projects quickly without paying for expensive cloud-based services.
*   **Accessibility Advocates:** People who need to create transcripts or subtitles for their content to make it more inclusive for all viewers.

### Key features
*   **Total Privacy:** Everything is processed locally on your hardware, so you never have to worry about your files being uploaded to a server.
*   **Direct Integration:** Works seamlessly with professional video editing tools to import and export subtitles directly into your timeline.
*   **Speaker Recognition:** Automatically identifies different people in your audio and labels them so you can style their text individually.
*   **Multilingual Support:** Transcribes and translates over 1,000 languages using a variety of specialized AI models.
*   **Flexible Export:** Save your work as standard subtitle files, plain text, or copy it directly to your clipboard.
