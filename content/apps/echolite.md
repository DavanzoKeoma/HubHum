### What it does in plain English
Echolite is a user-friendly application designed to download music tracks, albums, and playlists from multiple online platforms. It automatically saves essential song details such as artist names, release years, lyrics, and cover art right into the downloaded audio files. You can also organize your downloads into custom folders and adjust volume levels to match your listening preferences.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want to save offline copies of their favorite streaming tracks and playlists.
* Listeners who care about keeping their audio library neatly organized with proper tags, titles, and album artwork.
* Users looking for an easy-to-use window application to manage downloads across different platforms without complicated setups.

### Key features
* Simple graphical interface for quick and straightforward downloads.
* Supports popular platforms including Spotify and YouTube.
* Automatically embeds detailed song information like lyrics and cover art.
* Allows multiple simultaneous downloads to speed up the process.
* Flexible naming rules and subfolder creation based on track metadata.
