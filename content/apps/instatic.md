### What it does in plain English
Instatic is a complete, self-hosted platform that combines a visual design editor, a content management system, and a website publisher into one package. It allows you to build professional-looking websites without relying on complex, bloated software or third-party subscriptions. The final result is a clean, fast website that consists of simple files, ensuring you maintain full ownership and control over your digital content.

### Who it's for
* **Independent creators and small business owners** who want a professional website without monthly platform fees.
* **Web designers** who need a reliable, self-hosted environment to build and manage client sites with full control over the final code.

### Key features
* **Visual Canvas Editor:** Design your site in real-time with a live preview that lets you see exactly how your changes look across different screen sizes.
* **Clean Output:** The system generates standard, high-quality web code without unnecessary background software, ensuring your site loads almost instantly.
* **Integrated Data Management:** Create custom collections, blog posts, and contact forms directly within the platform without needing extra plugins or services.
* **Self-Hosted Ownership:** You run the software on your own server or preferred hosting provider, meaning you are never locked into a specific company's ecosystem.
* **AI-Assisted Building:** Use built-in tools to describe what you want to create, and the system will generate the corresponding layout and structure for you.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, no monthly subscription fees, produces extremely fast and clean web pages, and includes a powerful visual editor.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server cloud setup, and as a newer project, some advanced features are still being actively developed.
