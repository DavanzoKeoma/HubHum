### What it does in plain English
Sill turns your iPhone or iPad into a secondary touch-screen display for your Mac. It allows you to interact with your computer's windows using your fingers, a trackpad, or an Apple Pencil as if you were sitting right in front of your desk. The connection is private and direct, meaning your data stays between your devices without needing to pass through external servers.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who want to use their iPad as a portable touch-screen monitor for specific apps.
* Professionals who need to control their computer from another room or while moving around their workspace.

### Key features
* **Touch and Pencil Support:** Use your iPad screen to click, scroll, and draw inside Mac applications.
* **Flexible Connectivity:** Connect via USB cable, local Wi-Fi, or even over a private network connection when you are away from home.
* **No Middlemen:** The app does not require accounts or cloud servers, keeping your activity entirely local and private.
* **Versatile Layouts:** Automatically adjusts the interface for iPhone, iPad, and foldable devices to ensure the best viewing experience.
* **Clipboard Sharing:** Easily copy text on your Mac and paste it onto your mobile device, or vice versa.
