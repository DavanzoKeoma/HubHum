### What it does in plain English
Laminar acts as a control center for your AI agents, allowing you to see exactly what they are doing while they work. It records every step of an agent's process so you can spot errors, understand why a mistake happened, and fix it quickly. You can also set up alerts to get notified immediately if your agent gets stuck or behaves unexpectedly.

### Who it's for
* Software developers building custom AI-powered tools and automated assistants.
* Data scientists who need to test and improve the quality of AI responses.

### Key features
* **Live Activity Tracking:** See exactly how your AI agents process information as it happens.
* **Smart Alerts:** Get notified in your team chat if your agent runs into a loop or a specific error.
* **Performance Testing:** Use built-in tools to compare different versions of your AI to see which one works better.
* **Easy Search:** Quickly find specific past interactions to understand how your agent handled a particular task.
* **High Efficiency:** Built to handle large amounts of data without slowing down your systems.

### How to install (Download)
* **Managed Version:** The fastest way to start is by using the [Official Laminar Platform](https://laminar.sh).
* **Self-Hosted:** You can run Laminar on your own hardware using Docker. Follow the instructions in the [Laminar GitHub official project page](https://github.com/lmnr-ai/lmnr) to get started with a local setup.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely fast, works with most popular AI tools, and offers both managed and self-hosted options.
* **Cons:** Requires some initial setup to connect your AI agents to the platform.
