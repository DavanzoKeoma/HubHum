### What it does in plain English
IncarnaMind is an open-source desktop application that connects your local files with modern artificial intelligence tools. When an assistant drafts notes or answers questions, the app cross-references every sentence and figure directly against your document library. It operates securely on your computer, allowing you to organize files, review live meeting transcripts, and export edited documents without compromising your privacy.

### Who it's for
* **Researchers and Analysts:** Professionals who need to verify AI-generated summaries and claims against source documents.
* **Writers and Editors:** Individuals looking to manage a local research library and export clean Word documents with verified citations.
* **Privacy-Conscious Users:** People who want local AI workflows and secure file organization without uploading personal data to third-party servers.

### Key features
* **Sentence-Level Verification:** Automatically checks AI writing against source passages with one-click page navigation.
* **Unified Local Library:** Indexes and searches PDF, Word, PowerPoint, and Excel files right where they are stored.
* **Meeting Transcription:** Generates live meeting transcripts on your computer and cites who said what.
* **Flexible AI Integration:** Connects with local models via Ollama, existing subscriptions, or custom API keys.
* **Word Document Editing:** Exports edited documents while keeping untouched sections completely intact.

### How to install (Download)
You can download the latest installation files directly from the [Official Releases Page](https://github.com/IncarnaMind/IncarnaMind/releases). 
* **macOS:** Download the `.dmg` file for Apple Silicon processors.
* **Windows:** Download the `.exe` installer.
* **Linux:** Download the `.deb` package or the `.AppImage` executable.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license, works locally with total data privacy, provides precise sentence-level citations.
* **Cons:** Requires a compatible AI model key or local setup to generate answers, and test builds are currently unsigned.
