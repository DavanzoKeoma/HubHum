### What it does in plain English
Gifski is a handy Mac tool that converts standard video files into exceptionally smooth, high-quality animated GIFs. Instead of clunky, grainy color transitions, it blends thousands of colors per frame to keep your animations looking crisp and professional. You can easily adjust dimensions, playback speed, quality sliders, and looping effects to fit whatever project you are working on.

### Who it's for
* Content creators who want to showcase smooth design work or screen recordings on portfolio platforms.
* Social media managers needing quick, eye-catching moving images from standard video clips.
* Everyday Mac users looking for a straightforward, native way to share short video moments without heavy file sizes.

### Key features
* **Advanced Color Blending:** Produces animated GIFs using thousands of vibrant colors per frame for gorgeous visual results.
* **Share Extension & Quick Actions:** Convert videos instantly straight from your system share menu or Finder shortcuts without opening the main app window.
* **Bounce Playback:** Create looping animations that smoothly reverse back and forth like modern phone live photos.
* **Custom Adjustments:** Fine-tune video dimensions, frame rates up to 50 FPS, cropping, and compression quality with a handy slider.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Quickly copy your finished GIF directly to your clipboard or save it using simple keystrokes.

* **Pros:** Exceptional visual quality, native macOS integration, easy keyboard shortcuts, and multiple quick-conversion options.
* **Cons:** Limited to macOS users, and large video files can result in heavy GIF sizes if settings are not adjusted.
