### What it does in plain English
Fluent Reader acts as a central hub for all the websites and blogs you follow, pulling their latest updates into one single window. It allows you to read articles comfortably without having to visit dozens of different sites manually. You can organize your subscriptions into folders, save articles for later, and filter out content you aren't interested in.

### Who it's for
* People who follow many blogs, news sites, or journals and want to stay updated efficiently.
* Users who prefer a distraction-free, organized reading environment over browsing multiple tabs.

### Key features
* **Unified Reading:** Collects updates from all your favorite sources in one place.
* **Smart Organization:** Group your subscriptions into folders to keep your feed tidy.
* **Custom Rules:** Automatically hide or mark articles as read based on keywords.
* **Offline Access:** Read your saved articles even when you don't have an internet connection.
* **Modern Design:** A clean, visually pleasing interface that supports both light and dark modes.
