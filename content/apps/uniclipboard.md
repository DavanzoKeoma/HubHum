### What it does in plain English
UniClipboard allows you to copy text, images, or files on one computer or phone and instantly paste them on another. It works by creating a private, encrypted connection directly between your own devices. Because it does not rely on third-party cloud accounts, your data stays under your control and is never stored on a central server.

### Who it's for
*   **Remote workers** who frequently move files and text between a work laptop and a personal desktop.
*   **Privacy-conscious users** who want to sync data across devices without trusting a big tech company with their clipboard history.

### Key features
*   **Direct Sync:** Connects your devices directly over your local network or the internet for fast, real-time sharing.
*   **End-to-End Encryption:** All your copied items are scrambled so that only your own devices can read them.
*   **Searchable History:** Keep a private, encrypted log of everything you have copied, making it easy to find past items.
*   **No Accounts Required:** You do not need to sign up for any service; you simply pair your devices using a secure code.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly across Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile devices.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely private, no subscriptions or cloud accounts needed, and works offline on your local network.
*   **Cons:** Requires manual pairing of devices, and advanced features like command-line usage may have a slight learning curve for non-technical users.
