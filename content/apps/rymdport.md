### What it does in plain English
Rymdport is an application that lets you transfer files, entire folders, and blocks of text directly between different devices. All data sent across the network is protected with strong end-to-end encryption to keep your personal information completely private. It uses a trusted protocol that allows it to work seamlessly with compatible secure sharing tools on various operating systems.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who want a quick, private way to move photos or documents between a computer and another device.
* Remote workers and students needing a friction-free method to share text snippets or folders locally or across networks without setting up cloud accounts.

### Key features
* Send files, folders, and text securely with end-to-end encryption.
* Fully cross-platform support across major operating systems.
* Lightweight native performance with no background runtime dependencies.
* Compatible with other standard secure transfer tools and command-line clients.
