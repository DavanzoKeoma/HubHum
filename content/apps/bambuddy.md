### What it does in plain English
Bambuddy acts as a private command center for your 3D printers, allowing you to manage your devices directly over your local network. It removes the need for external cloud services, ensuring your print history, camera feeds, and printer settings remain entirely under your control. You can organize your entire print farm, schedule jobs, and monitor progress from a single, easy-to-use interface.

### Who it's for
* **Home hobbyists** who want to keep their 3D printing data private and offline.
* **Small business owners or makerspaces** managing multiple printers that need centralized control and job scheduling.

### Key features
* **Local Control:** Manage your printers directly via your local network without relying on manufacturer cloud servers.
* **Centralized Dashboard:** View and control your entire collection of printers from one screen, including live camera feeds.
* **Smart Scheduling:** Queue up multiple print jobs, set specific start times, and automate your workflow.
* **Print History:** Keep a detailed, searchable log of every print you have ever made, including material usage and cost tracking.
* **Flexible Access:** Set up user accounts with specific permissions to manage who can start prints or change settings.

* **Pros:** Keeps all your data private, works entirely offline, and offers advanced features for managing multiple printers.
* **Cons:** Requires a bit of initial setup to connect your printers, and some advanced features may take time to master.
