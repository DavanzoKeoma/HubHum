### What it does in plain English
Codex App Manager is a handy desktop assistant designed to handle the installation, updates, and clean removal of your favorite workspace tools. It checks your device for existing versions, downloads lightweight patches to save time, and helps keep everything running smoothly. You can also customize your workspace appearance with visual themes and launch your tools with a single click.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users who want a hassle-free way to keep their software fresh and up-to-date without technical headaches.
* Remote workers and enthusiasts who appreciate fast downloads and smooth software updates.

### Key features
* **One-Stop Control:** Automatically detects your current software setup and manages installations, updates, and removals in one place.
* **Smart Patching:** Downloads only the necessary update pieces to save bandwidth and speed up installation times.
* **Theme Customization:** Easily apply custom visual skins to change the look and feel of your workspace apps.
* **Automatic Self-Updates:** Keeps the manager itself fresh and secure using reliable global servers.
* **Multi-Language Support:** Automatically adjusts to your preferred system language for a comfortable experience.
