### What it does in plain English
OpenEMR is a comprehensive tool for medical practices that helps healthcare providers keep track of patient health records, appointments, and medical billing in one organized place. It eliminates the need for expensive commercial software by offering a completely open platform that handles day-to-day administrative tasks. Clinics and practitioners can easily customize the system to fit their unique workflow and securely store vital patient information.

### Who it's for
* Independent medical practitioners and small clinics looking for a reliable, no-cost record system.
* Healthcare administrators who need to manage patient scheduling and billing efficiently.
* IT professionals and developers setting up customized digital infrastructure for medical offices.

### Key features
* Complete electronic health record management for tracking patient history and treatment notes.
* Integrated scheduling calendar to manage patient appointments and provider availability.
* Built-in medical billing tools to streamline insurance claims and patient invoices.
* Multilingual support allowing medical teams to work in their preferred language.
* High level of customizability and security to protect sensitive healthcare data.
