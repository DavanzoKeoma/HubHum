### What it does in plain English
Unyo brings your favorite Japanese animation and illustrated comics together in a single, streamlined desktop application. It lets you browse, stream, and read without disruptive advertisements ruining the experience. By connecting directly to your tracking profile, it keeps your watched episodes and read chapters seamlessly updated in the background.

### Who it's for
* Anime enthusiasts who want a clean, ad-free desktop hub for watching their favorite shows.
* Manga readers looking for a unified application to catch up on serialized illustrated stories.
* Fans who want their viewing and reading history automatically synced with online tracking profiles.

### Key features
* Ad-free interface designed specifically for comfortable desktop viewing and reading.
* Seamless connection with online anime and manga tracking services for automatic list syncing.
* Extension support allowing you to expand your media library through community extensions.
* Smooth animations and a polished, easy-to-navigate user experience.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no ads, automatic watch-list synchronization, active community extension support.
* **Cons:** Requires a public or semi-public tracking profile to sync lists, unsigned builds may trigger initial security warnings on your operating system.
