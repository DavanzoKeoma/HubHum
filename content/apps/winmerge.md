### What it does in plain English
WinMerge is a helpful tool that lets you look at two files or folders side-by-side to see exactly what is different between them. It highlights changes in a clear, visual way so you can quickly spot what was added, removed, or modified. Once you see the differences, you can easily copy changes from one side to the other to keep your files perfectly synced.

### Who it's for
*   **Writers and Students:** Perfect for comparing different versions of a document to see exactly what edits were made.
*   **Computer Users:** Great for anyone who needs to organize folders and wants to quickly identify duplicate or updated files.

### Key features
*   **Side-by-Side Comparison:** View two files or folders at once with differences highlighted for easy reading.
*   **Merge Changes:** Easily move text or files from one side to the other with a simple click.
*   **Smart Filters:** Use custom rules to ignore specific file types or folders you don't want to compare.
*   **Right-Click Integration:** Access the tool directly from your Windows file menu for instant comparisons.
*   **Archive Support:** Look inside compressed folders to compare files without needing to extract them first.
