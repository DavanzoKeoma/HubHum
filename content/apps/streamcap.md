### What it does in plain English
StreamCap is a dedicated tool that watches your favorite live streaming channels and saves them to your computer the moment they go live. It works with over 40 popular global platforms, allowing you to schedule recordings or monitor multiple channels at once. Once the recording finishes, the app can even automatically convert the files into a standard format for easy viewing.

### Who it's for
* Content enthusiasts who want to save live broadcasts for offline viewing later.
* People who manage archives of live events and need a reliable, automated way to capture them.

### Key features
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works with over 40 different international and local live streaming websites.
* **Automatic Monitoring:** Keeps a constant watch on your chosen channels and starts recording instantly when a broadcast begins.
* **Flexible Scheduling:** Set specific time windows for the app to check for and record live content.
* **Format Conversion:** Automatically turns your saved recordings into common video or audio files like MP4.
* **Status Notifications:** Get alerts so you know exactly when your favorite streams are active.
