### What it does in plain English
This tool allows you to keep multiple Claude accounts logged in on your computer at the same time. It tracks how much you have used each account and can automatically switch you to a different one before you hit your usage limits. You can also run specific tasks using different accounts in separate windows without affecting your main setup.

### Who it's for
* Power users who frequently hit usage limits on their Claude accounts.
* Professionals who need to separate work and personal Claude sessions on the same machine.

### Key features
* **Automatic Rotation:** Switches accounts for you based on remaining quota so you never hit a hard stop.
* **Live Dashboard:** See exactly how much usage remains for every account at a glance.
* **Parallel Sessions:** Run different accounts in separate terminal windows simultaneously.
* **Easy Setup:** Add new accounts without having to sign out of your current one.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on macOS, Windows, and Linux.
