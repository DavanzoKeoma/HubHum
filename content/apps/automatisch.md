### What it does in plain English
Automatisch connects different web applications together so they can share information and perform actions automatically. When a specific event happens in one tool, it triggers an instant reaction in another without manual effort. You can set up custom pipelines for marketing, customer support, or data management right from your own hardware.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners looking to reduce manual data entry across different platforms.
* Privacy-conscious teams that handle sensitive customer information and need local data storage.
* Freelancers and creators wanting to eliminate routine administrative tasks without paying monthly tool fees.

### Key features
* Connects popular web apps and communication tools to pass data back and forth seamlessly.
* Visual workflow builder that requires zero programming or technical setup knowledge.
* Total data ownership by keeping all your automation logs and credentials on your own server.
* Full compliance support for strict data protection regulations like GDPR.
* Freedom to switch providers at any time without getting locked into a single proprietary ecosystem.
