### What it does in plain English
eul sits quietly in your computer's menu bar to give you a real-time overview of how hard your machine is working. It tracks important metrics like processor load, available memory, fan speeds, and battery percentages for both your laptop and connected accessories. With native support for modern dark themes and desktop widgets, it helps you keep an eye on your hardware health at a glance.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who want to keep track of system performance without opening heavy diagnostic tools.
* People who use wireless accessories and need quick visibility over Bluetooth battery levels.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, modern status viewer that blends seamlessly into the operating system.

### Key features
* Real-time monitoring of computer processor, memory, and storage usage.
* Bluetooth device battery level indicators right in your menu bar.
* Dark mode compatibility that automatically matches your system appearance settings.
* Built-in support for desktop widgets to view vital statistics instantly.
* Modern user interface designed specifically for macOS.

* **Cons:** Requires macOS 10.15 or newer, some advanced hardware sensor readings are limited.
