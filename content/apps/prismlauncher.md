### What it does in plain English
PrismLauncher acts as a central hub for your Minecraft experience, allowing you to keep different game setups completely separate. You can easily switch between different versions of the game, manage your custom modifications, and keep your worlds organized without any clutter. It simplifies the process of launching the game while giving you full control over your individual game environments.

### Who it's for
*   Players who enjoy using different game modifications and want to keep them organized in separate profiles.
*   Users who want a clean, reliable way to switch between different versions of Minecraft without manual file management.

### Key features
*   **Multiple Instances:** Create and manage as many separate Minecraft installations as you need.
*   **Easy Mod Management:** Add or remove modifications to your game setups with just a few clicks.
*   **Clean Interface:** A user-friendly dashboard that makes launching your preferred game version quick and simple.
*   **Customizable Settings:** Adjust memory usage, window size, and other game settings for each installation individually.
