### What it does in plain English
Piwigo is a personal photo gallery system that you host on your own web space. It helps you keep your digital memories organized with tags, albums, and powerful search tools. Because you control the software, you maintain full ownership and privacy over your entire photo library.

### Who it's for
*   **Families and Individuals:** People who want a private, ad-free space to share high-quality photos with friends and relatives.
*   **Organizations and Teams:** Groups that need a centralized, searchable database to manage professional assets or event photography.

### Key features
*   **Total Ownership:** You keep your photos on your own server, ensuring your data stays private and under your control.
*   **Smart Organization:** Use tags, categories, and dates to keep thousands of images easy to find.
*   **Customizable Look:** Choose from a wide variety of themes to make your gallery look exactly how you want.
*   **Extendable:** Access hundreds of community-made plugins to add new features like maps, calendars, or advanced slideshows.
*   **Easy Sharing:** Create private albums with specific permissions so only the people you choose can see your photos.

### How to install (Download)
*   **Ready-to-use:** If you prefer not to manage a server, you can use the [Piwigo Cloud](https://piwigo.org/get-piwigo#cloud) service.
*   **Self-Hosted:** Download the [latest stable version](https://piwigo.org/download/dlcounter.php?code=latest) to install on your own web server.
*   **Docker:** Follow the [official Docker installation guide](https://doc.piwigo.org/self-hosting-piwigo/install-guides/docker-install/) for a containerized setup.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open source, highly customizable, and gives you full privacy.
*   **Cons:** Requires your own web server and basic knowledge of how to set up a website.
