### What it does in plain English
Optimizer helps you take full control of your Windows computer by cleaning out unwanted background clutter and tightening your digital privacy. It removes built-in tracking features, stops annoying automatic updates, and turns off unwanted background tasks that slow down your machine. You can also use it to quickly install essential programs, manage startup items, and clean up your system drive in just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Everyday Windows users wanting to speed up a sluggish computer after a fresh setup.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to disable background tracking and data collection.
* Power users who want a single dashboard to manage system tweaks, DNS settings, and startup apps.

### Key features
* Protects your privacy by turning off built-in tracking and telemetry features.
* Boosts overall computer and network performance by disabling unneeded background services.
* Cleans system drives and browser caches to free up valuable storage space.
* Manages startup programs, browser settings, and network DNS servers easily.
* Fully supports multiple languages and allows custom automated configuration files.
