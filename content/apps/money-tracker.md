### What it does in plain English
Money Tracker is a handy digital tool designed to help you keep a close eye on your daily spending and earnings. It saves your financial information directly onto your device so you can view and update your budget even when you do not have an internet connection. Whenever you reconnect, your records can securely sync to the cloud so your data is always up to date across all your devices.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a straightforward, privacy-focused way to manage their monthly household budget.
* Freelancers who need a quick mobile-friendly tool to log variable income and business expenses on the go.
* Anyone wanting a free, offline-capable finance tracker without complex spreadsheets or invasive sign-ups.

### Key features
* **Offline-First Functionality:** Access and update your financial records anytime on your phone, tablet, or computer without needing Wi-Fi.
* **Local Data Storage:** Keep your personal monetary history safely stored right on your device for complete privacy.
* **Cloud Synchronization:** Seamlessly back up and sync your financial logs across multiple screens when connected.
* **Progressive Web App:** Install directly to your device home screen for a fast, app-like experience without app store downloads.
