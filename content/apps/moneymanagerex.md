### What it does in plain English
Money Manager Ex is a straightforward, open-source personal finance application designed to help you understand where your money goes. It lets you monitor checking accounts, credit cards, investments, and cash flow in one private place without requiring technical expertise. You can easily categorize transactions, set up bill reminders, and view clear graphs of your spending habits.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a free, offline-capable way to track everyday household expenses and income.
* Freelancers and families who want multi-account budgeting without paying for expensive subscription software.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer storing their financial records locally in an encrypted database.

### Key features
* **Complete Account Tracking:** Manage checking, savings, credit cards, investments, and asset accounts in a single dashboard.
* **Budgeting and Forecasting:** Plan your spending with built-in cash flow forecasting and scheduled payment reminders.
* **Visual Reports:** Generate clear, one-click charts and graphs to analyze your income and expenses instantly.
* **Secure Local Storage:** Keeps all your financial data safe in an encrypted, non-proprietary local database file.
* **Multi-Platform Access:** Use it across desktop and mobile devices seamlessly with data portability.
