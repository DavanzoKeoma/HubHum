### What it does in plain English
Palmier Pro is a modern video editing application designed specifically for Apple computers. It integrates intelligent generative technology directly into your timeline workspace so you can create visual clips seamlessly. You can also connect popular external assistant tools to work alongside you on video projects.

### Who it's for
* Content creators looking to speed up video production with intelligent automation
* Mac enthusiasts seeking a lightweight, native editing tool built for modern hardware
* Video producers experimenting with artificial intelligence models in their daily workflow

### Key features
* Native Swift performance built from the ground up for Apple Silicon processors
* Integrated generative AI models for creating visual assets right inside the timeline
* Intelligent agent support allowing external assistants to interact with your projects
* Smooth timeline workspace familiar to traditional professional video editors
