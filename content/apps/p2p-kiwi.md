### What it does in plain English
p2p.kiwi is a straightforward tool that lets you share your computer screen with others instantly. It connects you directly to the person you are talking to, meaning your data does not pass through a central company server. Because it requires no account or sign-up, you can start collaborating or troubleshooting with friends and colleagues in seconds.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers who need a quick way to show their screen to a teammate.
* Friends or family members helping each other with technical computer issues.

### Key features
* **No Account Required:** Start sharing immediately without providing personal information or creating a login.
* **Direct Connection:** Uses peer-to-peer technology to keep your screen data moving directly between participants.
* **Strong Privacy:** Built-in security ensures that your shared media remains private and protected from start to finish.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly across Windows, Mac, and Linux computers.
