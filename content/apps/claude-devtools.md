### What it does in plain English
When you use an AI assistant to write code, it often hides the detailed steps it takes, leaving you with only vague summaries. This tool reads the hidden logs on your computer and turns them into a clear, easy-to-read visual dashboard. You can see exactly which files were changed, how much "fuel" (tokens) was used, and what the AI was thinking at every step.

### Who it's for
* **Developers using AI coding assistants:** People who want to understand why their AI made a specific code change or error.
* **Technical teams:** Managers or leads who need to audit AI performance and token costs without digging through raw data.
* **Curious tech users:** Anyone who wants to peek behind the curtain of how AI tools interact with their computer files.

### Key features
* **Visual Session Log:** See a clean, step-by-step timeline of every action the AI took, instead of a wall of confusing text.
* **Token Usage Tracker:** Understand exactly where your AI budget is going by seeing a breakdown of token usage for each part of the conversation.
* **File Change Viewer:** View clear before-and-after comparisons of code edits, highlighting exactly what was added or removed.
* **Subagent Inspector:** Watch how the AI breaks down complex tasks into smaller jobs and track the progress of each sub-task.
* **One-Click Copy:** Easily copy code snippets and text from the AI's output without formatting errors or broken characters.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, open-source, and works locally on your machine without sending data to third-party servers. It provides a much clearer view of AI actions than standard terminal output.
* **Cons:** It is designed specifically for users of the "Claude Code" AI tool, so it won't work with other AI assistants. The interface is detailed and may take a moment to get used to if you are not familiar with developer tools.
