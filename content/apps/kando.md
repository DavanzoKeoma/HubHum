### What it does in plain English
Kando adds a customizable, circular menu to your computer screen that appears exactly where you need it. You can use it to open your favorite programs, trigger keyboard shortcuts, or manage files with a quick flick of your mouse or touch input. It is designed to make your daily computer tasks feel faster and more fluid.

### Who it's for
*   Power users who want to speed up their workflow by reducing mouse travel across the screen.
*   Creative professionals using tablets or touchscreens who prefer gesture-based controls over traditional menus.

### Key features
*   **Circular Interface:** A unique, space-saving menu layout that puts your most-used tools within easy reach.
*   **Universal Input:** Works seamlessly with a mouse, stylus, touchscreens, game controllers, or a standard keyboard.
*   **Fully Customizable:** Create your own menus tailored to your specific needs and daily habits.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Runs smoothly on Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
