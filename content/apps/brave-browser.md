### What it does in plain English
Brave Browser is a modern web browser designed to put your privacy first while speeding up your daily internet habits. It works behind the scenes to automatically block intrusive advertisements, pop-ups, and hidden tracking scripts that slow down page loading. Because these elements are removed before they reach your screen, your device uses less data and battery power while you browse.

### Who it's for
* Everyday internet users looking for a faster, cleaner, and less distracting browsing experience.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to stop companies from following their online activity across websites.
* Mobile and laptop users who want to reduce cellular data usage and preserve battery life while browsing on the go.

### Key features
* Automatically blocks ads, trackers, and malicious scripts to speed up page loads.
* Built-in privacy shields that let you customize how much protection you want per website.
* Saves mobile and desktop data by preventing unwanted background content from downloading.
* Familiar interface that supports standard web extensions and works just like other popular browsers.
