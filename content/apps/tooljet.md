### What it does in plain English
ToolJet helps you build custom internal dashboards, admin panels, and operational tools by dragging and dropping visual elements. You can connect these tools directly to your existing databases and business systems to manage data without manual work. When accessing these sensitive administrative tools from public networks, it is recommended to secure your connection using [PureVPN](https://billing.purevpn.com/aff.php?aff=49386762&subid=hubhum_01_MER047) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* **Business Owners:** Who need custom internal tools to track operations without hiring a large software team.
* **Operations Managers:** Who want to automate data entry and reporting workflows.
* **Developers:** Who want to speed up the creation of internal admin panels by using a visual interface.

### Key features
* **Visual Builder:** Drag and drop over 80 components like charts, tables, and forms to build your interface.
* **Data Connectivity:** Connect to over 90 different data sources, including databases and popular business software.
* **AI Assistance:** Generate entire pages, queries, or components simply by describing what you need in plain language.
* **SEO Optimization:** Once your web portal is live, improve search indexing and rich previews with [Rank Math Direct](https://rankmath.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER276) (#ad).
* **Collaboration:** Work with your team in real-time with shared editing and granular access controls.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Massive library of pre-built components, supports AI-driven app generation, and offers flexible self-hosting options.
* **Cons:** Advanced features and custom plugin development may require a brief learning period for non-technical users.
