### What it does in plain English
Chhoto URL transforms long, messy web addresses into short, tidy links that are easy to share anywhere. It runs independently on your own server, meaning your data never passes through third-party advertising networks. The system instantly redirects visitors to their destination without forcing them to wait on frustrating intermediary countdown screens.

### Who it's for
* Individuals and bloggers who want complete control over their shared links without relying on commercial tracking services.
* System administrators looking for an extremely lightweight, low-memory utility to manage custom short links.
* Privacy advocates who require a straightforward redirect tool with zero tracking, cookies, or intrusive popups.

### Key features
* **Lightning Fast:** Engineered for incredible speed while consuming minimal system memory.
* **Instant Redirects:** Sends visitors straight to the destination page without annoying wait screens.
* **Privacy-First:** Counts link visits securely without storing personal visitor data or tracking logs.
* **Custom Customization:** Set custom expiry dates, organize links with personal notes, and generate QR codes instantly.
* **Flexible Modes:** Run privately behind a secure password or open it up for public link generation.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely low resource usage, completely free of corporate tracking, and simple to set up with Docker.
* **Cons:** Does not include built-in multi-user management dashboards, and requires manual server setup.
