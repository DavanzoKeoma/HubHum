### What it does in plain English
Meelo is a personal music streaming hub that lets you host your entire audio collection on your own hardware. It automatically organizes your songs, albums, and music videos by reading embedded tags or file names. You can stream your favorite tracks securely from anywhere while enjoying a clean, organized library without duplicates.

### Who it's for
* Dedicated music collectors with large digital audio libraries stored locally
* Privacy-conscious listeners who prefer managing their own media servers
* Anyone looking for a dedicated home for songs, rare tracks, and music videos

### Key features
* Seamless music video integration alongside standard audio tracks
* Automatic detection of rare tracks, B-sides, and featured artists
* Advanced album release grouping to keep your browsing pages clutter-free
* Flexible metadata parsing supporting embedded tags, file names, and cover art
* Built-in transcoding to ensure playback compatibility across different devices

### How to install (Download)
Meelo is designed to run on your own server using Docker containers. Follow the step-by-step instructions in the [Official Setup Wiki](https://github.com/Arthi-chaud/Meelo/wiki). 

If you want to access your music on the go, you can also download the mobile companion app:
* [Download for Android (.apk)](https://github.com/Arthi-chaud/Meelo/releases/download/v3.13.2/meelo.apk)
* iOS users can join testing via [TestFlight Setup Discussion](https://github.com/Arthi-chaud/Meelo/discussions/1533)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, powerful metadata organization, handles music videos natively, no subscription required.
* **Cons:** Requires technical setup via Docker, initial configuration of file tags or regular expressions may take time.
