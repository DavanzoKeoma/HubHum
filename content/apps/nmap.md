### What it does in plain English
Nmap helps you see what devices are on a network and what services they offer.  
It can quickly scan a single host or an entire subnet.  
The results show open ports and potential security gaps.

### Who it's for
- Network administrators who need quick checks.  
- Home users curious about their own network.  
- IT support staff troubleshooting connectivity.

### Key features
- Fast scanning of IP ranges.  
- Detects open ports and running services.  
- Supports multiple scan types (TCP, UDP, ping).  
- Provides detailed output in text or XML.  
- Includes a graphical front‑end called Zenmap.
