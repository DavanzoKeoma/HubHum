### What it does in plain English
PrestaShop provides everything you need to start selling products on the internet. It acts as the engine for your online store, handling your product catalog, customer orders, and payment collections. Because it is highly customizable, you can change the look and feel of your shop to match your unique brand.

### Who it's for
*   Small business owners looking to launch their first professional online store.
*   Established retailers who want to move their inventory to a self-managed digital platform.

### Key features
*   **Product Management:** Easily organize your items, categories, and inventory levels.
*   **Global Payments:** Connects with major payment services to accept money from customers worldwide.
*   **Responsive Design:** Your store will look great on computers, tablets, and smartphones automatically.
*   **Customizable Look:** Choose from many themes to give your shop a professional and unique appearance.
*   **Multilingual Support:** Reach customers in different countries by offering your store in many languages.

### How to install (Download)
To set up your own store, visit the official website to download the latest stable version:
[Download PrestaShop 9.0](https://www.prestashop-project.org/releases/prestashop90/)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly flexible, and supported by a massive global community.
*   **Cons:** Requires your own web hosting space and basic knowledge of how to set up a website on a server.
