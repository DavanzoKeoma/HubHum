### What it does in plain English
OpenDesign brings your creative team and coding assistants together into a single open workspace to build interactive digital projects. You provide a brief or direction, and the built-in agents generate functional prototypes, website layouts, motion graphics, or slide decks. Team members can easily review the work, exchange live feedback, and refine designs directly inside the same project.

### Who it's for
* **Product teams and designers** wanting to quickly sketch out interactive app prototypes and websites using natural language prompts.
* **Content creators and marketers** needing to generate motion graphics, promotional videos, social carousels, and pitch decks effortlessly.
* **Developers** looking to bridge the gap between design systems and clean front-end code through automated coding assistant tools.

### Key features
* **Multi-Format Studio:** Create web prototypes, mobile app flows, presentations, images, and videos in one place.
* **Agent Integration:** Connects with popular coding assistants and command-line tools to turn ideas into reality.
* **Brand-Consistent Output:** Applies reusable design systems and custom rules so every output matches your brand guidelines.
* **Local-First Flexibility:** Run completely on your local computer to keep your projects secure and private.
* **Extensible Plugins:** Add custom skills and templates to fit your team's unique workflow.
