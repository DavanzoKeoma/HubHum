### What it does in plain English
Cofoundry provides a solid foundation for building custom websites and web applications with a friendly administration area. It lets creators organize text, images, and pages while giving developers complete freedom over the code and architecture. Instead of forcing you into a rigid template, it blends directly into your existing software projects without getting in the way.

### Who it's for
* Web developers looking for a lightweight system to manage content inside custom applications.
* Freelancers and agencies building tailored websites for clients who need an easy-to-use editing screen.
* Teams who prefer writing code first rather than relying on bloated pre-packaged themes.

### Key features
* Code-first development workflow that fits naturally into modern programming setups.
* Clean, intuitive management screen for everyday content editors.
* Flexible and modular architecture that scales alongside your project.
* Unobtrusive integration that works directly within your current software setup.
* Open-source foundation allowing deep customization and community collaboration.
