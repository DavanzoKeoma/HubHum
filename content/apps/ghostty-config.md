### What it does in plain English
Ghostty Config removes the stress of editing complex text files to change how your terminal looks and behaves. You can adjust fonts, colors, and transparency using simple sliders and pickers while seeing the changes happen instantly on screen. When you are happy with your design, you can copy or download the finished settings to use in your terminal immediately.

### Who it's for
* **Terminal enthusiasts** who want a unique and comfortable look for their command line without guessing syntax.
* **Developers** who need to quickly test different font sizes, color schemes, and cursor styles for better readability.
* **New users** of the Ghostty terminal who prefer clicking buttons over reading technical documentation.

### Key features
* **Live Visual Preview:** See exactly how your terminal will look as you change settings, including a working text area to test typing.
* **Easy Color Themes:** Choose from hundreds of pre-made color palettes or create your own custom colors with a simple picker.
* **Font Playground:** Try out different fonts and sizes instantly to find the most comfortable option for your eyes.
* **Smart Export:** The tool only saves the settings you changed, keeping your final file small and clean.
* **Shareable Links:** Create a link to your custom design so you can share it with friends or save it for later.

### How to install (Download)
This tool runs directly in your web browser, so there is nothing to install on your computer. Simply visit the official site to start customizing:

* [Open Ghostty Config in your Browser](https://ghostty.zerebos.com/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no account required, and provides immediate visual feedback for every change.
* **Cons:** Only works for the Ghostty terminal, so it is not useful if you use a different terminal application.
