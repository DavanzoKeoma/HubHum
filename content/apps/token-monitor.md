### What it does in plain English
Token Monitor is a desktop widget that tracks how much you use your various AI coding assistants. It automatically gathers data on token consumption, costs, and usage limits from over 50 different tools. You can view your activity history, set up multi-device synchronization, and keep an eye on your spending across all your AI services in one place.

### Who it's for
*   **Software Developers:** Professionals who use multiple AI coding assistants and need to manage their usage limits and costs.
*   **Power Users:** Individuals who want a clear, visual breakdown of their AI activity across different machines and projects.

### Key features
*   **Unified Dashboard:** See usage data for 56+ AI tools like Claude, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot in one interface.
*   **Live Tracking:** Monitor your token usage, generation speed, and remaining limits in real-time.
*   **Multi-Device Sync:** Keep your usage stats consistent across all your computers automatically.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** All your prompts, code, and session data stay local on your machine.
*   **Flexible Display:** Choose how you view your data, including menu bar popovers, floating bubbles, or screen-edge docks.
