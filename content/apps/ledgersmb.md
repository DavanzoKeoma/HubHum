### What it does in plain English
LedgerSMB is a powerful financial tool that helps small and medium businesses keep track of money, manage inventory, and handle daily transactions. It lets business owners create customer invoices, send orders, and monitor project finances directly through a web browser. Because everything is stored securely in an enterprise database, your financial records remain organized and accessible whenever you need them.

### Who it's for
* Small and medium business owners looking for complete financial control.
* Independent accountants managing double-entry bookkeeping and client accounts.
* Growing enterprises needing inventory tracking and order management in one place.

### Key features
* Complete double-entry accounting engine built for high-volume transactions.
* Built-in inventory tracking and shipping management tools.
* Professional quotations, orders, and customisable invoice templates.
* Direct email integration to send documents with PDF attachments to clients and vendors.
* Browser-based user interface accessible from anywhere with secure permissions.

### How to install (Download)
You can review the setup guide and download release files directly from the [LedgerSMB Installation Guide](https://ledgersmb.org/content/installing-ledgersmb-113). For quick evaluation, you can set it up locally using container tools as detailed on the official [LedgerSMB Website](https://ledgersmb.org/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever under an open-source license, handles large volumes of transactions smoothly, highly customizable output templates.
* **Cons:** Requires server setup or technical knowledge for permanent production use, and Safari requires a secure HTTPS connection.
