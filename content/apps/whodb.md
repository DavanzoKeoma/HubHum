### What it does in plain English
WhoDB acts as a central hub where you can view, edit, and organize your database information without needing complex software. It allows you to see how your data is connected through clear visual diagrams and lets you run searches or updates using a simple spreadsheet-style view. You can even use optional artificial intelligence tools to ask questions about your data in plain, everyday language.

### Who it's for
* **Data Analysts and Developers** who need a quick way to inspect or modify database records without setting up heavy professional tools.
* **Team Leads** who want to help colleagues explore unfamiliar data structures through a friendly, visual interface.

### Key features
* **Spreadsheet-style Editing:** Browse, filter, and update your database records directly in a familiar grid layout.
* **Visual Schema Maps:** See how your tables and data points relate to one another with interactive, easy-to-read diagrams.
* **Query Scratchpad:** Write and test your database commands in a dedicated space that keeps your history and results organized.
* **Flexible Data Handling:** Easily import, export, and generate sample data for testing purposes.
* **Optional AI Assistance:** Connect your own AI tools to ask questions about your data using simple, natural language.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, supports a wide variety of database types, and offers a clean, modern interface.
* **Cons:** Advanced features like AI integration require your own API keys or local setup, and the platform is best suited for those comfortable with basic database concepts.
