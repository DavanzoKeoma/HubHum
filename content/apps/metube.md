### What it does in plain English
MeTube gives you a simple web page to paste links from YouTube or other video sites, and it saves the files to your own computer or server. You can choose to download just the video, just the audio, or include subtitles and thumbnails for each file. It also lets you set up automatic subscriptions so that new videos from your favorite channels are saved to your library as soon as they are published.

### Who it's for
* **Home media enthusiasts** who want to build a personal library of videos and music without relying on temporary streaming links.
* **Tech-savvy users** who prefer to keep their data on their own hardware for privacy and control.
* **Content creators** who need to archive specific clips or full playlists for offline editing or reference.

### Key features
* **Wide site support:** Works with YouTube and hundreds of other popular video and audio platforms.
* **Flexible downloads:** Choose between video, audio-only, or subtitles, and select the quality and format for each download.
* **Automatic subscriptions:** Automatically detects and downloads new uploads from channels or playlists you follow.
* **Web-based interface:** Manage your downloads, queue, and history through a clean browser window without needing to type commands.
* **Customizable storage:** Set specific folders for videos and audio, and use custom naming rules to keep your files organized.

### How to install (Download)
MeTube is designed to run on your own server or home computer using a containerization tool called Docker. Since there are no traditional desktop installers, you will need to use the official Docker image.

* [View Official Releases and Docker Instructions](https://github.com/alexta69/metube/releases)
* [Read the Setup Guide in the Wiki](https://github.com/alexta69/metube/wiki)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, runs on your own hardware for total privacy, supports a huge range of websites, and includes automatic subscription tracking.
* **Cons:** Requires basic technical knowledge to set up Docker and a server, and it does not include built-in media management or tagging features.
