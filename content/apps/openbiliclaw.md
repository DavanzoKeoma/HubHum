### What it does in plain English
OpenBiliClaw acts as a personal assistant that understands your unique tastes by analyzing what you like and dislike across various websites. Instead of relying on standard algorithms that prioritize ads, it proactively searches for content that matches your personality and interests. Everything it learns about you stays strictly on your own device, ensuring your browsing habits remain private.

### Who it's for
*   **Curious Researchers:** People who want to break out of "filter bubbles" and find high-quality content across many different platforms.
*   **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Individuals who want the benefits of AI-driven recommendations without sharing their personal data with large tech companies.

### Key features
*   **Cross-Platform Discovery:** Gathers content from Bilibili, YouTube, X, Reddit, and many others into one unified feed.
*   **Psychological Profiling:** Builds a deep understanding of your preferences and cognitive style to refine future suggestions.
*   **Local-First Privacy:** All your history, interests, and personal data are stored locally on your machine in a secure database.
*   **Smart Feedback Loop:** You can easily "teach" the agent by liking, disliking, or explaining why you find certain content interesting.
*   **Customizable Sources:** Easily add your favorite websites or RSS feeds to the discovery engine with a simple chat command.
