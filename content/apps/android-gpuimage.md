### What it does in plain English
It lets Android apps change pictures with artistic effects.  
The changes appear instantly so you can see the result right away.  
It works on most Android phones that support basic graphics.

### Who it's for
* App makers who want to give users fun photo‑editing tools.  
* Hobbyist developers building picture‑enhancement features.  
* Anyone creating visual effects inside an Android application.

### Key features
- Over 70 ready‑made filters such as sepia, blur, sketch, cartoon, and more.  
- Filters run on the phone’s graphics hardware for fast, smooth results.  
- Easy to switch filters while previewing the picture.  
- Supports saving the edited picture directly to the device’s gallery.  
- Works on Android 2.2 and newer devices.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free and open source, many built‑in filters, fast performance, works on old Android versions.  
* **Cons:** needs some programming knowledge to add to an app, not a stand‑alone photo editor, a few filters are still missing.
