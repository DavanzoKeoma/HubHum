### What it does in plain English
Timelinize gathers your scattered digital information—like photos, social media posts, and messages—into one organized, chronological view. It pulls data from your various devices and online accounts, allowing you to keep a permanent, private copy on your own hardware. By bringing everything together, it helps you visualize your personal history and memories without relying on third-party cloud services.

### Who it's for
*   **Digital Archivists:** People who want to preserve their personal history and memories for the long term.
*   **Privacy Advocates:** Users who want to reclaim their data from large corporations and keep it stored locally on their own machines.

### Key features
*   **Unified Timeline:** View messages, photos, and location data together in one cohesive flow.
*   **Local Storage:** All your data stays on your computer, giving you full ownership and control.
*   **Entity Awareness:** Automatically links people, pets, and organizations across different data sources.
*   **Interactive Maps:** Explore your life in 3D, seeing where you were when specific messages or photos were created.
*   **Flexible Importing:** Easily pull in data from common social media archives and device exports.

### How to install (Download)
You can find the latest version and detailed setup instructions on the [official Timelinize website](https://timelinize.com).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, works offline, and provides a unique way to visualize personal history.
*   **Cons:** Currently in active development, which means you may need to reset your database when updating to newer versions.
