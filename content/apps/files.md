### What it does in plain English
Files replaces the standard Windows file explorer with a faster, more visually appealing tool for managing your digital life. It allows you to sort through thousands of photos, documents, and folders using clear tags and flexible views. The app is designed to feel intuitive, helping you find exactly what you need without digging through complex menus.

### Who it's for
* Students and professionals who need to quickly locate specific documents or project files.
* Creative users who manage large libraries of images and media and need efficient tagging systems.
* Tech enthusiasts who prefer a customizable, open-source alternative to the default system tools.

### Key features
* **Intuitive Design:** A clean, modern interface that makes navigating large folders easy on the eyes.
* **File Tagging:** Assign custom tags to files for faster searching and organization across different folders.
* **Multitasking:** Open multiple windows and tabs to work on different projects simultaneously.
* **Deep Integrations:** Connects smoothly with cloud storage services and other common Windows utilities.
* **Open Source:** Built by a community of developers, ensuring transparency and regular updates.

* **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly customizable, and offers a much smoother experience than the default Windows explorer.
* **Cons:** Some advanced customization options may take a little time to discover and configure.
