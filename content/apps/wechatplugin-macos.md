### What it does in plain English
This tool adds powerful helper functions to the desktop messenger app on your Mac computer. It stops deleted messages from disappearing so you can always see what was said. It also lets you run multiple accounts at once and set up automated replies for busy moments.

### Who it's for
* Busy professionals who want to manage multiple accounts on a single computer.
* Users who hate missing retracted messages and want full chat history visibility.
* People looking to automate simple replies when they step away from their desk.

### Key features
* **Anti-Recall Protection:** View messages that other people try to delete after sending.
* **Multiple Accounts:** Run and log into several accounts on your computer at the same time.
* **Automated Replies:** Set up custom keywords and timed responses for incoming chats.
* **Remote Control:** Send simple text commands to lock your screen or manage media players.
* **Quick Access Shortcuts:** Use launcher commands to find chats and send messages quickly.

### How to install (Download)
Follow the step-by-step instructions on the [Official Setup Guide](https://github.com/TKkk-iOSer/WeChatPlugin-MacOS/blob/master/Install.md) or download the files directly from the [Releases Page](https://github.com/TKkk-iOSer/WeChatPlugin-MacOS/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source; adds heavily requested productivity features to desktop chatting.
* **Cons:** Requires a terminal command line setup; needs occasional updates when the base messenger application updates.
