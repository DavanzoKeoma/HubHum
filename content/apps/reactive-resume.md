### What it does in plain English
Reactive Resume is a secure, free platform designed to help you write stunning resumes and cover letters with live previews as you type. It lets you import existing documents, track job applications, and check your work against common hiring software requirements without hidden subscription fees. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad). You own all of your information, and the application includes zero ads or hidden data tracking.

### Who it's for
* **Job Seekers:** People actively looking for employment who need an easy way to design professional, eye-catching resumes and manage applications.
* **Privacy-Conscious Professionals:** Individuals who want to keep their personal work history entirely secure and under their own control.
* **Recent Graduates:** Students preparing to enter the workforce who want guidance on formatting and writing standout application materials.

### Key features
* **Live Preview Editor:** Watch your formatting update instantly as you edit text, rearrange sections, and customize layouts.
* **Multiple Professional Templates:** Choose from various clean, modern designs built to pass automated hiring software checks.
* **Job Application Tracker:** Organize your job search from saved listings to scheduled interviews using built-in calendar and board views.
* **Optional AI Assistance:** Connect your own writing assistants to help polish phrases and tailor your experience to specific job postings.
* Ready-to-use digital planners, printable checklists, and clean document templates for daily organization via [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
