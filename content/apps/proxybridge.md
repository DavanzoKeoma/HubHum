### What it does in plain English
ProxyBridge is a handy tool that lets you send your computer or phone application traffic through a proxy server. It works quietly in the background to capture connections from specific programs, even if those programs do not normally include proxy settings. This allows you to manage how your data travels across the internet with complete control and zero complicated setup.

### Who it's for
* **Developers and Testers:** Who need to route specific software traffic through local debugging proxies for analysis.
* **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Who want to ensure certain desktop or mobile tools always connect through secure routing.
* **Network Administrators:** Who manage traffic paths and routing rules across individual client workstations.

### Key features
* **Per-Process Routing:** Choose exactly which applications use your proxy and which connect directly.
* **Works with Any App:** Intercepts traffic at the system level, meaning apps don't need built-in proxy options.
* **Handles TCP and UDP:** Supports everyday web browsing, secure shell sessions, databases, and media streams.
* **Flexible Custom Rules:** Match connections by specific programs, web addresses, port numbers, or network types.
* **Multi-Platform Support:** Available for major desktop and mobile operating systems with clean graphical controls.
