### What it does in plain English
Copybook Generator helps parents, teachers, and learners create personalized practice sheets for handwriting, letters, numbers, and math. You can easily adjust fonts, grids, colors, and question counts to match any learning stage. Once your layout looks right, you can preview the multi-page sheet and save it as a high-quality PDF ready for printing.

### Who it's for
* **Parents & Families:** Create custom handwriting and counting practice worksheets tailored to a child's unique learning pace.
* **Teachers & Tutors:** Generate clean, consistent teaching templates and homework sheets for students in the classroom.
* **Calligraphy Enthusiasts & Beginners:** Build custom tracing guides and pen control exercises to refine handwriting posture and stroke accuracy.

### Key features
* **Wide Variety of Practice Types:** Build sheets for Chinese characters, stroke orders, pinyin, English alphabets, vocabulary, math equations, and clock reading.
* **Flexible Customization:** Change fonts, grid styles, character sizes, text colors, and the number of practice questions per page.
* **Multi-Page Preview:** Inspect your entire worksheet layout across multiple pages before finalizing your document.
* **Instant PDF Export:** Save your custom practice books into standard multi-page PDF files for easy home or school printing.
