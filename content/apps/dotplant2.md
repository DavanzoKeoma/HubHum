### What it does in plain English
Dotplant2 provides an open-source foundation for building custom online stores and digital shops. It organizes product catalogs, handles custom checkout rules, and gives store owners deep control over how search engines view their pages. You can manage multi-currency pricing, flexible discounts, and customer groups from a central dashboard.

### Who it's for
* Independent store owners wanting complete control over their online shop infrastructure.
* Web developers building tailored e-commerce experiences for clients.
* Growing retail businesses migrating away from restrictive hosted shopping platforms.

### Key features
* Built-in search engine optimization controls for custom links, titles, and descriptions on every product page.
* Flexible checkout and order workflow matching your specific business rules.
* Multi-currency support with automatic currency rate updates.
* Pluggable payment gateways and diverse discount options for products and users.
* Modular content blocks for managing repeated text parts and interactive feedback forms.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, highly customizable structure, strong focus on search engine optimization.
* **Cons:** Requires technical server knowledge to set up and manage, demo data is primarily in Russian.
