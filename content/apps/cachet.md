### What it does in plain English
Cachet gives you a professional public dashboard to announce when your websites, apps, or services experience technical difficulties or maintenance. It keeps your customers and team fully informed with real-time updates and historical uptime metrics. Because you host it yourself, you keep total control over your monitoring data and system branding.

### Who it's for
* System administrators and IT teams needing an independent channel to broadcast outages.
* SaaS founders and web developers who want to build trust by being transparent about reliability.
* Community managers looking to reduce support ticket floods during scheduled server maintenance.

### Key features
* Customizable public dashboard to showcase the operational health of multiple services and systems.
* Incident management tools to record, update, and resolve service outages in real time.
* Scheduled maintenance planning so users can prepare ahead for expected downtime.
* Metrics tracking to display historical performance and uptime percentages over custom periods.
