### What it does in plain English
LittleLink provides a clean, minimalist way to display all your important links on a single, professional-looking page. Instead of using subscription-based services that track your visitors, you can host this lightweight page on your own server or through free web hosting platforms. It includes over 100 pre-designed button styles to ensure your page matches your personal brand perfectly.

### Who it's for
*   Content creators and influencers who want full control over their link-in-bio page.
*   Small business owners looking for a free, fast, and privacy-focused way to direct customers to their various online platforms.

### Key features
*   **Total Ownership:** Host the page yourself so you never have to worry about third-party service changes or fees.
*   **Visual Customization:** Use the built-in button builder to preview and generate styles that match your brand's colors.
*   **High Performance:** Built with minimal code to ensure your page loads instantly on any device.
*   **Accessibility First:** Includes automatic contrast adjustments to ensure your buttons are readable for everyone.
*   **Easy Setup:** No complex software required; you can get your page live in minutes using simple web hosting services.

### How to install (Download)
LittleLink is designed to be hosted on the web. You can get started by visiting the [Official LittleLink Website](https://littlelink.io) or by using one of the "One-Click cloud setup" buttons found on the [LittleLink GitHub official project page](https://github.com/sethcottle/littlelink).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to use, no tracking or advertisements, extremely fast loading times, and highly customizable.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of editing simple text files to update your links, and does not include a built-in dashboard for editing.
