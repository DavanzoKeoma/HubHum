### What it does in plain English
Animeko is a streamlined hub for discovering, tracking, and watching animated series across all your devices. It automatically synchronizes your viewing progress with popular community platforms so you never lose your place. You can stream shows directly through integrated video sources or peer-to-peer sharing networks while enjoying synchronized on-screen comments.

### Who it's for
* Anime fans who want a single app to track watchlists, discover new releases, and stream episodes smoothly.
* Viewers who enjoy reading synchronized floating comments and community reactions while watching shows.
* Users looking for a cross-platform media companion with offline caching for viewing on the go.

### Key features
* **Cloud Progress Sync:** Automatically updates your watched episodes so you can seamlessly switch between devices.
* **Aggregated Media Sources:** Pulls video streams from multiple networks and peer-to-peer data sources with automatic selection.
* **Live Community Comments:** Displays real-time scrolling comments sourced from public servers and databases.
* **Offline Caching:** Download episodes directly to your device storage for uninterrupted viewing without an internet connection.
* **Cross-Platform Design:** Built to run smoothly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
