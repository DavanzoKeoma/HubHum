### What it does in plain English
DeepChat is a desktop tool that brings all your favorite AI models together into one private, easy-to-use interface. It allows you to organize complex projects, automate repetitive tasks, and keep your conversation history searchable and recoverable. Because it runs locally on your computer, you maintain better control over your data and privacy while working with intelligent assistants.

### Who it's for
*   **Power Users:** People who want to switch between different AI models (like OpenAI or local models) without constantly opening new browser tabs.
*   **Developers and Creators:** Individuals who need a structured way to manage long-term AI projects, code snippets, and automated workflows.

### Key features
*   **Unified AI Hub:** Connect to mainstream cloud-based AI services or run your own models locally on your machine.
*   **Recoverable Sessions:** Keep track of your work with a structured history that allows you to pause, resume, and inspect your AI interactions.
*   **Customizable Skills:** Easily add specific capabilities to your AI, such as document analysis or coding assistance, by importing ready-made tools.
*   **Remote Control:** Manage your desktop AI sessions from your phone or other devices using popular messaging apps.
*   **Privacy-First Design:** Built with security in mind, offering local data storage and support for network proxies to keep your information safe.
