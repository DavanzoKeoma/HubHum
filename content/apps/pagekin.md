### What it does in plain English
Pagekin is a free desktop companion for authors writing long-form stories and web fiction with artificial intelligence. It guides you step-by-step from initial world-building and character timelines to chapter blueprints, drafting, review, and final editing. Your work remains entirely private and stored locally on your own computer while connecting securely to your preferred AI service providers.

### Who it's for
* Fiction writers and web novelists looking for structured assistance to maintain plot consistency across long manuscripts.
* Authors who want complete local ownership of their writing files without locking their data into proprietary web platforms.
* Creators who prefer bringing their own artificial intelligence subscriptions or running private models locally on their hardware.

### Key features
* **Local-first privacy:** All drafts, characters, and chapter notes stay safely stored on your device in local files and databases.
* **Structured chapter planning:** Build detailed story arcs, character relationship timelines, and plot point trackers before writing individual chapters.
* **Flexible model support:** Connect popular cloud services or run private models offline without any bundled vendor lock-in.
* **Controlled revision workflow:** Review changes paragraph by paragraph and inspect plot impact warnings before applying edits to your text.
* **Custom writing rules:** Import custom style instructions or writing rules to adapt the output style to your creative voice.
