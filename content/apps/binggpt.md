### What it does in plain English
BingGPT brings an AI-powered conversational assistant straight to your desktop as a standalone application. It frees you from being tied to a particular web browser or installing extra browser extensions just to chat. You can customize how it looks, export your discussions in various formats, and manage everything with handy keyboard shortcuts.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers looking for a distraction-free environment to brainstorm topics.
* Writers and content creators who want quick access to an intelligent assistant for drafting ideas.
* Everyday computer users who prefer dedicated desktop applications over keeping browser tabs open.

### Key features
* Standalone desktop experience that runs independently of specific web browsers.
* Export full conversation histories to Markdown documents, images, or PDF files.
* Fully adjustable appearance with customizable visual themes and font sizes.
* Comprehensive keyboard shortcuts for fast navigation and instant replies.
* Multi-platform availability covering Windows, macOS, and Linux operating systems.
