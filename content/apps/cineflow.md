### What it does in plain English
CineFlow is a clean, feature-rich desktop application designed to help you discover, organize, and watch your favorite movies and television shows in one place. It pulls accurate details, posters, and ratings from online databases while letting you bring your own media sources for seamless playback. A unique floating widget mode and smart natural language search make finding something to watch effortless and fun.

### Who it's for
* Movie and TV enthusiasts who want a centralized desktop hub to organize and stream their favorite media.
* Users looking for custom recommendation engines that learn from viewing history and personal preferences.
* Anyone who wants a distraction-free viewing experience with built-in remote casting and ad-filtering features.

### Key features
* **Personalized Discoveries:** Explore daily curated picks and custom recommendations based on your local viewing habits and favorite genres.
* **Smart Natural Language Search:** Find titles using regular keywords or everyday phrases like rainy afternoon, cooking background music, or weekend party.
* **Unified Source Streaming:** Search across multiple configured media sources simultaneously and jump straight into playback from a single line of results.
* **Handy Floating Widget:** Minimize the app into a compact floating island that stays out of your way while keeping controls accessible.
* **Built-In Resume and History:** Automatically keep track of watched episodes, progress, and preferred playback sources so you can pick up right where you left off.
