### What it does in plain English
linkding is a private web space that replaces cluttered browser bookmark folders with a clean, searchable library. Whenever you save a link, it automatically captures the page title, description, icons, and even creates a permanent backup archive. You can access your saved collection from any computer or phone using a web browser or handy browser extensions.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who need to collect, tag, and archive hundreds of reference links.
* Privacy-conscious internet users who want to keep their browsing history and saved resources away from big tech companies.
* Busy professionals looking for a lightning-fast 'read it later' system that works across all devices.

### Key features
* **Clean & Readable Interface:** Designed without clutter so you can focus entirely on your content and tags.
* **Automatic Page Archiving:** Saves a local copy or an Internet Archive snapshot of bookmarked web pages automatically.
* **Browser Integration:** Use dedicated extensions for Firefox and Chrome or a quick bookmarklet to save pages in one click.
* **Markdown Notes:** Add rich text notes and thoughts directly alongside your saved web links.
* **Multi-User Support:** Share specific bookmarks or invite guests and additional family members with user management controls.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, blazing fast search, automatic website archiving, and works great on mobile devices.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with setting up server software or container environments to host yourself.
