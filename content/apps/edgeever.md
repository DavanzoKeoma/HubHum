### What it does in plain English
EdgeEver is a flexible digital notebook that helps you capture ideas, organize documents, and manage complex information in one place. It features a clean, three-pane layout that makes it easy to navigate your notes while keeping your data private and under your control. With built-in smart assistants, it can help you summarize text, create diagrams, and keep your knowledge organized across all your devices.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to organize large amounts of information and research materials.
* Professionals who want a private, self-hosted alternative to commercial note-taking services.

### Key features
* **Three-Pane Workflow:** A classic, high-efficiency layout that keeps your folders, note list, and editor organized and accessible.
* **Smart AI Integration:** Use built-in assistants to generate mind maps, flowcharts, and structured tables from your notes.
* **Web Clipper:** Easily save articles, text, and images from the web directly into your private workspace.
* **Total Ownership:** Run the app entirely on your own terms using free cloud services or your own private server.
* **Cross-Platform Sync:** Access your notes seamlessly across your computer, tablet, and phone with instant updates.
