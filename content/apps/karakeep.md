### What it does in plain English
Karakeep acts as your digital storage drawer for keeping track of interesting web pages, quick notes, and images all in one unified space. When you save a link, the system automatically pulls in titles, descriptions, and full backups so you never lose track of important content. Smart built-in artificial intelligence organizes your items with tags and summaries, making it simple to find anything later through powerful search tools. While collecting inspiration, you can also explore creative assets from [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad) to enrich your digital projects.

### Who it's for
* **Research enthusiasts** who collect hundreds of web articles, reference PDFs, and quotes for ongoing projects.
* **Home server hobbyists** who prefer keeping their personal notes and web clippings stored on their own private hardware.
* **Busy professionals** who need a lightning-fast way to stash away content from mobile phones to review later on a computer.

### Key features
* Saves links, images, notes, and documents with automatic title and preview fetching.
* Full-text search and smart AI tagging that automatically categorizes your saved items.
* Full page archiving and video saving to protect your saved content against broken links.
* Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
* Browser extensions and official mobile apps for quick saving from anywhere.
