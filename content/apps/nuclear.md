### What it does in plain English
Nuclear lets you listen to music without ads or tracking. Search for any song or artist and add it to your playlist. Play on Windows, macOS, or Linux.  

### Who it's for
Music lovers who want a clean listening experience. Anyone who uses Windows, macOS, or Linux. People who enjoy building custom playlists.  

### Key features
- Search and stream music from any source.  
- Create and manage playlists.  
- Built‑in themes for light and dark mode.  
- Sync lyrics and track history.
