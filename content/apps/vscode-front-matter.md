### What it does in plain English
Front Matter acts as a control center for your website, allowing you to manage your articles and media files without leaving your text editor. It helps you organize your content, check for search engine optimization, and preview how your pages will look before you publish them. By bringing these tools into one place, it simplifies the workflow for anyone maintaining a modern website.

### Who it's for
*   Website owners and bloggers who use modern site-building tools and want a visual way to manage their content.
*   Content creators who prefer working within their code editor rather than switching between multiple browser-based dashboards.

### Key features
*   **Centralized Dashboard:** Search, filter, and organize all your website content in one convenient view.
*   **Live Preview:** See exactly how your content will look on your site without leaving the editor.
*   **SEO Assistant:** Get helpful checks for your titles, descriptions, and keywords to improve your search engine visibility.
*   **Media Management:** Easily upload and manage images and files associated with your articles.
*   **Multilingual Support:** Manage content across different languages with built-in translation tools.

*   **Pros:** Free to use, integrates perfectly into your existing workflow, and supports many popular website software foundation.
*   **Cons:** Requires the Visual Studio Code editor to function and may have a slight learning curve for those new to code-based site management.
