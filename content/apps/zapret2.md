### What it does in plain English
zapret2 is an autonomous network utility that helps bypass connection blocks, slowdowns, and network filtering. It operates entirely on your local device or home router without routing your traffic through third-party servers. By combining low-level packet handling with scriptable control, it gives users precise tools to keep their internet connections open and unhindered.

### Who it's for
* Advanced home network administrators and router owners looking to maintain open internet access.
* Tech-savvy privacy enthusiasts who want a self-hosted tool to defeat protocol analysis and traffic throttling.
* Developers and network hobbyists testing custom packet manipulation and firewall strategies.

### Key features
* Operates locally on your equipment with zero reliance on external proxy or VPN servers.
* Leverages scriptable logic for flexible, highly customizable traffic management strategies.
* Designed with a low resource footprint, ideal for small embedded hardware and home routers.
* Supports multi-platform environments including traditional Linux systems, FreeBSD, OpenBSD, and Windows.
