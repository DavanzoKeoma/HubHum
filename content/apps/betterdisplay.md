### What it does in plain English
BetterDisplay gives you absolute control over your Mac's built-in and external monitors straight from the menu bar. It lets you scale resolutions smoothly, unlock extra HDR brightness, and create virtual screens for remote setups. You can also adjust color profiles, organize multiple displays visually, and manage hardware settings effortlessly.

### Who it's for
* Mac owners using external monitors who want sharp resolutions and reliable scaling options.
* Creative professionals looking to fine-tune color profiles, brightness limits, and display arrangements.
* Power users who want virtual screens for remote work, streaming, or custom multi-monitor setups.

### Key features
* **Flexible HiDPI scaling:** Make external and built-in screens look crisp by unlocking smooth resolution scaling options.
* **Extra HDR brightness:** Push compatible high-dynamic-range displays to their peak performance limits.
* **Virtual screens:** Create custom virtual displays for remote connections or headless setups.
* **Hardware & audio control:** Manage display inputs, DDC hardware commands, and software audio adjustments directly.
* **Display arrangement & tools:** Organize multiple monitors visually, view picture-in-picture streams, and protect your layout settings.
