### What it does in plain English
JiwuChat is a versatile communication tool designed to help you stay connected with friends, family, or colleagues across all your devices. It combines traditional text messaging, voice calls, and file sharing with modern artificial intelligence assistants that can answer questions or help manage daily tasks. Whether you are chatting on your computer or your phone, your conversation history stays neatly synchronized and easy to navigate.

### Who it's for
* Remote teams and study groups looking for a private communication hub with built-in artificial intelligence support.
* Everyday users who want a lightweight, distraction-free messaging application that works seamlessly across phone and desktop.
* Individuals who enjoy experimenting with intelligent assistants and automated chat tools directly inside their messaging environment.

### Key features
* Real-time text, photo, video, and document sharing with instant delivery and read indicators.
* Built-in voice and video calling alongside screen sharing capabilities for collaboration.
* Integrated smart assistants to help answer questions, summarize text, or assist with chats.
* Multi-device synchronization so your conversations are always up to date wherever you log in.
* Customizable light and dark themes to match your personal visual preference.
