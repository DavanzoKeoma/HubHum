### What it does in plain English
Strawberry lets you listen to your personal music collection while automatically organizing your files into a neat, searchable library. It supports high-quality audio formats that many standard players ignore, ensuring you hear every detail in your favorite songs. You can also fix missing song information and download album covers directly within the app to keep your collection looking professional.

### Who it's for
* **Music enthusiasts** who have a large library of high-quality audio files and want a player that respects their format.
* **Organizers** who want to clean up messy file names and missing metadata without using complex spreadsheet tools.
* **Linux users** looking for a modern, reliable alternative to older music players with deep system integration.

### Key features
* **High-Format Support:** Plays a wide range of audio types, including FLAC, MP3, and high-resolution formats like DSD.
* **Automatic Tagging:** Finds missing song titles, artists, and album art using online music databases to keep your library tidy.
* **Smart Playlists:** Create dynamic playlists that update automatically based on your listening habits or specific criteria.
* **Volume Normalization:** Adjusts the volume of different songs so they all sound equally loud, preventing sudden jumps in audio levels.
* **Cross-Platform Availability:** Works on Linux, Windows, and macOS, with specific features tailored to each operating system.
