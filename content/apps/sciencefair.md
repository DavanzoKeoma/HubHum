### What it does in plain English
ScienceFair is a futuristic desktop application that helps you find, read, and collect scientific articles without relying on traditional corporate platforms. It connects directly to distributed networks to fetch research papers, allowing you to build your own private libraries. The app provides a clean, distraction-free reading space specifically designed for complex scientific documents.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who want an independent way to discover and organize academic papers.
* Independent science enthusiasts looking to explore peer-reviewed literature without paywalls or corporate oversight.
* Anyone needing a local, private library for research data and scientific reading materials.

### Key features
* **Optimized Reading Area:** Uses a specialized reader view to present dense scientific text clearly.
* **Instant Multi-Source Search:** Instantly look up articles across your local collection and decentralized remote databases.
* **Peer-to-Peer Sharing:** Access articles through secure network feeds that remain accessible even if original hosts go offline.
* **Built-in Analytics:** Includes basic statistical tools and bibliometrics for your saved literature collection.
* **Complete Privacy:** Keeps your reading lists and local collections strictly private by default.
