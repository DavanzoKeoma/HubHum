### What it does in plain English
noTunes stops iTunes or Apple Music from popping up on your Mac without your permission. It is especially useful when your Bluetooth headphones reconnect and trigger the music app to open automatically. You can easily turn this protection on or off with a single click in your menu bar.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who are tired of iTunes opening randomly when they plug in headphones.
* People who use a different music service and want to stop the default Apple app from interfering.
* Anyone who wants a clean, distraction-free desktop experience without complex settings.

### Key features
* Prevents iTunes and Apple Music from launching automatically.
* Includes a simple menu bar icon to toggle protection on or off instantly.
* Allows you to set a replacement app or website to open instead of iTunes.
* Can be set to start automatically when your Mac boots up.
* Lightweight and free to use with no subscription required.
