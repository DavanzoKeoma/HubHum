### What it does in plain English
Uptrace is a tool that keeps an eye on your software to make sure everything is running smoothly. It collects detailed information about how your applications behave and stores it in a way that is easy to search and understand. When something goes wrong, it provides clear insights and alerts so you can fix the problem quickly.

### Who it's for
* **Software Developers:** Who need to understand how their code is performing in real-time.
* **System Administrators:** Who want to keep track of server health and application logs without juggling multiple tools.

### Key features
* **Unified Dashboard:** View your activity logs, performance metrics, and system traces all in one single screen.
* **Smart Alerts:** Get notified via email or messaging apps the moment your system shows signs of trouble.
* **Visual Insights:** Automatically generated charts and service maps help you see exactly how your application components connect.
* **Efficient Storage:** Designed to handle massive amounts of data while keeping your storage costs low through advanced compression.
* **Flexible Integration:** Works with many common data collection tools, making it easy to plug into your existing setup.
