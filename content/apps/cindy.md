### What it does in plain English
Cindy acts as a central hub that connects your computer and phone to your favorite AI tools. It allows you to perform real work, such as managing files or running automated tasks, directly from your own devices. By bringing your own subscriptions and models, you can build a personalized assistant that understands exactly how you like to work.

### Who it's for
*   **Remote workers** who need to access their desktop files and tools from their phone or a different computer.
*   **Power users** who want to combine multiple AI services into one unified, automated workspace.

### Key features
*   **Multi-device sync:** Start a project on your desktop and pick up exactly where you left off on your phone.
*   **Built-in remote control:** Access your computer remotely to open apps and type as if you were sitting right in front of it.
*   **Flexible AI integration:** Connect the AI models and subscriptions you already pay for, or use local models for privacy.
*   **Task automation:** Create custom routines and skills to handle repetitive work automatically.
*   **Rich interaction:** View charts, interactive controls, and formatted documents directly within your chat sessions.
