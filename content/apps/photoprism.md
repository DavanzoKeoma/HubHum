### What it does in plain English
PhotoPrism is a smart photo organizer that helps you browse, search, and sort your entire image collection effortlessly. It automatically recognizes faces, labels objects, and maps your travels without sharing your data with big tech companies. You can safely host it yourself or open it right in the cloud to access your memories from any device. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Photography enthusiasts who want an independent, AI-assisted gallery for thousands of RAW and standard image files.
* Families looking for a private way to share vacation memories and automatically sort pictures by people and places.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer keeping their personal photo library away from public commercial storage services.

### Key features
* Smart recognition that automatically tags pictures by their content, colors, and geographic location.
* Face detection to easily find and group photos of your family and friends.
* Interactive world maps that let you explore your travels and relive past adventures.
* Direct syncing with mobile backup apps and computer file managers via standard web protocols.
* Ready-to-use digital planners, printable checklists, and clean document templates for daily organization via [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
