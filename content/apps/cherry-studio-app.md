### What it does in plain English
Cherry Studio Mobile lets you chat with AI models right on your phone.  
You can also generate images from prompts or photos.  
All conversations and files stay organized in one place.

### Who it's for
- Students and writers who need quick research and writing help.  
- Designers looking to prototype images on the go.  
- Anyone who wants to keep AI conversations handy without a laptop.

### Key features
- Chat with multiple AI models in one conversation space.  
- Create images from text or existing photos.  
- Connect extra tools like web search or plugins.  
- Save and share files, export chats as text or images.  
- Use light or dark theme and share content with your phone.
