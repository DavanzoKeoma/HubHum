This tool acts as an independent safety inspector for artificial intelligence assistants. It runs automated simulations of everyday business requests to catch mistakes, hidden security risks, and rule violations. You get a clear report and scorecard within two minutes to make sure your assistant behaves properly before real users interact with it.
