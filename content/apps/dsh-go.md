### What it does in plain English
dsh-Go brings an advanced AI assistant directly into your pocket with a built-in environment that runs locally on your phone. You can chat with your models, manage tasks, and even remote control your home or office computer on the go. Interactive animated companion characters accompany you while you work, keeping you updated on ongoing tasks and notifications.

### Who it's for
* Mobile power users who want a capable AI assistant running directly on their Android devices.
* Developers and remote workers needing to connect to and command desktop setups while away from their desks.
* Anyone looking for an adaptable chat interface with custom themes, chat history replays, and handy desktop shortcuts.

### Key features
* Run a local environment directly on your Android phone without needing complex setups.
* Securely connect to and control your computer or server remotely.
* Interject messages mid-reply and automatically collapse completed processes into tidy lines.
* Switch seamlessly between a native mobile interface and a web view with synchronized history.
* Customize your experience with multiple theme colors, icon styles, and animated companion characters.
