### What it does in plain English
FlyingMouse Format is a friendly, mouse-themed desktop application that lets you change files from one format to another entirely offline. You can drag and drop images, text files, office documents, spreadsheets, electronic books, and standard audio or video clips to convert them in batches. It also features text recognition (OCR) and smart table extraction so you can pull readable text and tables out of scanned pages or portable documents.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users who need a quick, reliable way to convert personal documents or photos without relying on sketchy web services.
* Students and office workers looking to switch between word processor files, spreadsheets, and Portable Document Format files securely.
* Anyone who prefers keeping their private files completely offline and secure on their own hard drive.

### Key features
* Works entirely offline on your computer to protect your personal privacy and data security.
* Converts a massive variety of everyday formats including images, documents, presentations, spreadsheets, audio, and video clips.
* Features a playful mouse character interface that animates depending on your upload and conversion status.
* Remembers your preferred target formats and save folders to speed up future workflows.
* Includes batch processing so you can queue up multiple files and convert them all at once.
