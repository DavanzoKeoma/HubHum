### What it does in plain English
Kiln provides a unified workspace for managing the entire artificial intelligence creation lifecycle on your personal computer. It allows product managers, subject experts, and quality testers to rate model outputs, build document search databases, and test performance without needing a developer's terminal. Engineers can then take those exact same projects into production using an open-source programming library that runs locally with your own keys.

### Who it's for
* Product managers and domain experts who need to test and rate artificial intelligence outputs without coding.
* Software developers looking for an easy way to build document search, test models, and handle fine-tuning.
* Teams that want to collaborate on artificial intelligence experiments locally while keeping their data completely private.

### Key features
* **Unified Workspace:** Handle evaluations, document search, multi-agent setups, and synthetic data generation in a single desktop application.
* **No-Code Testing:** Let non-technical teammates review and score model results to improve quality over time.
* **Document Search (RAG):** Drag and drop PDFs, images, and audio files to build searchable knowledge bases instantly.
* **Model Fine-Tuning:** Customize dozens of different models effortlessly without dealing with complex setup scripts.
* **Local-First Privacy:** Run tasks entirely on your machine using your own keys or local software like Ollama.

* **Cons:** Advanced optimization steps require familiarity with artificial intelligence concepts, and running large models locally demands decent computer hardware.
