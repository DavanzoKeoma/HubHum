### What it does in plain English
OpenSign provides a secure and free way to sign, send, and manage your important documents online. It replaces expensive commercial services by offering a professional tool that handles everything from simple signatures to complex multi-signer workflows. You can easily track document progress, set expiration dates, and store your files in a centralized, secure digital vault.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who need a cost-effective way to manage contracts and agreements.
* Freelancers and individuals who require a secure, professional method to sign documents remotely.

### Key features
* **Secure E-Signing:** Sign documents using hand-drawn, typed, or uploaded images with robust encryption for your privacy.
* **Multi-Signer Support:** Easily invite multiple people to sign documents in a specific order or share them via simple links.
* **Audit Trails:** Automatically generate a completion certificate with detailed logs, including timestamps and IP addresses, for every signed document.
* **Document Templates:** Create and save reusable PDF templates to save time on repetitive paperwork.
* **Guest Verification:** Ensure security by requiring guest signers to verify their identity with a unique code sent to their email.

### How to install (Download)
OpenSign is a web-based application. You can use the free cloud-hosted version directly or set it up on your own server:
* [Access the Free Cloud Version](https://www.opensignlabs.com/)
* [View Self-Hosting Installation Guide](https://docs.opensignlabs.com/docs/self-host/docker/run-locally/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, no subscription fees, includes professional audit trails, and supports custom branding.
* **Cons:** Self-hosting requires some technical familiarity with server management, and the free cloud version may have usage limits compared to a custom-hosted instance.
