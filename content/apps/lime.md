### What it does in plain English
Lime is a versatile digital companion that helps you tackle complex daily chores like writing code, searching the web, organizing files, and running terminal commands. You provide a clear goal and boundaries, and the software works step-by-step to complete the task while leaving behind fully inspectable outputs. You stay completely in control, reviewing every file edit, research report, or command result before moving forward.

### Who it's for
* Software developers looking to automate repetitive coding tasks, bug fixes, and terminal checks.
* Researchers and writers who want structured reports, organized references, and automated content drafts.
* Power users who need a local-first assistant to manage files, data workflows, and multi-step routines.

### Key features
* Full-stack capabilities covering code generation, file handling, terminal operations, and web research.
* Transparent review workflow that lets you inspect every change, diff, and output before approval.
* Flexible model integration allowing you to connect your preferred AI providers and custom tools.
* Local-first design keeping your project history, files, and configurations secure on your device.
* Cross-platform support available for modern desktop systems and command-line environments.
