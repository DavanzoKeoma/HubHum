### What it does in plain English
BrowserOS is a secondary web browser built specifically to help AI assistants complete tasks for you. It securely imports your existing logins and bookmarks, allowing your AI tools to interact with websites as if you were doing it yourself. You can watch your agents work in real-time or replay their actions later to see exactly what they accomplished.

### Who it's for
* **Power users** who want to automate repetitive web tasks like filing expenses or clearing out junk email.
* **AI enthusiasts** who use tools like Claude Code or other automation assistants and need a reliable, private environment for them to operate.

### Key features
* **Live Dashboard:** Monitor multiple AI agents at once to see exactly which websites they are visiting and what actions they are taking.
* **One-Click Setup:** Easily import your bookmarks, passwords, and extensions from your main browser to get started immediately.
* **Session Replay:** Every action taken by an agent is saved as a video, allowing you to review or audit their work at any time.
* **Privacy-First Design:** All your data, screenshots, and browsing history stay locally on your computer and are never uploaded to the cloud.
* **Parallel Processing:** Run several different tasks simultaneously in separate tabs without interrupting your own personal browsing.
