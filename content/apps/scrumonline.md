### What it does in plain English
ScrumOnline is a digital meeting assistant designed to help teams estimate the complexity of project tasks during planning sessions. The meeting leader creates a private session, and team members join using their own phones, tablets, or laptops. Once everyone has secretly submitted their estimate, the tool reveals all results at once to spark productive discussion.

### Who it's for
* Project managers and Scrum Masters facilitating team estimation meetings.
* Software development teams looking for a simple, responsive way to conduct planning poker remotely or in person.

### Key features
* **Real-time collaboration:** Everyone sees the same session updates instantly on their own devices.
* **Private voting:** Team members select their estimates privately to ensure independent thinking.
* **Instant reveal:** The meeting leader can flip all cards simultaneously to compare results.
* **Responsive design:** Works seamlessly on any device with a web browser, including phones and tablets.
* **Visual feedback:** Highlights the highest and lowest estimates to help guide team conversations.

### How to install (Download)
ScrumOnline is a web-based application. You do not need to download or install anything to use it. You can access the live service directly at [https://www.scrumpoker.online](https://www.scrumpoker.online).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, works on any device with a browser, and requires no setup for participants.
* **Cons:** Requires an active internet connection for all participants and relies on a web browser rather than a dedicated mobile app.
