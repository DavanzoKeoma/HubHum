### What it does in plain English
Nightingale acts as a central watchdog for your technology, checking your servers, logs, and databases for signs of trouble. Instead of juggling multiple monitoring tools, it brings all your warning signals into one clear dashboard. When an issue is detected, it automatically messages your team through Slack, email, or other apps you already use.

### Who it's for
*   **IT Administrators:** Who need to keep an eye on many different servers and databases without buying expensive commercial software.
*   **DevOps Engineers:** Who want to customize how alerts are sent and processed to fit their specific team workflows.
*   **Small Business Owners:** Who need reliable technical monitoring but prefer to keep their data private and self-hosted.

### Key features
*   **Multi-Source Monitoring:** Connects to various data types like metrics, logs, and database queries to find problems in one place.
*   **Smart Notifications:** Sends alerts to popular tools like Slack, Discord, and email, ensuring the right people get the message.
*   **Built-in AI Assistant:** Includes a helper that can investigate why an alert fired and explain the findings in plain language.
*   **Team Management:** Allows you to group alerts by project or team, so each group only sees the issues relevant to them.
*   **Flexible Setup:** Works with your existing data storage, so you don't have to move your data to a new system.

*   **Cons:** Requires a server to run, and setting up the initial connections to your data sources can take some technical effort.
