### What it does in plain English
OpenWiki is a desktop companion that lets you capture text, links, and images simply by copying them on your screen. Whenever you copy something, a gentle notification appears, allowing you to save the snippet with a single click. Artificial intelligence then reads through your saved items, sorting them into connected Wiki pages and generating helpful weekly insights.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who collect countless online articles and notes daily.
* Writers and content creators looking to organize raw research into structured thoughts.
* Anyone who wants to build a private, searchable personal library without manual folder management.

### Key features
* **Instant Capture Popup:** A small notification window appears when you copy content, ensuring you only save what you actually want.
* **Smart AI Wiki:** Automatically turns your saved snippets into interconnected concepts, topics, and structured pages.
* **Interactive Ask Sidebar:** Chat directly with your own saved library to find answers based on your collected research.
* **Privacy First:** All data is stored securely in a local database right on your device.
* **Comprehensive Insights:** Generates weekly summaries and behavioral habit reports to help you review your collected information.
