### What it does in plain English
Hex turns your spoken words into typed text instantly, right where your cursor is. It works completely offline on your computer, so your conversations never leave your device. You simply hold a key, speak, and the text appears in any app you are using.

### Who it's for
* **Writers and Students:** People who type a lot and want to speed up their workflow without looking at the keyboard.
* **Remote Workers:** Professionals who need to draft emails or notes quickly while keeping sensitive information private.
* **Accessibility Users:** Individuals who find physical typing difficult and prefer a hands-free way to communicate.

### Key features
* **100% Private Processing:** Your voice is converted to text on your own computer; no audio is sent to the internet.
* **Universal Text Paste:** Works in any application, from word processors to web browsers, by pasting text directly at your cursor.
* **Smart Vocabulary:** You can teach Hex specific names or terms so it spells them correctly every time.
* **Searchable History:** Keeps a record of your recent dictations for easy retrieval and editing later.
* **Customizable Output:** Allows you to set up automatic corrections or formatting rules for different apps.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly private since data stays on your device, and works across all major operating systems.
* **Cons:** The first time you use it, it downloads a speech model which takes up some disk space and time.
