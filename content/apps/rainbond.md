### What it does in plain English
It lets you put your software into a safe, self‑contained box that runs on any computer.  
It gives you a visual dashboard where you can start, stop, and update your programs with a few clicks.  
It handles the behind‑the‑scenes work so you don’t have to worry about servers or complex settings.  

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who want to host their own web tools.  
* Developers who need a quick way to share a prototype.  
* IT teams that must keep internal applications running securely.  

### Key features
* One‑click installation on your own machine.  
* Visual control panel for starting, stopping, and updating apps.  
* Ready‑made templates to set up common software quickly.  
* Easy moving of apps between computers or servers.  
* Works in offline environments where internet access is limited.  

### How to install (Download)
* **Use Rainbond Cloud (no setup needed):** [Run Rainbond in the cloud](https://run.rainbond.com)  
* **Install on your own server:** [Quick install guide](https://www.rainbond.com/docs/quick-start/quick-install?channel=github)  

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free to use, no subscription; visual dashboard makes management easy; works on your own hardware or cloud.  
* **Cons:** initial setup may need basic command‑line use; advanced features require learning the interface.
