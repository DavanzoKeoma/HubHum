### What it does in plain English
Shaarli is a personal space for you to store links you find online so you never lose track of them again. It is designed to be incredibly lightweight and fast, meaning it won't slow down your browsing experience. Because you host it yourself, you keep full control over your data without relying on large external companies.

### Who it's for
*   **Digital Researchers:** People who need a reliable, searchable way to catalog hundreds of articles and resources.
*   **Privacy Advocates:** Users who want to keep their browsing history and saved links on their own hardware rather than in the cloud.

### Key features
*   **Database-Free:** It runs without complex setup, making it very easy to manage and move.
*   **Lightning Fast:** Built for speed, so your list of links opens instantly every time.
*   **Personalized Tags:** Organize your saved links with custom labels to find exactly what you need in seconds.
*   **Sharing Options:** Choose to keep your links private or share them publicly with others.
*   **Minimalist Design:** A clean, distraction-free interface that focuses entirely on your content.

### How to install (Download)
Shaarli is a web-based tool that you host on your own server. You can find the full setup instructions and documentation on the [Official Documentation Page](https://shaarli.readthedocs.io/).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to use, lightweight, and gives you total ownership of your data.
*   **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of how to host a website or a small server to get it running.
