### What it does in plain English
This web application simplifies the process of finding and saving music tracks by pulling audio from video streaming sites. It goes a step further by automatically searching global music databases to embed accurate information like song titles, artist names, album art, and genres directly into the downloaded files. Users can easily access this streamlined music-gathering interface through a clean web browser layout without dealing with messy file tags.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want to organize their local audio libraries with complete, clean metadata.
* Listeners looking for a straightforward way to download tracks for offline playback with correct album artwork.
* Anyone who prefers a visual browser interface over complex command-line tools for managing music downloads.

### Key features
* Automatically embeds complete song metadata and high-resolution album artwork into files.
* Sources accurate track details from recognized online music directories like iTunes and LastFM.
* Completely redesigned user interface that runs significantly faster than the first version.
* Dedicated settings menu allowing users to customize their download preferences easily.
* Fully web-based experience accessible directly through your preferred browser or local server.
