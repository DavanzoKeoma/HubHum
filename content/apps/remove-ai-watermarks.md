### What it does in plain English
This tool helps you clean up digital content you have generated yourself by removing visible labels, hidden watermarks, and technical tracking data. It supports images, videos, audio recordings, and text documents across various popular formats. Whether you want to clear out a corner stamp or scrub invisible provenance signals, it provides straightforward commands to prepare your media files.

### Who it's for
* Content creators who want to strip system-generated labels from their personal media projects
* Researchers and privacy advocates studying digital media provenance and content markers
* Everyday users looking for a straightforward utility to clean hidden metadata from exported files

### Key features
* Detects and removes common visible stamps, corner badges, and text watermarks from images and videos
* Cleans hidden provenance tags, creator parameters, and tracking metadata without harming core quality
* Handles audio files to spot and mitigate compatible embedded signatures and stream markers
* Offers flexible installation modes so you can install only the specific features or models you need
* Operates directly through command-line commands, scripts, or local developer tools
