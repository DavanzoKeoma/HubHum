### What it does in plain English
KitchenOwl is a smart helper for organizing your household's food and shopping routine. It lets family members share a live grocery list, plan weekly meals, and save favorite recipes in one convenient place. You can also track household expenses and balances to keep sharing food costs fair and simple.

### Who it's for
* Families and roommates who want to coordinate grocery shopping and cooking without confusion.
* Home cooks who like to collect recipes and automatically turn them into shopping lists.
* Households looking to track food expenses and split costs evenly.

### Key features
* Real-time shopping list synchronization across multiple devices and users.
* Recipe manager that lets you add ingredients directly to your current grocery list.
* Weekly meal planner to organize what your household will cook each day.
* Household expense tracker to manage shared balances and spending history.
* Partial offline support so you can check items off your list even without an internet connection.
