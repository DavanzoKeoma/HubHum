### What it does in plain English
Macchina displays essential information about your computer, such as your processor load, memory usage, and system uptime. It is designed to be lightweight and fast, ensuring it does not slow down your machine while running. You can fully customize how the information looks to match your personal style.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want a quick glance at their system health without opening heavy diagnostic tools.
* Enthusiasts who enjoy customizing their desktop environment with personalized visual themes.

### Key features
* **Performance-focused:** Built to be extremely fast and efficient on system resources.
* **Highly customizable:** Includes a flexible theming system to change the visual layout.
* **Portable themes:** Themes are kept separate from settings, making it easy to share your look with others.
* **Diagnostic support:** Includes a built-in "doctor" tool to help troubleshoot if information fails to load.
