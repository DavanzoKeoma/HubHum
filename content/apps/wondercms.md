### What it does in plain English
WonderCMS gives you a lightning-fast way to build and manage simple websites using just a handful of core files. Instead of dealing with heavy databases or confusing control panels, you simply upload the files to your server and start editing your text right on the page. It stays entirely out of your way, keeping your pages responsive, private, and free from unnecessary tracking.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners looking to launch a clean, no-fuss informational website quickly.
* Bloggers and writers who want a minimalist space to publish text without clutter.
* Freelancers who need an ultra-simple portfolio site that is effortless to transfer between web hosts.

### Key features
* Zero configuration required: just unzip and upload the files to your web space.
* Extremely lightweight structure consisting of only five essential core files.
* Direct on-page editing that lets you click and change text instantly.
* Built-in security measures like login URL customization and smart password protection.
* Simple one-click updates and backups to keep your site safe with minimal effort.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, incredibly fast loading speeds, zero database configuration needed, and complete privacy with no tracking cookies.
* **Cons:** Not designed for massive online stores or complex multi-author blogs with advanced user permission roles.
