### What it does in plain English
Invoice-Downloader connects securely to your email inbox to scan, filter, and download digital invoice documents like PDFs and tax receipts. It uses smart recognition technology to read invoice details, automatically rename and sort them into appropriate categories, and generate a clear spreadsheet summarizing all your expenses. The tool runs locally on your computer to protect your privacy while eliminating tedious monthly paperwork.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and independent contractors who spend hours manually gathering receipts for tax season.
* Small business owners looking to streamline monthly expense reporting and record-keeping.
* Remote workers who need a hassle-free way to collect travel, lodging, and dining invoices from their email.

### Key features
* **Inbox Scanning:** Automatically connects via IMAP to collect receipts from supported email providers.
* **Smart Filtering:** Multi-layer funnel rules accurately weed out promotional emails and non-invoice attachments.
* **Automated File Organization:** Sorts documents into clear category folders and pairs related slips together.
* **Instant Expense Summaries:** Compiles all processed invoice data directly into a neat spreadsheet report.
* **Local Processing:** Keeps your personal financial documents secure by handling files right on your device.
