### What it does in plain English
AnkiDroid turns your Android phone into a pocket‑size study aid. It shows you flashcards at the right moment so the information sticks. You can add your own cards or download ready‑made decks for any subject.

### Who it's for
Students who need to memorize lecture notes, language learners building vocabulary, and professionals preparing for certifications.

### Key features
- Night‑mode for low‑light study  
- Built‑in whiteboard to draw answers  
- Syncs with AnkiWeb so cards are safe across devices  
- Text‑to‑speech reads cards aloud  
- Over 10,000 ready‑made decks available for any subject
