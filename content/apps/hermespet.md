### What it does in plain English
HermesPet transforms how you interact with artificial intelligence by placing a smart companion right inside your MacBook's menu area and screen edges. It lets you run multiple AI assistants side-by-side, view step-by-step task execution, and monitor your computer's health in real-time. Instead of jumping between browser tabs or chat windows, you can trigger quick questions, drop files, and manage your tasks without breaking your daily workflow.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who juggle multiple AI tools daily and want a unified, distraction-free control panel.
* Professionals and writers looking for quick screenshot analysis, voice dictation, and file processing powered by AI.
* Anyone who appreciates a lively desktop companion that tracks system resources like CPU, memory, and temperature.

### Key features
* **Screen Edge Control Panel:** Slide out from the edge of your screen for instant access to chat tools and live system diagnostics.
* **Multi-Assistant Support:** Connect online models and local coding terminals into one coordinated workspace.
* **Smart Quick Actions:** Capture screenshots, use voice-to-text input, or drag and drop files directly onto your desktop pet.
* **Long-Term Memory:** Keep your past conversations organized locally into searchable topic maps and knowledge graphs.
* **Live System Monitoring:** Keep an eye on your Mac's performance, network speed, and battery health at a glance.
