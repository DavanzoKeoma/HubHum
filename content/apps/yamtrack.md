### What it does in plain English
Yamtrack acts as a digital library where you can log your progress across movies, TV shows, books, and video games. It keeps track of what you have finished, what you are currently enjoying, and what you plan to experience next. Because you host it yourself, your personal data and viewing habits remain completely under your own control.

### Who it's for
*   **Media Enthusiasts:** People who want a single, organized list to track their progress across various hobbies like reading, gaming, and watching shows.
*   **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Individuals who prefer to keep their personal watch history and ratings on their own private server rather than on public social platforms.

### Key features
*   **All-in-One Tracking:** Manage movies, TV shows, anime, manga, games, books, comics, and board games in one interface.
*   **Automated Sync:** Connect your existing media server accounts to automatically update your progress when you finish a show or movie.
*   **Custom Lists:** Create personalized collections and share them with friends or family for collaborative tracking.
*   **Release Alerts:** Get notifications for upcoming releases so you never miss a new episode or book launch.
*   **Data Portability:** Easily import your history from other popular tracking sites or export your data to a simple file for safekeeping.

### How to install (Download)
Yamtrack is designed to be hosted on your own computer or server using Docker. 
*   Visit the [Official Setup Documentation](https://fuzzygrim.github.io/Yamtrack/release/setup/) for step-by-step instructions on how to start the application.
*   You can download the necessary configuration files directly from the [Official official project page](https://github.com/FuzzyGrim/Yamtrack).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely private and self-hosted, supports a wide variety of media types, and offers automated syncing with popular media servers.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of setting up server software, and initial configuration is necessary to get all features running.
