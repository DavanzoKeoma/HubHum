### What it does in plain English
Open Computer Use is a tool that gives your AI assistants the ability to see and interact with your computer screen. It allows these assistants to click buttons, type text, and manage windows to complete tasks on your behalf. By bridging the gap between AI and your desktop, it turns your computer into an active partner that can handle repetitive work automatically.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want to automate complex workflows across multiple desktop applications.
* Developers and enthusiasts looking to connect AI agents to their local operating system environment.

### Key features
* **Universal Control:** Works across macOS, Linux, and Windows to provide consistent automation capabilities.
* **Human-like Interaction:** Enables AI to perform mouse clicks, keyboard inputs, and window management.
* **Secure Operation:** Includes protective features like a screen shield when the AI is working in the background.
* **Flexible Integration:** Easily connects with various AI agent tools to extend their functionality.
