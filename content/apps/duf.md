### What it does in plain English
Duf helps you see exactly how much space is left on your computer drives and connected devices. It transforms complex system information into a colorful, organized table that is easy to understand at a glance. You can quickly filter or sort the results to find exactly the information you need about your files and folders.

### Who it's for
*   **Everyday users** who want to quickly check if their hard drive is getting full without reading confusing technical reports.
*   **System administrators** who need a fast, readable way to monitor storage across multiple drives or network locations.

### Key features
*   **Colorful, readable layout:** Automatically adjusts to your screen size and theme for the best viewing experience.
*   **Smart filtering:** Easily hide or show specific types of drives, such as network storage or temporary system files.
*   **Custom sorting:** Organize your list by size, available space, or file system type to find what matters most.
*   **Flexible output:** View your storage data as a simple table or export it as a data file for further use.

*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, lightweight, and significantly more readable than standard system tools.
*   **Cons:** Designed primarily for use in a command-line interface, which may require a brief learning curve for those used only to graphical file managers.
