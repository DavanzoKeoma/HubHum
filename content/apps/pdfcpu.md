### What it does in plain English
This tool helps you handle everyday document tasks by letting you combine multiple PDF files into one, split large files into smaller parts, and secure them with passwords. You can also extract individual pages, add custom watermarks, and optimize document sizes without needing heavy commercial software. It runs quickly on your machine to automate repetitive document chores seamlessly.

### Who it's for
* Office workers and administrators who regularly merge or split reports.
* Developers looking to integrate document manipulation capabilities into custom workflows.
* Anyone needing a reliable way to stamp, secure, or inspect PDF files without online services.

### Key features
* Merge multiple PDF files into a single organized document.
* Split, trim, and extract specific pages with custom rules.
* Encrypt and decrypt documents with passwords for better privacy.
* Add text or image watermarks and stamps across pages.
* Validate file structure and check digital signature integrity.

* **Cons:** Operates via text commands which may take a few minutes to learn for non-technical users.
