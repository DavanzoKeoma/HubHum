### What it does in plain English
Seanime acts as a personal media server that gathers your anime and manga files into one easy-to-navigate library. It includes a built-in player that lets you watch shows or read chapters directly through your web browser or desktop app. You can also connect it to your existing tracking lists to keep your progress synced automatically.

### Who it's for
* Anime and manga enthusiasts who want to organize their local file collections in one place.
* Users who prefer a custom, ad-free viewing experience across their computer, phone, and tablet.

### Key features
* **Unified Library:** Automatically scans and organizes your local anime and manga files without needing complex folder structures.
* **Built-in Player:** Includes a high-quality video player that supports advanced subtitles and custom visual settings.
* **Cross-Device Sync:** Access your library from a web browser, the desktop app, or the companion mobile app for on-the-go viewing.
* **Smart Tracking:** Integrates with your AniList account to keep your watching and reading progress updated in real-time.
* **Flexible Streaming:** Supports streaming from local files or external sources, with options to use your favorite external media players.
