### What it does in plain English
Sunnify is a user-friendly desktop application that lets you save your favorite music playlists, albums, and individual tracks directly onto your computer. It automatically embeds proper song titles, artist names, albums, and cover art into every downloaded file. The software runs entirely on your own device without requiring a paid subscription or separate helper tools.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want to keep local backup copies of their favorite online playlists and albums.
* Users looking for a straightforward desktop tool to organize music libraries with clean metadata.
* Individuals who prefer managing their audio files without creating online accounts or paying ongoing subscription fees.

### Key features
* Download entire music playlists, albums, or single tracks by simply pasting a link.
* Automatically saves cover art and proper tags directly into your audio files.
* Supports multiple audio formats including MP3, M4A, FLAC, and WAV.
* Includes parallel downloading to finish large playlists quickly and efficiently.
* Works completely locally on your machine with built-in tools and no external software required.
