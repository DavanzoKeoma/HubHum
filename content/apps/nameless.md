### What it does in plain English
NamelessMC is a dedicated website platform built specifically for Minecraft server communities to stay connected. It allows you to create forums, custom pages, and player profiles that sync directly with your game server. You can easily manage user accounts, display server status, and integrate with Discord to keep your community updated in one place.

### Who it's for
*   Minecraft server owners who want a professional home for their community.
*   Community managers looking to link their game server data with a website and Discord.

### Key features
*   **Community Forums:** Built-in discussion boards to keep your players engaged.
*   **Minecraft Integration:** Sync player ranks, verify accounts, and show live server status directly on your site.
*   **Discord Connectivity:** Link user accounts and sync roles between your website and your Discord server.
*   **Customizable Design:** Use templates and modules to change the look and functionality of your site to match your server's theme.
*   **Social Logins:** Allow your members to sign up or log in using their existing Discord or Google accounts.
