### What it does in plain English
BPB-Worker-Panel is a tool that helps you create and manage your own private internet connection settings to bypass restricted networks. It provides a simple dashboard where you can organize different connection types, such as VLESS or Trojan, to keep your browsing private. By using this panel, you can easily generate subscription links that work with many popular internet connection apps across your devices.

### Who it's for
This tool is for individuals living in regions with restricted internet access who need a reliable way to maintain connectivity. It is also perfect for privacy-conscious users who want to manage their own private proxy servers without relying on third-party services.

### Key features
* **Private Control Panel:** A password-protected dashboard to manage all your connection settings in one place.
* **Multiple Connection Types:** Supports various protocols like VLESS, Trojan, and Warp to ensure stable access.
* **Customizable Routing:** Includes built-in rules to block ads, malware, and phishing sites while bypassing regional restrictions.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Generates subscription links that work with most major cross-platform connection apps.
* **Private DNS:** Includes a built-in private DNS server to enhance your online privacy and security.
