### What it does in plain English
ClassicPress is an open-source website management platform designed for writers, bloggers, and independent creators. It offers a clean, lightning-fast alternative to mainstream publishing software by removing heavy modern page-building tools. Users get a distraction-free writing environment that loads quickly and stays reliable over time.

### Who it's for
* Bloggers and writers who prefer the traditional, distraction-free text editor over complex block interfaces.
* Small business owners looking for a lightweight, dependable website platform that loads fast.
* Developers and site administrators who want a secure, community-driven publishing tool with minimal bloat.

### Key features
* Retains the classic text editor by default for familiar and straightforward content creation.
* Significantly smaller codebase and lighter memory footprint than standard publishing platforms.
* Fully compatible with thousands of existing plugins and themes built for traditional setups.
* Community-governed development model ensuring the platform remains free and independent.
* Enhanced security focus and steady performance optimizations for everyday websites.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source forever, completely free of subscription fees, lightweight performance, familiar writing interface.
* **Cons:** Requires web hosting to run, fewer built-in modern drag-and-drop page creation tools out of the box.
