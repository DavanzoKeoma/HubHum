### What it does in plain English
TablePro helps you look at and change the information stored in your digital databases without needing complex technical skills. It works directly on your Mac, iPhone, iPad, or Linux computer, making it feel fast and natural to use. You can connect to different types of data storage systems, write simple commands to find what you need, and update records right from your screen.

### Who it's for
*   **Small business owners** who need to check customer lists or inventory levels on the go using their phone or tablet.
*   **Developers and IT staff** who manage multiple data systems and want a single, fast tool that works across different operating systems.
*   **Data enthusiasts** who want to explore and organize their personal data files without paying for expensive commercial software.

### Key features
*   **Multi-platform support:** Works natively on macOS, Linux, iPhone, and iPad, ensuring a smooth experience on all your devices.
*   **Wide database compatibility:** Connects to popular systems like PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, and MongoDB, among others.
*   **Easy data editing:** View tables like a spreadsheet and edit individual rows directly without writing complex code.
*   **Secure connections:** Supports secure tunneling and encryption to protect your data while connecting to remote servers.
*   **Smart assistance:** Includes optional AI help to suggest commands or explain data, available on Mac and Linux.
