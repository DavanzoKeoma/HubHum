### What it does in plain English
ChordSeqAI is a clever browser tool that helps songwriters and music lovers create smooth chord progressions. As you select or add chords to your timeline, artificial intelligence instantly recommends the next best musical fit. You can listen to your creation immediately, adjust timing, change styles, and export your work for use in professional music software.

### Who it's for
* Songwriters and composers looking for fresh inspiration when writing new songs
* Music hobbyists experimenting with harmony and chord structures
* Producers wanting a fast way to map out backing tracks before moving to a digital audio workstation

### Key features
* Intelligent AI suggestions that recommend fitting next chords based on your sequence
* Interactive timeline where you can add, delete, zoom, and adjust chord durations
* Multiple AI model styles and genre options to guide the mood of your music
* Built-in audio playback with a metronome and tempo controls to hear your ideas instantly
* MIDI and custom format file import and export for seamless workflow integration
