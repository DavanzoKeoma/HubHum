### What it does in plain English
WebCord gives you a dedicated desktop space for chatting and communicating with your online communities. It actively protects your personal information by blocking hidden tracking scripts and unauthorized camera access. You can customize your messaging experience with personalized styles and appearance settings.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious online community members who want better control over their personal data.
* Desktop users looking for a lightweight, responsive alternative chat interface.
* Anyone who wants to prevent intrusive tracking while staying in touch with friends and groups.

### Key features
* Blocks known online trackers and hidden fingerprinting methods to protect your identity.
* Manages permissions for sensitive device hardware like your microphone and camera.
* Uses modern security practices to keep your messaging environment safe and stable.
* Fully customizable appearance options and support for personal stylesheets.
* Responsive layout designed to work smoothly across various screen sizes.
