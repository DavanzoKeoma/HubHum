### What it does in plain English
BongoCat places an adorable animated cat right onto your computer screen. As you type, click your mouse, or press buttons on a gamepad, the cat mimics your actions in real time. You can freely reposition the companion window anywhere on your screen so it never gets in your way.

### Who it's for
* Streamers and content creators looking to add a cute, reactive overlay element to their setup.
* Office workers and students who want a playful, stress-relieving companion while typing away.
* Anyone who enjoys desktop customization and wants a lively touch on their screen.

### Key features
* Interactive animations that instantly match your keyboard, mouse, and gamepad actions.
* Support for custom character models so you can personalize your feline friend.
* Lightweight design that runs completely offline to protect your personal privacy.
* Completely free and open-source software with zero hidden data collection.
