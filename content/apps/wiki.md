### What it does in plain English
Wiki.js lets you set up a centralized digital workspace where your team can write, organize, and find important documents. It features a clean, modern interface that makes reading and editing notes feel completely natural. You can connect it to various storage services and security options to fit your exact workflow.

### Who it's for
* Remote development teams documenting internal workflows and project guidelines.
* Small businesses building a centralized customer support knowledge base.
* Educational groups or communities sharing guides, FAQs, and reference materials.

### Key features
* Modern, responsive interface optimized for any screen size or device.
* Built-in support for multiple writing formats like Markdown, Visual editors, and Code blocks.
* Flexible storage integrations to keep your files safely stored where you prefer.
* Advanced user management and access permissions to control who views or edits pages.
