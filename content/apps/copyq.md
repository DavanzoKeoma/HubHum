### What it does in plain English
CopyQ keeps a running list of everything you copy to your computer's clipboard, so you never lose important text or images again. You can organize these saved items into tabs, edit them, or search through your history to find something you copied hours or even days ago. It works quietly in the background and lets you quickly paste any previous item back into your work with a simple click or shortcut.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who frequently copy snippets from multiple sources for projects.
* Office workers and writers who need to manage multiple pieces of text or images throughout the day.

### Key features
* **History Tracking:** Automatically saves text, images, and other formats you copy.
* **Organized Storage:** Create custom tabs to categorize your saved clips for better organization.
* **Quick Access:** Use simple keyboard shortcuts to view your history and paste items instantly.
* **Editing Tools:** Modify saved text or add notes and tags directly within the app.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works consistently across Windows, macOS, and Linux.
