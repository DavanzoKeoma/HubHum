### What it does in plain English
Technitium DNS Server acts as a private gatekeeper for your internet connection, filtering out unwanted ads and malicious websites before they reach your devices. By managing how your computer finds websites, it prevents your internet provider from tracking your browsing habits. It works silently in the background to make your web pages load faster while keeping your digital footprint smaller.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious individuals** who want to stop their internet provider from monitoring their website visits.
*   **Families and small business owners** who want to block ads and harmful content across every device on their network at once.

### Key features
*   **Network-wide ad blocking:** Automatically stops ads and trackers for every device connected to your home or office network.
*   **Enhanced privacy:** Uses encrypted connections to ensure your browsing requests cannot be intercepted or modified.
*   **Easy management:** Includes a simple, user-friendly dashboard that works in any web browser to help you monitor network traffic.
*   **Faster browsing:** Speeds up your internet experience by remembering frequently visited websites locally.
*   **Cross-platform support:** Runs smoothly on Windows, Linux, macOS, and Raspberry Pi hardware.

*   **Docker:** Use the official image available on [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/r/technitium/dns-server).
*   **Linux/Raspberry Pi:** Follow the [official setup guide](https://blog.technitium.com/2017/11/running-dns-server-on-ubuntu-linux.html).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscription fees, and offers advanced privacy controls for power users.
*   **Cons:** Requires a device to be left running 24/7 to provide constant protection, and initial setup may require basic knowledge of network settings.
