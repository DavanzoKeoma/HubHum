### What it does in plain English
Editly is a tool that lets you create videos by providing a list of files and settings rather than dragging and dropping items on a timeline. It automatically handles tasks like resizing clips, adding smooth transitions, and mixing audio tracks to create a final result. This approach is perfect for generating consistent videos, slideshows, or promotional content quickly and efficiently.

### Who it's for
* Content creators who want to automate the production of repetitive video formats like slideshows or social media updates.
* People who prefer organizing their video projects as a structured list of instructions rather than using complex visual editing software.

### Key features
* **Automated Assembly:** Automatically scales, crops, and letterboxes your clips to fit any screen size or social media format.
* **Smart Audio:** Includes automatic audio mixing, crossfading, and volume leveling to ensure your background music and voice-overs sound balanced.
* **Dynamic Content:** Supports adding text overlays, subtitles, and custom visual effects using simple settings.
* **Fast Previewing:** Offers a fast mode that creates low-resolution previews so you can check your work without waiting for a full-quality render.
* **Flexible Output:** Can export your final project as a standard video file or an animated image.

### How to install (Download)
Editly is a tool designed for your computer's command terminal. To install it, ensure you have [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/en/) installed, then run the following command in your terminal:

`npm i -g editly`

For detailed usage instructions and examples, visit the [Official Editly official project page](https://github.com/mifi/editly).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly efficient for batch processing, and requires no subscription fees.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with using a computer terminal and is not a visual, point-and-click video editor.
