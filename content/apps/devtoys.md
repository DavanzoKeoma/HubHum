### What it does in plain English
DevToys brings together dozens of tiny, everyday coding utilities into a single, clean desktop application. Instead of jumping between random, untrustworthy web pages to decode text, format code, or compress images, you can do it all securely offline. The smart clipboard feature automatically detects what kind of data you copied and instantly recommends the best tool for the job.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and programmers juggling multiple data formats daily.
* Web designers and content creators needing quick image and text conversions.
* System administrators who frequently validate configurations, tokens, and hashes.

### Key features
* **Smart Detection:** Automatically reads your clipboard data and suggests the most relevant utility instantly.
* **Conversion Tools:** Seamlessly translate between data formats like JSON, YAML, dates, and number bases.
* **Encoding & Decoding:** Easily process HTML entities, URLs, Base64 strings, cryptographic hashes, and QR codes.
* **Graphics Utilities:** Inspect color palettes, simulate color blindness, and compress PNG or JPEG files.
* **Text Comparison:** Spot differences between two blocks of text or review Markdown previews side by side.
