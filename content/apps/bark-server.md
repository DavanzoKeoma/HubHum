### What it does in plain English
Bark Server is the backend component that powers the Bark mobile experience. It acts as a private bridge, allowing you to trigger custom alerts and messages to be sent to your iPhone. By hosting this server yourself, you maintain full control over your notification data and delivery flow.

### Who it's for
*   **Home Automation Enthusiasts:** People who want to receive alerts from their smart home devices directly on their phone.
*   **Developers and Power Users:** Individuals who want to create custom notification triggers for their own scripts or personal projects.

### Key features
*   **Self-Hosted Privacy:** Keep your notification data on your own hardware rather than relying on third-party services.
*   **Custom Alerts:** Send personalized messages to your iPhone whenever you need them.
*   **Flexible Storage:** Supports local file-based storage or external databases like MySQL.
*   **Universal Compatibility:** Works seamlessly with the Bark iOS application to deliver your messages.
