### What it does in plain English
Bloglite is a private, lightweight space for you to write down thoughts, recipes, or notes that you want to keep for later. Unlike social media platforms, you own your content entirely and keep it on your own server, protecting you from unexpected account bans. It is designed to be fast and easy to use, allowing you to publish posts from your phone or computer in just a few seconds.

### Who it's for
*   Individuals who want a private or public digital notebook to store ideas and knowledge.
*   Teachers or professionals who want a simple way to share recurring answers and resources with others.

### Key features
*   **Minimalist Design:** Uses very little memory, making it fast and inexpensive to run.
*   **Easy Writing:** Create and edit your posts using simple formatting tools directly in your browser.
*   **Built-in Math Support:** Easily display complex math equations using standard notation.
*   **Full Control:** Your data is stored in a simple format, and you can back it up automatically to your own files.
*   **Mobile Friendly:** Write, edit, and manage your blog from any device with a web browser.

### How to install (Download)
Bloglite is designed to be run as a containerized service. You can find the official images and setup instructions at the [Bloglite Docker Registry](https://zot.huijzer.xyz/image/bloglite). To get started, you will need a server environment that supports Docker Compose.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Complete ownership of your content, extremely lightweight, and no subscription fees.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup and maintenance to get it running.
