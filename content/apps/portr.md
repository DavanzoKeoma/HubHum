### What it does in plain English
Portr creates a secure bridge between your private computer and the public internet. This allows you to show your work-in-progress websites or services to colleagues or clients without needing to move them to a permanent server. It also includes built-in tools to help you watch and debug how your website handles incoming traffic.

### Who it's for
*   **Web Developers:** Perfect for showing a live preview of a website you are currently building on your own machine.
*   **Small Teams:** Ideal for groups that need to quickly share development progress without the complexity of setting up professional hosting.

### Key features
*   **Instant Public Access:** Turn any local web service into a public link with a single command.
*   **Live Traffic Inspector:** Watch incoming requests in real-time and replay them to see how your site responds.
*   **Request Logging:** Automatically saves your website's traffic history so you can review it later.
*   **Docker Support:** Easily connect your containerized services to the internet using simple labels.
*   **Team Management:** Includes a dashboard to help manage connections and team access.

### How to install (Download)
You can find the official installation instructions and setup guides on the [Portr Documentation Website](https://portr.dev/docs/client/installation).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Very fast to set up, includes helpful debugging tools, and works with existing local services.
*   **Cons:** Designed for development and testing, so it is not intended to be used for permanent public-facing websites.
