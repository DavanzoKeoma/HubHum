### What it does in plain English
Lowdefy allows you to build functional business applications by defining how they look and behave using simple text settings. Because these settings are easy to read, they are perfect for both humans to manage and artificial intelligence to generate. This approach keeps your projects organized, consistent, and much easier to update over time compared to traditional methods.

### Who it's for
* **Business owners** who need custom internal tools to manage data without hiring a large team of specialized programmers.
* **Product teams** who want to quickly prototype and launch web applications that can be easily maintained or modified later.

### Key features
* **Config-First Design:** Build entire interfaces using structured text files that are easy to review and audit.
* **Ready-to-Use Components:** Access over 70 built-in elements like charts, tables, and forms to get your app running immediately.
* **Seamless Data Connections:** Easily link your app to common databases, spreadsheets, and online services without complex setups.
* **Built-in Security:** Includes ready-to-use user login and permission settings to ensure your data stays protected.
* **AI-Friendly:** The simple structure makes it easy for AI tools to help you write and update your application settings.

### How to install (Download)
Lowdefy is a web-based tool. You can get started by visiting the official website or by using the command line to initialize a project:
* [Visit the Official Website](https://lowdefy.com)
* [View the Getting Started Tutorial](https://docs.lowdefy.com/tutorial-start)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Significantly faster development time, highly maintainable, and prevents common coding errors through strict validation.
* **Cons:** Requires learning the specific configuration structure, which may take a short time to master for those new to this approach.
