### What it does in plain English
AirSim creates a realistic virtual world where you can pilot drones and cars without the risk of real-world damage. It is designed to help researchers and enthusiasts train smart systems to recognize their surroundings and navigate complex environments. By using this tool, you can gather data and refine your vehicle's behavior in a controlled, digital space.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students working on artificial intelligence and robotics.
* Hobbyists interested in experimenting with drone flight or autonomous car navigation.

### Key features
* **High-Fidelity Physics:** Simulates realistic movement and environmental conditions for both cars and drones.
* **Data Collection:** Easily record video and sensor information to train your own smart systems.
* **Flexible Control:** Pilot vehicles manually using a remote control or keyboard, or use automated commands.
* **Weather Effects:** Change environmental conditions like rain, snow, or fog to test how your vehicle handles different settings.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Works with popular game engines to provide visually stunning and accurate simulations.
