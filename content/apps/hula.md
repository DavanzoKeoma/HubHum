### What it does in plain English
HuLa is a modern messaging system that allows you to chat, share files, and stay connected with friends or colleagues. It is built for speed and performance, ensuring your conversations remain smooth regardless of the device you are using. The application supports everything from one-on-one private chats to group discussions and media sharing.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a lightweight, fast alternative to mainstream messaging apps.
* Teams or groups who need a reliable, cross-platform tool for daily communication and file sharing.

### Key features
* **Multi-device support:** Seamlessly sync your chats across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
* **Rich communication tools:** Includes group chats, message replies, read receipts, and fun stickers.
* **Integrated AI assistant:** Get help with tasks or questions directly within your chat interface.
* **Customizable experience:** Switch between light and dark themes to suit your visual preference.
* **Media and file management:** Easily share images, documents, and files with a built-in viewer.
