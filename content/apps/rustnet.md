### What it does in plain English
RustNet acts like a live dashboard for your computer's internet traffic, showing you exactly which programs are sending and receiving data. It identifies the type of connection, such as web browsing or file transfers, so you can see what is happening behind the scenes. This helps you quickly notice if a program is trying to contact a suspicious server or using more data than expected.

### Who it's for
*   **Curious users** who want to understand which background apps are quietly using their internet connection.
*   **Security-conscious individuals** who want to verify that their devices are not leaking data to unknown servers.
*   **Home network managers** who need to troubleshoot why certain websites or services are slow or unresponsive.

### Key features
*   **Live Activity View:** See real-time data usage for every running program on your computer.
*   **Protocol Detection:** Automatically recognizes common internet activities like web browsing, secure logins, and domain name lookups.
*   **Easy Filtering:** Quickly narrow down the list to see only specific apps, ports, or types of connections.
*   **Data Export:** Save a record of your network activity to share with IT support or analyze later.
*   **Secure by Design:** The tool reduces its own permissions after starting to minimize risk to your system.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, works on all major operating systems, and provides a clear visual overview of network traffic without complex setup.
*   **Cons:** Requires specific permissions to access network data, and the interface is designed for text-based terminals, which may feel unfamiliar to users who prefer graphical mouse-driven apps.
