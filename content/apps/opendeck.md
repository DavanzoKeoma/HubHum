### What it does in plain English
OpenDeck is a tool that allows you to use programmable button controllers to manage your computer's functions. It works by letting you assign specific actions, like opening apps or controlling media, to the physical buttons on your device. It is designed to be compatible with many existing plugins, making it easy to customize your workflow regardless of your operating system.

### Who it's for
* Content creators who want to manage their streaming and recording equipment with physical buttons.
* Power users who want to automate repetitive computer tasks and switch between app-specific profiles automatically.

### Key features
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works on Linux, Windows, and macOS, allowing you to use your controller across different systems.
* **Plugin Support:** Compatible with most plugins designed for popular stream controller ecosystems.
* **Custom Profiles:** Create and switch between different button layouts automatically based on the app you are currently using.
* **Open Source:** The software is free and open for anyone to inspect, ensuring transparency and community-driven improvements.
* **Flexible Control:** Includes features like multi-action buttons, brightness adjustment, and toggle switches for total device management.
