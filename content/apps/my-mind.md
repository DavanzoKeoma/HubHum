### What it does in plain English
My Mind is a digital workspace designed to help you turn complex thoughts into structured, visual diagrams. It allows you to create branching maps that connect ideas, tasks, and notes in a way that is easy to understand at a glance. You can use it to brainstorm for work, plan personal goals, or simply keep your information organized in one place.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to organize study notes or complex topics.
* Professionals and project managers who want to map out workflows and brainstorm new ideas.

### Key features
* **Visual Organization:** Create intuitive branching diagrams to represent your thoughts.
* **Web-Based:** Access your maps directly through your browser without needing to install complex software.
* **Customizable Layouts:** Easily add, move, and connect nodes to reflect how your ideas evolve.
* **Open and Free:** The software is free to use and allows you to explore its inner workings.

### How to install (Download)
You can use the tool immediately without any installation by visiting the [Official Web Application](https://my-mind.github.io/). If you prefer to host it on your own web server, you can [download the source files here](https://github.com/ondras/my-mind/archive/refs/heads/master.zip).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no account setup required for the web version, and very lightweight.
* **Cons:** Requires a local web server if you choose to host it yourself, and it does not offer cloud-based account syncing.
