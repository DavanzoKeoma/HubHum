### What it does in plain English
ChatOllama is a private interface that allows you to talk to your own documents and files using intelligent AI models. It runs entirely on your own hardware, ensuring that your sensitive information never leaves your machine. You can create persistent assistants that remember your conversations and help you complete tasks using built-in tools.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who need to organize and query large collections of PDF or text documents.
* Privacy-conscious professionals who want the power of AI assistants without relying on external cloud services.

### Key features
* **Local Privacy:** Keeps all your data and conversation history stored safely on your own computer.
* **Document Intelligence:** Upload PDFs or text files and ask questions to get answers with specific source citations.
* **Flexible AI Models:** Connect to your preferred local or remote AI services to power your conversations.
* **Task Automation:** Use built-in tools to manage files and shell commands, or add custom skills to extend what your agent can do.
* **Persistent Memory:** Your tasks and conversations stay active and ready for you even after you close your browser.

### How to install (Download)
ChatOllama is designed to be run as a local web service. To get started, please visit the official project page to access the setup instructions:
[Official Setup Guide and official project page](https://github.com/sugarforever/chat-ollama)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete data privacy, supports local document indexing, and highly customizable with various AI models.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with setting up software on your computer and is intended for local use rather than public web hosting.
