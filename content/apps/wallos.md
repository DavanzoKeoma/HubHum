### What it does in plain English
Wallos helps you organize your monthly subscriptions and recurring bills so you never lose track of where your money is going. It provides a visual dashboard to see your total spending, categorize expenses, and receive alerts before payments are due. Because you host it yourself, your private financial data stays entirely under your control.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want to stop losing money on forgotten subscriptions or unused services.
* Households looking for a private, secure way to manage shared budgets and recurring costs.

### Key features
* **Subscription Dashboard:** View all your recurring payments in one place with clear sorting and filtering options.
* **Smart Notifications:** Get reminders via email, Discord, Telegram, or other services before a payment is due.
* **Financial Insights:** Use built-in statistics to understand your spending habits and identify areas to save.
* **Currency Support:** Manage expenses in multiple currencies with automatic exchange rate updates.
* **AI Assistance:** Optionally connect to AI tools to receive personalized tips on how to save money on your subscriptions.

### How to install (Download)
Wallos is designed to be hosted on your own server or computer. You can find the full setup instructions and the source code on the [Official Wallos Website](https://wallosapp.com). For those who want to test it first, a live demo is available at [https://demo.wallosapp.com](https://demo.wallosapp.com) (use username `demo` and password `demo`).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, keeps your financial data private, highly customizable, and includes helpful notification features.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup or Docker to host it yourself, and it is not a "plug-and-play" mobile app.
