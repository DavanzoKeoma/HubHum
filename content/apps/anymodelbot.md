### What it does in plain English
AnyModelBot lets you create and run custom artificial intelligence assistant teams directly on your computer. You can chat with them inside the app or connect them to popular messaging platforms like WeChat, Telegram, and Discord. They remember your preferences, organize files, run background tasks, and even operate your desktop safely with your permission.

### Who it's for
* **Power users** who want personalized AI assistants that remember context and manage local computer tasks.
* **Privacy-conscious professionals** who prefer keeping their documents, conversation logs, and API keys securely on their own devices.
* **Remote workers** who want to trigger automated background workflows and receive updates directly in their preferred chat apps.

### Key features
* **Multi-model support:** Connect with leading artificial intelligence engines through a unified gateway or your own developer keys.
* **Local-first privacy:** Keep your chats, memory files, and personal data stored locally on your machine.
* **Desktop and file automation:** Allow your bots to read, write, and organize files with built-in safety confirmation prompts.
* **Cross-platform messaging:** Access your custom assistants via desktop applications or external chat platforms.
* **Background tasks:** Set up scheduled routines and long-running jobs that notify you only when results are ready.
