### What it does in plain English
OxiCloud is a streamlined tool that lets you store and manage your personal files, photos, and calendars on your own hardware. It is designed to be fast and efficient, avoiding the bloated features found in many commercial alternatives. By using standard web protocols, it allows you to connect your devices directly to your data without needing extra software.

### Who it's for
*   **Home lab enthusiasts** who want to manage their own data without heavy system requirements.
*   **Small teams** looking for a private, reliable space to share documents and coordinate schedules.

### Key features
*   **Standard Compatibility:** Works seamlessly with common file and calendar apps on your phone or computer.
*   **Essential Tools:** Includes file sharing, photo previews, trash recovery, and search functionality.
*   **Modern Security:** Protects your data with secure login options and user role management.
*   **Office Integration:** Supports collaborative document editing when paired with compatible office tools.
*   **Efficient Design:** Built to be lightweight, ensuring quick startup times and low resource usage.

### How to install (Download)
OxiCloud is designed for flexible setup. You can get started using the official [Docker Compose setup guide](https://github.com/AtalayaLabs/OxiCloud#docker-compose) or by downloading the [latest prebuilt binary](https://github.com/AtalayaLabs/OxiCloud/releases).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Extremely lightweight, supports standard protocols, and focuses on core functionality without unnecessary bloat.
*   **Cons:** Does not include a dedicated mobile app yet, and requires some basic knowledge of server setup.
