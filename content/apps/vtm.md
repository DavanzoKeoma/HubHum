### What it does in plain English
vtm is a compact tool that brings a desktop-like experience to your text-based command windows. It allows you to organize multiple programs within a single window, making it easier to manage your tasks without needing a heavy graphical interface. Because it is a single, lightweight file, it runs smoothly on almost any computer, whether you are using Windows, Linux, or macOS.

### Who it's for
* Power users who prefer working in text-based environments but want the organization of a desktop.
* People who need a consistent, lightweight workspace that they can take with them across different operating systems.

### Key features
* **Unified Workspace:** Organize multiple text-based programs in one place with a clean, desktop-like layout.
* **Portable Design:** The entire program is contained in one small file, making it easy to move and run anywhere.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works identically on Windows, Linux, and macOS, ensuring your setup feels the same regardless of the machine.
* **Efficient Remote Access:** Includes a specialized connection method that makes working on remote computers feel fast and responsive.

* **Pros:** Extremely lightweight, works on almost any hardware, and offers a consistent experience across different operating systems.
* **Cons:** Primarily designed for text-based workflows, which may have a learning curve if you are used to traditional mouse-driven graphical desktops.
