### What it does in plain English
This application mirrors your phone or tablet screen onto your computer monitor in real time. You can use your physical keyboard and mouse to type messages, click through menus, or play mobile games smoothly. It operates with virtually zero delay and leaves no traces or apps installed on your mobile device.

### Who it's for
* Mobile app testers who need a fast way to interact with software builds on real hardware.
* Content creators recording tutorials or gaming walkthroughs directly from a mobile device.
* Everyday users who want to reply to text messages or view notifications on a larger desktop screen.

### Key features
* Ultra-low delay responsiveness for fluid navigation and gaming.
* Audio forwarding so phone sounds play through your computer speakers.
* Built-in screen and camera recording capabilities.
* Physical keyboard and mouse simulation for complete desktop control.
* Works completely offline with no ads, accounts, or internet connection required.
