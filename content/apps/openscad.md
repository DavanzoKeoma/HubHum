### What it does in plain English
OpenSCAD is a tool for creating 3D models by writing simple instructions rather than using a mouse to draw shapes. It acts like a 3D-compiler, turning your written code into precise geometric objects that you can view and adjust in real-time. This approach is perfect for designing mechanical parts or objects that require exact measurements and repeatable patterns.

### Who it's for
* Engineers and hobbyists who need to design precise parts for 3D printing.
* People who prefer a logical, code-based approach to design over traditional artistic modeling tools.

### Key features
* **Code-driven design:** Define your objects using clear, readable instructions that you can easily edit or reuse.
* **Precise control:** Perfect for creating parts with exact dimensions and complex geometric relationships.
* **Parametric modeling:** Easily change the size or shape of your entire model by updating a single variable.
* **Standard file support:** Import and export common 3D file formats like STL, making it easy to prepare models for printing.
