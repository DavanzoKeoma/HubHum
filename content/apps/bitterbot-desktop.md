### What it does in plain English
Bitterbot is a personal artificial intelligence assistant that lives directly on your computer and keeps track of your ongoing projects and conversations. While you are away, it consolidates memories, reviews past tasks, and tests out new capabilities to help you work more efficiently. You remain in complete control of your data, choosing which model to use and ensuring everything stays stored securely on your own hardware.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users looking for a private, self-hosted assistant that remembers personal preferences across sessions.
* Developers and professionals who want an extensible tool to automate multi-step research, file management, and routine tasks.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer keeping their personal data and conversation history stored locally on their own disk.

### Key features
* **Local Memory Management:** Retains context and canonical facts across conversations using local files and SQLite databases on your disk.
* **Autonomous Dream Engine:** Organizes accumulated memories, consolidates workflows, and prepares useful briefs during idle time.
* **Flexible Model Support:** Connects easily with leading commercial language providers or fully local inference software.
* **Multi-App Integration:** Communicates comfortably through a web interface, chat applications, and automated browser tasks.
* **Peer-to-Peer Circles:** Connects safely with independently owned agents belonging to colleagues, friends, or family members.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, flexible choice of underlying models, local-first memory retention, and open-source transparency.
* **Cons:** Advanced automation features require technical comfort, and initial setup involves configuring API keys or local runtimes.
