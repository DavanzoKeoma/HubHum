### What it does in plain English
This tool acts as a personal assistant that scans millions of job postings to find roles that actually match your skills and experience. Once you select the jobs you like, it automatically fills out the company's application forms and submits them for you.

### Who it's for
* Job seekers who want to save hours of time spent manually filling out repetitive online forms.
* Professionals looking to broaden their search across dozens of job boards simultaneously.

### Key features
* **Smart Matching:** Analyzes your resume to score job postings, ensuring you only apply to roles that fit your profile.
* **Automated Submission:** Handles the tedious task of filling in required fields on company websites.
* **Application Tracking:** Keeps a record of every application sent, including screenshots of the process for your reference.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, you will need to use your terminal (PowerShell for Windows or Terminal for Linux/macOS). Please note that you will need an API key from [OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/ref/ref_accd17afb78fc95ac4d7) to process your resume and applications.

* **Windows:** Run the following command in PowerShell:
  `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/feder-cr/Jobs_Applier_AI_Agent/main/run.ps1 | iex`
* **Linux/macOS:** Run the following command in your terminal:
  `curl -LsSf https://raw.githubusercontent.com/feder-cr/Jobs_Applier_AI_Agent/main/run.sh | sh`
* **Technical Manifest:** [Download (manifest.json)](https://github.com/feder-cr/Jobs_Applier_AI_Agent/releases/download/jobs-latest/manifest.json)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Significantly reduces time spent on job hunting; highly targeted job matching; keeps a clear history of all sent applications.
* **Cons:** Requires a small amount of credit for the AI processing; the automated browser feature is not yet available for macOS users.
