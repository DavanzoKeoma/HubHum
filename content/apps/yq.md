### What it does in plain English
yq helps you manage structured data files by allowing you to quickly search for specific information within them. You can use it to update values inside your files without opening them manually in a text editor. It also acts as a bridge, letting you convert data between different common formats like YAML, JSON, and XML effortlessly.

### Who it's for
*   **System Administrators:** People who need to quickly tweak configuration files across many servers.
*   **Data Enthusiasts:** Anyone who works with data exports and needs to reformat them for different software tools.

### Key features
*   **Multi-Format Support:** Works seamlessly with YAML, JSON, XML, CSV, and more.
*   **Simple Updates:** Modify file contents directly without needing to rewrite the entire document.
*   **Portable Design:** A single, lightweight file that runs on your computer without needing complex installations.
*   **Powerful Filtering:** Easily extract specific pieces of information from large, complex data files.
*   **Format Conversion:** Quickly turn a file from one format (like JSON) into another (like YAML) with a single command.
