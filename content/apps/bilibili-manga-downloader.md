### What it does in plain English
BiliBili Manga Downloader is a convenient desktop application that brings a clean visual interface to browsing and saving comic chapters. Instead of dealing with complicated command-line setups or browser clutter, you can search by keywords and download your favorite titles quickly. It helps you manage your local comic library with adjustable download speeds and flexible file formats.

### Who it's for
* Avid readers who want to build and manage a personal offline collection of their favorite digital comics.
* Users who prefer a simple point-and-click graphical program over complex technical scripts or web interfaces.

### Key features
* Simple graphical window that replaces complicated setup steps and terminal commands.
* Keyword search functionality with highlighted search terms to find titles easily.
* Multi-threaded downloading with automatic retry logic to handle network interruptions smoothly.
* Multiple saving options and file formats to suit your storage and reading preferences.
* Built-in local library management that organizes your downloaded chapters and metadata cleanly.
