### What it does in plain English
This tool lets you use your personal note-taking workspace as a backend to write and organize stories. It automatically reads your published entries and builds a fast, lightweight website ready to share with the world. You manage your articles directly inside a familiar document layout, keeping your workflow completely unified.

### Who it's for
* Writers and creators who already plan and draft content using online note organizers.
* Developers and hobbyists looking for a straightforward way to publish static articles without database headaches.
* Anyone who wants a minimalist, distraction-free reading experience for their audience.

### Key features
* Generates lightweight web pages directly from an online public database template.
* Keeps cached copies of your articles so updates finish quickly.
* Fully supports custom themes, fonts, and layouts to match your preferred style.
* Organizes posts with tags, custom web addresses, and menu visibility controls.
* Compatible with standard online hosting providers for instant publishing.
