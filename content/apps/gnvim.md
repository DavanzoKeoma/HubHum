### What it does in plain English
Gnvim provides a clean, native desktop window for the powerful Neovim text editor. It strips away unnecessary background weight to ensure your coding or writing workspace remains lightning-fast. You get all the advanced text editing capabilities you love wrapped in a modern, streamlined display.

### Who it's for
* Programmers and software developers who rely on keyboard-driven text editing environments.
* Writers and tech enthusiasts looking for a distraction-free and snappy writing workspace.
* Power users who want a native visual window for Neovim without web-based bloat.

### Key features
* Lightweight native interface built with modern toolkit standards.
* Designed specifically to keep your text editing lightning fast and responsive.
* Clean visual layout that integrates smoothly with your desktop environment.
* Optimized connection to the text editing engine for seamless typing.

* **Pros:** Extremely fast performance, completely free and open source, zero web bloat.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern version of the text editing engine already installed on your system.
