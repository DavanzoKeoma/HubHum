### What it does in plain English
EnBizCard lets you build a personal web page that works like a digital business card. Visitors can tap buttons to call, email, or message you, and even download your contact details. You can also add photos, videos, or any other content you like.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers who want a simple online showcase of their services.  
* Small business owners looking for a free way to share contact info.  
* Creatives such as artists or musicians who want to display portfolios alongside their contact details.

### Key features
- One‑click buttons for phone, WhatsApp, email, and more.  
- Downloadable contact file that saves your info to a phone.  
- QR‑code and shareable link so anyone can get your card instantly.  
- Ability to embed photos, videos, music, or any HTML you want.  
- Free to host on your own website, keeping your data private.

### How to install (Download)
- **Use the online version:** [Open EnBizCard online](https://enbizcard.vercel.app/)  
- **Run it yourself:** Follow the simple Docker guide on the project page – see the instructions on the GitHub official project page.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** completely free, no subscription, your data stays with you, highly customizable.  
* **Cons:** you need a website or a Docker environment to host it yourself, self‑hosting may require a few extra steps.
