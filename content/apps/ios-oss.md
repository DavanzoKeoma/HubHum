### What it does in plain English
The app lets you browse thousands of creative projects from artists, makers, and entrepreneurs. You can pledge money to the ideas you like and watch their updates as they grow. It also lets creators share their stories and manage their campaigns right from their phone.

### Who it's for
* Anyone who loves finding new products and wants to support creators.  
* Artists, designers, and entrepreneurs who need a simple way to run a Kickstarter campaign from their iPhone.

### Key features
- Browse projects with photos, videos, and detailed descriptions.  
- Back projects with a few taps and track your pledges.  
- Receive updates and notifications about the projects you follow.  
- Create, edit, and publish your own campaign directly from the app.  
- View rewards, shipping details, and project timelines in one place.

### How to install (Download)
- [Visit Kickstarter for iOS](https://www.kickstarter.com/mobile)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free to use, open‑source code, works on any iPhone, community‑driven development.  
* **Cons:** requires an iOS device and internet connection, some desktop‑only features are missing.
