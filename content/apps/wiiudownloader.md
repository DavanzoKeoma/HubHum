### What it does in plain English
WiiUDownloader provides a simple way to browse and download your Wii U game library, including updates and extra content. It connects directly to official servers to retrieve files, removing the need for complicated manual key management. The application handles the decryption process automatically, making your files ready to use immediately.

### Who it's for
*   Wii U console owners who want to manage their game library and updates easily.
*   Users of game emulators who need a straightforward way to obtain and prepare game files.

### Key features
*   **Full Library Access:** Browse and search for games, updates, downloadable content, and demos.
*   **No Extra Keys Needed:** Automatically handles decryption without requiring you to hunt for external security keys.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on Windows, macOS, and Linux (including Steam Deck).
*   **Batch Processing:** Queue multiple items to download and decrypt them in one go.
*   **Space Management:** Option to automatically remove encrypted files once they have been decrypted to save storage space.
