### What it does in plain English
eSearch is a powerful desktop tool that lets you capture your screen, record videos, and extract text from images using offline technology. It allows you to instantly search or translate any text you see on your monitor, even inside videos or games. You can also pin important information to your screen so it stays visible while you work on other tasks.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to quickly copy text from images or videos.
* Office professionals who want to streamline their workflow with fast screen captures and instant translations.

### Key features
* **Smart Screen Capture:** Take precise screenshots with advanced editing tools like blurring, drawing, and geometric shapes.
* **Offline Text Recognition:** Extract text from any image or screen area without needing an internet connection.
* **Instant Translation:** Translate text directly on your screen, perfect for gaming or watching foreign-language content.
* **Screen Pinning:** Keep important snippets or images floating on top of your windows for easy reference.
* **Versatile Recording:** Record your screen, webcam, and audio with options to highlight mouse movements and keystrokes.
