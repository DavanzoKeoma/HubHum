### What it does in plain English
Whalebird is a desktop application that brings your favorite decentralized social networks together in one organized place. It lets you monitor timelines, post updates, and receive alerts without needing a web browser tab open. The layout is designed like a familiar team chat application, making it simple to keep track of conversations across different communities.

### Who it's for
* Fediverse enthusiasts who use multiple accounts across different servers and want a unified desktop space.
* People who prefer dedicated desktop applications over browser tabs for managing their social feeds.
* Users looking for a clean, distraction-free interface to follow Mastodon, Pixelfed, and compatible networks.

### Key features
* Multi-account management lets you switch between different profiles and servers effortlessly.
* Chat-style multi-column layout helps you view multiple feeds or direct messages simultaneously.
* Real-time streaming updates bring new posts and interactions to your screen instantly.
* Desktop notifications alert you immediately when someone mentions or replies to you.
* Broad compatibility supports popular decentralized networks including Mastodon, Pleroma, Pixelfed, and more.
