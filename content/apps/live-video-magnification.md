### What it does in plain English
Live Video Magnification is a desktop tool that lets you see things normally invisible to the human eye, such as a pulse in someone's face or the tiny vibrations of a mechanical part. It connects directly to your webcam or opens recorded video files to process and exaggerate subtle physical changes in real time. You can choose different viewing modes to compare the original footage with the enhanced version side-by-side.

### Who it's for
* **Engineers and researchers** looking to analyze structural vibrations and mechanical stress tests.
* **Biomedical enthusiasts** wanting to visualize pulse rates and blood flow changes.
* **Hobbyists and educators** exploring advanced video processing and physics demonstrations.

### Key features
* **Three magnification algorithms**: Choose between motion-based Laplace or Phase methods, and a color-based blood flow tracker.
* **Live webcam support**: Capture direct feeds from connected cameras via standard system drivers.
* **Flexible comparison views**: See processed, original, or side-by-side frames locked to the exact same moment in time.
* **Multiple export formats**: Save your processed visual results directly to standard video files.
* **Adjustable processing controls**: Tweak resolution, select specific areas of interest, and adjust frequency bands instantly.
