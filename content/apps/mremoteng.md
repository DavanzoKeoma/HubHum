### What it does in plain English
It lets you keep all your remote desktop, server and web sessions in one place. You can switch between them with tabs, just like a web browser. It works with many connection types so you don’t need separate tools.

### Who it's for
* IT support staff who need to hop between many machines.  
* Freelancers who manage client servers from home.  
* Home users who want a simple way to reach their work PC.

### Key features
- Tab‑based interface for quick switching between sessions.  
- Supports RDP, VNC, SSH, Telnet, HTTP/HTTPS and many other protocols.  
- Save and organize connections with folders, icons and notes.  
- Portable version that runs without installation.  
- Built‑in password manager to store credentials safely.
