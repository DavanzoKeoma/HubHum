### What it does in plain English
Ventoy is a handy utility that turns a single USB drive into a universal system installer. Instead of wiping and formatting your drive every time you want to try a different operating system, you simply copy your downloaded files straight onto it. When you restart your computer, an easy menu appears letting you choose which system you want to run or install.

### Who it's for
* IT professionals and computer technicians who frequently install various operating systems.
* Everyday computer users who like experimenting with different operating systems or testing computer rescue tools.
* Students or hobbyists who want to keep multiple repair and diagnostic kits handy on a single pocket-sized drive.

### Key features
* No reformatting needed every time you add a new operating system file.
* Supports hundreds of different operating systems and repair tools out of the box.
* Works smoothly with both older computer setups and modern secure boot systems.
* Allows you to store and run files larger than standard storage limits.
* Keeps your regular USB storage usable for normal everyday files alongside your boot files.
