### What it does in plain English
This application gives you a centralized hub to search for online stories, download complete books, and read them comfortably without an internet connection. It also organizes your personal library, tracks your progress, and supports comics or short videos in a clean, unified interface. You can easily switch between reading modes, export files, and keep your history safely backed up across devices.

### Who it's for
* Avid readers who want to store their favorite stories locally for offline reading during travel or low-connectivity situations.
* Book lovers looking for a single organized tool to manage local files, comics, and reading history across multiple operating systems.

### Key features
* Search online book catalogs, view detailed chapters, and download single works for offline access.
* Export and save books in common text formats or import your own local files into the library.
* Personalize your reading experience with adjustable font sizes, screen brightness, and flexible page-turning options.
* Maintain a synchronized reading history and manage local backups securely.
* Enjoy a responsive design that adapts smoothly to desktop screens, tablets, and mobile phones.
