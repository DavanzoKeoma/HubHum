### What it does in plain English
Cryptii provides a simple way to change how information looks or is hidden using various methods like ciphers and encoding styles. You can drag and drop different "bricks" to create a custom process for your data, such as turning text into Morse code or securing it with modern encryption. Everything happens directly inside your web browser, ensuring your private information never leaves your computer.

### Who it's for
* Students or hobbyists interested in learning how ciphers and data encoding work.
* Privacy-conscious users who need to quickly scramble or format text without using untrusted online services.

### Key features
* **Privacy-First:** All processing happens locally in your browser, meaning no data is ever sent to a server.
* **Modular Design:** Combine different tools (bricks) in a sequence to create custom data pipelines.
* **Wide Variety:** Supports everything from classic historical ciphers like Enigma to modern digital encoding like Base64 and AES.
* **Real-Time Feedback:** See your data change instantly as you adjust settings or add new steps to your process.

### How to install (Download)
Cryptii is a web-based application. You can use it immediately without any installation by visiting the official website: [Open Cryptii in your browser](https://cryptii.com/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, no account required, and highly secure since it works offline in your browser.
* **Cons:** The interface can be slightly overwhelming at first due to the large number of available tools.
