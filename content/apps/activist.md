### What it does in plain English
Activist is a community-driven digital workspace that brings together grassroots groups, organizers, and everyday supporters. It provides secure tools to discover local movements, plan events, share resources, and collaborate on social or ecological campaigns. By prioritizing privacy and open standards, the platform removes technical barriers so movements can grow safely and transparently.

### Who it's for
* Grassroots organizers and movement leaders looking for secure collaboration tools.
* Volunteers and citizens wanting to discover local events, protests, and educational meetings.
* Non-profit groups needing a private hub to manage tasks, discussions, and shared resources.

### Key features
* **Organization Profiles:** Easy-to-use pages to describe your mission, onboard new helpers, and share team structures.
* **Flexible Events:** Create educational seminars or action-oriented volunteer meetups with real-time organizer feeds and QR code flyers.
* **Resource Hub:** Keep important documents and group links organized in one private or public location.
* **Interactive Mapping:** Easily find initiatives and community gatherings using list, map, and calendar views.
* **Task Boards:** Organize day-to-day work using collaborative kanban boards attached to your groups and events.

### How to install (Download)
You can explore and use the platform directly online by visiting the [Official Activist Website](https://activist.org). Developers and self-hosters can also review the code and setup instructions on the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/activist-org/activist).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, open-source, strong focus on data privacy, and community-governed.
* **Cons:** Setting up a custom self-hosted version requires technical server knowledge.
