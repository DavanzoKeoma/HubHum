### What it does in plain English
PowerToys gives you extra tools that make Windows feel more like your own. It helps you move windows, pick colors, rename files, and many other everyday chores faster. All the tools work together inside the same program.

### Who it's for
People who like to fine‑tune their PC, remote workers who need quick shortcuts, and anyone who wants everyday tasks to be quicker.

### Key features
- Snap windows into custom zones for better multitasking.  
- Pick any color on the screen and copy its code.  
- Rename many files at once with smart patterns.  
- Keep a window always on top with a single click.  
- Search and launch apps instantly with a simple shortcut.
