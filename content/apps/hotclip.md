### What it does in plain English
HotClip takes long videos, podcast recordings, or live stream replays and automatically converts them into punchy vertical short clips. It uses advanced artificial intelligence to find the most exciting moments, adds stylish animated captions, and crops widescreen frames into vertical formats. Everything runs entirely on your own computer, ensuring your footage remains private with zero watermarks or subscription fees.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and livestreamers who want to repurpose hours of recorded footage into short-form highlights without spending hours manually editing.
* Podcasters looking to turn audio discussions into engaging vertical video summaries with automated waveforms and synchronized subtitles.
* Marketing teams and social media managers who need to quickly batch-produce promotional clips for multiple platforms.

### Key features
* **100% Local Processing:** Transcribes, edits, and exports your files directly on your computer to keep sensitive or unreleased footage completely private.
* **Smart Highlight Detection:** Automatically scans transcripts and visual cues to pinpoint the most engaging moments with clear reasoning for every cut.
* **Dynamic Captions:** Generates word-by-word highlighted subtitles and text overlays that keep viewers hooked from the first second.
* **Automatic Framing:** Adjusts widescreen footage into a vertical format while keeping the main speaker or active subject safely centered on screen.
* **No Limits or Subscriptions:** Enjoy free, open-source editing with no hidden export limits, credit systems, or mandatory account creation.
