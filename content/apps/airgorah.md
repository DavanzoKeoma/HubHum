### What it does in plain English
Airgorah helps you check how secure your wireless network is by looking at nearby traffic and connected devices. It gives network owners a clear way to spot vulnerabilities and test connection strength right from their desktop.

### Who it's for
* Home network owners wanting to test their wireless password strength
* Tech hobbyists exploring network traffic and security protocols on Linux
* IT students learning about wireless network administration and safety

### Key features
* Captures nearby wireless network traffic for analysis
* Discovers all devices currently connected to your access points
* Modern graphical interface built for smooth daily usage on Linux
