### What it does in plain English
Lan Mouse lets you glide your mouse pointer seamlessly off the edge of one computer screen and onto another, instantly taking control of that machine with your keyboard too. It connects your devices securely over your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet network with zero noticeable lag. All traffic moving between your screens is fully encrypted for your privacy and safety.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers and multi-device owners who juggle a personal laptop and a work desktop side by side.
* Power users who want to declutter their physical desk by ditching extra keyboards and mice.
* Anyone who prefers hardware-free workspace sharing across different operating systems like Windows, Linux, and macOS.

### Key features
* **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** Move your cursor smoothly between Windows, macOS, and popular Linux desktop environments.
* **Encrypted Network Traffic:** Keeps your keystrokes and pointer movements completely private using secure DTLS encryption.
* **Modern Graphical Interface:** Easy-to-use control panel built with native desktop toolkits for quick device pairing.
* **Flexible Positioning:** Arrange your screens in any layout—left, right, top, or bottom—to match your exact physical desk setup.
* **Background Operation:** Runs smoothly in the background as a lightweight system service or via command-line options.
