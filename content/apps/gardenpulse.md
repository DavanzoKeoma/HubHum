### What it does in plain English
GardenPulse acts as a personal assistant for your plants, whether they are in pots, hydroponic systems, or your garden. It uses your phone's camera to identify plants and check for health issues while keeping your photos private on your own device. The app also monitors local weather to suggest the best times to water and care for your greenery.

### Who it's for
*   Urban gardeners living in apartments with limited space or windowsill herbs.
*   Plant enthusiasts managing complex setups like hydroponics or large indoor collections.

### Key features
*   **Smart Plant Identification:** Snap a photo to identify your plant and detect potential issues like pests or nutrient needs.
*   **Weather-Aware Scheduling:** Automatically adjusts your watering and care reminders based on local weather forecasts.
*   **Privacy-First Diagnostics:** All leaf analysis happens directly on your phone, ensuring your photos are never sent to a cloud server.
*   **Growth Tracking:** Create beautiful time-lapse videos of your plants as they grow over time.
*   **Adaptive Growing Profiles:** Switch between different modes depending on whether you are growing in soil, containers, or water.

### How to install (Download)
GardenPulse is currently available for developers and enthusiasts to build from the source code. You can access the project files and setup instructions here: [Download and Setup via GitHub](https://github.com/AhmadHassan-BTed/GardenPulse/releases).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely private AI diagnostics, highly customizable for different growing methods, and helpful weather integration.
*   **Cons:** Requires some technical setup to configure API keys, and it is designed for users comfortable with following installation guides.
