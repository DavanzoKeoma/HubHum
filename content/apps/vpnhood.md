### What it does in plain English
VpnHood creates a secure, private tunnel for your internet traffic to keep your browsing habits hidden from prying eyes. It is specifically built to bypass advanced network firewalls and restrictions that might otherwise block your access. By using specialized connection methods, it ensures your activity remains undetectable and fast.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who need to access the internet freely without being blocked by restrictive network settings.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to ensure their online traffic is encrypted and hidden from their local network provider.

### Key features
* **One-click connection:** Get protected instantly without needing to configure complex settings.
* **Undetectable traffic:** Uses advanced methods to hide your VPN usage from network monitoring systems.
* **Flexible tunneling:** Supports multiple connection protocols to ensure you can always find a way to stay connected.
* **Smart split-tunneling:** Choose exactly which apps or countries use the secure connection, while letting others use your regular internet.
* **Cross-platform support:** Works seamlessly across Windows, Android, iOS, and Apple TV.
