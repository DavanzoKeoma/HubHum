### What it does in plain English
CubicSDR allows you to connect a radio hardware device to your computer to scan and listen to various radio frequencies. It provides a visual display that shows you exactly where signals are active across the airwaves in real-time. You can easily tune into these signals to hear everything from local broadcasts to distant transmissions.

### Who it's for
* Radio hobbyists and enthusiasts interested in exploring the airwaves.
* Students or researchers looking for a visual way to study radio frequency signals.

### Key features
* **Visual Spectrum Display:** See a live, colorful map of radio activity to easily spot and select signals.
* **Wide Compatibility:** Works with a variety of common radio hardware devices.
* **Cross-Platform:** Runs smoothly on Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
* **User-Friendly Tuning:** Click and drag on the display to quickly change frequencies and listen to what you find.

* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, provides a clear visual interface for complex data, and supports many different hardware devices.
* **Cons:** Requires specific radio hardware to function, and the setup process for some advanced features may require a bit of technical reading.
