### What it does in plain English
GEOFlow acts as a command center for businesses to organize their internal knowledge and turn it into high-quality articles using smart technology. It includes built-in quality checks and human review steps to ensure everything you publish is accurate and professional. Once approved, the platform helps you distribute your content across your own websites and other channels while tracking how well it performs.

### Who it's for
* **Content Marketing Teams:** Professionals who need to manage large volumes of articles and ensure consistent brand messaging across multiple sites.
* **Website Managers:** Individuals responsible for maintaining company portals or industry information hubs who want a streamlined workflow from drafting to publishing.

### Key features
* **Unified Content Hub:** Import documents like PDFs or Word files to create a searchable knowledge base that powers your content production.
* **AI Quality Control:** Automatically check drafts against your brand rules and factual evidence before they reach the human review stage.
* **Multi-Site Distribution:** Manage publishing, internal linking, and site maps for several websites or channels from one single dashboard.
* **Performance Insights:** Track how your content is discovered by search engines and readers to make data-driven improvements.
* **Human-in-the-loop:** Keep full control over your brand voice with built-in workflows that require human approval before any content goes live.
