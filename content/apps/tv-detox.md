### What it does in plain English
This tool helps you breathe new life into an aging Android television by clearing out sluggish background apps and obnoxious sponsored rows. You simply paste a short prompt into an assistant tool on your computer while connected to the same wireless network. The system gently disables unnecessary software instead of permanently deleting it, keeping your files safe while ensuring speedy performance.

### Who it's for
* Owners of older smart televisions or streaming boxes struggling with laggy menus and constant advertisements.
* Tech enthusiasts who want a clean, minimalist home screen showing only their installed apps and inputs.

### Key features
* Strips away stubborn bloatware, video sponsor rows, and distracting promotional material.
* Replaces cluttered manufacturer menus with a streamlined, peaceful interface displaying a simple clock and your favorite software.
* Safely turns off system components rather than deleting them, letting you reverse changes at any moment.
