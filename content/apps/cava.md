### What it does in plain English
Cava listens to the music playing on your computer and displays it as moving bars on your screen. It helps you see the rhythm and energy of your favorite songs in real-time. The program is designed to look nice and responsive, making your listening experience more engaging.

### Who it's for
* **Music lovers** who want a visual companion to their playlists or live streams.
* **Streamers and content creators** looking for a simple, lightweight background visual for their videos.
* **Tech enthusiasts** who enjoy customizing their desktop environment with unique, open-source tools.

### Key features
* **Cross-platform support:** Runs smoothly on Windows, macOS, Linux, FreeBSD, and Android.
* **Multiple display modes:** Can run in a standard window or directly in your terminal for a retro look.
* **Customizable appearance:** Adjust colors, bar height, and sensitivity to match your personal style.
* **Wide audio compatibility:** Works with most common audio systems on your device, including PulseAudio, ALSA, and Core Audio.
* **Lightweight performance:** Uses minimal system resources, so it won't slow down your music player or other apps.

* **Pros:** Completely free to use, works on almost any device, and offers a simple way to visualize sound without complex setup.
