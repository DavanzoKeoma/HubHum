### What it does in plain English
vAPI Network acts as a secure bridge between your digital wallet and various AI services, allowing you to pay for only the specific tasks you need. It gives you full control over your spending by letting you set strict limits on every payment you make. Because it is non-custodial, your keys stay on your own device, ensuring that you remain the only person with access to your funds.

### Who it's for
* Developers and AI enthusiasts who want to manage payments for multiple AI tools from one central place.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer to keep their digital keys and payment history stored locally on their own computer.

### Key features
* **Local Security:** Your digital keys are stored securely on your own machine, not on a third-party server.
* **Spending Control:** Set custom caps on your payments to ensure you never spend more than you intend.
* **Unified Discovery:** Search across multiple AI service catalogs in one place to find the tools you need.
* **Detailed Receipts:** Keep a clear, local record of every payment, including costs and performance data.
* **Agent Support:** Easily assign dedicated, capped accounts to your AI agents for automated tasks.

### How to install (Download)
To install vAPI Network, ensure you have [Node.js (version 22 or newer)](https://nodejs.org) installed on your computer. You can then install the tool by running the following command in your terminal:

`npm i -g vapi-network`

Alternatively, if you prefer not to install it globally, you can run it directly using:
`npx vapi-network setup`

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, keeps your private keys on your local device, and provides granular control over AI spending.
* **Cons:** Requires basic comfort with a terminal interface, and users must manage their own recovery phrases carefully.
