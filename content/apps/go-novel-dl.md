### What it does in plain English
go-novel-dl is a central hub that helps you find and save stories from various online novel websites. It allows you to search across multiple platforms at once and download your favorite chapters into clean, readable formats like EPUB or text files. You can also use its built-in reader to enjoy your collection with customizable settings for a comfortable reading experience.

### Who it's for
* Readers who want to collect and archive chapters from multiple online sources into a single, organized library.
* Users who prefer reading offline on their e-readers or mobile devices without needing to visit different websites.

### Key features
* **Unified Search:** Look for books across many different websites simultaneously and get the best results in one list.
* **Flexible Formats:** Save your novels as EPUB, HTML, or plain text files to suit your preferred reading device.
* **Built-in Reader:** Use the included web interface to read chapters directly with adjustable text size, themes, and background colors.
* **Smart Organization:** Keep your library tidy with automatic sorting and the ability to group books into folders.
* **Offline Access:** Download entire books or specific chapters to read whenever and wherever you want.
