### What it does in plain English
Cobalt lets you grab public videos and audio clips from your favorite websites with zero friction. You simply paste your link into the clean interface and immediately receive your file. It completely avoids annoying pop-ups, hidden paywalls, user tracking, and forced advertisements.

### Who it's for
* Everyday internet users looking for a clean way to save public video and audio clips
* People tired of cluttered downloader websites filled with misleading buttons and trackers
* Anyone who values a fast, straightforward tool that respects their privacy

### Key features
* Lightning-fast link processing with an uncluttered interface
* Zero ads, paywalls, analytics trackers, or hidden nonsense
* Direct streaming proxy approach that does not cache your files
* Completely free to use right in your web browser

### How to install (Download)
You can use the official web app directly in your browser without installing anything. Visit the official website at [cobalt.tools](https://cobalt.tools).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, no ads, no trackers, extremely fast and simple to use.
* **Cons:** Requires an internet connection and only works with publicly accessible free content.
