### What it does in plain English
Oh My PPT is a private desktop application that turns your text prompts or documents into stunning, editable slide decks. It runs locally on your computer to keep your data secure while artificial intelligence plans layouts, color schemes, and matching imagery. You can easily tweak every element, add interactive animations, and export your final presentations to standard formats for any meeting.

### Who it's for
* Teachers and students preparing engaging educational lessons and visual classroom materials.
* Business professionals and founders building rapid project reports, pitches, and conference keynotes.
* Anyone tired of tedious slide formatting who wants professional layouts generated instantly offline.

### Key features
* **Local-first privacy:** Your presentations, documents, and personal notes remain securely stored on your own device.
* **Smart layout generation:** Type a topic or upload documents, and artificial intelligence designs complete, structured slide decks.
* **Complete visual editing:** Drag and drop elements, modify styles on the fly, or chat with the assistant to update specific pages.
* **Flexible export options:** Save your completed slides as standard presentation files, high-resolution images, PDF documents, or video.
* **Built-in style library:** Choose from dozens of unique professional design themes or bring your own custom style packages.
