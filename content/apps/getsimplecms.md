### What it does in plain English
GetSimpleCMS is a streamlined publishing tool designed to help you build and manage small websites without the headache of complex database setups. Instead of storing your content on a heavy server database, it saves your pages directly as individual files for quick loading and easy backups. It provides an intuitive, user-friendly control panel that lets you edit text, organize menus, and publish updates in just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who want an easy way to manage their company website without learning complicated software.
* Freelancers and designers building lightweight, fast-loading brochure sites for local clients.
* Bloggers and hobbyists looking for a straightforward publishing system that runs smoothly on basic web hosting.

### Key features
* Operates completely without a database by saving content files directly on your web server.
* Features a clean, straightforward control panel designed to be friendly for everyday users.
* Includes a built-in text editor that makes formatting articles and web pages effortless.
* Supports customized page layouts and themes to match your unique brand identity.
* Allows you to extend functionality through community-made plugins and widgets.

### How to install (Download)
To get started with the software, visit the [Official GetSimple Website](https://get-simple.info) to download the package. You can also review the complete instructions on the [GetSimple Installation Guide](http://get-simple.info/docs/installation).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely fast loading speeds, simple file backups, and no complex database configuration required.
* **Cons:** Best suited for smaller websites rather than massive multi-author portals, and requires basic PHP hosting support.
