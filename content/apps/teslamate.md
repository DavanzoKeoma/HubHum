### What it does in plain English
TeslaMate is a private application that records and analyzes your vehicle's driving and charging data. It securely connects to your car to track trips, battery health, and charging expenses without draining your battery while parked. All of your driving history and statistics are presented in clear, customizable visual dashboards.

### Who it's for
* Tesla owners who want detailed insights into their driving habits and vehicle efficiency.
* Privacy-focused users who prefer to store their car telemetry on their own hardware rather than third-party cloud services.
* Smart home enthusiasts looking to integrate vehicle data into home automation systems.

### Key features
* High-precision drive and route data recording with automatic address lookup.
* Intelligent sleep mode design that prevents unnecessary battery drain when the vehicle is parked.
* Comprehensive dashboards for battery degradation, charging statistics, and vampire drain.
* Seamless integration with smart home platforms and messaging tools via local message brokers.
* Support for multiple vehicles under a single account with charge cost tracking.

### How to install (Download)
You can view the official setup instructions and cloud setup guidelines on the [Official TeslaMate Documentation](https://docs.teslamate.org).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** 100% free and open-source, complete privacy over your driving data, no external cloud dependencies.
* **Cons:** Requires a server or computer to run locally or on a private cloud network, initial setup involves technical configuration.
