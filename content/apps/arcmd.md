### What it does in plain English
ArcMD is a fast local text editor designed to make writing feel smooth and effortless. It works directly with standard document files on your computer without locking your data into private formats. With colorful custom spaces and instant previews, it keeps your notes organized and distraction-free.

### Who it's for
* Writers and students who want a clean, fast space to take notes and draft long-form texts.
* Developers and researchers who love standard text files and want seamless compatibility with common storage folders.
* Anyone looking for a responsive desktop writing tool with beautiful customization options.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast response times that handle large documents effortlessly.
* Direct editing of standard text files so your data stays fully portable.
* Customizable workspace themes, color gradients, and sidebars.
* Built-in math formulas, interactive task lists, and hand-drawn annotations.
* Split screens, quick search commands, and distraction-free modes.
