### What it does in plain English
This tool acts as a pre-submission guard for mobile developers, catching common reasons why Apple and Google reject new apps. It checks your project against a massive database of over 100 known rejection patterns, privacy requirements, and global regulatory deadlines. By running this check before you submit, you can fix critical issues immediately instead of waiting days for a rejection email.

### Who it's for
* **Independent App Developers** who want to avoid the frustration and time loss of repeated rejection cycles.
* **Non-technical App Owners** who need a clear, plain-English checklist to ensure their app meets store standards before handing it off for publication.

### Key features
* **Automated Guard:** Scans your project for critical risks and provides the exact fix for every issue found.
* **Regulatory Tracking:** Monitors deadlines for global laws like the EU AI Act, COPPA, and various age-related privacy regulations.
* **AI Agent Integration:** Can be wired into popular AI coding tools to automatically audit your code every time you prepare a submission.
* **Comprehensive Checklist:** Includes a manual, easy-to-follow guide for store listing and account readiness that code scans cannot verify.
* **Transparent Citations:** Every rule and rejection pattern is linked to official Apple or Google documentation, ensuring you are acting on real requirements.

* **Pros:** Free and open-source, prevents costly review delays, and provides actionable fixes rather than vague error messages.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with terminal commands for the automated guard; some store-listing checks must still be performed manually.
