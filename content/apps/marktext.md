### What it does in plain English
MarkText is a streamlined writing tool that lets you create documents using simple text-based formatting. It shows you exactly how your final document will look while you type, removing the need for complex menus or hidden settings. You can easily save your work as professional-looking files for sharing or printing.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to organize notes quickly.
* Writers and bloggers who want a clean interface to focus entirely on their content.

### Key features
* **Real-time preview:** See your formatted text instantly as you type.
* **Distraction-free mode:** A minimalist interface that hides unnecessary buttons.
* **Versatile export:** Save your writing as standard web files or professional documents.
* **Smart image handling:** Paste images directly into your notes from your clipboard.
* **Customizable themes:** Choose between light and dark styles to suit your environment.
