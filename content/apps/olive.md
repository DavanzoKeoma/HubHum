### What it does in plain English
Olive is a video editing program that allows you to import your raw footage and arrange it on a timeline. You can trim clips, add transitions, and adjust your audio to create a finished video file. It provides the essential tools needed to turn your home movies or creative projects into polished content.

### Who it's for
* Content creators looking for a free way to edit videos without expensive monthly subscriptions.
* Students or hobbyists who want to learn the basics of video editing on Windows, macOS, or Linux.

### Key features
* **Timeline Editing:** Drag and drop your video and audio files to arrange them exactly how you want.
* **Multi-Platform:** Works consistently across Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
* **Flexible Controls:** Adjust clip speed, volume, and visual layout with intuitive tools.
* **Open Format:** Designed to be a transparent and accessible tool for the creative community.

### How to install (Download)
You can download the latest versions and installers directly from the official website: [Download Olive Video Editor](https://olivevideoeditor.org/download).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no subscription fees, and works on all major computer operating systems.
* **Cons:** The software is currently in an early testing phase and may be unstable, so save your work frequently.
