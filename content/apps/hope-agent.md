### What it does in plain English
Hope Agent is a smart assistant that lives on your computer to help you manage long-term projects and daily tasks. Unlike simple chat tools, it can remember your preferences over time and work on goals even when you are not actively using the app. You can start a task on your desktop and continue it later from your phone or a web browser, ensuring your work stays organized and accessible.

### Who it's for
* **Independent creators and researchers** who need to organize notes, draft documents, and manage complex projects in one place.
* **Home server enthusiasts** who want to host a private, always-on AI assistant that they can access from any device in their home.

### Key features
* **Long-term memory:** The app learns your communication style and project history, so it gets more helpful the more you use it.
* **Autonomous goal tracking:** You can set a goal, and the assistant will break it down into steps and work toward the result in the background.
* **Cross-device access:** Start your work on your main computer and pick up exactly where you left off from a browser or mobile chat app.
* **Privacy-focused:** Your data stays on your own machine by default, and you have full control over which tools the assistant can access.
* **Versatile design tools:** Create websites, presentations, and documents directly within the app using simple instructions.
