### What it does in plain English
Moodiary is a private space where you can record your daily life using text, images, audio, and video. It helps you stay organized by allowing you to categorize entries, search through your history, and map out the places you have visited. Your data remains secure with optional password protection and flexible backup choices to keep your memories safe.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a private, distraction-free space to document their daily experiences.
* People who want to organize their memories with multimedia support and location tracking.

### Key features
* **Multimedia Journaling:** Easily add photos, audio clips, and videos to your daily entries.
* **Smart Organization:** Use categories and full-text search to find specific memories quickly.
* **Privacy First:** Protect your entries with a password or biometric security.
* **Flexible Backups:** Sync your data using your own storage services or local transfers.
* **Smart Tools:** Includes weather integration, map views for your travels, and optional AI-powered writing assistance.
