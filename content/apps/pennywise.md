### What it does in plain English
Pennywise creates a small, dedicated window for any website or video that stays visible even when you are working in other programs. It eliminates the need to constantly switch between windows just to check a reference or watch a tutorial. You can easily adjust the size and transparency of this window so it never blocks your main workspace.

### Who it's for
* Students or professionals who need to watch instructional videos while working in other software.
* People who want to keep a calendar, checklist, or live dashboard visible at all times while they focus on other tasks.

### Key features
* **Always on Top:** Keeps your chosen content pinned above all other active windows.
* **Adjustable Transparency:** Change how see-through the window is so you can peek at the content underneath.
* **Distraction-Free Mode:** Automatically simplifies video streaming pages to show only the player.
* **Customizable Size:** Resize the window to fit perfectly into any corner of your screen.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Quickly manage your view and settings without needing to use your mouse.
