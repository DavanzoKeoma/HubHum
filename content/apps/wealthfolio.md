### What it does in plain English
Wealthfolio is a secure personal finance application designed to keep your financial records completely private on your own device. It allows you to monitor your investments, calculate your total net worth, organize spending habits, and run future retirement simulations. Because everything is stored locally, your sensitive banking data never leaves your personal hardware unless you choose an optional encrypted sync.

### Who it's for
* **Privacy-Conscious Investors:** People who want to track their stock portfolios and asset allocation without uploading sensitive financial details to third-party cloud services.
* **Budget-Focused Families:** Households looking to categorize monthly expenses, set savings goals, and monitor overall net worth in one unified dashboard.
* **Retirement Planners:** Users planning for long-term financial independence who want to run simulations and track progress toward specific monetary targets.

### Key features
* **Complete Portfolio Tracking:** Monitor investments across diverse asset options, stocks, and short positions in multiple currencies.
* **Net Worth Overview:** Combine your investments with real estate, alternative vehicles, loans, and mortgages in a single view.
* **Spending and Budgets:** Categorize daily transactions using custom rules and set budgets to understand your cash flow.
* **Financial Planning:** Run simulations for long-term savings goals, retirement milestones, and independence targets.
* **Strict Privacy Controls:** Store all data locally on your device with optional database encryption, app lock, and separate user profiles.
