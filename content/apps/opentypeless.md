### What it does in plain English
OpenTypeless lets you speak naturally instead of typing on your keyboard. Simply press a keyboard shortcut, talk into your microphone, and watch your spoken words appear as clean, polished text in whatever program you are currently using. It also understands context to fix your grammar, rewrite paragraphs, translate languages, and answer quick questions on the fly.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and writers who want to draft articles, emails, and notes at the speed of speech.
* Programmers and remote workers who need fast, accurate text input and document editing across chat tools and code editors.
* Anyone looking to bypass slow typing speeds by using their voice for everyday desktop tasks.

### Key features
* **Anywhere Dictation:** Press a global shortcut to start recording audio and automatically type clean text directly into any active window.
* **App-Aware AI Polish:** Automatically adapts your tone and structure based on the specific software you are currently using.
* **Ask Anything Q&A:** Speak a quick voice question and receive a concise, one-shot answer inside a small floating panel.
* **Custom Vocabulary & Corrections:** Add specialized industry terms, names, and personal rules to eliminate recurring transcription errors.
* **Flexible Provider Choices:** Connect your own speech recognition and intelligence keys or use cloud services for ultimate control.
