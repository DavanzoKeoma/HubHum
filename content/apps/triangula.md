### What it does in plain English
Triangula takes any standard image and recreates it using a series of triangles or polygons. It uses a smart, iterative process to find the best way to represent your photo with geometric shapes. The result is a stylized, modern version of your original picture that looks like a hand-crafted digital mosaic.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists looking to add a geometric, low-poly aesthetic to their portfolio.
* Designers who need a quick way to create abstract backgrounds or stylized portraits from existing photography.

### Key features
* **Geometric Transformation:** Converts complex photos into clean, sharp, triangle or polygon-based artwork.
* **Customizable Detail:** Easily adjust the number of points and complexity to get the exact level of detail you want.
* **Desktop Speed:** The dedicated desktop application is significantly faster than browser-based alternatives.
* **High-Quality Output:** Generates professional-grade results suitable for digital projects or high-resolution printing.
