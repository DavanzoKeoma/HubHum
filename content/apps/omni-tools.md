### What it does in plain English
Omni-tools gives you a private collection of everyday file and text utilities that run entirely inside your web browser. Because all processing happens on your device, your documents and photos are never uploaded to any remote server. It brings together dozens of single-purpose helpers into one clean, ad-free interface that you can use instantly or host yourself.

### Who it's for
* Students and office workers who need quick file format conversions without installing heavy software.
* Developers and writers looking for fast text formatters, case converters, and data calculators.
* Privacy-conscious users who want to edit or split PDFs and images without sending files to third-party websites.

### Key features
* Client-side processing ensures complete privacy since nothing ever leaves your device.
* Wide variety of built-in utilities for images, videos, audio, text, PDFs, dates, and math.
* Completely free and ad-free experience with a clean, easy-to-use interface.
* Lightweight self-hosted option via Docker for private local or home server setups.

### How to install (Download)
You can use the official public version instantly or self-host it on your own server.
* [Open Official Web Application](https://omnitools.app)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, completely ad-free, runs locally in your browser for total privacy.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern web browser, and very large video or PDF files depend on your device memory.
