### What it does in plain English
ZapFast provides a fast, dedicated messaging window for your computer without relying on heavy web browser engines. It links directly to your mobile phone as a companion device, opening almost instantly while using a fraction of the memory. You can type messages, share media, play voice notes, and manage your everyday chats seamlessly from your desktop.

### Who it's for
* People who spend all day chatting on desktop and want a faster alternative that uses less computer memory.
* Users who prefer dedicated desktop programs over keeping chat tabs open inside a web browser.
* Anyone looking for a clean, distraction-free environment to manage multiple phone numbers in one place.

### Key features
* Opens instantly and runs efficiently with minimal memory usage compared to standard web versions.
* Handles formatted text, photos, documents, stickers, GIFs, polls, and voice messages seamlessly.
* Plays voice notes, videos, and animated media directly inside your chat history.
* Secures your chats with built-in privacy options like custom screen locks and encrypted local history storage.
* Allows you to link multiple messaging numbers and switch between them effortlessly.
