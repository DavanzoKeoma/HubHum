### What it does in plain English
Memmy connects different artificial intelligence assistants so they share one unified memory of your preferences, ongoing projects, and daily tasks. Instead of starting from scratch every time you switch programs, your context carries forward automatically. Everything stays safely stored on your own device under your full control.

### Who it's for
* Software creators and digital workers who juggle multiple artificial intelligence coding assistants.
* Privacy-conscious users who want their personal digital tools to remember context without sharing data with third-party cloud servers.
* Power users looking to streamline their daily workflows across different task assistants.

### Key features
* **Unified context:** Keeps your background history synchronized across various tools and chat interfaces.
* **Local privacy:** Stores all memory records directly on your machine with complete ownership.
* **Tool switching:** Move seamlessly from one coding assistant or companion app to another without losing progress.
* **Flexible setup:** Works smoothly as a desktop application or as a command-line utility for advanced configurations.
