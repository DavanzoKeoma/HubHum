### What it does in plain English
Moji is a fast desktop application designed for reading, writing, and organizing Markdown text files. It lets you open notes or documentation with instant previews, clean diagrams, and smooth multi-tab navigation. You can also export your work into portable formats like PDF, HTML, or images with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Writers and bloggers drafting long-form content or notes.
* Software developers reviewing documentation and diagrams.
* Students organizing notes and study materials locally.

### Key features
* Multi-document workspace with tab management and unsaved change protection.
* Live split-view editing with synchronized scrolling between code and preview.
* Support for Mermaid diagrams, math formulas, and code syntax highlighting.
* Automatic recovery for untitled drafts so you never lose your work.
* Export active documents to HTML, PDF, or PNG formats easily.
