### What it does in plain English
AWX acts as a central command center that allows you to organize and run repetitive computer tasks through a simple web page. It helps teams coordinate their work by providing a clear view of what is happening across their digital infrastructure. ### Who it's for
* System administrators who need to manage multiple servers from one location.
* IT professionals looking to standardize and track their team's automated processes.

### Key features
* **Visual Dashboard:** Monitor the status of all your automated tasks in real-time.
* **Centralized Management:** Keep all your automation scripts in one secure, accessible place.
* **Task Scheduling:** Set up recurring jobs to run automatically at specific times.
* **Creative Integration:** Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
