### What it does in plain English
So Novel is a versatile utility designed to capture web content and structure it neatly into standard electronic book files. It allows you to collect reading materials from online sources and export them into popular formats like EPUB, TXT, or PDF. Whether you prefer a simple text interface, a command line tool, or a web dashboard, it adapts to your preferred way of working.

### Who it's for
* Avid readers who want to build personal libraries of their favorite serialized web stories.
* Book lovers who need to convert messy web pages into clean, readable formats for their e-reader devices.
* Users looking for flexible tools to extract and archive web content effortlessly.

### Key features
* Multiple export options including EPUB, TXT, and PDF formats.
* Choose between a terminal interface, a command-line utility, or a web-based dashboard.
* Flexible installation methods including Docker containers and package managers.
* Custom configuration support to tailor rules and settings to your reading preferences.
