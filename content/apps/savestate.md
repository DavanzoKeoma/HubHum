### What it does in plain English
SaveState is a free application that watches over your video game progress so you never lose hours of gameplay. You simply drop a game shortcut into the window, and the program instantly locates where your game saves are hidden on your computer. With a single click, it compresses and stores your save files safely, allowing you to restore them whenever needed or sync them to cloud storage.

### Who it's for
* PC and Linux gamers who play games without built-in cloud backup features.
* Retro gaming enthusiasts who manage saves across dozens of different console emulators.
* Players who want a foolproof way to move game progress between multiple computers.

### Key features
* **One-Click Backups:** Quickly save and restore game progress compressed neatly into standard archive files.
* **Drag and Drop Setup:** Simply drop a game shortcut from your desktop or launcher to set up a new tracking profile instantly.
* **Smart Detection:** Automatically discovers save folders for major platforms like Steam, Epic, GOG, and dozens of emulators.
* **Optional Cloud Sync:** Keep your backups synchronized across Google Drive, network storage folders, or private Git project pages.
* **No Admin Rights Needed:** Runs safely out of the box without requiring administrator privileges or complex installation wizards.
