### What it does in plain English
Capacitor acts as a bridge that lets you run websites and web-based tools as if they were native apps on your phone. Instead of writing separate code for every device, you can use your existing web skills to create one project that works everywhere. It handles the connection between your web content and the specific features of your phone, such as the camera or GPS.

### Who it's for
* Web designers and creators who want to share their work as mobile apps.
* Small business owners who want to offer a mobile experience without hiring separate teams for iPhone and Android.

### Key features
* **Single Codebase:** Write your app once and run it on both iOS and Android.
* **Native Access:** Easily connect your web app to phone hardware like cameras, microphones, and location services.
* **Flexible Design:** Works with any modern web project, so you aren't locked into a specific way of building your site.
* **App Store Ready:** Packages your content so it can be submitted to official mobile app stores.

### How to install (Download)
Capacitor is a professional tool for creators. To get started, visit the [Official Capacitor Documentation](https://capacitorjs.com/docs) for step-by-step setup instructions.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, saves significant time by reusing web code, and supports a wide range of phone features.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of how to manage web projects on a computer; does not provide a visual "drag-and-drop" interface for beginners.
