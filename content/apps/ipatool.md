### What it does in plain English
This software acts as a direct link to Apple's digital storefronts right from your command line. It lets you search for mobile, tablet, and desktop software across Apple's extensive catalog. Once found, you can easily pull the installation files directly onto your local machine.

### Who it's for
- Software testers who need to archive specific application versions for compatibility checks.
- Developers looking to manage and verify their digital purchases or inspect package metadata.
- Power users who prefer text-based commands to browse and collect digital software packages.

### Key features
- Search for applications across multiple Apple operating systems from one unified interface.
- Download standard installation packages securely using your existing verified account credentials.
- View and verify available software versions alongside detailed metadata.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, supports automation scripts, and provides quick command-line access to app packages.
* **Cons:** Requires a valid personal Apple account with prior store purchases, and advanced commands take a few minutes to learn.
