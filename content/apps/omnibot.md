### What it does in plain English
OmniBot is an on-device digital assistant built for your phone that combines chat, smart tool execution, and local file organization into one single application. Instead of just answering questions in a text window, it actively understands your requests, makes decisions, and performs actions across your device. It connects with your calendar, notifications, terminal, and browser to automate everyday chores right from your pocket.

### Who it's for
* Android users looking for an advanced, customizable digital assistant that runs locally on their mobile device.
* Power users who want to automate repetitive mobile tasks, schedule reminders, and interact with terminal commands on the go.
* Anyone interested in exploring on-device artificial intelligence agents with local file and workspace management.

### Key features
* **Extensible skill ecosystem:** Connect browser access, specialized skills, and system-level tools to expand what your assistant can do.
* **System-level actions:** Schedule tasks, manage alarms, query or update your calendar, and control audio playback effortlessly.
* **Personal context integration:** Access authorized calendars, contacts, notifications, and location data securely without requiring a rooted device.
* **Built-in workspace and terminal:** Read and write local files, browse your workspace folders, and interact with an integrated terminal environment.
* **Smart memory system:** Maintain both short-term and long-term memory to keep your conversations relevant and context-aware over time.
