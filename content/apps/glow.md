### What it does in plain English
Glow transforms plain text markdown files into gorgeously styled documents that you can read seamlessly inside your terminal. It automatically discovers documentation and notes tucked away in your folders or code projects. ### Who it's for
* Software developers and system administrators who spend their day inside terminal windows and need to read project manuals.
* Writers and technical authors who want to preview notes and guides without opening heavy document processing software.

### Key features
* Renders markdown files with rich colors, lists, and formatting right in your console window.
* Automatically searches through local directories and version control project pages to find your notes.
* Lets you customize styles, text wrapping, and line numbers to match your reading preferences.
