### What it does in plain English
RemoveMacAI lets you turn off built-in artificial intelligence features on your Mac and delete their heavy background data files. It gives you a clear preview of every change before it happens so you always stay in control. You can also quiet annoying pop-ups, stop hidden background trackers, and safely restore gigabytes of disk space.

### Who it's for
* Mac owners who want to reclaim precious disk space taken up by unused artificial intelligence files.
* Privacy-conscious users looking to minimize background data collection and system analytics.
* Power users who prefer a native application or terminal commands to streamline and debloat macOS.

### Key features
* Safely uninstalls Apple Intelligence models and blocks them from downloading again.
* Turns off telemetry, personalized ads, and distracting notification pop-ups.
* Cleans out old system updates, application caches, and unused language assets.
* Fully reversible changes with a built-in journal that restores your previous settings.
* Available as both a user-friendly desktop program and a command-line utility.
