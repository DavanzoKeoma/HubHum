### What it does in plain English
Parabolic helps you save videos and music from the web.  
It works with many sites, so you don't need separate tools.  
You can pick the file type you want.

### Who it's for
- People who want to keep videos for offline viewing.  
- Hobbyists who collect music or tutorials.  
- Anyone who uses the internet and wants quick downloads.

### Key features
- Supports hundreds of sites.  
- Download in MP4, MP3, WebM, Opus, FLAC, and WAV.  
- Start several downloads at the same time.  
- Keeps subtitles and other useful information.
