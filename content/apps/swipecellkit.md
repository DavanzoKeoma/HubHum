### What it does in plain English
SwipeCellKit lets you add swipe‑to‑delete or other actions to table and collection views.  
It works like the Mail app on iPhone, showing buttons when you swipe left or right.  
You can customize the buttons, transitions, and haptic feedback to match your app’s look.

### Who it's for
- iOS developers building list interfaces.  
- App makers who want quick actions without writing a lot of code.  
- Teams needing consistent swipe behavior across multiple screens.

### Key features
- Swipe left or right to reveal action buttons.  
- Buttons can show text, an image, or both.  
- Three built‑in transition styles: Border, Drag, and Reveal.  
- Customizable expansion when the swipe passes a threshold.  
- Works with both UITableView and UICollectionView.

### How to install (Download)
- [Download from CocoaPods](https://cocoapods.org/pods/SwipeCellKit) – the recommended way.  
- [View on GitHub](https://github.com/SwipeCellKit/SwipeCellKit) – source code and documentation.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, MIT license, no subscription.  
* **Cons:** requires Swift 5.0 and Xcode 10.3+, needs integration via CocoaPods or Swift Package Manager, learning curve for custom transitions.
