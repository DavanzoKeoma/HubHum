### What it does in plain English
This project provides a massive, automatically updated collection of network routing rules designed to organize how your web traffic travels. It sorts websites and services into direct connections, proxy tunnels, and blocked unwanted ad domains to streamline your internet experience. The rules are structured as lightweight modular files that seamlessly plug into your existing network setup without messing up your personal settings.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who want automatic, hassle-free traffic splitting between local connections and proxy tunnels.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to automatically block thousands of unwanted tracking domains and advertisements.
* Advanced users seeking modular configuration files to keep their network setups clean, concise, and easy to tweak.

### Key features
* **Daily Automated Updates:** Fresh rules are built and refreshed daily to capture new domains and changes.
* **Massive Coverage:** Includes over one hundred thousand rejection entries, tens of thousands of proxy targets, and direct connection rules.
* **Modular Design:** Use individual plug-and-play files instead of rewriting your entire setup from scratch.
* **Custom Routing:** Easily switch between whitelist and blacklist modes depending on your browsing habits.
* **Open Sources:** Aggregates trusted community lists into a single, cohesive, ready-to-use format.
