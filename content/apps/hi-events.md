### What it does in plain English
Hi.Events provides a complete system for selling tickets and organizing everything from small workshops to large festivals. It allows you to keep your own customer data and branding instead of relying on third-party platforms that charge high fees. You can either use their managed cloud service or host the software on your own server for total independence.

### Who it's for
*   **Event Organizers:** People running concerts, conferences, or club nights who want to avoid high service fees.
*   **Small Businesses:** Venues and community groups that need a professional, branded way to manage registrations and attendee lists.

### Key features
*   **Flexible Ticketing:** Create free, paid, or donation-based tickets with custom promo codes and tiered pricing.
*   **Complete Branding:** Design your own event pages and ticket widgets that match your organization's look and feel.
*   **Attendee Management:** Easily check guests in at the door using a phone camera or scanner and send automated updates.
*   **Data Ownership:** Keep your attendee information private and export it whenever you need to for your own records.
*   **Global Support:** The platform is available in over 20 languages to help you reach audiences worldwide.

### How to install (Download)
*   **Use the Managed Cloud Version:** [Get started on Hi.Events Cloud](https://app.hi.events/auth/register?utm_source=gh-readme)
*   **Self-Host (Advanced):** Follow the [Full Installation Guide](https://hi.events/docs/getting-started?utm_source=gh-readme) to set it up on your own infrastructure.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** No per-ticket fees, full control over your customer data, and highly customizable branding.
*   **Cons:** Self-hosting requires some technical knowledge to set up and maintain the server environment.
