### What it does in plain English
Pluely is a lightweight desktop assistant that listens to conversations or lets you ask questions via a discreet overlay on your screen. It transcribes audio live, generates helpful follow-up responses, and stays completely hidden during video calls and screen recordings. Your conversations and data remain entirely private on your own device.

### Who it's for
* Job seekers preparing for live technical interviews and needing quick reference help.
* Sales professionals who want real-time conversation prompts and automated transcripts during client calls.
* Developers and remote workers needing instant answers to tricky on-screen questions without breaking their workflow.

### Key features
* **Invisible overlay:** Completely hidden from video calls, screenshots, and screen shares so no meeting bot ever joins your call.
* **Live transcription:** Transcribes microphone and system audio in real time with helpful speaker labels.
* **Instant answers:** Stream answers and follow-up suggestions directly onto your screen using global keyboard shortcuts.
* **Local privacy:** Keeps your chats, transcripts, and documents securely saved in a local database on your computer.
* **Flexible models:** Use hundreds of hosted models or connect your own provider keys and local tools.
