### What it does in plain English
Mangayomi is a single, free application that gathers your favorite manga, novels, and anime series in one place. It allows you to read and watch content directly within the app, even when you are offline. You can also link your account to popular tracking sites to automatically update your progress and keep your library tidy.

### Who it's for
* **Manga and Anime Fans:** Readers who want a unified, ad-free experience for their favorite series without switching between multiple websites or apps.
* **Offline Readers:** People who want to download chapters or episodes to enjoy during commutes, flights, or in areas with poor internet connection.
* **Library Organizers:** Users who want to track their progress on services like MyAnimeList or AniList directly from their reading app.

### Key features
* **Multi-Format Support:** Read manga, webtoons, comics, and novels, or watch anime and movies in a single interface.
* **Offline Access:** Download content to your device to read or watch without an internet connection.
* **Customizable Reader:** Choose from different viewing modes, reading directions, and themes (light or dark) to suit your preference.
* **Tracker Integration:** Connect to MyAnimeList, AniList, SIMKL, Trakt, and Kitsu to sync your progress automatically.
* **Library Organization:** Use categories to sort your content and create local backups to protect your reading history.
