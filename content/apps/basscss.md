### Who it's for
* Web designers who want to prototype new layouts directly in the browser without writing heavy custom code.
* Frontend developers looking for a stable, lightweight styling foundation that stays out of their way.
* Creators building clean, responsive websites who want to avoid bloated stylesheets and complicated setup steps.
