### What it does in plain English
slack-term brings workplace messaging right into your text-only terminal window. It lets you read messages, switch between channels, and reply to coworkers using simple keyboard shortcuts. This tool is designed to keep you focused on your work without the distraction of a full desktop application.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who spend their entire day working inside terminal windows
* System administrators managing servers who need quick communication access
* Keyboard enthusiasts who prefer text-based interfaces over mouse-heavy applications

### Key features
* Lightweight text-based interface that consumes minimal computer resources
* Custom keyboard shortcuts for navigating channels, reading, and sending text
* Search functionality to quickly find previous messages or specific keywords
* Thread support to keep conversations organized and easy to follow

### How to install (Download)
To get started, [Download (slack-term-darwin-amd64)](https://github.com/jpbruinsslot/slack-term/releases/download/v0.5.0/slack-term-darwin-amd64) for macOS or [Download (slack-term-linux-amd64)](https://github.com/jpbruinsslot/slack-term/releases/download/v0.5.0/slack-term-linux-amd64) for Linux. Place the downloaded file into a directory accessible by your command line, such as `/usr/local/bin`, and configure your account token to start chatting.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely fast and lightweight, highly customizable key bindings, keeps you away from resource-heavy apps
* **Cons:** Requires familiarity with command-line tools, initial setup requires generating a custom access token
