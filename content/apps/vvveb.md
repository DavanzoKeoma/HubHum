### What it does in plain English
Vvveb helps you put together a professional‑looking website, a blog, or an online store without writing code. You simply drag the pieces you want onto the page and see the result instantly. It also takes care of things like images, product details and multilingual support for you.

### Who it's for
- Small business owners who need an online shop.  
- Hobby bloggers who want a simple, attractive blog.  
- Local groups or clubs that need a quick, easy website.

### Key features
- Drag‑and‑drop editor that shows changes right away.  
- Ready‑made themes and templates you can apply with one click.  
- Built‑in tools for handling images, products and multilingual text.  
- Automatic backups and easy import/export for moving your site.  
- Fast performance that works even on low‑cost shared hosting.

### How to install (Download)
1. Download the latest package: [Download latest version](https://vvveb.com/download.php)  
2. Upload the files to your web server or local host.  
3. Open the site in a browser (e.g., `http://localhost/` or `http://myserver.com/`) and follow the on‑screen guide.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, no subscription, works on cheap shared hosting.  
* **Cons:** requires a web server with PHP, initial setup may need a few extra steps for complete beginners.
