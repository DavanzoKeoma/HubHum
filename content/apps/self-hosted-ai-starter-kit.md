### What it does in plain English
The Self-hosted AI Starter Kit lets you set up your own private artificial intelligence workspace right on your computer. It combines a user-friendly visual automation platform with local language models and secure data storage. This ensures your sensitive documents and files never leave your device while you build custom intelligent assistants.

### Who it's for
* **Privacy-Conscious Developers:** Professionals who want to experiment with artificial intelligence without sending private data to third-party cloud servers.
* **Small Business Teams:** Groups looking to build custom internal chatbots and document summarizers without incurring monthly subscription fees.
* **Automation Enthusiasts:** Makers eager to connect visual workflow builders with local language models for personal projects.

### Key features
* **Complete Local Stack:** Combines a visual workflow automation platform, language model runner, vector database, and database engine out of the box.
* **Zero Data Leaks:** Keeps all conversations, text parsing, and file searches strictly on your own hardware.
* **Pre-Built Automation Templates:** Includes starter flows for chatting with documents, summarizing files, and building smart assistants.
* **Hardware Flexible:** Supports standard central processors as well as accelerated graphics hardware for faster performance.

### How to install (Download)
To set up your local environment, ensure you have Docker installed on your system. Then, visit the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/n8n-io/self-hosted-ai-starter-kit) page to clone the project files and start the containers using your terminal. For more details on the automation platform, check out [n8n Website](https://n8n.io).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely open-source, keeps all data private on your own device, and includes ready-to-use automation templates.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with command-line tools and initial model downloads can take a few minutes depending on your internet connection.
