### What it does in plain English
Documenso allows you to send, sign, and manage important documents directly from your web browser. It removes the need to pay monthly fees to large commercial platforms for basic signing tasks. By keeping the software open, it ensures your documents and personal information remain private and under your control.

### Who it's for
*   **Small business owners** who need to sign contracts and invoices without paying per-document fees.
*   **Privacy-conscious individuals** who want to manage legal documents without sharing data with unknown third parties.
*   **Teams and organizations** looking for a transparent, self-managed solution for internal document workflows.

### Key features
*   **Secure Digital Signatures:** Create legally binding signatures for PDFs and other document types with ease.
*   **Full Data Ownership:** You can host the software on your own servers, ensuring your files never leave your control.
*   **Simple Interface:** A clean, user-friendly design that makes sending and signing documents straightforward for non-technical users.
*   **Team Collaboration:** Invite multiple people to sign the same document and track who has completed their part.
*   **Open Source Code:** The entire system is available for review, allowing experts to verify its security and reliability.

### How to install (Download)
Since Documenso is a web application, you do not download a file to your computer. Instead, you can start using it immediately by visiting the official website.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Free to use for individuals, no per-page fees, high level of privacy, and fully customizable if you choose to host it yourself.
*   **Cons:** Setting up your own private version requires some technical knowledge of server management, and the free online version may have usage limits compared to paid enterprise plans.
