### What it does in plain English
This application brings popular artificial intelligence chat tools directly to your computer desktop as a standalone program. Instead of keeping a browser tab open, you can launch a dedicated window to brainstorm ideas, draft emails, or write code. It acts as a clean, streamlined wrapper that keeps your conversations organized and easily accessible.

### Who it's for
* Writers and content creators looking for instant brainstorming and editing feedback.
* Programmers needing quick answers and code snippets while working.
* Everyday users who want a convenient desktop shortcut for quick questions and answers.

### Key features
* Runs natively as a desktop window on Windows, Mac, and Linux computers.
* Keeps your workspace clutter-free by separating chat tools from your internet browser tabs.
* Provides quick keyboard shortcut access to start new conversations instantly.
* Offers a lightweight and distraction-free interface focused entirely on text interaction.
