### What it does in plain English
Pangolin lets you securely connect to your private servers, databases, and internal web tools from anywhere without opening risky doors to the public internet. Instead of using traditional, wide-open network tools, it gives each person access only to the specific items their role requires. Every sign-in and connection request is checked against strict identity rules and safely recorded for review.

### Who it's for
* System administrators who want to manage internal infrastructure without traditional, complicated virtual private networks.
* Remote team members who need simple, browser-based access to company tools and development environments.
* Security-conscious organizations looking to monitor who accesses sensitive databases and coding assistants.

### Key features
* **Instant browser access**: Reach web panels, terminals, and remote desktops directly inside your web browser with a simple link.
* **Smart traffic routing**: Punches through restrictive firewalls automatically so you can connect to private networks without public IP addresses.
* **Identity-aware AI gateway**: Route requests to coding assistants and self-hosted models under strict budget and role controls.
* **Role-based permissions**: Grant access to specific resources rather than opening up entire network ranges to a single login.

* **Cons:** Advanced enterprise configurations and identity provider integrations require initial technical setup time.
