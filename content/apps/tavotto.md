### What it does in plain English
Tavotto is a free desktop tool designed to bridge the gap between raw programmatic plots and final journal submissions. It opens existing Python script figures, allowing you to visually tweak titles, legends, curves, and page layouts on an interactive canvas. Your original code remains untouched while all adjustments are saved separately and validated against strict journal guidelines before export.

### Who it's for
* **Academic researchers** preparing publication-quality multi-panel plates for scientific journals.
* **Data scientists** who want to refine generated charts visually without rewriting plotting code.
* **Students** looking to streamline the formatting of academic reports and thesis figures.

### Key features
* **Visual Canvas Editing:** Double-click any panel to move elements, adjust font sizes, or change colors instantly.
* **Multi-Panel Layouts:** Arrange and snap figures onto precise millimetre-measured pages with automated panel labeling.
* **Preflight Journal Checks:** Automatically scan your layout for common formatting pitfalls like low resolution or undersized text.
* **Code Preservation:** Keep your automation scripts intact while storing visual overrides separately.
* **Flexible Export Options:** Output publication-ready vector PDFs, high-resolution PNGs, or SVGs effortlessly.
