### What it does in plain English
Outline provides the tools to build your own private internet connection, giving you full control over your online traffic. You can use the Manager app to set up a server on your own terms and the Client app to connect to it from any device. It is designed to help you maintain access to an open internet while keeping your connection private.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want to manage their own private server for secure browsing.
* People who need to share a reliable and private internet connection with friends, family, or colleagues.

### Key features
* **Simple Setup:** The Manager app guides you through the process of creating your own server without needing advanced technical skills.
* **Cross-Platform:** Connect to your server from Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, or iOS devices.
* **Full Control:** You decide who has access to your server, making it easy to manage your own private network.
* **Open Standards:** The software is built on secure, well-tested technology that ensures your connection remains private.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, puts you in control of your own server, and works across all major devices.
* **Cons:** Requires you to have or rent a server (hosting) to function, which may involve a small monthly cost from a third-party provider.
