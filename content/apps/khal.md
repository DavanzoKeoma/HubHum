### What it does in plain English
Khal is a text-based calendar program designed for users who prefer working within their computer's terminal. It allows you to quickly view, add, and edit your events without needing to open a heavy graphical application. By connecting it with other synchronization tools, you can keep your calendar updated across multiple devices and services.

### Who it's for
This tool is perfect for power users, developers, and system administrators who spend most of their time in a terminal environment. It is also ideal for anyone who prefers minimalist, keyboard-driven software over traditional mouse-heavy calendar apps.

### Key features
*   **Keyboard-focused:** Manage your entire schedule using simple keyboard shortcuts.
*   **Interactive mode:** Includes an interactive interface for browsing and editing events easily.
*   **Fast entry:** Quickly add new appointments without navigating through complex menus.
*   **Flexible syncing:** Works with standard calendar formats to keep your data in sync with other services.
*   **Lightweight:** Uses minimal system resources compared to standard calendar software.

### How to install (Download)
Khal is available through most standard package managers on Linux and macOS. You can find specific instructions for your system on the [Official Installation Guide](https://khal.readthedocs.io/en/latest/install.html).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, very fast, and highly efficient for keyboard users.
*   **Cons:** Not available for Windows, requires some comfort with text-based interfaces, and does not currently support editing event timezones.
