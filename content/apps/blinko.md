### What it does in plain English
Blinko helps you quickly jot down random thoughts, links, and reminders the moment they come to mind. It securely stores everything on your own equipment so your private notes never leak to third-party companies. Plus, built-in artificial intelligence lets you search through your past notes simply by asking questions in everyday language.

### Who it's for
* Writers, researchers, and students who need a reliable digital notebook for random brainstorms.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer keeping their personal journals and notes on their own hardware.
* Anyone tired of messy folders who wants a lightning-fast search system for their thoughts.

### Key features
* **Smart AI Search:** Find any past note quickly by typing natural questions instead of exact keywords.
* **Complete Privacy Control:** Keep all your information safely stored on your own self-hosted setup.
* **Instant Note Capture:** Jot down ideas in a flash with full formatting support for clean presentation.
* **Cross-Device Access:** Use the app seamlessly across your computer, phone, and tablet.
