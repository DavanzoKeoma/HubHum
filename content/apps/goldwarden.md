### What it does in plain English
Goldwarden is a specialized tool designed to work alongside your existing password vault to provide advanced features that aren't available in standard versions. It focuses on high-level security by keeping your sensitive information encrypted in your computer's memory and only decrypting it when absolutely necessary. It also helps you work faster by automating logins and providing secure ways to sign into your accounts.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want more control over their password vault security.
* Professionals who need advanced features like SSH agent integration and biometric authentication for their daily workflow.

### Key features
* **Biometric Unlock:** Use your fingerprint or face recognition to access your vault.
* **System-Wide Auto-Type:** Automatically fill in your credentials across different applications and windows.
* **Enhanced Memory Security:** Keeps your keys protected at a deep system level to prevent unauthorized access.
* **SSH Integration:** Simplifies secure connections and code signing using your vault credentials.
* **Passwordless Login:** Allows you to approve logins and access your vault without typing a master password every time.
