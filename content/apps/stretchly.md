### What it does in plain English
Stretchly is a quiet helper that runs in the background of your computer to remind you when it is time to step away. It triggers short mini breaks and longer resting intervals throughout your day so you can stretch your legs and rest your eyes. You can easily adjust how often the breaks happen, skip them if you are busy, or customize the break ideas with your own routines.

### Who it's for
* Office workers and programmers who spend long hours sitting in front of a computer screen.
* Remote professionals who want to maintain healthy work habits and prevent physical fatigue.
* Students and writers looking for a gentle nudge to move around and stay balanced during study marathons.

### Key features
* Customizable schedules for short rest intervals and longer breaks.
* Quiet background operation with non-intrusive reminder windows and break ideas.
* Automatic idle detection that pauses reminders when you step away from your keyboard.
* Do Not Disturb integration to respect your focus time and meetings.
* Light and dark mode themes that match your operating system preferences.
