### What it does in plain English
This tool removes restrictions from music files downloaded from major streaming services, allowing you to play them on any device. It converts locked files into standard formats like MP3 or FLAC while keeping your song titles and album art intact. Everything happens directly on your own computer, ensuring your files remain private and never leave your machine.

### Who it's for
* Music collectors who want to play their purchased tracks on offline MP3 players or car stereos.
* Users who need to move their music library between different devices or editing software without format errors.

### Key features
* **Drag-and-Drop Interface:** Simply drag files or entire folders into your browser to start the conversion process.
* **Privacy First:** The tool runs locally on your computer, meaning your music files are never uploaded to a server.
* **Batch Processing:** Handle large collections at once with automatic organization and naming.
* **Format Flexibility:** Choose to keep the original quality or convert to common formats like MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV, or OGG.
* **Metadata Preservation:** Automatically keeps your song information, artist names, and album covers attached to the converted files.
