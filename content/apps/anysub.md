### What it does in plain English
Anysub takes your video and audio files and automatically generates synchronized subtitles using advanced speech recognition technology. You can review the results inside a web browser using an intuitive waveform timeline editor to fix timing or text errors. Once you are satisfied with the edits, you can easily export your completed subtitles in various standard file formats.

### Who it's for
* **Content Creators:** YouTubers and podcasters looking to generate accurate captions for their video and audio publications.
* **Video Editors:** Professionals who need a reliable, self-hosted workspace to quickly clean up and time subtitle files.
* **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Individuals who want complete control over their media files by running transcription entirely on their own hardware.

### Key features
* **AI-Powered Transcription:** Utilizes versatile speech recognition models ranging from lightweight options to high-accuracy engines.
* **Interactive Waveform Editor:** Fine-tune timing and text with visual waveform cues, autosave protection, and undo support.
* **Multiple Export Options:** Save your finished work as standard SRT, WebVTT, LRC, plain text, or structured JSON files.
* **Built-in Translation:** Translate transcripts into other languages directly on your server or via external chat models.
* **Smart Quality Warnings:** Automatically detects and flags subtitles that run too fast, exceed length limits, or overlap.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete data privacy on your own server, highly customizable recognition models, and a robust visual editor.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern Linux environment with container management experience, and local processing benefits greatly from a compatible graphics card.
