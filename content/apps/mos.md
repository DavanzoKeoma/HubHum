### What it does in plain English
Standard computer mice often feel choppy and rigid when used on a Mac because they lack the precise tracking built into trackpads. Mos solves this by intercepting your mouse movements and instantly converting them into smooth, continuous motion. It also gives you total freedom to reverse scroll directions, assign custom shortcuts to mouse buttons, and adjust settings for individual apps.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who prefer using a traditional physical mouse instead of the built-in trackpad.
* Professionals and creators who want to eliminate jerky scrolling and customize mouse button actions for specific programs.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, free tool to fine-tune their daily desktop navigation workflow.

### Key features
* **Smooth Scrolling:** Transforms ordinary wheel movement into a fluid experience with adjustable speed, step size, and duration.
* **Independent Axes:** Configure vertical and horizontal scrolling settings separately to fit your exact preference.
* **Per-App Settings:** Customize rules globally or apply unique scrolling behaviors and button bindings for specific applications.
* **Button & Key Remapping:** Assign keyboard shortcuts, system controls, or scripts to your extra mouse buttons.
* **Menu Bar Convenience:** Runs quietly in the background right from your menu bar without cluttering your workspace.

### How to install (Download)
You can download the latest version directly from the [GitHub Releases Page](https://github.com/Caldis/Mos/releases) or visit the [Official Mos Website](https://mos.caldis.me). 

If you prefer using the Homebrew package manager, you can install it by running:
```bash
brew install --cask mos
```
*(Note: Upon first launch, macOS will request accessibility permissions so the app can read and smooth your scroll events.)*

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, lightweight resource usage, highly customizable per application.
* **Cons:** Requires one-time accessibility permission setup in system settings; designed exclusively for macOS.
