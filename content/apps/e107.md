### What it does in plain English
e107 is a content management system that lets you build and maintain your own website or blog through a simple web interface. It handles the technical heavy lifting, allowing you to focus entirely on writing and organizing your content. You can easily create professional-looking sites, intranets, or community hubs without ever needing to touch a line of computer code.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who need a professional website without hiring a developer.
* Hobbyists and bloggers who want a reliable, self-hosted space for their content.
* Community managers looking to build a private or public online discussion hub.

### Key features
* **User-Friendly Interface:** Manage your site content through an intuitive dashboard.
* **Flexible Design:** Use themes to change the look and feel of your site quickly.
* **Community Tools:** Includes built-in features for user accounts, forums, and private messaging.
* **Self-Hosted Control:** Keep full ownership of your data by hosting the software on your own web server.
* **Extensible:** Add extra functionality to your site using a variety of community-made plugins.

### How to install (Download)
1. Visit the [Official e107 Download Page](https://e107.org/download) to get the latest stable version.
2. Upload the files to your web server's public directory.
3. Open your website in a web browser and follow the on-screen setup wizard to connect your database and finish the installation.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly customizable, and does not require coding knowledge.
* **Cons:** Requires your own web hosting and database setup, which may be challenging for absolute beginners.
