### What it does in plain English
YPImagePicker is a ready-to-use component for mobile applications that brings familiar media selection features straight to your users. It gives app developers a straightforward way to let people capture new photos, record video clips, browse device libraries, and apply filters. The entire system integrates cleanly into native workflows without requiring complex custom coding from scratch.

### Who it's for
* Mobile application creators looking to add fast photo and video uploading.
* Software developers building social or media-sharing apps on Apple devices.
* Creators wanting a polished, modern image selection interface for their projects.

### Key features
* **All-in-One Media Browsing:** Lets users pick items from their device library or capture new pictures and videos directly.
* **Built-in Editing Tools:** Includes cropping, rotating, and video trimming capabilities right out of the box.
* **Customizable Filters:** Apply visual adjustments to photos before finalizing selections.
* **Flexible Multi-Select:** Allow users to choose single items or batch-select multiple photos and videos simultaneously.
* **Pure Swift Construction:** Built natively in Swift for optimal performance and smooth integration into modern mobile codebases.
