### What it does in plain English
Caesium is a simple tool that shrinks the file size of your images while keeping them looking sharp and clear. It is perfect for when you need to send photos via email or save space on your computer without sacrificing visual quality. You can process multiple images at once, making it quick and easy to organize your digital photo collection.

### Who it's for
* **Casual photographers** who want to share photos online without hitting file size limits.
* **Office workers** who need to attach images to emails without clogging up the recipient's inbox.

### Key features
* **Smart Compression:** Reduces file size significantly while maintaining the original look of your photos.
* **Batch Processing:** Allows you to compress hundreds of images at the same time with just a few clicks.
* **Format Support:** Works with popular image types including JPG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF.
* **Customizable Settings:** Gives you the choice to adjust resolution and compression levels to suit your specific needs.
* **Privacy Focused:** Your images are processed directly on your computer, so your private photos never leave your device.
