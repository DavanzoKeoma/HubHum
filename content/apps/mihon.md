### What it does in plain English
Mihon is a clean, free application designed for Android devices that brings all your favorite graphic novels and comic series into one convenient place. It lets you organize your digital collection cleanly into personalized categories and read smoothly without interruptions. You can also track your reading progress across popular tracking platforms automatically.

### Who it's for
* Comic and manga enthusiasts who want a clean reading experience on their Android phones or tablets.
* Readers who like to organize huge offline libraries into custom categories and shelves.
* Fans who want to keep track of every chapter they finish across different online tracking services.

### Key features
* Configurable reading layout with multiple viewing modes and reading directions.
* Custom categories to sort and organize your personal digital library easily.
* Integration with popular reading trackers to keep your progress updated automatically.
* Local backup creation so you can read your collection completely offline anytime.
* Clean light and dark visual themes to match your personal viewing comfort.
