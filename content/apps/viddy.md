### What it does in plain English
Viddy runs a command on your computer over and over again, showing you the results in a clear, colorful window. Unlike standard tools, it records every change so you can scroll back in time to see exactly what happened a few seconds ago. It turns a simple list of text into an interactive video-like experience that makes spotting errors or updates much easier.

### Who it's for
*   **System Administrators:** People who need to keep an eye on server logs or system status without constantly refreshing the screen.
*   **Developers:** Individuals who want to see how their code changes affect output in real-time, with the ability to review previous states.
*   **Curious Learners:** Users who want to understand how data changes over time by visually tracking the differences between updates.

### Key features
*   **Time Machine Mode:** Rewind through the history of command outputs like a video player to inspect past states.
*   **Visual Diff Highlighting:** Changes in the text are highlighted in color, making it easy to see exactly what has changed since the last update.
*   **Interactive Pager:** Scroll through long outputs line by line or page by page using familiar keyboard shortcuts.
*   **Search and Filter:** Quickly find specific words or patterns within the command output to focus on relevant information.
*   **Customizable Interface:** Adjust colors, keybindings, and behavior to match your personal preferences and workflow.
