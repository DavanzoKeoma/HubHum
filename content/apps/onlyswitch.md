### What it does in plain English
OnlySwitch puts everyday Mac controls—like hiding desktop icons, keeping your computer awake, and managing system dark mode—into a single convenient menu bar panel. You can easily add, remove, and rearrange switches, trigger custom shortcuts with hotkeys, or place helpful widgets right on your desktop. It also lets you control your Mac remotely from an iPhone or iPad over your local Wi-Fi network.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who want quick, one-click access to hidden system settings and everyday controls.
* Power users looking to organize their menu bar and automate routine desktop tasks.
* Anyone who wants to manage music playback, sound volume, and computer performance without digging through deep menus.

### Key features
* Customizable menu bar panel packed with dozens of built-in switches and toggles.
* Sound Mixer to adjust volume, mute states, and audio routing for individual apps.
* Schedules tool to automate recurring desktop actions and system scripts automatically.
* Remote control support via companion apps to operate your Mac from mobile devices.
* Desktop widget integration for fast access to your favorite controls and monitors.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, highly customizable, lightweight footprint on your system.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS permissions for advanced window and screen features, some toggles rely on specific operating system versions.
