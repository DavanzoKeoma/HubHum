### What it does in plain English
Grex helps you create complex text-matching patterns, often called regular expressions, without needing to write them from scratch. You simply provide a list of sample text strings, and the tool generates a pattern that matches those examples. It is a helpful assistant for anyone who needs to find or filter specific data but finds the manual creation of these patterns difficult.

### Who it's for
* Data analysts who need to extract specific information from large text files.
* Developers or power users who want to save time when creating search patterns for their projects.

### Key features
* **Automatic Generation:** Instantly turns a list of text examples into a functional search pattern.
* **Customizable Matching:** Offers options to detect digits, spaces, words, and repeated patterns automatically.
* **Readable Output:** Includes a "verbose" mode that formats the generated pattern to make it easier for humans to read.
* **Unicode Support:** Fully handles modern character sets, including complex symbols and emojis.
* **Flexible Input:** Can read your test cases directly from your typing or from a saved text file.

* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, works offline, and significantly speeds up pattern creation.
* **Cons:** The generated patterns may sometimes be longer than those written by an expert, and it requires basic familiarity with command-line usage.
