### What it does in plain English
SmartTube lets you watch online video content directly on your living room television using a clean interface built specifically for TV remotes. It automatically skips sponsored segments during videos and removes clutter so you can focus entirely on watching. You can adjust playback speeds, watch high-definition video formats, and link your phone to control playback easily from your couch.

### Who it's for
* Cord-cutters and movie enthusiasts who want a streamlined, distraction-free video experience on their Android TV boxes or streaming sticks.
* Viewers who want automated skipping of sponsored segments and promotional intros in long-form videos.

### Key features
* Distraction-free interface designed specifically for big screens and TV remotes.
* Automatic skipping of sponsored messages and promotional segments using community databases.
* High-resolution video playback including support for ultra-sharp formats and high frame rates.
* Adjustable playback speeds that save your personal preferences across videos.
* Phone-to-TV connection codes so you can search and queue videos from your mobile device.
