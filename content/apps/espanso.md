### What it does in plain English
This clever utility watches what you type and instantly swaps out custom keywords for the full text you want to write. You can set up shortcuts for your email address, standard replies, code snippets, or tricky emojis across any application on your computer. Everything runs completely on your own device, meaning your personal thoughts and passwords stay strictly private.

### Who it's for
* Writers and customer support professionals who find themselves typing the same phrases repeatedly
* Programmers looking for a quick way to drop in code templates anywhere
* Anyone who wants to save time and prevent repetitive typing strain throughout the workday

### Key features
* Works smoothly across Windows, macOS, and Linux
* 100% private and local with no external tracking or cloud dependency
* Compatible with almost every application installed on your system
* Built-in search bar to quickly find and trigger your saved snippets
* Supports dates, custom scripts, emojis, and dynamic forms
