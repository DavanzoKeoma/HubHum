### What it does in plain English
Vifm is a file manager designed for people who prefer using their keyboard instead of a mouse to navigate their computer. It allows you to move, copy, delete, and organize your files using simple, quick commands. Because it is highly customizable, you can set it up to work exactly the way you want it to.

### Who it's for
*   People who want to speed up their daily file management tasks by keeping their hands on the keyboard.
*   Users who appreciate a minimalist, text-based interface that doesn't clutter their screen.

### Key features
*   **Keyboard-first navigation:** Perform all file operations without ever needing to reach for your mouse.
*   **Customizable interface:** Tailor the look and behavior of the program to suit your personal workflow.
*   **Efficient file handling:** Quickly move between folders and manage large groups of files with ease.
*   **Lightweight performance:** Runs smoothly on almost any system without consuming significant memory.
