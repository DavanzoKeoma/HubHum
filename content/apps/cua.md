### What it does in plain English
Cua acts as a bridge that lets you link multiple computers together into a single, unified workspace. It provides tools for your automated assistants to interact with your desktop programs, browsers, and files just like a human would. You can easily move your active sessions between devices, allowing you to pick up exactly where you left off on any machine you own.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers who need to access their desktop environment from different computers.
* Power users who want to integrate automated assistants into their daily software workflows.

### Key features
* **Unified Spaces:** Create virtual desktops that run on your own hardware, keeping your work sessions accessible across different machines.
* **Smart Teleporting:** Move active applications like web browsers or messaging tools between computers while keeping your login sessions intact.
* **Multiplayer Control:** Collaborate on the same desktop screen with your automated assistants, with each having its own cursor.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works across macOS, Windows, and Linux to ensure your tools follow you regardless of your operating system.
* **Local Privacy:** Keeps your sensitive session data encrypted on your own machine rather than relying on external cloud storage.
