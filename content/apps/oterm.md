### What it does in plain English
Oterm brings artificial intelligence chat assistants straight into your computer terminal. It connects seamlessly with local language models as well as major cloud-based intelligence providers. You can manage multiple chat sessions, switch between different providers, and keep your conversations organized without ever leaving your text interface.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who prefer working entirely within command-line environments.
* Power users looking for a fast, lightweight interface for local or cloud AI models.
* Anyone who wants distraction-free access to artificial intelligence assistants without browser bloat.

### Key features
* Connects directly to local language model runners and major cloud providers.
* Runs entirely inside your standard text terminal interface.
* Organizes multiple separate chat conversations effortlessly.
* Lightweight design that launches instantly with minimal resource usage.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, follow the instructions and guides on the official [oterm Documentation Website](https://ggozad.github.io/oterm/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Fast keyboard-driven interface, supports multiple AI providers, runs locally or in the cloud.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with text command environments.
