### What it does in plain English
mactop gives you a live, detailed look at how your Apple Silicon computer is performing under the hood. It displays real-time statistics on processor activity, core speeds, and energy consumption right on your screen. You can easily spot which apps or tasks are demanding the most system resources without digging through complicated menus.

### Who it's for
* Mac owners who want to keep an eye on battery life and processor efficiency
* Developers and power users tracking hardware performance during heavy workloads
* Anyone curious about how their computer's chips handle everyday tasks

### Key features
* Real-time tracking of processor core activity and speeds
* Live overview of power consumption and energy usage
* Clean, lightweight text display designed for efficiency
* Easy installation and configuration for daily use
