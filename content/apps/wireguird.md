### What it does in plain English
Wireguird is a friendly desktop companion for Linux that gives you a graphical menu bar interface to control your secure virtual private network connections. Instead of typing commands into a terminal every time you want to connect or disconnect, you can manage everything with a quick click. The status icon in your system tray even changes color to let you know at a glance whether your secure tunnel is currently active.

### Who it's for
* Linux desktop users who want a visual way to toggle secure connections without using command-line tools.
* Remote workers and privacy enthusiasts who rely on standard virtual private network tunnels configured in their system directories.

### Key features
* **System Tray Control:** Features a simple status icon that turns red when connected and black when disconnected.
* **Familiar Experience:** Modeled after official desktop helper tools to provide a seamless and recognizable layout.
* **Automatic Detection:** Automatically scans and lists all available network tunnels directly from your system folders.
* **Reliable Engine:** Operates smoothly behind the scenes using standard system networking helpers.
