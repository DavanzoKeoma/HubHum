### What it does in plain English
Deva brings advanced artificial intelligence assistants directly to your desktop through a familiar chat window. You simply describe a goal, and the system breaks it down into research, coding, and writing tasks without requiring tedious technical setup. When projects grow larger, it automatically brings in specialized team members to work alongside you and summarize their progress.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and technical researchers looking for a local, context-aware coding partner.
* Writers and data analysts who want an autonomous helper to gather information and draft documents.
* Anyone who prefers managing complex multi-step workflows through simple chat conversations instead of manual tools.

### Key features
* **Team Collaboration:** Add multiple AI helpers to a single chat session to handle separate parts of your project simultaneously.
* **Local Data Privacy:** All your conversations, personal memories, and settings stay secure on your own machine.
* **Smart Planning & Checkpoints:** Review step-by-step implementation plans before any changes are made, and easily step backward if needed.
* **Extensible Customization:** Connect your own favorite AI models, create specialized roles, and hook up automated tasks.
* **Remote Access:** Continue your work seamlessly on mobile devices using popular instant messaging bots.

* **Pros:** Free and open-source, keeps data local on your machine, supports multiple AI service providers.
* **Cons:** Requires your own API keys for desired AI models, advanced multi-agent features take a moment to learn.
