### What it does in plain English
Automa lets you set up tiny blocks that work together, so repetitive clicks become a thing of the past.  
It can automatically fill out forms, grab screenshots, or pull information from a site for you.  

### Who it's for
- People who spend a lot of time filling online forms or copying data.  
- Content creators who need quick screenshots or data extracts while researching.  
- Anyone who wants to schedule a browser task to run at a specific time.

### Key features
- Drag‑and‑drop blocks to build a workflow without writing code.  
- Schedule a workflow to run later or repeat daily.  
- Save and share your workflows in the Automa marketplace.  

### How to install (Download)
- **Chrome:** Install from the Chrome Web Store – [Add to Chrome](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/automa/infppggnoaenmfagbfknfkancpbljcca).  
- **Firefox:** Install from Firefox Add‑ons – [Add to Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/automa/).  

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, no subscription, works in major browsers.  
* **Cons:** Advanced workflows may need a bit of trial and error to perfect.
