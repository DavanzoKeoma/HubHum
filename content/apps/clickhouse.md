### What it does in plain English
ClickHouse acts as a high-speed digital filing cabinet that allows you to search through millions of records instantly. It is perfect for businesses that need to see trends or customer behavior as it happens. * Data analysts who need to generate reports from massive datasets without waiting for slow processing.
* Website owners and developers who want to track user activity and system performance in real-time.
