### What it does in plain English
FSNotes is a high-speed writing companion for Mac and iPhone that keeps all your thoughts organized without locking you into a closed system. Because every note is saved as a standard, portable text file, you can open and edit your writing using any compatible program, now or years from now. It combines a clean, distraction-free writing environment with powerful search capabilities and deep formatting options like checklists, diagrams, and encrypted privacy protection.

### Who it's for
* **Writers and researchers** who need to manage thousands of text files and markdown documents without lag.
* **Privacy-conscious users** who want their personal thoughts secured with robust encryption and stored locally on their own devices.
* **Apple ecosystem users** who want seamless, lightning-fast note synchronization between their Mac computers and iPhones.

### Key features
* **Complete file ownership:** Keep all your notes as standard text files in folders you control, with zero locked databases.
* **Blazing-fast search:** Instantly filter through tens of thousands of notes as you type.
* **Rich Markdown formatting:** Render code blocks, math equations, diagrams, and interactive task lists effortlessly.
* **Flexible synchronization:** Connect your files securely using iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or private folders.
* **Enhanced security:** Lock sensitive personal entries behind password-based encryption.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete control over your files, lightning-fast search speed, zero subscription lock-in for local files, and clean native design.
* **Cons:** Native apps are built exclusively for Apple devices (Mac and iOS), meaning Windows and Android users cannot run them.
