### What it does in plain English
HTMLy is a super fast blogging tool that lets you publish articles and run a personal website without needing a complex database setup. Instead of storing your writing in a database, it saves everything directly as text files on your server. This makes your site load instantly and makes backups as simple as copying a folder.

### Who it's for
* Bloggers and writers who want a straightforward, distraction-free space to publish posts.
* Hobbyists and developers looking for a low-maintenance website tool that runs smoothly on inexpensive hosting.
* Anyone who prefers managing content with standard text files rather than dealing with complex administration panels.

### Key features
* Operates entirely without a database for maximum loading speed and simple management.
* Organizes posts efficiently by date, category, tag, or author even with thousands of pages.
* Includes an easy online setup script to get your site running in just a few clicks.
* Supports customizable themes and built-in management dashboards for user posts.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, incredibly fast performance, no database maintenance required.
* **Cons:** Requires a PHP-enabled web server, fewer built-in extensions compared to massive enterprise platforms.
