### What it does in plain English
Paperling is a lightweight desktop and mobile application designed to open and edit text-based markdown files without complicated setups or heavy folder structures. It renders equations, chemistry formulas, and diagrams in real-time right beside your raw text so you can review your notes smoothly. An optional local assistant can also propose helpful writing edits that you can accept or reject directly inside your document.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need a quick way to read notes containing complex math, chemistry notation, or diagrams.
* Writers and developers looking for a fast, distraction-free program to open single text files without dealing with heavy note management software.
* Anyone who wants an offline-first writing space with clean themes, tabbed organization, and seamless cross-platform syncing.

### Key features
* **Instant document viewing:** Open any text document and view formatted content immediately with live split-screen or reader options.
* **Live scientific rendering:** See math equations, chemical equations, and flowcharts update instantly as you type.
* **Optional AI writing helper:** Connect your preferred language model to review, summarize, or suggest text improvements with inline review controls.
* **Clean visual themes:** Choose from multiple built-in color schemes including dark, light, paper, and high-contrast styles.
* **Cross-platform access:** Run the app natively across Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile devices.
