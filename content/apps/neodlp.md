### What it does in plain English
NeoDLP is a desktop application that helps you save video and audio clips from thousands of online platforms onto your computer. It connects directly with your web browser so you can send links over with a quick click or keyboard shortcut. Everything is set up right out of the box, letting you choose your preferred file formats and output quality without messing with complicated commands.

### Who it's for
* Content creators looking to archive their own published videos or download reference media.
* Students and researchers who want to save educational lectures for offline viewing.
* Everyday internet users who want a simple way to keep favourite music tracks and clips on their local hard drive.

### Key features
* Download media from thousands of popular websites with full format control.
* Seamlessly integrate with web browsers to trigger downloads using right-clicks or keyboard shortcuts.
* Combine separate video and audio streams automatically for the highest available quality.
* Embed subtitles, chapters, and rich metadata directly into your downloaded files.
* Built-in sponsor blocking and proxy settings for custom network control.
