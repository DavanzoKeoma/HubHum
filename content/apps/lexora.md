### What it does in plain English
Lexora is a personal assistant that lives on your computer to help you tackle everyday tasks like organizing files, writing documents, and planning schedules. You simply describe your goal in plain words, and the assistant breaks it down into steps while working alongside you on your desktop. It keeps your data stored safely on your machine while allowing you to plug in local or online intelligence models to get things done.

### Who it's for
* Writers and content creators looking for a dedicated workspace to draft, edit, and organize research materials.
* Programmers and tech enthusiasts who want an autonomous helper to read code logs, run commands, and verify changes locally.
* Everyday users who want to manage documents, plan personal projects, and generate images without sending private files to random cloud servers.

### Key features
* **Local-First Privacy:** Keeps your project files, chat history, and API keys securely stored on your own device.
* **Autonomous Task Management:** Breaks down big goals into manageable steps, letting you review progress and adjust instructions anytime.
* **Specialized AI Crew:** Combines different roles and specialized assistants to handle exploration, research, coding, and final review.
* **Extensible Plugins:** Add custom panels, specialized tools, or workflow scripts directly from the marketplace or generate them on the fly.
* **Flexible Model Choice:** Connect to online services or run models locally on your hardware for complete offline privacy.
