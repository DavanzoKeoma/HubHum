### What it does in plain English
This application gives you a personalized artificial intelligence assistant that lives entirely on your own computer and respects your privacy. It isolates each background task inside a secure virtual container so your personal files and computer remain completely protected. You can talk to your assistant effortlessly through familiar chat applications like Slack, WhatsApp, or Telegram to automate daily routines and manage projects. ### Key features
* **Complete Isolation:** Every helper agent runs inside its own lightweight Linux container with strict filesystem boundaries.
* **Multi-Channel Integration:** Chat with your helper via Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, and other popular messaging tools.
* **Automated Scheduling:** Set up recurring background tasks like weekly summaries, news briefings, or document updates without manual oversight.
