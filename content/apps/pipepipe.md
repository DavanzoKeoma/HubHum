### What it does in plain English
PipePipe lets you watch YouTube and many other video sites on your Android phone without the usual ads.  
It adds handy features like skipping sponsor segments, background playback, and a sleep timer.  
You can also filter out shorts, block unwanted channels, and download whole playlists.

### Who it's for
* People who want to enjoy videos on Android without interruptions.  
* Users who like to listen to music from videos while doing other tasks.  
* Anyone who wants more control over playback and the ability to save playlists for offline viewing.

### Key features
* Skip sponsor segments and hide ads.  
* Play videos in the background or set a sleep timer.  
* Filter out shorts, paid videos, or channels you don’t like.  
* Download full playlists and sort them locally.  
* Add new services through community plugins.
