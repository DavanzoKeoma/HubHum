### What it does in plain English
GoBlog allows you to create and manage your own personal blog from a single, efficient program. It lets you write posts using simple formatting, organize your thoughts, and share them with the world on your own terms. Because it is designed for personal ownership, you keep all your data and images on your own server rather than relying on large social media companies.

### Who it's for
*   **Independent Writers:** People who want a clean, distraction-free space to publish their thoughts without ads or tracking.
*   **Tech-Savvy Hobbyists:** Users who enjoy hosting their own websites and want to connect their blog to the broader decentralized web.

### Key features
*   **Markdown Writing:** Write your posts using simple text formatting that is easy to read and edit.
*   **Full Ownership:** All your posts, images, and settings are stored locally on your own machine or server.
*   **Fediverse Ready:** Easily connect your blog to social networks like Mastodon to share your updates automatically.
*   **Flexible Design:** Customize the look and feel of your site through simple settings and custom styling.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** Includes optional features like password protection and secure login methods to keep your site private.

*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, gives you full ownership of your data, and supports modern web standards.
