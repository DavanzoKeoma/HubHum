### What it does in plain English
Fladder acts as a clean, easy-to-use interface for your existing personal media server. It allows you to stream your movies, shows, and even comic books directly to your phone, computer, or television. You can manage your library, keep track of what you have watched, and even download files to enjoy when you are offline.

### Who it's for
* People who host their own home media collections and want a consistent, modern way to view them.
* Families who want a simple, unified interface for watching movies and reading digital comics across different devices.

### Key features
* **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on computers, mobile phones, tablets, and smart TVs.
* **Flexible Playback:** Supports streaming directly, converting files for better compatibility, or downloading for offline viewing.
* **Smart Organization:** Includes features to skip intros or credits and keeps your progress synced across all your devices.
* **Personalized Experience:** Offers light and dark themes, plus support for multiple user profiles.
* **Extra Content:** Includes basic support for reading digital comic book files.
