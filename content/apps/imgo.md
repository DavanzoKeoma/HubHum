### What it does in plain English
imgo is a simple tool that helps you shrink the file size of your photos and graphics without losing quality. You can process dozens of images at once, change their dimensions, or convert them into different file formats. Because the app runs entirely on your own device, your private images are never uploaded to the internet.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and content creators who need to quickly resize or compress large batches of images for websites.
* Everyday users who want to save storage space on their computer by shrinking high-resolution photos.

### Key features
* **Batch Processing:** Drag and drop multiple files to compress or convert them all at once.
* **Privacy First:** All work happens locally on your computer, so your files stay offline.
* **Flexible Controls:** Easily adjust image quality, resize dimensions, and remove extra metadata.
* **Wide Format Support:** Works with popular formats like JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, and HEIC.
* **Versatile Access:** Use the installable desktop app or the convenient web version directly in your browser.
