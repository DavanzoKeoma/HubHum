### What it does in plain English
EcoPaste is a handy desktop tool that quietly remembers everything you copy, from simple text and images to files and links. It organizes your clipboard history in a clean, searchable list so you can easily find and reuse past items. Best of all, it keeps your private information secure by automatically skipping sensitive passwords and secret keys.

### Who it's for
* Writers and researchers who frequently collect snippets, web links, and references throughout the day.
* Programmers and system administrators who copy code blocks, tokens, and technical notes across different applications.
* Everyday computer users who want a reliable way to retrieve items they copied hours or days ago.

### Key features
* Automatically saves text, images, formatted content, and files as you copy them.
* Fast search using local database technology to find old items instantly.
* Built-in privacy protection that ignores sensitive secrets like private keys and security tokens.
* Dedicated preview window for inspecting long text, images, and documents.
* Favorites, pinned items, and custom groups to keep your most important snippets organized.
