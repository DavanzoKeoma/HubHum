### What it does in plain English
VoiceInk is a native macOS application that instantly turns spoken words into written text using powerful artificial intelligence running right on your computer. Because everything is processed locally on your device, your private thoughts and conversations never leave your Mac. It features intelligent modes that adapt to whatever app you are using, helping you dictate emails, messages, and documents effortlessly.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who want to dictate long documents, emails, and notes faster than typing.
* Professionals and writers who value absolute data privacy and offline voice transcription.
* Anyone looking for a reliable, subscription-free speech-to-text tool on macOS.

### Key features
* **Instant Local Transcription:** Converts your voice to text immediately with high accuracy using offline artificial intelligence.
* **Complete Privacy:** 100% offline processing ensures your sensitive data never leaves your computer.
* **Context Aware:** Automatically adapts its behavior and formatting depending on the application or website you are currently using.
* **Personal Dictionary:** Train the software to recognize specialized industry terms, custom words, and unique spelling preferences.
* **Global Shortcuts:** Set custom keyboard or mouse triggers to start recording instantly from anywhere on your Mac.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Runs completely offline for maximum privacy, highly accurate speech recognition, customizable shortcuts and personal dictionary.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS 15.0 or newer to run, initial setup requires downloading local artificial intelligence models.
