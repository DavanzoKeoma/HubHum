### What it does in plain English
Spotube gathers songs from the services you already use and plays them in one place.  
You can create and save personal playlists that stay on your device, and the app shows the lyrics in time with the music.  
It works on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iPhone without sending any of your data elsewhere.

### Who it's for
* Casual listeners who want all their favorite tracks in one app.  
* Music lovers who like to keep personal playlists and see synced lyrics.  
* Travelers who need a lightweight player that works offline.

### Key features
- Play songs from many online music services using simple add‑ons.  
- Create and save personal playlists that stay on your device.  
- See lyrics appear in sync with the music.  
- Works on computers, phones and tablets with a small download size.  
- No hidden tracking or data collection.
