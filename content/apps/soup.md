### What it does in plain English
Soup removes the complexity from training and customizing artificial intelligence models. It uses a smart technique to run large-scale training tasks on standard home computers by managing memory efficiently. You can set up and start your training process with just one simple configuration file and a single command.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and developers who want to customize AI models on their own hardware.
* Students or hobbyists who have limited computer memory but want to experiment with advanced AI technology.

### Key features
* **Simple Setup:** Uses a single, easy-to-read file to manage all your training settings.
* **Hardware Friendly:** Specifically designed to train large models on laptops with limited memory.
* **All-in-One Workflow:** Handles everything from data preparation to testing and sharing your finished model.
* **No Cloud Required:** Keep your data and your training process entirely on your own machine.
* **Interactive Dashboard:** Includes a visual interface to monitor your training progress and chat with your model.

### How to install (Download)
Soup is installed via the Python package manager. Open your terminal or command prompt and run:

* **Core version:** `pip install soup-cli`
* **Full training version:** `pip install "soup-cli[train]"`
* **All-inclusive version (UI + training):** `pip install "soup-cli[all]"`

For detailed installation guides and troubleshooting, visit the [Official Website](https://trysoup.dev).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, works on standard home hardware, no subscription fees, and highly efficient memory usage.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with using a terminal, and training large models can still take time depending on your computer's power.
