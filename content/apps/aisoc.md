### What it does in plain English
AiSOC acts as a central hub that gathers security information from your various digital tools to spot potential threats. It uses intelligent agents to examine these alerts, explaining exactly why it flagged them so you can see the reasoning behind every decision. You remain in full control, as the system only takes action once a human reviews and approves the suggested response.

### Who it's for
* Security analysts looking for a transparent, auditable way to manage and investigate digital alerts.
* Privacy-conscious teams who want to run advanced security operations on their own hardware without relying on external cloud subscriptions.

### Key features
* **Automated Triage:** Uses local AI models to analyze alerts and provide clear, human-readable explanations.
* **Full Audit Trail:** Every step taken by the AI is recorded in a ledger, ensuring you can verify how a conclusion was reached.
* **Human-in-the-loop:** No automated actions occur without your explicit approval, keeping you in charge of your infrastructure.
* **Self-Hosted Privacy:** Runs entirely on your own systems, meaning no sensitive data is sent to third-party providers.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Connects with dozens of common security tools to normalize and correlate data from different sources.
