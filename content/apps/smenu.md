### What it does in plain English
smenu helps you quickly pick items from long lists directly within your computer's command window. It creates a clean, scrolling menu that lets you find exactly what you need using simple search or navigation keys. Because it works right where you are, it keeps your workflow smooth without cluttering your screen or forcing you to switch windows.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want to speed up their daily tasks by creating custom selection menus.
* People who work with long lists of files or data and need a faster way to pick specific items.

### Key features
* **Easy Navigation:** Move through lists using your keyboard arrows or your mouse.
* **Smart Search:** Find items instantly by typing a few letters, with support for simple or advanced search styles.
* **Flexible Layouts:** Display your lists in columns, rows, or tables to best suit your screen space.
* **Multiple Selections:** Pick one or many items at once using simple tagging or pinning methods.
* **Visual Customization:** Adjust colors and display settings to make your menus look exactly how you want.
