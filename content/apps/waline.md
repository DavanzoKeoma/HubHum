### What it does in plain English
Waline is a tool that lets you easily add a comment section to your personal website or blog. It keeps your site fast and lightweight while providing powerful features to manage and moderate discussions. You can choose from many different ways to store your data and host the service, giving you full control over your content.

### Who it's for
*   Bloggers and writers who want to engage with their readers without complex setups.
*   Website owners looking for a privacy-focused, customizable alternative to standard comment platforms.

### Key features
*   **Lightweight Design:** Built to be fast and efficient so it does not slow down your website.
*   **Strong Security:** Includes built-in tools to filter spam and manage unwanted content effectively.
*   **Flexible Hosting:** Compatible with a wide variety of hosting providers and database types.
*   **Rich Formatting:** Supports standard text styling so readers can use bold, italics, and lists in their comments.
*   **Notification System:** Automatically alerts you via email or messaging apps when someone leaves a new comment.

### How to install (Download)
Waline is a web-based service. You can get started by visiting the official documentation to choose your preferred hosting method: [Visit Waline Setup Guide](https://waline.js.org/en/)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly customizable, and supports many different storage options.
*   **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of how to set up a web service, as it is not a simple "plug-and-play" widget for non-technical users.
