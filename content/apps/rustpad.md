### What it does in plain English
Rustpad provides a shared workspace where you and your team can edit text and code files at the same time. It works directly in your web browser, ensuring that everyone sees changes instantly as they happen. Because it is designed to be self-hosted, you keep full control over your data without needing to rely on third-party services.

### Who it's for
*   **Software developers** who need a quick way to pair-program or share code snippets with colleagues.
*   **Students and small teams** looking for a simple, private alternative to large, complex commercial editing platforms.

### Key features
*   **Real-time collaboration:** Multiple users can type in the same document simultaneously with smooth synchronization.
*   **Minimalist design:** A clean, distraction-free interface that focuses entirely on the text you are writing.
*   **Self-hosted privacy:** You run the software on your own hardware, meaning your documents stay on your own servers.
*   **No database required:** The system is highly efficient and runs entirely in memory for maximum speed.
*   **Flexible storage:** Optional support for saving your work to a file so that your documents persist even after a restart.

### How to install (Download)
Rustpad is designed to be run as a web service. You can access the official public instance or host your own:
*   **Try the live version:** [Visit Rustpad.io](https://rustpad.io)
*   **Self-host with Docker:** Use the official image `ekzhang/rustpad` from [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/r/ekzhang/rustpad/).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Extremely lightweight, no complex database setup needed, and offers excellent real-time performance.
*   **Cons:** By default, documents are temporary and will be cleared after 24 hours of inactivity unless you configure persistent storage.
