### What it does in plain English
RyTuneX is a user-friendly application designed to help you speed up and clean up Windows 10 and 11 computers. It safely removes pre-installed unnecessary programs, clears hidden system caches to free up valuable storage space, and blocks intrusive background telemetry. With an organized control panel, everyday users can fine-tune their computer performance, manage startup items, and safeguard personal privacy with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users looking to breathe new life into a sluggish laptop or desktop.
* Gamers and power users wanting to minimize system lag, reduce background distractions, and maximize hardware responsiveness.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want an easy way to disable unwanted data collection features built into modern operating systems.

### Key features
* **Deep Cleanup Engine:** Safely clears out temporary files, browser leftovers, and leftover shader caches to recover hard drive space.
* **Unwanted Program Removal:** Eliminates pre-installed software and unwanted utilities that consume background resources.
* **Privacy Controls:** Blocks background tracking features, advertising hooks, and telemetry to keep your personal data private.
* **Startup and Process Management:** Controls which apps launch when your computer starts up to ensure fast boot times.
* **Hardware Health Monitoring:** Displays real-time metrics for your processor, memory, and storage drives.
