### What it does in plain English
Bottom is a visual tool that shows you exactly what your computer is doing at any given moment. It displays easy-to-read charts for your processor, memory, and disk usage, helping you spot what might be slowing your system down. You can also use it to manage your running programs, allowing you to stop tasks that are using too many resources.

### Who it's for
*   **Power users** who want to keep a close eye on their system's health and performance.
*   **Troubleshooters** who need to identify and close programs that are causing their computer to lag or freeze.

### Key features
*   **Live Charts:** Real-time visual graphs for CPU, memory, network, and disk activity.
*   **Process Management:** Easily view, sort, and stop individual programs or background tasks.
*   **Customizable Layout:** Rearrange the information panels to show exactly what matters most to you.
*   **Battery & Temperature:** Dedicated sections to monitor your laptop battery health and hardware temperatures.
*   **Keyboard Friendly:** Navigate and control the entire interface using simple keyboard shortcuts.
