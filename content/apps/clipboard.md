### What it does in plain English
Clipboard is an advanced utility that upgrades how your computer handles copied items by giving you infinite storage slots and history. Instead of replacing your copied text every time you copy something new, it lets you store, organize, and recall multiple texts, files, and directories. You can easily interact with your saved items through straightforward terminal commands or integrate them into automated scripts.

### Who it's for
* System administrators and power users who spend a lot of time working in the command line.
* Developers and scripters who want to automate repetitive copy-and-paste tasks.
* Everyday computer users who need a reliable way to keep track of multiple copied texts and files at once.

### Key features
* Save and manage an unlimited number of temporary or persistent clipboards with full history.
* Copy and paste text, files, directories, and binary data without restrictions.
* Create custom notes and attach them directly to your saved clipboard items.
* Filter out sensitive information automatically using pattern matching.
* Smooth integration with existing desktop environments and terminal systems.

* **Cons:** Primarily operated via command-line interface, which requires basic familiarity with terminal usage.
