### What it does in plain English
Caprine brings Facebook Messenger to your desktop so you can chat without opening a browser. It hides when you’re not active and blocks Facebook from tracking what you click. You can switch chats, use dark mode, and set shortcuts to work faster.

### Who it's for
People who chat with friends on Messenger and want a tidy desktop app. Professionals who use Messenger for work and need quick shortcuts. Anyone who cares about keeping their chat activity private.

### Key features
- Works on Windows, macOS and Linux.  
- Dark mode and optional vibrancy for a comfortable look.  
- Hide read receipts and typing indicators to protect privacy.  
- Keyboard shortcuts for common actions like new chat and search.  
- Option to keep the app on top of other windows.
