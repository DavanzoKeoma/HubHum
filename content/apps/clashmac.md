### What it does in plain English
ClashMac provides a native macOS control center for managing your network traffic and proxy routes. It features an interactive visual map, live connection monitoring, and automated system rules to keep your browsing private and fast. Running quietly from your menu bar, it configures background helpers automatically so you can focus on your work without complex setups.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who need reliable network traffic routing and location masking.
* Remote workers looking to bypass regional connection slowdowns securely.
* Privacy-conscious individuals wanting detailed visibility over active app connections.

### Key features
* **Visual Route Map:** Watch active traffic paths move across the globe in real-time with adjustable city routing.
* **Live Connection Grid:** Inspect bandwidth consumption, speeds, and routing rules for every active application.
* **Speed Curves & Dashboards:** Monitor upload and download speeds cleanly with detailed performance leaderboards.
* **Anti-Detect Fingerprint Browser:** Align timezones, languages, and profiles automatically to prevent cross-region tracking.
* **Menu Bar Integration:** Access quick controls, toggle rules, and manage helpers right from your Mac status bar.
