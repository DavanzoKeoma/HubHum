### What it does in plain English
Project Maya allows you to run massive, high-performance AI models directly on your own computer hardware. It intelligently manages your memory to keep the most important parts of the AI active on your graphics card while storing the rest on your drive for smooth performance. Because everything runs locally, your conversations and data never leave your machine.

### Who it's for
* **Power Users:** People who want to run advanced AI tools without relying on external cloud services or subscriptions.
* **Developers:** Coders who need an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible interface to power their own local agents and automated workflows.

### Key features
* **Hardware Optimization:** Automatically measures your computer's components to tune itself for the best possible speed.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works with both NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards, as well as Windows and Linux systems.
* **Built-in Dashboard:** Includes a user-friendly web interface for chatting, monitoring performance, and managing your model library.
* **Flexible API:** Provides a local interface that plugs directly into existing coding tools and agent software.
* **Privacy First:** All processing happens offline, ensuring your data remains completely under your control.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, ensure you have a compatible graphics card and a fast NVMe SSD. Follow the official quick-start guide:
1. Open your terminal and run: `git clone https://github.com/mw00/project-maya.git && cd project-maya`
2. For Linux, run `./setup.sh`. For Windows, run `START-MAYA.bat`.
3. Once the setup completes, open `http://127.0.0.1:8080` in your web browser.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, complete data privacy, and supports high-end models on consumer hardware.
* **Cons:** Requires a powerful graphics card and significant storage space; initial setup and model downloading can take time.
