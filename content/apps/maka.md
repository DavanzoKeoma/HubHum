### What it does in plain English
Maka watches what you do on your computer and writes it down.  
It keeps a clear, searchable record of every command, approval, and tool you use.  
You can later open the history to see exactly what happened and why.  

### Who it's for
Anyone who wants a safety net for their computer work—writers, freelancers, students, or anyone who worries about losing track of what they’ve done.  

### Key features
- Automatically notes every step you take on your computer.  
- Stores the notes in a safe local database you control.  
- Lets you search the history by date, program, or keyword.  
- Shows a simple window to browse and replay past actions.  
- Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux without needing extra services.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, keeps a reliable history, works offline, cross‑platform.  
* **Cons:** requires a bit of technical setup, currently only for desktop computers, no mobile app.
