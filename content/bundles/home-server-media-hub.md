### Why this curated guide
Turn an inexpensive mini-PC or cloud VPS into a clean household streaming center without recurring platform fees or tracking.

### Featured highlights
* **[Audiobookshelf](/HubHum/apps/audiobookshelf/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Host your own private streaming server for audiobooks and podcasts with automatic progress syncing and multi-user support.
* **[Kavita](/HubHum/apps/kavita/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Organize and stream your personal comic, manga, and book library across all your devices using a private server.
* **[Navidrome](/HubHum/apps/navidrome/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Turn your computer into a personal streaming server to enjoy your entire music collection from any browser or mobile device.
* **[Calibre-Web-Automated](/HubHum/apps/calibre-web-automated/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Calibre-Web-Automated turns your digital book collection into a fully managed, self-hosted library that organizes and updates itself.
* **[Suwayomi-Server](/HubHum/apps/suwayomi-server/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Suwayomi-Server acts as a personal home library for your digital comics, allowing you to organize and read them across any device.
* **[Immich](/HubHum/apps/immich/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Manage and back up your photos and videos on your own private cloud storage without sharing your personal memories.
