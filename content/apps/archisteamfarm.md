### What it does in plain English
ArchiSteamFarm is a lightweight tool that automatically collects digital trading cards from your game library. It works in the background for multiple accounts at once, meaning you do not need to keep the official game launcher running or even have it installed. Because it operates independently, it is perfect for running on home servers or older computers that you want to keep running efficiently.

### Who it's for
*   Gamers who manage multiple accounts and want to collect digital items without manual effort.
*   Users who want to save system resources by avoiding the heavy official game client.

### Key features
*   **Multi-Account Support:** Manage an unlimited number of Steam accounts simultaneously from a single interface.
*   **Low Resource Usage:** Runs efficiently in the background without needing the official game client open.
*   **Privacy Focused:** Designed to be secure and safe, ensuring your accounts remain protected.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on Windows, Linux, and macOS.
*   **Extensible:** Includes a plugin system if you want to add custom functionality or advanced automation.
