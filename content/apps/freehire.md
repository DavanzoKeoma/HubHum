### What it does in plain English
Freehire acts as a direct bridge between you and company career pages, gathering millions of live job postings into one searchable place. By pulling data straight from the source, it eliminates the clutter of recruiter reposts and outdated listings. It also provides tools to help you organize your applications, tailor your resume, and track your progress throughout the hiring process.

### Who it's for
*   **Job Seekers:** Professionals looking for accurate, up-to-date tech roles without the noise of third-party job boards.
*   **Career Changers:** Individuals who want to streamline their application workflow by keeping all their job leads and documents in one organized space.

### Key features
*   **Direct Sourcing:** Every listing is pulled directly from company career sites, ensuring the job is real and the link works.
*   **Smart Deduplication:** If a company posts the same job on multiple platforms, Freehire collapses them into a single, clean entry.
*   **Advanced Filtering:** Easily narrow down results by region, work format, seniority, and specific skills.
*   **Application Management:** Includes a built-in board to track your application stages and an inbox that links recruiter replies to specific jobs.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** The platform is open-source, meaning you can use the hosted version or run your own instance for total control.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely transparent data sources, no recruiter spam, and powerful tools for tracking applications.
*   **Cons:** Advanced AI-powered features (like automated CV tailoring) require a paid subscription, and self-hosting requires some technical familiarity with server management.
