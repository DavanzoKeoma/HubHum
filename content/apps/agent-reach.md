### What it does in plain English
Agent-Reach acts as a bridge that lets your AI assistant see and read information from websites it normally cannot access. It handles the complex task of navigating social media, video platforms, and news sites so you don't have to do it manually.

### Who it's for
*   **AI Power Users:** People who want their automated assistants to research current trends, news, or video content.
*   **Researchers:** Users who need to gather structured data from specific platforms like forums or social networks without manual copying.

### Key features
*   **Broad Platform Support:** Access data from sites like YouTube, Reddit, Bilibili, and GitHub with ease.
*   **Automated Navigation:** The tool manages the technical side of visiting sites, including handling logins and anti-bot measures.
*   **Structured Results:** It converts messy web pages into clean, readable information that your AI can process instantly.
