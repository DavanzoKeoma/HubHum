### What it does in plain English
BookReader is a mobile reading application designed to bring your digital library right to your Android device. It lets you open standard text, PDF, and e-book files with smooth visual effects that mimic real paper pages. You can also transfer files easily from your computer over a local wireless network without needing cables.

### Who it's for
* Avid readers who want a clean, customizable mobile reading space for their personal library.
* People who like reading on the go and want an easy way to manage offline document files on Android.

### Key features
* Supports popular document formats including text files, PDFs, and standard e-books.
* Implements realistic 3D page-turning animations for an authentic reading feel.
* Includes a wireless transfer feature to upload files directly from a computer via Wi-Fi.
* Offers adjustable fonts, background themes, and screen brightness controls.
* Features a convenient day and night mode to protect your eyes in low light.
