### What it does in plain English
Presenterm turns ordinary markdown text files into sleek, fully interactive slideshows that run directly inside your computer screen's terminal window. It handles rich media such as images and animated GIFs, applies color coding to computer instructions, and even lets you export your slides into PDF documents. You can update your presentation text on the fly, and the slideshow refreshes instantly as you save your changes.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and technical writers who prefer writing content in markdown rather than using traditional slide software.
* Linux, macOS, and Windows users who love working inside command-line environments.
* Presenters who want lightweight, fast, and distration-free slideshow tools for tech talks and meetups.

### Key features
* Build entire presentations out of one or more structured markdown text files.
* Display inline images and animated GIFs on compatible terminal apps.
* Choose from a variety of built-in visual themes or customize your own colors and layouts.
* Highlight code snippets automatically for a wide range of programming languages.
* Convert your finished slideshows effortlessly into PDF or HTML formats.

* **Pros:** Free and open-source, lightning fast, highly customizable themes, works entirely offline without bulky office suites.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern terminal emulator for advanced image rendering, best suited for users comfortable with text editing.
