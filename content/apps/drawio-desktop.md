### What it does in plain English
This application gives you a complete workspace for drawing flowcharts, floor plans, organizational charts, and technical diagrams right on your computer. It runs completely disconnected from the internet to keep all your work private and secure. Your sensitive drawings never leave your device unless you choose to export and share them manually.

### Who it's for
* Project managers sketching out team workflows and project timelines
* Software developers mapping out database structures and system architectures
* Students and educators organizing ideas and study notes into visual charts

### Key features
* Complete offline privacy with zero external tracking or hidden data collection
* Massive library of built-in shapes, icons, and templates for any project type
* Supports opening and exporting common file formats like images, PDFs, and vector graphics
* Runs smoothly across major desktop operating systems without requiring constant internet access
