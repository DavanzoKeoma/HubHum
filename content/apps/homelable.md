### What it does in plain English
Homelable creates a clear visual map of all the computers, smart devices, and services connected to your home network. It automatically scans your setup to identify equipment and keeps track of whether your services are online or offline. You can also use it to write notes and manuals for your devices, ensuring you always know how your home setup is configured.

### Who it's for
*   **Home Tech Enthusiasts:** People who manage their own servers, smart home hubs, or complex network equipment and want a clear overview.
*   **Home Office Managers:** Individuals who need to keep track of multiple connected devices and ensure their essential services are running smoothly.

### Key features
*   **Interactive Network Maps:** Drag-and-drop your devices onto a canvas to visualize how everything is connected.
*   **Live Status Monitoring:** See at a glance which of your services are currently running and which might be down.
*   **Automated Discovery:** Automatically scan your network to find and identify new devices without manual entry.
*   **Integrated Documentation:** Keep notes, setup guides, and troubleshooting steps right next to the device icons on your map.
*   **Physical Rack View:** Design and manage your physical equipment racks, including cable connections and port patching.

### How to install (Download)
Homelable is designed to be hosted on your own hardware. You can find the official installation instructions and setup guides on the [Homelable Website](https://homelable.net).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Keeps all your network documentation in one place, provides real-time status updates, and offers a clean visual interface.
*   **Cons:** Requires some initial setup time to configure the network scan and organize your devices, and it is best suited for users comfortable with basic self-hosting concepts.
