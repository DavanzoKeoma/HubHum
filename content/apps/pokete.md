### What it does in plain English
Pokete brings a classic creature-collection role-playing experience straight to your computer text window. Players travel across a retro world, encounter wild creatures in tall grass, and build a powerful team of fighters. You can challenge wandering trainers, heal your squad at local stations, and trade or evolve your companions as you journey to become the ultimate trainer.

### Who it's for
* Retro gaming enthusiasts who love classic monster-battling mechanics
* Command-line hobbyists looking for a fun distraction during breaks
* Casual gamers who enjoy lightweight pixel art role-playing titles

### Key features
* Engage in turn-based battles using a wide variety of elemental creature types
* Explore a grid-based world with wandering non-player characters and hidden items
* Catch wild creatures, level them up, and evolve them into stronger forms
* Heal and manage your entire collection at dedicated resting stations
* Support for custom player-created mods and expansions

* **Pros:** Lightweight, free forever, nostalgic retro gameplay, runs completely offline.
* **Cons:** Requires a terminal window (avoid fullscreen mode for best results), windows antivirus may occasionally misidentify the audio library.
