### What it does in plain English
CourseLit provides an all-in-one platform for educators and creators to launch their own branded teaching website. It handles everything from organizing course lessons and managing student accounts to processing secure payments and publishing blog posts. You can customize your storefront pages to match your personal brand without dealing with complicated software.

### Who it's for
* Independent educators and teachers launching online classes
* Content creators selling digital guides and downloadable assets
* Bloggers looking to add structured courses to their existing audience

### Key features
* Complete course authoring tools to organize lessons and modules
* Built-in payment processing to accept secure purchases from students
* Integrated website builder for designing custom landing and sales pages
* Student management dashboard to track enrollments and progress
* Blogging capabilities to publish articles and attract new visitors

### How to install (Download)
* Try the cloud-hosted version directly on the [CourseLit Website](https://courselit.app).
* For self-hosting instructions and server setup guides, visit the [CourseLit Documentation](https://docs.courselit.app).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete control over your branding, open-source flexibility, easy student management.
* **Cons:** Advanced analytics are currently limited, self-hosting requires technical setup knowledge.
