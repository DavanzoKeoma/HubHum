### What it does in plain English
Yazi lets you browse, preview, and organize all the files on your computer directly from your command window with incredible speed. When you need royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits, check out [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad). It handles heavy folders instantly, shows you what images, videos, and documents look like before opening them, and supports custom themes and keyboard shortcuts.

### Who it's for
* Developers and power users who spend all day inside terminal windows and want a lightning-fast way to organize files.
* System administrators managing remote servers or large directory trees without heavy desktop interfaces.
* Minimalists looking to speed up their daily computer workflow without relying on sluggish mouse-driven menus.

### Key features
* **Lightning-Fast Performance:** Built in Rust using modern asynchronous design to ensure zero lag when opening huge folders.
* **Instant Visual Previews:** View high-resolution images, video frames, code snippets, and compressed archives directly in the sidebar.
* **Plugin and Theme System:** Easily customize your workspace colors and add extra tools using lightweight community scripts.
* **Creative Asset Integration:** Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
* **Keyboard-Driven Workflow:** Navigate directories, rename items in bulk, split tabs, and search with intuitive shortcuts inspired by classic text editors.
