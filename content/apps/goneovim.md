### What it does in plain English
Goneovim acts as a bridge between the powerful, keyboard-driven Neovim text editor and your computer's visual desktop environment. It allows you to use familiar mouse interactions and window management while keeping the core speed of your text editing intact. By providing a graphical layer, it makes advanced text editing tools more accessible and visually comfortable for daily use.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want the power of Neovim but prefer a traditional windowed application experience.
* Writers or power users who need a highly customizable text environment that supports modern features like smooth scrolling and font ligatures.

### Key features
* **Smooth Visuals:** Enjoy pixel-perfect scrolling and animated cursor movements that make navigating long documents easier on the eyes.
* **Flexible Layouts:** Manage multiple editing windows within a single workspace and use your mouse to interact with menus and tabs.
* **Customizable Appearance:** Easily adjust font styles, sizes, and window transparency to suit your personal workspace preferences.
* **Modern Integration:** Supports standard features like drag-and-drop file opening, desktop notifications, and clipboard sharing with other applications.
