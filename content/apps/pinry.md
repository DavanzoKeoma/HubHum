### What it does in plain English
Pinry acts as a personal digital scrapbook where you can collect images, videos, and entire webpages from across the internet. It organizes these items into a neat, tiled grid that makes it easy to browse your collection at a glance. Because you host it yourself, you keep full control over your data and who gets to see your saved boards.

### Who it's for
* Creative professionals who need to gather visual inspiration and reference materials for projects.
* Researchers or students who want to keep track of interesting articles and media in a highly visual, searchable format.

### Key features
* **Visual Organization:** View your saved content in a clean, tiled layout that is easy to skim.
* **Smart Tagging:** Assign custom labels to your items so you can find exactly what you need later.
* **Browser Integration:** Use browser extensions to quickly save content to your board while you surf the web.
* **Privacy Controls:** Choose whether your boards remain private for your eyes only or share them publicly with others.
* **Multi-User Support:** Allow multiple people to contribute to the same collection if you are working on a team project.

### How to install (Download)
Pinry is designed to be self-hosted on your own server or computer. The most reliable way to set it up is using Docker, which handles the technical requirements for you.
* [View Official Installation Guide](https://pinry.github.io/pinry/install-with-docker/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, gives you full ownership of your data, and offers a distraction-free way to organize media.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup or Docker to get started, and you are responsible for maintaining your own hosting environment.
