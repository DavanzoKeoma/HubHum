### What it does in plain English
StarNet turns your desktop into a living space station where you can create, organize, and supervise multiple artificial intelligence workers. Each worker operates in its own dedicated workspace with real capabilities, handling tasks like writing files, checking information, and running automated routines. Instead of dealing with confusing chat logs, you simply arrange your digital crew in rooms and hallways to design your ideal automated workflow.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who want to automate repetitive digital chores using intelligent assistants without writing code.
* Power users and developers who need to coordinate multiple AI helpers running concurrently on their local machine.
* Anyone who enjoys retro pixel-art visuals while managing serious productivity workflows.

### Key features
* **Live Pixel-Art Workspace:** Watch your digital crew move through rooms and workstations that reflect what the agents are currently doing.
* **Bring Your Own Models:** Connect your preferred artificial intelligence accounts or run completely free and private models locally.
* **Multi-Agent Collaboration:** Run several workers at once, each with distinct instructions, memory transcripts, and bounded permissions.
* **External Messaging:** Send instructions and receive updates through popular chat apps while away from your primary computer.
* **Night Shift Controls:** Let your digital crew handle scheduled background tasks safely within strict, adjustable safety limits.
