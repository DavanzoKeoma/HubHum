### What it does in plain English
Leon acts as your very own open-source helper, running securely on your computer to answer questions, manage plans, and execute routines. It securely keeps track of your preferences and daily habits to offer tailored assistance whenever you need it.

### Who it's for
- Busy individuals wanting a private, self-hosted digital helper that keeps their personal data secure.
- Productivity enthusiasts looking for an extensible assistant that connects with their favorite daily tools.
- Open-source advocates who prefer software they can inspect, modify, and run completely on their own terms.

### Key features
- **Conversational Intelligence:** Chat naturally to brainstorm ideas, plan schedules, and retrieve information quickly.
- **Visual Answer Cards:** Generates helpful trend charts, progress bars, and interactive forms directly inside your conversation flow.
- **Custom Tool Connections:** Easily link external services like calendars, media players, and custom utilities to expand what your assistant can do.
- **Selective Memory:** Remembers important personal facts, preferences, and recent context safely on your machine without leaking data to third parties.
