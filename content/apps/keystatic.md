### What it does in plain English
Keystatic provides a visual way to manage the text and images on your website without requiring complex technical setups. It connects directly to your existing project files stored on GitHub, ensuring your content stays organized and easy to update. Because it doesn't use a traditional database, your site remains fast, secure, and simple to maintain.

### Who it's for
* Website owners who want to update their content without touching code.
* Developers who want to offer a simple editing experience to their clients or team members.

### Key features
* **Visual Editing:** Change your website text and images through a clean, user-friendly interface.
* **GitHub Integration:** Syncs your content changes directly with your project files.
* **No Database Required:** Keeps your site lightweight by storing content in standard file formats.
* **Developer Friendly:** Designed to integrate smoothly with modern web-building tools.

### How to install (Download)
You can get started by visiting the official website: [Keystatic Official Site](https://keystatic.com). Follow the documentation guides there to set up the tool for your specific project.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely lightweight, keeps content in your own files, and offers a great user experience for non-technical editors.
* **Cons:** Still in active development, so some features may change or be experimental.
