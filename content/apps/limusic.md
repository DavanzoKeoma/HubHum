### What it does in plain English
Limusic is a fast, native desktop application that streams audio and video directly from popular music sources without any ads or cluttered browser tabs. It features synchronized scrolling lyrics, a built-in karaoke mode to remove vocals, and tools to listen together with friends in real time. Everything runs smoothly on your computer with minimal resource usage and full support for customized themes and media keys.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want an ad-free desktop experience with advanced lyrics support.
* People who love listening to music together with friends across synchronized virtual rooms.
* Users looking for a lightweight, customizable music player that respects system appearance settings.

### Key features
* Ad-free playback straight from source audio streams with gapless transitions.
* Word-by-word synchronized scrolling lyrics and built-in vocal removal for karaoke.
* Collaborative listening rooms to share queue control and music with friends.
* Scrobbling integration with Last.fm and ListenBrainz to track listening history.
* Highly customizable interface with dynamic color accents that match album artwork.
