### What it does in plain English
Grav is a modern tool that helps you build websites by storing your content in simple files rather than a complicated database. Because it doesn't rely on a database, it is incredibly fast and easy to move between different web servers. You can customize your site's look and functionality using a wide variety of themes and plugins that are simple to install.

### Who it's for
* Individuals or small business owners who want a fast, reliable website without the overhead of heavy software.
* Writers and bloggers who prefer to focus on creating content using simple text formatting.

### Key features
* **No Database Required:** Everything is stored in files, making your site faster and easier to back up.
* **Simple Content Creation:** Write your pages using standard text formatting that is easy to read and edit.
* **Easy Customization:** Use a built-in manager to browse and install themes and plugins with just a few clicks.
* **High Performance:** Built with modern technology to ensure your pages load quickly for your visitors.
* **Flexible Design:** Powerful tools allow you to control exactly how your site looks and behaves.

### How to install (Download)
You can get started by downloading the official package directly from the [Grav Downloads page](https://getgrav.org/downloads).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely fast, no database setup required, and very easy to back up or move.
* **Cons:** Requires a web server that supports PHP, and some advanced customization may require a basic understanding of how files are organized.
