### What it does in plain English
Smart Video Wallpaper Reborn is a customization plugin that lets you play videos as your computer desktop background, lock screen, or login screen. It includes smart features like automatically pausing videos when a window is open in full screen or when your laptop is running low on battery. You can also set up custom playlists, adjust playback speeds, and change wallpapers depending on the time of day.

### Who it's for
* Linux computer users running the Plasma desktop environment who want to personalize their screens with moving artwork or personal video clips.
* Customization enthusiasts looking for advanced display options like day-and-night background scheduling and multi-monitor setups.
* Laptop users who want dynamic backgrounds without sacrificing battery life or system performance during heavy work sessions.

### Key features
* Play a single video file or cycle through a custom playlist of your favorite clips.
* Automatically pause video playback when apps run in full screen or active windows are open.
* Day-night scheduling to switch background videos based on sunrise, sunset, or custom hours.
* Battery-saving threshold that pauses video playback and disables blur effects when unplugged.
* Hardware video acceleration support to keep computer processor usage low.

### How to install (Download)
* **KDE Store:** [Get Smart Video Wallpaper Reborn from the KDE Store](https://store.kde.org/p/2139746) directly through your desktop settings by right-clicking your desktop, choosing Desktop and Wallpaper, and selecting Get New Plugins.
* **Arch Linux:** Install via your package manager using `yay -S plasma6-wallpapers-smart-video-wallpaper-reborn`.
* **Fedora & openSUSE:** Download packages or view setup instructions from the [openSUSE Software official project page](https://software.opensuse.org//download.html?project=home%3Aluisbocanegra&package=plasma-smart-video-wallpaper-reborn).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, packed with intelligent power-saving options, highly customizable across multiple monitors and screens.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern Linux desktop environment and proper media codecs installed on your system to function smoothly.
