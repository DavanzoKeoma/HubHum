### What it does in plain English
This project serves as a comprehensive learning map that organizes all the essential topics needed to become a proficient app developer for Apple devices. It breaks down complex concepts into manageable study areas, allowing you to track your progress as you master each skill. By following this structured path, you can efficiently prepare for job interviews or build a strong foundation for your own software projects.

### Who it's for
* Beginners who want to start a career in mobile app development.
* Experienced developers looking to refresh their knowledge or prepare for technical interviews.

### Key features
* **Structured Learning Path:** A logical sequence of topics that guides you from beginner concepts to advanced expertise.
* **Visual and Text Guides:** Includes both easy-to-read diagrams and detailed text documents to suit different learning styles.
* **Curated Resources:** Links to high-quality study materials for every topic on the roadmap.
* **Progress Tracking:** Allows you to mark off completed sections so you can see exactly how far you have come.

### How to install (Download)
This project is a documentation-based guide rather than a software application. You can access the full roadmap directly through the official website:
* [View the iOS Developer Roadmap](https://github.com/BohdanOrlov/iOS-Developer-Roadmap)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, community-driven, and provides a clear sense of direction for learners.
* **Cons:** Requires self-discipline to follow the path and does not include interactive lessons or built-in exercises.
