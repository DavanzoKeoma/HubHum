### What it does in plain English
eSpeak NG turns written text into spoken words using a small, efficient computer program. It supports over 100 different languages and accents, making it a versatile tool for hearing written content. Because it is designed to be compact, it works smoothly on many different types of devices.

### Who it's for
* People who use screen-reading software to navigate their computers.
* Language learners who want to hear how words and sentences sound in different languages.

### Key features
* **Wide Language Support:** Includes voices for over 100 languages and accents.
* **Small Footprint:** The entire program and its data take up very little space on your device.
* **Flexible Output:** Can read text aloud directly or save the speech as a digital audio file.
* **Customizable Voices:** Allows you to adjust the characteristics of the voices to suit your preferences.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works on Windows, Linux, and Android, and can be integrated into other programs.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, very lightweight, and supports a massive range of languages.
* **Cons:** The synthesized voice sounds more robotic compared to modern, large-scale AI voice models.
