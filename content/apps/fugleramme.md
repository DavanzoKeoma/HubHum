### What it does in plain English
Fugleramme uses a microphone to listen for bird calls in your garden and identifies the species in real time. It then creates a digital collage using authentic, hand-cut natural history illustrations from the 1800s. You can display these beautiful, live-updating collages on a dedicated e-ink frame, a spare tablet, or your television.

### Who it's for
* Nature enthusiasts who want to track local bird activity without needing to look outside.
* Home automation hobbyists looking for a creative, aesthetic project for their Raspberry Pi or spare hardware.

### Key features
* **Real-time identification:** Uses local audio analysis to detect bird species as they visit your area.
* **Vintage aesthetic:** Features a curated collection of nearly 1,200 high-quality, non-AI historical illustrations.
* **Flexible display:** Works on almost any screen, including low-power e-ink panels, old tablets, or standard computer monitors.
* **Privacy-focused:** Runs entirely on your own equipment without needing to send data to external services.
* **Customizable:** Allows you to choose your location and language to ensure the birds shown are relevant to your region.

### How to install (Download)
Fugleramme is designed to run on a Raspberry Pi or within a container on your existing home server. 
* **For Raspberry Pi:** Run the official installation script in your terminal: `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/arnegiacomo/fugleramme/main/install.sh | bash`
* **For Docker/Containers:** Use the official image `ghcr.io/arnegiacomo/fugleramme` with your container management software.
* **Full Documentation:** For step-by-step setup guides, hardware lists, and configuration, visit the [Official Documentation](https://arnegiacomo.dev/fugleramme/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Beautiful, non-AI artwork; works fully offline; supports a wide range of hardware; highly customizable.
* **Cons:** Requires a microphone and some basic setup; best results require a compatible e-ink display for the "frame" look.
