## Quick Answer: Navidrome vs spotify-tui

By contrast, **spotify-tui** runs as a **CLI Tool** on **Windows, macOS, Linux** (19,434 GitHub stars): Manage your music player with a lightning-fast interface that runs entirely inside your text terminal.
