### What it does in plain English
Docspell acts as a central hub for all your important paperwork, whether it comes from a scanner, your email inbox, or digital files on your computer. It uses smart technology to read your documents, automatically suggesting labels and dates so you don't have to type everything in by hand. Once organized, you can use a powerful search tool to find any specific document in seconds, no matter where it came from.

### Who it's for
* **Home users and families** who want to digitize and organize household bills, medical records, and personal correspondence.
* **Small business owners or freelancers** who need a reliable way to manage invoices and client documents without complex software.

### Key features
* **Smart Organization:** Uses machine learning to guess tags and identify important details like dates and sender names automatically.
* **Unified Search:** Provides a single search bar to find information across all your uploaded files and emails.
* **OCR Support:** Automatically converts images of text into searchable documents so you can find content inside scanned pages.
* **Flexible Access:** Includes a mobile-friendly web interface and a dedicated mobile app for uploading documents on the go.
* **Privacy Focused:** Keeps your data under your control by allowing you to host the system on your own hardware or server.
