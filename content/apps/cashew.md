### What it does in plain English
Cashew is a personal finance application designed to help you stay on top of your money without complicated spreadsheets. It lets you create custom budgets, log everyday purchases, and watch your progress toward savings goals. With support for multiple currencies, recurring bills, and automatic data syncing, keeping your finances organized becomes completely effortless.

### Who it's for
* Individuals and families wanting an easy way to monitor monthly spending and stick to savings goals.
* Freelancers and multi-currency earners who need to manage finances across different accounts and currencies.
* Privacy-conscious users looking for an open-source tool with biometric security and optional cloud backups.

### Key features
* **Custom Budgets and Time Periods:** Set up flexible budgets tailored to monthly, weekly, or special travel timeframes.
* **Transaction Management:** Easily categorize expenses, track upcoming bills, handle debts, and attach custom tags or search filters.
* **Multi-Currency Support:** Effortlessly switch between accounts and currencies with automated, up-to-date conversion rates.
* **Enhanced Security:** Protect your private financial records using biometric locks and secure Google sign-in.
* **Material You Design:** Enjoy a modern, responsive user interface with customizable accent colors and full light and dark modes.
