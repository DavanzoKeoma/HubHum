### What it does in plain English
This tool lets you search for a specific username or person across more than a thousand different social media websites and online platforms simultaneously. It runs intelligent checks to verify whether a profile actually exists while minimizing false alarms. You can view extracted details, metadata, and even capture screenshots of active pages to assist with online investigations.

### Who it's for
* Security researchers conducting open-source investigations
* Journalists verifying online identities and public records
* Privacy-conscious individuals checking their own digital footprint

### Key features
* Scans over a thousand online networks and social platforms
* Intelligent rating system to filter out false profile matches
* Extracts metadata, patterns, and visual profile information
* Exports clean reports in structured formats for your records
* Command-line interface and local web dashboard options
