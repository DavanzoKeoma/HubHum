### What it does in plain English
OpenCove is a spatial workspace application designed for people who collaborate with artificial intelligence tools every day. Instead of hiding your projects inside separate browser tabs or linear chat logs, it places your active assistants, command-line terminals, task trackers, and notes onto a single infinite visual board. This setup lets you monitor parallel progress, keep all project details in clear view, and resume your exact layout whenever you return.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and technical researchers managing multiple automated coding agents simultaneously
* Power users who want to replace messy tab switching with a spatial, board-based project layout
* Anyone looking to keep terminal outputs, task lists, and notes organized in a single persistent view

### Key features
* **Infinite spatial canvas**: Arrange your terminals, notes, tasks, and assistant chats freely across an expansive board.
* **Built for command-line tools**: Optimized to run multiple developer agents and terminal processes side by side.
* **Persistent workspaces**: Automatically saves your board layout, terminal outputs, and session states across restarts.
* **Space archives**: Snapshot and revisit previous workspace layouts whenever you need to jump back into older project phases.
* **Global search and control**: Quickly search across your entire canvas and manage active sessions from a central hub.
