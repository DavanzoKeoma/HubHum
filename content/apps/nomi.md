### What it does in plain English
Nomi is a desktop application that acts as a video production studio powered by artificial intelligence. You simply describe the story or film you want to make, and its built-in assistant breaks down the narrative into individual shots, generates the visual elements, and arranges everything on a real editing timeline. Your projects and settings remain entirely on your computer, meaning you keep complete control over your files and prompts.

### Who it's for
* Independent creators and filmmakers looking to storyboard and generate video clips efficiently.
* Digital artists who want to coordinate multiple generation models under a single timeline workflow.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer a local application without mandatory cloud accounts or subscriptions.

### Key features
* **Storyboard Control:** Organizes your narrative into distinct shots where you can lock character faces and settings for consistency.
* **Flexible Model Integration:** Connects with mainstream video generation engines or your own local setups directly.
* **Timeline Editing:** Lets you arrange generated clips, review takes, and export finished sequences as standard video files.
* **Local-First Design:** Stores all prompts, projects, and media files directly on your hard drive without tracking your work.
