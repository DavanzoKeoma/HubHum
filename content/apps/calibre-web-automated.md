### What it does in plain English
Calibre-Web-Automated acts as a smart manager for your digital books, automatically sorting and labeling files as you add them to your collection. It handles tricky tasks like converting file formats, fixing broken book data, and cleaning up cover images without you needing to lift a finger. By keeping your library organized and synced, it ensures your reading experience is smooth across all your devices.

### Who it's for
*   **Book Lovers:** People who have large collections of digital books and want them perfectly organized without manual effort.
*   **Self-Hosting Enthusiasts:** Users who prefer to run their own private library server at home rather than relying on commercial services.

### Key features
*   **Automatic Ingest:** Simply drop a book file into a folder, and the system automatically adds it to your library.
*   **Smart Conversion:** Automatically converts books into your preferred format (like EPUB or PDF) during the import process.
*   **Metadata Enforcement:** Ensures that the cover art and book details you see in your library are actually saved into the book files themselves.
*   **Duplicate Detection:** Uses smart matching to find and merge duplicate books, keeping your library clean.
*   **eReader Syncing:** Easily sends books to your reading devices and keeps your progress synced across different platforms.

### How to install (Download)
This application is designed to be run as a containerized service. You can find the official setup instructions and the required configuration template on the [Official Project Page](https://github.com/crocodilestick/Calibre-Web-Automated).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open source, saves massive amounts of time on manual organization, and works with almost any eReader.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of setting up server software, and initial configuration can take a little time to get just right.
