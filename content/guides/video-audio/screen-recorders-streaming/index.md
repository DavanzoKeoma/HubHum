### All Qualifying Software in This Topic

- **[ijkplayer](/HubHum/apps/ijkplayer/)** — Smoothly play online and local videos on mobile devices with hardware acceleration and robust format support. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (GPL v2))*
- **[Animeko](/HubHum/apps/animeko/)** — Discover, stream, and keep track of your favorite animated series with synchronized cloud progress and community comments. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (AGPL v3))*
- **[GSYVideoPlayer](/HubHum/apps/gsyvideoplayer/)** — An advanced video playback toolkit for mobile devices supporting multiple video engines, caching, and custom visual filters. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (Apache 2.0))*
- **[Kap](/HubHum/apps/kap/)** — An open-source screen recorder for macOS that lets you capture your desktop with ease using a clean interface. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[ani-cli](/HubHum/apps/ani-cli/)** — A simple tool that lets you search for and stream your favorite anime directly from your computer terminal. *(CLI Tool · License: Open Source (GPL v3))*
- **[Streamlink](/HubHum/apps/streamlink/)** — Streamlink lets you watch live video content directly in your favorite media player instead of using resource-heavy web browsers. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (BSD))*
- **[HGhub](/HubHum/apps/hghub/)** — HGhub is a flexible media player that lets you organize and watch content from various sources in one unified, easy-to-use interface. *(Desktop App · License: Custom Open License)*
- **[OpenScreen](/HubHum/apps/openscreen/)** — OpenScreen is a free, open-source tool that lets you record your screen and turn your captures into polished product demos and walkthroughs. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[Snow Shot](/HubHum/apps/snow-shot/)** — Capture your computer screen, add helpful annotations, and extract text instantly with a free and open-source tool for Windows and Mac. *(Desktop App · License: Custom Open License)*
- **[anyRTC-RTMP-OpenSource](/HubHum/apps/anyrtc-rtmp-opensource/)** — Build live video broadcasting and playback applications for mobile and desktop devices with this open-source streaming toolkit. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (GPL v3))*
- **[Seanime](/HubHum/apps/seanime/)** — Seanime organizes your local media library and provides a unified interface for streaming anime and reading manga on any device. *(Self-Hosted Web App · License: Open Source (GPL v3))*
- **[Yattee](/HubHum/apps/yattee/)** — Enjoy seamless video playback across iOS, tvOS, and macOS with built-in ad and sponsor skipping. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (AGPL v3))*
