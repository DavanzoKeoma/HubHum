### What it does in plain English
BookOrbit acts as a central hub for your entire collection of digital books, comics, and audiobooks. It keeps track of your reading progress, notes, and highlights, ensuring you can pick up exactly where you left off on any device. Because you host it yourself, you maintain full control over your private reading data and files.

### Who it's for
*   **Avid readers** who want a unified way to manage ebooks, audiobooks, and comics in one place.
*   **Digital collectors** who prefer to own their library files rather than relying on commercial bookstore clouds.

### Key features
*   **Universal Sync:** Automatically keeps your reading position and notes in sync between web browsers, e-readers, and mobile devices.
*   **Native Mobile Support:** Includes a dedicated iPhone and Apple Watch app for reading and listening on the go, even offline.
*   **Wide Format Support:** Handles almost any file type, including EPUB, PDF, comic formats, and various high-quality audio formats.
*   **Smart Organization:** Automatically fetches book covers and details from multiple online sources to keep your library looking professional.
*   **Reading Insights:** Provides detailed statistics, reading streaks, and achievements to help you track your reading habits over time.

### How to install (Download)
BookOrbit is a web-based platform designed to be hosted on your own computer or server.
*   **Get the iPhone/Apple Watch App:** [Download from the Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bookorbit-the-official-app/id6811807346)
*   **Self-Hosting Guide:** Follow the [Official Installation Instructions](https://bookorbit.app/installation) to set up your own server using Docker.
*   **Try the Demo:** You can explore the interface without installing anything by visiting the [Live Demo](https://demo.bookorbit.app/magic?token=2d92cb900e184cf0eb8b11f72cffc6011673d1016e1b300d750eb3d76abc1572).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, excellent cross-device synchronization, and support for a wide variety of file formats.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic technical knowledge to set up and maintain your own server; network-attached storage is not supported.
