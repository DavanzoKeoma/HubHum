### What it does in plain English
Immich lets you automatically back up all the photos and videos from your phone directly to your own computer or home server. It organizes your memories using smart face recognition, locations, and search tools so you can find any picture in seconds. You can also securely share albums with family and friends without relying on big tech cloud companies. ### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious families who want complete control over their personal photo albums and memories without paying monthly cloud storage fees.
* Photography enthusiasts who need a lightning-fast way to browse, organize, and search through thousands of high-resolution RAW image files.

### Key features
* Automatic background photo and video backup the moment you open the app or connect to Wi-Fi.
* Smart facial recognition and object search to instantly locate specific people, places, or things.
* Multi-user support with private libraries and shared family albums for everyone in your household.
