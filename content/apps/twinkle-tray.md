### What it does in plain English
Twinkle Tray solves the problem of not being able to easily change the brightness of external monitors through Windows. It places a small icon in your taskbar that opens a simple slider menu for every screen you have connected. You can adjust each monitor individually or change them all at the same time with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Office workers who use multiple monitors and need to adjust lighting throughout the day.
* Home users who want to quickly dim their screens in the evening without navigating through complex monitor buttons.

### Key features
* **Simple controls:** Adds easy-to-use brightness sliders directly to your system tray.
* **Multi-monitor support:** Manage the brightness of all your screens from one convenient menu.
* **Automatic adjustments:** Set your monitors to change brightness based on the time of day.
* **Custom shortcuts:** Create keyboard hotkeys to dim or brighten your displays instantly.
* **Seamless design:** The app automatically matches the look and theme of your Windows taskbar.

* **Pros:** Completely free to use, lightweight, and integrates perfectly with Windows 10 and 11.
* **Cons:** Requires monitors that support standard communication protocols (DDC/CI), which may need to be enabled in your monitor's physical settings menu.
