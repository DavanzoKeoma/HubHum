### What it does in plain English
Hatamoto is a complete website management application that combines writing tools, page design layouts, and traffic analytics into a single software package. It allows you to build and run professional blogs or business sites without needing complex third-party software stacks or external web services. You can easily import existing websites, track how people and digital assistants view your content, and publish updates securely.

### Who it's for
* Independent bloggers and writers who want a fast, distraction-free publishing space.
* Small business owners looking to run a secure, self-hosted website without maintenance headaches.
* Content creators migrating from older publishing systems who need clean URL redirects and SEO tracking.

### Key features
* Built-in visual page editor with drag-and-drop sections and responsive preview tools.
* Comprehensive visitor analytics that separate human readers from automated AI assistant crawlers.
* Automated import assistant to migrate existing content smoothly from legacy blogging platforms.
* Sandboxed plugin and theme support for safe customization without touching low-level code.
* High-performance page caching and image optimization working right out of the box.
