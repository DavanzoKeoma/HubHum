### What it does in plain English
Proxyman acts as a visual window into the internet traffic flowing through your Mac, iPhone, and Android devices. It captures every web request and response, allowing you to see exactly what data is being sent and received. This makes it easy to spot errors, slow connections, or security issues without needing to read difficult technical logs.

### Who it's for
*   **App Developers:** People building software who need to verify that their apps are sending and receiving data correctly.
*   **Web Designers:** Professionals checking how websites load and ensuring that all images and scripts are working as intended.
*   **Tech-Savvy Users:** Individuals who want to understand why a specific website or app is failing to connect on their devices.

### Key features
*   **Multi-Device Capture:** Connect your Mac, iPhone, and Android devices to view their network activity in one place.
*   **Visual Interface:** A clean, modern dashboard that displays web traffic in an easy-to-read format rather than raw text.
*   **Traffic Manipulation:** Tools to pause, modify, or block specific web requests to test how an app behaves under different conditions.
*   **Quick Search:** A command palette that lets you find specific requests or responses instantly using your keyboard.
*   **Automatic Setup:** One-click configuration to start capturing traffic from common development environments like NodeJS and Python.
