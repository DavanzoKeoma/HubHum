### What it does in plain English
FoloUp is an intelligent assistant designed to help companies streamline their hiring process through automated voice interviews. It automatically generates tailored interview questions from job descriptions and shares unique links with candidates. The built-in artificial intelligence then conducts natural, conversational phone or web interviews, analyzing responses to provide detailed performance insights.

### Who it's for
* **Hiring Managers and HR Teams:** Professionals looking to save time on initial candidate screening rounds.
* **Startup Founders:** Small business owners who need to conduct efficient interviews without a dedicated recruiting department.
* **Recruitment Agencies:** Agencies managing high volumes of job applicants who want consistent preliminary evaluations.

### Key features
* **Automated Question Generation:** Instantly creates custom interview questions based on any provided job description.
* **One-Click Sharing:** Generates unique, secure interview links to send directly to candidates.
* **Conversational AI Voice Calls:** Conducts dynamic, natural-sounding voice interviews that adapt to candidate answers.
* **Smart Analysis & Scoring:** Evaluates responses automatically to provide comprehensive performance insights and grades.
* **Comprehensive Dashboard:** Tracks all candidate statuses, recordings, and performance statistics in one centralized view.
