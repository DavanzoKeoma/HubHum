### What it does in plain English
Feynman acts as a personal research partner that handles the heavy lifting of gathering and analyzing information. It can scan vast amounts of scientific literature and web sources to provide you with clear summaries, consensus reports, and critical reviews. By automating the search and verification process, it saves you hours of manual reading and data organization.

### Who it's for
*   Academic students and researchers who need to quickly synthesize literature reviews.
*   Professionals and curious learners who want to dive deep into complex technical topics without getting lost in search results.

### Key features
*   **Deep Research:** Performs planned investigations across multiple sources with built-in citation checks.
*   **Literature Reviews:** Identifies consensus, disagreements, and open questions within a specific field of study.
*   **Paper Critique:** Provides structured feedback on drafts and compares research claims against public data.
*   **Versatile Sources:** Accesses major scientific databases, academic archives, and local documents to ensure comprehensive results.
*   **Customizable Workflows:** Allows you to add your own tools and libraries to tailor the research process to your specific needs.

### How to install (Download)
Feynman is designed to be installed via your computer's command line interface. Please visit the [official installation guide](https://feynman.is/docs/getting-started/installation) for the most reliable setup instructions for your operating system.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely open source, handles complex multi-step research tasks, and provides verifiable citations.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic comfort with a command line interface and setup of a model provider account to function.
