### What it does in plain English
Soul of Waifu is a personal desktop companion application that lets you interact with customizable AI characters through text, voice, and visual avatars. It remembers your conversations over time, builds a continuous shared history, and can even help manage your computer tasks or run tabletop roleplaying adventures. Everything runs entirely on your own computer by default, keeping your conversations private and completely under your control.

### Who it's for
* **Roleplay Enthusiasts:** People who enjoy deep, persistent storytelling and interactive narrative adventures with consistent characters.
* **Customization Fans:** Users who love personalizing visual avatars, setting up custom character traits, and configuring local artificial intelligence tools.
* **Desktop Productivity Explorers:** Anyone curious about having an interactive assistant live right on their screen to help with everyday tasks.

### Key features
* **Persistent Memory:** Characters remember past conversations, mood changes, and shared experiences over long periods of time.
* **Animated Avatars:** Support for dynamic 2D and 3D character models that react and move as you talk.
* **Voice and Text Chat:** Speak out loud or type messages with real-time audio responses and speech recognition.
* **Tabletop Roleplay Engine:** Built-in tools for running structured storytelling games complete with dice rolls and interactive world states.
* **Autonomous Desktop Assistant:** Optional screen and file interaction features that let your companion help with computer tasks safely.
