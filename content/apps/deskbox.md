### What it does in plain English
DeskBox is a lightweight tool that adds helpful, modern widgets directly to your Windows desktop. It lets you group files, manage tasks, and keep track of information like weather or clipboard history without replacing your standard desktop experience. Because it works locally on your computer, your data stays private and your system remains fast and responsive.

### Who it's for
*   People who want a cleaner, more organized desktop without moving their files into a new, complex system.
*   Users who enjoy having quick access to notes, music controls, and task lists right on their wallpaper.

### Key features
*   **Flexible File Widgets:** Create organized areas on your desktop that act like folders, allowing you to manage files exactly where they are.
*   **Unified Search:** A quick, powerful search tool that finds your files, notes, and apps in one place.
*   **Handy Productivity Tools:** Includes a clipboard history, task lists, weather updates, and media controls.
*   **Native Design:** The widgets are built to match the look and feel of Windows 11, so they blend in perfectly with your system.
*   **Privacy Focused:** Everything runs locally on your machine, meaning no accounts or cloud subscriptions are required to use the core features.
