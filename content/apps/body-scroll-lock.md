### What it does in plain English
When you open a pop-up window, menu, or side panel on a website, the background page often moves around annoyingly under your fingertips. This handy tool freezes that background page completely, even on tricky mobile touchscreens. Best of all, it keeps your pop-up window or menu scrolling smoothly so visitors never get stuck.

### Who it's for
* Frontend web developers building responsive websites with modal windows.
* Mobile web designers striving for native-feeling app interactions on iOS and Android.
* Creators building custom navigation menus, image galleries, and sliding drawers.

### Key features
* Freezes background scrolling without disabling movement inside your target pop-up window.
* Works seamlessly across mobile and tablet devices, including tricky iOS touchscreens.
* Compatible with plain JavaScript as well as popular tools like React, Angular, and Vue.
* Reserves scrollbar width automatically to prevent annoying layout jumps or flickering.
* Supports nested elements so multiple overlapping windows behave correctly.
