### What it does in plain English
Shadowsocks-android is a specialized network tunneling tool designed for Android devices. It helps users route their mobile internet traffic through a secure proxy server to maintain privacy. The app runs quietly in the background, integrating smoothly with the system network settings.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious mobile users looking to secure their data traffic on public Wi-Fi networks.
* People needing a reliable way to connect to remote private servers and secure networks from an Android device.
* Android TV and Chrome OS users who require secure proxy tunneling on alternate form factors.

### Key features
* Lightweight background service that routes all system or selected app traffic securely.
* Built-in support for multiple connection configurations and encryption protocols.
* User-friendly interface tailored specifically for Android phones, tablets, and TVs.
* Open-source code base ensuring full transparency and community audits.
* Low battery consumption and minimal impact on overall system performance.
