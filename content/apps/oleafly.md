### What it does in plain English
Oleafly is a desktop workspace that lets researchers write, format, and compile complex academic documents without relying on costly online platforms. It supports multiple typesetting formats right out of the box and compiles your files securely on your own computer. You can also connect to a shared server with your team to review changes, leave comments, and edit documents simultaneously.

### Who it's for
* **Academic Researchers:** Perfect for writing journal articles, conference papers, and extensive theses with rigorous reference checking.
* **University Students:** Great for formatting thesis projects, lab reports, and academic presentations using standard document layouts.
* **Research Groups:** Ideal for lab teams looking to collaborate on shared papers and manage version history securely.

### Key features
* Compile academic documents locally with built-in typesetting engines and instant preview capabilities.
* Collaborate in real time with co-authors using shared cursors, comments, and project chat.
* Connect your reference manager to cite sources instantly as you type and search scholarly archives.
* Run automated preflight checks to spot missing figures, broken references, or formatting issues before submission.
* Keep every project safely stored in a local version control system with automated checkpoints.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, works offline without requiring an account, and includes robust reference and formatting tools.
* **Cons:** Advanced formatting languages can take some time to learn if you are entirely new to academic typesetting.
