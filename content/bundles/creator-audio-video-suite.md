### Why this curated guide
Equip creators and podcasters with an integrated, zero-watermark production stack using official desktop releases.

### Featured highlights
* **[Shotcut](/HubHum/apps/shotcut/)** (*Desktop App*): Shotcut is a free, powerful tool that lets you trim, edit, and polish your video projects on any computer.
* **[Olive](/HubHum/apps/olive/)** (*Desktop App*): Olive is a free tool that lets you cut, arrange, and polish your video clips into professional-looking projects on your computer.
* **[LosslessCut](/HubHum/apps/losslesscut/)** (*Desktop App*): Trim, split, and merge large videos in seconds with zero quality loss and no slow re-encoding wait times.
* **[HandBrake](/HubHum/apps/handbrake/)** (*Desktop App*): Convert and optimize your video files effortlessly so they play smoothly on your phone, tablet, computer, or television screen.
* **[Audacity](/HubHum/apps/audacity/)** (*Desktop App*): Record your voice, remove background noise, mix tracks, and export podcasts or music free on Windows, Mac, and Linux.
* **[WhisperSubTranslate](/HubHum/apps/whispersubtranslate/)** (*Desktop App*): Extract accurate subtitles from your videos and translate them into multiple languages right on your computer without sharing data or needing subscriptions.
