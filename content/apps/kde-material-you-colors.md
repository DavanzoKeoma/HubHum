### What it does in plain English
This utility automatically reads your current computer wallpaper and extracts a harmonious color palette using Google's color design system. It instantly transforms your entire desktop interface, window borders, and terminal windows to match your wallpaper's aesthetic. Whenever you change your background image, your system colors adapt on the fly without requiring manual adjustment.

### Who it's for
* Linux users running the KDE Plasma desktop environment who love personalized aesthetics.
* Customization enthusiasts who want their system themes and terminal applications to match their background images.
* Users looking for an automated way to switch between light and dark styling based on their wallpaper mood.

### Key features
* Automatically generates matching color schemes whenever your desktop wallpaper changes.
* Includes a handy control panel widget for easy on-the-fly toggling and configuration.
* Supports colorizing terminal applications and text highlighters alongside your main desktop interface.
* Offers fine-tuned controls for adjusting theme vibrancy, contrast, and dark or light modes independently.
* Works smoothly with popular scheduling tools and transparent window style addons.

* **Pros:** Free and open source, fully automated color extraction, extensive customization options for terminal and desktop apps.
