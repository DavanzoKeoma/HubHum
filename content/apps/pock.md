### What it does in plain English
Pock brings your favorite controls, apps, and live information directly onto your MacBook's Touch Bar. It lets you interact with your desktop dock, media playback, weather, and system settings without taking up space on your main screen. You can fully customize which mini-tools appear and arrange them to fit your daily routine.

### Who it's for
* MacBook Pro owners with a Touch Bar who want to make better use of that touch strip.
* People who love keeping their main screen clutter-free while maintaining instant access to everyday tools.
* Users looking to customize their keyboard strip with media controls, weather updates, and quick app launchers.

### Key features
* **Dock widget:** Access your computer's dock apps and live notification badges right from your keyboard.
* **Now Playing widget:** See current song info and control your music with simple touch gestures.
* **Status & Control Center:** Monitor your battery, Wi-Fi, time, screen brightness, and volume instantly.
* **Weather widget:** Check current local forecasts at a glance without opening extra browser tabs.
* **Custom layouts:** Easily rearrange, add, or remove widgets to create your ideal setup.

### How to install (Download)
1. Go to the [official website](https://pock.app) and click download.
2. Unzip the downloaded archive file by double-clicking on it.
3. Move the extracted application file into your `/Applications` folder, then open it.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, highly customizable, keeps your main screen clean.
* **Cons:** Requires a MacBook model equipped with a physical Touch Bar, advanced configuration options take a few moments to learn.
