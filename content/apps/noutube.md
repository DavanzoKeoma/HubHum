### What it does in plain English
NouTube brings video and music streaming together into a single application designed to eliminate unwanted ads and interruptions. It allows you to keep listening to your favorite tracks and podcasts even when your screen is locked or you are using other programs. You can organize your favorite channels, manage your watch history, and download content locally without needing to log into an account.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want uninterrupted listening sessions in the background while multitasking on their devices.
* Privacy-conscious viewers who prefer managing subscriptions and watching media without signing into an account.
* People who want to reduce visual clutter by hiding short-form video distractions and customizing their viewing layout.

### Key features
* Blocks video and banner advertisements automatically during playback.
* Supports background audio playback so you can listen while using other tools or with the screen turned off.
* Allows offline saving of videos and tracks for viewing without an active internet connection.
* Built-in feed reader to follow favorite video creators through RSS updates.
* Clean interface options to hide short-form video distractions and tailor the layout using custom styles.
