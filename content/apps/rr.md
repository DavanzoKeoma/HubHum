### What it does in plain English
This tool records everything your program does while running on your computer. When something goes wrong, you can replay the exact same execution sequence backward in time to see where things broke. It turns frustrating guesswork into a straightforward visual tracking process.

### Who it's for
* Software developers trying to isolate tricky, hard-to-reproduce bugs in complex applications.
* Systems engineers analyzing performance bottlenecks and low-level thread interactions.

### Key features
* **Record and Replay:** Capture exact execution steps so you can rerun them identically on demand.
* **Reverse Execution:** Step backward through code history rather than just moving forward.
* **Hardware Support:** Works efficiently with modern processor watchpoints and performance counters.
* **Multi-Threaded Tracking:** Follows complex trees of processes and threads smoothly.
