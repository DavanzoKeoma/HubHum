### What it does in plain English
NeutralPress is a modern publishing platform that combines the ease of traditional blogging tools with the high performance of modern web technology. You can write posts, design custom pages visually by dragging elements around, and organize your media files without needing any technical server knowledge. It updates your pages instantly when you make changes while keeping the lightning-fast loading speeds of a static website.

### Who it's for
* Independent writers and bloggers who want a fast, clean space for their thoughts without complex server maintenance.
* Designers and developers looking to showcase projects, code portfolios, and photo galleries in one unified place.
* Anyone wanting a free, private community and digital portal that they fully own and control.

### Key features
* Visual drag-and-drop page builder with real-time previews and live layout editing.
* Complete writing workspace with support for Markdown formatting, drafts, and version history.
* Built-in visitor analytics tracking popular articles, search keywords, and traffic sources.
* Multi-user account management with secure sign-in options, permissions, and visitor registration.
* Automated link directory checking and healthy connection monitoring for your favorite sites.
