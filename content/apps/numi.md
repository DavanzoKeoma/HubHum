### What it does in plain English
Numi acts as a smart assistant that performs calculations based on how you naturally speak or write. Instead of typing complex formulas, you can simply type phrases like "20 dollars minus 5 percent" or "today plus two weeks." It instantly provides the result, making math feel like a conversation rather than a chore.

### Who it's for
* **Office workers** who need to quickly calculate discounts, taxes, or currency conversions.
* **Students and planners** who want to manage dates and time-based calculations without using a calendar app.

### Key features
* **Natural Language Processing:** Understands descriptive text, units of measurement, and currency symbols.
* **Date and Time Math:** Easily add or subtract days, weeks, or months from specific dates.
* **Unit Conversion:** Automatically handles conversions between different measurement systems like inches to centimeters.
* **Clean Interface:** A distraction-free design that stays out of your way while you work.
