## Quick Answer: n8n vs Open WebUI

Comparing **n8n** and **Open WebUI** comes down to how you prefer to work. **n8n** stands out as a top community favorite (211,468 GitHub stars) and runs as a **Cloud Web App**: Connect your favorite online tools and artificial intelligence models to automate tedious tasks without writing complex code. Meanwhile, **Open WebUI** (159,122 GitHub stars) gives you another trusted free option: Run smart artificial intelligence models completely offline or connect to cloud services with a friendly, feature-packed web control panel.
