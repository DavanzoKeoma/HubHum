### What it does in plain English
Feishin is a sleek music player designed to stream songs directly from your own personal home server. It connects smoothly to popular music server systems so you can organize, browse, and listen to your audio files from a single beautiful application. Whether you are at your desk or on the go, it gives you complete control over your personal listening library without relying on commercial streaming giants.

### Who it's for
* People who host their own music libraries on personal servers and want a polished, modern app to listen to them.
* Music enthusiasts who prefer keeping complete ownership of their audio files and listening history.
* Users looking for a customizable alternative to mainstream cloud-based music subscription services.

### Key features
* Connects seamlessly to popular self-hosted music servers like Navidrome, Jellyfin, and OpenSubsonic systems.
* Modern user interface built for smooth navigation through large albums, artist profiles, and playlists.
* Built-in support for synchronized and unsynchronized song lyrics as you listen.
* Smart playlist editor to automatically build custom queues and rotation lists.
* Flexible playback engine options including a robust desktop audio backend and a web-based player.
