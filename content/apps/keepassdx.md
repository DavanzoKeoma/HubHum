### What it does in plain English
KeePassDX is a lightweight application for Android that lets you store and organize all your digital keys inside a single encrypted database. You only need to remember one master passphrase or use a special key file to unlock all your stored credentials. It seamlessly fills in forms and login fields across your phone while keeping your personal data completely private on your device.

### Who it's for
* Everyday mobile users tired of remembering dozens of different passwords for various websites and apps.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer storing their confidential data locally rather than trusting cloud servers.
* People looking for a reliable, open-source tool to manage multi-factor authentication codes and passkeys on the go.

### Key features
* Unlocks your password vault instantly using fingerprint or face recognition.
* Automatically fills in login forms inside mobile apps and web browsers.
* Generates and manages one-time verification codes for secure two-factor logins.
* Supports standard database formats compatible with popular computer password managers.
* Operates entirely offline without tracking your information or displaying advertisements.
