### What it does in plain English
py-xiaozhi acts as a central brain for your digital and physical environment, allowing you to control your computer and smart devices using natural voice commands. It processes your speech in real-time to perform tasks like checking the weather, managing applications, or interacting with hardware sensors. Because it is designed to be modular, you can easily extend its capabilities to suit your specific home or office setup.

### Who it's for
*   **Home Automation Enthusiasts:** People who want to build custom voice-controlled setups for their smart home hardware.
*   **Tech Hobbyists:** Users who enjoy experimenting with AI tools on small computers like Raspberry Pi or desktop systems.

### Key features
*   **Real-time Voice Interaction:** Responds to your voice with very low delay for a natural conversation experience.
*   **Smart Device Control:** Connects with physical hardware and sensors to manage your environment.
*   **Multi-modal Understanding:** Can process both audio and visual information to better understand your requests.
*   **Extensible Plugin System:** Easily add new tools and features to customize how the assistant behaves.
*   **Cross-platform Support:** Works reliably on Windows, macOS, and various Linux-based systems.
