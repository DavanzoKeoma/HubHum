### What it does in plain English
SketchyBar replaces the standard top bar on your Mac computer with a blank canvas that you can design from scratch. It responds instantly to commands and scripts running in the background, allowing you to show music tracks, system speeds, or custom icons. Every single element can be moved, resized, or animated to match your personal workflow.

### Who it's for
* Mac power users who love tailoring every detail of their operating system appearance.
* Developers and enthusiasts who enjoy writing small scripts to display real-time computer stats.
* Minimalists seeking a clean, uncluttered desktop layout that hides distractions.

### Key features
* Full control to add, remove, and rearrange any visual element on the screen.
* Smooth built-in animations for expanding menus and changing states.
* Event-driven scripting support to display live updates like weather or battery levels.
* Interactive mouse support for clicking elements to trigger custom actions.
* Lightweight design optimized to run efficiently without slowing down your computer.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, infinite customization possibilities, very lightweight.
* **Cons:** Requires familiarity with command-line tools and scripting to configure properly.
