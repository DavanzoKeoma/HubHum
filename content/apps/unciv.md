### What it does in plain English
Unciv is a retro turn-based strategy game where you guide a civilization from the ancient stone age all the way to the space age. You build cities, manage resources, research new technologies, and command armies on a grid-based map. Because it focuses on clean pixel graphics and streamlined mechanics, it runs smoothly on almost any computer or smartphone.

### Who it's for
* Classic turn-based strategy fans who enjoy deep tactical choices without heavy hardware requirements.
* Players looking for a completely free, open-source game with zero advertisements or hidden purchases.
* Retro gaming enthusiasts who appreciate classic grid-based empire building and map exploration.

### Key features
* Classic turn-based empire building inspired by legendary desktop strategy titles.
* Lightweight design that runs smoothly on older computers, budget phones, and tablets.
* Highly customizable rules and active community-made modifications.
* Playable across multiple operating systems including Windows, Linux, and Android.
* Completely free and open-source with no microtransactions or locked content.
