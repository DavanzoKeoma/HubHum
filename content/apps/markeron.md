### What it does in plain English
MarkerOn is a featherlight desktop application that lets you draw, highlight, and point at anything right on your computer screen. You can instantly activate drawing mode with a quick keyboard shortcut, sketch shapes or notes over any app, and even click straight through your drawings to control underlying windows. It runs quietly in your system tray so it is always ready whenever you need to explain an idea during a meeting or tutorial.

### Who it's for
* Teachers and online tutors who need to point out specific details during live screen-sharing sessions.
* Software developers and product creators recording crisp video demonstrations and software walkthroughs.
* Professionals leading remote meetings who want to visually emphasize key points on their desktop.

### Key features
* **Ultra-lightweight design:** Uses minimal memory and runs quietly in your background without slowing down your computer.
* **Annotate anywhere:** Draw freely over any open application, desktop background, or system taskbar.
* **Click-through capability:** Interact with underlying apps while keeping your ink drawings visible on top.
* **Versatile toolset:** Includes pens, highlighters, arrows, shapes, text boxes, stamps, and an eraser.
* **Keyboard-first workflow:** Quickly switch tools, undo strokes, change colors, or clear the screen using simple hotkeys.
