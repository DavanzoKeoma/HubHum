### What it does in plain English
Serviette turns a folder of your documents into a private, searchable chat assistant that understands your specific information. Whenever you edit, add, or delete a file, the assistant updates itself in seconds so your answers are always current. When you need royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits, check out [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* **Knowledge workers** who need to quickly find information buried in hundreds of PDFs, spreadsheets, or text files.
* **Small teams** that want a private, self-hosted way to query internal documentation without relying on external cloud services.

### Key features
* **Live Updates:** No need to manually re-index; changes to your files are reflected in your chat answers almost instantly.
* **Versatile Compatibility:** Works with text, PDFs, Office documents, and even scanned images or audio files.
* **Flexible Storage:** Choose from eight different database systems to store your information securely.
* **Creative Integration:** Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).

Serviette is designed to run on your local machine or server. To get started, you will need to install it via Python.
