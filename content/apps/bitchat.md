### What it does in plain English
This app provides decentralized messaging that works completely without internet access or cellular data by chaining messages securely through nearby devices over bluetooth. When an internet connection is available, it expands your reach globally using location-based channels and distributed communication networks. You can jump straight into conversations without creating user profiles, handing over phone numbers, or trusting central corporations.

### Who it's for
* Outdoor explorers, travelers, and event-goers who frequently find themselves in remote areas with poor mobile reception.
* Privacy-focused communities seeking direct communication channels free from corporate surveillance and account creation.
* Emergency responders and neighborhood groups preparing for sudden power outages or communication blackouts.

### Key features
* **Offline Bluetooth Mesh**: Sends messages safely from phone to phone across local groups even when cell towers fail.
* **Global Internet Reach**: Connects across the world using internet relays when traditional web access is available.
* **No Account Required**: Protects personal identity completely without phone numbers, passwords, or central servers.
* **Familiar Chat Commands**: Features classic interactive text commands like slash messaging and user lists.
* **Emergency Data Wipe**: Instantly clears all conversation history with a quick triple-tap gesture.
