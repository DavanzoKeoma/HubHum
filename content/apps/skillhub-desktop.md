### What it does in plain English
SkillHub Desktop provides a centralized hub to discover, install, and synchronize custom coding instructions and workflows across various AI-powered development tools. Instead of configuring each assistant separately, you can manage your entire toolkit from one simple interface. It helps developers keep their favorite prompts and shortcuts neatly organized and up to date everywhere they work.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use multiple AI coding assistants daily and want unified control.
* Tech enthusiasts building and sharing custom automation instructions for AI tools.
* Programmers looking to quickly discover new workflow prompts to boost their productivity.

### Key features
* **Catalog Discovery:** Browse and search a wide collection of specialized programming instructions and skills.
* **One-Click Installation:** Add new skills to multiple coding assistants simultaneously without manual file copying.
* **Custom Creation:** Build your own personalized routines and prompts using built-in helper tools.
* **Seamless Syncing:** Keep your favorite shortcuts and instructions consistent across different programming environments.
* **Organized Collections:** Group your preferred instructions into neat categories for quick access.
