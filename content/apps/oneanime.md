### What it does in plain English
oneAnime is a clean, modern application designed for streaming Japanese animated shows without any disruptive advertisements. It connects directly to popular catalog sources so you can browse release schedules, search for specific titles, and watch episodes smoothly. It also integrates community chat comments directly onto the screen so you can read live reactions while watching.

### Who it's for
* Anime enthusiasts who want an uncluttered, ad-free streaming experience on their desktop or mobile device.
* Viewers who enjoy reading real-time on-screen community comments while watching episodes.
* Users looking for a customizable media player with variable playback speeds and hardware acceleration.

### Key features
* **Ad-Free Streaming:** Watch your favorite series without commercial interruptions or pop-ups.
* **Community Comments:** Read live on-screen comments synchronized with your video playback.
* **Release Schedule:** Keep track of newly aired episodes with an integrated calendar.
* **Customizable Playback:** Adjust playback speeds, enable hardware acceleration, and match high-refresh-rate displays.
* **Modern Design:** Enjoy a clean interface built following modern visual design guidelines.
