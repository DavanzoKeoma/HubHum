### Why this curated guide
Keep full ownership of family memories, notes, websites, and server panels while running cleanly with 1-click cloud hosting or local Docker.

### Featured highlights
* **[Immich](/HubHum/apps/immich/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Manage and back up your photos and videos on your own private cloud storage without sharing your personal memories.
* **[CasaOS](/HubHum/apps/casaos/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): CasaOS turns any computer into a simple, personal data center for managing your files and smart home devices with ease.
* **[Coolify](/HubHum/apps/coolify/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Coolify lets you host and manage your own websites, databases, and apps on any server without needing expensive commercial platforms.
* **[Ghost](/HubHum/apps/ghost/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Launch a fast blog, digital magazine, or paid newsletter while keeping complete ownership of your reader list.
* **[Linkwarden](/HubHum/apps/linkwarden/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): An open-source tool to collect, read, annotate, and archive important web pages and articles in one secure place.
* **[Memos](/HubHum/apps/memos/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Memos is an open-source, self-hosted note-taking app that brings your everyday thoughts together in a personal timeline while keeping your data.
