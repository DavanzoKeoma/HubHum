### What it does in plain English
OpenNOW provides a dedicated desktop interface for your existing cloud gaming subscription. It allows you to browse your game library, adjust streaming quality settings, and launch titles directly from your computer. The app is built to be faster and more flexible than standard web-based alternatives.

### Who it's for
*   Gamers who want a more responsive, native desktop experience for their cloud gaming library.
*   Users who prefer to switch between keyboard-and-mouse controls and controller-based console layouts within a single app.

### Key features
*   **Native Performance:** Uses a custom streaming engine designed for smooth gameplay and low latency.
*   **Flexible Layouts:** Seamlessly switch between a standard desktop view and a controller-friendly console interface.
*   **Media Tools:** Built-in options to capture screenshots and record your gameplay sessions directly to your device.
*   **Custom Settings:** Full control over resolution, frame rate, and streaming quality to match your hardware.
