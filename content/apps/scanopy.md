### What it does in plain English
Scanopy acts as a digital mapmaker for your computer network, automatically discovering every device and connection. It constantly scans your setup to ensure your network diagrams stay accurate, even as you add or move equipment. Instead of manually updating charts, you get a live, visual overview of your physical hardware, logical connections, and software services.

### Who it's for
* **IT Administrators:** Professionals who need to keep accurate records of complex office or data center networks without spending hours on manual documentation.
* **Managed Service Providers (MSPs):** Teams that manage networks for multiple clients and need a reliable, professional way to provide clear documentation.

### Key features
* **Live Topology Maps:** Automatically generates physical and logical network diagrams that update as your network changes.
* **Workload & App Tracking:** Visualizes how virtual machines, containers, and software services are connected and where they live.
* **Device Inventory:** Keeps a searchable list of every host, IP address, and hardware detail on your network.
* **Audit-Ready Snapshots:** Records the state of your network at specific times, which is helpful for security compliance and troubleshooting.
* **Flexible Sharing:** Allows you to export maps for reports or embed live, read-only views into your team's internal documentation.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Eliminates manual documentation, provides real-time accuracy, and keeps all your data private on your own hardware.
* **Cons:** Requires a small amount of initial setup to connect to your network equipment, and the free version has specific licensing requirements.
