### What it does in plain English
EverythingToolbar brings lightning-fast file searching directly into your Windows taskbar. It connects with powerful background tools to instantly locate any document, image, or folder on your computer as you type. You can customize the look to match your system theme and use handy shortcuts to preview or open files without hassle.

### Who it's for
* Office workers and researchers who need to locate buried documents in seconds.
* Power users who want custom search filters and quick keyboard navigation.
* Anyone tired of slow default system searches and cluttered file management.

### Key features
* Seamlessly integrates into your Windows taskbar as a clean search box or clickable icon.
* Automatically matches your light or dark system theme settings.
* Supports advanced filters, case matching, whole words, and regular expressions.
* Lets you preview files or open containing folders with simple keystrokes.
* Customizable global shortcuts so you can summon your search window from anywhere.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, make sure you have the required runtime and file search helper installed on your computer. Then, choose the installer that matches your system architecture:

* [Download for Windows (.exe) - ARM64](https://github.com/srwi/EverythingToolbar/releases/download/3.5.0/EverythingToolbar-3.5.0-arm64.exe)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, lightweight performance, highly customizable interface, great keyboard shortcut support.
* **Cons:** Requires a separate background file indexing engine to operate, manual setup steps needed on older Windows versions.
