### What it does in plain English
FreeCAD is an open-source tool for designing real-world objects of any shape or size. It lets you draw precise 2D outlines and turn them into complex 3D models, or modify your work later by changing its underlying parameters. Whether you are building mechanical parts, custom furniture, or architectural plans, the software gives you complete freedom to create and export your designs.

### Who it's for
* Hobbyists and makers designing custom items for 3D printing or woodworking.
* Students and educators learning mechanical engineering, product design, or architecture.
* Professionals seeking a flexible, customizable alternative to commercial modeling software.

### Key features
* Modify past design steps easily by changing numerical parameters in the model history.
* Build solid 3D structures using constraints-based 2D sketching tools.
* Generate production-ready technical drawings and blueprints from your 3D models.
* Run custom scripts and automate repetitive tasks using built-in Python integration.
* Work smoothly across Windows, macOS, and Linux computers.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source with no subscription fees, highly customizable via Python, and runs natively on multiple operating systems.
* **Cons:** Advanced parametric workbenches take time to learn, and complex assemblies can occasionally require careful constraint management.
