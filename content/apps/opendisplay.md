### What it does in plain English
OpenDisplay turns your old iPhone, iPad, or spare Mac into a real, high-resolution second monitor for your primary computer. It connects seamlessly via a standard USB cable or Wi-Fi network without requiring special hardware dongles or accounts. You can drag windows, arrange your desktop, and use touch controls directly on your secondary screen.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers and students who need a portable multi-monitor setup while traveling.
* People who own an older iPad or iPhone sitting in a drawer and want to repurpose it productively.
* Users looking for an open alternative to built-in casting features that works across different device types.

### Key features
* **True screen extension:** Operates as an actual extended desktop rather than a basic mirrored image.
* **Low-latency USB connection:** Plugs in directly via a data cable to bypass wireless lag and interference.
* **Retina crispness:** Matches your device panel pixel-for-pixel so text remains sharp and legible.
* **Touch and scroll support:** Turns compatible touchscreens into interactive work surfaces for your apps.
* **Completely private:** Operates locally on your network without sending personal data to external servers.

* **Pros:** Free and open-source, works over a simple USB cable, no cloud account needed, supports touch input.
* **Cons:** Requires initial permission setup on your operating system, advanced tweaks occasionally require troubleshooting network settings.
