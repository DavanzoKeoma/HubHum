### What it does in plain English
Android FOSS provides a comprehensive, community-curated directory of free and open-source software designed for everyday smartphones. It helps users discover privacy-friendly alternatives to mainstream applications across dozens of practical categories. Every listed project respects your digital rights and keeps your personal data away from trackers.

### Who it's for
* Privacy advocates seeking complete transparency on their mobile devices
* Smartphone users looking to replace commercial apps with free alternatives
* Anyone wanting to explore alternative app stores and safe utilities

### Key features
* Carefully organized catalog spanning privacy, productivity, tools, and media
* Links to trusted independent app distribution channels and stores
* Focus on applications that do not track user behavior or require ads
* Regular community updates highlighting active open-source projects

### How to install (Download)
Browse the complete collection and discover official installation links directly on the [Android FOSS Official Site](https://offa.github.io/android-foss/). You can also inspect the source code and contribute via the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/offa/android-foss).

* **Pros:** Completely free forever, strictly privacy-oriented, wide variety of categories
* **Cons:** Requires exploring individual project pages for specific setup instructions
