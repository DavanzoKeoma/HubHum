### What it does in plain English
Peach helps you keep all your video files tidy by automatically finding titles, performers and cover images.  
It lets you browse, search and play those videos right from your computer.  
You can also follow creators and add new titles to a personal wish list.

### Who it's for
* Home movie collectors who want everything organized in one place.  
* Small indie filmmakers managing their own footage library.  
* Anyone who keeps personal videos and wants easy playback with subtitles and speed control.

### Key features
- Automatically fills in titles, performers, studios and cover pictures from several sources.  
- Plays videos with subtitles, speed control, rotation and resume‑where‑you‑left‑off.  
- Shows files from local drives, network shares and cloud folders together.  
- Tracks creator updates and lets you keep a “want to watch” list.  
- Helps you find duplicates, manage tags and delete groups of videos safely.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** completely free, runs locally without a subscription, works with many storage types.  
* **Cons:** desktop builds are unsigned (Windows) and not notarized (macOS); some advanced options need a few minutes to learn.
