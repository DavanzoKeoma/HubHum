### What it does in plain English
Deco provides a visual interface that allows you to change your website's content and design without needing to write complex code. It works by connecting your visual edits directly to your project's underlying files, ensuring that your changes are saved and organized automatically. This approach helps teams build and update professional websites quickly while maintaining high performance.

### Who it's for
* **Content Editors:** Marketing and business professionals who need to update website text, images, and layouts independently.
* **Web Developers:** Professionals who want to provide a user-friendly editing experience for their clients without sacrificing site speed or flexibility.

### Key features
* **Visual Editor:** A user-friendly dashboard that lets you see and change your website layout in real-time.
* **Automatic Saving:** Every change made in the editor is automatically saved to your project files, keeping your history clean and organized.
* **Performance Focused:** Built to ensure that your website remains fast and responsive for all visitors.
* **Flexible Integrations:** Easily connect your site to various data sources and services to pull in products or content dynamically.
* **Team Collaboration:** Includes built-in tools for managing roles, so you can safely allow team members to edit content without breaking the site structure.

### How to install (Download)
Deco is a web-based platform. To get started, visit the [official website](https://deco.cx) and click on the "Get Started" or "Login" buttons to create your first project. You can choose from a variety of templates to launch your site in under a minute.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely fast performance, intuitive visual editing for non-technical users, and seamless integration with modern web standards.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of web project structures for initial setup, and some advanced customization features are best suited for those with some technical background.
