### What it does in plain English
Sandstorm acts as a personal digital workspace that you control entirely on your own hardware. It provides a secure environment where you can install various productivity tools, such as document editors and task managers, without relying on external companies. By keeping your data on your own server, you maintain full ownership and privacy over your digital life.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to own their data instead of storing it on large commercial platforms.
* Small teams or families who need a shared, secure space to collaborate on documents and projects.

### Key features
* **App Store Simplicity:** Install and manage web-based tools with a single click, similar to how you use a smartphone.
* **Total Data Control:** Your information stays on your server, ensuring you are not locked into a specific company's ecosystem.
* **Security-Focused:** Each application runs in a protected environment, preventing different tools from interfering with each other.
* **Self-Hostable:** Designed to run on standard Linux hardware, giving you full authority over your digital infrastructure.

### How to install (Download)
You can find the official installation guide and setup instructions on the [Sandstorm Documentation page](https://docs.sandstorm.io/en/latest/install/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, user-friendly interface, and a wide variety of available productivity tools.
* **Cons:** Requires a Linux-based server to operate and some initial setup time to configure your own hardware.
