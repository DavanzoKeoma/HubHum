### What it does in plain English
Xplorer is a modern replacement for your computer's default file browser, designed to make finding and organizing your documents much faster. It includes a built-in AI assistant that can answer questions about your files, summarize documents, and help you find exactly what you need using natural language. The app is highly customizable, allowing you to change how your files look and add new features through a simple extension system.

### Who it's for
* **Students and Researchers:** People who need to quickly search through large collections of documents and want an AI assistant to help summarize or explain complex files.
* **Power Users and Developers:** Individuals who want advanced control over their file system, including split-pane views, keyboard shortcuts, and integrated tools for managing code projects.

### Key features
* **Smart AI Search:** Find files by their meaning or content rather than just their name, and chat with your documents to get quick insights.
* **Flexible Views:** Choose from six different ways to display your files, including grid, list, gallery, and tree views to suit your workflow.
* **Extensible Design:** Personalize your experience with themes and add extra functionality like specialized editors or cloud storage tools from the marketplace.
* **Fast Performance:** Built for speed, it handles large file operations efficiently and supports multiple tabs and split-screen browsing.
