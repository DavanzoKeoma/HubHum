### What it does in plain English
ZapZap brings the popular web messaging service directly to your computer desktop as a standalone application. It frees you from keeping a web browser tab open while adding handy features like multiple accounts and system tray integration. You get a clean, dedicated space for all your chats that fits right into your daily workflow.

### Who it's for
* Busy professionals who manage personal and work messaging accounts side-by-side on their computer.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer a dedicated desktop environment over leaving browser tabs open.
* Anyone looking for a customizable messaging experience with native desktop notifications and tray controls.

### Key features
* Standalone desktop application that runs independently of your web browser.
* Multi-account support to manage different profiles in isolated sessions.
* Native system tray integration and reliable desktop alerts.
* Custom theme support including light, dark, and system matching modes.
* Built-in spell checking and custom appearance styling options.
