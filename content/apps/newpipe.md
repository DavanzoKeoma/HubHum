### What it does in plain English
NewPipe lets you watch videos and listen to music from sites like YouTube and SoundCloud without signing in.  
It works on Android phones even if you don’t have Google apps installed.  
You can also download videos or audio to watch later offline.

### Who it's for
* Anyone who wants to watch YouTube videos without a Google account.  
* Users who prefer a lightweight app that saves mobile data.  
* People who value privacy and don’t want their usage tracked.

### Key features
- Play videos up to 4K resolution.  
- Listen to audio in the background to save data.  
- Picture‑in‑picture (floating) player for multitasking.  
- Download videos or audio files for offline viewing.  
- Subscribe to channels and get notifications without logging in.
