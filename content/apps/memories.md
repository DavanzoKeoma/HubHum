### What it does in plain English
Memories turns your private cloud storage into a professional-grade photo and video gallery. It automatically sorts your media by date and location, making it easy to find specific moments from your past. The app handles large collections with high speed and allows you to share your favorite albums with friends and family securely.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious individuals** who want to host their own photo library instead of relying on big tech companies.
*   **Nextcloud users** looking for a faster, more feature-rich alternative to standard file browsing for their images and videos.

### Key features
*   **Smart Timeline:** Automatically organizes your media based on when it was captured.
*   **Interactive Map:** View your photos on a world map to see exactly where your memories were made.
*   **AI-Powered Tagging:** Automatically identifies people and objects in your photos to help you search your library.
*   **High-Performance Streaming:** Uses advanced video technology to ensure your home videos play smoothly without long loading times.
*   **Flexible Sharing:** Create and share albums with others, even if they don't have an account on your system.

### How to install (Download)
Memories is designed to run as an app within your existing Nextcloud installation.
*   **Install via Nextcloud:** Navigate to your Nextcloud "Apps" menu, search for "Memories," and click "Download and enable."
*   **Mobile Access:** You can access your library on the go using the [Memories Android App](https://f-droid.org/packages/gallery.memories/) or the official Nextcloud mobile apps for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nextcloud.client) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nextcloud/id1125420102).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely private, handles massive photo libraries with ease, and offers a modern, fast interface.
*   **Cons:** Requires an existing Nextcloud server to function and involves a few manual configuration steps after installation.
