### What it does in plain English
Super Productivity helps you organize your daily tasks, plan schedules, and track how much time you spend on your projects. You can easily capture quick to-do items, set reminders, and sync with popular developer tools without creating an external user account. Your information stays completely on your own device unless you choose to set up a private synchronization service.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and remote workers who need to track billable hours and manage complex daily routines.
* Developers and professionals wanting to integrate personal task lists with platforms like GitHub, GitLab, or Jira.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking for a reliable organizer that never tracks personal data or requires cloud accounts.

### Key features
* **Offline-First Privacy:** Operates entirely on your local machine with zero mandatory accounts or data collection.
* **Integrated Time Tracking:** Measure time spent per task, run focus sessions like Pomodoro, and generate clean timesheets.
* **Task Scheduling:** Lay out your day on a clear timeline alongside your personal calendar events.
* **External Tool Connectors:** Pull in assigned items automatically from tools like Jira, GitHub, Trello, and GitLab.
* **Flexible Customization:** Organize work using Kanban boards, tags, sub-tasks, notes, and custom color codes.
