### What it does in plain English
Nod is a smart personal assistant that lives on your computer to tackle time-consuming web chores like tracking down unpaid invoices, researching people before your meetings, and dealing with online forms. It connects with everyday tools like calendars, email, and chat apps to gather context and draft responses automatically. Best of all, it operates under your strict supervision, pausing to check with you before sending messages or spending money.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and small business owners looking to automate tedious billing and administrative errands.
* Busy professionals who want automated research briefs compiled before every call.
* Privacy-conscious users who want powerful AI help that runs directly on their own hardware.

### Key features
* Operates inside its own secure local environment with built-in sandbox isolation.
* Remembers your personal preferences and writing style without snooping on private data.
* Integrates smoothly with your chosen AI model provider, including major services or local options.
* Asks for manual approval before making changes, sending emails, or triggering actions.
* Keeps a detailed history log of everything it does so you stay in total control.
