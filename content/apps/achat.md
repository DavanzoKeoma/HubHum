### What it does in plain English
AChat provides a single, organized home for all your team's AI-powered conversations. It allows businesses to keep track of past interactions while ensuring that sensitive information remains private and secure. The platform also includes built-in tools to manage usage costs and assign specific roles to team members.

### Who it's for
*   **Business Teams:** Companies looking to centralize their AI usage and monitor team productivity.
*   **Project Managers:** Leaders who need to oversee AI-assisted workflows and manage access permissions for their staff.

### Key features
*   **Centralized Management:** Keep all team AI dialogues in one place for easy tracking and collaboration.
*   **Organized History:** Quickly search and categorize past interactions to find information when you need it.
*   **Built-in Cost Control:** Integrated tools to monitor AI usage, billing, and expenses.
*   **Team Permissions:** Assign specific roles and access levels to ensure secure collaboration.
*   **Privacy Focused:** Designed with data protection in mind to keep your internal communications safe.

### How to install (Download)
AChat is a self-hosted web platform. You can find the setup instructions and documentation on the official website: [Visit AChat Documentation](https://manual.sku.moe/project/chatgpt-admin-web).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Keeps team AI usage organized, provides clear cost management, and offers robust privacy controls.
*   **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up and host on your own servers.
