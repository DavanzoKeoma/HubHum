### What it does in plain English
InstallerX-Revived replaces your phone's limited built-in app installer with a much cleaner and more capable tool. It handles complicated file types like split packages and zip archives smoothly in one go. You can also customize how every single application gets installed on your device using advanced background options and safety checks.

### Who it's for
* Android power users who frequently install split application bundles or custom packages.
* People looking for a cleaner, more responsive alternative to their phone's default manufacturer installer.
* Enthusiasts who want advanced control over installation permissions, backgrounds, and silent app updates.

### Key features
* Supports diverse package formats including standard APKs, APKS, XAPK, and split archives.
* Choose between different installation authorizers such as Root, Shizuku, or standard system modes.
* Create custom profiles to handle target users, automatic deletions, and security policy gates.
* Modern visual styles including Material 3 design, dark themes, and dynamic color palettes.
* Advanced safety controls with package-name blacklists and signature mismatch checks.
