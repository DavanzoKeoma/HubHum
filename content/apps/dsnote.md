### What it does in plain English
dsnote is a privacy-first desktop application that lets you take notes using your voice, read text aloud, and translate between multiple languages. Everything is processed entirely offline on your own computer, meaning no audio or text data ever gets sent over the internet. It comes equipped with a built-in model browser so you can easily download speech and language engines tailored to your preferred languages.

### Who it's for
* Journalists and writers who want to dictate notes quickly and keep their audio recordings completely private.
* Language learners and polyglots looking for a reliable offline translation and text-reading tool.
* Linux desktop users who prefer local artificial intelligence utilities that do not require cloud accounts.

### Key features
* **Offline Speech-to-Text:** Dictate text locally using powerful engines like Vosk, Whisper, or Coqui without any cloud connection.
* **Text-to-Speech Reading:** Listen to your notes or documents read aloud with synchronized subtitle support.
* **Built-in Machine Translation:** Translate text across dozens of languages right on your machine.
* **Global Shortcuts and Active Window Injection:** Trigger voice typing anywhere on your desktop using convenient keyboard shortcuts.
* **Custom Model Manager:** Easily browse and download language packs directly from within the application interface.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely offline operation ensures total privacy; supports dozens of languages; highly customizable speech engines.
* **Cons:** Advanced model packages and GPU add-ons can require significant disk space; Wayland users may need an extra daemon for active window text insertion.
