### What it does in plain English
kittenTricks is a free, open-source starting point for building mobile apps that work on both iOS and Android. It comes pre-loaded with more than 40 professionally designed screens, so you do not have to start from a blank page. You can switch between light and dark color themes instantly without restarting the app, ensuring a consistent and modern look for your users.

### Who it's for
* **Independent developers** who want to save time by using pre-built layouts instead of designing every screen from scratch.
* **Small teams** looking for a consistent, professional design foundation that is easy to customize for different industries like fitness or social media.
* **App enthusiasts** who want to explore how modern mobile interfaces are structured and styled.

### Key features
* **40+ Ready-to-Use Screens:** Includes a wide variety of layouts for common app needs, such as login pages, dashboards, and settings menus.
* **Dynamic Theming:** Supports both light and dark modes that can be toggled in real-time without reloading the application.
* **TypeScript Support:** Built with a modern coding language that helps catch errors early and makes the code easier to manage.
* **Consistent Design System:** Uses the Eva Design System to ensure all buttons, icons, and colors look cohesive and professional.
* **Cross-Platform Compatibility:** One codebase works for both Apple and Android devices, reducing development effort.

* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, includes a large library of pre-designed screens, and allows for instant theme switching.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of mobile development to modify, and the pre-built screens may need customization to fit very specific business needs.
