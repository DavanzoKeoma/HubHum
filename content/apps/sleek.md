### What it does in plain English
Sleek helps you keep track of your daily responsibilities by using a straightforward, text-based checklist system. It strips away complicated menus and hidden features, presenting your tasks in a clean and distraction-free layout. You can quickly filter items by tags, search through your entire list instantly, and archive finished items without missing a beat.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and remote workers looking for a lightweight task organizer that stores data locally.
* Fans of plain-text organization systems who want a visual interface without sacrificing simplicity.
* Anyone who prefers open-source software that respects privacy and requires no online account setup.

### Key features
* Built around the universal plain-text checklist format for maximum portability and future-proofing.
* Instant full-text search and flexible tag filtering to find information in seconds.
* Clean visual layout with customizable grouping options to fit your personal workflow.
* Automatic background scanning of your task files to stay instantly synchronized with other apps.
* Complete desktop support across Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
