## Quick Answer: Spotube vs Pear Desktop

Comparing **Spotube** and **Pear Desktop** highlights two distinct approaches in **Audio Editors & Music Players**. **Spotube** runs as a **Desktop App** on **Windows, macOS, Linux, Android** (50,882 GitHub stars): Spotube lets you listen to music from many sources, keep your own playlists, and enjoy lyrics without any tracking. By contrast, **Pear Desktop** runs as a **Desktop App** on **Windows, macOS, Linux** (34,107 GitHub stars): Pear Desktop provides a dedicated, native music player experience with custom themes and powerful plugin support for your computer.
