### What it does in plain English
Butterfly provides a limitless digital workspace where you can freely combine handwritten notes, typed text, and sketches. Instead of being restricted to standard pages, you can arrange your ideas, images, and documents anywhere on the screen. It is designed to keep your work private, allowing you to store everything locally on your own device.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to organize complex projects with diagrams and handwritten notes.
* Creative professionals who want a flexible space for brainstorming, storyboarding, or sketching out new concepts.

### Key features
* **Infinite Workspace:** Move beyond fixed pages with a canvas that grows as your ideas expand.
* **Versatile Tools:** Use a mix of handwriting, typed text, shapes, and images to capture information exactly how you want.
* **Always Editable:** Easily move, resize, or change the color of any element on your canvas at any time.
* **Privacy Focused:** Keep your files under your control by working offline or using your own preferred storage services.
* **Flexible Export:** Save your projects as images or documents to share with others easily.

* **Pros:** Completely free with no subscriptions, works offline, and offers a highly customizable drawing experience.
* **Cons:** The infinite canvas can feel overwhelming for simple, linear lists; requires a bit of practice to master the advanced layout tools.
