### What it does in plain English
App Privacy Policy Generator helps developers quickly create standard legal documents like privacy policies and terms of use for their software. You simply fill out an online questionnaire detailing what kind of data your application gathers from users. The tool then instantly compiles these answers into professional, ready-to-use text files.

### Who it's for
* Mobile and web app developers looking to satisfy app store submission requirements
* Independent creators who need clear legal terms without hiring a lawyer
* Project teams wanting a quick way to generate compliant GDPR notices

### Key features
* Interactive wizard that walks you through data collection choices
* Supports multiple platforms including mobile operating systems and the web
* Built-in options for privacy standards, tracking disclosures, and age limits
* Exports completed documents directly as HTML or Markdown files
* Works completely offline as a progressive web application

### How to install (Download)
This service runs directly in your browser as a web application with no installation needed. Access the online generator immediately at the [Official Web App](https://app-privacy-policy-generator.nisrulz.com/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, no subscription or registration required, highly customizable output.
* **Cons:** Requires you to accurately know and input your app's data collection practices.
