### What it does in plain English
SpotX is a handy tool that modifies the official desktop music player on Windows to remove unwanted promotional banners, audio interruptions, and video ads. It lets you hide distracting elements like podcasts or audiobooks from your main screen and stops background analytics tracking. You can easily choose between different installation styles depending on whether you prefer the newest layout or a classic appearance.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want an ad-free listening experience on their Windows computers.
* Listeners who prefer a clean interface without extra recommended podcasts or audiobooks cluttering the home screen.
* Users looking for simple scripts to customize their desktop music software without complicated setup procedures.

### Key features
* **Ad-Free Listening:** Blocks banner promotions, commercial video breaks, and audio interruptions.
* **Clutter Control:** Optionally hides podcasts, episodes, and audiobooks from your main view.
* **Update Management:** Prevents automatic software updates if you prefer to keep your current setup.
* **Privacy Focused:** Disables background analytics telemetry collection.
* **Flexible Layouts:** Offers choices for standard layouts, classic themes, or full automated customization.

### How to install (Download)
Make sure you have the official desktop version of the music player installed (the Microsoft Store version is not supported) along with PowerShell 5.1 or higher. 

Choose your preferred installation file from the official releases:
* [Download (Install_New_theme.bat)](https://github.com/SpotX-Official/SpotX/releases/download/1.9/Install_New_theme.bat) - Installs the patch with the new interface theme.
* [Download (Install_Old_theme.bat)](https://github.com/SpotX-Official/SpotX/releases/download/1.9/Install_Old_theme.bat) - Installs the patch using the classic interface theme.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, offers flexible customization parameters, removes annoying audio interruptions.
* **Cons:** Requires the desktop version rather than the store version, needs manual re-application if the music player is completely reinstalled.
