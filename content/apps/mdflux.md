### What it does in plain English
mdflux is a local desktop application that transforms complex files like scanned PDFs, office spreadsheets, and presentations into structured, readable Markdown text. It runs entirely on your own computer without uploading sensitive personal files to external cloud servers. By processing documents locally and extracting clean text, it reduces the token overhead required when sharing files with artificial intelligence models.

### Who it's for
* **AI and RAG builders:** Feed clean, perfectly structured source documents directly into language models instead of dealing with messy raw text.
* **Researchers and students:** Batch-convert heavy academic papers, reports, and image-based document archives into easily searchable text files.
* **Privacy-conscious professionals:** Convert sensitive contracts, financial records, and proprietary decks with absolute confidence since nothing leaves your machine.

### Key features
* **Local-first processing:** Your documents remain securely on your personal hardware without requiring account logins or external cloud subscriptions.
* **Built-in text recognition:** Recovers clear, usable text from image-only scanned PDFs that standard extractors treat as completely blank.
* **Multiple file formats:** Handles standard documents, spreadsheets, slides, ebooks, web pages, images, and audio recordings seamlessly.
* **Folder batching:** Process entire directories of mixed files simultaneously with live progress updates and detailed per-file diagnostics.
* **Optional AI cleanup:** Choose between rule-based cleanup or local/API language models to polish extracted text while reviewing exact changes.
