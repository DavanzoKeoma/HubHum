### What it does in plain English
VidBee acts as a central hub for your video and audio collection, allowing you to save content from hundreds of websites directly to your computer. It automatically converts spoken words into searchable text transcripts, complete with speaker identification and timestamps. You can then use your preferred AI tools to summarize, translate, or ask questions about the media you have saved.

### Who it's for
* **Researchers and Students:** Perfect for turning long lectures or interviews into organized, searchable notes and summaries.
* **Content Creators:** Ideal for managing media libraries, creating transcripts for accessibility, and repurposing video content into written formats.

### Key features
* **Local Transcription:** Converts audio to text directly on your machine using advanced models, ensuring your data stays private.
* **AI Integration:** Connect your own AI accounts to summarize, paraphrase, or extract information from your transcripts.
* **Universal Downloader:** Save videos, audio, and entire playlists from over 1,000 different websites with ease.
* **Smart Organization:** Manage your library with RSS feed subscriptions, custom file naming, and automatic metadata tagging.
* **Searchable Playback:** Click any part of a transcript to jump instantly to that exact moment in the video or audio file.
