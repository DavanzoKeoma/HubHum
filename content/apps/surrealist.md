### What it does in plain English
Surrealist acts as a visual control center for your database, allowing you to see and manage your information easily. It replaces the need for typing complex instructions by providing a friendly interface to explore tables and organize your data. Please note that this tool has been succeeded by SurrealDB Studio, which offers improved performance and modern features.

### Who it's for
* Data enthusiasts who prefer a visual dashboard over text-based commands.
* Individuals or small teams looking for a straightforward way to view and organize their database records.

### Key features
* **Visual Data Exploration:** Easily browse through your tables and records in a clean, organized view.
* **Simplified Management:** Perform common database tasks through a user-friendly interface.
* **Schema Design:** Create and modify the structure of your data without manual coding.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Connect to your database instances directly from your desktop or browser.
