### What it does in plain English
Pile is a desktop application designed to help you build a reflective writing routine. It stores all your personal notes and journal entries directly on your computer so your private thoughts stay entirely under your control. If you choose, you can also connect external artificial intelligence tools to review past entries, ask questions about your journal, or get thoughtful prompts.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a clean, distraction-free space to record daily thoughts and memories
* People who value data privacy and want their personal journal stored locally on their own computer
* Writers and thinkers interested in exploring their past journal entries using modern artificial intelligence tools

### Key features
* Keeps all your written journals and reflections stored locally on your machine for maximum privacy
* Clean, minimalist workspace designed to help you focus entirely on your writing
* Optional artificial intelligence integration to analyze past entries or answer questions about your journal
* Cross-platform desktop support with simple installation for both Windows and Mac computers
