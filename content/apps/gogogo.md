### What it does in plain English
GoGoGo allows you to tell your Android phone that you are in a different place than you actually are. You can pick a specific spot on a map, type in coordinates, or draw a path to walk along. It also includes a floating control stick that lets you move your simulated location while using other apps.

### Who it's for
*   **App Developers:** Test how your application behaves when a user is in a specific city or moving along a route.
*   **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Prevent apps from tracking your real-time location by providing a fake, static, or moving position.
*   **Gamers and Enthusiasts:** Explore map-based games or applications that rely on GPS data without physically traveling.

### Key features
*   **Flexible Location Selection:** Choose a spot by tapping the map, searching for a place, or entering exact latitude and longitude numbers.
*   **Route Simulation:** Create paths with multiple stops to simulate walking, running, or cycling along a specific track.
*   **Floating Joystick:** A draggable control overlay lets you steer your simulated position in real-time while looking at other screens.
*   **Data Management:** Save favorite locations, import/export route files (GPX), and keep a history of past simulations.
*   **Customizable Settings:** Adjust speed, altitude, and accuracy to make the simulated location look more realistic to other apps.
