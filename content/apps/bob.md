### What it does in plain English
Bob sits quietly in your computer's menu bar, ready to help you understand foreign languages and extract text from any image in seconds. Whenever you encounter unfamiliar words in an article, app, or picture, you can simply select or capture the area to see an instant translation. It bridges language barriers across your entire desktop workflow without requiring you to open heavy web browsers or separate translation websites.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers reading foreign-language documents or scanning textbook pages.
* International professionals communicating across different languages or reviewing overseas applications.
* Everyday Mac users who want a fast, convenient way to copy text out of images, videos, or locked PDFs.

### Key features
* **Instant selection translation:** Highlight text anywhere on your screen and press a quick shortcut to see its meaning right away.
* **Screen capture translation and OCR:** Grab any region of your display to instantly translate text or pull raw words into your clipboard.
* **Multiple provider choices:** Connect to various popular translation engines and text-recognition services based on your personal preference.
* **Quiet background scanning:** Extract text from screenshots silently without opening extra pop-up windows.
* **File recognition:** Select image files directly from your system file manager to process multiple pages at once.
