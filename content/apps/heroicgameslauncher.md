### What it does in plain English
Heroic Games Launcher brings together your favorite digital game collections from multiple stores into a single, clean dashboard. You can easily install, update, and launch titles you own on Epic Games, GOG, and Amazon without keeping multiple heavy apps open. It works seamlessly across Windows, macOS, and Linux to give you total control over your gaming library.

### Who it's for
* PC gamers who own titles spread across Epic Games, GOG, and Amazon stores.
* Linux and Steam Deck users looking for a native, user-friendly way to run Windows games.
* Players who prefer open-source software without intrusive ads or corporate telemetry.

### Key features
* Combines multiple game storefront accounts in one unified dashboard.
* Installs, updates, repairs, and organizes your local game collection.
* Synchronizes game save data securely with cloud backups.
* Supports custom themes to personalize the look and feel of your library.
* Integrates compatibility databases so you can check how games run on your system.
