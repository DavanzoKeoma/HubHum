### What it does in plain English
auto-cpufreq is a background helper for Linux laptops that watches your processor temperature, battery status, and everyday workload in real time. It automatically adjusts processor speeds up or down to keep your computer cool and quiet while making your battery last significantly longer. You simply install it, and it handles all the technical speed adjustments smoothly in the background without requiring your constant attention.

### Who it's for
* Linux laptop owners who want better battery life when working on the go without sacrificing speed when plugged in.
* Users who are tired of manually switching power modes or dealing with complex energy-saving command tools.
* Anyone looking to prevent their laptop from overheating and running fans at maximum volume during everyday tasks.

### Key features
* Real-time monitoring of processor usage, temperatures across every core, and battery levels.
* Intelligent switching between high performance and energy-saving modes depending on power connection.
* Automatic management of turbo boost features to prevent unwanted heat generation on battery power.
* Support for custom configuration files if you want to set specific speed boundaries or preferences.
* Includes both a modern graphical interface and easy command-line tools for checking system status.

* **Pros:** Completely automatic operation, noticeably extends laptop battery life, reduces fan noise and overheating, and offers a user-friendly graphical interface.
* **Cons:** Requires a Linux operating system, and replacing existing power managers like TLP requires following cleanup steps to prevent conflicts.
