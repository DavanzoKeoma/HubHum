### What it does in plain English
AlterSend allows you to move files directly from one device to another without using cloud storage services. Because the files travel directly between your devices, there are no file size limits and no account sign-ups required. Every transfer is protected by end-to-end encryption, ensuring that only you and the recipient can access the data.

### Who it's for
*   **Remote workers** who need to share large project files or documents instantly with colleagues.
*   **Privacy-conscious individuals** who want to move photos or personal data between their own devices without involving third-party servers.

### Key features
*   **Direct Transfers:** Files move directly from your device to the recipient, bypassing cloud storage entirely.
*   **No Size Limits:** Send files of any size, from small documents to massive video archives, without restrictions.
*   **Privacy First:** No accounts, no logins, and no personal data collection are required to use the service.
*   **End-to-End Encryption:** All data is encrypted during transit, ensuring your files remain private and secure.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS devices.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no file size limits, and no data is stored on external servers.
*   **Cons:** Both devices must be online simultaneously to complete the transfer, as there is no "offline" cloud storage to hold the files.
