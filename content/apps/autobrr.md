### What it does in plain English
Autobrr acts as a smart assistant that watches for new file announcements from your favorite sources. Instead of waiting for slow periodic updates, it detects new uploads in real-time and sends them directly to your download software. This ensures you get the files you want as soon as they are released, without needing to check manually.

### Who it's for
*   Media enthusiasts who want to stay up-to-date with new releases without manual intervention.
*   Power users who manage large collections and need a reliable way to keep their libraries current.

### Key features
*   **Real-time monitoring:** Scans for new uploads instantly rather than waiting for scheduled updates.
*   **Flexible filtering:** Uses simple rules to ensure you only download exactly what you are looking for.
*   **Broad compatibility:** Works with a wide variety of download programs and media management tools.
*   **User-friendly dashboard:** Includes a clean, mobile-friendly web interface to manage your settings and history.
*   **Instant notifications:** Sends alerts to your phone or computer via popular messaging apps when a download starts.
