### What it does in plain English
This tool provides a simple, visual way to interact with your tracked files and project history. Instead of typing complex commands, you can select files, view changes, and manage your work through a clean, searchable menu. It makes keeping track of your progress much faster and less prone to errors.

### Who it's for
*   People who manage many files and want a clearer way to see what has changed.
*   Users who prefer selecting items from a list rather than memorizing long text commands.

### Key features
*   **Visual Selection:** Choose files to track or ignore using a searchable, interactive list.
*   **Easy History Viewing:** Quickly browse through your past work and see exactly what was modified.
*   **Branch Management:** Switch between different versions of your project with simple menu selections.
*   **Lightweight:** It runs quickly and integrates directly into your existing command-line environment.
*   **Customizable:** You can easily change how the menus look or add your own shortcuts to match your workflow.

### How to install (Download)
To use this tool, you must first have [fzf](https://github.com/junegunn/fzf) installed on your computer. Once that is ready, you can install forgit using your preferred method:

*   **For macOS users (Homebrew):** Run `brew install forgit` in your terminal.
*   **For Arch Linux users:** Install the `forgit` package via the AUR.
*   **For other systems:** You can add it as a plugin to your shell (such as Zsh, Bash, or Fish) by following the instructions in the [Official Source](https://github.com/wfxr/forgit).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, significantly speeds up common file-tracking tasks, and works with most popular shells.
*   **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of how to use a terminal, and you must install the fzf requirement first.
