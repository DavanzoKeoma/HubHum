### What it does in plain English
Pegasus Frontend brings all your video games and emulators together into a single, beautiful menu. Instead of jumping between different apps to play your favorite titles, you can organize everything in one place. It lets you completely transform how the screen looks using custom themes so your game library feels tailored just for you.

### Who it's for
* Retro gaming enthusiasts with large collections across multiple older consoles and systems.
* Users who want a unified living room setup or handheld device launcher like a dedicated console.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, highly customizable menu system that supports gamepads out of the box.

### Key features
* **Unified Game Library:** Browse and launch titles from various emulators seamlessly from a single application.
* **Complete Visual Customization:** Change themes, layouts, animations, and menus to match your personal aesthetic.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Runs smoothly on Windows, Linux, Android, and popular single-board computers.
* **Gamepad Friendly:** Full support for gamepads and controllers with easy setup options.
* **Compatible with Existing Tools:** Works directly with standard metadata and gamelist formats without requiring a complex setup.
