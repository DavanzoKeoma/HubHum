### What it does in plain English
Runtipi turns an ordinary spare computer into a fully functional personal home server without requiring complex technical knowledge. It provides a clean, user-friendly dashboard that lets you search, install, and manage your favorite self-hosted applications with a single click. You maintain complete control over your private data while enjoying the convenience of a modern app store right in your own living room.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to keep their personal files and photos away from large public cloud companies.
* Tech hobbyists looking to explore self-hosted tools, media streaming, and automation without struggling through complicated command-line setups.
* Families wanting a centralized shared storage and media hub running locally on a dedicated spare machine.

### Key features
* Simple web dashboard designed for managing all your self-hosted programs in one central place.
* Extensive app store offering instant installation for popular media servers, file sharing tools, and productivity utilities.
* Automated maintenance and background updates to keep your services running smoothly with minimal effort.
* Secure local architecture ensuring your private data stays on your own hardware.
* Community-driven ecosystem allowing advanced users to create and share custom application packages.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, intuitive web control panel, massive library of ready-to-run applications.
* **Cons:** Requires a dedicated always-on computer or spare server machine, advanced custom networking configurations may take time to learn.
