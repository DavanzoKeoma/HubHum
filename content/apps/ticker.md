### What it does in plain English
Ticker brings live financial market updates and portfolio tracking straight into your command-line window. It pulls real-time quotes for stocks, cryptocurrencies, and digital assets without needing a browser or desktop application. You can organize your favorite investments into watchlists, track your cost basis, and see total profits or losses instantly.

### Who it's for
* Everyday investors who love using command-line tools and want a quick glance at their portfolios.
* Cryptocurrency enthusiasts monitoring spot and derivative prices in real time.
* Traders who prefer minimalist, distraction-free workspaces without graphical clutter.

### Key features
* Live price updates for global stocks and popular cryptocurrencies.
* Portfolio position tracking with support for multiple purchase lots and fees.
* Customizable layouts supporting summaries, market tags, and fundamental data.
* Group organization to easily switch between different market watchlists using keyboard tabs.
* Currency conversion tools to view international assets in your local currency.
