### What it does in plain English
Adblock4limbo is a community-maintained collection of rules and scripts designed to strip annoying popups, banner ads, and video commercials out of popular mobile websites. It targets streaming video sites, search engine result pages, and content networks to give you a clean, distraction-free reading and viewing experience. By working alongside established mobile network utilities, it keeps web pages loading fast without intrusive clutter.

### Who it's for
* **Mobile browsing enthusiasts** who want a cleaner, faster reading experience on their smartphones or tablets.
* **Streamers and readers** who want to skip repetitive video commercials and page overlays on entertainment platforms.
* **Power users** utilizing network utility apps on iOS who want automated content filtering rules.

### Key features
* **Extensive website coverage:** Targets dozens of popular video, entertainment, search, and reading websites.
* **Multi-platform support:** Compatible with popular traffic management tools on mobile devices as well as browser script extension runners.
* **Search cleanup:** Removes content farm clutter and promotional links from major search engine results.
* **Regular updates:** Maintained by the community to adapt when target websites change domains or update their layout.
