### What it does in plain English
TSMessages shows a small banner at the top of an iPhone app.  
The banner can be styled for success, error, warning or simple messages.  
Users can tap or swipe the banner away.

### Who it's for
* iPhone app developers who want to give users quick feedback.  
* Small teams building internal tools that need a polished look.  
* Hobbyists adding a professional touch to personal projects.

### Key features
- Displays a brief banner at the top of the screen.  
- Four ready‑made styles: success, error, warning, and plain message.  
- Easy to create custom styles with your own colors and icons.  
- Users can dismiss the banner by tapping or swiping it away.  
- Optional button can be added to perform an action when tapped.

### How to install (Download)
- **CocoaPods:** Add the library via CocoaPods – see the pod page [CocoaPods TSMessages](https://cocoapods.org/pods/TSMessages).  
- **Source code:** Browse or clone the project on GitHub – [TSMessages on GitHub](https://github.com/KrauseFx/TSMessages).  
- **Project website:** More information at the author’s site – [KrauseFx website](https://krausefx.com).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, easy to add, customizable appearance, works with Objective‑C projects.  
* **Cons:** No longer maintained, limited to iOS, may need manual updates for newer iOS versions.
