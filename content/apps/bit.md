### What it does in plain English
Working with code history can sometimes be complicated and full of hard-to-remember commands. This tool upgrades your standard version control experience by offering intelligent command suggestions, file autocompletion, and handy shortcuts. It works seamlessly alongside your usual setup to reduce merge mistakes and save you time every day.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want a faster, friendlier command-line experience.
* Programmers looking to avoid complex branch merging errors and typos.
* Teams who appreciate helpful suggestions and interactive prompts while coding.

### Key features
* Intelligent command and flag suggestions to help you navigate options easily.
* Autocompletion for filenames and branch names when switching or adding files.
* Built-in sync commands to combine pulling and pushing safely in one step.
* Full compatibility with standard version control commands so you never get stuck.
* Interactive prompts and custom color themes to match your terminal style.
