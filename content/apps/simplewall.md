### What it does in plain English
Simplewall acts as a gatekeeper for your computer's network activity. By default, it blocks all programs from sending or receiving data, allowing you to manually approve only the apps you trust. It is a highly efficient tool that works quietly in the background to ensure no unwanted software communicates online without your permission.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious users who want to stop background software from "phoning home" or sending telemetry data.
* Users who want to restrict internet access for specific applications to save bandwidth or improve security.

### Key features
* **Strict Control:** Blocks all network traffic by default, giving you complete authority over your system's connections.
* **Lightweight Design:** The application is extremely small and uses very few system resources.
* **Privacy Focused:** Includes an optional internal list to automatically block common tracking and data-collection services.
* **Detailed Logging:** Provides clear information about which programs are trying to connect to the internet and when.
* **Flexible Rules:** Allows you to create custom rules for specific programs or global network settings.
