### What it does in plain English
AndroidAutoSize automatically adjusts your mobile app's user interface to fit seamlessly on phones and tablets of all shapes and sizes. Instead of manually writing complex calculations or separate stylesheets for dozens of devices, it scales your layout dimensions proportionally based on your design specifications. This ensures that buttons, text, and images always appear in the exact correct proportions whether a user has a small budget phone or a massive premium device.

### Who it's for
* **Mobile Developers:** Software creators who want to save countless hours spent writing device-specific layout adjustments.
* **UI/UX Designers:** Visual creators who want their phone mockups to translate into the final app without unwanted stretching or shrinking.
* **Indie App Creators:** Solo developers looking for a lightweight, reliable solution to reach users on every Android device.

### Key features
* **One-Step Setup:** Initialize the library simply by adding a few lines of configuration text to your project setup file.
* **Flexible Unit Support:** Works natively with standard sizing units as well as alternative measurement standards like points or millimeters.
* **Per-Screen Customization:** Easily adjust or turn off scaling rules for specific app screens or individual visual components as needed.
* **Real-Time Visual Preview:** Test how your screens look directly inside your development workspace before installing the app on a physical phone.
