### What it does in plain English
This tool acts as a helpful skill for artificial intelligence assistants, allowing them to answer tricky questions with a single, beautifully formatted web page. Instead of overwhelming you with a massive wall of text in a chat window, the system automatically builds structured layouts, visual flowcharts, and clear comparison tables. It saves processing time and bandwidth by letting the AI write simple notes while a smart engine handles the visual design.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and technical writers who frequently ask complex architectural questions.
* Students and researchers looking for clear, visual breakdowns of difficult concepts.
* Anyone who wants easier-to-read summaries from AI chat assistants without wading through long blocks of text.

### Key features
* **Visual diagrams:** Automatically generates sequence charts, architecture flows, and state machines.
* **Lightning fast:** Reduces the volume of text the assistant needs to write, delivering results faster.
* **Multiple themes:** Includes built-in designs like clean cards, engineering blueprints, and readable paper styles.
* **Interactive elements:** Features built-in support for decision forms, light/dark mode toggles, and copy buttons.
* **Works offline:** Outputs self-contained documents that require no internet connection or external font servers.
