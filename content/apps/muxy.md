### What it does in plain English
Muxy lets you open many command‑line windows inside one app.  
It keeps those programs running even after you close the main window.  
You can jump between the windows instantly while using almost no memory.  

### Who it's for
* People who work with several command‑line tools at the same time.  
* Anyone who wants their command‑line work to stay alive after closing the app.  
* Users who prefer a clean, low‑memory interface on macOS or Linux.  

### Key features
* Keep programs running in the background after the main window is closed.  
* Connect to your sessions from another computer or device.  
* Split the screen into multiple panes to see several programs side by side.  
* Choose from hundreds of ready‑made color themes.  
* Add extra helpers such as AI assistants or custom extensions.
