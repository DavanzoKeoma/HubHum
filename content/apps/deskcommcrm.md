### What it does in plain English
DeskcommCRM acts as a central hub for your business, using intelligent AI agents to handle customer inquiries and qualify leads automatically through WhatsApp. It organizes your sales pipeline into a clear visual layout, ensuring no potential customer is left waiting for a response. Because you host it yourself, you keep complete ownership of your business data and avoid recurring monthly software costs.

### Who it's for
*   **Small business owners** who rely on WhatsApp to communicate with clients and want to automate their follow-up process.
*   **Sales teams** looking for a structured way to track leads from initial contact to final sale without paying for expensive commercial subscriptions.

### Key features
*   **AI Sales Agents:** Intelligent assistants that can qualify leads, answer questions, and move customers through your sales funnel automatically.
*   **WhatsApp Integration:** Connect your existing number to manage all customer chats in one organized inbox.
*   **Visual Sales Pipeline:** A clear, drag-and-drop dashboard to track where every lead is in your buying process.
*   **Self-Hosted Privacy:** Run the software on your own server to ensure your customer data stays private and secure.
*   **Automated Follow-ups:** Smart reminders that reach out to customers who haven't replied, helping you close more deals.

### How to install (Download)
You can set up DeskcommCRM on your own server using the official installation guide. For detailed instructions and to start your setup, visit the [Official Website](https://deskcomm.com.br).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** No monthly subscription fees, full data ownership, and powerful AI automation built-in.
*   **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of server management or a VPS to host, and initial setup takes a small amount of time.
