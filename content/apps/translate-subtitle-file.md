### What it does in plain English
Translate-Subtitle-File is a helpful desktop and web utility designed to translate standard video subtitle documents into multiple languages effortlessly. Users simply drag and drop their subtitle files into the app and connect their preferred modern artificial intelligence translation services. By plugging in personal keys directly, users avoid inflated middleman pricing and gain access to the most affordable translation rates available.

### Who it's for
* Video creators and translators wanting to generate bilingual subtitles quickly.
* Fans and enthusiasts looking to watch foreign-language videos with clear translated text.
* Professionals creating rough initial drafts for manual subtitle editing and proofreading.

### Key features
* **Wide Format Support**: Drag and drop standard subtitle file formats to output bilingual or single-language results.
* **Extensive Provider Choices**: Connect to dozens of major artificial intelligence models and traditional translation services.
* **Cost-Effective Design**: Direct billing through your chosen provider eliminates hidden platform markups.
* **Multilingual Output**: Translate content smoothly across major global languages based on provider capabilities.
