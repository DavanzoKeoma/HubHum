### What it does in plain English
Lip Gloss is a styling tool that makes text in your terminal look clean, modern, and easy to read. It allows you to add colors, borders, padding, and alignment to your text output without needing complex code. It works like a design language for your command-line environment, turning plain text into organized, visual layouts.

### Who it's for
*   **Software developers** who want to make their command-line tools look professional and user-friendly.
*   **System administrators** who need to create readable, color-coded logs or status reports in the terminal.

### Key features
*   **CSS-like styling:** Use familiar concepts like padding, margins, and borders to structure your text.
*   **Rich color support:** Automatically handles different color levels, from simple 16-color palettes to full-spectrum true color.
*   **Flexible layout tools:** Easily align text, create tables, or join multiple paragraphs together.
*   **Smart formatting:** Includes built-in support for bold, italic, underline, and even clickable hyperlinks.
*   **Automatic adaptation:** Gracefully adjusts colors and styles based on the capabilities of your specific terminal.

### How to install (Download)
Lip Gloss is a library for the Go programming language. To use it in your projects, you can add it to your environment using the following command:

`go get charm.land/lipgloss/v2`

For detailed documentation and examples, visit the [Official Documentation](https://pkg.go.dev/charm.land/lipgloss/v2).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Extremely lightweight, highly customizable, and makes terminal output look polished and professional.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of the Go programming language to implement; not a standalone app for non-programmers.
