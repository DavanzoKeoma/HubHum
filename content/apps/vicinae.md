### What it does in plain English
Vicinae acts as a single, fast window where you can type to find any file, app, or piece of text on your computer instantly. It replaces the need to open multiple separate programs by combining a calculator, emoji picker, and clipboard history into one place. You can also use it to chat with AI or switch between browser tabs without leaving your current task.

### Who it's for
* **Busy professionals** who want to save time by quickly accessing files, apps, and notes without clicking through menus.
* **Students and writers** who need fast access to text snippets, emojis, and a calculator while working on documents.
* **Power users** who like customizing their workflow with extra tools and scripts to fit their specific needs.

### Key features
* **Instant Search:** Quickly find files, folders, and installed applications by typing a few letters.
* **Built-in Utilities:** Access a calculator, emoji picker, and clipboard history directly from the launcher.
* **AI Chat:** Use a built-in AI assistant to answer questions or help with tasks without opening a web browser.
* **Text Snippets:** Save and insert frequently used text blocks, like email signatures or code lines, with a single command.
* **Extensible:** Add new features using scripts or compatible extensions from online stores to customize your experience.
