### What it does in plain English
FluidVoice is a desktop dictation tool that converts your spoken voice into written text directly on your computer without sending private audio to the cloud. It features on-device speech recognition to type into any application you already use. Additionally, it offers local text enhancement to automatically remove filler words and format your sentences cleanly.

### Who it's for
* Writers, journalists, and professionals looking to draft documents quickly using natural speech without privacy risks.
* Remote workers and meeting participants who need searchable local transcripts and summaries of discussions.
* Anyone who prefers typing alternative input methods via voice commands across everyday desktop applications.

### Key features
* **On-Device Speech Recognition:** Translates your spoken audio locally into text for complete personal data privacy.
* **Smart Text Cleanup:** Automatically strips out filler words, false starts, and formats lists cleanly.
* **Global Hotkey & Overlay:** Trigger voice capture from anywhere with a customizable shortcut and real-time live preview.
* **Command & Edit Modes:** Control desktop actions by voice or rewrite text directly inside active text boxes.
* **Meeting Recording:** Record, transcribe, and review online or in-person meetings securely on your machine.

* **Pros:** Free and open source under GPLv3, local-first privacy with no mandatory cloud accounts, multiple offline speech models available.
* **Cons:** Advanced on-device AI cleanup requires compatible hardware resources, initial setup requires system accessibility permissions.
