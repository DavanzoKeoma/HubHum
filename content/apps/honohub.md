### What it does in plain English
Honohub helps you keep the words, pictures and other pieces of your website in one easy place.  
You can add new pages, edit existing ones, and arrange them however you like.  
It works quickly and doesn’t need a lot of technical setup.  

### Who it's for
- Small business owners who want to update their site themselves.  
- Bloggers who need a simple way to publish posts.  
- Developers looking for a lightweight tool to add content features to their projects.  

### Key features
- Add, edit, and delete pages through a simple interface.  
- Organize pages into a clear hierarchy.  
- Support for images and other media files.  
- Runs fast on modern hosting services.  
- Optional add‑ons let you extend functionality when needed.  

### How to install (Download)
- **Visit the official website:** [Honohub website](https://honohub.dev)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, lightweight, easy to extend, fast performance.  
* **Cons:** still in early “alpha” stage, may change without notice, requires some coding knowledge to integrate.
