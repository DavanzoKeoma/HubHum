### What it does in plain English
LibreDesk brings all your customer conversations from email, live chat, and messaging apps into a single, easy-to-use inbox. It allows your team to collaborate on replies, track customer issues, and provide quick answers using a built-in knowledge base. Because you host it yourself, you keep full control over your data and customer interactions without relying on expensive third-party services.

### Who it's for
* Small to medium-sized business owners who want to manage customer support without monthly subscription fees.
* Privacy-conscious teams that need to keep their communication data on their own servers.

### Key features
* **Unified Inbox:** View and respond to emails, live chats, and WhatsApp messages in one place.
* **Help Center:** Create a searchable, multilingual library of articles to help customers find answers on their own.
* **Smart Automation:** Automatically tag, assign, and route incoming messages to the right team members.
* **AI Assistance:** Use smart tools to draft replies and summarize long conversations to save time.
* **Performance Tracking:** Monitor response times and customer satisfaction ratings to improve your service quality.

* **Pros:** Free to use, complete data ownership, and includes powerful AI features for agents.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up and maintain your own server environment.
