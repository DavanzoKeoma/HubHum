### What it does in plain English
Homer provides a clean, visual landing page that acts as a central hub for all the different services running on your home server. You can organize your links into groups and customize the look to match your personal style. It is designed to be lightweight and fast, ensuring you can find and launch your favorite tools without any clutter.

### Who it's for
* Home server enthusiasts who want a tidy way to manage their various self-hosted applications.
* Power users who need a quick, searchable interface to navigate their local network services.

### Key features
* **Fast and Lightweight:** Built to load instantly without heavy background processes.
* **Easy Organization:** Group your links and services into logical categories for better navigation.
* **Smart Search:** Use the built-in search bar to find specific services in seconds.
* **Customizable Themes:** Change the appearance to fit your personal preferences.
* **Keyboard Friendly:** Navigate and launch your services using simple keyboard shortcuts.
