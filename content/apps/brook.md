### What it does in plain English
Brook is a tool designed to help you manage how your devices connect to the internet. It allows you to create secure, private paths for your data to travel, ensuring your online activity remains protected. It works across many different types of computers and mobile devices to keep your connection consistent.

### Who it's for
* People who want more control over their personal network traffic and data privacy.
* Users who need a reliable way to connect their devices securely while traveling or using public networks.

### Key features
* **Cross-platform support:** Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
* **Encryption:** Keeps your data private by wrapping it in a secure layer during transit.
* **Programmable:** Offers advanced options for users who want to customize how their network traffic is handled.
* **Lightweight:** Designed to run efficiently without slowing down your device.
