### What it does in plain English
Aictionary is a fast, feature-rich offline dictionary designed to help you truly understand language rather than just memorizing basic translations. It instantly looks up words using a massive local database that works completely without an internet connection. If you encounter a rare term or slang word not found in the standard database, you can connect an artificial intelligence service to generate custom explanations on the fly.

### Who it's for
* Language learners seeking comprehensive usage contexts, definitions, and word frequency data
* Students and professionals reading foreign documents who need quick lookups without breaking their workflow
* Avid readers who want to build personal flashcard decks for spaced repetition review

### Key features
* Lightning-fast local lookups powered by a robust offline database with over eighty thousand entries
* Built-in natural text-to-speech pronunciation supporting multiple voices and services
* One-click card export to desktop flashcard software for easy language review
* Floating lookup window that lets you check words instantly over other active applications
* Detailed corpus statistics displaying word commonality scores and frequency metrics
