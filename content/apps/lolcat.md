### What it does in plain English
This fun utility reads text from standard inputs and outputs it to your screen in vibrant rainbow colors. It works seamlessly inside your command-line workspace to transform dull text logs or files into colorful displays. You can customize the speed of the color shifts and animate your output for a cheerful visual experience.

### Who it's for
* Developers and system administrators who want to inject a bit of fun into text outputs.
* Command-line enthusiasts looking to customize their workspace appearance.
* Anyone who enjoys playful software themes and colorful terminal displays.

### Key features
* Automatically colors any text output with smooth, shifting rainbow gradients.
* Supports customizable animation speeds to cycle colors dynamically.
* Integrates directly into your daily command-line workflows and scripts.
* Lightweight and simple to run with straightforward command arguments.
