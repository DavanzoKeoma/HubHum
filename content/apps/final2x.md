### What it does in plain English
Final2x helps you take small or blurry images and make them larger and sharper. It uses advanced processing models to fill in missing details, making your photos look much clearer than the original. You can use the built-in settings or add your own custom models to get the exact look you want.

### Who it's for
* Photographers or hobbyists who need to enlarge older, low-resolution photos for printing.
* Digital artists who want to upscale their work for high-definition displays.

### Key features
* **High-Quality Upscaling:** Increases image resolution while maintaining sharp edges and textures.
* **Custom Model Support:** Allows you to load your own specialized models for unique image styles.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works smoothly on Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** Provides a clean, straightforward layout for quick image processing.
