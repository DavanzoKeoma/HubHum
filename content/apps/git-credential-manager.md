### What it does in plain English
Git Credential Manager acts as a secure digital vault for your coding account passwords and access tokens. Instead of typing your username and password every time you interact with your online code storage, this tool handles the authentication process automatically in the background. It works seamlessly with major platforms to ensure your sign-in experience is both fast and protected.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use multiple code hosting services and want to avoid repetitive login prompts.
* Professionals who prioritize account security and want to use modern, multi-factor authentication methods for their work.

### Key features
* **Automatic Authentication:** Handles sign-ins for popular platforms like GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, and Azure DevOps automatically.
* **Multi-Factor Support:** Fully supports modern, secure login methods, including multi-factor authentication.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works consistently across Windows, macOS, and Linux environments.
* **Secure Storage:** Uses your computer's built-in, encrypted storage systems to keep your credentials safe.
* **Set and Forget:** Once configured, it runs silently in the background without needing manual user intervention.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, significantly improves security, and saves time by automating logins.
* **Cons:** Requires a one-time setup process, and it is designed to work specifically with HTTP(S) connections rather than SSH.
