### What it does in plain English
vCards lets you import local contact pictures and phone numbers into your phone. The contacts show up with clear photos when you receive calls or messages. It works on both iPhone and Android devices.

### Who it's for
- Anyone who wants a photo to appear when a local number calls or texts.  
- Small business owners keeping a directory of local phone numbers.  
- Users who like to organize their personal contacts with pictures.

### Key features
- Add local contact photos and numbers with a single file.  
- Works with the built‑in address book on iOS and Android.  
- Supports automatic updates via CardDAV subscription.  
- Simple setup using a QR code or manual entry.  
- Free to download and use.

### How to install (Download)
- **Download the contact file:** [Download vCard file (default.vcf)](https://github.com/metowolf/vCards/releases/download/2026.10.28-003420/default.vcf)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, no subscription, works on iPhone and Android, easy to add photos.  
* **Cons:** requires manual import or QR‑code steps, automatic updates need CardDAV setup, limited to contact list management.
