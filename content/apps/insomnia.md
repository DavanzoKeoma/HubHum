### What it does in plain English
Insomnia provides a clean workspace to send test messages to online services and check how they respond. It helps developers and creators verify that their software tools communicate correctly without errors. You can organize your requests neatly and preview visual designs before putting your projects online.

### Who it's for
* Software creators and web developers testing online services
* Quality assurance testers checking digital communication channels
* Technical writers exploring online service documentation

### Key features
* Send requests to various online service protocols and examine responses
* Organize saved requests into clean folders and projects
* Preview visual design specifications for software connections
* Keep your private settings securely stored on your own device
* Extend functionality using community-made add-ons
