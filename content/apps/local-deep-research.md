### What it does in plain English
Local Deep Research is a smart assistant that handles complex web and academic investigations entirely on your hardware. It automatically searches across multiple sources, gathers verified facts, and compiles comprehensive reports complete with proper citations. Because everything runs on your personal machine or private server, your information remains strictly encrypted and confidential.

### Who it's for
* **Researchers and academics** who need to scour scholarly databases and papers while maintaining complete data privacy.
* **Privacy-conscious professionals** who want to query private documents alongside live web results without sending data to third parties.
* **Hobbyists and developers** exploring local artificial intelligence models on their own consumer graphics cards.

### Key features
* **Fully Local Execution:** Operates smoothly on a single consumer graphics card using your preferred open-weight models.
* **Multiple Search Sources:** Connects to academic databases, web search providers, and your own private document libraries.
* **Encrypted Personal Storage:** Secures all your research history and notes in an encrypted database that only you can unlock.
* **Advanced Research Modes:** Switch easily between quick summaries and comprehensive multi-step analytical reports.
* **No Telemetry or Tracking:** Contains zero tracking scripts, telemetry, or external phone-home data collection.
