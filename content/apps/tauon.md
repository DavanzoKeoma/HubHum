### What it does in plain English
Tauon is a powerful music player designed to make listening to your favorite tunes effortless on the desktop. It organizes your local music collection and supports seamless streaming from your personal media servers. With a clean, responsive layout and automatic theming, it lets you enjoy uninterrupted listening with advanced audio features.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts with large local collections of high-quality audio files.
* Listeners who want to connect and stream tunes directly from their home media servers.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, modern desktop application dedicated purely to music playback.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast user interface with automatic theme styling and responsive controls.
* Gapless playback support for listening to live albums or classical pieces without interruptions.
* Easy drag-and-drop song management and one-click archive extraction for new music downloads.
* Direct streaming integration for popular personal media servers like Plex, Jellyfin, and Airsonic.
* Built-in visualizers and handy shortcut tools to lookup track info or artist lyrics online.
