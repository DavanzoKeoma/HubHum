### What it does in plain English
It shows pictures as perfect circles, perfect for profile photos.  
It works fast and doesn’t slow down your app.  
You can add a thin border or change its color easily.

### Who it's for
* Android developers who need circular avatars in their apps.  
* Hobbyists building personal projects that display user pictures.  

### Key features
* Displays any picture as a smooth circle.  
* Keeps the app fast by avoiding extra image copies.  
* Lets you set border width and color.  
* Works with popular image loaders like Picasso or Glide.  

### How to install (Download)
* **Gradle dependency:** Add `implementation 'de.hdodenhof:circleimageview:3.1.0'` to your project.  
* **Releases page:** [View releases and download source]((https://github.com/hdodenhof/CircleImageView/releases))

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, no ads, lightweight, easy to integrate.  
* **Cons:** scale type is fixed to CENTER_CROP, cannot change view bounds, needs fade‑animation disabled in image loaders.
