### What it does in plain English
MangoDisk scans your computer to find hidden storage hogs like forgotten application caches, old build files, and duplicate documents. It safely displays what can be removed without risking your important personal files or system stability. With clear visual charts and safety-checked rules, you can reclaim gigabytes of disk space in just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users trying to free up space when their hard drive runs low on storage.
* Developers and creators who accumulate massive temporary files, local AI models, and compiler caches.
* Anyone who wants an easy way to clean up browsing traces, manage startup programs, and uninstall applications completely.

### Key features
* **Deep Cleanup:** Instantly target hidden browser caches, developer artifacts, unused AI models, and system temporary files.
* **Storage Distribution:** Visualize your drive usage through interactive charts to pinpoint the largest folders and files.
* **Duplicate File Detection:** Safely locate identical files and clear duplicates while always preserving at least one clean copy.
* **Privacy & Optimization:** Remove browsing traces, clean up startup items, and manage app removals with total peace of mind.
* **Safety First:** Relies on read-only scanning by default and validated rules to ensure your critical data is never accidentally touched.
