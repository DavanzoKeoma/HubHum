### What it does in plain English
Shiba acts as a companion preview window for your text documents, automatically updating the display the moment you save a file in your favorite text editor. It supports advanced formatting like tables, mathematical formulas, and diagrams while offering smooth scrolling and automatic positioning. Because it is controlled primarily through keyboard shortcuts, you never have to reach for your mouse just to find your place in a long document.

### Who it's for
* Writers and bloggers drafting documentation or notes in text files
* Software developers looking for a fast, dedicated Markdown preview window
* Anyone who prefers keyboard-driven navigation over clicking through menus

### Key features
* Instantly updates your document view whenever underlying files or folders change
* Full support for rich text elements, math formulas, and flow diagrams
* Completely controllable via keyboard shortcuts for effortless navigation
* Automatically scrolls to match your most recently edited section
* Fast performance powered by Rust and modern web display technology
