### What it does in plain English
Tomato helps you organize your workday into balanced focus intervals and refreshing breaks using the classic Pomodoro technique. It tracks your completed sessions automatically, giving you clear insights into your daily and weekly productivity trends. With its modern, clean interface, keeping up with your tasks and maintaining deep concentration becomes completely effortless.

### Who it's for
* Students managing heavy study schedules and exam prep sessions.
* Remote workers and freelancers seeking to avoid burnout and track daily output.
* Anyone looking to improve personal focus habits without complicated setups.

### Key features
* Clean and modern interface built using fresh design guidelines.
* Comprehensive productivity charts displaying daily, weekly, and monthly focus statistics.
* Customizable timer parameters to fit your personal workflow.
* Support for modern live update notifications on compatible mobile systems.
