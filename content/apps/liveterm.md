### What it does in plain English
LiveTerm lets you design a personal website that looks like a computer terminal. You only edit one simple JSON file, and the site updates instantly. It works on any device with a web browser.

### Who it's for
* Anyone who wants a unique online résumé or portfolio.  
* Hobbyists who enjoy retro computer aesthetics.  
* People needing a quick personal landing page without learning to code.

### Key features
* One‑file configuration – just edit `config.json`.  
* Ready‑made color themes you can pick and change.  
* Shows your name, social links, and a custom ASCII banner.  
* Works on desktop and mobile browsers.  
* Can be hosted for free on services like Vercel.

### How to install (Download)
* **Get the files:** [Download source from GitHub](https://github.com/Cveinnt/LiveTerm)  
* **See it in action:** [Live demo site](https://liveterm.vercel.app)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** completely free, no subscription, easy to customize, works offline after download.  
* **Cons:** you need to edit a JSON file, you must host the site yourself, no built‑in visual editor.
