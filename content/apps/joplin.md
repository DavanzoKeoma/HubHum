### What it does in plain English
Joplin is a digital notebook that keeps all your notes, reminders, and documents safely stored on your computer or phone. Every notebook can be protected with encryption so only you can read your private ideas, even when you sync across devices. Your entire archive stays searchable and available offline without relying on paid cloud subscriptions.

### Who it's for
* Students looking for a tidy, searchable folder system for lecture notes and study checklists.
* Everyday professionals who want full ownership of meeting notes and personal plans.
* Anyone moving away from paid note apps toward a free, privacy-first alternative.

### Key features
* Write clean, structured notes with checklists, tables, images, and attachments.
* Import existing notebooks smoothly from commercial note-taking services.
* Sync your archive privately across desktop and mobile using your own cloud folder.
* Clip full web pages or screenshots straight from your browser into any notebook.
* Protect your connection when syncing over public Wi-Fi with [PureVPN](https://billing.purevpn.com/aff.php?aff=49386762&subid=hubhum_01_MER047) (#ad).
