### What it does in plain English
jfinal_cms is a robust website publishing platform that lets you manage articles, photos, videos, and comments from a single dashboard. It offers flexible layout templates and a structured administrative area to handle user accounts, permissions, and site statistics effortlessly. Whether you are running a personal blog or a multi-section content portal, it provides all the necessary tools out of the box.

### Who it's for
* Content creators looking to launch an organized blog, photo gallery, or video portal.
* Small business owners needing a customizable information site with user management.
* Java enthusiasts wanting a ready-to-run website publishing solution.

### Key features
* Comprehensive administration dashboard for managing content, categories, and announcements.
* Built-in support for multiple visual templates including blogs, photo albums, and video channels.
* User account management with role-based access control and secure authentication options.
* Traffic analytics and visitor statistics tracking built directly into the system.
* Multi-site capabilities allowing you to manage multiple distinct domains from one place.
