### What it does in plain English
Orfeo is a friendly desktop application that helps you learn piano songs using digital music files. As a song plays, visual notes fall onto a virtual keyboard in real time, lighting up the keys so you can easily follow along. You can slow down difficult passages, loop specific sections, and connect a real digital keyboard to practice at your own comfort level.

### Who it's for
* **Piano learners** who want a clear visual way to practice their favorite songs step by step.
* **Music hobbyists** looking for a free, distraction-free tool to explore scales, chords, and sheet music.
* **Teachers and students** needing a reliable practice companion that works entirely offline without account walls.

### Key features
* **Falling-Note Display:** Watch notes flow smoothly onto a 61, 73, or 88-key virtual keyboard colour-coded by track.
* **Speed Control:** Slow down any song or loop tricky sections without altering the pitch.
* **Real Keyboard Support:** Connect a hardware MIDI keyboard to play along with true touch response and low latency.
* **Chord and Scale Explorers:** Browse musical chords, scales, and circle of fifths interactively.
* **Completely Private:** Runs entirely on your device with no accounts, no subscriptions, and zero tracking.
