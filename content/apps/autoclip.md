### What it does in plain English
Autoclip is an open-source desktop tool that takes a single link to a long video—like a podcast or lecture—and automatically chops it up into engaging short clips. It handles formatting for popular platforms, generates stylish captions, designs cover images, and creates ready-to-use publishing descriptions. All editing and rendering happen right on your local machine to keep your workflow private and flexible.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and podcasters looking to repurpose long-form episodes for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
* Educators and course creators who want to share bite-sized highlights of their video lessons online.
* Social media managers who need a streamlined, automated way to produce multiple vertical video variants from a single recording.

### Key features
* One-click conversion from a single video link to multiple formatted short clips.
* Automatic generation of subtitles, title suggestions, descriptions, and hashtags.
* Intelligent speaker tracking that frames subjects properly in vertical formats.
* Local processing option for editing and rendering securely on your personal computer.
* Flexible support for cloud intelligence providers or local AI models depending on your preference.
