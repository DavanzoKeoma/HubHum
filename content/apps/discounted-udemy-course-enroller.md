### What it does in plain English
Discounted-Udemy-Course-Enroller is a handy software application designed to automatically claim free or heavily discounted online courses. It scans popular coupon sources and securely adds valid offers directly to your personal learning account with just one click. You can run it through an intuitive graphical window or use advanced settings to automate the entire process.

### Who it's for
* Lifelong learners who want to build new skills without breaking the bank
* Students looking to supplement their education with diverse subject materials
* Hobbyists wanting to explore photography, coding, business, and creative arts for free

### Key features
* Simple graphical interface for easy everyday navigation
* One-click login support using your existing browser session cookies
* Automated coupon gathering from popular web directories
* Advanced filters to select only the topics you care about
* Command-line options for advanced users who prefer automation
