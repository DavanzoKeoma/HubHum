### What it does in plain English
DocuSeal is an open-source platform that simplifies the process of creating, filling, and signing digital documents. You can easily build form templates, send them to multiple people, and collect secure electronic signatures on any device. It helps individuals and businesses replace paper paperwork with a fast, mobile-friendly online workflow.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners managing client agreements and contracts without expensive software.
* Human resources teams collecting employee onboarding paperwork and signed tax forms.
* Freelancers and contractors looking for a straightforward way to get project proposals signed.

### Key features
* Interactive form builder with a wide selection of customizable fields like signatures, dates, and checkboxes.
* Support for multiple signers per document with automated email notifications.
* Secure storage options that let you keep files locally or connect cloud storage providers.
* Mobile-optimized signing experience that works smoothly on phones, tablets, and computers.
* Built-in signature verification to ensure document authenticity and compliance.
