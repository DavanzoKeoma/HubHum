### What it does in plain English
BoxPlayer acts as a centralized home for all your personal digital files, streaming servers, and favorite reading materials. Instead of jumping between different apps for your cloud storage, movies, music, and electronic books, you can browse, organize, and play everything from a single unified workspace. It also includes intelligent assistant features to help you quickly search through your library and manage documents without hassle.

### Who it's for
* Movie and TV enthusiasts who want to organize local files and cloud storage into a gorgeous, poster-rich library.
* Music listeners and book lovers looking for a clean, single-window app to enjoy albums and read digital documents.
* Anyone juggling multiple online storage accounts who wants an easy way to manage files and search across all of them at once.

### Key features
* **Unified Media Library:** Scans your storage and local folders to automatically fetch posters, episode details, and metadata for movies and TV shows.
* **Multi-Cloud Integration:** Connects with popular cloud storage services and media servers like Jellyfin, Emby, and Plex in one place.
* **Versatile Playback:** Built-in video player supporting subtitles, audio tracks, variable speeds, and continuous playback.
* **Music & Reading:** Dedicated sections for your music collection with custom playlists, plus an electronic book reader for various formats.
* **AI-Powered Assistance:** Smart search and tools to help you organize your files, answer questions about documents, and discover content.
