### What it does in plain English
IGdm provides a focused, clutter-free space on your computer to handle your Instagram direct messages. It allows you to chat with friends and followers without needing to keep a web browser tab open. The app also includes helpful tools to track who has stopped following your account.

### Who it's for
* Social media enthusiasts who prefer using a keyboard for typing long messages.
* Content creators who need to manage multiple conversations efficiently while working on their desktop.

### Key features
* **Dedicated Chat Interface:** A clean, distraction-free window for all your direct messages.
* **Message Quoting:** Easily reply to specific messages to keep your conversations organized.
* **Unfollower Tracking:** Quickly identify which accounts have recently stopped following you.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Works consistently across Windows, Mac, and Linux systems.
