### What it does in plain English
Beszel gives you a clean, easy-to-read command center for watching over your computers and virtual servers in real time. It collects performance numbers over time—like processor usage, memory consumption, and disk health—so you can spot problems before they cause outages. Whenever something goes wrong, it sends instant notifications straight to your preferred messaging apps or email. ### Who it's for
* **Home Lab Enthusiasts:** People running self-hosted applications on home servers who want to keep tabs on container health and resource usage without installing heavy software.
* **System Administrators:** IT professionals managing multiple remote machines who need a quick, unified view of system temperatures, disk capacity, and load averages.
* **Developers:** Creators hosting web applications who want reliable uptime checks and immediate warnings if server resources spike unexpectedly.

### Key features
* **Featherweight footprint:** Uses minimal computer resources compared to traditional tracking platforms, leaving maximum power for your actual apps.
* **Detailed container tracking:** Keeps history of processor, memory, and network utilization for each individual software container you run.
* **Hardware health warnings:** Monitors drive temperature, fan speeds, and S.M.A.R.T. disk status to help prevent sudden hardware failures.
* **Flexible notifications:** Sends alerts through popular messaging services the moment performance thresholds are crossed.
