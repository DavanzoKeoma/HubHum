### What it does in plain English
SquirrelDisk scans your computer to show you exactly which files and folders are taking up the most room. It displays this information in easy-to-read, colorful charts that let you zoom in on specific areas of your drive. Once you find the clutter, you can quickly move unwanted items to the trash or delete them permanently to regain your storage space.

### Who it's for
*   **Everyday computer users** who want to free up space but aren't sure which files are safe to remove.
*   **Power users and IT enthusiasts** who need a fast, visual way to manage large collections of files across local drives or cloud storage.

### Key features
*   **Interactive Visuals:** View your storage as a sunburst or treemap, making it simple to spot the largest folders at a glance.
*   **Safe Cleanup:** Includes built-in protections for system files and settings to prevent accidental deletion of important data.
*   **Cloud and Server Support:** Easily scan connected cloud storage accounts or remote servers over a network.
*   **Secure Deletion:** Offers an optional secure erase feature that overwrites deleted files to ensure they cannot be recovered.
*   **Fast and Lightweight:** Built for speed, the app starts instantly and remains responsive even when scanning millions of files.
