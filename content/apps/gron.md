### What it does in plain English
gron takes complex, nested data files and flattens them into a clear, line-by-line format. This allows you to use basic search tools to quickly locate specific pieces of information within large files. Once you have found what you need, it can even convert that data back into its original format.

### Who it's for
* Data analysts or researchers who need to explore large, undocumented data files.
* Anyone who works with web data and needs a simple way to find specific values without complex software.

### Key features
* **Simplifies Data:** Converts deeply nested data structures into a flat, readable list.
* **Easy Searching:** Works seamlessly with standard text-searching tools you likely already have.
* **Two-Way Conversion:** Can turn your filtered results back into the original data format.
* **No Dependencies:** A lightweight tool that runs on its own without needing extra software installed.
