### What it does in plain English
Workout.cool is a modern fitness tracking platform designed to help you organize and elevate your physical routines. It lets you build customized workout schedules, monitor your ongoing progress over time, and explore a vast library of detailed exercise instructions. Whether you want to lift weights, practice bodyweight training, or maintain an active lifestyle, this tool keeps all your fitness data neatly organized in one place.

### Who it's for
* Fitness enthusiasts looking for a reliable, privacy-focused way to log their gym sessions and training milestones.
* Personal trainers and coaches who want a structured platform to manage routines and explore diverse exercise movements.
* Self-hosters and open-source advocates who want full control over their fitness data without relying on locked-down commercial apps.

### Key features
* **Comprehensive Exercise Database:** Browse through a wide variety of exercises complete with detailed descriptions and instructions.
* **Custom Workout Planner:** Design, organize, and schedule your personal workout routines tailored to your specific fitness goals.
* **Progress Tracking:** Log your training sessions and monitor your physical improvement over time.
* **Open Source and Self-Hostable:** Easily run your own private instance using Docker or Node.js to keep your health data entirely under your control.
* **Data Import Support:** Quickly populate your exercise catalog using convenient CSV import tools.

### How to install (Download)
You can access the platform directly or host it yourself. Visit the [Official Website](https://workout.cool) to learn more, or check out the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/Snouzy/workout-cool) for self-hosting guides and Docker setup instructions.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no mandatory subscriptions or hidden paywalls, highly customizable self-hosting options.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge or a server setup if you prefer hosting it privately instead of using the main web platform.
