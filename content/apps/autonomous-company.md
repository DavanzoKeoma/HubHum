### What it does in plain English
Autonomous Company creates a team of digital experts that work together to complete tasks like research, coding, and project management on your own machine. You set the goals and boundaries, and the system runs in a continuous loop to execute work, track progress, and update you on the results. It acts as a persistent digital workforce that operates independently while you focus on higher-level decision-making.

### Who it's for
*   **Software Developers** who want to automate repetitive coding tasks, testing, or project documentation.
*   **Product Managers and Entrepreneurs** who need to rapidly prototype ideas or conduct market research using a team of specialized AI personas.

### Key features
*   **Expert Personas:** Includes 14 distinct AI roles—such as CEO, CTO, and Marketing Expert—that use specific mental models to approach problems.
*   **Continuous Loop:** Operates as a background service that automatically cycles through planning, execution, and review phases.
*   **Local Dashboard:** Provides a visual interface to monitor project timelines, review work history, and manage active tasks.
*   **Flexible Workflows:** Supports various operational chains, from new product evaluation to feature development and launch preparation.
*   **Safety Controls:** Built-in guardrails prevent agents from performing destructive actions like deleting system files or force-pushing code.
