### What it does in plain English
Hongguo Player is a dedicated desktop application that lets you watch popular short drama series directly on your Windows or macOS computer. Instead of reaching for your phone or running heavy virtual phone software, you can use your mouse wheel and keyboard shortcuts to effortlessly browse, watch, and binge shows. It provides an immersive viewing experience with automatic episode progression, custom window sizing, and clean fullscreen controls.

### Who it's for
* Everyday entertainment fans who prefer watching short video series on a comfortable computer screen rather than a mobile phone.
* Multitaskers who want to keep an episode running in a small pinned window while working or browsing the web.

### Key features
* **Immersive Desktop Viewing:** Browse recommendations and switch episodes smoothly using your mouse wheel or keyboard shortcuts.
* **Automatic Episode Progression:** Automatically jumps to the next video as soon as the current one finishes playing.
* **Vertical and Mini Window Modes:** Automatically adjusts window dimensions for vertical videos and supports always-on-top mini player view.
* **Interactive Community Features:** View comments, leave feedback, and interact with fellow viewers right from your desktop.
* **Flexible Customization:** Supports automatic updates, dark and light themes, and high-quality picture enhancements.
