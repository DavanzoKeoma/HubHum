### What it does in plain English
DoKit is a comprehensive development companion designed to simplify how development teams build, test, and polish software applications. It bundles dozens of everyday troubleshooting utilities—such as performance monitors, visual alignment tools, and network traffic inspectors—directly into your app's testing environment. Instead of building custom diagnostic screens for every project, developers can instantly plug in this toolkit to streamline testing, catch bugs early, and collaborate smoothly.

### Who it's for
* Mobile and web app developers looking to save time on repetitive debugging tasks.
* Quality assurance testers who need quick access to device logs, network conditions, and performance metrics.
* Product designers checking layout spacing, color values, and user interface consistency on real devices.

### Key features
* **Performance Monitoring:** Keep track of app speed, memory consumption, processor load, and network traffic in real time.
* **Visual Inspection Tools:** Use color eyedroppers, alignment rulers, and element borders to verify user interface layouts instantly.
* **Network Mocking:** Intercept app network requests to simulate data responses without modifying backend code.
* **Sandbox Browser:** Inspect, preview, and clean internal app files, databases, and local storage directly from your device.
* **Custom Integrations:** Easily add your own team-specific diagnostic tools into the unified floating control panel.

### How to install (Download)
To add DoKit to your projects, visit the official setup instructions and release notes available on the [DoKit Releases Page](https://github.com/didi/DoKit/releases) and the [DoKit Official Website](https://www.dokit.cn/). Please ensure you integrate the toolkit strictly within your testing or debug environments according to the official documentation.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Packed with dozens of built-in helpers, reduces duplicate testing code, works across multiple platforms.
* **Cons:** Must be carefully removed or disabled before releasing apps to the public app stores.
