### What it does in plain English
Isso allows website owners to add a comment section to their pages that respects user privacy. Unlike many popular comment services, it does not track your visitors or display advertisements. It is designed to be a lightweight, self-hosted solution that you control entirely.

### Who it's for
* Bloggers and website owners who want to engage with readers without compromising their privacy.
* Developers looking for a simple, self-hosted alternative to third-party comment platforms.

### Key features
* **Privacy-Focused:** Does not track users or serve ads, keeping your site clean and fast.
* **Easy Migration:** Effortlessly import existing comments from other popular comment services.
* **Markdown Support:** Allows your readers to format their comments using simple text-based styling.
* **Moderation Tools:** Includes a built-in queue to review and approve comments before they appear publicly.
* **Lightweight:** The client-side code is very small, ensuring your website remains fast for your visitors.

### How to install (Download)
Isso is a self-hosted web application. You can find the official installation guides and setup instructions at the [Isso Documentation website](https://isso-comments.de/docs/reference/installation/). For those who prefer containerized setups, the official image is available on the [Isso GitHub Packages page](https://github.com/isso-comments/isso/pkgs/container/isso).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no tracking, full control over your data, and supports easy migration.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up on your own server, as it is not a "plug-and-play" hosted service.
