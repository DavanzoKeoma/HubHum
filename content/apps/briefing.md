### What it does in plain English
Briefing is a private video chat tool that lets you connect with others directly through your web browser. It uses modern technology to ensure your conversations remain secure without requiring you to download or install any complex programs. You can start a meeting instantly by simply sharing a link with your participants.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a quick, no-fuss way to host private video meetings.
* Website owners who want to embed a custom video chat feature into their own online spaces.

### Key features
* **Browser-based:** Works instantly in all modern web browsers with no installation required.
* **Privacy-focused:** Built using open technologies designed to keep your data secure.
* **Versatile tools:** Includes built-in features like screen sharing and text chat for better collaboration.
* **Easy sharing:** Start a conversation and invite others by simply sending them a link.
* **Customizable:** Can be embedded into your own website or hosted on your own server for full control.

### How to install (Download)
* **Use it online:** Start a meeting immediately at [Brie.fi/ng](https://brie.fi/ng).
* **Mobile access:** Download the [free iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/app/briefing-video-chat/id1510803601).
* **Self-hosting:** For advanced users, instructions for hosting on your own server are available in the [official documentation](https://github.com/holtwick/briefing/tree/main/docs/installation).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no account setup required, and works directly in the browser.
* **Cons:** Advanced customization or self-hosting requires some technical knowledge.
