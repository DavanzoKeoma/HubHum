### What it does in plain English
Nectarlink bridges your Android device and computer so they function as a single connected system. You can view phone notifications, read and reply to messages, make calls, and transfer files instantly over your local network. It also lets you mirror your phone screen or use your phone camera as a webcam on your computer without needing any cloud accounts.

### Who it's for
* Smartphone users who want to check text messages and notifications on their computer screen without picking up their phone.
* People who frequently need to transfer photos, documents, and other files back and forth between mobile and desktop devices.
* Privacy-conscious users looking for a secure tool that keeps their data local without forcing them to create external user accounts.

### Key features
* **Unified Notifications & Messaging:** See phone notifications, reply to texts, and handle chat apps directly from your computer desktop.
* **Instant File & Clipboard Sharing:** Move files at Wi-Fi speeds and sync text or images between your devices safely.
* **Phone Screen Mirroring:** View your entire phone screen in a dedicated window and control it using your computer mouse and keyboard.
* **Webcam & Camera Continuity:** Use your high-quality phone camera as a wireless webcam for video meetings and recording.
* **Local-First & Encrypted:** Operates entirely over your home network or USB cable with end-to-end encryption and no cloud tracking.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, local-first design with no required accounts or cloud subscriptions.
* **Cons:** Initial setup requires pairing devices and granting local network permissions, and some advanced features need wireless debugging enabled on Android.
