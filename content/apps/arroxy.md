### What it does in plain English
Arroxy is a free application that lets you save videos, music, and entire playlists from thousands of popular websites directly to your computer. It includes a built-in web browser so you can log into your accounts and safely download private or member-only content without complicated setups. The software processes everything locally on your device, ensuring complete privacy with zero advertisements or hidden subscription fees.

### Who it's for
* Content enthusiasts who want to save educational lectures, music, and favorite videos for offline viewing.
* People looking for a straightforward way to grab audio tracks or subtitles from online media platforms.
* Users who value privacy and prefer an ad-free desktop tool over clunky online conversion websites.

### Key features
* Built-in web browser allowing direct downloads and personal account sign-ins for private media.
* Support for thousands of popular online video and audio platforms.
* High-resolution output capabilities including high frame rates and surround sound audio tracks.
* Convenient tools to extract audio tracks into standard formats like MP3.
* Automated subtitle extraction and sponsor segment skipping options.
