### What it does in plain English
LibrePods unlocks the full potential of your AirPods by letting them work seamlessly with Android and Linux devices. It gives you access to essential controls like noise cancellation, battery levels, and automatic ear detection that are usually locked to Apple products. This tool ensures you get the same premium audio experience without needing to own an iPhone or Mac.

### Who it's for
*   **Android users with AirPods:** People who want to use their Apple earbuds with their Android phone without losing key features.
*   **Linux enthusiasts:** Desktop users who want to manage their Bluetooth audio devices with the same precision as on a laptop.
*   **Tech-savvy individuals:** Users who prefer open-source tools and want to customize their audio setup beyond standard Bluetooth limits.

### Key features
*   **Noise Control:** Switch between active noise cancellation, transparency, and off modes directly from your phone or computer.
*   **Battery Monitoring:** See the exact battery percentage for both earbuds and the charging case in real-time.
*   **Ear Detection:** Automatically pause and resume music when you put your AirPods in or take them out.
*   **Customization:** Rename your AirPods, adjust volume swipe speeds, and configure call controls to suit your preferences.
*   **Cross-Platform Support:** Works on both Android and Linux, with specific features like head gestures available on Android.
