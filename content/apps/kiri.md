### What it does in plain English
Kiri is a versatile desktop utility designed to capture your screen, record video clips, and pull readable text out of images instantly. It lets you snap windows or specific areas, stitch scrolling pages together, and organize all captured material neatly in a local library. Everything runs directly on your computer so your personal files and recordings stay entirely under your control.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to quickly extract text from lecture slides or reference images.
* Content creators and support staff who frequently capture screens, record short video tutorials, and annotate images.
* Anyone looking for an all-in-one local tool to manage screenshots and recordings without relying on cloud services.

### Key features
* Capture any window or custom screen area and instantly annotate it with text, arrows, or blur effects.
* Scroll and stitch long pages together into a single seamless screenshot automatically.
* Extract editable text from images and read QR codes using local text recognition.
* Record clean desktop video clips with system and microphone audio, or save animations as silent GIFs.
* Organize, tag, search, and manage all your captured files inside a secure local media library.
