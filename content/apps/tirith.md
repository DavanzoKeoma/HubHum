### What it does in plain English
Tirith monitors the commands you type into your terminal to spot hidden dangers that standard systems often miss. It identifies deceptive web addresses, malicious hidden text, and risky instructions that could steal your data or damage your files. By acting as a gatekeeper, it stops these threats instantly before they have a chance to affect your computer.

### Who it's for
* **Software developers** who frequently run commands from the internet and want to avoid accidental system compromise.
* **AI power users** who use automated agents to perform tasks and need an extra layer of safety to ensure those agents don't execute harmful code.

### Key features
* **Homograph Protection:** Detects web addresses that use look-alike characters to trick you into visiting malicious sites.
* **Command Inspection:** Blocks dangerous patterns like base64-encoded scripts or commands that attempt to send your private files to external servers.
* **Silent Operation:** Stays invisible while you work, only alerting you when it detects a genuine security risk.
* **Threat Database:** Uses a verified list of known malicious packages and infrastructure to keep your system protected against the latest threats.
