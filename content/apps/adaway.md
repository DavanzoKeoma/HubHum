### What it does in plain English
AdAway is a handy application designed for Android devices that stops annoying advertisements from loading in your apps and web browsers. It runs quietly in the background using local filtering techniques to redirect ad requests away from your device. You gain a cleaner, faster digital experience without paying for a subscription or seeing intrusive banners.

### Who it's for
* Everyday Android users tired of intrusive pop-ups and video ads inside mobile applications.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to cut down on invisible data trackers running on their phones.
* People who want a free, open-source tool to keep their phone screens clean and save mobile data bandwidth.

### Key features
* **System-wide blocking:** Stops unwanted promotional content across both web browsers and installed apps.
* **Open-source transparency:** Built completely in the open with community-driven lists and regular updates.
* **Custom filtering lists:** Lets you add your own preferred sources or whitelist specific apps and websites.
* **Lightweight operation:** Designed to run efficiently in the background without draining your battery quickly.
* **Backup and restore:** Easily save your custom preferences and blocklists before switching or updating devices.
