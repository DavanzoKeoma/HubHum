### What it does in plain English
MarkFlowy is a versatile writing tool that helps you organize your thoughts and documents directly on your computer. It allows you to switch between a clean visual view and a technical formatting view, depending on how you prefer to work. Because it stores everything locally, your files remain private and accessible even without an internet connection.

### Who it's for
*   **Writers and Students:** People who need a distraction-free environment to draft essays, articles, or personal notes.
*   **Researchers and Developers:** Users who want to manage large collections of documents with advanced features like math support, diagrams, and quick search.

### Key features
*   **Flexible Editing:** Switch easily between visual editing, raw formatting mode, and a focused reading view.
*   **High Performance:** Designed to open and scroll through large documents smoothly without lag.
*   **Local Control:** Keep your files in your own folders, giving you full ownership and privacy over your data.
*   **Customizable Workspace:** Use tabs, split-screen views, and bookmarks to organize your projects exactly how you like.
*   **Optional AI Assistance:** Integrate your own AI services for summarizing or translating text if you choose to do so.
