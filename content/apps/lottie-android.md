### What it does in plain English
Lottie turns designer‑made motion graphics into tiny files that play directly inside Android apps.  
It lets the same animation look great on phones, tablets, and even other platforms without extra effort.  
You get smooth, high‑quality motion without needing to film or code each frame yourself.

### Who it's for
* Mobile app designers who want their animations to appear exactly as designed.  
* Developers who need an easy way to add motion without writing complex code.  
* Small teams that want a fast, lightweight solution for animated UI elements.

### Key features
- Plays complex motion graphics without slowing down the app.  
- Uses tiny JSON files instead of large video clips.  
- Works on Android phones and tablets out of the box.  
- The same animation can be reused on iOS and the web.  
- Add it with just one line in your app’s setup.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, open source, tiny file size, works across multiple platforms.  
* **Cons:** requires adding a small helper to your project, only works with animations exported in the supported format, needs AndroidX‑compatible projects.
