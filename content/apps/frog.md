### What it does in plain English
Frog makes grabbing text from uncopyable places as simple as taking a snapshot. Just capture any area on your screen containing words, video subtitles, or QR codes, and the application instantly copies the clean text to your clipboard. It removes the frustration of retyping information from pictures or video frames manually.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to copy quotes from lecture slides, scanned articles, or videos.
* Translators and writers working with physical document photos or reference images.
* Anyone who frequently encounters QR codes on desktop videos or images and needs them decoded.

### Key features
* Instantly extracts text from photos, screenshots, videos, and PDFs.
* Built-in QR code decoder to read codes directly from your screen.
* Clean, minimalist interface designed specifically for modern desktop environments.
* Lightweight operation that runs smoothly without slowing down your computer.
