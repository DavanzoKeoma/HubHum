### What it does in plain English
Wego is a lightweight weather checker designed specifically for text-based computer windows. It fetches current conditions, multi-day forecasts, wind speeds, and temperatures for any city worldwide. You can customize how the information looks using simple text art or colorful icons.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and system administrators who live inside command-line interfaces.
* Minimalist computer users who prefer fast text-based tools over heavy desktop widgets.
* Anyone looking for a quick, distraction-free way to check local weather before stepping outside.

### Key features
* Displays temperature ranges, wind direction, viewing distance, and rain chances.
* Supports multiple weather data providers, including free options that require no setup keys.
* Offers flexible display styles like ASCII art tables, compact emojis, and markdown.
* Saves your preferred default locations and settings automatically for instant future checks.
* Caches downloaded weather details locally to save internet bandwidth and speed up responses.
