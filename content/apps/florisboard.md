### What it does in plain English
FlorisBoard replaces your standard phone keyboard with a modern alternative that places your personal privacy first. It functions entirely on your device without sending what you type to external servers or cloud networks. You can easily tweak its appearance, manage copied text snippets, and type with complete peace of mind.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious smartphone users who want to prevent corporate data harvesting while typing.
* People who love personalizing their phone interface with custom themes and layouts.
* Users looking for handy built-in tools like a fast clipboard history manager and emoji picker.

### Key features
* **Strict Privacy:** Operates entirely offline with no built-in data collection or keystroke tracking.
* **Clipboard History:** Safely view, pin, and paste previously copied text snippets directly from the keyboard.
* **Advanced Theming:** Customize colors, button styles, and layouts to match your favorite aesthetic.
* **Emoji Support:** Quickly search and insert emojis with dedicated quick-access menus.
