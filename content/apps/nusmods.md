### What it does in plain English
NUSMods is a digital platform designed to help students organize their academic lives. It provides a comprehensive module search engine, a course catalogue, and an interactive timetable builder. Students can visualize their weekly schedules, avoid time clashes, and plan their academic journey efficiently.

### Who it's for
* University students looking to organize their semester schedules without headaches
* Academic advisors and peers helping others choose optimal modules
* Anyone wanting a streamlined way to explore university courses and timetables

### Key features
* Interactive timetable builder with visual drag-and-drop clarity
* Comprehensive module search and detailed course information catalogue
* Mobile-friendly web interface that pins easily to phone home screens
* Export tools to seamlessly sync your academic schedule with calendar apps
* Open-source community project built by students, for students

### How to install (Download)
NUSMods runs entirely as a web application and requires no installation. You can access the platform instantly through your web browser by visiting the [NUSMods Website](https://nusmods.com).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no account required to start building timetables, highly responsive design for phones and computers.
* **Cons:** Specifically tailored for a single university institution, requires an internet connection to sync live course data.
