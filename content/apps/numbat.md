### What it does in plain English
Numbat is a specialized programming language designed for math, physics, and engineering calculations. It understands physical measurements like speed, time, and money automatically, checking your work as you type. If you accidentally try to add meters to hours, it immediately alerts you so your results are always correct.

### Who it's for
* Students and teachers tackling physics, chemistry, or engineering homework.
* Scientists and researchers performing complex everyday unit conversions.
* Engineers and developers who need a strict, reliable calculation environment.

### Key features
* Automatically tracks physical dimensions like length, weight, and temperature as you calculate.
* Converts between different unit systems seamlessly using simple arrow commands.
* Includes a massive built-in library of universal physical constants and formulas.
* Strictly enforces math rules so you never get silent, incorrect calculation results.
* Offers an interactive command-line experience with history and auto-complete.
