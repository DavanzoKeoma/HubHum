### What it does in plain English
Provenance is a versatile game emulator hub designed for Apple devices, bringing over fifty classic video game systems directly to your phone, tablet, or living room television. It neatly organizes your digital game collection with automatic box art, descriptions, and metadata while letting you save your progress at any time. You can easily connect standard wireless controllers, sync your saved games across multiple Apple devices using your personal cloud storage, and enhance the visual style with custom video filters.

### Who it's for
* Retro gaming enthusiasts who want to revisit classic console and handheld titles on modern Apple hardware.
* Apple TV owners looking to transform their living room setup into an all-in-one retro entertainment center.
* Gamers who appreciate neat library organization, automatic artwork downloading, and flexible controller support.

### Key features
* Supports over fifty vintage console and handheld systems including classic Nintendo, Sega, and Sony platforms.
* Cross-device synchronization for your game files, save states, artwork, and settings via cloud integration.
* Custom video filter options including authentic display shaders and high-definition enhancements.
* Automatic library cataloging that fetches box art and game details from online databases.
* Flexible import methods allowing you to load game files easily via local storage or wireless transfer.
