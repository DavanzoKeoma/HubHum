### What it does in plain English
Memos gives you a private, distraction-free space to record quick thoughts, daily notes, and longer journal entries. Every note lands neatly on a personal timeline organized by day, making it effortless to look back through your memories. You decide whether each thought stays private, gets shared with close friends.

### Who it's for
* **Journal keepers** who want a fast, daily thought log that they fully own and control.
* **Writers and researchers** looking to capture web snippets, ideas, and references using tags and text formatting.
* **Privacy-focused individuals** who prefer keeping their personal notes on their own server rather than renting a closed commercial service.

### Key features
* **Daily timeline feed:** Jot down a quick sentence or a lengthy entry, letting your thoughts naturally flow onto a calendar-backed timeline.
* **Flexible sharing controls:** Easily switch individual notes between private, shared with peers, or fully public.
* **Web clipper integration:** Save articles, highlights, and images directly from your browser straight into your personal note archive.
* **Chat and script connections:** Send quick thoughts via chat bots or connect external scripts using modern developer tools.

### How to install (Download)
Memos runs smoothly as a web service on your own machine or private server. Prefer not to manage servers by hand? You can also view setup guides and checksum verification files directly through the [Official Demo and Documentation](https://usememos.com/docs).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source forever, complete data ownership, flexible sharing options.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of server setup or container tools to self-host.
