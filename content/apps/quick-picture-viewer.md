### What it does in plain English
Quick Picture Viewer is a streamlined desktop application designed to open and browse image files smoothly on Windows computers. It eliminates the slow loading times of built-in system software, letting you flip, rotate, crop, and convert photos without hassle. You can also organize slideshows, pin images on top of other windows, and apply handy visual effects using built-in plugins.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and hobbyists who need to quickly inspect large batches of image files.
* Everyday computer users looking for a faster, more responsive photo viewer to replace standard operating system defaults.
* Anyone who wants a clean, distraction-free interface for browsing pictures without heavy software bloat.

### Key features
* Supports a wide variety of standard and advanced image formats, including photos, icons, and graphic designs.
* Provides basic editing tools such as cropping, rotating, flipping, and changing file formats.
* Features a convenient picture-in-picture mode and an always-on-top window option.
* Includes customizable slideshow capabilities and dark mode styling.
* Expandable with optional plugins for extra visual effects and screenshot tools.
