### What it does in plain English
GodotProjectZero is an experimental, text-driven idle adventure game inspired by classic minimalistic browser games. Players slowly uncover a mysterious world by gathering basic resources, establishing passive generation, and upgrading their equipment. As you progress, you unlock new mechanics, fight ancient colossals, and unravel the story hidden within the dark forest.

### Who it's for
* Fans of text-based idle games and incremental progression loops.
* Players looking for a lightweight, atmospheric adventure experience.
* Gamers interested in community-driven open-source indie projects.

### Key features
* Minimalistic text-based interface that lets you focus on strategy and resource management.
* Idle progression system to gather resources passively over time.
* Engaging combat encounters against ancient bosses and unique creatures.
* Multilingual support including English, French, Chinese, Portuguese, Thai, and Spanish.
* Open-source community project built entirely with the Godot engine.
