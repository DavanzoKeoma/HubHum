### What it does in plain English
Blue Archive Auto Script is a desktop companion designed to handle repetitive gameplay chores without manual intervention. It interacts with your computer's screen to manage daily rewards, character schedules, shop purchases, and battle stages automatically. By setting up your preferences in a friendly control panel, you can effortlessly keep your game characters leveled up and well-equipped while you focus on other things.

### Who it's for
* Dedicated mobile gamers who want to save time on repetitive daily resource farming.
* Busy players who need an automated way to collect daily rewards and clear standard stages.
* Fans looking for a customizable assistant to handle character affection and routine tasks seamlessly.

### Key features
* **Automated Daily Tasks:** Takes care of routine check-ins, mail collection, shop purchases, and daily reward claims.
* **Character Progression:** Manages student schedules, affection boosts, crafting queues, and chat conversations effortlessly.
* **Stage Clearing:** Automatically runs main story chapters, special commissions, and event stages using custom rules.
* **Flexible Display Support:** Compatible with standard 16:9 screen ratios across various windowed setups and emulators.
* **Custom Scheduling:** Lets you configure exact refresh times and execution intervals to match your personal routine.
