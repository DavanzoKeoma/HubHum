### What it does in plain English
Kimai is a professional tool designed to help you track exactly how much time you spend on various projects and tasks. It allows you to organize your work, create clear reports for your clients, and generate invoices based on the time you have recorded. Whether you are working alone or managing a large team, it provides a centralized way to keep your project finances and schedules in order.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers who need to track billable hours for multiple clients.
* Small businesses and organizations that need to manage team timesheets and project budgets.

### Key features
* **Flexible Time Tracking:** Start and stop timers easily or manually enter your hours for any task.
* **Professional Invoicing:** Turn your tracked time directly into clear, professional invoices for your clients.
* **Detailed Reporting:** Access advanced insights into how your time is spent across different projects and customers.
* **Team Management:** Set up custom roles and permissions to manage large groups of users across different time zones.
* **Highly Customizable:** Use tags, categories, and various plugins to tailor the system to your specific workflow.

### How to install (Download)
Kimai is a web-based application. You can get started in two ways:
* **Cloud Version:** Use the [Kimai Cloud](https://www.kimai.cloud/) for a ready-to-use service without managing your own server.
* **Self-Hosted:** If you prefer to host it yourself, follow the [Official Installation Guide](https://www.kimai.org/documentation/installation.html) or use the [Official Docker Images](https://hub.docker.com/r/kimai/kimai2).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly scalable for teams of any size, and supports multi-language interfaces.
* **Cons:** Requires a web server and basic technical knowledge to set up if you choose to host it yourself.
