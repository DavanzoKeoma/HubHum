### What it does in plain English
Jotsy is a clean, distraction-free space where you can write down your thoughts and ideas. Because it is self-hosted, you keep full control over your data rather than storing it on a third-party service. It focuses on speed and simplicity, ensuring that your writing experience remains smooth and lightweight.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want a private, secure place to store personal notes.
* Tech-savvy users who prefer hosting their own tools to maintain data privacy.

### Key features
* **Distraction-Free Writing:** A clean interface that lets you focus entirely on your text.
* **Flexible Formatting:** Supports both standard plain text and markdown for structured notes.
* **Multi-User Support:** Allows multiple people to have their own private accounts on the same instance.
* **Resource Efficient:** Built to be extremely fast and light, so it runs smoothly on modest hardware.
* **Secure Access:** Includes built-in login and session management to keep your notes protected.

### How to install (Download)
Jotsy is a web-based application designed to be hosted on your own server or computer. You can access the project and setup instructions at the [Official Jotsy Website](https://app.jotsy.dev).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, very lightweight, and puts you in total control of your data.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup and maintenance to get it running.
