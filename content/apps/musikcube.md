### What it does in plain English
Musikcube is a high-performance music player that runs directly in your computer's command window, keeping your desktop clean and distraction-free. It automatically scans your music folders to build a searchable library and includes a built-in server to stream your songs to other devices. You can control the entire experience using simple keyboard shortcuts, making it perfect for power users who want speed and efficiency.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who prefer a fast, keyboard-controlled interface over heavy graphical software.
* People who want to turn a spare computer or a small device like a Raspberry Pi into a dedicated home music server.

### Key features
* **Efficient Library Management:** Automatically indexes your audio files for instant searching and browsing.
* **Keyboard-First Navigation:** Manage your entire music collection without ever needing to touch your mouse.
* **Built-in Streaming:** Share your music library across your local network to other devices or computers.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Works consistently on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Raspberry Pi devices.
* **Remote Control:** Use the companion mobile app to control your music playback from anywhere in your home.
