### What it does in plain English
SwiftyBeaver shows you colorful notes about what your app is doing while you write code.  
It can write those notes to the Xcode window, to a file on your computer, or send them to an online service.  
This helps you find problems fast and keep a record of what happened.

### Who it's for
* iOS app developers who want quick, readable feedback while coding.  
* Server‑side Swift programmers who need to keep logs on disk or in the cloud.  
* Anyone who prefers clear, colorful messages instead of plain text.

### Key features
* Bright, easy‑to‑read messages appear in Xcode.  
* Save a copy of every message to a file for later review.  
* Send logs to cloud services such as Google Cloud with a single line of code.  
* Choose how each message looks, even turn it into JSON.  
* Works with Swift 3, 4 and 5 on iPhone, Mac and servers.
