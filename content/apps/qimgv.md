### What it does in plain English
qimgv is a streamlined application that lets you browse through your photos and images with high speed. It keeps your workspace clean by hiding menus until you actually need them. Beyond viewing, it allows you to quickly sort, move, or perform simple edits like cropping and rotating your files.

### Who it's for
* Casual users who want a faster, more responsive alternative to their default system photo viewer.
* Photographers and hobbyists who need to quickly sort through large folders of images and perform basic cleanup tasks.

### Key features
* **Clean Interface:** A minimalist design that stays out of your way, showing tools only when you need them.
* **Efficient Management:** Easily move or copy files into specific folders using simple keyboard shortcuts.
* **Basic Editing:** Perform quick adjustments like cropping, resizing, and rotating images without needing heavy software.
* **High-Quality Display:** Features advanced scaling filters to ensure your photos look sharp and clear.
* **Video Support:** Includes experimental playback capabilities for video files within the same interface.
