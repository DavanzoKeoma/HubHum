### What it does in plain English
Anubias provides a friendly visual workspace where you can drag and drop components to design fully functional applications. It bridges the gap between creative design and native app development across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers. By offering pre-made templates and a simplified scripting language, it lets creators bring their software ideas to life quickly.

### Who it's for
* Independent creators and hobbyists who want to build custom mobile or desktop applications without learning advanced programming languages.
* Designers looking for a practical way to turn visual layouts into functional software prototypes.
* Small business owners seeking a straightforward tool to create simple internal utilities or client apps.

### Key features
* **Drag-and-Drop Interface:** Easily arrange components visually on your screen to design custom user layouts.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Build applications targeted for mobile platforms as well as desktop operating systems.
* **Free and Accessible:** Completely free software tool designed to make app creation open to everyone.
* **Pre-Made Templates:** Kickstart your projects using a variety of included starter layouts and templates.
* **Simplified Scripting:** Utilizes an intuitive scripting style designed to feel familiar and straightforward.
