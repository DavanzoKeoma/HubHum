### What it does in plain English
This tool takes your standard images and intentionally introduces data errors to create a "glitched" look. It works directly in your web browser, allowing you to experiment with different levels of distortion. The result is a stylized, artistic version of your original photo that captures a modern digital aesthetic.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and graphic designers looking for quick, unique visual effects.
* Social media creators who want to give their photos a retro or experimental edge.

### Key features
* **Browser-based:** No need to install complex software to start creating.
* **Instant Preview:** See how your image changes in real-time as you adjust settings.
* **Customizable Distortion:** Control the intensity and style of the glitch effect.
* **Desktop Option:** A separate version is available for those who prefer a standalone desktop application.

### How to install (Download)
* **Web Version:** You can use the tool immediately in your browser at the [Official Online Demo](https://snorpey.github.io/jpg-glitch/).
* **Desktop Version:** Download the standalone application for your computer at the [Desktop App Page](https://snorpey.github.io/jpg-glitch-electron/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, requires no technical knowledge, and works directly in your browser.
* **Cons:** The effect is randomized, so you cannot precisely control every pixel, and it is limited to the JPEG file format.
