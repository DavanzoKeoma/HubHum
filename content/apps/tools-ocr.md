### What it does in plain English
Tree Hole OCR allows you to capture any part of your screen or open a document to instantly turn images into editable text. Because it processes everything locally on your own machine, your data never leaves your computer for external servers. It is a simple, efficient way to copy text from sources that do not normally allow it, such as videos, protected PDFs, or static images.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to quickly digitize notes or textbook excerpts.
* Office workers who frequently deal with scanned documents or images containing important data.

### Key features
* **Total Privacy:** Operates entirely offline, ensuring your sensitive documents remain on your device.
* **Flexible Capture:** Use a simple keyboard shortcut to select any area of your screen for immediate text extraction.
* **Document Support:** Easily process PDF files alongside standard image formats.
* **Precision Controls:** Fine-tune your selection area using keyboard shortcuts for perfect accuracy.

### How to install (Download)
You can download the latest version of the application from the [official release page](https://github.com/litongjava/tools-ocr/releases/). Please ensure you unzip the downloaded folder and avoid using Chinese characters in the file path where you save the program.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, works offline, and respects user privacy by not sending data to the cloud.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern operating system (Mac OS X 12.6 or newer) and may require manual permission settings for screen recording on some systems.
