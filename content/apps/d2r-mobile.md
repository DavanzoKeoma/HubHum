### What it does in plain English
This project lets you run your legally owned console adventure game natively on modern mobile phones, tablets, and personal computers. It translates system commands and graphics on the fly without heavy emulation, ensuring smooth 60 frames-per-second gameplay. You simply bring your own game files, and the software handles the touch controls, widescreen layouts, and extra endgame features.

### Who it's for
* Console game owners who want to enjoy their favorite title on mobile devices and laptops during travel or away from home.
* Mobile gamers looking for deep, full-scale role-playing experiences with fully customized touch controls and gamepad support.
* Enthusiasts who want to experience classic action gameplay with modern quality-of-life additions like enhanced stash tabs and automated buffs.

### Key features
* **Native execution:** Runs the original game program directly on your device hardware without lagging CPU emulation layers.
* **Custom touch interface:** Features gesture controls, virtual joysticks, and rearranged menus designed specifically for touchscreens.
* **Controller support:** Instantly switches to standard gamepad layouts the moment you plug in an Xbox or PlayStation controller.
* **Expanded endgame modes:** Adds fresh challenges including custom rifts, extended stash spaces, and automated inventory management.
* **Multi-platform support:** Compatible with mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers using your own game files.
