### What it does in plain English
Arwes helps you design web pages that look and sound like advanced technology from science fiction movies and games. It provides ready-made visual components featuring futuristic panels, lines, and audio feedback. When you need royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits, check out [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Web designers looking to build immersive, game-like portfolio sites or dashboard interfaces.
* Creative developers experimenting with futuristic aesthetics and cyber-themed digital experiences.

### Key features
* Futuristic design elements inspired by science fiction universes and space exploration titles.
* Built-in sound effects and smooth animations that react to user interactions.
* Support for popular JavaScript tools to help structure modern web pages quickly.
