### What it does in plain English
connmap puts a tiny world map on your screen. It updates every second to show where each device you’re talking to is located. You can move the map around just like any other window.

### Who it's for
- Anyone who likes to see where their online friends or work computers are located.  
- Hobbyists who enjoy visualizing active network connections.  
- Users curious about the geographic spread of their internet traffic.

### Key features
- Shows a world map right on your desktop.  
- Updates every second so locations stay current.  
- You can drag the map to any spot on the screen.  
- Works on Linux systems using X11 or Wayland.  
- Simple text file lets you change size, colors, and refresh speed.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** completely free, no subscription, open source, lightweight.  
* **Cons:** works only on Linux desktops, shows only IPv4 addresses, requires a few manual setup steps.
