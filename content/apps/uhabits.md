### What it does in plain English
Loop Habit Tracker is a clean and lightweight mobile application designed to help you form positive daily routines and break unwanted ones. It monitors your consistency over time using smart scoring formulas that forgive occasional missed days without ruining your progress. Everything works completely offline on your device, ensuring your personal routine data remains entirely private.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking to build lasting personal fitness, study, or wellness routines.
* People who value complete data privacy and want a mobile tracker that does not require an online account.
* Users who prefer a distraction-free, ad-free experience without hidden fees or premium subscriptions.

### Key features
* **Smart Habit Scoring:** Calculate your routine strength accurately while avoiding severe penalties for occasional missed days.
* **Flexible Schedules:** Track daily habits or set custom schedules like specific days of the week or every other day.
* **Interactive Home Screen Widgets:** Check off your habits directly from your phone screen without opening the app.
* **Custom Reminders:** Receive gentle notifications at tailored times of day so you never forget your goals.
* **Complete Data Ownership:** Export your history easily to spreadsheets or database files whenever you choose.
