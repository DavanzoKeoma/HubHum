### What it does in plain English
Clipy sits quietly in your menu bar, remembering every piece of text and image you copy throughout your workday. Whenever you need an older item, a simple keyboard shortcut opens a neat menu containing your past clipboard history. You can also save frequently used snippets of text to paste instantly with just a couple of keystrokes.

### Who it's for
* Writers and bloggers who frequently reuse paragraphs, links, or templates.
* Developers and designers managing multiple code snippets and color codes.
* Everyday computer users who hate losing text they copied just a few minutes ago.

### Key features
* **Clipboard History:** Stores a searchable record of text and images you have copied over time.
* **Snippets Support:** Organizes frequently used text blocks into custom folders for rapid insertion.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Quick hotkeys let you open menus instantly without touching your mouse.
* **Lightweight & Native:** Designed specifically for macOS with a clean, unobtrusive menu bar presence.
