### What it does in plain English
InvokeAI provides a complete workspace for generating, editing, and refining images using modern artificial intelligence models. It features an intuitive web-based interface that lets you combine node-based workflows with advanced brush tools for painting and fixing pictures. Artists can organize their generated artwork seamlessly while experimenting with different AI generation styles and formats.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and illustrators exploring AI-assisted creation
* Professional designers building custom generation pipelines
* Hobbyists and enthusiasts looking to generate high-quality visual art locally

### Key features
* Unified Canvas for seamless image editing, inpainting, and outpainting
* Flexible node-based workflow system for custom generation pipelines
* Organized gallery system to store, search, and remix previous creations
* Support for a wide variety of popular image and video generation models
* Built-in upscaling tools and model management utilities
