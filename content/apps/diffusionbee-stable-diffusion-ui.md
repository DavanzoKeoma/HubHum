### What it does in plain English
DiffusionBee gives you a friendly, easy-to-use window to generate custom art using artificial intelligence directly on your own computer. You simply type what you want to see, and the app creates a unique picture in seconds. Everything runs privately on your Mac hardware, meaning your creations and ideas stay entirely on your device.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and illustrators looking for free, unlimited AI image generation tools on macOS.
* Content creators and hobbyists who want to experiment with advanced image models without dealing with complex command-line setups.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer generating artwork locally rather than uploading prompts and pictures to third-party web services.

### Key features
* Complete data privacy with local execution on your computer's processor.
* Clean visual window with a simple one-click setup procedure.
* Support for advanced techniques like image modification, inpainting, and outpainting.
* Built-in model manager to download different art styles directly inside the application.
* Generation history tracker so you can easily review and save your favorite past creations.
