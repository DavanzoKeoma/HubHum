### What it does in plain English
Tinycast is a native command center for your Mac that brings your favorite shortcuts, files, and tools right to your fingertips. It opens instantly with a keystroke and runs entirely on your device without slowing down your computer or tracking your activity. Best of all, it seamlessly runs community extensions so you can customize your daily workflow without hassle.

### Who it's for
* Everyday Mac users who want to open apps, search files, and run tasks without lifting their hands from the keyboard.
* Professionals and remote workers looking for a lightweight tool to speed up repetitive daily computer actions.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want a fast launcher that respects their data and contains zero background tracking.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast native design built specifically for modern macOS computers.
* Completely private with zero data collection or background telemetry.
* Compatible with existing productivity extensions to expand what you can do.
* Extremely lightweight on system memory compared to web-based alternatives.
* Free and open source software with transparent development.
