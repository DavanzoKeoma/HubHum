### What it does in plain English
Your Spotify acts as a personal record-keeper for your music streaming activity. It connects to your account to log every song you play and organizes this data into clear, visual charts. You can even import your past listening history to see trends from years ago.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want to analyze their listening patterns and discover their most-played artists.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer to host their own data rather than relying on third-party tracking services.

### Key features
* **Automatic Tracking:** Keeps a continuous log of your music activity in the background.
* **Visual Statistics:** View your top songs, artists, and listening trends on a clean dashboard.
* **Historical Import:** Easily upload your official streaming history files to see data from the beginning of your account.
* **User Management:** Control who can access your dashboard and disable new registrations for added security.
* **Customizable Timezones:** Adjust settings so your statistics accurately reflect your local time.

### How to install (Download)
This application is designed to be hosted on your own computer or server. You can find the setup instructions and the necessary files on the [Official GitHub official project page](https://github.com/Yooooomi/your_spotify). Most users will use the provided `docker-compose` configuration to get the service running quickly.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, no monthly subscription fees, and highly detailed historical analysis.
* **Cons:** Requires basic technical knowledge to set up and maintain, and you must create a developer account with the music service to generate the required access keys.
