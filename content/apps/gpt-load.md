### What it does in plain English
GPT-Load acts as a central control panel that connects all your different AI accounts and subscription keys into one organized system. It automatically handles traffic, switches between accounts if one fails, and keeps track of how much you are spending. You can manage everything from a single, private dashboard that you host yourself.

### Who it's for
* **Power Users:** Individuals who juggle multiple AI subscriptions and want to consolidate them into one place.
* **Small Teams:** Developers or project managers who need to share AI access across a team while monitoring usage costs and performance.

### Key features
* **Unified Access:** Use one single address for all your AI tools, regardless of the provider.
* **Smart Scheduling:** Automatically routes requests to the best available account to ensure your work never stops.
* **Cost & Usage Tracking:** View clear charts showing your spending and token usage over time.
* **Privacy-Focused:** All your credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your own machine or server.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works with major AI providers and supports standard industry communication formats.
