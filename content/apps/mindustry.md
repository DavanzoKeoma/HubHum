### What it does in plain English
Mindustry combines resource management, factory building, and strategic tower defense into a single experience. Players must drill for materials, route them through complex conveyor belts, and manufacture ammunition to power sprawling defense networks. As enemy waves attack your structures, you must continually optimize your production lines and expand your territory to survive.

### Who it's for
* Strategy and puzzle enthusiasts who love optimizing complex logistical systems and conveyor layouts.
* Tower defense fans looking for a deeper, resource-driven survival challenge.
* Gamers who appreciate open-source titles with multiplayer support and active community mods.

### Key features
* Construct intricate conveyor networks to transport items and liquids across your map.
* Produce dozens of advanced materials to fuel turrets, manufacturing blocks, and units.
* Command units, construct mechs, and build helper structures to automate your bases.
* Play across multiple sprawling campaigns or jump into multiplayer battles with friends.
* Enjoy a completely open-source project free from intrusive ads or artificial intelligence-generated assets.
