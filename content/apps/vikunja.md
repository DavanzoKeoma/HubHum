### What it does in plain English
Vikunja is a digital workspace that helps you organize your daily tasks, projects, and reminders in one place. It allows you to view your work through different layouts like lists, boards, or calendars to match your personal workflow. Because you own the data, you can keep your information private and secure without relying on big corporate platforms.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want a clean, distraction-free way to manage personal errands and long-term goals.
* Teams looking for a private, self-hosted space to track shared projects and deadlines.

### Key features
* **Flexible Views:** Switch between list, table, calendar, or board views to see your tasks in the way that makes the most sense for you.
* **Smart Reminders:** Set custom due dates and reminders so you never miss an important deadline.
* **Organized Projects:** Group related tasks into projects and use labels to keep everything categorized.
* **Full Privacy:** Since you can host it yourself, your task data stays on your own terms rather than on a third-party server.
