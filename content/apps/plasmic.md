### What it does in plain English
Plasmic is a visual editor that allows you to build websites and applications by dragging and dropping elements onto a canvas. It bridges the gap between design and programming by letting you integrate your own custom components directly into the workspace. This means you can create polished, professional projects without being limited by the rigid templates found in standard website builders.

### Who it's for
* **Designers** who want to build functional, live websites without waiting for a developer to write every line of code.
* **Developers** who want to speed up their workflow by visually building user interfaces while still maintaining full control over their project's logic.
* **Content Creators** who need to update or create new landing pages for their business without needing to touch the underlying technical setup.

### Key features
* **Visual Drag-and-Drop:** Build complex layouts and interfaces using a modern, intuitive design interface.
* **Codebase Integration:** Seamlessly weave your own custom components into the visual editor to extend functionality.
* **Figma Import:** Convert your existing design files directly into functional web elements.
* **Flexible Publishing:** Host your projects anywhere you choose, giving you full ownership of your work.
* **Content Management:** Create simplified editing modes for non-technical team members to update content safely.
