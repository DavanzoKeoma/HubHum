### What it does in plain English
Claurst is a smart assistant that lives in your computer's command window to help you write, fix, and organize your programming projects. It works alongside you to handle repetitive tasks, explain complex code, and suggest improvements in real-time. Because it runs locally on your machine, you stay in full control of your files and privacy while you work.

### Who it's for
*   Software developers who want an AI partner that integrates directly into their existing terminal workflow.
*   Students or hobbyists looking for a fast, privacy-focused way to get help with coding tasks without needing a web browser.

### Key features
*   **Terminal Integration:** Works right where you already type your commands, keeping your focus on your project.
*   **Privacy-First:** Operates locally on your machine with no hidden tracking or data collection.
*   **Multi-Provider Support:** Connects to various AI services so you can choose the one that works best for your needs.
*   **Safe Execution:** Includes built-in permission checks to ensure you approve every file change or command before it runs.
*   **Advanced Planning:** Features a specialized mode that breaks down complex goals into manageable steps and verifies the results.
