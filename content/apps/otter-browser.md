### What it does in plain English
Otter Browser is a web tool designed to bring back the efficient, user-friendly interface found in classic browsers of the past. It focuses on giving you full control over your settings and layout while keeping your internet browsing fast and lightweight. By prioritizing your preferences over corporate tracking, it provides a clean and distraction-free way to explore the web.

### Who it's for
* People who miss the highly customizable layouts and toolbars of older web browsers.
* Users who want a fast, lightweight tool that doesn't track their behavior or clutter the screen with unnecessary features.

### Key features
* **Classic Interface:** Recreates the beloved, functional design of early 2010s browsers.
* **Highly Customizable:** Allows you to move toolbars, buttons, and menus exactly where you want them.
* **Lightweight Performance:** Designed to run smoothly without hogging your computer's memory or processing power.
* **User-Centric:** Built to ensure the software works for you, not for advertisers or data collectors.
* **Open Source:** Developed by the community to ensure transparency and long-term reliability.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly customizable interface, very low system resource usage.
* **Cons:** Lacks some of the modern, automated features found in mainstream commercial browsers, requires a bit of manual setup to get the perfect look.
