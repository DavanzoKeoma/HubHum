### What it does in plain English
Spacedrive is a modern file manager that brings all your scattered digital files together into a single, organized view. It connects your local computers, external hard drives, network storage, and cloud accounts without forcing you to hand over your personal data. You can search, browse, and move files across all your connected devices through one clean and private application.

### Who it's for
* **Privacy-conscious users:** People who want to organize and sync their files across multiple devices without relying on big tech companies or cloud surveillance.
* **Creative professionals:** Photographers and video editors who juggle massive media libraries scattered across multiple external hard drives and local backups.
* **Power users:** Individuals managing multiple computers, servers, and storage drives who need an advanced, lightning-fast file navigator.

### Key features
* **Unified file view:** Browse and organize files from local folders, external drives, and cloud services in one place.
* **Lightning-fast search:** Instantly find files across all connected devices and storage sources without endless indexing delays.
* **Complete data privacy:** Operates locally on your devices without forced user accounts, subscriptions, or hidden data tracking.
* **Duplicate finder:** Quickly identify and clean up duplicate files to free up valuable storage space safely.
* **Cross-device connection:** Link multiple computers securely to manage and share files directly between your own devices.
