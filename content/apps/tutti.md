### What it does in plain English
Tutti is a visual workspace designed to bring your favorite artificial intelligence assistants together in one unified hub. Instead of manually copying text and files between different tools, you can let your assistants share context, pass work directly to each other, and execute tasks in parallel. It features a clean, user-friendly interface so you can manage your projects, design mockups, and generate content without touching a command line.

### Who it's for
- Independent software developers who want different coding assistants to collaborate on projects without re-explaining background details.
- Product managers and designers who want to turn initial project plans into visual mockups and functional code smoothly.
- Content creators looking to organize research, generate graphics, and draft documents inside a single connected workspace.

### Key features
- **Real-time shared workspace:** Keep multiple assistants aware of the same project files, running tasks, and conversation history.
- **Cross-assistant referencing:** Easily pull in past conversations, files, or app outputs using quick mention commands.
- **Built-in application ecosystem:** Access integrated tools for generating visuals, designing layouts, and drafting documents directly through your assistants.
- **Automated goal breakdown:** Describe your project objectives and let the workspace divide them into manageable sub-tasks.
- **Unified control center:** Monitor active agent conversations, pending approvals, and running tasks from a single dashboard.
