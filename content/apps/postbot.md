### What it does in plain English
PostBot helps you save time by managing all your social media posts from one central location. Instead of logging into every site individually, you can create your content once and send it to all your favorite platforms simultaneously. It works by using your existing browser sessions, ensuring your account information stays private and secure on your own device.

### Who it's for
*   **Content Creators:** People who manage multiple social media channels and want to avoid repetitive manual posting.
*   **Small Business Owners:** Teams that need to share updates, news, or product announcements across various platforms efficiently.

### Key features
*   **One-Click Publishing:** Write your post once and distribute it to all connected platforms at the same time.
*   **Broad Compatibility:** Supports a wide range of domestic and international social media and video platforms.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** Operates directly through your browser's local login state, meaning your credentials are not stored on external servers.
*   **Format Flexibility:** Easily handles various content types, including text, Markdown, images, audio, and video files.
*   **Extensible Design:** Advanced users can integrate custom automation engines or AI tools to tailor the publishing workflow to their specific needs.

### How to install (Download)
PostBot is primarily designed as a browser extension. You can access the latest version and installation instructions via the official project page:
[Download and Install PostBot](https://github.com/gitcoffee-os/postbot/releases)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscription fees, and keeps your account data local for better security.
*   **Cons:** Requires a modern web browser to function, and setting up advanced automation workflows may require a brief learning period.
