### What it does in plain English
Background Music solves the frustrating problem of having to manually mute your favorite songs every time a video or notification plays. It gives you a separate volume slider for every single program running on your computer, so you can keep your music loud while lowering the sound of a noisy video call. It also lets you record the audio coming from your computer, which is perfect for saving lectures or online meetings.

### Who it's for
*   **Students and professionals** who need to record online classes or meetings without buying expensive hardware.
*   **Music lovers** who want their playlists to pause automatically when a video starts playing, without any manual intervention.
*   **Multitaskers** who need to balance the sound levels of different apps, such as keeping a podcast audible while a game is running quietly in the background.

### Key features
*   **Automatic Music Pausing:** Detects when other sounds are playing and pauses your music player (like Spotify or iTunes) instantly, then resumes it when the other sound stops.
*   **Per-App Volume Control:** Adjust the loudness of each application individually, allowing you to boost quiet apps or lower noisy ones without affecting your overall system volume.
*   **System Audio Recording:** Capture any sound playing on your Mac, including internal audio, using standard recording tools like QuickTime.
*   **No Restart Required:** The tool installs and starts working immediately without needing to reboot your computer.
