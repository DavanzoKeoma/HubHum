### What it does in plain English
MiaoYan lets you write and organize your thoughts using simple text files that stay on your own computer. It provides a clean, fast interface where you can see your formatted notes instantly while typing. Because it works on Mac, iPhone, and iPad, you can start a note on your phone and finish it on your desktop without any hassle.

### Who it's for
* **Students and researchers** who need to take structured notes and organize them by topic without relying on cloud-only services.
* **Writers and bloggers** who prefer writing in plain text and want a quick way to preview how their articles will look.
* **Privacy-focused users** who want full control over their data and prefer keeping their personal notes stored locally on their devices.

### Key features
* **Local-first storage:** Your notes are saved as standard text files in a folder you choose, ensuring you always have access to your data.
* **Cross-device sync:** Seamlessly switch between Mac, iPhone, and iPad using iCloud Drive or other cloud storage services.
* **Instant preview:** See your formatted notes, including math formulas and diagrams, update in real-time as you type.
* **Presentation mode:** Turn your notes into simple slide decks for quick presentations without switching to other software.
* **Lightweight design:** Built natively for Apple devices, it runs smoothly and uses less battery than web-based alternatives.
