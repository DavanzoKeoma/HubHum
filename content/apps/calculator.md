### What it does in plain English
Windows Calculator is a modern tool designed to handle all your daily mathematical needs right from your computer. It goes far beyond basic arithmetic by offering scientific formulas, programmer calculation modes, and instant unit conversions. Whether you are balancing a budget, solving homework equations, or converting currencies, it gives you reliable and precise results instantly.

### Who it's for
* Students and teachers tackling math, physics, and science homework.
* Programmers and developers who need quick base conversions.
* Everyday users who frequently convert currencies, measurements, or calculate dates.

### Key features
* **Standard & Scientific Modes:** Perform quick everyday math or switch to advanced scientific operations that follow strict order of operations.
* **Programmer Mode:** Easily convert numbers between binary, decimal, hexadecimal, and octal bases.
* **Unit and Currency Conversions:** Convert measurements like length, weight, and temperature, plus get real-time currency conversions.
* **Date Calculations:** Determine the exact difference between two dates or find out what a specific date will be after adding or subtracting days.
* **Calculation History:** Review past equations and keep track of memory states so you never lose your place.
