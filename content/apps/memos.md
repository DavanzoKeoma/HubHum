### What it does in plain English
Memos is a private, self-hosted space designed to capture your everyday ideas, notes, and links without relying on commercial tracking services. It organizes every entry chronologically on a clean timeline, allowing you to easily browse through past thoughts and memories. You stay fully in control of your records, deciding whether individual entries remain strictly private, open to close friends, or published directly to the web. When administering your notes remotely, you can protect your connection and secure admin sessions using [PureVPN](https://billing.purevpn.com/aff.php?aff=49386762&subid=hubhum_01_MER047) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* **Journal writers and diarists** who want a secure, private timeline for daily reflections and quick thoughts.
* **Knowledge workers** seeking a lightweight personal database to store bookmarks, snippets, and markdown notes.
* **Privacy-conscious users** who prefer hosting their own data rather than depending on third-party cloud note apps.

### Key features
* **Personal Timeline View:** Automatically organizes your thoughts by date so you can effortlessly trace back ideas across weeks and months.
* **Flexible Privacy Controls:** Choose visibility settings for each individual note, from completely private to shared spaces or public web pages.
* **Markdown Formatting:** Write quick single-sentence thoughts or detailed multi-paragraph entries using clean markdown structuring.
* **Web Clipper Integration:** Save web pages, selected text, and images directly into your notes using browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox.
