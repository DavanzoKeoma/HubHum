### What it does in plain English
Filee is a free, open-source utility that lets you convert hundreds of different file formats simply by dragging and dropping them right at your cursor. It runs completely offline on your computer, meaning your personal documents, images, and videos are never uploaded anywhere. You can quickly change images, documents, audio, video, and archives into the exact format you need without installing bulky software or using online conversion websites.

### Who it's for
* Students and professionals who frequently need to convert between PDF, Word, Excel, and image formats.
* Privacy-conscious users who want to process sensitive files locally without risking cloud uploads.
* Anyone looking for a fast, hassle-free way to handle everyday file format conversions without dealing with complicated menus.

### Key features
* **Cursor-friendly interface:** Hold a key and drop files onto a visual ring of options that appears right where your mouse is pointing.
* **Over 260 supported formats:** Handle images, documents, spreadsheets, e-books, archives, audio, and video effortlessly.
* **Complete privacy:** Everything stays on your local machine with zero uploads, internet tracking, or data collection.
* **Batch processing:** Convert multiple files simultaneously in parallel to save time on large projects.
* **Drag straight from browsers:** Grab images or file links right out of your web browser and drop them straight into the app.
