### What it does in plain English
The Personal Management System is a private digital workspace designed to help you consolidate scattered information from various cloud services into one place. It functions like a personal administrative center where you can track everything from daily tasks and health appointments to household expenses and secure passwords. By hosting this on your own home network, you maintain complete control over your data without relying on external companies.

### Who it's for
*   Individuals who want to stop spreading their personal data across multiple cloud providers and social media platforms.
*   Privacy-conscious users who prefer to keep their sensitive notes, financial records, and documents on their own private hardware.

### Key features
*   **Comprehensive Modules:** Includes built-in tools for managing contacts, notes, recurring schedules, and personal goals.
*   **Financial Tracking:** Monitor your spending, track who owes you money, and manage product prices in one interface.
*   **Secure Storage:** Keep your passwords encrypted and store important documents, photos, and videos locally.
*   **Work-Life Balance:** Dedicated sections for tracking work hours, holiday time, and personal achievements.
*   **Customizable:** Designed to be easily extended by users who want to add specific features for their own unique needs.
