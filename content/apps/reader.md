### What it does in plain English
Reader is a clean, distraction-free web tool designed for reading standard text files and compressed book archives right inside your web browser. It lets you import your personal reading collection instantly without installing heavy software or uploading your books to an external server. The app keeps all your personal reading progress and settings private and securely stored right on your own device.

### Who it's for
* Avid readers who want to enjoy e-books and text documents on any device with a web browser.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer keeping their personal reading libraries strictly local.
* Anyone looking for a fast, customizable reading interface with text-to-speech support.

### Key features
* **Complete Offline Access:** Open and read TXT and compressed text files directly in your browser without requiring a constant internet connection.
* **Fully Customizable Layouts:** Personalize fonts, colors, line spacing, and viewing modes to create your ideal reading comfort.
* **Built-in Speech Playback:** Listen to your favorite stories using your browser's native text-to-speech capabilities.
* **Traditional and Simplified Conversion:** Easily convert text between traditional and simplified Chinese characters on the fly.
* **Total Data Privacy:** All your documents and reading bookmarks remain stored securely on your local device.

### How to install (Download)
This application runs directly inside your web browser as a web app. You can use it instantly online or add it to your device's home screen for quick offline access.
* [Open Reader Web App](https://tiansh.github.io/reader)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, requires no installation, works completely offline, and respects your privacy.
* **Cons:** Advanced formatting options and settings require editing an INI text configuration block.
