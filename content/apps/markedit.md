### What it does in plain English
MarkEdit lets you open a plain‑text window and type Markdown with instant formatting help.  
It saves your work in the usual Mac folders, so you never lose a file.  
The app stays tiny and runs instantly, even with very large documents.

### Who it's for
* Writers who need a clean space to draft notes, blogs, or documentation.  
* Students and professionals who keep their research or reports in Markdown.  
* Anyone who likes the simplicity of TextEdit but wants proper Markdown support.

### Key features
- **Very small download** – the installer is only about 4 MB.  
- **Lightning‑fast** – opens and edits even million‑line files without lag.  
- **No data collection** – your writing stays private on your computer.  
- **Native Mac look** – matches the appearance and shortcuts you already know.  
- **Extension manager** – add extra tools like live preview or AI‑assisted writing.
