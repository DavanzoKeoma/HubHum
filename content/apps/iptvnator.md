### What it does in plain English
IPTVnator is a free tool that lets you watch live television, movies, and series using your own existing media sources. It connects to your personal playlists or account logins so you can view content on your computer, phone, or smart TV. The app helps you organize channels, find specific shows, and remember where you left off in a movie or episode.

### Who it's for
*   **Cord-cutters:** People who have cancelled traditional cable or satellite TV and want to watch live channels using their own internet connection.
*   **Media enthusiasts:** Users who have specific playlists or accounts for international channels, niche sports, or foreign language content that are not available on mainstream streaming services.
*   **Tech-savvy families:** Households that want to share a media library across multiple devices, including smart TVs and phones, without paying for multiple separate subscriptions.

### Key features
*   **Multi-source support:** Connects to local files, remote playlists, and various online account types to keep all your channels in one place.
*   **Live TV guide:** Displays program schedules so you can see what is on now and what is coming up next, with options to catch up on missed shows.
*   **Movie and series library:** Organizes films and TV shows with posters and season lists, allowing you to track your watch history and resume playback easily.
*   **Flexible playback:** Works on Windows, Mac, Linux, and in web browsers, with options to use built-in players or connect to external video software.
*   **Customizable interface:** Offers light and dark themes, multiple language options, and keyboard shortcuts to help you navigate quickly.
