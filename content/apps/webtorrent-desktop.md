### What it does in plain English
WebTorrent Desktop is a simple media player that lets you start watching videos or listening to audio files the moment you open them. You no longer need to wait for a file to finish downloading before you can enjoy the content. It works seamlessly on your computer, making the process of viewing shared media as easy as using a standard video player.

### Who it's for
* People who want to preview or watch media files without waiting for long download times.
* Users looking for a lightweight, simple, and clean interface to manage their media playback.

### Key features
* **Instant Playback:** Start watching or listening to your files immediately while they are still downloading in the background.
* **Simple Interface:** A clean, clutter-free design that focuses purely on your media.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works consistently on Windows, Mac, and Linux computers.
* **Privacy-Focused:** The app does not track which files you add or collect personal identifying information.
* **Lightweight:** Designed to be efficient and easy to use without unnecessary system bloat.
