### What it does in plain English
Modern computers collect a lot of personal data without you asking for it. privacy.sexy scans your system and applies hundreds of recommended security tweaks to stop this tracking. It gives you full control over your digital footprint by making these changes easy to apply and reverse.

### Who it's for
*   Everyday users who want to protect their personal information without becoming a computer expert.
*   Privacy-conscious individuals who want to ensure their operating system is configured to minimize data collection.
*   Tech-savvy users who want a transparent, open-source way to audit and harden their system settings.

### Key features
*   **One-Click Hardening:** Applies hundreds of privacy and security improvements to your system instantly.
*   **Full Transparency:** You can see exactly what each change does before you apply it, with no hidden surprises.
*   **Easy Reversal:** If a setting causes a problem, you can easily undo the changes to restore your previous state.
*   **Cross-Platform Support:** Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux, ensuring consistent privacy protection across devices.
*   **Open Source:** The entire tool is free and its code is publicly available for anyone to inspect and verify.
