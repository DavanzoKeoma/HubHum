### What it does in plain English
Television is a powerful search tool designed to help you locate files, text, and system data in real-time. It works by scanning your computer's information instantly, allowing you to filter through large amounts of data without waiting. You can customize how it searches to match your specific daily workflows and habits.

### Who it's for
*   **Power users and developers** who spend significant time navigating through many folders and text files.
*   **System administrators** who need to quickly locate specific configuration files or running processes on their machines.

### Key features
*   **Instant Results:** Provides near-instant search feedback as you type, saving you time.
*   **Highly Adaptable:** Create your own custom search channels to browse specific types of information like notes, logs, or project folders.
*   **Portable Design:** Works efficiently across different computer setups without requiring heavy system resources.
*   **Smart Integration:** Connects directly to your existing tools, including popular code editors and shell environments.
