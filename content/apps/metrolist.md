### What it does in plain English
Metrolist turns your device into a clean, ad-free music player for YouTube Music. It works on your phone, tablet, and computer, giving you access to the same songs and playlists you know. You can listen to music, read lyrics, and even share a listening session with friends in real-time.

### Who it's for
*   **Music lovers** who want to listen to YouTube Music without interruptions from ads or the need to sign in.
*   **Tech-savvy users** who prefer open-source software and want to customize their music experience with different themes and fonts.
*   **Social listeners** who enjoy syncing their music with friends or family using the "Listen Together" feature.

### Key features
*   **Ad-free streaming:** Enjoy YouTube Music without any commercial interruptions or mandatory sign-ins.
*   **Cross-platform support:** Available for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.
*   **Rich listening experience:** Includes lyrics with translation, music recognition, and video playback on supported devices.
*   **Shared listening:** Use the "Listen Together" feature to play music in sync with others online.
*   **Customization:** Change app icons, fonts, and player styles to match your personal taste.
