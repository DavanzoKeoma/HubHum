### What it does in plain English
Lap is a desktop photo manager designed to handle massive local picture libraries smoothly and privately. It connects directly to your existing folders without forcing you to move files, upload them to the cloud, or pay a monthly subscription. You can search your images using on-device artificial intelligence, compare similar shots side-by-side, and clean up duplicates quickly.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and hobbyists managing massive local photo collections on their computers.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to organize pictures without relying on external cloud storage.
* Anyone looking for a fast, subscription-free tool to browse, tag, and sort RAW images and videos.

### Key features
* Browse existing local folders directly without moving your original files or importing them into a closed ecosystem.
* Search images using on-device artificial intelligence that recognizes text descriptions, visual similarity, and faces locally.
* Clean up duplicate photos easily with custom keep rules and reclaimable space summaries.
* Compare multiple photos simultaneously using a multi-pane viewer with instant keyboard shortcuts.
* Support for dozens of standard image, video, and professional camera RAW formats out of the box.
