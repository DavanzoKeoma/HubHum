### What it does in plain English
Double Commander helps you organize your computer files by showing two folders side-by-side on your screen. This layout makes it much faster to move, copy, or compare files between different locations on your hard drive. It is a reliable, free tool designed to give you more control over your digital workspace.

### Who it's for
*   People who frequently move or organize large collections of photos, documents, or music.
*   Users who find standard system file browsers too limiting for managing multiple folders at once.

### Key features
*   **Dual-Pane View:** Work with two folders simultaneously to drag and drop files between them effortlessly.
*   **Built-in Viewer:** Quickly peek inside text files and images without needing to open separate programs.
*   **Batch Renaming:** Change the names of many files at once using simple rules.
*   **Customizable Interface:** Adjust the colors and layout to match how you prefer to work.
*   **Search Tools:** Find specific files quickly across your entire computer with advanced search options.
