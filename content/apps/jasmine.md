### What it does in plain English
Jasmine is a stylish visual template designed specifically for Typecho blogging software. It prioritizes clean typography and a simple layout to ensure your writing remains the main focus. The theme includes built-in features to help your blog look professional on any device without requiring complex adjustments.

### Who it's for
* Personal bloggers who want a clean, modern aesthetic for their writing.
* Writers who need a distraction-free environment for their readers.

### Key features
* **Responsive Design:** Automatically adjusts to look great on phones, tablets, and desktop computers.
* **Night Mode:** Includes a built-in toggle to switch between light and dark color schemes for comfortable reading.
* **Smart Formatting:** Features automatic code highlighting and multiple layout options for your articles.
* **Search Engine Friendly:** Built with clean structure to help your blog content get discovered more easily.
* **Pinned Posts:** Allows you to highlight your most important content at the top of your blog.

### How to install (Download)
1. Download the [Jasmine theme package (.zip)](https://github.com/liaocp666/Jasmine/releases/latest/download/Jasmine.zip).
2. Unzip the file and rename the folder to `jasmine`.
3. Upload this folder to the `usr/themes/` directory of your Typecho installation.
4. Log in to your Typecho dashboard, go to the Appearance settings, and activate the Jasmine theme.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly readable design, and includes a built-in dark mode.
* **Cons:** Requires manual file uploading to your server, which may be unfamiliar to absolute beginners.
