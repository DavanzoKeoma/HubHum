### What it does in plain English
Kuro gives Linux users a dedicated desktop space to manage their everyday tasks and lists without needing to open a web browser. It connects directly with your existing task accounts so you can check off items, organize daily projects, and stay on schedule. The app runs smoothly in the background and blends nicely into your preferred desktop setup.

### Who it's for
* Linux users who rely on task lists to organize their daily work and personal errands.
* People who prefer dedicated desktop apps over keeping browser tabs open for organization.
* Anyone looking for a clean, customizable task manager tailored specifically for open-source operating systems.

### Key features
* Dedicated desktop experience built specifically for Linux environments.
* Custom theme support to match your preferred visual style and desktop setup.
* Global keyboard shortcuts for quick task entry from anywhere on your system.
* Lightweight installation options including AppImage and Debian packages.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, native Linux performance, customizable themes, helpful keyboard shortcuts.
* **Cons:** Requires a separate Microsoft account to sync tasks online, limited to Linux desktop environments.
