### What it does in plain English
marka.md is a desktop notepad built specifically for writing and organizing text files that you plan to share with artificial intelligence tools. It runs completely offline on your computer, meaning your private writing never leaves your device until you choose to copy it. You can view your formatted text side-by-side with a live preview, organize folders of notes, and bundle multiple files together into a single organized package for easy AI uploading.

### Who it's for
* Writers, developers, and researchers who frequently share notes, logs, or project files with artificial intelligence chat assistants.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want a local-first note-taking tool that stores everything directly on their hard drive without cloud tracking.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, distraction-free markdown reader and editor with support for diagrams and clean PDF exports.

### Key features
* **Live Side-by-Side Preview:** See your markdown text format instantly as you type with clean typography and syntax highlighting.
* **AI Context Management:** Stage multiple files in a sidebar tray and combine them into a single, structured bundle with file paths.
* **Diagram and Media Support:** Render flowchart diagrams, code blocks, task lists, and media previews seamlessly.
* **Tabbed Workspace:** Open multiple files in tabs, search folders quickly, and keep your workspace organized.
* **Complete Privacy:** 100% local operation with no mandatory accounts, cloud sync, or background telemetry.
