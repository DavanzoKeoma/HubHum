### What it does in plain English
Antares is a sleek, modern workspace designed to help you connect and interact with multiple databases in one place. It lets you write queries, inspect tables, and test information without wrestling with cluttered menus or complicated windows. Because it focuses heavily on user experience and speed, you can handle your everyday data tasks smoothly and efficiently.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who need to check and update database records frequently across multiple projects.
* Data analysts looking for a clean, distraction-free environment to run queries and review tables.
* Students and hobbyists learning database management who want a modern tool that is free forever.

### Key features
* Connect to multiple different databases at the exact same time in a clean tabbed layout.
* Manage tables, indexes, views, foreign keys, and stored routines easily.
* Generate fake data automatically to fill your tables for testing and development.
* Search through past queries in your history and save your frequent notes or snippets.
* Secure your remote database connections smoothly using built-in SSH tunnel support.
