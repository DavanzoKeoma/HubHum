### What it does in plain English
This desktop application translates academic research documents into dozens of different languages without requiring any complicated technical setup or programming knowledge. It automatically preserves complex mathematical formulas, data tables, and multi-column layouts so you can read foreign research smoothly. Users can drag and drop documents directly into the window, choose from multiple popular translation engines, and instantly receive clean, professionally structured results.

### Who it's for
* Graduate students and university researchers reading foreign academic papers.
* Professionals and engineers reviewing technical documentation and patent filings.
* Anyone who needs to translate lengthy PDF documents while keeping charts and formulas intact.

### Key features
* **Zero Technical Setup:** Completely self-contained application requiring no external programming environments or complicated configuration steps.
* **Layout Preservation:** Intelligently keeps mathematical equations, charts, tables, and side-by-side structures completely aligned.
* **Multiple Translation Engines:** Connects with dozens of free and premium language services, including advanced artificial intelligence providers.
* **Reference Manager Integration:** Deep connection with popular research reference software to automatically organize translated attachments.
* **Batch and Long Document Support:** Effortlessly handles massive textbooks with thousands of pages using intelligent memory management.
