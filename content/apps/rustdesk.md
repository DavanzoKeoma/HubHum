### What it does in plain English
This application lets you safely view and control any computer or mobile device from a distance as if you were sitting right in front of it. You can easily share your screen, send files back and forth, or help family members fix computer issues from afar. Best of all, you can keep your data completely private by setting up your own private connection server instead of relying on third-party companies.

### Who it's for
* IT support professionals and system administrators managing multiple computers
* Remote workers who need reliable access to their office workstation from home
* Individuals helping friends and family members troubleshoot device problems remotely

### Key features
* Works smoothly right out of the box with zero complicated setup required
* Gives you complete ownership and control of your data and relay servers
* Built-in file transfer capabilities to easily move documents between connected devices
* Secure connection channels designed to protect your remote sessions from unauthorized access
* Cross-platform compatibility supporting Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile devices

* **Cons:** Setting up your own custom hosting server requires some technical knowledge, though default servers work immediately.
