### What it does in plain English
Flectar Mail brings all your email accounts, calendars, and contacts together into one unified workspace that opens instantly. Unlike traditional email applications built with heavy web browser technology, this app is written natively from the ground up for extreme speed. It keeps your device running smoothly while using only a fraction of the memory required by other desktop mail tools.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to keep their messages local and block invisible tracking pixels.
* People juggling multiple personal and work email accounts who want a unified inbox without clutter.
* Users tired of sluggish, memory-hogging mail software that slows down their computers.

### Key features
* **Instant startup:** Opens immediately without waiting for heavy background browser engines to load.
* **Extremely low memory usage:** Uses roughly 20 megabytes of private memory at idle, saving your computer's resources.
* **All-in-one workspace:** Seamlessly switch between your email, calendar, contacts, and files in one app.
* **Offline capability:** Stores your mailbox and calendar locally so you can read and manage your schedule without an internet connection.
* **Privacy protection:** Automatically blocks remote tracking images by default to prevent senders from knowing when you open messages.
