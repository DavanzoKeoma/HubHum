### What it does in plain English
Rubick is a handy desktop launcher that sits quietly in the background until you need it. By pressing a simple keyboard shortcut, an input box pops up allowing you to instantly search for applications, open folders, or locate files on your computer. You can also customize your workspace by visiting the built-in marketplace to install extra add-ons that handle everyday tasks.

### Who it's for
- Busy professionals looking to speed up their daily workflow with keyboard shortcuts.
- Power users who love customizing their workspace with lightweight, specialized add-ons.
- Anyone who wants a centralized hub to quickly find files, apps, and tools without clicking through endless menus.

### Key features
- **Quick Launcher:** Instantly find and open local programs, folders, and documents with a quick keystroke and keyword search.
- **Flexible Add-ons:** Discover and install community-created plugins as easily as installing software packages.
- **Secure Synchronization:** Keep your preferences and plugin data synchronized across multiple devices using cloud storage protocols like WebDAV.
- **Multilingual Support:** Enjoy using the interface in your preferred language for a comfortable workflow.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source with no subscriptions, lightweight performance, highly customizable via plugins, and cross-device data syncing.
* **Cons:** Requires restarting the application for certain system-level plugins to take effect, and some advanced configurations require manual steps.
