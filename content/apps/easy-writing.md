### What it does in plain English
Easy Writing is a desktop application built specifically for authors and storytellers to plan, draft, and organize books directly on their own computer. All written text, outlines, and character notes remain private inside a local database without requiring any online registration. Writers can also connect their own artificial intelligence keys to brainstorm plot ideas, continue paragraphs, or analyze popular storytelling trends.

### Who it's for
* Fiction writers and web novelists who want total ownership of their draft files and creative notes.
* Authors looking for a distraction-free writing interface equipped with word counts and milestone goals.
* Creators who want to experiment with artificial intelligence assistance while keeping their personal writing data strictly on their own machine.

### Key features
* **Local data ownership:** Keeps all books, character sheets, timelines, and writing histories stored securely on your hard drive.
* **Artificial intelligence integration:** Connects with popular intelligent services using your own access keys or local software runners.
* **Distraction-free editor:** Features rich formatting tools, automatic background saves, typing sound effects, and focus modes.
* **Writing statistics:** Tracks daily word counts, streaks, writing calendars, and separate tracking for manual versus machine-assisted output.
* **Reference tools:** Manages outlines, character relationship maps, world settings, and plot timelines in one unified space.
