### What it does in plain English
Mimi listens to audio playing on your computer or spoken through your microphone and displays live translated subtitles on your screen. It helps you watch foreign-language movies, join online lectures, or follow live streams without needing built-in translation features. You can customize subtitle appearance, choose specific audio sources, and even save transcripts or audio recordings for later note-taking.

### Who it's for
* Language learners watching foreign videos or video streams without official subtitles.
* Students attending online lectures or global webinars in another language.
* Creators and viewers who need real-time accessibility overlays on everyday desktop media.

### Key features
* **Live Dual-Language Subtitles:** Display original text and translated text simultaneously or individually.
* **Flexible Audio Selection:** Target specific app sounds on your computer or capture microphone input directly.
* **Customizable Overlay:** Adjust subtitle size, color, position, and click-through transparency to stay immersed.
* **Transcript Export:** Optionally save subtitle logs or record audio tracks to review later as text or audio files.
* **Multiple Platforms:** Runs on major desktop systems as well as mobile devices.
