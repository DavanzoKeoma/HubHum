### What it does in plain English
jrnl is a lightweight tool designed for anyone who loves keeping a daily log without opening complex software. You simply type your thoughts into your command window, and the program automatically timestamps them and organizes them into an easy-to-read file. All entries are saved as plain text on your computer, meaning you always own and control your personal words.

### Who it's for
* Command-line enthusiasts who prefer typing notes in a terminal window over heavy word processors.
* Writers, developers, and thinkers who want a distraction-free way to log daily thoughts or project updates.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to keep their journals stored locally without relying on closed cloud services.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast text entry with automatic date and time detection.
* Stores everything in human-readable plain text files for long-term accessibility.
* Optional security protection using standard encryption to keep private entries secure.
* Flexible search tools to easily look back at past entries by date, tag, or keyword.
* Integrates seamlessly with your preferred external text editing tools.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, complete ownership of local text files, extremely lightweight.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with a computer command window to get started.
