### What it does in plain English
Many software tools and operating systems download updates from servers that may be slow depending on your location. chsrc automatically tests available mirrors and switches your settings to the fastest one for your specific needs. It works across almost any device, including Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android, to ensure your downloads are as quick as possible.

### Who it's for
*   **Developers and Students:** People who frequently install programming languages or software packages and want to avoid slow download speeds.
*   **System Administrators:** Users who manage multiple operating systems and need a reliable way to keep their software sources optimized.

### Key features
*   **Automatic Speed Testing:** Intelligently finds and selects the fastest download mirror for your connection.
*   **Universal Support:** Works with over 65 different software types, including programming languages, package managers, and operating systems.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Designed to run on Windows, Linux, macOS, BSD, and Android.
*   **Customizable:** Allows you to set specific mirrors manually or reset to default settings with a single command.
*   **Lightweight:** A simple, efficient tool that performs its tasks without unnecessary background processes.
