### What it does in plain English
Fleet helps you watch over every computer, phone and tablet your organization uses. It lets you push updates, install software, and check security settings without touching each device. All of this is done from one central screen you can access from anywhere.

### Who it's for
IT teams that need to look after many devices, small‑business owners who want to keep employee computers safe, and schools that manage laptops for students.

### Key features
- Update operating systems and apps on all devices at once.  
- Install or remove programs remotely.  
- See detailed reports on device health and security.  
- Enforce rules like password requirements or encryption.  
- Turn features on or off so you only use what you need.
