### What it does in plain English
ScreenTinker is a central control system for managing digital displays like menu boards, office signs, and information kiosks. You can upload videos, images, and slides to create custom playlists that play automatically on your screens. It works on almost any device, including smart TVs, media sticks, and standard web browsers, without requiring expensive hardware or monthly subscription fees.

### Who it's for
*   **Small business owners** who want to display menus or promotions on shop TVs without complex software.
*   **Office managers** who need to show announcements or schedules on lobby displays across multiple locations.

### Key features
*   **Drag-and-drop dashboard:** Easily organize your content into playlists and schedule them to appear at specific times.
*   **Works on any screen:** Compatible with Android TV, Fire TV, Raspberry Pi, and standard web browsers.
*   **Remote control:** Update your screens from anywhere in the world and check their status in real-time.
*   **Offline resilience:** Your content keeps playing even if the internet connection drops, ensuring your signs never go blank.
*   **No hidden costs:** A free, open-source tool that lets you keep your data on your own equipment.
