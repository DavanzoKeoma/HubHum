### What it does in plain English
Over time, your computer accumulates massive amounts of hidden development folders, temporary caches, and old project files that take up gigabytes of space. This utility scans your storage directories at lightning speed, shows you exactly how much space each old folder is wasting, and lets you wipe them out safely with a couple of keystrokes. You can review when you last touched a project so you never accidentally delete anything important.

### Who it's for
* Software creators and developers struggling with shrinking hard drive capacities due to leftover project dependencies.
* Power users who want a visual, interactive way to clean out hidden caches and temporary directories without digging through folders manually.

### Key features
* **Deep storage scanner:** Automatically hunts down heavy forgotten folders, system caches, and temporary files across your drives.
* **Interactive visual navigation:** Browse through found items cleanly, view when they were last modified, and select what to keep or delete.
* **Multi-select cleanup:** Easily flag multiple heavy directories at once and wipe them in a single action to save time.
* **Multi-language ecosystem profiles:** Target specific types of development residues from various programming environments.
* **Safety filters:** Highlights critical system folders so you can clean your drives with absolute peace of mind.

### How to install (Download)
This tool runs directly from your command-line environment using Node.js. If you have Node.js installed on your computer, simply open your terminal and run:

```bash
npx npkill
```

For more details, local setup options, and documentation, visit the [Official Website](https://npkill.js.org).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, lightning-fast scanning performance, helps recover gigabytes of lost storage instantly.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic command-line environment to launch, results should always be reviewed manually before deleting.
