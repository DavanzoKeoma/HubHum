### What it does in plain English
psmux allows you to organize your Windows terminal workspace by splitting it into multiple windows and panes. It lets you keep several tasks running at once in a single window, making it easier to monitor different processes simultaneously. It is designed specifically for Windows, ensuring it works smoothly with your existing terminal settings.

### Who it's for
*   Power users and developers who want to manage multiple command-line tasks in one view.
*   People who frequently use PowerShell or Windows Terminal and want a more efficient way to organize their workspace.

### Key features
*   **Split Screen:** Divide your terminal window into multiple panes to see different tasks at the same time.
*   **Session Persistence:** Keep your work running even if you close your terminal window.
*   **Mouse Support:** Navigate and resize your panes easily using your mouse.
*   **Customizable:** Supports themes and configurations to match your preferred look and feel.
*   **Native Performance:** Built specifically for Windows, providing a fast and smooth experience without needing extra compatibility layers.
