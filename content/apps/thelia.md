### What it does in plain English
Thelia is an open-source platform designed to help you build and run your own online store from scratch. It provides a complete administrative area to manage products, categories, customers, and orders effortlessly. Developers and store owners can customize layouts and features using flexible templates to fit unique business needs.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners looking to launch an independent online storefront without hefty recurring subscription fees.
* Web developers who want a robust, customizable platform to build tailor-made shopping experiences for clients.
* Digital merchants seeking complete control over their store data, inventory, and customer relationships.

### Key features
* Complete administration dashboard to track sales, manage inventory, and handle customer accounts.
* Flexible template system allowing full control over the look and feel of both the store front and admin panel.
* Secure user authentication and structured database management to keep customer information safe.
* Modular extension system enabling easy addition of new payment gateways, shipping methods, and features.

### How to install (Download)
To set up your project skeleton and begin building your shop, visit the official setup guide and project page on [Thelia Project official project page](https://github.com/thelia/thelia-project). You can also view technical release information and components via [Download (sbom.cdx.json)](https://github.com/thelia/thelia/releases/download/3.4.0/sbom.cdx.json).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely open-source with no mandatory software licenses; highly customizable for unique workflows; gives full ownership of your store data.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge or a web developer to set up and configure; hosting and server maintenance are managed independently.
