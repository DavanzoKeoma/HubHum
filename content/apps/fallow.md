### What it does in plain English
Fallow scans your JavaScript and TypeScript projects to give you a complete picture of code quality, hidden complexity, and unused files. It builds a map of your project modules and dependencies so you can easily catch design inconsistencies before they cause problems. You can run checks directly from your command line, inside automated testing pipelines, or within your favorite code editor.

### Who it's for
* **Frontend and Backend Developers:** Programmers working with JavaScript or TypeScript who want to maintain clean, manageable codebases.
* **Engineering Teams:** Groups collaborating on software products who need consistent quality standards across suggested update.
* **Technical Leads:** Managers looking for clear health metrics, duplication tracking, and architectural boundaries without tedious configuration.

### Key features
* **Unused Code Detection:** Automatically discovers dead files, forgotten exports, and unused dependencies to keep projects lightweight.
* **Complexity Heatmaps:** Identifies functions and modules that are overly complicated and difficult to change safely.
* **Architecture Guards:** Enforces structural boundaries and flags circular dependencies before they disrupt your application.
* **Duplication Finder:** Locates copied blocks of code across your components, styles, and scripts.
* **Instant Start:** Works right out of the box with zero required configuration files or complex compiler setups.
