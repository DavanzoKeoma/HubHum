### What it does in plain English
Munder Difflin transforms your desktop into a visual virtual office where a team of intelligent agents handles your daily workloads. You give instructions in plain words to an orchestrator agent who then delegates tasks to specialized workers on your screen. Each worker operates locally on your machine while utilizing your existing artificial intelligence service plans securely.

### Who it's for
* Independent developers and creators looking to automate repetitive coding and research tasks.
* Productivity enthusiasts who want a visual dashboard to manage multiple AI assistants simultaneously.
* Remote professionals who need a local task board to coordinate complex multi-step writing and planning projects.

### Key features
* **Visual Office Floor:** Watch virtual characters at their desks manage tasks, write code, and organize your files.
* **Smart Orchestration:** Talk to a central coordinator who breaks down your requests and assigns them to the right worker.
* **Local Data Control:** Keep all your task boards, chat histories, and agent memories stored securely as plain files on your own device.
* **Multi-Service Support:** Connect your existing assistant plans seamlessly without purchasing separate model access.
* **Built-in Safety Guard Rails:** Monitor operations automatically with circuit breakers that prevent runaway loops and unexpected token usage.
