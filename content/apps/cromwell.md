### What it does in plain English
Cromwell is a website management system that gives you a familiar, easy-to-use dashboard for building modern, high-performance sites. It allows you to design pages and manage your content using a simple drag-and-drop interface. Whether you are starting a blog or launching an online shop, it provides all the necessary tools to get your project online quickly.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who want to manage their own online store without hiring a developer.
* Content creators and bloggers who need a fast, reliable platform to publish their work.

### Key features
* **Drag-and-drop editor:** Easily arrange your website layout without touching any code.
* **All-in-one management:** Handle your blog posts, product listings, and site settings from a single dashboard.
* **Ready-to-use themes:** Choose from a variety of free designs to give your site a professional look instantly.
* **Flexible database support:** Works with several standard database types to keep your information secure and organized.
* **Extensible system:** Add new functionality to your site through a simple plugin system.

### How to install (Download)
You can get started by visiting the official setup guide: [Cromwell Installation Guide](https://cromwellcms.com/docs/overview/installation)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, very fast performance, and includes a user-friendly visual editor.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of web hosting environments to set up initially.
