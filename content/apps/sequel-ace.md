### What it does in plain English
Sequel-Ace is a dedicated tool for Mac users who need to interact with their website or application databases. It provides a visual interface that allows you to browse, edit, and organize your data without needing to type complex commands. It is designed to be fast and reliable, making it easy to keep your information organized and accessible.

### Who it's for
* Web developers and designers who need to manage site data locally or on a server.
* Small business owners or hobbyists who maintain their own content management systems and need to perform occasional database updates.

### Key features
* **User-friendly Interface:** Navigate through your tables and data with a clean, familiar Mac-style layout.
* **Direct Management:** Easily add, remove, or update records in your databases with just a few clicks.
* **Secure Connections:** Connect to your databases safely using standard encryption methods.
* **AI Integration:** Includes an optional feature to allow AI assistants to help you query your data securely.
* **Migration Support:** Simple tools to help you move your existing saved connections from older database software.

### How to install (Download)
* **Official App Store:** [Download from the macOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sequel-ace/id1518036000?ls=1)
* **Community Install (Homebrew):** If you use the Homebrew package manager, you can install it by running `brew install --cask sequel-ace` in your terminal.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, lightweight, and specifically optimized for macOS.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS 13.5 or newer for the latest version; older versions are available for legacy systems but do not receive new updates.
