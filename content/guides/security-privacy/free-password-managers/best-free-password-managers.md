## Quick Answer: Gitleaks vs KeePassXC

Comparing **Gitleaks** and **KeePassXC** highlights two distinct approaches in **Free Password Managers & Auth Vaults**. **Gitleaks** runs as a **CLI Tool** on **Windows, macOS, Linux** (31,205 GitHub stars): Scan your code repositories and directories to find accidentally exposed passwords, API keys, and sensitive tokens before they leak. By contrast, **KeePassXC** runs as a **Desktop App** on **Windows, macOS, Linux** (30,048 GitHub stars): Store all your logins, PINs, and recovery codes in an encrypted local vault on your device with zero monthly fees.
