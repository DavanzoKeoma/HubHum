### What it does in plain English
Flextype is a lightweight content management tool that organizes your website text and media directly in simple files instead of a heavy database. This unique approach lets you easily move your entire site between different hosting providers just by copying files over. It gives you total freedom to design your data structure exactly how you want while keeping everything fast and portable.

### Who it's for
* Bloggers and writers who want a simple, database-free platform to publish articles.
* Independent web creators looking for an easily portable publishing system.
* Developers needing a minimalist, file-based core structure to build custom client websites.

### Key features
* Stores all information in clean, readable files instead of requiring a complex database setup.
* Completely portable structure allowing effortless migration between different web hosting providers.
* Simple and powerful programming interface designed for easy custom extension.
* Free and open-source foundation backed by a welcoming community.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no database required, extremely easy to migrate between hosts.
* **Cons:** Requires manual file permission setup during initial installation, fewer out-of-the-box templates than mainstream alternatives.
