### What it does in plain English
Museeks plays music files stored on your computer.  
It automatically finds new songs you add to your folders.  
You can organize tracks into playlists, shuffle them, or repeat them.

### Who it's for
Anyone who wants a straightforward music player without extra clutter.  
Home listeners who enjoy creating simple playlists.  
People who prefer a clean window that just works.

### Key features
- Plays most common audio formats such as MP3, FLAC, WAV, and OGG.  
- Automatically refreshes the music library when new files appear.  
- Lets you build and save playlists, as well as queue songs on the fly.  
- Offers shuffle, repeat, dark theme, and playback speed control.  
- Supports cover‑art display and can import or export `.m3u` playlists.
