### What it does in plain English
Recordly is a simple tool that lets you record your computer screen and turn the footage into a polished presentation. It automatically adds helpful touches like smooth zooms, cursor highlights, and stylish frames to make your videos look professional. You can easily trim your clips, add webcam bubbles, and export your work as a video or a looping animation.

### Who it's for
* Software developers or product managers who need to create quick walkthroughs for their apps.
* Content creators who want to make high-quality tutorials without spending hours on complex video editing software.

### Key features
* **Smart Motion:** Automatically adds zooms and smooth cursor movements to highlight important actions.
* **Easy Styling:** Place your recording inside a custom frame with backgrounds, shadows, and rounded corners.
* **Webcam Integration:** Add a webcam bubble overlay that you can resize, move, and style to match your video.
* **Simple Timeline:** Drag-and-drop editing tools to trim clips, adjust speed, and add text or image annotations.
* **Flexible Export:** Save your final project as a high-quality video or a lightweight looping animation.
