### What it does in plain English
lib.reviews is an open-source platform designed for gathering and sharing honest reviews about any topic or product imaginable. It operates independently to ensure that information stays free, transparent, and completely owned by the community. Users can read opinions, compare thoughts, and contribute their own constructive feedback in a collaborative space.

### Who it's for
* Everyday consumers looking for honest, unfiltered opinions on products and services
* Communities wanting a neutral space to discuss and review shared interests
* Open-source advocates seeking a transparent alternative to traditional review sites

### Key features
* Free and open platform dedicated to community-driven reviews
* Neutral environment designed to prevent corporate bias
* Collaborative information sharing covering absolutely any topic
* Built as part of the broader Permacommons ecosystem
* Maintained openly with community contribution guidelines

### How to install (Download)
You can explore and use the live platform directly by visiting the [Official lib.reviews Website](https://lib.reviews/). Please note that account creation currently requires an invite code, which you can request by pinging their official community channels.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free forever, community-driven, no corporate ads or hidden biases.
* **Cons:** Requires an invite code to set up a new account, advanced features and self-hosting require technical setup.
