### What it does in plain English
Soundux gives you a convenient way to organize and trigger sound clips, memes, and voice drops during online calls or gaming sessions. It connects directly to your communication apps or system audio output so everyone can hear your clips. You can search through your sound collection, manage multiple playing tracks, and control volumes seamlessly through a clean interface.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and streamers looking to add live sound effects to their broadcasts.
* Gamers who want to share funny soundbites with teammates during voice chats.
* Everyday users who enjoy quick access to a personalized soundboard for chats and calls.

### Key features
* Simple and clean user interface with dark and light theme options.
* Direct audio routing to specific applications or virtual audio cables.
* Built-in search tool to quickly find and trigger your favorite audio clips.
* Support for playing multiple sounds simultaneously with individual pause and stop controls.
* Cross-platform compatibility working smoothly across Linux and Windows environments.

* **Cons:** Requires initial setup of virtual audio cables on Windows or PulseAudio/PipeWire on Linux.
