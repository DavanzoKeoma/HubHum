### What it does in plain English
This application automatically translates comics, manga, and graphic novels from all over the world into your preferred language. It cleans up original text bubbles smoothly and replaces them with translated text using advanced artificial intelligence language models. You can easily open files in standard image formats or comic archives and read them seamlessly.

### Who it's for
* Manga and comic fans who want to read foreign publications in their native language without waiting for official translations.
* Language learners looking to read international stories with side-by-side linguistic clarity.
* Content creators and hobbyists working with international sequential art formats.

### Key features
* Supports translation across numerous major languages including English, Korean, Japanese, French, German, and Spanish.
* Cleans up original speech bubbles cleanly before rendering fresh, neatly wrapped translated text.
* Includes a manual correction mode to handle tricky text areas or unique font styles.
* Works with common digital comic file formats as well as standard image folders.
