### What it does in plain English
Herdr acts as a dedicated control center that keeps your automated programming assistants running smoothly even when you close your window or lose connection. It organizes multiple terminal windows across different machines into a single clean view, instantly highlighting which tasks are actively working or waiting for your input.

### Who it's for
* **Software developers** who rely on automated coding assistants and need a reliable way to keep multiple terminal sessions organized.
* **Power users** managing workflows across local machines and remote servers simultaneously from one unified window.

### Key features
* **Background persistence:** Keep your terminal environments running safely in the background when you detach or lose your network connection.
* **Unified multi-machine view:** Monitor and control workflows across your local computer and remote servers within one single interface.
* **Activity indicators:** Quickly spot which task is actively processing, idling, or blocked and waiting for a human answer.
* **Agent-native control:** Allow automated assistants to programmatically manage panes, prompt each other, and coordinate actions.
