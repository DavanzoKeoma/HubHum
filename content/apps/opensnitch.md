### What it does in plain English
OpenSnitch watches which programs on your computer try to connect to the internet. It pops up a simple question asking if you want to allow or block that specific connection. This gives you full control over your digital privacy without needing technical knowledge.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious users:** Individuals who want to know exactly what data their apps are sending out.
*   **Linux enthusiasts:** Users who want a user-friendly alternative to complex command-line firewall tools.
*   **Security-focused professionals:** People who need to monitor and restrict network traffic on their workstations.

### Key features
*   **Interactive prompts:** Get a clear notification every time a program tries to make a new internet connection.
*   **Block lists:** Easily block ads, trackers, and known malicious websites across your entire system.
*   **Centralized management:** Manage firewall rules for multiple computers from a single, easy-to-use interface.
*   **System integration:** Works seamlessly with your existing Linux firewall settings to provide comprehensive protection.
