### What it does in plain English
WalletWasabi is a digital wallet designed to help you store and manage your Bitcoin on your personal computer. It ensures that you remain the sole owner of your funds by keeping your digital keys under your own control at all times. The software includes built-in features to help you keep your financial activity private from outside observers.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want full control over their digital currency without relying on third-party services.
* Privacy-conscious users who want to manage their Bitcoin transactions while keeping their history confidential.

### Key features
* **Non-custodial control:** You hold your own keys, meaning no company can freeze or access your funds.
* **Privacy-focused design:** Built-in tools help obscure your transaction history to protect your financial footprint.
* **Desktop integration:** Works natively on Windows, macOS, and Linux for a consistent experience.
* **Open-source transparency:** The underlying code is public, allowing anyone to verify how the software handles your data.
