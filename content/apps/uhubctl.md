### What it does in plain English
This utility lets you turn the electrical power on and off for individual USB ports on supported smart hardware hubs. Instead of manually unplugging tangled cables, you can send quick commands to restart crashed gadgets, cycle charging ports, or cut power to idle peripherals.

### Who it's for
* Hardware tinkerers and home automation hobbyists who build custom lighting, sensors, or device clusters.
* System administrators who need to remotely reboot unresponsive USB modems, cameras, or external storage drives.
* Power users looking to eliminate phantom power draw from connected phone chargers and accessories.

### Key features
* Turn power completely on or off for specific USB ports using straightforward terminal commands.
* Automatically cycle power to force stubborn hardware components to reset and re-initialize.
* Target individual ports or ranges across multiple connected smart hubs seamlessly.

### How to install (Download)
You can easily install this utility through standard package managers on supported operating systems, or compile it directly from the source code. For detailed setup instructions and the complete list of verified hardware, visit the [Official Releases and Site](https://github.com/mvp/uhubctl/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, enables remote hardware resets without physical intervention, supports a wide range of popular smart USB hubs.
* **Cons:** Requires specific hardware that natively supports per-port power switching, and certain operating systems may need manual permission configuration.
