### What it does in plain English
Mealie is a private digital kitchen assistant that brings all your favorite recipes, meal schedules, and grocery lists into one organized place. Instead of bookmarking dozens of cooking blogs or losing paper notes, you can paste any recipe link and watch the app instantly extract the ingredients and instructions. It also helps you plan your weekly menu ahead of time and builds an organized shopping list sorted by supermarket aisle.

### Who it's for
* Home cooks who want to organize scattered family recipes and online bookmarks into a single searchable library.
* Busy parents and individuals looking to plan out weekly meals to save time and reduce food waste.
* Families who want a shared household grocery list that updates instantly whenever someone notices an empty pantry item.

### Key features
* **One-Click Recipe Import:** Grab ingredients and instructions instantly by pasting a link from almost any cooking website.
* **Weekly Meal Planner:** Assign breakfast, lunch, and dinner to specific days to take the guesswork out of daily cooking.
* **Smart Shopping Lists:** Automatically convert planned recipes into organized grocery shopping lists grouped by supermarket sections.
* **Digital Cookbooks:** Group your recipes into custom collections for baking, holidays, or quick weeknight dinners.
* **Multi-Language Support:** Fully translated into dozens of languages for cooks around the world.

### How to install (Download)
Mealie is designed to run on your own home server or private cloud using Docker. To check system requirements, container setup guides, and detailed instructions, visit the [Mealie Documentation Website](https://docs.mealie.io/). You can also test out the interface firsthand using the [Mealie Live Demo](https://demo.mealie.io/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, respects your personal privacy, and features a clean modern interface for the whole family.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of server hosting or Docker to set up on your own hardware.
