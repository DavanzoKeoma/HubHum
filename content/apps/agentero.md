### What it does in plain English
Agentero brings your research documents, personal notes, and AI conversations together into a single organized workspace. When you are reading complex PDF papers, you can instantly highlight text, extract charts, or query an AI assistant about specific sections without jumping between apps. Everything stays stored safely on your local computer so your personal library remains entirely under your control.

### Who it's for
* **Academic researchers and students** who need to read dense scientific papers, manage reference notes, and explore citations efficiently.
* **Writers and knowledge workers** looking to connect reference documents with flexible markdown notes and AI analysis tools.
* **Privacy-conscious professionals** who prefer keeping their document libraries and reading history stored locally on their own devices.

### Key features
* **Structured PDF Reading:** Jump through document chapters easily, highlight text, search full text, and extract formulas or tables into clean formats.
* **Direct AI Collaboration:** Send selected paragraphs, charts, or notes straight to your preferred AI assistant to analyze arguments or translate text.
* **Interlinked Notes:** Create markdown notes with bi-directional links and real-time equation previews to build a connected web of ideas.
* **Literature Discovery:** Browse new academic papers, track updates, and import references directly into your personal library.
* **Local and Flexible Storage:** Keep your files transparently stored on your hard drive with optional cloud synchronization support.
