### What it does in plain English
Gold Miner is a large collaborative community platform that translates premium English technical articles into Chinese. It gathers essential guides, official documentation, and deep dives across various software fields so readers can access cutting-edge knowledge easily. Dedicated volunteers work together to translate, review, and share valuable learning resources for everyone.

### Who it's for
* Software developers looking to read global technical insights in their native language.
* Translators and reviewers wanting to contribute to open educational projects and sharpen their skills.
* Tech enthusiasts eager to explore artificial intelligence, web development, and system design guides.

### Key features
* Vast collection of thousands of translated articles covering web development, mobile apps, and artificial intelligence.
* Curated official documentation and comprehensive guides for modern software foundation and tools.
* Active community-driven peer review system to ensure translation accuracy and readability.
* Structured categorization including blockchain, backend engineering, product management, and design.

### How to install (Download)
* Browse and read all available translated articles directly on the [Juejin Translation Tag Page](https://juejin.cn/tag/掘金翻译计划).
* Read contributor guidelines and join the community translation effort via the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/xitu/gold-miner).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to read, covers a massive range of modern topics, updated regularly by active contributors.
* **Cons:** Primarily focused on translating English content into Chinese, reading materials are hosted across external community platforms.
