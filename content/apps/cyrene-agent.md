### What it does in plain English
Cyrene-Agent is a friendly desktop companion that places an animated virtual character right on your computer screen. She chats naturally with you, helps organize your daily schedule, assists with writing and editing code, and even helps you study new topics using your own notes. She remembers your preferences over time and can interact through text, voice, or connected messaging apps.

### Who it's for
* Fans of interactive desktop characters who want a lively and helpful companion while working or browsing.
* Developers and writers looking for an intelligent assistant that can read local folders, search the web, and help manage files securely.
* Students who want a patient study partner to organize notes, explain concepts, and track learning progress.

### Key features
* **Live2D Desktop Companion:** An animated character living on your screen who reacts with expressions, movement, and voice feedback.
* **Versatile Chat Modes:** Switch easily between casual conversation, general work assistance, code collaboration, and study support.
* **Personalized Memory:** Retains important details about your preferences and past interactions to make every conversation feel natural.
* **Voice and Chat Integration:** Supports voice calls, speech recognition, and connection to popular messaging platforms.
* **Extensible Ecosystem:** Add custom tools, plugins, and web services to expand what your companion can do.
