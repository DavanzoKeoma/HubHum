### What it does in plain English
Gloomberb is a fast, keyboard-driven finance workspace that brings professional market data directly into your command line, desktop app, or browser. You can look up company details, inspect interactive price charts, read filings, and monitor your personal portfolio without reaching for the mouse. It streamlines everyday market research by placing all essential financial metrics into a single, uncluttered screen.

### Who it's for
* Day traders and individual investors who prefer keyboard shortcuts over cluttered web interfaces
* Financial analysts and hobbyists who want quick quotes and company financials inside their command line workspace
* Power users who enjoy lightweight desktop utilities and customizable terminal environments

### Key features
* Navigate quotes, charts, financials, options, and news entirely using fast keyboard commands
* Render financial charts as clear images inside modern terminal windows or as clean text layouts elsewhere
* Manage and monitor your personal portfolio and watchlists in real time
* Expand functionality easily with community plugins or custom data sources
* Access the workspace seamlessly across your terminal, desktop application, or web browser
