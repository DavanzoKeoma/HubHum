### What it does in plain English
OncePower is a desktop utility that helps you clean up messy filenames and organize folders in bulk. It provides a visual interface to preview photos and videos, add sequential numbers, change dates, and move files into structured categories. You can preview all changes instantly before applying them to your storage drive.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and hobbyists with thousands of camera files that need neat naming.
* Office workers and students who want to tidy up cluttered download folders quickly.
* Anyone looking for an easy way to sort, group, and rename computer documents without complex technical steps.

### Key features
* Batch rename files using replacement rules, prefixes, suffixes, custom sequences, or date adjustments.
* Visual preview mode to inspect photos and videos before renaming them.
* Advanced mode to delete specific text segments, insert random numbers, or format words automatically.
* Organization mode to automatically sort renamed files into category folders based on type, date, or custom groups.
* Preset system to save and reuse your favorite renaming workflows for future projects.
