### What it does in plain English
NotificationBanner is a developer tool that helps app creators show elegant drop-down alerts and notification messages inside their mobile applications. It handles multiple messages neatly by organizing them in a queue so they never overlap or hide one another. Developers can easily adjust colors, add custom icons, or change how long each message stays on the screen.

### Who it's for
* **iOS App Developers:** Programmers building iPhone and iPad apps who need a reliable way to show alerts.
* **Mobile Product Teams:** Teams looking to add polished user feedback messages without designing alert systems from scratch.

### Key features
* **Custom Styles:** Choose from pre-built success, warning, danger, and info designs, or create your own.
* **Smart Queueing:** Automatically manages multiple alerts so they appear one after another or stack smoothly.
* **Flexible Placement:** Show alerts from the top or bottom of the screen, or right below navigation bars.
* **Rich Content Support:** Easily include scrolling titles, subtitles, side images, and custom views.
* **User Interaction:** Let users tap or swipe up to dismiss alerts instantly or trigger custom actions.

* **Pros:** Lightweight, highly customizable, includes built-in haptic feedback and queue management.
* **Cons:** Requires familiarity with Swift and Xcode development tools to integrate into mobile projects.
