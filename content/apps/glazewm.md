### What it does in plain English
GlazeWM takes control of your desktop workspace by automatically snapping open application windows into neat, structured grids. Instead of dragging and resizing windows with your mouse, you can instantly move, resize, and switch between them using simple keyboard commands. It brings advanced workspace organization and multi-monitor efficiency directly to your everyday computing environment.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want to manage heavy multitasking without touching the mouse.
* Programmers, writers, and analysts who need multiple applications visible simultaneously on large or multi-monitor setups.
* Anyone looking to eliminate desktop clutter and speed up their daily workflow.

### Key features
* Fully automatic window tiling that eliminates manual dragging and overlapping.
* Simple text-based configuration files to customize keybindings and gaps.
* Seamless multi-monitor support for expansive workspaces.
* Custom rules to force specific apps into fullscreen, floating, or designated spaces.
* Optional status bar integration to monitor system stats and active spaces.
