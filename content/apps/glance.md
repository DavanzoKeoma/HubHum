### What it does in plain English
Glance brings all your daily information into one clean, organized portal so you do not have to check multiple websites. It pulls together news updates, video channel uploads, weather forecasts, and financial market prices onto a single custom screen. Everything updates smoothly and loads instantly, keeping your daily reading and monitoring in one comfortable place. ### Who it's for
* Information enthusiasts who want a clean, single-page overview of their favorite news sites and social forums.
* Busy professionals tracking weather, markets, and server status boards all at once.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer a self-hosted alternative to commercial browser start pages.
