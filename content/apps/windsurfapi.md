### What it does in plain English
WindsurfAPI acts as a bridge that connects your private AI accounts to various third-party software. It takes the advanced models you already pay for and makes them compatible with standard industry interfaces. This allows you to use your preferred AI services inside tools that might not normally support them.

### Who it's for
* Developers who want to use specific AI models within their preferred coding editors.
* Power users who want to consolidate multiple AI subscriptions into a single, unified interface.

### Key features
* **Universal Compatibility:** Converts proprietary AI access into standard formats used by most modern software.
* **Account Management:** Handles multiple accounts with built-in features for switching and reliability.
* **Privacy Focused:** Operates as a self-hosted service, meaning you keep control over your own data and credentials.
* **Broad Model Support:** Provides access to over 100 different AI models, including the latest versions from major providers.

* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no subscription fees for the software itself, and works locally on your machine.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of how to run software on your computer and is intended for users who already have their own AI subscriptions.
