### What it does in plain English
Opengist provides a private, self-hosted space where you can save and organize pieces of code or text. It keeps your information secure on your own hardware while allowing you to manage your notes with the same ease as professional coding tools. You can easily share your snippets with others or keep them strictly for your own reference.

### Who it's for
* Developers and students who need a reliable place to store code snippets for later use.
* Tech enthusiasts who prefer to host their own data rather than relying on third-party services.

### Key features
* **Total Privacy:** Keep your snippets on your own server, ensuring you have full control over your data.
* **Flexible Sharing:** Choose whether your snippets are public, private, or unlisted.
* **Smart Organization:** Add topics to your snippets to keep your library tidy and searchable.
* **Version History:** Track changes to your code over time with built-in revision history.
* **Easy Access:** Use the web interface or standard file management tools to interact with your saved snippets.

### How to install (Download)
Opengist is designed to be hosted on your own server or computer. You can find the official installation guides and the latest releases on the [Official Opengist Website](https://opengist.io).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, gives you complete ownership of your data, and supports modern login methods.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup or container software to get started.
