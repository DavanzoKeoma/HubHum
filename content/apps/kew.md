### What it does in plain English
kew is a streamlined music player designed for people who want to listen to their own music collection without internet-based tracking. It runs directly in your command interface, providing a clean and fast way to organize and play your audio files. Because it works entirely offline, you have complete control over your listening experience without any unwanted suggestions or data collection.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who keep large collections of local audio files and want a fast, no-nonsense way to play them.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer tools that do not track their listening habits or require an account.

### Key features
* **Total Privacy:** Operates entirely offline with no algorithmic manipulation or data tracking.
* **Versatile Playback:** Supports a wide range of audio formats including MP3, FLAC, OGG, and M4A.
* **Smart Organization:** Automatically generates playlists based on your search terms and supports easy playlist editing.
* **Visual Experience:** Includes a built-in spectrum visualizer and displays album cover art.
* **Customizable:** Offers theme generation based on album colors and supports custom layouts to suit your style.
