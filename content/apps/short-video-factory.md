### What it does in plain English
Short Video Factory is a desktop tool that simplifies the process of making short videos for marketing or social media. You provide a simple text prompt and your video clips, and the software automatically handles the writing, voiceover, and editing. It creates professional-looking videos with subtitles and effects without requiring you to be a video editing expert.

### Who it's for
*   Small business owners who need to create product marketing videos quickly.
*   Content creators who want to automate the editing of repetitive video content.

### Key features
*   **AI-Powered Writing:** Automatically generates scripts based on your topic or prompt.
*   **Automated Editing:** Handles the cutting, mixing, and arrangement of your video clips.
*   **Natural Voiceovers:** Converts your script into high-quality, realistic speech.
*   **Batch Processing:** Allows you to create multiple videos in one go to save time.
*   **Local Privacy:** Runs directly on your computer, keeping your video files and data private.
