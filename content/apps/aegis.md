### What it does in plain English
Aegis helps you protect your online accounts by generating the temporary security codes required for two-factor authentication. It keeps these codes safe on your phone using strong encryption that only you can unlock with a password or your fingerprint. You can easily back up your data and move your accounts between devices whenever you need to.

### Who it's for
*   Privacy-conscious individuals who want full control over their security data.
*   Android users looking for a reliable, open-source alternative to mainstream authentication apps.

### Key features
*   **Strong Security:** Protects your data with industry-standard encryption and optional biometric locking.
*   **Easy Migration:** Allows you to import your existing accounts from many other popular authentication apps.
*   **Flexible Backups:** Keeps your information safe by allowing you to create manual or automatic backups.
*   **Customizable Interface:** Offers a clean, modern design with light, dark, and AMOLED themes.
*   **Privacy Focused:** Prevents screen captures to ensure your codes remain visible only to you.
