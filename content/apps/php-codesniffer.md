### What it does in plain English
PHP_CodeSniffer helps you maintain high-quality work by scanning your files for inconsistent formatting or style mistakes. It acts like a digital proofreader for your programming projects, highlighting areas that don't match your chosen standards. Additionally, it includes a helper that can automatically rewrite your files to fix these common errors instantly.

### Who it's for
* Web developers who want to keep their project files neat and uniform.
* Teams that need to ensure every member follows the same formatting rules.

### Key features
* **Automated Scanning:** Quickly identifies style violations across your entire project.
* **Automatic Corrections:** Includes a tool that can fix most formatting issues for you.
* **Customizable Standards:** Supports various industry-standard styles to match your specific needs.
* **Multi-language Support:** Works with PHP, JavaScript, and CSS files to keep your web projects tidy.
