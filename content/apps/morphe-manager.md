### What it does in plain English
Morphe Manager lets Android users modify popular media and social apps directly on their phones. It works by applying custom patches right onto your device without sending any personal data elsewhere. You can easily remove distracting ads and customize your everyday viewing experience with just a few taps.

### Who it's for
* Android users looking to eliminate advertisements from their favorite media apps.
* People who prefer a simple, automated setup or advanced manual control over app modifications.
* Users who want a clean, distraction-free viewing experience on mobile devices without needing root access.

### Key features
* Simple mode for fast, one-tap patching with smart default settings.
* Expert mode for granular control over individual patches, custom color themes, and live logs.
* Batch processing to queue multiple app updates at once.
* Built-in support for multiple installation methods including standard files and advanced device setups.
* Automatic background update alerts when fresh patch options become available.

* **Pros:** Completely free to use, runs entirely locally for privacy, and offers both beginner-friendly and advanced patching modes.
* **Cons:** Requires allowing installations from unknown sources on Android, and original app files must be provided by the user.
