### What it does in plain English
Photos and videos often contain hidden personal data, such as the exact GPS location where they were taken, the device used, and timestamps. This app lets you drag and drop your media files to safely erase all that hidden baggage in seconds. It runs completely offline on your computer, meaning your private memories never get uploaded to any third-party server or cloud network.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and creators who want to share work online without exposing location coordinates.
* Everyday internet users concerned about digital privacy and tracking.
* Professionals handling sensitive internal documents or client photos before public release.

### Key features
* **Simple Drag and Drop:** Drop individual files or entire folders to process hundreds of items simultaneously.
* **Complete Privacy:** Zero internet connection required, zero telemetry, and zero tracking.
* **Inspect Changes:** Expand any processed file to view a clear before-and-after comparison of what was removed.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works smoothly on Windows, macOS, and Linux computers.
* **Flexible Controls:** Choose whether to keep file creation timestamps, preserve image orientation, or save cleaned files as separate copies.
