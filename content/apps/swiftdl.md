### What it does in plain English
SwiftDL is a modern download manager that splits large files into multiple simultaneous streams to dramatically increase download speeds. If your connection drops or your computer crashes, it safely picks up right where it left off instead of starting over. It also integrates smoothly with your web browser to automatically catch links and handle media files.

### Who it's for
* Power users who frequently download heavy software installers, games, or media files.
* People with slow or unstable internet connections who need reliable download recovery.
* Anyone looking for a clean, ad-free desktop application to organize and queue their downloads.

### Key features
* **Multi-connection acceleration:** Divides files into separate streams to maximize available bandwidth.
* **Instant resume:** Automatically saves progress every second so interruptions never waste your data.
* **Browser integration:** Seamlessly catches downloads from major browsers using your active cookies.
* **Media capturing:** Easily grabs video and audio files from supported streaming and sharing sites.
* **Organized file sorting:** Automatically categorizes finished downloads into dedicated folders.
