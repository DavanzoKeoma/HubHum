### What it does in plain English
Textbee turns any Android phone into a personal messaging hub that connects to your computer or online services. Instead of paying expensive fees for professional messaging services, you use your existing phone plan to send and receive texts. It provides a simple dashboard and tools to automate alerts, verification codes, or customer replies directly from your own device.

### Who it's for
* **Developers and small business owners** who need a cost-effective way to send automated notifications or verification codes to users.
* **Home automation enthusiasts** who want to receive system alerts or control smart devices via text messages.

### Key features
* **Direct Integration:** Send and receive messages using your own phone number and carrier plan.
* **Automation Ready:** Works with popular automation tools and AI agents to handle messages without manual input.
* **Bulk Messaging:** Upload contact lists to send messages in batches while staying within carrier limits.
* **Full History:** View all sent and received messages in a clean, organized web dashboard.
* **Privacy Focused:** Choose between a free hosted service or self-hosting the entire system on your own hardware for total data control.

* **Pros:** No monthly per-message fees, uses your existing phone number, and supports both cloud and self-hosted setups.
* **Cons:** Requires an Android device to function, and self-hosting requires basic knowledge of server management.
