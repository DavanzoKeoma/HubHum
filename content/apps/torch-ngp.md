### What it does in plain English
This tool turns ordinary photos and videos of real-world objects or spaces into realistic, fully interactive 3D digital models. It uses advanced mathematical techniques to learn what a scene looks like from every angle in just a few minutes. Users can explore, rotate, and view these detailed 3D reconstructions in real time using an included visual interface.

### Who it's for
* 3D artists and game designers looking to quickly turn physical objects into digital models.
* Researchers and students experimenting with modern computer vision and neural graphics.
* Photography enthusiasts wanting to create immersive digital memories from standard video clips.

### Key features
* Rapid training speeds that turn standard photo collections into 3D scenes in minutes.
* Built-in visual interface to watch and test your 3D models being built live.
* Support for dynamic scenes and moving objects captured on video.
* Compatibility with popular 3D dataset formats and custom camera setups.
* Efficient data processing that balances high visual fidelity with smooth rendering performance.
