### What it does in plain English
This application gives you a friendly web page to create stunning digital artwork using powerful artificial intelligence models right on your own computer. It is designed to work smoothly with various graphics cards—including both AMD and Nvidia chips—while providing a reliable backup mode if you only have a standard processor. You can easily type in descriptive text, modify existing pictures, add custom visual elements, and manage your creative projects through a clean browser interface.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and hobbyists who want to generate AI artwork locally without relying on expensive online subscription services.
* PC owners with AMD or Nvidia graphics cards who need a reliable tool that supports hardware acceleration.
* Creators looking for an organized workspace to experiment with different image styles, upscaling, and blending features.

### Key features
* Simple browser-based interface to generate, view, and manage your AI artwork history.
* Full hardware acceleration support for both Nvidia and AMD graphics cards, plus a fallback mode for regular processors.
* Comprehensive set of creative tools including text-to-image, image-to-image, inpainting, and upscaling.
* Ability to download and integrate your own custom models, artistic styles, and additional weight files.
* Built-in server capabilities that allow remote access and background processing for your creative tasks.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Runs locally on your own hardware, supports both AMD and Nvidia graphics cards, free and open-source.
* **Cons:** Requires a dedicated graphics card for fast results, initial setup involves downloading large model files.
