### What it does in plain English
AriaNg-Native gives you a dedicated desktop workspace to oversee your file downloads smoothly. Instead of juggling browser tabs, you get a standalone application that handles direct files, torrents, and magnet links right out of the box. It runs quietly in the background and alerts you the moment your files are safely saved on your computer.

### Who it's for
* Power users who frequently download large files, software distributions, or media and want a dedicated tracker.
* People who prefer a clean, independent desktop app over managing downloads inside a web browser.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight tool to handle torrents and direct download links in one single place.

### Key features
* Operates as a standalone desktop application for Windows and macOS without requiring a browser window.
* Supports drag-and-drop actions for files, web links, torrents, and metalink packages.
* Integrates directly with your system to handle magnet links and torrent file associations automatically.
* Minimizes neatly to the taskbar tray so it stays out of your way while working.
* Plays custom audio notifications to let you know precisely when a download finishes.
