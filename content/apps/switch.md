### What it does in plain English
Switch turns your team's chat channels into collaborative workspaces where AI assistants work alongside humans. Instead of switching between different programs, you can invite AI teammates directly into Slack, Teams, Discord, or other messaging apps to help with daily tasks. These assistants share the same context as your team, allowing them to help with project updates, file management, and technical troubleshooting in real-time.

### Who it's for
* **Project Managers** who want to automate routine status updates and ticket tracking.
* **Software Development Teams** who need AI help with code reviews, bug reporting, and incident management without leaving their chat platform.

### Key features
* **Seamless Integration:** Works inside the messaging apps you already use, so no one has to learn a new interface.
* **Flexible Agent Support:** Connects with a wide variety of AI models and custom-built agents to suit your specific needs.
* **Shared Context:** Agents see the same conversation history and documents as your team, ensuring they provide relevant and accurate help.
* **Privacy-Focused:** You can host the software on your own servers to ensure your team's data stays under your control.
* **Role-Based Access:** Admins can decide exactly which agents have access to specific channels and what tasks they are allowed to perform.
