### What it does in plain English
Auto Dark Mode manages your computer's visual appearance so you don't have to change settings manually. It detects when the sun rises or sets in your area and adjusts your screen brightness and colors accordingly. This helps reduce eye strain by ensuring your display is always comfortable for your current environment.

### Who it's for
* Office workers and students who spend long hours at their computers.
* People who prefer a bright screen during the day and a softer, darker interface at night.

### Key features
* **Smart Scheduling:** Automatically switches themes based on your local sunrise and sunset times.
* **Customization:** Change your desktop wallpaper, mouse cursor, and accent colors alongside the theme.
* **Gaming Mode:** Prevents the theme from switching while you are playing games to ensure smooth performance.
* **Battery Efficiency:** Can automatically enable dark mode when your device is running on battery power.
* **Lightweight:** Runs quietly in the background without requiring administrator permissions.
