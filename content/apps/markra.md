### What it does in plain English
Markra is a modern writing application designed for people who take notes and draft documents using Markdown. It combines a clean, user-friendly live preview with intelligent artificial intelligence tools that help polish and expand your writing. Your files stay securely stored on your local drive while giving you the option to connect your preferred AI services.

### Who it's for
* Bloggers and content creators drafting articles in plain text formats.
* Students and researchers organizing study notes and technical documentation.
* Writers who want a distraction-free space with optional AI assistance for editing.

### Key features
* Seamless live preview that hides complex code until you need to edit it.
* Built-in artificial intelligence assistant to rewrite, summarize, or translate your text.
* Local-first storage keeping all your files safely on your hard drive.
* Support for tables, mathematical equations, and visual diagrams.
* Custom styling options and clean export tools for sharing your work.
