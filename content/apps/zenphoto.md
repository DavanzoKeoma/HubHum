### What it does in plain English
Zenphoto is a straightforward website manager designed to help you build stunning online galleries without dealing with bloated software. It organizes your photos, video clips, and audio tracks neatly while staying out of your way. Built-in blogging tools also let you publish news updates and custom pages right alongside your creative portfolios.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and visual artists wanting a clean portfolio to display their work.
* Musicians and filmmakers looking to stream audio and video clips directly on their own domain.
* Illustrators and designers who need an independent space to publish custom pages and news updates.

### Key features
* Built-in support for multiple image, video, and audio formats.
* Integrated news section and blogging tools via the Zenpage plugin.
* Clean, minimalist setup that keeps management options out of sight until needed.
* Perfect organizational structure for creative portfolios and personal showcases.
* Completely self-hosted solution giving you full control over your media files.

### How to install (Download)
To set up Zenphoto on your web hosting server, visit the official website and follow the setup instructions provided at the [Zenphoto Official Website](http://www.zenphoto.org). Detailed setup guides and upgrade steps are available on the [Zenphoto Installation Page](http://www.zenphoto.org/news/installation-and-upgrading).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, complete ownership of your media, tailored specifically for creative portfolios.
* **Cons:** Requires a compatible web hosting server to set up, not a hosted SaaS platform.
