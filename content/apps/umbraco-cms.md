### What it does in plain English
Umbraco is a powerful tool designed to help you build and manage professional websites. It provides a user-friendly interface that allows you to organize your content and design your pages without unnecessary complexity. By focusing on a clean experience, it helps you deliver high-quality digital content to your audience.

### Who it's for
*   Small business owners looking to manage their own professional website content.
*   Web designers who want a flexible foundation to build custom, high-performance sites.

### Key features
*   **User-Friendly Editing:** An intuitive interface that makes updating text and images simple.
*   **Highly Flexible:** Allows you to customize the structure of your website to fit your specific needs.
*   **Community Support:** Access to a large, active group of users and helpful forums for guidance.
*   **Professional Standards:** Built on reliable technology that ensures your website remains stable and secure.

### How to install (Download)
Umbraco is designed to be set up on your own server or hosting environment. To get started, visit the [official website](https://umbraco.com) to review the requirements and follow the installation guide provided in their [official documentation](https://docs.umbraco.com/umbraco-cms/fundamentals/setup/install).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly customizable, and backed by a large supportive community.
*   **Cons:** Requires some technical knowledge to set up and maintain compared to simple drag-and-drop website builders.
