### What it does in plain English
PicView opens any picture on your computer in an instant.  
It lets you browse, zoom, crop, rename and even batch‑process many files at once.  
All of this works without ads, tracking or hidden fees.

### Who it's for
- People who love looking at family photos on a PC or Mac.  
- Hobby photographers who need quick edits without learning complex software.  
- Anyone who wants a simple, fast way to view and organize images.

### Key features
- Supports almost every picture format, including animated GIF and WebP.  
- Switch between light and dark themes, or hide the interface for a clean view.  
- Edit images: crop, resize, rename, convert, compress, and add ratings.  
- Browse collections with a bottom gallery and handy keyboard shortcuts.  
- Batch process many files to resize, convert or create thumbnails.
