### What it does in plain English
ytDownloader is a friendly desktop application designed to grab video and audio clips from hundreds of popular online platforms. Instead of navigating confusing web tools or command-line windows, you simply paste a web link into a clean screen to save your media files. It includes built-in features to compress large video files, select specific clips, and grab full playlists with just a couple of clicks.

### Who it's for
* **Content creators** who need to archive their own media or collect reference footage for future editing projects.
* **Students and teachers** who want to save educational lectures and tutorials for offline viewing during travel or weak internet spots.
* **Music and video enthusiasts** looking for a clean, tracker-free way to keep their favorite online entertainment accessible locally.

### Key features
* **Wide compatibility:** Works seamlessly with hundreds of popular video and social media sites across the internet.
* **Built-in video compressor:** Shrinks large video files using hardware acceleration so they take up less space on your drive.
* **Playlist support:** Grabs entire collections of videos or songs all at once without requiring manual downloads for each file.
* **Custom selection:** Lets you choose specific timestamp ranges or download accompanying subtitles alongside your media.
* **Privacy-focused:** Contains zero hidden trackers or annoying pop-up advertisements to keep your workflow clean.
