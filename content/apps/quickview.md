### What it does in plain English
QuickView is a high-performance image viewer designed specifically to open your photos, artwork, and camera files without waiting around. It skips heavy editing tools to focus purely on a lightning-fast, smooth viewing experience, whether you are browsing a single snapshot or thousands of comic book pages. Leveraging modern computer graphics technology, it handles everything from tiny web icons to massive ultra-high-definition camera files with zero lag.

### Who it's for
* **Photographers and hobbyists:** Quickly preview large camera RAW files, check color accuracy, and compare multiple shots side by side.
* **Digital artists and designers:** Reference artwork easily using semi-transparent overlay modes and instant zooming tools.
* **Comics and manga readers:** Seamlessly browse archived digital books and multi-page stories stored in zip or rar formats without unpacking them.

### Key features
* **Instant loading speed:** Opens even the largest digital photos and modern compressed formats immediately using multi-threaded decoding.
* **Extensive format compatibility:** Supports classic images, modern web standards, and professional camera RAW files out of the box.
* **Side-by-side comparison:** Compare two images simultaneously with synchronized zooming, panning, and visual metrics.
* **Geek visualization HUD:** Inspect real-time color histograms, metadata details, and performance metrics with a single keystroke.
* **Portable and lightweight:** Runs cleanly on your system without cluttering your hard drive or bogging down background memory.
