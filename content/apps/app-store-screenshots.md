### What it does in plain English
App Store Screenshots is a visual editor tool that turns raw application captures into polished marketing slides designed to catch eyes on digital store shelves. It lets you arrange device frames, large readable captions, and decorative backgrounds across a connected canvas. When your layout looks perfect, you can instantly export precise image bundles tailored for Apple and Google app stores in all required sizes and languages.

### Who it's for
* Independent app developers looking to showcase their software professionally without hiring a designer.
* Startup founders and marketers wanting to test different visual styles and captions for their app store listings.
* Designers seeking an efficient way to layout connected screenshot strips that span across multiple preview screens.

### Key features
* **Connected canvas:** View your entire screenshot sequence side by side, allowing visual elements to span naturally across screen boundaries.
* **18 named visual styles:** Instantly apply professionally curated themes, color palettes, and typographic rhythms to match your app's vibe.
* **Multi-platform support:** Build preview assets for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, Apple TV, Android phones, tablets, and store feature graphics.
* **Comprehensive device frames:** Render your app inside authentic device bezels with adjustable shadows, glows, and 3D tilts.
* **One-click export bundles:** Automatically generate and download perfectly dimensioned PNG image packs organized by device, resolution, and language.
