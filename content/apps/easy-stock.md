### What it does in plain English
easy-stock is an intelligent desktop application designed to help investors research stocks, monitor market trends, and review their investment portfolios using artificial intelligence. It aggregates real-time market signals, financial news, and expert commentaries into a single unified workspace. By evaluating data patterns and verifying underlying evidence, the software provides structured insights to support your financial decision-making process.

### Who it's for
* Individual investors looking for a comprehensive workspace to analyze market trends and stock performance.
* Traders who want artificial intelligence assistance to summarize expert commentaries and check portfolio risks.
* Users who prefer a local application to organize and review financial research without manual web scraping.

### Key features
* **Market Overview & Trends:** Aggregates major indices, financial news, fund flows, and sector radar into a single research hub.
* **Stock Analysis:** Evaluates companies using multi-tiered research levels, checking supporting and opposing evidence.
* **Portfolio Inspection:** Reviews asset allocation, calculates multi-dimensional risk scores, and tracks performance metrics.
* **Automated Reviews:** Collects and summarizes expert commentaries and market opinions from multiple online platforms.
* **Local Privacy:** Keeps your research data, analysis history, and custom portfolio setups securely on your personal computer.
