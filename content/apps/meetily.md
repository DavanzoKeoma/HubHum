### What it does in plain English
Meetily is a privacy-first assistant that records and transcribes your professional meetings directly on your computer. It turns spoken conversations into searchable text and clear bullet-point summaries using on-device artificial intelligence. Because all processing happens locally, your sensitive audio files never leave your machine unless you explicitly choose an external cloud provider. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad).

### Who it's for
- Business executives, legal professionals, and healthcare providers who handle confidential client discussions and need strict compliance.
- Remote workers and project managers looking for automated, searchable records of daily team syncs without expensive subscriptions.
- Security-conscious organizations that want automated meeting notes while retaining complete ownership of their data infrastructure.

### Key features
- **Local Transcription:** Uses advanced on-device speech models to convert spoken dialogue into text without depending on internet access.
- **AI-Powered Summaries:** Generates concise action items and key takeaways using local models or custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints.
- **Simultaneous Audio Mixing:** Captures both your microphone and system audio cleanly with built-in volume balancing and noise protection.
- **Import and Enhance:** Easily import past audio recordings to re-transcribe them using different languages or updated models.
- Ready-to-use digital planners, printable checklists, and clean document templates for daily organization via [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
