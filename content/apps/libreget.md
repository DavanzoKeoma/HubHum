### What it does in plain English
LibreGet is a free desktop application that lets you easily download online courses, videos, music, images, and torrents from over a thousand websites. Instead of juggling multiple browser extensions or command-line scripts, you simply paste any link into a clean window.

### Who it's for
- Students and professionals who want to back up online courses and educational videos before platforms remove them.
- Content creators and researchers looking for a straightforward tool to grab media clips, social media stories, and audio tracks.
- Everyday computer users who want an all-in-one download helper that handles torrents, documents, and subtitle files without ads or subscriptions.

### Key features
- Paste any link to instantly inspect, select qualities, and download videos, courses, or audio files.
- Built-in media player, document readers, and an expansive tools section for trimming, converting, and editing files.
- Companion browser extension that passes page cookies securely for downloading members-only or logged-in content.
- Fully open-source with local storage, ensuring your downloads and account credentials never leave your machine.
