### What it does in plain English
GithubStarsManager transforms messy lists of bookmarked software projects into an organized, searchable library. It connects to your account, automatically groups items by category, and generates clear summaries using artificial intelligence. You can easily find the exact project you need later by searching using plain everyday language instead of remembering exact names.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and engineers who bookmark countless code libraries and lose track of them over time.
* Tech enthusiasts and researchers who collect useful tools, scripts, and guides on software platforms.
* Anyone who saves interesting links online and needs a smarter way to categorize and retrieve them.

### Key features
* **Smart AI organization:** Automatically writes summaries, tags, and categories for everything you save.
* **Semantic search:** Finds saved projects based on the meaning of your search terms rather than exact keywords.
* **Release tracking:** Keeps track of software updates and new versions from the projects you follow.
* **Local-first data privacy:** Keeps your personal data stored locally on your device for complete privacy and control.
* **Assistant Q&A:** Answers straightforward questions about specific projects based on their latest code updates.
