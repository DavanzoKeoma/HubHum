### What it does in plain English
TaxHacker helps you manage your business finances by automatically reading information from photos of receipts and PDF invoices. It extracts key details like dates, amounts, and vendors, then stores them in a clean, searchable list. Because you host it yourself, your sensitive financial data stays entirely under your control.

### Who it's for
* **Freelancers and Independent Creators:** People who need to track business expenses quickly without manual data entry.
* **Small Business Owners:** Anyone looking for a private, self-hosted way to organize invoices and prepare for tax season.

### Key features
* **Smart Document Scanning:** Upload photos or PDFs and let AI pull out the important numbers and details automatically.
* **Custom Organization:** Create your own categories, projects, and custom labels to match your specific business needs.
* **Global Currency Support:** Automatically converts transactions to your preferred currency using historical exchange rates, including support for major cryptocurrencies.
* **Total Privacy:** Since you host the software on your own hardware, your financial records never leave your possession.
* **Flexible Exporting:** Easily download your organized data to share with an accountant or move to other financial software.
