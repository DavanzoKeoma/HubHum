### What it does in plain English
Kap is a lightweight screen recording tool designed specifically for macOS. It sits quietly in your menu bar until you need it, letting you select a specific window or a custom area of your desktop. With a single click, you can record crisp video clips and turn them into shareable formats right away.

### Who it's for
* Content creators making software tutorials and demonstration videos
* Support teams recording quick visual bug reports for engineers
* Everyday users wanting to share funny moments or clips from their desktop

### Key features
* Sleek, minimalist menu bar control that stays out of your way
* Custom selection area for recording either full screens or specific windows
* Plugin support to extend functionality and share recordings instantly
* Open-source code built with modern web technologies
