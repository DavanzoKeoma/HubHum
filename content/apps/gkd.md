### What it does in plain English
gkd is an automation helper for Android devices that watches your screen and performs taps automatically based on your custom instructions. It lets you skip unwanted intro sequences, bypass annoying pop-ups, and automate repetitive clicks in your favorite apps. By utilizing advanced selectors and subscription rules, the app precisely detects specific text or visual elements on your screen to trigger actions instantly.

### Who it's for
* Android users who want to skip repetitive startup prompts, splash screens, and annoying dialogs.
* Power users looking to automate everyday device workflows using custom text and node selection rules.

### Key features
* Automatically tap or click specific screen elements based on advanced text matching rules.
* Skip annoying startup animations and promotional flows effortlessly.
* Support for local rule creation and remote subscription lists to keep your automations updated.
* Built-in snapshot review tools to inspect screen layouts and create precise rules.
