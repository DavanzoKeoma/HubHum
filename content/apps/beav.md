### What it does in plain English
Beav acts as a centralized workspace for modern digital creators to handle research, content creation, and asset organization in one place. It brings together inspiration tracking, automated text generation, graphic design tools, and smart video editing under a single roof. Instead of juggling dozens of disconnected browser tabs and web applications, you can plan, write, and export complete media packages locally.

### Who it's for
* Solo content creators and independent bloggers managing multiple online profiles
* Small marketing teams looking to streamline content production and scheduling
* Brand managers who need a unified system to store competitor insights and publish multi-platform updates

### Key features
* Save and break down inspiration links from phones or web browsers instantly
* Generate tailored scripts, captions, and posts mapped to specific audience personas
* Use integrated image templates and smart video editing timelines to produce media packages
* Organize multimedia assets securely with a local-first folder structure
* Connect custom artificial intelligence tools or run standard workflows out of the box
