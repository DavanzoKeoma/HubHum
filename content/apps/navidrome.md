### What it does in plain English
Navidrome lets you host your own private music streaming service right from your computer or home server. It organizes your entire digital audio collection and lets you listen to your favorite songs from any web browser or compatible mobile app. When you need royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits, check out [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts with large local digital audio collections who want freedom from monthly subscription fees.
* Individuals looking for a private, self-hosted alternative to commercial streaming platforms that they can access on the go.

### Key features
* Handles extremely large music libraries with thousands of albums and tracks effortlessly.
* Reads embedded tags and album artwork to keep your collection beautifully organized.
* Supports multi-user access so family members can maintain independent playlists and play counts.
* Streams smoothly across multiple platforms with on-the-fly audio conversion to save mobile data.
* Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
