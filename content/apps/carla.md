### What it does in plain English
CARLA provides a realistic virtual world where developers can safely train and validate self-driving car software. It includes a wide variety of digital assets like city layouts, buildings, and vehicles to mimic real-world traffic scenarios. Researchers use this platform to experiment with different sensors and weather conditions without the risks associated with physical road testing.

### Who it's for
* Robotics and artificial intelligence researchers developing autonomous navigation systems.
* Students and engineers working on machine learning models for vehicle automation.

### Key features
* **Realistic Environments:** Access to diverse urban layouts, buildings, and traffic assets.
* **Sensor Customization:** Flexible options to simulate various camera, radar, and lidar sensor suites.
* **Environmental Control:** Ability to adjust weather, lighting, and traffic conditions for comprehensive testing.
* **Open Assets:** A library of free, high-quality digital assets for building custom simulation maps.
* **Research-Ready:** Built-in support for training and validating complex autonomous driving stacks.

### How to install (Download)
CARLA is a professional research tool that requires a manual setup process. Please visit the [Official CARLA Website](https://carla.org) to access the latest documentation and installation guides for Windows and Linux.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly realistic physics, and widely used in academic research.
* **Cons:** Requires a powerful computer with a high-end graphics card and involves a technical setup process.
