### What it does in plain English
When an app needs time to fetch data, SkeletonView fills the empty spots with shimmering placeholders. This lets users see that something is happening instead of a blank screen. The placeholders disappear automatically when the real content appears.

### Who it's for
* iPhone and iPad developers who want a quick way to add loading screens.  
* Designers who need a consistent look for waiting states.  

### Key features
* Simple three‑step setup.  
* Works with any view, tables and collections.  
* Custom colors, gradients and animations.  
* Works on both iPhone and iPad.  
* Adjustable line spacing for text placeholders.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, no subscription, works on all iOS devices, highly customizable.  
* **Cons:** requires adding a few lines of code or setting properties in Interface Builder, advanced custom animations need some Swift knowledge.
