### What it does in plain English
Codewhale acts as an intelligent programming partner right inside your computer's terminal. It reads your project files, writes code edits, and runs commands automatically to solve programming tasks. When working on larger projects, you can even split a single task across multiple coordinated agents. ### Who it's for
* Software developers looking for an open, flexible terminal assistant that works with various AI models.
* Programmers who want to automate repetitive coding chores and test running directly from the command line.

* Compatible with more than 40 AI providers, plus local models running directly on your hardware.
