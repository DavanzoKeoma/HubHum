### What it does in plain English
wger is a comprehensive digital assistant that helps you organize your exercise routines and track your daily nutrition. It allows you to log your progress with photos and measurements so you can see how your fitness journey evolves over time. Because it is open-source, you have full control over your personal health data without worrying about hidden tracking or subscriptions.

### Who it's for
*   **Fitness Enthusiasts:** People who want to log their gym progress, track weight progression, and manage complex workout routines.
*   **Health-Conscious Individuals:** Anyone looking to monitor their daily calorie intake and body measurements in a private, self-managed environment.

### Key features
*   **Custom Routines:** Build flexible workout plans with automatic rules for increasing weights over time.
*   **Nutrition Tracking:** Log your meals and calories using a large, community-maintained food database.
*   **Progress Gallery:** Upload photos to visually track your physical changes and body measurements.
*   **Exercise Library:** Access a built-in collection of exercises with instructions and tips.
*   **Private & Portable:** Use the mobile apps for on-the-go tracking or host your own private instance for complete data ownership.

### How to install (Download)
*   **Mobile Apps:** Download for [Android (Google Play)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.wger.flutter), [iOS (App Store)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wger-workout-manager/id6502226792), or via [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/de.wger.flutter/).
*   **Desktop/Linux:** Available as a flatpak on [Flathub](https://flathub.org/apps/de.wger.flutter).
*   **Self-Hosting:** For those who want to run their own server, follow the [official Docker setup guide](https://wger.readthedocs.io/en/latest/installation/docker.html).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscription fees, highly customizable, and respects your privacy.
*   **Cons:** Self-hosting requires basic knowledge of server management, and the feature-rich interface may take a little time to master.
