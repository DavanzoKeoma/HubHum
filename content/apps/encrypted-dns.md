### What it does in plain English
It gives you ready‑made files that let your iPhone or Mac look up website addresses through a secure channel.  
You simply tap or double‑click the file and the device starts using the encrypted service.  
You can switch between different providers whenever you like.

### Who it's for
* iPhone and iPad owners who want their internet look‑ups to stay private.  
* Mac users who prefer a built‑in way to protect DNS without extra software.  
* Families or anyone who wants easy filtering options (ads, adult content, malware).

### Key features
- One‑tap install on iPhone or iPad via Safari.  
- Double‑click install on macOS with a standard system prompt.  
- Choose from many providers: privacy‑focused, family‑safe, ad‑blocking, etc.  
- Switch or remove profiles at any time from the device settings.  
- Works with the native iOS/macOS network configuration, no extra apps needed.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** completely free, no subscription, uses only built‑in iOS/macOS settings, many provider options.  
* **Cons:** works only on iPhone, iPad and macOS; some apps (e.g., browsers with their own DNS settings) may bypass it; TLS‑only profiles can be blocked on restrictive networks.
