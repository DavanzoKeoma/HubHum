### What it does in plain English
MediaCMS provides a complete system for you to host, organize, and share your own video, audio, and image collections. It gives you full control over your content, ensuring your media stays private or public exactly as you choose. You can customize the look and feel of your portal to match your organization's brand or personal style.

### Who it's for
* **Educational Institutions:** Schools and universities that need a secure, ad-free space to share lectures and learning materials.
* **Content Creators & Organizations:** Individuals or groups who want to host sensitive or community-focused media without relying on third-party video sites.

### Key features
* **Full Ownership:** Host your content on your own hardware or server, keeping your data under your control.
* **Flexible Access:** Set your media to be public, private, or unlisted, and manage who can view or edit specific content.
* **Built-in Tools:** Includes automatic transcription, video trimming, and playlist creation to keep your library organized.
* **Customizable Design:** Easily change logos, fonts, and themes to make your video portal look unique.
* **Wide Compatibility:** Supports various media types including video, audio, images, and PDFs, with tools to embed content into learning systems.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, no advertisements, highly customizable, and supports professional features like HLS streaming.
* **Cons:** Requires a server to host, and setting up the initial environment is best suited for those comfortable with basic server management.
