### What it does in plain English
This tool helps you turn folders filled with tens of thousands of pictures into a smooth, zoomable visual map. It analyzes the visual content of each photo and automatically places similar pictures next to each other on a 2D screen. You can click on any image to inspect details, view descriptive labels, and navigate vast archives with incredible speed.

### Who it's for
* Photographers and artists organizing massive digital portfolios.
* Researchers and historians browsing large digital photo archives.
* Anyone who wants to visualize thousands of images in an engaging, interactive gallery.

### Key features
* Groups similar photos together using intelligent visual analysis.
* Renders tens of thousands of items smoothly using hardware-accelerated graphics.
* Supports custom descriptive labels so you can filter and click for extra details.
* Handles online image collections through standard catalog manifests.
* Works right inside standard web browsers without installing extra plugins.
