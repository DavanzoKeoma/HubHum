### What it does in plain English
ByeByeDPI is a utility for Android that runs locally on your phone or tablet to help manage how your device handles network traffic. It uses a local connection mode to process data directly on your hardware rather than routing it through a remote service. Because it operates entirely on your device, it does not encrypt your traffic or hide your identity online.

### Who it's for
* Android users who want to manage their device's network traffic locally.
* Users of Android TV or Android-based media boxes looking for a way to handle connection settings.

### Key features
* **Local Processing:** Operates entirely on your device without external servers.
* **Automatic Startup:** Can be set to launch automatically when your device turns on.
* **Custom Control:** Allows you to save and manage specific command-line settings.
* **App Filtering:** Supports choosing which specific apps should use the service.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works on phones, tablets, and Android TV devices.
