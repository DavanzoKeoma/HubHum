### What it does in plain English
Skykeep allows you to access your Apple notes, reminders, calendar, contacts, photos, and files directly on your Windows or Linux computer. It creates individual, native-feeling apps for each service so you can manage your digital life without needing a web browser. Everything stays synced with your iPhone, iPad, and Mac while working perfectly even when you are offline.

### Who it's for
* People who use an iPhone or Mac but prefer using a Windows or Linux computer for their daily work.
* Users who want a faster, more reliable way to access their personal files and notes without relying on a web browser.

### Key features
* **Native Experience:** Each service (Notes, Photos, Drive, etc.) opens in its own window, just like a standard desktop program.
* **Offline Access:** Your data is stored locally, allowing you to view and edit your information even without an internet connection.
* **Seamless Sync:** Changes made on your computer are automatically updated across all your Apple devices.
* **Privacy Focused:** The app connects directly to your account without using third-party servers or tracking your activity.
* **File Integration:** iCloud Drive and Photos appear as real folders in your computer's file manager for easy access.

* **Cons:** Requires a manual sign-in process, and because it is not an official Apple product, you may occasionally need to re-authenticate your account.
