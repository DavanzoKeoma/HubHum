### What it does in plain English
FreeChat lets you use WeChat on your computer.  
It works on macOS and Linux.  
You can chat, see contacts, and get notifications.

### Who it's for
- People who use WeChat for work or family and prefer a desktop app.  
- Users who want a stable, offline‑friendly way to stay in touch.  
- Anyone who misses the web version and wants a native experience.

### Key features
- Desktop chat interface for WeChat.  
- Mini contact list view like on phone.  
- Minimize to system tray.  
- Desktop notifications for new messages.  
- Easy to install from AppImage or .deb.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, no subscription.  
* **Cons:** only works on Linux and macOS, requires internet to login, may stop working if WeChat changes its web interface.
