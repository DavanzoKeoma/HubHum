### What it does in plain English
sysPass provides a centralized, secure space to store and organize passwords for both personal and professional use. It uses strong encryption to ensure that your sensitive information remains protected from unauthorized access. You can easily manage access for different team members, ensuring that everyone has the right level of permission to view or edit specific accounts.

### Who it's for
*   Small to medium-sized business owners who need a secure way to share login credentials among staff.
*   Individuals who want a self-hosted, private alternative to commercial password management services.

### Key features
*   **Strong Encryption:** Uses high-grade security standards to keep your stored data safe.
*   **Team Collaboration:** Allows for detailed user and group management with various access levels.
*   **Two-Factor Authentication:** Adds an extra layer of security to your login process.
*   **Organized Storage:** Keep everything tidy with tags, custom fields, and a history log of all activity.
*   **Flexible Access:** Supports integration with common directory services for easier user management.

### How to install (Download)
sysPass is a web-based application. You can find the official installation guide and setup documentation at [https://doc.syspass.org](https://doc.syspass.org). For those who prefer containerized setups, an official image is available on [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/r/nuxsmin/docker-syspass/).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Free and open-source, offers granular control over user permissions, and supports multi-user environments.
*   **Cons:** Requires a web server and database to host, which may be challenging for users without technical experience.
