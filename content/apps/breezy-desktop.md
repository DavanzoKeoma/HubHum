### What it does in plain English
Breezy Desktop lets you plug in supported smart glasses to create floating virtual screens and multi-monitor setups on your Linux computer. It bridges your desktop environment—whether you use KDE Plasma or GNOME—directly into your wearable display glasses. You can arrange multiple virtual monitors in space around you for enhanced multitasking or immersive gaming sessions.

### Who it's for
* Linux enthusiasts and professionals who want a multi-screen workstation using lightweight smart glasses instead of physical monitors.
* Steam Deck and portable Linux gamers looking to play games on a massive virtual floating screen anywhere they go.
* Remote workers who need privacy and extra screen real estate while working on the go.

### Key features
* Multi-monitor virtual workspace support for KDE Plasma and GNOME desktop environments.
* Specialized Vulkan rendering support for smooth floating screens during gaming.
* Compatibility with popular smart glasses providing motion tracking and head stabilization.
* Customizable keyboard shortcuts for toggling display modes and re-centering screens.
* Easy setup scripts for popular Linux distributions and the Steam Deck.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Enables multi-monitor setups using portable glasses, supports both productivity and gaming, compatible with major Linux desktop environments.
* **Cons:** Requires specific supported hardware glasses, requires Wayland display server for virtual screens on certain setups, advanced positioning features require a paid license after trial.
