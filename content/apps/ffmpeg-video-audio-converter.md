### What it does in plain English
FFmpeg Video & Audio Converter is a handy Windows application that lets you change video and audio formats without dealing with complicated technical commands. It brings the power of professional media processing into a clean, visual window where you can drag and drop files. You can queue up multiple files to convert all at once, check detailed file information, and even test video quality metrics with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and hobbyists who need to convert video or audio formats quickly on Windows.
* Anyone who wants the power of advanced media encoding tools without learning command-line instructions.
* Videographers and editors looking to batch process multiple files and analyze compression quality.

### Key features
* 🎬 **Visual Video Conversion:** Easily change your video files into popular multimedia formats using a straightforward desktop layout.
* 🎵 **Audio Extraction & Encoding:** Convert audio tracks seamlessly with flexible format and quality adjustments.
* 📦 **Batch Queue Management:** Line up numerous conversion jobs to run sequentially without manual intervention.
* ⚙️ **Custom Parameter Support:** Fine-tune your encoding settings with your own custom parameters when advanced control is needed.
* 📊 **Live Process Logs & Inspector:** Monitor conversion progress in real-time and inspect detailed technical properties of any file.
