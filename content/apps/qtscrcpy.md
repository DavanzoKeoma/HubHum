### What it does in plain English
QtScrcpy connects your Android phone to your computer via USB or wireless network so you can see and control it seamlessly. It lets you type with your computer keyboard, click with your mouse, and even drag and drop files or install apps instantly. Everything runs smoothly with high resolution and barely noticeable delay, without requiring administrative root permissions on your device.

### Who it's for
* Mobile gamers who want to play Android titles on a bigger screen using a keyboard and mouse.
* Professionals and developers who need to demonstrate, test, or manage multiple mobile devices from their desktop.
* Anyone looking to easily type text messages, reply to chats, or transfer files between a phone and a computer.

### Key features
* Real-time high-definition display of Android device screens with minimal delay.
* Full keyboard and mouse control, including custom button mapping for games and apps.
* Wireless or wired connection options without installing extra apps on the mobile device.
* Drag-and-drop file transfers and direct software installation.
* Handy shortcuts for taking screenshots, recording videos, and synchronizing clipboards.
