### What it does in plain English
Lago is an open-source tool that helps businesses track how much their customers use a product and charge them accordingly. It connects user activity—like API calls, data storage, or time spent—directly to automated invoices and secure payment collection. Companies can easily adjust their pricing rules or launch custom subscription tiers without rewriting their core software code.

### Who it's for
* Software developers building modern cloud applications with usage-based charges.
* Finance and operations teams looking to automate customer invoicing and payment tracking.
* AI product creators who need precise metering for model tokens, compute time, and requests.

### Key features
* Real-time consumption tracking and event deduplication for accurate billing.
* Flexible support for subscription fees, prepaid credits, tiered volumes, and flat overages.
* Headless system design that integrates smoothly with popular external payment processors.
* Comprehensive dashboard and analytics tools to monitor revenue growth and customer activity.
* Developer-friendly software development kits and automated commands for quick integration.
