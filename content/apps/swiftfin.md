### What it does in plain English
Swiftfin is a sleek and modern app designed to stream your personal video collection directly to your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV. It connects straight to your home media server so you can enjoy your movies, shows, and home videos anywhere. The app provides a smooth viewing experience with native controls built specifically for Apple devices.

### Who it's for
* Home media enthusiasts who want a clean, dedicated app to watch their self-hosted video libraries on Apple devices.
* Apple TV owners looking for a fast and reliable way to stream movies and TV shows from their home server.
* People who prefer a native, distraction-free interface without ads or complicated setup procedures.

### Key features
* Connects directly to your home media server for instant streaming.
* Built specifically for iOS and tvOS with native look and feel.
* Smooth video playback supporting a wide range of media formats.
* Clean library organization for movies, television series, and episodes.
* Active community development with regular updates and beta testing options.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, no subscription fees, native performance on Apple hardware.
* **Cons:** Requires a separate home media server setup to function.
