### What it does in plain English
MiroTalk SFU is a private video conferencing system that you host on your own server, ensuring your conversations remain strictly under your control. It provides a high-quality alternative to mainstream meeting services, offering features like screen sharing, file transfers, and interactive whiteboards. Because you own the platform, there are no time limits, subscription fees, or third-party data tracking.

### Who it's for
* **Privacy-conscious organizations** that need to keep internal communications secure and off public cloud servers.
* **Small teams and communities** looking for a reliable, unlimited meeting space without monthly subscription costs.

### Key features
* **High-Definition Video:** Supports crystal-clear video streaming up to 8K resolution at 60 frames per second.
* **Interactive Tools:** Includes a collaborative whiteboard, rich text editing, and file sharing for productive meetings.
* **Smart Enhancements:** Features built-in video effects, background blurring, and AI-powered speech recognition.
* **Total Privacy:** Keeps your data on your own infrastructure with no third-party data processing or hidden tracking.
* **Flexible Customization:** Allows for full branding and integration into your existing websites or applications.

### How to install (Download)
MiroTalk SFU is a web-based platform designed to be hosted on your own server. You can view the live demo or follow the setup instructions below:
* [Try the Live Demo](https://sfu.mirotalk.com)
* [Official Self-Hosting Documentation](https://docs.mirotalk.com/mirotalk-sfu/self-hosting/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no time limits on meetings, full data ownership, and highly scalable.
* **Cons:** Requires basic technical knowledge to set up and maintain your own server, or a hosting provider to manage it for you.
