### What it does in plain English
Reactive Resume helps you design professional resumes and cover letters while keeping complete ownership of your personal data. You can watch your document update live on screen as you type, choose from multiple clean design templates, and check your work against job descriptions. When you are done, export your clean PDF or share it instantly with a secure public link.

### Who it's for
* Job seekers wanting a professional, modern resume without paying recurring subscription fees.
* Privacy-conscious professionals who want to host their own career documents without corporate tracking.
* Students and career changers looking for clear templates and helpful feedback tools to land their next role.

### Key features
* Live page preview that shows your finished resume layout instantly as you write.
* Multiple templates with customizable fonts, colors, and layout sections to match your personal style.
* Built-in job application tracker to manage interviews, notes, and stages from saved to offer.
* Readability checks and keyword matching to help your resume pass automated hiring software.

### How to install (Download)
You can jump right in and use the cloud version directly in your browser:
- [Open Reactive Resume in the Cloud](https://rxresu.me)

If you prefer to run the application on your own server or private computer, you can set it up easily using Docker.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free forever with no hidden paywalls, zero ads, and strict privacy controls.
* **Cons:** Advanced features like optional AI assistance require connecting your own external provider keys.
