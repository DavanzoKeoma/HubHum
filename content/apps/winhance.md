### What it does in plain English
Winhance helps you take control of your Windows experience by removing unwanted built-in programs and streamlining system settings. It lets you tweak performance, adjust privacy preferences, and customize the look of your taskbar and menus all in one place. You can also save your favorite configurations to quickly apply them again on future setups or new computers.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users looking to speed up a sluggish PC and reduce unwanted background noise.
* Power users and IT technicians who want a reliable tool to configure multiple Windows systems efficiently.
* Anyone who prefers a clean, organized operating system without pre-installed apps they never use.

### Key features
* **App Management:** Easily view and remove pre-installed Windows apps or install popular third-party software using package management.
* **Performance Boosts:** Adjust privacy settings, gaming boosts, power plans, and system updates through simple toggles.
* **Visual Customization:** Switch between dark and light themes, and personalize your taskbar and start menu layout.
* **Advanced ISO Tools:** Create custom Windows installation media with integrated drivers and automated setup scripts.
* **Configuration Profiles:** Export your personal optimization preferences to a file for fast setup on future machines.
