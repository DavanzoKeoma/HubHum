### What it does in plain English
Free The Web Apps lets you strip away the standard browser window frame, tabs, and navigation bars from any website so it runs like a dedicated desktop program. You can easily launch your favorite online tools directly from your computer's application menu just like regular software. It works by configuring modern browsers to open specific sites in clean, standalone mode while keeping all your existing cookies and saved data intact.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers who want to keep chat tools, document editors, and dashboards cleanly separated in their app launcher.
* Power users who want a distraction-free workspace without cluttered browser tabs.
* Linux and desktop enthusiasts looking to organize web tools right alongside native software.

### Key features
* **Frameless Display:** Runs websites without browser tabs, address bars, or extra buttons for a clean native look.
* **App Launcher Access:** Integrates smoothly with your computer search menu, Spotlight, or launcher so tools are always a keystroke away.
* **Keeps Your Data:** Retains your browser cookies, login sessions, and settings automatically.
* **Curated App Store:** Discover dozens of web services tested to work seamlessly in standalone mode.

### How to install (Download)
Visit the [Free The Web Apps Official Website](https://ftwa.mathix.dev) to browse the catalog, configure your preferences, and generate an easy installation script for your computer. You can also explore the [Free The Web Apps Store](https://ftwa.mathix.dev/store) to find pre-configured web tools.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, keeps login sessions intact, reduces browser tab clutter.
* **Cons:** Requires a compatible browser already installed on your system.
