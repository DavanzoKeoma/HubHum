### What it does in plain English
LinkStack lets you create a simple personal website that lists all your social media profiles, blogs, and other important links in one spot. Instead of having to switch between different apps or websites, visitors can click through to everything you share from a single, clean page. This specific version is designed to run on your own hardware, giving you full control over your data and appearance without relying on third-party services.

### Who it's for
*   **Content Creators:** Individuals who want a central hub to share their YouTube, Instagram, Twitter, and other profiles without paying for a subscription.
*   **Privacy Advocates:** Users who prefer to host their own digital presence on a personal server or home computer to avoid tracking and data collection by large platforms.
*   **Tech Enthusiasts:** People comfortable using Docker who want to experiment with self-hosting a modern, customizable link-sharing tool.

### Key features
*   **Unlimited Links:** Add as many clickable buttons or links as you need to your profile page.
*   **Customizable Design:** Change colors, fonts, and layouts to match your personal brand or aesthetic.
*   **Docker Ready:** Comes pre-packaged in a lightweight container that is easy to start, stop, and update.
*   **Secure Hosting:** Supports HTTPS out of the box, ensuring your connection is encrypted and secure.
*   **One-Click Updates:** Includes a built-in updater in the admin panel to keep your software current with minimal effort.

### How to install (Download)
This software is distributed as a Docker image. You will need Docker installed on your computer or server to run it.

*   [View Official Docker Setup Guide](https://linkstack.org/docker)
*   [View Source Code on GitHub](https://github.com/LinkStackOrg/linkstack-docker)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Free to use, no monthly subscription fees, full ownership of your data, and highly customizable interface.
*   **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up Docker and manage a server, and you are responsible for your own security and backups.
