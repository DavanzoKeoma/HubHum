### What it does in plain English
DesktopNaotu is a dedicated desktop application that lets you design, edit, and review mind maps entirely offline. It provides a smooth local environment for capturing ideas, structuring thoughts, and saving map files directly to your hard drive. Because it runs locally, you can brainstorm on the go without worrying about cloud uptime or internet access.

### Who it's for
* Students organizing study notes and project research offline.
* Writers and planners outlining book chapters, articles, or project timelines.
* Professionals structuring meeting agendas and brainstorming sessions privately.

### Key features
* Works completely offline without requiring an active internet connection.
* Supports standard map file operations, including drag-and-drop file opening.
* Automatically saves your progress so you never lose valuable ideas.
* Cross-platform support across major operating systems including Windows, macOS, and Linux.
* Lightweight interface focused on fast, distraction-free brainstorming.
