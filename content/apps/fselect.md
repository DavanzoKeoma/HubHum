### What it does in plain English
It lets you ask your computer to show files that match a description you write in everyday language.  
You can look for pictures, music, documents, or any other file by name, size, date, or hidden details.  

### Who it's for
* Anyone who regularly organizes photos, music, or documents and wants to find a missing file fast.  
* People who clean up old or duplicate files on their computer.  
* Users who prefer typing a short question instead of scrolling through folders.

### Key features
- Ask for files by simple patterns like `*.jpg` or `report-2022.pdf`.  
- Search by size, date, or type such as images, audio, or archives.  
- Look inside zip archives without opening them first.  
- Combine several conditions to narrow results (for example, “photos larger than 2 MB created last month”).  
- Get results as plain text, CSV or JSON for easy copying.

* **Pros:** works on many operating systems, supports many file attributes, free and open‑source.  
* **Cons:** runs from a terminal window, so it needs a little practice to write the queries.
