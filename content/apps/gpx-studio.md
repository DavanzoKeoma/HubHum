### What it does in plain English
gpx.studio allows you to create new GPS routes or edit existing ones directly in your web browser. You can merge multiple files, trim unwanted sections, or adjust your path to follow specific roads and trails. It provides a visual interface to manage your outdoor adventures without needing complex software.

### Who it's for
* Cyclists, hikers, and runners who want to plan their own custom routes.
* Outdoor enthusiasts who need to combine or clean up GPS data from their fitness trackers.

### Key features
* **Interactive Map Editing:** Draw paths manually or snap them to existing roads and trails.
* **File Management:** Easily merge, split, or trim multiple GPS files in one place.
* **Visual Data:** View elevation profiles and statistics for your routes instantly.
* **Privacy-Focused:** The tool runs directly in your browser, keeping your data local to your device.
* **Versatile Export:** Save your finished routes in the standard format used by most navigation devices.

### How to install (Download)
This is a web-based application. You can use it immediately without any installation by visiting the official website: [Open gpx.studio](https://gpx.studio/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no account required, works directly in any modern web browser.
* **Cons:** Requires an internet connection to load map tiles, and advanced routing features depend on the availability of map data.
