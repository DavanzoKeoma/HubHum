### What it does in plain English
Popcorn Time acts as a central hub for your entertainment, bringing your favorite movies and shows together in one organized space. It is built by the community to provide a clean, modern way to manage and view your media collection. Unlike commercial directories that only point you to other services, this application focuses on providing a unified experience for your own content.

### Who it's for
* Media enthusiasts who want a single, organized dashboard for their digital library.
* Users who prefer open-source software built by the community rather than corporate-controlled platforms.

### Key features
* **Unified Interface:** View your movies and shows in one clean, modern layout.
* **Community-Driven:** Developed by volunteers with a focus on transparency and user needs.
* **Cross-Platform:** Designed to work smoothly across desktop, mobile, and television screens.
* **Local Playback:** Supports playing your own media files directly within the application.
* **Open Data:** Provides weekly database updates for researchers and developers to explore.
