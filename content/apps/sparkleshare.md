### What it does in plain English
SparkleShare creates a dedicated folder on your computer that automatically stays in sync with your other devices. It works by connecting to your own private storage space, giving you full control over where your data lives. Whenever you add or change a file in that folder, the update is instantly shared with your other connected computers.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious users who want to manage their own data storage without relying on third-party cloud companies.
* People who need a reliable way to keep files synchronized across multiple computers, including Linux, macOS, and Windows systems.

### Key features
* **Self-Hosted Control:** You decide exactly where your files are stored, ensuring maximum privacy and security.
* **Automatic Syncing:** Files are updated in real-time across all your linked devices as soon as changes are made.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Works seamlessly across Linux, macOS, and Windows environments.
* **Open Standards:** Built on proven technology that allows for high-speed transfers and efficient file management.

### How to install (Download)
* **Official Website:** Visit [SparkleShare.org](https://sparkleshare.org/) to get started.
* **Linux:** We recommend using the Flatpak version for the latest updates via [Flathub](https://flathub.org/apps/org.sparkleshare.SparkleShare).
* **macOS:** Download the latest installer directly from the [official releases page](https://github.com/hbons/SparkleShare/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, free to use, and works on your own terms.
* **Cons:** Requires setting up a host (like a personal server or a private storage space) to function, which may be slightly more involved than simple "plug-and-play" services.
