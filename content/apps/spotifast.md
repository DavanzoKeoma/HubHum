### What it does in plain English
Spotifast provides a snappy, native desktop home for your music library, allowing you to browse playlists, search tracks, and control playback directly from your computer. It bypasses heavy browser engines to deliver responsive performance, local audio file playback, and hardware visualizers.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want a lightweight desktop client that launches instantly and uses fewer system resources.
* Audiophiles who enjoy retro visualizers, customizable skins, and local music file playback alongside their streaming library.
* Power users looking for a clean, distraction-free interface with robust keyboard shortcuts and global media key support.

### Key features
* **Native Desktop Experience:** Built for speed without running a resource-heavy web browser software foundation in the background.
* **Winamp Mini Player & MilkDrop:** Enjoy classic visual skins, animated sound displays, and reactive full-screen music visuals.
* **Local Files Support:** Play your own locally stored audio files seamlessly alongside your online music catalog.
* **Spotify Connect Integration:** Easily control playback across different devices, speakers, and smart home audio groups.
