### What it does in plain English
CompanyOS gives your team a fleet of intelligent digital workers that run 24 hours a day on secure cloud computers. You talk to a main assistant that instantly hands off research, writing, and coding tasks to specialized workers running quietly in the background.

### Who it's for
* Startup founders and team leaders who want to automate repetitive workflows without complex technical setups.
* Growing businesses looking to coordinate tasks across hundreds of apps from one shared dashboard.
* Developers and creators who want self-hosted, private tools to manage projects securely.

### Key features
* **Always-On Digital Workers:** Autonomous helpers run in parallel on isolated cloud computers and wake up automatically when new events occur.
* **Shared Team Memory:** Everyone on your team works with the exact same context, stored securely in files you fully own.
* **Thousands of App Connections:** Link your favorite communication tools, file storage systems, and daily software effortlessly.
* **Smart Review System:** Every major change proposed by a digital worker goes through a quick human approval step before publishing.
