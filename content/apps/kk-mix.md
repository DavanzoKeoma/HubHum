### What it does in plain English
kk-mix is a desktop tool for Windows that lets you process entire folders of video clips at once without wrestling with complex timelines. You simply select your source folders, pick from 15 built-in batch tasks like merging, cropping, or adding titles, and let the software generate finished videos automatically. Everything runs locally on your computer with a friendly interface and clear progress tracking.

### Who it's for
* Content creators managing multi-account short video publishing workflows
* E-commerce sellers preparing standardized product demonstration clips at scale
* Podcasters and educators slicing long recordings into bite-sized segments

### Key features
* **Folder-based batching:** Reads and writes entire folders of video files in name order for effortless organization.
* **15 video operations:** Handles transitions, narration syncing, side-by-side splits, picture-in-picture, speed changes, and aspect ratio cropping.
* **Brand customization:** Easily stamp videos with text titles, image watermarks, background music, or volume adjustments.
* **Delivery optimization:** Compress file sizes, convert between popular video containers, or extract still frames for thumbnails.
* **Control and transparency:** Pause or stop processing mid-batch while viewing a live activity log for every file.
