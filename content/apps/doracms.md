### What it does in plain English
DoraCMS provides a complete environment for creating and maintaining professional websites. It includes a user-friendly dashboard to manage your articles, pages, and media files without needing to write complex code. The system is designed to be fast and reliable, making it a great choice for businesses or individuals who want full control over their online presence.

### Who it's for
*   **Small business owners** who need a reliable platform to manage their company website and blog.
*   **Content creators** looking for a clean, organized space to publish articles and manage digital media.

### Key features
*   **Intuitive Dashboard:** A clean interface for managing all your website content and user settings.
*   **Flexible Database Support:** Works with different database types to suit your hosting needs.
*   **Modern Design Tools:** Built with current web standards to ensure your site looks great on all devices.
*   **AI-Ready:** Includes features to assist with content creation and management.
*   **Customizable:** Easily extendable to fit specific project requirements.

### How to install (Download)
