### What it does in plain English
AudioMuse-AI scans your personal music library to understand the unique sonic signature of every song you own. It uses this information to build custom playlists, group similar tracks together, and even help you find songs by humming or recording a short clip. Because it runs on your own hardware, your music data stays private and does not rely on external services or metadata.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who have large, disorganized digital libraries and want to rediscover hidden gems.
* People who use self-hosted media servers like Jellyfin, Plex, or Navidrome and want smarter, AI-driven listening experiences.

### Key features
* **Smart Clustering:** Automatically groups songs that sound similar, regardless of genre or tags.
* **Visual Music Map:** Explore your entire collection through an interactive 2D map of your music's sonic landscape.
* **Search by Sound:** Find songs by recording a short clip or searching for specific moods, instruments, or themes.
* **Cross-Server Integration:** Connect multiple music servers to one dashboard and manage your entire library in one place.
* **Private and Local:** All analysis happens on your own machine, ensuring your listening habits remain completely private.
