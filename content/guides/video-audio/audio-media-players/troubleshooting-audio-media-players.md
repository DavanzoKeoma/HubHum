## Fixing Common Problems with Free Audio Editors & Music Players

Moving away from expensive commercial software occasionally brings small setup questions. Here are the most common hurdles everyday users face with **Audio Editors & Music Players** and how to avoid them:

### 1. Opening a Command-Line Tool Expecting Visual Buttons
Some top tools (marked **CLI Tool**) do not open a traditional window—double-clicking their executable flashes a terminal and closes. Check the format badge before downloading: choose a **Desktop App** if you prefer visual buttons, or open a terminal prompt when running CLI tools.

### 2. Choosing Between Self-Hosted Server Tools and Instant Web Apps
Tools marked **Self-Hosted Web App** require Docker or local server setup on your machine or a private host rather than working like a public SaaS website.

### 3. Picking the Right Installer for Your Device
Windows users usually look for `.exe` or `.msi` installers, macOS users look for `.dmg`, and Linux/Android users have their own dedicated packages when listed in Quick Specs.

### Recommended Alternatives Covered

- **[Navidrome](/HubHum/apps/navidrome/)** (Self-Hosted Web App) — Turn your computer into a personal streaming server to enjoy your entire music collection from any browser or mobile device.
- **[spotify-tui](/HubHum/apps/spotify-tui/)** (CLI Tool) — Manage your music player with a lightning-fast interface that runs entirely inside your text terminal.
- **[Audacity](/HubHum/apps/audacity/)** (Desktop App) — Record your voice, remove background noise, mix tracks, and export podcasts or music free on Windows, Mac, and Linux.
- **[Spotifast](/HubHum/apps/spotifast/)** (Desktop App) — Spotifast provides a snappy, native desktop home for your music library, allowing you to browse playlists, search tracks, and control playback directly from your computer.
- **[FineTune](/HubHum/apps/finetune/)** (Desktop App) — Take independent volume control of every app on your Mac, boost quiet sounds, and route audio effortlessly from your menu bar.
