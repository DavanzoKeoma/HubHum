### What it does in plain English
Nexe takes your software projects and packs them into one single file that you can easily share or move around. This means you don't have to worry about installing complex extra software on the computers where you want your program to run. It simplifies the process of sharing your work by making your applications self-contained and ready to use immediately.

### Who it's for
*   Software creators who want to share their tools with others without requiring them to install specific environments.
*   People who need to distribute their programs across different computers and want to ensure they run consistently every time.

### Key features
*   **Single-file output:** Combines your entire project into one portable executable.
*   **No extra dependencies:** Your program runs independently without needing the original software environment installed on the target machine.
*   **Cross-platform support:** Build your programs to work across different operating systems.
*   **Fast cloud setup:** Start and run your applications instantly without complex setup steps.
*   **Resource bundling:** Easily include extra files like images or data within your single executable.

### How to install (Download)
Nexe is primarily installed via the command line. Ensure you have the necessary environment tools installed, then run the following command in your terminal:

`npm i nexe -g`

For specific pre-built binaries (advanced users), you can view the [official releases page](https://github.com/nexe/nexe/releases/tag/v3.3.3).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source; creates clean, portable files; reduces setup time for end-users.
*   **Cons:** Requires some initial setup of build tools; advanced customization options may have a learning curve for beginners.
