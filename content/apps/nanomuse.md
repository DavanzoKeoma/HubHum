### What it does in plain English
nanoMuse is an open-source personal digital assistant that connects your phone, computer, and tablet into a single, unified helper. It remembers your preferences, runs background tasks even when you close the app, and pauses to ask for your permission before taking any irreversible steps. You can interact with it across different operating systems and chat apps to manage daily chores effortlessly.

### Who it's for
* Busy professionals looking to automate everyday computer and phone tasks without relying on expensive commercial services.
* Privacy-conscious users who want an intelligent helper running on their own devices or private servers.
* Technology enthusiasts who enjoy customizing digital assistants with open-source software and personal models.

### Key features
* **Cross-device synchronization:** Share conversations and tasks seamlessly between your phone, tablet, and desktop computer.
* **Smart safety checks:** Always asks for your approval before performing major actions like deleting files, sending messages, or making purchases.
* **Continuous background operation:** Keeps running routines, scheduled goals, and morning updates even when the application window is closed.
* **Customizable appearance and memory:** Personalize your assistant's look and store your preferences in readable files.
* **Flexible model support:** Use free community relay allowances or connect your own preferred language model provider.
