### What it does in plain English
Locally Uncensored brings powerful artificial intelligence tools directly to your desktop, allowing you to chat, create images, and write code entirely on your own machine. Because everything runs locally, your data stays private and you do not need to worry about subscriptions or cloud-based restrictions. The app handles all the technical heavy lifting, so you can start using advanced AI models immediately after a simple installation.

### Who it's for
*   **Creative professionals and hobbyists** who want to generate images or videos without content filters or monthly fees.
*   **Developers and students** who need a local coding assistant to help manage projects and review code changes privately.

### Key features
*   **All-in-one interface:** Manage chat, image generation, video creation, and coding tasks in a single, unified window.
*   **Total privacy:** All processing happens on your own hardware, meaning your conversations and files never leave your computer.
*   **No technical hurdles:** The app automatically detects and sets up the necessary components, avoiding the need for complex command-line tools.
*   **Flexible model support:** Easily download and switch between various AI models based on your computer's performance capabilities.
*   **Cross-platform:** Works seamlessly on Windows, Linux, and macOS, with an additional companion app available for Android.
