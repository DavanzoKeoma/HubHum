### What it does in plain English
Dawarich is a personal web application that records and displays where you travel over time. It lets you import your past location data from mainstream services or companion mobile apps to view everything on an interactive map. You can organize your journeys into trips, calculate statistics like distance traveled, and even share updates with family members.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious travelers who want complete ownership of their personal location records instead of storing them on third-party cloud services.
* Photography enthusiasts looking to match their photo libraries with geographic coordinates to see pictures directly on a travel map.
* Families who want a shared way to keep track of each other's whereabouts without relying on corporate tech giants.

### Key features
* Interactive map views featuring custom layers like heatmaps, points, and travel routes.
* Comprehensive travel insights and statistics showing visited countries, cities, and total distance.
* Seamless integration with popular photo organization tools to display pictures from your trips.
* Flexible data import options supporting past archives from major mapping platforms and standard GPS files.
* Optional family sharing features that let trusted members view each other's locations with individual consent controls.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, complete data privacy, supports imports from multiple tracking apps and file formats.
* **Cons:** Requires a server or always-on computer to host, setting up reverse proxy and tracking apps takes some configuration time.
