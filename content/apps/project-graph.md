### What it does in plain English
Project Graph provides an infinite digital canvas where you can map out your thoughts, notes, and project plans exactly how you see them. Instead of being forced into a list or a rigid folder system, you can freely connect ideas, group related items, and visualize complex relationships. It is designed to help you move from scattered, messy thoughts to a clear, organized structure at your own pace.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to organize large amounts of information and study notes.
* Project managers or creative professionals who need to map out workflows, dependencies, and brainstorming sessions.

### Key features
* **Infinite Canvas:** Move, zoom, and arrange your content anywhere without being restricted by page boundaries.
* **Flexible Connections:** Link any two pieces of information to show how they relate to each other.
* **Multimedia Support:** Easily drag and drop text, images, and other media directly into your workspace.
* **Customizable Layouts:** Use automatic tools to organize your nodes or manually place them to suit your personal thinking style.
* **Keyboard-Driven:** Quickly grow your idea trees and navigate your workspace using simple keyboard shortcuts.
