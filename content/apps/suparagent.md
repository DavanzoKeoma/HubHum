### What it does in plain English
Suparagent acts as a digital office where you can create and manage multiple AI "coworkers" to handle specific jobs. You assign each agent a role and a workspace, allowing them to collaborate on tasks while you maintain full control over their actions. Every significant move an agent makes requires your explicit approval, ensuring you stay in the loop on all project progress.

### Who it's for
*   **Project Managers:** People who need to coordinate multiple automated tasks and want a central hub to review progress and delegate work.
*   **Power Users:** Individuals who want to build custom AI workflows that remember past conversations and perform recurring duties without manual oversight.

### Key features
*   **Custom AI Coworkers:** Create agents with specific roles and instructions to handle different parts of your workflow.
*   **Human-in-the-loop Approval:** Set agents to "Ask first" mode so you can review and approve their actions before they are finalized.
*   **Shared Memory:** Agents can save notes, search through past conversations, and recall project context to stay consistent over time.
*   **Automated Routines:** Schedule recurring tasks or trigger workflows based on specific events to save time on repetitive work.
*   **Flexible Setup:** Use the desktop app for a local experience or host your own server to keep your data and agents running independently.

### How to install (Download)
*   **Web Version:** Access the service directly at [suparagent.ai](https://suparagent.ai).
*   **Desktop App:** Download the latest experimental installers from the [official GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/Anil-matcha/suparagent/releases).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Offers complete control over agent permissions, supports local data storage, and provides a collaborative environment for AI agents.
*   **Cons:** The software is in early development, installers are currently unsigned, and it requires you to provide your own AI service accounts or keys.
