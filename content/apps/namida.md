### What it does in plain English
Namida is an all-in-one entertainment application designed to play both your personal music collection and videos effortlessly. It connects to online streaming platforms so you can enjoy your favorite content, read synchronized lyrics, and organize your files neatly. With built-in customization options and advanced sound adjustments, it transforms how you experience audio and video on your devices.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want precise control over sound equalizer presets, audio quality, and local file organization.
* Everyday listeners looking for an ad-free, modern interface to enjoy local tracks alongside streaming content.
* Users who love customizing their media playback experience with themes, animated backgrounds, and synchronized lyrics.

### Key features
* Powerful library indexer that organizes tracks, albums, artists, genres, and folders while preventing duplicates.
* Advanced audio settings featuring an equalizer, gapless playback, crossfade, and speed control.
* Seamless online streaming integration with video playback, subtitle support, and offline caching.
* Dynamic themes that adapt their color palette to match your current album artwork.
* Cross-device synchronization and persistent queues to keep your listening sessions right where you left them.
