### What it does in plain English
Aerion is a standalone, lightweight email application that helps you organize multiple inbox accounts cleanly and efficiently. It focuses on low resource consumption so your computer stays fast while you check, read, and write messages. Enjoy modern features like conversation threads, dark mode, and reliable search without unnecessary background bloat.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious users looking for an independent email app that avoids heavy system tracking.
* People managing multiple work and personal email addresses who want a single unified inbox.
* Anyone tired of sluggish, resource-heavy desktop mail tools that drain battery life.

### Key features
* **Unified Inbox:** View and color-code all your separate email accounts in one convenient screen.
* **Low Resource Usage:** Built to consume minimal battery, memory, and processor power.
* **Privacy Focused:** Includes basic tracking element removal and encryption support.
* **Keyboard Friendly:** Navigate your messages quickly using intuitive keyboard shortcuts.
* **Broad Provider Support:** Connect easily with Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Proton Mail, and standard mail servers.
