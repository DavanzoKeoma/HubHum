### What it does in plain English
SurfSense is a desktop application that acts as your private research and editing assistant. It safely indexes your files directly onto your computer without sending your data to external servers. You can ask questions, compare text, or transform files like PDFs, spreadsheets, and presentations entirely offline using local or remote artificial intelligence models.

### Who it's for
* **Researchers and Students:** People who need to organize, search, and extract insights from large volumes of reference documents securely.
* **Legal and Administrative Professionals:** Anyone handling sensitive contracts and reports who requires strict data privacy without cloud uploads.
* **Content Creators:** Writers and organizers who want to turn web pages, notes, and media files into structured formats or clean summaries.

### Key features
* **Complete Data Privacy:** Runs locally on your device with zero data collection or mandatory online accounts.
* **Multi-Format Support:** Works with PDFs, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, presentations, audio recordings, books, and images.
* **Document Editing and Conversion:** Automatically generates tracked changes, merges files, stamps pages, and converts document formats safely.
* **Flexible AI Models:** Connects with local intelligence models or standard external APIs depending on your preferences.
* **Built-in Plugins:** Integrates optional tools to pull data from public web searches, maps, and online reference platforms.
