### What it does in plain English
Ludusavi is a helpful utility designed to keep your video game progress safe from accidental loss or system crashes. It scans your computer to find save files for thousands of different games across major gaming platforms. With just a click, it bundles everything into a reliable backup so you can restore your progress whenever you need.

### Who it's for
* PC gamers who play across multiple game stores and want a single, centralized way to protect their save data.
* Handheld gaming device users who want to easily move their progress between portable systems and desktop setups.

### Key features
* Supports backups for over 19,000 games from popular platforms like Steam, Epic, GOG, and more.
* Lightning-fast scanning and backup generation compared to traditional tools.
* Includes both a user-friendly visual interface and an advanced command-line tool for scripting.
* Fully compatible with the Steam Deck and portable storage setups.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, download the portable program for your operating system directly from the [Official Releases Page](https://github.com/mtkennerly/ludusavi/releases). Because it is completely portable, you do not need a complicated installation wizard—simply place the downloaded file anywhere on your system and run it.
