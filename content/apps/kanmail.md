### What it does in plain English
Kanmail turns a standard email inbox into an interactive kanban board, letting you sort, move, and track messages just like project tasks. Instead of endless scrolling through an unorganized list, you view emails inside visual columns and folders. It brings project management clarity directly to your daily communication workflow.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and project managers who want to treat emails like actionable to-do items.
* Busy professionals looking to reduce inbox overwhelm with a visual tracking layout.
* Anyone who prefers columns and status boards over traditional chronological mailboxes.

### Key features
* Organizes emails into visual kanban board columns and custom folders.
* Cross-platform desktop support for Mac, Windows, and Linux computers.
* Helps convert incoming messages into trackable action items.
* Clean visual interface with dedicated light and dark display modes.
