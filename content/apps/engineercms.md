### What it does in plain English
EngineerCMS provides a centralized online workspace for civil engineers, designers, and project managers to store, track, and share technical documentation. It lets team members collaborate on text documents simultaneously, view project maps, and maintain clear audit trails for construction schedules and blueprints. Everything runs efficiently from a lightweight setup, keeping project files organized and accessible across teams.

### Who it's for
* Civil engineers and design consultants managing complex project paperwork
* Construction supervisors and inspectors needing on-site mobile access to plans
* Project administrators looking to organize shared archives and document workflows

### Key features
* Real-time collaborative document editing for office files and reports
* Infinite hierarchical folder structures with granular permission controls
* Integrated mapping tools for viewing on-site building locations and coordinates
* Companion mobile access via a WeChat mini-program for logs and updates
* Project timeline tracking, milestone logging, and progress statistics
