### What it does in plain English
tgpt lets you talk to AI assistants right inside the black text window on your computer screen. You can type questions or requests just like you would in a normal chat app, but without needing to open a web browser. It works on Windows, Mac, and Linux, giving you quick access to AI help wherever you are working.

### Who it's for
*   **Students and researchers** who need quick AI assistance while working in code editors or text-based environments.
*   **Developers** who prefer staying in the command line and want to ask AI for help with coding or documentation without switching windows.
*   **Tech-savvy users** who enjoy using command-line tools and want a lightweight, free way to interact with AI models.

### Key features
*   **Multiple AI Providers:** Supports various AI services, so you can choose the model that works best for your needs.
*   **Cross-Platform Support:** Runs smoothly on Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
*   **Simple Setup:** Easy to install using standard package managers or direct download links.
*   **Proxy Support:** Allows you to route your connection through a proxy for privacy or network flexibility.
*   **Lightweight Design:** No heavy interface or browser required, keeping your system fast and responsive.
