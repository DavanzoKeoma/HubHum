### What it does in plain English
This project breathes new life into a beloved turn-based fantasy strategy game by modernizing its core engine. It allows players to run the classic game smoothly on current operating systems, including Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android devices. It also supports custom user-made modifications, new maps, and extended screen resolutions for a better gameplay experience.

### Who it's for
* Longtime fans of classic turn-based fantasy strategy games who want to play on modern hardware.
* Gamers looking to enjoy community-created campaigns, towns, and expansions.
* Players who want to experience legendary strategy gameplay on their smartphones or tablets.

### Key features
* Fully open-source recreation of a classic turn-based strategy game engine.
* Cross-platform support for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android.
* Support for high resolutions and modern screen sizes.
* Built-in modding tools and support for custom user-created content.
* Active community updates and regular feature additions.
