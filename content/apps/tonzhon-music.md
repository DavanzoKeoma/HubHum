### What it does in plain English
Tonzhon provides a focused, clutter-free environment for searching and listening to music. It removes the social media feeds, live streams, and advertisements found on mainstream platforms to ensure you stay immersed in your audio. You can easily organize your library, create custom playlists, and follow along with scrolling lyrics as you listen.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want a simple, ad-free interface for daily listening.
* People who find modern music platforms too overwhelming or distracting.

### Key features
* **Ad-free experience:** No social media, live streaming, or intrusive advertisements.
* **Smart organization:** Easily create and manage your own personal music playlists.
* **Lyrics support:** View scrolling lyrics and download them for your favorite tracks.
* **Minimalist design:** A clean, intuitive interface that keeps the focus entirely on the music.
* **Local storage:** Your listening lists are saved directly in your browser for quick access.

### How to install (Download)
Tonzhon is a web-based application. You can access it directly in your browser without needing to install any software:
[Access Tonzhon Music Web App](https://tonzhon.whamon.com)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, zero advertisements, and very lightweight.
* **Cons:** Requires an active internet connection to stream music, and it is a web-only tool.
