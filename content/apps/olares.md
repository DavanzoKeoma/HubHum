### What it does in plain English
Olares acts as a private command center for your digital life, allowing you to run powerful AI tools directly on your own hardware. It organizes your files, photos, and documents while providing a secure space to host your own automated assistants. By keeping everything on your own devices, you maintain full control over your data without relying on external companies.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious individuals** who want to use AI tools without sharing their personal data with large tech corporations.
*   **Small teams or families** looking for a unified, self-hosted space to share files, manage projects, and run automation tools.

### Key features
*   **Local AI Control:** Run your own AI assistants and models locally, ensuring your conversations and data never leave your home or office.
*   **Unified Dashboard:** Manage your files, storage, and installed applications through a simple, browser-based interface.
*   **Flexible Storage:** Easily connect and manage local drives or external network storage in one central location.
*   **Secure Access:** Access your private cloud from any device, anywhere, with built-in tools to keep your connection safe.
*   **One-Click Apps:** Quickly install and update a variety of tools and services from the built-in marketplace.

### How to install (Download)
*   **For Linux (Ubuntu/Debian):** Run the following command in your terminal: `curl -fsSL https://olares.sh | bash -`
*   **Manual Installers:** You can download the official setup scripts directly from the project release page:
    *   [Download Linux Installer (install.sh)](https://github.com/beclab/Olares/releases/download/1.12.8/install.sh)
    *   [Download Windows/PowerShell Installer (install.ps1)](https://github.com/beclab/Olares/releases/download/1.12.8/install.ps1)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Keeps your data private on your own hardware, free to use, and offers a professional-grade interface for managing complex tasks.
*   **Cons:** Requires dedicated hardware (at least 8GB RAM and 150GB SSD) to run smoothly, and setting up a personal server requires basic technical comfort.
