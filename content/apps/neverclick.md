### What it does in plain English
Neverclick is a Windows application that frees you from needing a physical mouse for everyday tasks. It scans your screen using local computer vision and places neat letter hints over buttons, links, and windows. By typing those letters on your keyboard, you can click, scroll, select text, and drag items instantly without strain.

### Who it's for
* Desk workers and writers looking to prevent or recover from repetitive strain injuries (RSI).
* Power users who want to speed up their workflow by keeping their hands on the keyboard.
* People who prefer navigating their computer desktop and apps entirely without a mouse.

### Key features
* **Instant computer vision:** Automatically detects interactive items on your screen without any noticeable delay.
* **Flexible navigation modes:** Switch instantly between window mode, full screen mode, or a multi-purpose screen grid.
* **Text and drag tools:** Easily highlight text passages or drag files between folders using simple keyboard sequences.
* **Customizable hotkeys:** Tailor your shortcuts and keybinds visually using an interactive virtual keyboard.
* **100% offline privacy:** Runs entirely on your local machine with zero data sent to external servers.

### How to install (Download)
1. Visit the official website download page at [Neverclick Website](https://www.neverclick.com/).
2. Download the official installer for Windows.
3. Run the installer and follow the on-screen prompts to set it up on your system.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use with no accounts or subscriptions, works entirely offline, highly responsive interface.
* **Cons:** Currently available for Windows only, does not support alternative operating systems yet.
