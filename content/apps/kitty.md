### What it does in plain English
Kitty is a modern command-line workspace designed for people who spend their day typing computer commands. It leverages your computer's graphics card to draw text instantly, resulting in a buttery-smooth viewing experience. You can easily divide your workspace into multiple panels, change color themes, and view high-resolution images right inside your command window.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and programmers who manage code through text commands.
* System administrators who monitor servers and juggle multiple windows at once.
* Power users who want a deeply customizable and responsive command-line environment.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast rendering powered by your computer's graphics hardware for zero lag.
* Ability to split the main window into multiple flexible tabs and panes.
* Direct display of high-resolution images and graphics within the command interface.
* Extensive customization options for keyboard shortcuts, colors, and fonts.
