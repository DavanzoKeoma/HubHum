### What it does in plain English
Floral Notepaper provides a simple, clutter-free space to jot down ideas, to-do lists, or temporary text snippets. You can instantly bring your notes to the front of your screen using a keyboard shortcut, making it perfect for quick reminders. It also allows you to pin notes directly to your desktop so your most important information is always within reach.

### Who it's for
* Students or professionals who need a quick way to save text while researching or browsing.
* Anyone who wants a distraction-free space to keep daily tasks visible without opening heavy software.

### Key features
* **Quick Access:** Use a simple keyboard shortcut to open your notes instantly from anywhere.
* **Desktop Pinning:** Keep specific notes "floating" on your screen so you never lose track of your tasks.
* **Markdown Support:** Write and format your notes using simple text styles, with a live preview mode.
* **Import and Export:** Easily save your notes as files or bring existing text documents into the app.
