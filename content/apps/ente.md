### What it does in plain English
Ente provides a fully open-source, end-to-end encrypted home for your digital life, ensuring that only you can see your personal files. It combines secure photo storage, two-step verification code management, and confidential document vaults into a single ecosystem. Because your data is scrambled with keys only you control, the service provider cannot read your files or hand them over to outsiders.

### Who it's for
* Everyday smartphone users looking for a trustworthy alternative to mainstream photo galleries and cloud backup services.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want a secure, open-source vault to store sensitive personal documents and account login codes.
* Families wanting to share photo albums and memories privately without sacrificing personal data ownership.

### Key features
* End-to-end encryption across all your devices for complete privacy and data sovereignty.
* Secure photo gallery alternative featuring automatic background uploads and smart search tools.
* Built-in two-step verification code generator to protect your online accounts.
* Confidential document locker to keep your most important credentials and notes safe.
* Independent security audits by renowned cybersecurity experts to verify safety claims.
