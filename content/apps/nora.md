### What it does in plain English
Nora is a beautiful, modern music player designed to bring your offline audio collection to life on your computer. It organizes your songs, albums, and artists neatly while offering synced lyrics and smooth playback controls. You can easily switch between light and dark themes to match your mood and desktop style.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who prefer keeping and organizing their own local audio file collections instead of using streaming services.
* People looking for a distraction-free listening companion with a clean, modern interface and a mini-player mode.
* Listeners who enjoy following along with synced song lyrics while working or relaxing at their desk.

### Key features
* Organize and browse songs, artists, albums, and playlists effortlessly.
* Follow along with built-in support for synchronized song lyrics.
* Keep track of your listening history with Last.FM scrobbling integration.
* Use the compact mini-player mode for distraction-free background listening.
* Switch seamlessly between comfortable Light and Dark visual themes.

* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source with no subscription fees, gorgeous modern user interface, built-in synced lyrics support.
* **Cons:** Metadata editing is currently limited to MP3 files, does not stream music from online subscription services.
