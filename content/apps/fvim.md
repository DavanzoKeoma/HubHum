### What it does in plain English
FVim provides a gorgeous, high-performance graphical wrapper around the powerful Neovim text editing engine. It enhances your typing workspace with hardware-accelerated rendering, crisp font display with ligatures, and flexible window management. You can easily detach windows across multiple monitors, customize your background style, and connect to remote editing sessions seamlessly.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and writers who rely heavily on Neovim for daily text editing.
* Power users looking for a modern graphical face for their favorite terminal text editor.
* Multi-monitor enthusiasts who want to detach and organize editing windows freely.

### Key features
* **Hardware-accelerated rendering:** Enjoy low-latency performance that stays smooth even on high-resolution displays.
* **Advanced font handling:** Renders character ligatures and supports special symbols without requiring complicated font patching.
* **Window flexibility:** Detach your active workspace grids into separate operating system windows with simple shortcuts.
* **Customizable visuals:** Tailor your workspace with adjustable backgrounds, smooth cursor animations, and custom theme colors.
* **Remote connection support:** Easily attach to remote servers via secure shell connections or Windows Subsystem for Linux.
