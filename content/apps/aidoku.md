### What it does in plain English
Aidoku is a dedicated reading companion built specifically for manga enthusiasts using Apple devices. It brings together content from multiple online services and local files into a single organized library. Readers can customize their layout, download chapters for offline enjoyment, and automatically synchronize their reading progress.

### Who it's for
* Manga fans who want an ad-free, clean reading interface on their iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
* Readers who like to organize their personal comic collection from local files and self-hosted servers.
* Users who want to keep their reading history synchronized with popular tracking platforms automatically.

### Key features
* Completely ad-free interface designed for smooth scrolling and page turning.
* Support for reading local comic and manga files directly on your device.
* Built-in integration with popular reading servers and external content providers.
* Offline downloading so you can catch up on your favorite stories without an internet connection.
* Automatic reading progress syncing with major tracking services.
