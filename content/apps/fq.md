### What it does in plain English
fq acts like a magnifying glass for computer files that aren't readable as plain text. It allows you to look inside complex formats—like images, videos, or system files—to see exactly how they are organized. You can search for specific pieces of information, extract parts of a file, or simply explore the data in a clear, readable way.

### Who it's for
* **Digital Archivists and Researchers:** People who need to verify the contents of old or obscure file formats.
* **Tech Enthusiasts:** Users who want to peek under the hood of their files to understand how data is stored on their computer.

### Key features
* **Deep Inspection:** Automatically recognizes and decodes hundreds of different file types.
* **Searchable Data:** Use simple commands to find specific information hidden deep within binary files.
* **Interactive Exploration:** Includes a built-in viewer that lets you navigate through file structures with ease.
* **Data Transformation:** Easily extract, cut, or convert parts of binary files into other formats.
