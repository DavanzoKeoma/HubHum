### What it does in plain English
Skills Hub is a desktop companion designed to help you organize and distribute your artificial intelligence coding assistant skills from a single location. Instead of manually copying files into different tool folders, you add your modules once and automatically push them to numerous compatible software programs. It acts as a central library that keeps your custom shortcuts and helper routines perfectly aligned across all your everyday working environments.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use multiple AI coding assistants and want to share shortcut bundles between them.
* Technical power users who enjoy collecting specialized task workflows and automation scripts.
* Teams and hobbyists looking for an organized way to backup and distribute their favorite assistant prompts.

### Key features
* Central library for browsing, searching, and organizing task-focused assistant skills.
* Instant synchronization across dozens of popular coding tools and custom project folders.
* Bulk management tools for enabling, disabling, and updating multiple selected modules at once.
* Built-in assistant integration so you can organize your library using conversational text commands.
* Cloud official project page syncing to keep your configuration files aligned across multiple computers.
