### What it does in plain English
ExpressionEngine is a self-hosted content publishing platform that lets creators, developers, and agencies build completely custom websites. It gives you full control over your layout, tags, and structure without forcing you to fit into rigid templates. Editors can easily update content directly on the live website using a clean publishing interface.

### Who it's for
* Web developers and digital agencies building tailor-made sites for clients.
* Content editors who need a straightforward, secure way to manage articles and media.
* Organizations wanting complete ownership of their data on a self-hosted platform.

### Key features
* **Custom content modeling:** Define your own data types and relationships for articles, products, or portfolios.
* **Readable template tags:** Build layouts using standard HTML and clean tags rather than complex code.
* **In-context editing:** Update text and images directly while browsing the live website pages.
* **Built-in security:** Protect your site with multi-factor authentication and fine-grained user permissions.
* **Extensible architecture:** Add ecommerce, advanced forms, and custom extensions as your website grows.

* **Pros:** Complete control over markup, strong built-in security, highly flexible data modeling, active community support.
* **Cons:** Requires hosting setup and basic PHP knowledge, not an instant click-to-install hosted SaaS service.
