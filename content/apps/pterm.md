### What it does in plain English
PTerm is a tool designed to make the text shown in your computer's command window look much better. It allows you to easily add charts, progress bars, interactive menus, and colorful headers to your terminal output. This makes it much easier for people to read and interact with the information your programs provide.

### Who it's for
* **Software creators** who want to make their command-line tools look professional and user-friendly.
* **System administrators** who need to display complex data or progress updates in a clear, visual way.

### Key features
* **Interactive Elements:** Includes menus, text input fields, and confirmation prompts to make your tools feel like real applications.
* **Visual Data:** Easily generate bar charts, heatmaps, and progress bars directly in the terminal.
* **Highly Customizable:** You can change colors, styles, and layouts to match your specific needs without complex setup.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works consistently across Windows, macOS, and Linux environments.
* **Clean Design:** Uses standard color schemes to ensure your output looks great on any terminal.

### How to install (Download)
PTerm is a library for the Go programming language. To add it to your project, run the following command in your project folder:

`go get github.com/pterm/pterm`

For detailed instructions and examples, visit the [official documentation](https://docs.pterm.sh/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, open-source, and highly flexible for creating modern terminal interfaces.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of the Go programming language to implement.
