### What it does in plain English
File Converter Pro is a handy desktop application that lets you change your documents, pictures, music, and videos into different formats right on your computer. Because everything happens locally, you never have to upload your private files to random websites or worry about internet connections. It integrates neatly into your system so you can transform files with a simple click from your right-click menu.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users who need a reliable way to open or share incompatible document and media formats.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer keeping personal photos, videos, and paperwork entirely on their own machine.
* Professionals and students looking for a quick, distraction-free tool to process batches of files without installing heavy software suites.

### Key features
* **100% Offline Processing:** Converts all your media and documents locally without sending any data over the internet.
* **All-in-One Format Support:** Handles a wide variety of document types, image formats, audio tracks, and video clips seamlessly.
* **Windows Right-Click Integration:** Quickly start a conversion directly from your standard system file menu.
* **Automation and Watch Folders:** Set up automated background tasks to convert incoming files without manual intervention.
* **Gamified Statistics Dashboard:** Track your conversion counts and unlock fun achievements as you use the application.
