### What it does in plain English
Tianti is a clean, lightweight publishing platform built using standard Java technologies to help creators launch websites quickly. It separates the behind-the-scenes management area from the public-facing pages, giving you full control over articles, categories, and user permissions. The built-in templates automatically adjust to look great on both traditional desktop monitors and mobile phones.

### Who it's for
* **Java Developers:** Programmers looking for a solid foundation to build custom web applications and client portals.
* **Content Creators:** Bloggers and editors who need a straightforward dashboard to publish articles and organize site categories.
* **Small Business Owners:** Teams wanting an open, self-hosted website structure that can be easily tailored to their brand.

### Key features
* **Ready-to-Use Layouts:** Launch a functional website structure straight out of the box without changing core code.
* **Mobile Friendly:** Automatically adapts content presentation for visitors browsing on phones, tablets, or desktop computers.
* **Visual Themes:** Switch easily between pre-configured interface color styles such as blue and red accents.
* **User Permission Management:** Assign granular roles and administrative privileges to different members of your team.
* **Structured Codebase:** Organized neatly into independent modules so you can extend features to match your specific business requirements.
