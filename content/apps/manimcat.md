### What it does in plain English
ManimCat is an intelligent workspace that turns your everyday text descriptions into engaging educational math videos and diagrams. It automates the complex coding typically required for mathematical animations by handling typesetting and rendering behind the scenes. Educators and students can simply type what they want to illustrate, review the generated plan, and produce polished visual aids for lessons or presentations.

### Who it's for
* Math teachers looking to create engaging classroom visuals without spending hours writing code.
* Students wanting to visualize complex geometric proofs, calculus concepts, or algebraic equations.
* Content creators who need clear, precise mathematical animations for tutorials or online courses.

### Key features
* Dual-mode workspace allowing both quick direct generation and collaborative agent-driven studio sessions.
* Automated typesetting for flawless rendering of Chinese text, symbols, and mathematical equations together.
* Built-in error correction loop that automatically fixes and retries failed render tasks.
* Interactive canvas for drawing reference sketches, shapes, and custom function plots.
* Natural speech synthesis synchronized with sentence-level cues to narrate your educational videos.
