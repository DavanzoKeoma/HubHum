### What it does in plain English
MaterialFiles provides a clean and organized way to view, move, and manage all the files stored on your Android device. It follows modern design standards to ensure that navigating through your folders feels intuitive and visually consistent. Because it is open-source, you can trust that it manages your data transparently without hidden tracking or unnecessary complexity.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who want a better, more organized way to browse their phone's storage.
* Power users who need advanced features like network server access or root file management in a clean, user-friendly interface.

### Key features
* **Modern Design:** A clean, uncluttered look that matches the style of your Android operating system.
* **Network Support:** Easily connect to and manage files on your home or office network servers (FTP, SFTP, SMB, and WebDAV).
* **Archive Handling:** Quickly open, extract, or create compressed files like ZIP or TAR.
* **Advanced Control:** Includes support for root access, symbolic links, and detailed file permissions for advanced users.
* **Customizable:** Personalize your experience with different color themes and a dedicated night mode.
