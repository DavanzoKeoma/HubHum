### What it does in plain English
Pake takes any website or web-based tool and transforms it into a dedicated, standalone application that runs right on your computer. Instead of keeping dozens of tabs open in a heavy web browser, you get a clean, independent window for each service. ### Who it's for
* **Productivity Enthusiasts:** People who want dedicated windows for chat tools, notes, and task managers without cluttering their main web browser.
* **Writers and Researchers:** Users who need to keep reference sites or writing platforms open in separate, distraction-free desktop spaces.

### Key features
* **Extremely Lightweight:** Generates packages that are significantly smaller than traditional web-based desktop programs, saving valuable hard drive space.
* **Lightning Fast:** Relies on your computer's built-in system viewing engine rather than bundling a massive browser inside every single app.
* **Clean Interface:** Supports custom window styling, keyboard shortcuts, ad removal, and hidden window frames for a native feel.
