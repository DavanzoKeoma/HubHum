### What it does in plain English
This tool acts as an intelligent upgrade to your standard folder-switching commands by quietly learning which directories you visit most often. Instead of typing out long file paths every single time, you can simply type a unique fragment of the folder name to jump straight there. It seamlessly integrates into your existing command-line environment and supports interactive selections so you never lose track of your work space.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and engineers managing multiple project directories across deep folder structures.
* System administrators and power users who spend their entire working day navigating through terminal windows.
* Anyone who wants to eliminate repetitive typing and speed up daily file management tasks.

### Key features
* **Smart Ranking:** Automatically tracks your frequency of use to prioritize the folders you care about most.
* **Interactive Selection:** Hooks into fuzzy-search tools to let you visually pick from matching directory options on the fly.
* **Universal Compatibility:** Works smoothly across all popular command-line shells and operating systems.
* **Easy Data Import:** Allows you to effortlessly bring over your historical path data from older navigation tools.
