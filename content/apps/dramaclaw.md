### What it does in plain English
DramaClaw acts as a digital film studio that helps you manage the entire creative process from a single screen. You can input a story idea or script, and the system helps you generate storyboards, voiceovers, and video clips to bring your vision to life. It organizes your assets and scenes, allowing you to produce high-quality short dramas, advertisements, or interactive stories without needing a massive production crew.

### Who it's for
*   **Independent Content Creators:** Filmmakers and writers who want to produce visual stories without the high costs of traditional studio equipment.
*   **Marketing Teams:** Professionals looking to quickly generate product videos, social media ads, or character-driven promotional content.

### Key features
*   **Director Agent:** An intelligent assistant that helps plan your project workflow and executes tasks based on your creative brief.
*   **Infinite Canvas:** A visual workspace where you can connect images, video, audio, and 3D scenes to build your story step-by-step.
*   **3D Previsualization:** A built-in stage to block out scenes, set camera angles, and test character movements before generating final video assets.
*   **Interactive Storytelling:** Tools to create branching narratives where the audience can make choices that change the outcome of the story.
*   **Centralized Asset Library:** Keeps your characters, scenes, and voices consistent across every episode of your project.
