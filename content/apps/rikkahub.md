### What it does in plain English
RikkaHub is a handy mobile companion that brings multiple conversational artificial intelligence services together into a single app. Instead of jumping between different web pages or apps, you can easily switch between your favorite conversational models on your phone. It includes helpful extras like image creation, file-based memory, and neat formatting for code and text.

### Who it's for
* Mobile users who want to talk to different conversational intelligence models on the go without installing separate apps.
* Creative individuals and enthusiasts looking to generate images or manage custom assistants directly from their phones.

### Key features
* Switch seamlessly between multiple conversational intelligence providers and custom setups.
* Clean, modern visual design that fits right into your phone's interface.
* Create and view media like images and videos directly through conversation.
* Handy file-based memory and advanced text rendering with code highlighting.
* Import character cards and easily export your settings using QR codes.
