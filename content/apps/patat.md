### What it does in plain English
patat lets you create and show slide decks right inside your computer's command line window. You write your slides using simple text formatting, and the tool turns them into a clean, readable presentation instantly. It even lets you see your notes on a second screen while you talk, so you never lose your place.

### Who it's for
* **Developers and tech enthusiasts** who prefer working in the terminal and want a lightweight way to share code or technical updates.
* **Students and educators** who need a fast, distraction-free tool for creating lecture slides or study notes.
* **Minimalists** who want to present ideas without the clutter of heavy desktop software or internet connectivity.

### Key features
* **Live editing:** The slides update automatically as you type, so you can fix mistakes or add content without restarting the program.
* **Code highlighting:** It colors code snippets in nearly 100 different programming languages, making technical presentations much easier to read.
* **Speaker notes:** You can keep private notes visible only to you on a second monitor, keeping your audience focused on the main slides.
* **Flexible formatting:** It understands standard Markdown and can automatically adjust text width to fit your screen size perfectly.
* **Custom themes:** Change colors and styles to match your personal taste or your organization's branding.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, runs offline, very fast, and keeps you in your familiar terminal environment.
* **Cons:** Requires some familiarity with command-line tools, and visual effects are limited compared to graphical presentation software.
