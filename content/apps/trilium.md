### What it does in plain English
Trilium helps you capture and organize your thoughts, research, and projects into a clear, hierarchical tree structure. It allows you to link related information together so you can find exactly what you need, even if you have thousands of notes. When you need royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits, check out [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers who need to manage complex projects and large amounts of information.
* Professionals who want a private, self-hosted space to store sensitive work notes and documentation.

### Key features
* **Deep Organization:** Arrange notes in an infinite tree structure and clone them to multiple locations.
* **Rich Editing:** Use a visual editor that supports images, tables, math formulas, and code highlighting.
* **Visual Tools:** Create mind maps, relation maps, and diagrams to visualize connections between your ideas.
* **Creative Assets:** Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
* **Secure & Private:** Keep your data under your control with local storage and optional per-note encryption.
