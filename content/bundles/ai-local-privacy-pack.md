### Why this curated guide
Local AI desktops and self-hosted chat workspaces let everyday users harness modern language models while keeping files and prompts strictly private.

### Featured highlights
* **[Jan](/HubHum/apps/jan/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Jan is an open-source alternative to online smart assistants that runs completely offline on your own personal computer.
* **[ChatOllama](/HubHum/apps/chat-ollama/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): ChatOllama lets you run smart AI agents on your own computer to chat with your documents and manage tasks without sending data to the cloud.
* **[aider](/HubHum/apps/aider/)** (*CLI Tool*): Collaborate with advanced artificial intelligence directly inside your computer terminal to create programs and update your software projects seamlessly.
* **[OpenWhispr](/HubHum/apps/openwhispr/)** (*Desktop App*): OpenWhispr turns your voice into text and notes while keeping your data private on your own computer.
* **[IncarnaMind](/HubHum/apps/incarnamind/)** (*Desktop App*): An open-source desktop app that checks what your AI assistant writes sentence by sentence against your personal document library.
* **[Atomic Chat](/HubHum/apps/atomic-chat/)** (*Desktop App*): Run open-source artificial intelligence models directly on your computer without sending personal data to the cloud.
