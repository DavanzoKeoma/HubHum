### What it does in plain English
Wakapi keeps a detailed log of your coding activity, showing you exactly which languages and projects take up most of your day. It works in the background to provide clear, visual reports on your work habits without sending your data to third-party companies. You can host it on your own computer or server to maintain full control over your personal statistics.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want to understand their productivity patterns.
* Privacy-conscious coders who prefer to keep their work data off public cloud services.

### Key features
* **Detailed Insights:** See breakdowns of your time by project, programming language, and operating system.
* **Privacy First:** Since you host it yourself, your coding history stays entirely under your control.
* **Visual Reports:** View your progress through clear charts and receive helpful weekly email summaries.
* **Seamless Integration:** Works with your existing text editors and coding tools to track activity automatically.
* **Customizable:** Easily export your data or connect it to other dashboard tools if you want to display your stats elsewhere.

### How to install (Download)
* **Use the Hosted Service:** You can sign up for a free account at [Wakapi.dev](https://wakapi.dev) to get started immediately without setting up your own server.
* **Self-Hosting:** If you prefer to run it yourself, you can use [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/) or download the latest release from the [Official Source](https://github.com/muety/wakapi).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscription required, and gives you total ownership of your data.
* **Cons:** Requires basic technical knowledge to set up and maintain if you choose to host it on your own server.
