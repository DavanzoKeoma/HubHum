### What it does in plain English
Swift AI helps you teach computers to recognize patterns, like reading handwriting or spotting images.  
It runs fast on any Apple device you own, so you don’t need extra hardware.  
You can use it to add smart features to apps you already have.

### Who it's for
- iPhone and Mac app creators who want to add intelligent behavior.  
- Hobbyists experimenting with AI on their personal computers.  
- Teachers showing students how machines can learn from data.

### Key features
- Works natively on macOS, iOS, and iPadOS, using Apple’s built‑in speed boosters.  
- Simple way to build and train neural networks.  
- Ready‑made example that recognizes handwritten digits.  
- Runs trained models directly on the device without needing an internet connection.  
- Clear documentation for each component.
