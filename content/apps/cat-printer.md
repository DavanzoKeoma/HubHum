### What it does in plain English
Cat-Printer lets you take full control of popular pocket-sized Bluetooth thermal printers directly from your computer or phone. It provides a straightforward browser-based dashboard or mobile app so you can test your device, configure settings, and print images quickly. The software bridges the gap between various operating systems and quirky mini hardware without requiring complicated setup routines.

### Who it's for
* Hobbyists and crafters who own portable pocket thermal printers and want an easy way to print from phones or computers.
* Makers looking for a lightweight tool to send custom text and pictures to low-cost wireless printers.

### Key features
* Simple browser-based control panel that works directly on computers and mobile devices.
* Direct Android application for printing on the go.
* Compatible with multiple popular models of Bluetooth thermal printers.
* Supports light and dark interface themes with multi-language translation options.
* Completely free open-source tool with no hidden subscriptions or proprietary ads.
