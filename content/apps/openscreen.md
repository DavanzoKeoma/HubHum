### What it does in plain English
OpenScreen allows you to record your desktop activity with high-quality, native capture tools. Once you finish recording, you can easily trim clips, add professional zoom effects, and include webcam overlays to create engaging content. It automatically generates captions and provides a simple timeline to help you export polished videos for social media or product documentation.

### Who it's for
* **Software Developers** who need to showcase new features or bug fixes in suggested update.
* **Content Creators** who want to produce high-quality software tutorials without expensive subscriptions.

### Key features
* **Professional Editing:** Easily trim, crop, and add speed adjustments to your video segments.
* **Smart Zooming:** Automatically follow your cursor or manually set zooms to highlight specific areas of your screen.
* **AI Assistance:** Use an optional AI assistant to help you edit your timeline, apply cuts, or frame your camera view.
* **Automatic Captions:** Generate subtitles for your voiceovers on-device, ensuring your privacy remains intact.
* **Custom Overlays:** Add webcam picture-in-picture, custom backgrounds, and motion blur for a polished, professional look.
