### What it does in plain English
UmbrelOS turns an ordinary mini computer or spare machine into your very own personal cloud center. It lets you store your photos, back up files, and run hundreds of helpful applications without handing your private data over to big tech corporations. You get all the convenience of online storage and modern apps while keeping your information safely inside your own walls.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to keep their personal photos, videos, and documents away from corporate cloud storage providers.
* Households looking for a centralized family server where everyone can have a private account and shared media folders.
* Tech hobbyists and makers who want an easy, beautiful way to experiment with self-hosted applications on mini computers or Raspberry Pi devices.

### Key features
* **Automatic Phone Backups:** Send your iPhone camera roll straight to your home server so your memories are always safe and local.
* **Massive App Library:** Install over 300 popular open-source tools, media organizers, and smart assistants with a single click.
* **Multi-User Households:** Give every family member their own secure login, private home folder, and personalized home screen.
* **Flexible Storage Management:** Combine multiple hard drives into one large storage pool and protect your data against drive failures.
* **Virtual Machines:** Run full desktop operating systems right inside your browser window alongside your everyday apps.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to install on your own hardware, incredibly polished visual interface, and massive selection of built-in apps.
* **Cons:** Requires a dedicated computer or device to run 24/7, and cannot be installed on top of an already running traditional Linux system.
