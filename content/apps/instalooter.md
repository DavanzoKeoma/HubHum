### What it does in plain English
InstaLooter is a tool designed to help you save media content from Instagram to your local storage. It functions by gathering pictures and videos from specific user profiles, hashtags, or individual post links. The program operates independently of official social media interfaces to retrieve your requested files.

### Who it's for
* Content archivists who want to keep a local backup of their own or public media collections.
* Researchers or hobbyists who need to gather visual data from specific hashtags for offline viewing.

### Key features
* **Flexible Downloading:** Save entire profiles, specific hashtags, or individual posts by providing a link.
* **Batch Processing:** Use configuration files to download content from multiple sources in one go.
* **Session Management:** Securely log in to access content that requires an account, with options to clear your session data easily.
* **Customizable Output:** Choose exactly where your files are saved on your computer and how many items to retrieve.

### How to install (Download)
InstaLooter is a command-line tool intended for users familiar with Python. You can install it by running the following command in your terminal:
`pip install --user instalooter --pre`

For detailed setup instructions and guidance on how to use the command-line interface, please refer to the [Official Documentation](http://instalooter.readthedocs.io/en/latest/install.html).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly efficient for bulk downloading, and does not require a browser.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of using a terminal or command prompt, and may be affected by changes to social media platform policies.
