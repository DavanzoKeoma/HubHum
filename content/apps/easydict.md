### What it does in plain English
Easydict is a clean, easy-to-use menu bar and window tool for Apple computers that bridges multiple online dictionaries and translation engines. Whenever you encounter a foreign word or paragraph, you can highlight it, take a screenshot, or type it directly to view translations from dozens of popular services simultaneously. It automatically detects your source language and saves you time by putting all your favorite linguistic resources into one neat window.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers reading foreign language papers or articles on their computer
* Professionals communicating across language barriers who prefer checking multiple translation models at once
* Language learners looking for a fast, desktop-integrated dictionary tool with built-in text-to-speech

### Key features
* **Instant Input and Selection Translate:** Type or highlight any text to immediately pull up definitions and translations without opening a browser.
* **OCR Screenshot Translation:** Capture any image, photo, or protected text on your screen to extract and translate the words instantly.
* **Multi-Service Query:** Compare results from twenty different translation and artificial intelligence engines side-by-side.
* **System Integration:** Seamlessly works with native Apple dictionaries and translation tools already installed on your Mac.
* **Audio Pronunciation:** Listen to correct word pronunciations using built-in voice services.

### How to install (Download)
* **Homebrew Installation (Recommended):** Open your terminal and run `brew install --cask easydict`
* **Manual Installation:** [Download for Mac (.dmg)](https://github.com/tisfeng/Easydict/releases/download/2.26.0/Easydict.dmg) from the official release page.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, supports dozens of translation engines out of the box, fast keyboard shortcuts.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS 13.0 or newer, some online services may require personal account keys or tokens.
