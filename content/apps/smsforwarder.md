### What it does in plain English
SmsForwarder is a helpful companion app for Android that monitors your incoming text messages, phone calls, and app notifications. Based on rules you set up, it instantly sends those alerts to alternative destinations like chat groups, webhooks, or email addresses. It also allows you to remotely check your device status or manage tasks without picking up your phone.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who keep a spare secondary phone plugged in at home and need important alerts sent to their primary device.
* Small business owners who want to route customer text notifications straight to shared team chat channels.
* Users who love automating everyday tasks and want a reliable way to keep track of missed calls and notifications remotely.

### Key features
* Monitors incoming text messages, phone calls, and application notifications in real time.
* Forwards alerts flexibly to various chat platforms, webhooks, emails, and messaging channels.
* Supports remote commands to check battery level, view call logs, or send text messages.
* Includes automated task helpers to make your daily routine smoother and hassle-free.
* Completely free and open-source software with no hidden tracking or data collection.
