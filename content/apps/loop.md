### What it does in plain English
Loop is a sleek utility designed to help Mac users organize their screen space effortlessly. By holding down a chosen key and moving your mouse or trackpad, a visual menu pops up allowing you to instantly snap, resize, or move any window into place. You can also customize the look and feel of the menus, set up personalized keyboard shortcuts, or temporarily tuck away messy windows out of sight.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who juggle multiple open programs and want a faster way to arrange their workspace.
* Professionals and students who prefer keeping their windows neatly snapped into split-screen or grid layouts.
* Anyone looking for a free, highly customizable alternative to standard window arrangements on macOS.

### Key features
* **Radial Menu:** Trigger an intuitive visual menu with your mouse or trackpad to snap windows in any direction instantly.
* **Live Preview:** See an outline of how your window will resize before you update to the new layout.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Assign custom key combinations to perform complex window arrangements with a single keystroke.
* **Window Stashing:** Quickly push windows out of the way to the edge of your screen to instantly clear your desktop clutter.
* **Fully Theemable:** Customize colors, padding, and corner radii to match your personal aesthetic.

### How to install (Download)
* **Homebrew:** Open your terminal and run `brew install loop`.
* **Manual Download:** Grab the latest app package directly from the [Official Release Page](https://github.com/MrKai77/Loop/releases/latest) or [Download Loop for macOS (.zip)](https://github.com/MrKai77/Loop/releases/latest/download/Loop.zip).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, highly customizable visual interface, lightweight performance.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS 13 or later, takes a short moment to learn the various trigger key combinations.
