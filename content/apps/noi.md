### What it does in plain English
Noi creates a dedicated space on your computer to manage your web-based tasks and AI conversations without the clutter of a standard web browser. It keeps your work sessions separate and organized, ensuring that your history and settings remain private on your own device. You can easily switch between different projects or tools, helping you stay focused and productive throughout your day.

### Who it's for
*   **Power users** who juggle multiple web-based projects and want to keep their workspaces tidy.
*   **AI enthusiasts** who need a centralized, organized way to manage prompts and chat sessions across different services.

### Key features
*   **Multi-window management:** Run different projects in separate windows to keep your workflow organized.
*   **Private and local:** Your history, prompts, and settings are stored directly on your computer, not in the cloud.
*   **Customizable workspace:** Choose from various themes and visual styles to match your personal preference.
*   **Built-in tools:** Includes a quick-access terminal for running local commands and scripts.
*   **Prompt organization:** Save and reuse your favorite AI prompts to speed up your daily interactions.
