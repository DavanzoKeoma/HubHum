### What it does in plain English
This community-driven manual provides practical instructions for hardening macOS against modern digital threats. It walks you through setting up hardware-backed security features, adjusting built-in privacy settings, and keeping your personal files away prying eyes. Whether you are an everyday user or an experienced professional, it helps you take full control of your computer's defenses.

### Who it's for
* Everyday Mac owners who want to keep their personal browsing history and photos private from intruders.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to minimize telemetry tracking and data collection by software companies.
* Advanced users and system administrators seeking structured checklists to enforce organizational security standards.

### Key features
* Step-by-step guidance on setting up threat models and evaluating personal risk levels.
* Clear instructions for activating built-in disk encryption, system integrity protections, and firewall options.
* Recommendations for secure web browsing, encrypted messaging apps, and private DNS configurations.
* Best practices for separating administrator and standard user accounts to prevent accidental system damage.
