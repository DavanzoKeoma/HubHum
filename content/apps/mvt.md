### What it does in plain English
MVT scans Android and iOS devices for signs of spyware.  
It automates forensic checks and looks for known indicators.  
The tool is free and open source.

### Who it's for
Investigators working with mobile devices.  
Security analysts looking for evidence of spyware.  
Researchers studying mobile threats.

### Key features
- Automated forensic checks for Android and iOS.  
- Supports public indicators of compromise.  
- Command‑line interface for batch processing.  
- Extensible with plugins.  
- Detailed logs and reports.
