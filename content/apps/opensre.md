### What it does in plain English
OpenSRE acts as a smart assistant that watches over your software development process. When a project fails to build or run correctly, it automatically reads the error logs to find the root cause. It then suggests or applies a fix so you can spend less time troubleshooting and more time creating.

### Who it's for
*   Software developers who want to spend less time manually fixing repetitive build errors.
*   Engineering teams looking to keep their project progress moving without getting stuck on technical roadblocks.

### Key features
*   **Automatic Investigation:** Reads complex error logs to identify exactly why a process failed.
*   **Proactive Fixes:** Suggests or pushes code changes to resolve issues directly in your project.
*   **Clear Insights:** Provides helpful summaries of why failures happened, making them easier to understand.
*   **Broad Compatibility:** Connects with dozens of common professional tools, cloud services, and communication platforms.
*   **Privacy Focused:** Designed to run on your own infrastructure, keeping your data and models under your control.
