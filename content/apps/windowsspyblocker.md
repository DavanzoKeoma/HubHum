### What it does in plain English
WindowsSpyBlocker is a simple application designed to stop your computer from sending usage data and telemetry back to Microsoft. It works by quietly watching the network traffic generated by your system and identifying which addresses are trying to collect your personal habits. By creating smart blocking rules, it cuts off these unwanted background connections so your activity stays private.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious computer owners who want to prevent their operating system from sharing background telemetry.
* Everyday users looking for a straightforward way to lock down default privacy settings on their PC.
* System administrators who need a reliable method to audit and block unwanted background tracking traffic.

### Key features
* Analyzes live network traffic to discover hidden spying and tracking connections.
* Blocks unwanted background data collection using carefully sorted rules.
* Runs as a portable single executable file that requires no complicated installation.
* Lightweight design that uses minimal system resources while running in the background.
* Open-source approach lets anyone inspect how traffic is monitored and filtered.
