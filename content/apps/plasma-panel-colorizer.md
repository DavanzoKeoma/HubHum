### What it does in plain English
Plasma Panel Colorizer is an extension for your computer desktop that lets you completely redesign how your taskbars and status bars look. You can easily adjust colors, add background shapes, and make your panels float or become transparent without complex setup steps. It acts as an all-in-one styling studio that brings advanced dock features directly to your standard desktop panels.

### Who it's for
* Linux desktop users who love personalizing their visual workspace to match custom themes.
* People migrating from alternative desktop setups who miss advanced dock customization options.
* Anyone looking to clean up their screen layout by floating, hiding, or reshaping system panels.

### Key features
* Apply built-in visual styles or create your own custom presets.
* Automatically switch panel styles based on whether windows are maximized or touching the panel.
* Add blur effects, custom background shapes, margins, and borders behind panel icons.
* Force consistent icon and text colors even when certain mini-apps try to override them.
* Control panel visibility and transparency smoothly using keyboard shortcuts or automated triggers.
