### What it does in plain English
Notesnook lets you jot down ideas, write journals, and save web pages without sacrificing your personal privacy. Every single note you create is locked with heavy-duty encryption directly on your device before it ever touches the cloud. It serves as a comfortable, feature-packed alternative to standard commercial note applications while keeping your data strictly in your own hands.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to keep their journals and notes entirely hidden from corporate data tracking.
* Students and professionals looking for a reliable, clean place to organize research, lists, and documents.
* Anyone searching for a seamless substitute for commercial note-keeping software without hidden subscriptions or data harvesting.

### Key features
* Complete end-to-end encryption ensures that only you can read your notes and attachments.
* Cross-platform syncing allows you to access your personal thoughts smoothly across all your devices.
* Built-in web clipping tools let you save full articles and web pages directly into your private notebooks.
* Organization options include tags, notebooks, and color coding to keep your workspace tidy.
* Fully open-source foundation means independent security experts can verify its safety claims at any time.
