### What it does in plain English
This application provides a dedicated desktop environment for watching Apple developer conference videos and keynote presentations. It allows users to search through multi-language transcripts, save specific video timestamps with personal notes, and keep track of watched sessions. Everything stays organized locally and synchronizes automatically between your different computers using cloud storage.

### Who it's for
* Software creators and engineers exploring new platform technologies
* Tech enthusiasts who want to follow annual developer presentations comfortably on desktop
* Learners who need searchable transcripts and playback speed controls to study technical talks

### Key features
* Stream live keynote presentations and access an extensive archive of past session videos
* Control playback speeds from half-speed up to double speed with picture-in-picture mode
* Search through multi-language text transcripts to jump straight to exact discussion points
* Create timestamped bookmarks with custom notes inside any video
* Synchronize favorites, notes, and viewing progress seamlessly across multiple computers
