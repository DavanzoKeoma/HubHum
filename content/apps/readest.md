### What it does in plain English
Readest is a flexible digital book reader that supports almost any file format you might have in your collection. It allows you to organize your library, highlight important passages, and even listen to your books with built-in narration tools. You can easily sync your reading progress and notes across your computer, phone, and tablet to keep your place wherever you go.

### Who it's for
*   Avid readers who want a single, clean app to manage books from many different sources.
*   Students or researchers who need to annotate, highlight, and look up definitions while they read.

### Key features
*   **Wide Format Support:** Opens almost any file type, including EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and even web articles.
*   **Smart Tools:** Includes built-in dictionary lookups, translation features, and text-to-speech narration.
*   **Customizable Experience:** Adjust fonts, colors, and layout styles to make your reading time comfortable.
*   **Library Management:** Keep your collection organized with easy sorting and search tools.
*   **Syncing:** Keep your bookmarks, notes, and reading progress consistent across all your devices.
