### What it does in plain English
Evidence is a tool that helps you turn raw data into professional, interactive reports. Instead of clicking and dragging items in a visual interface, you write simple instructions using standard text and database language. This allows you to create clean, fast-loading dashboards that are easy to update and share with your team.

### Who it's for
* Data analysts who prefer writing code over using traditional drag-and-drop software.
* Teams that need to create consistent, repeatable business reports directly from their data sources.

### Key features
* **Code-based reports:** Build your entire dashboard using familiar text-based formats.
* **Fast performance:** Generates highly optimized reports that load quickly for your audience.
* **Flexible hosting:** Keep your data private by hosting it on your own servers or publish it easily through their official platform.
* **Agent-ready:** Designed to work seamlessly with modern AI coding assistants to speed up your report creation.

### How to install (Download)
You can get started by visiting the [Official Evidence Website](https://evidence.dev) to view the setup guide. The tool is installed via your computer's command line interface, which is detailed in their [official documentation](https://docs.evidence.studio).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, creates very fast reports, and allows for version control of your work.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of database queries (SQL) and text formatting, which may be a learning curve for non-technical users.
