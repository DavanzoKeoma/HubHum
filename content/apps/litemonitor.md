### What it does in plain English
LiteMonitor sits quietly on your Windows desktop or taskbar, giving you a live look at how hard your computer is working. It tracks essential components like your main processor, graphics card, memory usage, and internet speed without slowing down your system. You can easily customize how it looks, set color warnings for high temperatures, and even clean up memory with a single click.

### Who it's for
* Gamers who want to keep an eye on their frame rates, temperatures, and hardware load while playing.
* Power users who like to monitor internet speeds, disk activity, and system resources in real-time.
* Anyone who wants a neat, unobtrusive way to check their computer's health without opening heavy diagnostic programs.

### Key features
* **Live Performance Tracking:** Keeps a constant watch on your processor, graphics card, memory, disk activity, and network speeds.
* **Flexible Display Options:** Arrange your monitors in horizontal strips, vertical blocks, or place them directly onto your Windows taskbar.
* **Custom Themes & Styling:** Personalize colors, fonts, transparency levels, and window sizes to match your desktop setup.
* **Smart Color Alerts:** Automatically shifts colors when components reach high temperature or usage thresholds.
* **Helpful Extras:** Includes memory cleanup tools, traffic statistics, and support for custom add-ons like weather or currency rates.
