### What it does in plain English
Flow.Launcher is a lightning-fast desktop search tool for Windows that lets you open applications, find deep file contents, and browse web bookmarks without touching your mouse. Press a simple keyboard shortcut to bring up a clean search bar anywhere on your screen. Type a few letters to instantly jump to files, execute system commands, perform math calculations, or use community-made plugins.

### Who it's for
* **Power users** who want to navigate their Windows PC entirely through quick keyboard shortcuts.
* **Professionals and students** who need to locate deeply buried files, bookmarks, and applications in seconds.
* **Customization enthusiasts** who love tailoring their desktop workflow with themes, hotkeys, and community plugins.

### Key features
* **Instant File & App Search:** Find installed programs, folders, and text inside files quickly using fast system indexing.
* **Extensive Plugin Ecosystem:** Expand functionality with community tools for Spotify, GitHub, Steam, clipboard history, and more.
* **Built-in Calculator & Shell:** Evaluate math expressions instantly and run batch or PowerShell commands directly from the search bar.
* **Preview Panel:** Press keys like F1 to view media files, text previews, and markdown documentation without opening separate windows.
* **Game Mode & Customization:** Automatically pause hotkeys during gaming sessions and customize themes to match your desktop style.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, highly responsive interface, massive plugin library, highly customizable hotkeys and themes.
* **Cons:** Windows only, initial indexing of large drives may take a few moments.
