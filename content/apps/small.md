### What it does in plain English
Small is a specialized tool designed to help software creators organize complex mobile applications by splitting them into smaller, independent pieces. By breaking a large project into these modular parts, it becomes much easier to update specific features without needing to rebuild the entire application. This approach helps keep mobile software lightweight and efficient for the end user.

### Who it's for
* Mobile application developers looking to simplify their project structure.
* Teams working on large-scale apps who want to manage updates and feature additions more effectively.

### Key features
* **Modular Architecture:** Allows you to divide a single large application into several smaller, distinct components.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Works consistently across both Android and iOS environments.
* **Lightweight Design:** Focuses on minimal overhead to ensure your application remains fast and responsive.
* **Simplified Updates:** Enables you to update or modify specific parts of your app independently.
