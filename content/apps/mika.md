### What it does in plain English
Mika is an on-device personal assistant for your Android phone that listens to your plain-text requests and navigates apps or websites to finish tasks for you. It runs on a separate background screen so you can continue using your phone normally without interruption. You can ask it to read chat summaries, compare prices, or fetch updates while maintaining complete control over when it executes or pauses.

### Who it's for
* Busy smartphone users who want to automate repetitive navigation and messaging tasks.
* Productivity enthusiasts looking for an open-source automation helper that respects user privacy.
* Android owners who want a hands-free way to interact with web pages and installed applications.

### Key features
* Operates mobile applications and web pages naturally by reading the screen interface.
* Runs workflows on a hidden background screen so your main view stays completely free.
* Connects directly to popular AI model providers or personal accounts using your own secure keys.
* Remembers user preferences and builds helpful habits to make future tasks run faster.
* Keeps all personal chat history, configurations, and encrypted memories securely on your device.
