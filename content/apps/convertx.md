### What it does in plain English
You often have a file that needs to be a different format, like turning a Word doc into a PDF.  
Instead of hunting for many separate programs, ConvertX lets you do the conversion right in your web browser.  
It works with thousands of file types, so you can handle images, videos, ebooks, and more in one place.  

### Who it's for
- Home users who need occasional file conversions.  
- Small business owners who share documents with clients.  
- Teachers preparing study materials in different formats.

### Key features
- Convert many files at once.  
- Keep your files safe with password protection.  
- Create separate user accounts for family or team members.  
- Works with images, videos, ebooks, documents and more.  

### How to install (Download)
Prefer not to manage servers by hand?
If you prefer to host it yourself, follow the simple steps on the official release page: [Visit the official release page](https://github.com/C4illin/ConvertX/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, works in any browser, supports thousands of formats, you keep full control of your files.  
* **Cons:** needs a little technical setup to run, no ready‑made desktop installer, you must provide your own server or cloud host.
