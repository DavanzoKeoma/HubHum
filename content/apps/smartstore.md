### What it does in plain English
Smartstore provides everything you need to build a professional online shop, from product catalogs to secure checkout systems. It includes smart tools that use artificial intelligence to help you write product descriptions and create marketing images automatically. The platform is designed to grow with your business, allowing you to manage multiple stores, languages, and currencies from one central dashboard.

### Who it's for
*   Small to medium business owners looking for a robust, self-hosted alternative to subscription-based shop services.
*   Web developers and agencies who need a highly customizable and scalable foundation for building custom e-commerce websites.

### Key features
*   **AI-Powered Content:** Generate product descriptions, meta tags, and marketing images directly within the shop manager.
*   **Flexible Catalog Management:** Support for unlimited products, bundles, variants, and complex pricing structures.
*   **Visual Page Builder:** Create beautiful, custom shop pages using a simple drag-and-drop style editor without needing to write code.
*   **Global Readiness:** Built-in support for multiple languages, currencies, and tax regulations, including full compliance with European laws.
*   **High Performance:** Optimized for speed with advanced caching and a modern, mobile-friendly design that looks great on any device.
