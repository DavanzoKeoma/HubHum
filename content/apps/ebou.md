### What it does in plain English
Ebou is a desktop client for decentralized microblogging networks that organizes posts by author rather than a traditional chronological timeline. By grouping updates together similarly to modern group messaging apps, it helps you keep track of your favorite creators and conversations. It also includes dedicated conversation views so you can easily follow replies within their original context.

### Who it's for
* Active participants on decentralized social networks who prefer a conversation-focused reading experience.
* Users looking for an alternative desktop app to keep up with friends and creators without clutter.

### Key features
* Groups posts by author into clean, messenger-style chat threads for better readability.
* Interactive conversation tree view that maps out replies and discussion threads clearly.
* Standard timeline view for catching up on chronological updates.
* Quick posting with support for image and video attachments via drag-and-drop.
* Support for handling bookmarks, favorites, boosts, and user profile management.

* **Pros:** Clean messaging-style interface, unique author-grouped timelines, and open-source codebase.
* **Cons:** Still in active experimental development with missing features and occasional bugs.
