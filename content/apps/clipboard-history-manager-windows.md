### What it does in plain English
Windows Clipboard History Manager is a free desktop tool that automatically saves everything you copy so you are never limited to just your last copied item. It keeps a searchable archive of text, links, images, and files right on your device. You can organize your clips with custom tags, edit saved text before pasting it again, and filter out apps you do not want to track.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who collect numerous text snippets and reference links daily.
* Writers and editors who frequently juggle and reuse multiple paragraphs across various documents.
* Anyone on Windows who wants a straightforward way to find past copied items without complex setup.

### Key features
* **Unlimited Clipboard History:** Automatically retains an extensive log of everything you copy over time.
* **Instant Search:** Quickly finds specific text, links, or images using keywords.
* **Image and File Support:** Preserves screenshots, bitmap images, PNG files, and formatted text.
* **Content Editing:** Modify saved text right inside the app before pasting it elsewhere.
* **Custom Tags and Filters:** Organize your snippets neatly and control which apps contribute to your history.

### How to install (Download)
1. Go to the official [Releases](https://github.com/Umbra9179/clipboard-history-manager-windows/releases) page.
2. Download the latest release archive named `Windows Clipboard History Manager 4.2.1.zip`.
3. Extract the downloaded archive into a folder of your choice on your computer.
4. Ensure `Windows Clipboard History Manager 4.2.1.exe` and `libvlc.dll` remain together in that folder.
5. Double-click `Windows Clipboard History Manager 4.2.1.exe` to launch the application.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, open-source transparency, no subscription fees, works locally without cloud accounts.
* **Cons:** Windows only, advanced organization rules take a few moments to learn.
