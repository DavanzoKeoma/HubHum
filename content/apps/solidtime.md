### What it does in plain English
Solidtime provides a simple way to track exactly how much time you and your team spend on specific tasks and projects. It helps you stay organized by linking your time logs directly to clients and billable rates, making invoicing much easier. You can use it as a hosted service or set it up on your own private server to keep full control of your data.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers who need to track billable hours for multiple clients.
* Small agencies looking for a clean, organized way to manage team projects and tasks.

### Key features
* **Simple Time Tracking:** Start and stop timers with an easy-to-use, modern interface.
* **Project Management:** Create projects, assign team members, and track progress on specific tasks.
* **Flexible Billing:** Set custom hourly rates for different projects, team members, or organizations.
* **Data Portability:** Easily import your existing time tracking data from other popular services.
* **Multi-Organization Support:** Manage multiple business entities or client groups from a single account.

### How to install (Download)
Solidtime is a web-based application. You can get started immediately by signing up for the hosted version or by following the self-hosting guide if you prefer to run it on your own infrastructure:
* [Sign up for Solidtime Cloud](https://www.solidtime.io/)
* [Self-hosting documentation and guides](https://docs.solidtime.io/self-hosting/intro)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely open-source, modern and intuitive design, supports data imports from other tools.
* **Cons:** Self-hosting requires some technical knowledge of server management, no native desktop app (runs in your web browser).
