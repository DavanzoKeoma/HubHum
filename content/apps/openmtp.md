### What it does in plain English
OpenMTP solves the common headache of moving photos, videos, and documents between your Mac and your Android phone. Instead of using slow wireless methods or buggy official apps, it connects directly through your USB cable for instant access. You can browse, copy, rename, and delete files on your phone as easily as you would on your computer.

### Who it's for
* **Mac users with Android phones:** Anyone who needs to regularly back up photos or transfer large video files without waiting for cloud uploads.
* **Content creators:** Photographers and videographers who need to quickly offload high-resolution media from their devices to their editing workstation.
* **Tech-savvy individuals:** Users who prefer free, open-source software that respects their privacy and doesn't collect personal data.

### Key features
* **High-Speed USB Transfer:** Moves files directly via cable, supporting large files over 4GB without the limitations of older tools.
* **Simple File Browser:** Features a split-pane view that shows your Mac and Android device side-by-side for easy drag-and-drop operations.
* **Full File Management:** Allows you to rename, delete, and create new folders directly on your Android device.
* **Privacy-Focused:** No ads, no tracking, and no collection of personally identifiable information.
* **Modern Interface:** Supports dark mode and offers both grid and list views for better visibility.

* **Pros:** Completely free, open-source, fast USB speeds, no ads or tracking, and supports large file transfers.
* **Cons:** Only available for macOS (not Windows or Linux), and requires a physical USB cable connection.
