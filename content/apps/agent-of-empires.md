### What it does in plain English
Agent of Empires acts as a central control hub for all your different AI coding assistants. It keeps these programs running in the background even if you close your main window or disconnect from your computer. You can then check on their progress or give them new instructions through a simple dashboard on your laptop or mobile phone.

### Who it's for
* Developers who use multiple AI tools and want to keep them organized in one place.
* Professionals who need to monitor long-running AI tasks while away from their primary workstation.

### Key features
* **Persistent Sessions:** Keeps your AI tools running in the background so they never stop when you close your terminal or disconnect.
* **Universal Dashboard:** View and manage your AI agents from a clean web interface on your phone or desktop.
* **Safe Sandboxing:** Allows you to run AI tasks inside isolated containers to keep your main computer files secure.
* **Multi-Device Access:** Switch between your terminal and a web browser seamlessly to check on your work.

### How to install (Download)
This tool is designed for Linux and macOS. You can install it using the Homebrew package manager by running `brew install aoe` in your terminal. For manual installation or other methods, please visit the [official installation guide](https://www.agent-of-empires.com/docs/installation/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Keeps tasks running in the background, works on mobile, and supports many different AI tools.
* **Cons:** Requires the use of a terminal to set up, and it is not natively supported on Windows (though it works via WSL2).
