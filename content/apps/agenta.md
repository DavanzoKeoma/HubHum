### What it does in plain English
Agenta provides a shared workspace where you can build specialized AI agents simply by chatting with them. You can connect these agents to your existing tools and apps, allowing them to handle recurring tasks or research on your behalf. Once created, your team can interact with these agents directly through familiar messaging platforms like Slack, Telegram, or WhatsApp.

### Who it's for
* **Teams and Departments:** Perfect for groups in sales, HR, or operations that need to automate repetitive tasks without writing complex code.
* **Project Managers:** Ideal for those who want to coordinate multiple specialized AI agents to handle research, lead qualification, or data organization.

### Key features
* **Chat-Based Creation:** Build and refine your agents by simply describing what you need in plain language.
* **Multi-Platform Integration:** Talk to your agents where you already work, including Slack, Telegram, and WhatsApp.
* **Team Collaboration:** Share agents across your organization with clear roles, permissions, and audit logs for every action taken.
* **Flexible Tooling:** Connect agents to over 1,000 apps or give them access to a virtual browser and file system to handle tasks on any website.
* **Transparent Control:** Easily track agent versions, costs, and decision-making processes to ensure everything stays on track.
