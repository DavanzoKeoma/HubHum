### What it does in plain English
Stats sits quietly in your Mac's menu bar, giving you a quick look at how your computer is working. It shows you if your processor is busy, how much memory is being used, and how much battery life you have left. This helps you spot problems early, like when your fan starts spinning loudly or your internet connection slows down.

### Who it's for
*   **Everyday Mac users** who want to know why their computer feels slow or hot without opening complex settings.
*   **Remote workers** who need to keep an eye on battery life and network speed while working from cafes or home offices.
*   **Tech enthusiasts** who like to see detailed information about their hardware, such as temperature sensors and fan speeds.

### Key features
*   **Real-time performance view:** See CPU, GPU, and memory usage at a glance in the menu bar.
*   **Battery and power insights:** Monitor battery level, charging status, and power consumption to extend your Mac's life.
*   **Network details:** Track your internet speed, Wi-Fi signal strength, and public IP address.
*   **Hardware health:** View temperature sensors and fan speeds to ensure your computer isn't overheating.
*   **Customizable widgets:** Choose which data points you want to see and hide the ones you don't care about.

*   **Pros:** Completely free, very lightweight on system resources, and offers a clean, easy-to-read interface.
*   **Cons:** Only available for macOS (not Windows or Linux), and some advanced features like fan control are no longer actively updated.
