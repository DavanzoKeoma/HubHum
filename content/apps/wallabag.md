### What it does in plain English
wallabag lets you save web pages and articles with a single click so you can read them whenever you have free time. It automatically strips away distracting advertisements, menus, and pop-ups, leaving only the clean article text. You can organize your saved content with tags and categories to keep your reading library neat and searchable.

### Who it's for
* Avid readers who want to archive interesting web content before it disappears or gets paywalled.
* Researchers and students collecting reference materials from across the internet into one searchable place.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer keeping their personal reading habits and bookmarks on their own private hardware.

### Key features
* Distraction-free article extraction that removes pop-ups, sidebars, and ads automatically.
* Tagging and categorization system to easily sort and search through your saved reading archive.
* Companion mobile apps and browser extensions for saving content on the go.
* Self-hosted architecture ensuring complete control and ownership over your personal data.
* Open export formats so you can back up or move your reading list at any time.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, complete ownership of your reading data, clean reading mode.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge and a web server to set up on your own hardware.
