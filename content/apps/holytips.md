### What it does in plain English
HolyTips acts as a digital notebook for people who check websites for safety issues. It provides ready-made lists and guides that show you exactly what to look for when testing a site. This saves you time by removing the guesswork from finding common security mistakes.

### Who it's for
*   **Curious learners:** People who want to understand how websites can be protected without needing a computer science degree.
*   **Freelance testers:** Individuals who check websites for clients and need a consistent way to document their findings.
*   **Developers:** Builders who want to see how attackers think so they can write safer code from the start.

### Key features
*   **Practical Checklists:** Step-by-step lists that guide you through testing different parts of a website.
*   **Real-World Examples:** Written accounts of actual security issues found in the past, explaining how they were discovered.
*   **Organized Notes:** A structured collection of tips that makes it easy to find specific information quickly.
*   **Open Access:** All content is freely available to read and use for personal or professional learning.

### How to install (Download)
This is a web-based resource, so there is no software to install. You can read the full collection directly in your browser at the official source.

*   [View the Collection](https://github.com/HolyBugx/HolyTips)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to access, constantly updated with new findings, and written in clear, understandable language.
*   **Cons:** It is a reference guide rather than an automated tool, so you must perform the tests manually.
