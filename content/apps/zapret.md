### What it does in plain English
Zapret is a versatile system tool that helps you bypass rigid network filters, deep packet inspection, and website slowdowns. It works locally by modifying and segmenting your outgoing data packets so network filtering systems cannot recognize or block your traffic. Because it runs independently on your own hardware, you do not need to rely on external proxy servers or subscription-based tunneling services.

### Who it's for
* Home lab enthusiasts and power users running custom router firmware who want a local network-wide solution.
* Individuals experiencing arbitrary website blocking, service censorship, or connection throttling by their internet provider.
* Tech-savvy users looking for a lightweight, highly configurable background utility for Linux, Windows, or BSD systems.

### Key features
* Operates locally on your device or home router without requiring remote server connections.
* Employs advanced packet splitting, desynchronization, and header modification tactics.
* Supports automatic domain filtering and custom host lists to target specific restricted websites.
* Compatible with low-power embedded network hardware and standard desktop operating systems.
* Lightweight design ensures minimal impact on everyday system performance and memory usage.
