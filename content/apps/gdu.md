### What it does in plain English
Gdu scans your computer storage devices at high speed to show you exactly which files and folders are consuming the most room. It presents this information either in a friendly text-based window or directly inside your web browser. You can easily navigate through your directories, clean out old caches, and safely delete unwanted data without hassle.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users looking to quickly clear out space on a full laptop or desktop.
* System administrators and power users managing crowded server storage or multiple local drives.
* Developers needing a lightweight tool to clean up heavy build folders and temporary caches.

### Key features
* Blazing fast scanning performance optimized for modern solid-state drives.
* Interactive navigation letting you browse, sort, and manage large folder trees easily.
* Optional web browser interface featuring visual charts and clean directory maps.
* Smart cleanup suggestions to help identify safe files and old caches you can remove.
* Safe deletion options with built-in confirmation dialogues and background processing.
