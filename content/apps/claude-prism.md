### What it does in plain English
ClaudePrism is a desktop application designed for writing academic papers, research articles, and theses completely on your local computer. It combines a fast local text compiler with a built-in Python environment and an intelligent writing assistant. Your files remain safely stored on your hard drive while you benefit from automated references, live document previews, and smart editing suggestions.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and university students writing complex LaTeX papers, theses, or grant proposals.
* Data scientists and analysts who need to run Python scripts, generate plots, and draft reports in one place.
* Academics looking for a private, offline-capable alternative to cloud-based document editors.

### Key features
* **Offline LaTeX Compilation:** Embedded compiler handles your formatting and references locally without requiring heavy external software packages.
* **Built-in Python Tools:** Integrated package manager sets up a project virtual environment with a single click for data analysis and chart generation.
* **Advanced AI Assistant:** Chat directly with intelligent models to draft sections, summarize references, and suggest improvements.
* **Visual Document History:** Built-in local version control lets you review changes, track progress, and restore previous snapshots easily.
* **Interactive PDF Viewer:** Live document rendering with instant synchronization between your source text and the final layout.
