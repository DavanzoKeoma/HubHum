### What it does in plain English
This tool turns your computer into a smart sensor that detects exactly which room you are in. By analyzing the unique patterns of nearby Wi-Fi signals, it learns to distinguish between different locations like your kitchen, bedroom, or living room. Once trained, your computer can automatically identify your location and trigger other tasks based on where you are.

### Who it's for
* Home automation enthusiasts who want their smart devices to react based on which room they are currently in.
* People who want to log their time spent in different areas of their home or office for productivity tracking.

### Key features
* **High Precision:** Capable of distinguishing between locations as close as two meters apart.
* **Smart Learning:** Uses intelligent pattern recognition to improve accuracy over time as you visit different spots.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works reliably on Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.
* **Flexible Integration:** Can be easily connected to other software to automate lights, sounds, or notifications based on your location.

### How to install (Download)
This tool is installed via the Python package manager. Open your computer's command terminal and enter the following command:
`pip install whereami`

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly accurate for indoor spaces, and does not require external hardware sensors.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with command-line tools to set up and train your locations.
