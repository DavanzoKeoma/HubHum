### What it does in plain English
LibreTube is a lightweight mobile app designed to let you enjoy online videos without the usual data collection and targeted advertising. It connects directly to video sources while masking your personal information and browsing habits. Enjoy modern features like background listening and ad blocking built right into a clean, easy-to-use interface.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious smartphone users who want to avoid invasive online tracking and targeted profiling.
* People looking for a streamlined, modern video player with built-in ad-skipping and background playback tools.
* Users who prefer an open-source alternative to official media streaming applications.

### Key features
* Complete ad blocking and tracking protection during video playback.
* Support for background audio playback and picture-in-picture mode.
* Integration with community-driven tools to skip sponsor segments and view accurate dislike ratings.
* Subscription management and custom playlist organization.
* Modern, fluid design that adapts to your mobile device seamlessly.
