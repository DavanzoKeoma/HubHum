### What it does in plain English
This tool brings a touch of joy to your computer screen by showing a moving, colorful bird. It works directly inside the text-based window you use to type commands, known as the terminal. You do not need to install any software; you just type a short web address to see the animation start immediately.

### Who it's for
* **Curious tech enthusiasts** who enjoy discovering hidden easter eggs and fun tools within their operating systems.
* **Developers and students** who spend long hours in command-line interfaces and want a small break or a smile during their work.
* **Casual users** who are experimenting with basic computer commands and want to see a visual result without complex setup.

### Key features
* **Instant access:** The animation appears as soon as you run the command, with no waiting for downloads or installations.
* **Zero installation:** It runs entirely through your existing internet connection and command-line tool, keeping your computer clean of extra files.
* **Cross-platform compatibility:** It works on most major computer systems, including Windows, Mac, and Linux, as long as you have a basic command-line tool available.
* **Simple usage:** The entire process requires typing only one short line of text, making it accessible even to beginners.
