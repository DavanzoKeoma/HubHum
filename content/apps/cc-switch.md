### What it does in plain English
CC Switch acts as a central control center for managing multiple artificial intelligence coding assistants and chat applications. Instead of manually editing complicated text configuration files every time you want to change providers, you simply pick your preferred service from a clean visual list. It lets you test different AI models, keep track of your usage tokens, and organize helpful background extensions all in one place.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and technical hobbyists who juggle multiple artificial intelligence coding tools on a daily basis.
* Power users looking to easily compare responses and costs across various model providers without rewriting setup files.
* Anyone who wants a streamlined way to manage helper plugins, custom instructions, and conversation history across different desktop programs.

### Key features
* One-click switching between dozens of popular artificial intelligence service presets and custom endpoints.
* Centralized dashboard for organizing prompt templates, background extensions, and helper tools.
* Local connection routing that converts formats so you can run models from one provider inside another tool.
* Built-in session reading view that turns raw conversation logs into clean, easy-to-read text histories.
* Usage and token tracking dashboards that display your remaining quotas and spending metrics at a glance.
