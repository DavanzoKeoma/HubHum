### What it does in plain English
This tool helps you manage your cloud service login credentials without leaving them exposed on your hard drive. It stores your sensitive information in your computer's native secure storage system. When you need to work, it creates temporary, short-lived access keys that expire automatically, keeping your account safer from unauthorized use.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who need to manage multiple cloud accounts securely.
* System administrators who want to follow best practices for protecting access keys.

### Key features
* **Secure Storage:** Uses your operating system's built-in security features to lock away your credentials.
* **Temporary Access:** Generates short-lived keys that expire, reducing the risk of permanent credential leaks.
* **Easy Organization:** Allows you to switch between different profiles and accounts with simple commands.
* **Multi-Factor Support:** Works seamlessly with your existing security devices to ensure an extra layer of protection.
