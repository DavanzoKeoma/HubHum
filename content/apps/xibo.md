### What it does in plain English
Xibo allows you to create, schedule, and manage digital content for screens, such as menu boards, information displays, or advertising panels. It acts as a central hub where you can organize your media and decide exactly when and where it appears on your connected displays. The platform is designed to handle everything from a single screen to large networks of displays across multiple locations.

### Who it's for
* Business owners who need to manage store-front or office information screens.
* IT administrators looking for a reliable way to control multiple displays from one central location.

### Key features
* **Centralized Control:** Manage all your screens and content from a single, easy-to-use dashboard.
* **Flexible Scheduling:** Plan your content cycles in advance to ensure the right message appears at the right time.
* **Wide Compatibility:** Works with various screen types and hardware setups to fit your existing equipment.
* **Scalable Design:** Whether you have one screen or hundreds, the system grows with your needs.
* **Media Support:** Easily display images, videos, and custom layouts to keep your audience engaged.
