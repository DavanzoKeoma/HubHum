### What it does in plain English
NS-USBloader acts as a bridge between your computer and your gaming console, allowing you to transfer game files over a USB cable or local network. It also includes built-in tools to split massive files into smaller chunks or merge them back together when needed. Additionally, it features a specialized tool to send startup payloads directly to your device.

### Who it's for
* Console owners who want a straightforward desktop application to transfer game files from their computer.
* Users who need to manage or split oversized game archives that exceed standard storage limits.
* Enthusiasts looking for a reliable utility to send system recovery or startup payloads via USB.

### Key features
* Simple graphical interface to select, queue, and transfer multiple game files at once.
* Built-in file splitter and merger for handling oversized archives effortlessly.
* Support for both USB cable and wireless local network file transfers.
* Dedicated recovery mode payload injector compatible with multiple operating systems.
* Lightweight design with multi-language support and built-in dark or light themes.
