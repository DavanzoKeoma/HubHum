### What it does in plain English
ModStartCMS is a flexible website creation platform designed to help you launch blogs, member portals, and business pages without starting from scratch. It features a rich marketplace of pre-built add-ons that you can install or remove with a single click in your control panel. Developers and site owners can easily manage users, upload large files with progress bars, and structure their content cleanly.

### Who it's for
* **Web Developers:** Looking for a solid, modular foundation to build custom client websites and business applications faster.
* **Small Business Owners:** Needing an all-in-one platform to run a company blog, customer portal, or content site without complex coding.
* **Content Creators:** Wanting a reliable system to publish articles, manage media libraries, and interact with online subscribers.

### Key features
* **Modular Add-On Marketplace:** Easily browse and install extra features like forums, blogs, and video courses with one click.
* **Flexible Administration:** Control user permissions securely with a built-in role management system that works on phones, tablets, and computers.
* **Cloud Storage Ready:** Connect popular cloud storage providers seamlessly to handle large file uploads and media management.
* **Third-Party Login and Pay:** Integrate common social logins and secure payment gateways right out of the box.
* **Developer Friendly:** Speed up custom feature creation with built-in data tables, form generators, and automatic tools.
