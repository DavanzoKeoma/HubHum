### What it does in plain English
Notable is a note-taking tool that lets you write and organize your thoughts using simple text formatting. Because your notes are saved as standard files directly on your computer, you are never locked into a specific program or subscription service. It provides a clean, focused environment for writing while keeping your data portable and easy to back up.

### Who it's for
* Writers and students who want a distraction-free space to organize research and ideas.
* People who value data privacy and want to keep their notes stored as plain files on their own hard drive.

### Key features
* **Markdown Support:** Write using standard text formatting, including support for math formulas and diagrams.
* **No Vendor Lock-in:** Your notes are just files on your computer, meaning you can open them with any other text editor at any time.
* **Zen Mode:** A minimalist view that hides all buttons and menus so you can focus entirely on your writing.
* **Powerful Search:** Quickly find any note or perform bulk actions across your entire collection.
* **Split Editor:** View your formatted notes side-by-side with your raw text while you work.
