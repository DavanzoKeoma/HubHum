### What it does in plain English
Subtitle Translator is a privacy-first application that handles batch subtitle translation for video creators, fansubbers, and media enthusiasts. It automatically extracts dialogue text from standard subtitle formats while keeping all timecodes completely untouched locally in your browser. You can drop hundreds of files at once, choose from dozens of artificial intelligence engines or traditional translation services, and export fully translated files in seconds.

### Who it's for
* **Video Creators and Editors:** Quickly localize video content into multiple languages for international audiences without manual text splitting.
* **Fansub Groups:** Translate entire seasons of television shows in a single pass while preserving complex styling and precise timing.
* **Language Learners:** Generate dual-language subtitle files to study foreign media alongside native translations.

### Key features
* **Whole Season Batching:** Drop hundreds of subtitle files at once and have them translate and download independently in moments.
* **Timeline Protection:** Local parsing ensures timecodes, cue numbers, and structural tags are never altered by the translation engine.
* **Multi-Language Export:** Translate into multiple target languages simultaneously, automatically appending language codes to output files.
* **Format Versatility:** Automatically detects, converts, and formats popular subtitle types like SRT, ASS, VTT, and LRC.
* **Client-Side Privacy:** Your personal translation keys and dialogue content stay securely in your browser storage.
