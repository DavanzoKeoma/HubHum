### What it does in plain English
JS Paint brings the beloved retro drawing experience right into your web browser and desktop. It recreates every classic tool, menu, and hidden feature of old Windows paint programs with absolute pixel perfection. On top of that, it adds modern conveniences like transparency support, infinite undo history, and helpful accessibility features.

### Who it's for
* Pixel art enthusiasts who want a lightweight, nostalgic digital canvas
* Educators and students looking for an easy, distraction-free drawing tool
* Anyone who misses the straightforward, reliable interface of classic operating system paint programs

### Key features
* Pixel-perfect recreation of every classic drawing tool and vintage color palette
* Full support for editing transparent images and cropping with precision
* Nonlinear undo history tree allowing you to jump back to any previous editing step
* Modern web integrations including URL image loading, Imgur uploading, and animated GIF creation
* Accessibility controls like voice recognition commands and automatic dwell clicking
