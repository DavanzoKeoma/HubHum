### What it does in plain English
VPNHotspot lets you take the secure internet connection from your phone's VPN and broadcast it to other devices nearby. It is perfect for connecting gadgets that do not have their own VPN settings, such as streaming sticks or smart home devices. This tool effectively turns your phone into a secure gateway for your entire local network.

### Who it's for
* Users who need to connect devices like smart TVs or game consoles to a VPN but cannot install software on those devices.
* People who want to bypass mobile carrier tethering restrictions while keeping their traffic secure.

### Key features
* **VPN Sharing:** Easily share an active VPN tunnel over your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot.
* **Repeater Mode:** Extend your Wi-Fi range while maintaining your VPN connection.
* **Traffic Control:** Identify, monitor, and block specific devices connected to your hotspot.
* **Bypass Restrictions:** Helps get around tethering limits imposed by mobile service providers.
* **Advanced Customization:** Offers deep control over network settings for power users.
