### What it does in plain English
Doxx lets you open, read, and search through Microsoft Word documents directly inside your command line window. It renders text, formatting, lists, and tables cleanly without requiring any bulky office suite installed on your computer. You can also quickly convert documents into other formats or copy content with simple keyboard shortcuts.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and system administrators who prefer living inside the terminal.
* Writers and researchers who need to quickly preview text, tables, or equations in document files without delays.
* Power users looking for a lightweight tool to extract data from office files into markdown, CSV, or JSON formats.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast startup that displays documents instantly compared to traditional office applications.
* Smart table rendering with proper alignment and clean borders.
* Built-in search functionality to find text matches quickly with clear highlighting.
* Support for LaTeX equations, lists, and custom color schemes.
* Versatile export options to convert documents into Markdown, plain text, CSV, JSON, or colored output.
