### What it does in plain English
Sharry is a private web space that lets you share large files with anyone by sending them a simple link. You can set expiration dates and passwords on your shared files to keep them secure. It also lets people securely send files back to you using dedicated collection pages without needing a password.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and small teams who want to share large project files with clients securely without relying on third-party storage companies.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer keeping all their shared data on their own personal servers.
* Anyone who needs a reliable way to receive files from external collaborators without complicated setup steps.

### Key features
* **Secure File Links:** Upload files and instantly generate hard-to-guess web links to share via email or chat.
* **Expiration Controls:** Automatically delete shared files after a specific time period for better privacy.
* **Incoming Collection Pages:** Create private alias pages that allow trusted visitors to upload files directly to your account.
* **Resilient Uploads:** Resume interrupted transfers automatically if your internet connection drops mid-upload.
* **Password Protection:** Add optional passwords to download pages for an extra layer of security.

* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to host and maintain a personal web server.
