### What it does in plain English
Supersonic acts as a dedicated home for your music, connecting directly to your personal media servers to play your files. It provides a smooth, clutter-free interface that lets you browse your albums, artists, and playlists without relying on big-tech streaming services. You can enjoy high-quality audio playback and manage your entire collection from one simple, responsive application.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who host their own digital audio libraries at home.
* People who want a dedicated, lightweight desktop player for services like Jellyfin or Navidrome.

### Key features
* **Seamless Streaming:** Connects to your own music servers to play your library with gapless audio.
* **Customizable Look:** Includes both light and dark themes to match your desktop setup.
* **Smart Controls:** Works with your keyboard's media keys and supports advanced audio settings like a 15-band equalizer.
* **Flexible Browsing:** Easily sort your tracks, filter your albums, and view lyrics while you listen.
* **Portable Access:** Allows you to download your favorite songs or playlists for listening when you are away from your server.
