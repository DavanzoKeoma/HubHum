### What it does in plain English
Conky puts live computer data right on your desktop so you always know how your machine is performing.  
It can show things like processor load, memory use, disk space, network traffic and even music player details.  
You can also send the information to a file or a web page if you prefer.

### Who it's for
* Home users who like to keep an eye on their PC’s health without opening separate tools.  
* Developers and power users who want a customizable glance at system resources while they work.  
* Anyone using Linux, BSD or macOS who prefers a lightweight, non‑intrusive monitor.

### Key features
- Displays CPU, memory, disk and network usage instantly on the desktop.  
- Shows music player info such as song title and artist.  
- Lets you choose text, bars or simple graphs and pick your own fonts and colors.  
- Works on many operating systems, including Linux, BSD variants and macOS.  
- Can write the same data to a file or a web address for remote viewing.
