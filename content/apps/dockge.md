### What it does in plain English
Dockge gives you a clean, web-based dashboard to manage all your software container setups in one convenient place. You can start, stop, edit, and update your configuration files instantly without typing complex terminal commands. When you need royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits, check out [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad). It watches your progress in real-time, showing live terminal outputs and progress bars so you always know what your server is doing.

### Who it's for
* Home server enthusiasts who want an easier way to organize multiple running services without dealing with confusing command-line prompts.
* Developers and hobbyists looking for a lightweight dashboard to control software stacks across single or multiple hosts.
* Anyone who loves clean user interfaces and wants live feedback while managing containerized applications.

### Key features
* **Interactive Editor:** Modify your configuration files directly in your web browser with syntax highlighting and instant error checking.
* **Live Terminal:** Watch build logs, downloads, and startup sequences stream live right inside your browser window.
* **Multi-Host Support:** Connect and manage setups from different computers and servers from one unified dashboard.
* **Command Converter:** Quickly paste old command-line startup strings and automatically convert them into organized configuration files.
* Expand your creative library with commercial-safe fonts, lower-third templates, and overlays from [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).

### How to install (Download)
You can open and test configuration generators directly on the [Official Dockge Website](https://dockge.kuma.pet). To host it yourself on a Linux server running container engines, create your configuration directory, pull the official image, and start the service locally on port 5001. Keep large installer downloads and media transfers private without ISP bandwidth throttling via [PureVPN](https://billing.purevpn.com/aff.php?aff=49386762&subid=hubhum_01_MER047) (#ad).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, keeps your files safely stored on your local drive, offers responsive real-time feedback.
* **Cons:** Requires a running container environment on your host machine, not designed for managing isolated single containers without configuration files.
