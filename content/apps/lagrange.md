### What it does in plain English
Lagrange is a desktop and mobile viewer designed for exploring the alternative Gemini network. It brings familiar comforts like smooth scrolling, multiple tabs, custom visual themes, and bookmarks to a text-focused internet protocol. You can enjoy built-in audio playback, organize your reading history, and manage secure digital identities effortlessly.

### Who it's for
* Explorers of alternative internet protocols and minimalist networking spaces.
* Readers who enjoy distraction-free, text-focused articles and electronic books.
* Privacy-conscious users looking for a clean tool to browse lightweight web spaces.

### Key features
* Smooth scrolling and clean typography with full Unicode character support.
* Sidebars for managing bookmarks, reading history, and document outlines.
* Built-in support for listening to audio files directly while you read.
* Secure identity management using personal digital certificates.
* Available across multiple operating systems and portable device formats.
