### What it does in plain English
Subrion helps you build and manage websites of any size, whether you need a personal blog or a large company portal. It runs on your own web server, giving you full control over your content and design without monthly software fees. This free tool handles everything from writing articles to managing user accounts, making it a versatile choice for independent web creators.

### Who it's for
*   **Independent Bloggers:** Individuals who want a free, customizable platform to share their writing and media without relying on commercial blogging services.
*   **Small Business Owners:** Entrepreneurs seeking a cost-effective way to maintain a professional online presence with full ownership of their data.
*   **Web Enthusiasts:** Tech-savvy users who prefer open-source solutions and want the flexibility to customize their website's structure and features.

### Key features
*   **Versatile Website Creation:** Supports a wide range of site types, from simple personal blogs to complex corporate portals with multiple sections.
*   **Free and Open Source:** Completely free to use with no hidden subscription fees, distributed under the GPL v3 license for transparency.
*   **User-Friendly Management:** Includes intuitive tools for writing content, managing media files, and handling user permissions.
*   **Customizable Design:** Allows you to tailor the look and feel of your site to match your brand or personal style.
*   **Community Support:** Backed by an active community and regular updates to ensure security and new features.

*   **Pros:** Completely free forever, no subscription costs, highly flexible for different website types, and backed by an open-source community.
*   **Cons:** Requires a web server with PHP and MySQL to run, which may involve some technical setup if you are not familiar with web hosting.
