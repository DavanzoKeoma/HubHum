### What it does in plain English
Captura is a user-friendly desktop application that lets you record everything happening on your screen. You can capture full displays, specific windows, or customized regions while simultaneously recording audio from your microphone or speakers. It also features helpful visual indicators like tracking mouse clicks, keystrokes, and webcam feeds to make tutorials and presentations much clearer.

### Who it's for
* Educators and teachers creating instructional video tutorials or software walkthroughs.
* Content creators and gamers recording gameplay footage or desktop demonstrations.
* Remote professionals recording virtual meetings or software bug reports.

### Key features
* Record full desktop screens, active windows, or custom selected areas.
* Capture audio from both microphones and system speaker output simultaneously.
* Overlay mouse cursor movements, clicks, and keyboard strokes onto recordings.
* Save recordings directly into popular video or animated image formats.
* Configure quick keyboard shortcuts to start, pause, and stop captures instantly.
