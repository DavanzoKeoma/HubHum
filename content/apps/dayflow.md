### What it does in plain English
Dayflow runs quietly in the background on your Mac and watches what appears on your screen. It turns those moments into easy‑to‑read cards that show exactly what you worked on. You end up with a private timeline you can read anytime without writing notes or starting timers.

### Who it's for
- Professionals who want a hassle‑free record of their daily work.
- Freelancers who need quick stand‑up updates without manual tracking.
- Anyone who prefers their activity data to stay on their own computer.

### Key features
- Automatically captures short screen snippets and creates readable activity cards.  
- Summarizes the real work you were doing, not just the app that was open.  
- Provides a daily overview you can copy for stand‑up meetings.  
- Lets you ask simple questions about your day and receive answers from your own journal.  
- Shows weekly charts that reveal focus patterns and distraction periods.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, data stays on your Mac, works offline with local AI, no subscription required.  
* **Cons:** only available for macOS, needs screen‑recording permission, AI analysis may require extra setup.
