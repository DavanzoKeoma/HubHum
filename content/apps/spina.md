### What it does in plain English
Spina is a content management system designed to help you organize and publish website material without unnecessary clutter. It offers a clean, professional workspace that lets you focus entirely on your writing and page structure. By keeping the interface simple, it ensures that managing your site remains an intuitive and efficient task.

### Who it's for
* Website owners who want a minimalist and distraction-free editing environment.
* Developers looking for a lightweight, flexible content management solution for their projects.

### Key features
* **Distraction-free interface:** A clean design that prioritizes your content over complex menus.
* **Flexible structure:** Does not force a specific design style, giving you full control over your site's appearance.
* **User-friendly setup:** Includes an interactive installer to help you get your first user account running quickly.
* **Modern standards:** Built to work seamlessly with current web technologies and modern browser standards.

### How to install (Download)
Spina is designed to be integrated into a Ruby on Rails project. To get started, visit the [Official Documentation](https://www.spinacms.com/docs) for the full setup guide. You can add it to your project by including `gem 'spina'` in your Gemfile and running the command `rails spina:install`.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly customizable, and features an exceptionally clean user interface.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of web project setup and the Ruby on Rails environment to install.
