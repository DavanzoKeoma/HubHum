### What it does in plain English
Handy is a desktop tool that turns your spoken words into typed text inside any application instantly. You simply press a keyboard shortcut, talk into your microphone, and your speech appears right where your cursor is resting. Because it processes everything locally on your computer, no audio recordings ever get sent to external servers or the cloud.

### Who it's for
* Writers and professionals looking to speed up daily document drafting and email composition.
* Privacy-conscious users who want speech transcription without sharing voice data with external web services.
* People who prefer using accessibility tools that run completely offline without hidden subscription fees.

### Key features
* **Complete Offline Privacy:** Processes all speech locally on your computer without transmitting audio data to the internet.
* **Universal Text Entry:** Automatically pastes your transcribed words directly into whatever active text field you are using.
* **Custom Shortcuts:** Easily configure your preferred keyboard hotkeys to start, pause, or stop dictation.
* **Flexible Voice Models:** Choose from multiple local transcription engines tailored for speed and performance.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Works smoothly across Windows, macOS, and Linux operating systems.
