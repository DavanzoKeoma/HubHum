### What it does in plain English
Miniserve gives you a lightning-fast way to send files or entire folders from your computer to anyone else on your network or the internet. Instead of setting up complex web servers, you just download a single tiny file, point it at what you want to share, and receive a web link. It handles all the behind-the-scenes details automatically so you can start sharing right away.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who need to quickly transfer a large folder between computers without plugging in a USB drive.
* Remote workers or students who want to share documents or media privately with colleagues or friends over a local network.
* Developers and testers who need a temporary web server to check out web pages or mobile uploads on the fly.

### Key features
* Instantly serves any file or folder on your computer through your web browser.
* Requires no complicated installation or extra software dependencies.
* Supports optional password protection to keep your shared files private.
* Allows trusted visitors to upload files or create new folders right from their browsers.
* Automatically compresses folders into zip or tar archives for easy downloading.
