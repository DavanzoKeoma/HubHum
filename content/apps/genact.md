### What it does in plain English
Genact generates realistic-looking terminal activity that mimics complex computer processes. It is designed to make your screen look busy with scrolling code, loading bars, and technical logs. You can run it to create the appearance of intense productivity or to simply enjoy the aesthetic of a "hacker" workspace.

### Who it's for
* Office workers who want to look busy during long meetings.
* Enthusiasts who enjoy the visual style of classic terminal-based computing.

### Key features
* **Multiple Scenes:** Includes various fake activities like simulated code compilation, network scanning, and data processing.
* **Customizable Speed:** Adjust how fast the fake tasks appear to run on your screen.
* **Lightweight:** Runs instantly without needing to be installed or configured.
* **Web-Ready:** Can be used directly in a web browser without downloading any files.
