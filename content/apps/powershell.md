### What it does in plain English
PowerShell is an advanced command-line tool that lets you talk directly to your computer using simple text commands instead of clicking through endless menus. It is designed to take over boring, repetitive computer tasks so you do not have to do them by hand. * System administrators who need to manage multiple computers and server networks efficiently.
