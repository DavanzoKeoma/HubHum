### What it does in plain English
YTLite transforms the official YouTube app on iOS by adding over a hundred customization features and quality-of-life enhancements. It lets users download videos, audio tracks, and thumbnails directly to their devices. Additionally, it integrates community-driven tools to block interruptions, unlock higher video resolutions, and customize the viewing interface.

### Who it's for
* iPhone and iPad users who want advanced control over their video viewing experience.
* Content enthusiasts looking to save videos and audio tracks for offline viewing.
* Viewers who prefer an uncluttered interface without distracting promotional elements.

### Key features
* Download videos, audio streams, thumbnails, and profile pictures directly.
* Built-in SponsorBlock to automatically skip promotional segments.
* Unlock higher video resolutions and native picture-in-picture mode.
* Clean up the interface by hiding unwanted feed elements or tabs.
* Save, load, and back up your custom preferences easily.
