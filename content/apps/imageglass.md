### What it does in plain English
ImageGlass is a modern photo viewer designed to replace slower, built-in system tools with a much faster and more responsive experience. It handles over 90 different file types, including common photos, animated files, and professional design formats, without lag. You can easily organize, rotate, resize, and view your images using a clean interface that you can customize to fit your personal workflow.

### Who it's for
* **Everyday users** who want a faster, more reliable way to open and browse photos on their computer.
* **Designers and photographers** who need to quickly preview specialized or high-quality image formats without opening heavy editing software.

### Key features
* **Wide Compatibility:** Opens over 90 image formats, including modern web formats and professional raw files.
* **High Performance:** Uses smart technology to load images instantly, even when browsing large folders.
* **Customizable Interface:** Change themes, layouts, and keyboard shortcuts to make the app work exactly how you want.
* **Built-in Tools:** Quickly perform basic tasks like cropping, rotating, resizing, or picking colors directly in the viewer.
* **Animated Support:** Smoothly plays animated files like GIFs and web-based animations.
