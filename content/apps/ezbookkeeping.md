### What it does in plain English
ezBookkeeping is a private, self-hosted tool designed to help you manage your personal finances with ease. It allows you to record your daily transactions, import data from various financial formats, and analyze your spending patterns through clear, customizable charts. Because you host it yourself, your financial data remains entirely under your control and away from third-party services.

### Who it's for
*   Individuals who want to track their personal spending without relying on cloud-based services that store their data.
*   Home server enthusiasts who want a lightweight, efficient tool to manage their budget on devices like a Raspberry Pi or a personal NAS.

### Key features
*   **Total Privacy:** Keep your financial records on your own hardware, ensuring your data is never shared with outside companies.
*   **Smart Analysis:** Visualize your financial health with built-in charts and custom queries that help you understand where your money goes.
*   **Flexible Data Handling:** Easily import your existing records from Excel, CSV, and many other financial software formats.
*   **Mobile-Friendly:** Use the app on your phone just like a native application, thanks to its responsive design and mobile-optimized interface.
*   **AI-Assisted:** Utilize modern features like receipt image recognition and AI-generated charts to make bookkeeping faster and more insightful.

### How to install (Download)
You can run ezBookkeeping on your own hardware using Docker or by downloading the latest version for your operating system.
*   **For Docker users:** [Visit the official Docker Hub page](https://hub.docker.com/r/mayswind/ezbookkeeping) for setup instructions.
*   **For Desktop/Server binaries:** [Download the latest release here](https://github.com/mayswind/ezbookkeeping/releases).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open source, respects your privacy, highly customizable, and runs on low-power hardware.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of how to set up and maintain your own server software.
