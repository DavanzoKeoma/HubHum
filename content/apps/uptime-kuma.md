### What it does in plain English
Uptime Kuma is a friendly, self-hosted dashboard that checks whether your websites, servers, and online services are running smoothly. If a service goes down or experiences issues, it immediately sends alerts to your favorite communication apps. It features a fast, reactive interface that gives you a clear visual overview of your entire digital infrastructure at a glance, while keeping admin dashboards secure on remote networks using [PureVPN](https://billing.purevpn.com/aff.php?aff=49386762&subid=hubhum_01_MER047) (#ad).

### Who it's for
- Website administrators and small business owners who need to know instantly if their online store or portal goes offline.
- Home lab enthusiasts and developers managing multiple private servers, containers, and web applications.

### Key features
- Tracks availability for standard websites, secure web links, local network devices, and game servers.
- Sends instant alert notifications through dozens of popular chat and messaging platforms.
- Provides gorgeous, public-facing status pages that you can map directly to custom web addresses.
- Displays helpful response time charts and digital certificate expiration warnings.
- Once your web portal is live, improve search indexing and rich previews with [Rank Math Direct](https://rankmath.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER276) (#ad).
