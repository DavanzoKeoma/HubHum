### What it does in plain English
Vibe listens to your audio or video files and writes down everything that is said into a readable text document. Because it runs entirely on your own computer, your private recordings never leave your device or get uploaded to a cloud server. It supports many different languages and can even summarize long recordings or translate them into English for you.

### Who it's for
* Students or journalists who need to quickly turn recorded interviews or lectures into written notes.
* Content creators who want to generate accurate subtitles or transcripts for their videos without paying for monthly services.

### Key features
* **Total Privacy:** Everything happens offline on your machine, ensuring your data stays completely secure.
* **Versatile Formats:** Save your transcripts in common document types like text files, PDFs, or subtitle formats.
* **Batch Processing:** Easily transcribe multiple files at once to save time on large projects.
* **Smart Summaries:** Automatically generate quick summaries of your long transcripts to get the main points instantly.
* **Flexible Input:** Record directly from your microphone, capture system audio, or pull content from popular video websites.
