### What it does in plain English
Paseo acts as a command center for your digital coding assistants, allowing you to run several of them at the same time. It provides a unified workspace where you can edit files, review changes, and manage tasks across different devices. Because it works on both your computer and your phone, you can start a project at your desk and continue managing it while on the go.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use multiple AI coding tools and want to streamline their workflow.
* Tech-savvy individuals who want to manage complex projects across different devices without switching between multiple apps.

### Key features
* **Unified Control:** Run and coordinate multiple coding assistants simultaneously within a single, organized interface.
* **Cross-Device Sync:** Start your work on a desktop computer and pick up exactly where you left off using the mobile app.
* **Flexible Workspace:** Arrange your file editors, terminal windows, and task lists in split-screen views that suit your needs.
* **Voice Commands:** Use your voice to dictate tasks or discuss problems, keeping your hands free while you work.
* **Privacy Focused:** The app operates locally on your machine without tracking your activity or requiring forced logins.
