### What it does in plain English
NeriPlayer brings all your music together by connecting your local files, online streaming platforms, and personal music servers into one seamless interface. It handles everything from high-quality audio playback and synchronized lyrics to managing your personal music collections without relying on a central company server. You can enjoy a unified listening experience that keeps your data private while providing advanced features like custom sound settings and real-time listening sessions with friends.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want to manage local files and online streaming services within a single, clean app.
* Users who prefer self-hosting their music collections and want a reliable, privacy-focused way to access them on their Android devices.

### Key features
* **Unified Library:** Play music from local storage, online streaming services, and your own music servers in one queue.
* **Advanced Lyrics:** Supports multiple formats like LRC and YRC with features for translation, karaoke-style highlighting, and floating windows.
* **High-Fidelity Audio:** Includes a built-in equalizer, support for high-resolution audio output, and USB digital-to-analog converter compatibility.
* **Privacy-Focused Sync:** Keep your playlists and listening statistics synced across devices using your own private storage (GitHub or WebDAV).
* **Customizable Interface:** Choose between different visual themes and enjoy fluid, responsive backgrounds that adapt to your music.
