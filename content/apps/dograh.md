### What it does in plain English
Dograh provides a visual workspace where you can build, test, and launch AI-powered voice assistants that handle phone calls or customer inquiries. It allows you to create custom conversation flows, define how the AI should respond, and connect it to your own preferred voice and language services. Because it is open-source, you have full control over your data and can host the entire system on your own equipment.

### Who it's for
* **Small business owners** who want to automate customer support or lead qualification calls without relying on expensive, closed-off services.
* **Developers and hobbyists** who want to experiment with voice AI and build custom telephony workflows that they can fully own and modify.

### Key features
* **Visual Workflow Builder:** Design complex conversation paths and logic using a simple drag-and-drop interface.
* **Full Ownership:** Host the software yourself to ensure your data stays private and you are not locked into a single provider.
* **Flexible Integrations:** Connect your own preferred services for speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and phone connectivity.
* **Built-in Testing:** Use the integrated audio and chat tools to talk to your agent and refine its responses in real time.
* **AI-Assisted Setup:** Use modern coding assistants to help configure and troubleshoot your agent workflows through natural language.

### How to install (Download)
You can get started immediately by visiting the [Official Cloud Version](https://app.dograh.com) to try it without any setup. For those who prefer to host it themselves, follow the [Official Documentation](https://docs.dograh.com/deployment/docker) to run it on your own server or local machine.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely open-source, no vendor lock-in, highly customizable, and offers both cloud and self-hosted options.
* **Cons:** Self-hosting requires basic knowledge of server management, and advanced features may take some time to master.
