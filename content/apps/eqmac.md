### What it does in plain English
eqMac acts as a central hub for all the sound coming out of your Mac. You can tweak the bass, mids, and treble to make music sound richer or clearer. It also lets you set different volume levels for each app, so your music doesn't drown out your video calls.

### Who it's for
* **Music lovers:** People who want to fix the sound of their specific headphones or speakers without buying new gear.
* **Remote workers:** Users who need to balance the volume of their microphone, video calls, and background music simultaneously.
* **Casual Mac users:** Anyone who finds the standard Mac volume slider too limiting for their daily listening needs.

### Key features
* **System-wide Equalizer:** Adjust bass, mids, and treble for all audio playing on your computer at once.
* **Per-App Volume Control:** Set a specific volume level for each application, such as keeping your browser quiet while your music plays loud.
* **Volume Booster:** Push the audio output beyond the standard 100% limit for louder listening (use with caution).
* **AutoEQ Integration:** Automatically applies a sound profile based on your specific headphone model to correct for known audio flaws.
* **HDMI Support:** Control the volume and balance for audio sent to external displays or TVs via HDMI.
