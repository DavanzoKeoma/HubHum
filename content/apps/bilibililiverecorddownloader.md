### What it does in plain English
This tool watches your favorite Bilibili channels and starts recording the moment a stream goes live. It saves the video as a standard MP4 file so you can watch it later on any device. It also captures the live chat comments and turns them into subtitles for your recording.

### Who it's for
*   Fans of specific Bilibili creators who want to keep a personal archive of their favorite streams.
*   Content curators who need to save live events for offline viewing or editing.
*   Users who want to preserve the live chat experience alongside the video content.

### Key features
*   Automatically detects when a stream starts and begins recording without manual intervention.
*   Converts recorded streams into MP4 format for easy playback and sharing.
*   Captures live chat messages and generates subtitle files that can be burned into the video.
*   Supports recording multiple live streams at the same time.
*   Runs quietly in the background and can start automatically when your computer turns on.
