### What it does in plain English
palera1n allows users to gain deeper control over the internal software of specific older Apple phones, tablets, and computers. It works by unlocking restricted areas of the system that are usually locked by the manufacturer. This gives advanced users the freedom to customize their device experience in ways not normally supported.

### Who it's for
* Enthusiasts who want to customize the interface or functionality of older Apple devices.
* Users who own legacy hardware that is no longer receiving official software updates and want to extend its utility.

### Key features
* Supports a wide range of older iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV models.
* Compatible with Apple T2 security chip-based computers.
* Enables deeper system-level modifications for advanced personalization.
* Provides a command-line interface for precise control over the modification process.
