### What it does in plain English
Bangumi is an independent mobile companion designed to help you organize and track your entertainment progress across anime, manga, books, and games. It connects to popular community databases so you can discover trending titles, rate what you have watched, and read discussions. Built with a focus on speed and clarity, it offers a completely ad-free experience tailored specifically for phones and tablets.

### Who it's for
* Anime and manga fans who want a dedicated mobile tracker to log episodes and volumes on the go.
* Gamers and readers looking for a single organized space to keep tabs on their completed and upcoming titles.
* Community members who enjoy reading reviews, discussions, and ratings without annoying advertisements.

### Key features
* **Progress Tracking:** Easily check off watched episodes, read chapters, and completed milestones for all your favorite media.
* **Extensive Discovery:** Explore daily broadcast schedules, high-ranking charts, curated lists, and personalized recommendations.
* **Community Discussions:** Read user reviews, participate in forum topics, and explore character insights.
* **Custom Layouts:** Enjoy a mobile-first interface designed to make browsing smooth, responsive, and comfortable.
* **Ad-Free Experience:** Enjoy an entirely hobby-driven application free from commercial interruptions and tracking pop-ups.
