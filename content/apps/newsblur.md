### What it does in plain English
NewsBlur gathers all your favorite news sites, blogs, and email newsletters into one organized feed so you can read them in one place. It learns what you enjoy and what you don't, allowing you to highlight stories you like and hide the ones you want to skip. You can read content directly in the app or view the original website exactly as it was intended to be seen.

### Who it's for
*   **Information Junkies:** People who follow dozens of blogs and news sites and want a single, organized feed to stay updated.
*   **Newsletter Readers:** Anyone tired of digging through their email inbox to find their favorite recurring newsletters.

### Key features
*   **Personalized Filtering:** Train the app to hide topics or authors you aren't interested in and highlight the ones you love.
*   **Original View:** Easily toggle between a clean reading mode and the original website layout.
*   **Newsletter Support:** Bring your email newsletters into the reader so they don't get lost in your inbox.
*   **Cross-Platform Sync:** Your reading progress and saved stories stay in sync across your computer, phone, and tablet.
*   **Social Sharing:** Share interesting stories with friends or create your own public blog of curated content.
