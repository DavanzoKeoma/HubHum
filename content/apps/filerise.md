### What it does in plain English
FileRise lets you take complete control of your files by turning any private server or computer into a personal cloud storage hub. You can organize documents, upload large files with automatic pause and resume, and securely share links with friends or clients. It also allows you to mount your storage as a network drive and optionally encrypt sensitive folders so your data stays completely private.

### Who it's for
* Home lab enthusiasts and self-hosters looking for a clean, fast alternative to public cloud drives.
* Freelancers and small businesses needing a secure, brandable portal for client file uploads.
* Anyone who wants to access, search, and manage their documents from any web browser or network device.

### Key features
* Clean drag-and-drop file uploads with automatic pause and resume if your internet drops.
* Detailed folder permissions so you can precisely control who can view, edit, or delete files.
* Optional folder-level encryption that protects your sensitive files directly on the disk.
* Network drive support allowing you to mount your storage hub like a local folder on your computer.
* Built-in file preview tools for documents, images, and text editing directly in your web browser.
