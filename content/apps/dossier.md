### What it does in plain English
Dossier keeps your entire career history in one private profile that you control. When you find a job you like, it reads the posting and writes a custom resume and cover letter that highlight your relevant skills. You can then track every step of your application process, from the first draft to the final job offer.

### Who it's for
* Job seekers who want to keep their professional data private and organized.
* Professionals who are tired of manually rewriting their resume for every single job application.

### Key features
* **Tailored Documents:** Automatically drafts resumes and cover letters that match specific job requirements using only your verified facts.
* **Application Tracking:** A visual board to manage your applications from the initial draft through to the final offer.
* **Job Search Tools:** Search multiple job portals at once and save your favorite opportunities in one central dashboard.
* **Privacy Focused:** You can run it entirely on your own computer or server, ensuring your personal data never leaves your control.
* **Interactive Refinement:** Use a chat interface to tweak your documents or adjust the tone of your cover letters before sending.
