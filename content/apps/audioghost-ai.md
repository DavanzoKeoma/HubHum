### What it does in plain English
AudioGhost AI lets you pull individual sounds—like vocals, traffic, or a dog barking—out of any audio or video recording by simply typing what you want to hear. The application separates your file into distinct tracks so you can adjust volumes, mix elements, and export a clean soundtrack or video. It runs locally on your own computer graphics card without requiring online accounts or monthly subscriptions.

### Who it's for
* **Video creators and podcasters** who need to clean up background noise, isolate spoken dialogue, or remix soundtracks.
* **Musicians and producers** wanting to separate specific instruments or vocals from existing audio tracks.
* **Hobbyists and sound designers** looking to experiment with smart sound separation right on their desktop.

### Key features
* **Text-guided separation:** Describe any sound using everyday language to isolate it instantly.
* **Multi-track workspace:** Place multiple isolated sounds onto separate tracks alongside a background track.
* **Interactive audio mixer:** Mute, solo, adjust levels, or compare tracks to the original recording in real time.
* **Flexible exports:** Save your final mix as high-quality audio files or export updated videos directly.
* **Complete local privacy:** Operates directly on your hardware without storing your personal files on cloud servers.
