### What it does in plain English
LinkAce acts as a private library for the websites you visit, allowing you to store links so they don't get lost in your browser history. It automatically saves copies of your pages to ensure you can still view the content even if the original site goes offline. You can organize your collection with custom tags and lists, making it easy to find exactly what you need later.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who need to keep track of important articles and sources.
* Internet power users who want to build a permanent, searchable collection of their favorite online discoveries.

### Key features
* **Smart Organization:** Use tags and lists to keep your bookmarks tidy and easy to navigate.
* **Content Preservation:** Automatically archives saved websites so you can access them even if the original link disappears.
* **Link Monitoring:** Get notified if a saved website goes offline or moves to a new address.
* **Flexible Sharing:** Choose which collections to keep private and which to share with friends or colleagues.
* **Browser Integration:** Use a simple bookmark button in your browser to save pages instantly as you browse.

### How to install (Download)
LinkAce is designed to be hosted on your own server. You can find the complete setup instructions and installation methods on the [Official LinkAce Documentation Page](https://www.linkace.org/docs/v2/setup/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete control over your data, no subscription fees, and reliable long-term archiving.
* **Cons:** Requires your own server or hosting space to run, which may involve a small learning curve for beginners.
