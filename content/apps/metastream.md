### What it does in plain English
Metastream lets you and your friends watch online videos together from different locations. It keeps everyone's video playback perfectly synchronized so you never miss a moment. You can chat in real time, build shared video queues, and manage who controls the playback.

### Who it's for
* Long-distance friends and family members who want to watch movies and shows together online.
* Online gaming groups looking to enjoy video watch parties during breaks.
* Content enthusiasts who want a shared viewing experience with live discussion.

### Key features
* Keeps video playback perfectly aligned for everyone in the session.
* Supports public, private, and offline viewing rooms.
* Includes a real-time chat room for reactions and discussions.
* Lets everyone contribute to a collaborative media playlist.
* Gives the host permission controls to manage viewers and kick disruptive users.

### How to install (Download)
Access the official web application directly to start a session: [Official Metastream Website](https://getmetastream.com/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Easy to use for group watch parties, supports multiple popular video websites, includes real-time text chat.
* **Cons:** Open source version is archived with no new feature updates, requires participants to use supported web browsers.
