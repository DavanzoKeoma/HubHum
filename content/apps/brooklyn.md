### What it does in plain English
Brooklyn turns your Mac screen into a calm, moving display of simple geometric shapes. It is designed to look like the visuals shown at a major tech company's 2018 presentation. You can use it to keep your screen active without showing distracting content.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who want a clean, professional look for their idle screens.
* People who enjoy minimalist design and smooth visual loops.
* Individuals who want a screen saver that does not require an internet connection.

### Key features
* Works completely offline, so it runs smoothly without internet access.
* Includes both light and dark themes to match your room's lighting.
* Allows you to choose which specific animations you want to see.
* Lets you set how many times each animation loops before moving to the next.
* Offers an option to randomize the order of animations for variety.
