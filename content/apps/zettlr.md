### What it does in plain English
Zettlr is a dedicated writing environment that helps you organize complex research, notes, and long-form documents in one place. It keeps your files private and local while offering professional tools to manage citations and references. When you are ready to share your work, it allows you to export your writing into various professional document formats with ease.

### Who it's for
* **Academic researchers and students** who need to manage large collections of notes and citations.
* **Professional writers and authors** who want a distraction-free space to draft and format their manuscripts.

### Key features
* **Privacy-First:** Your files stay on your own computer, ensuring total control over your data.
* **Citation Management:** Seamlessly connects with popular reference managers to handle your bibliography automatically.
* **Flexible Exporting:** Easily convert your drafts into professional document formats for school or work.
* **Knowledge Organization:** Built-in support for advanced note-linking techniques to help you connect your ideas.
* **Customizable Interface:** Includes themes, dark mode, and support for custom styles to fit your personal workflow.
