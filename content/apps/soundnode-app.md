### What it does in plain English
Soundnode App is a dedicated desktop player that lets you enjoy your online music library without keeping a web browser tab open. It connects directly to your music profile so you can manage playlists, discover new tracks, and hit play using your keyboard's media keys. Everything is wrapped in a lightweight application window designed to blend smoothly into your desktop workspace.

### Who it's for
* Dedicated music fans who want a standalone desktop player for their favorite streaming tracks.
* People who prefer using keyboard media shortcuts to pause, play, and skip songs while working.
* Users who want to keep their browser tab clutter-free while listening to albums and playlists.

### Key features
* Standalone desktop application window for your online music stream.
* Native keyboard shortcut support for quick media playback control.
* Easy navigation through personal streams, likes, history, and playlists.
* Ability to follow other creators, like tracks, and save them instantly.
* Open-source code structure designed for community transparency.

* **Pros:** Free open-source software, eliminates browser clutter, includes media keyboard shortcuts.
* **Cons:** Relies on third-party music service rules which may occasionally require manual configuration updates.
