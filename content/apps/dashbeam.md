### What it does in plain English
Dashbeam lets you send files, folders, and documents directly from one device to another without ever uploading them to a public cloud storage provider. It creates a secure, encrypted connection straight between the sending and receiving devices, ensuring your personal data stays completely private. Whether you are sharing large video files across the room or sending archives across the internet, transfers happen at maximum network speeds with zero account requirements.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers and creatives who frequently need to send massive multi-gigabyte project folders to clients or colleagues.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to share personal documents without handing their data over to third-party tech corporations.
* Everyday users looking for a hassle-free way to move photos and files between their phones, tablets, and computers.

### Key features
* **Direct device-to-device transfers:** Moves files straight across the network without intermediary cloud servers or storage limits.
* **Resumable downloads:** Automatically picks up interrupted transfers right where they left off without restarting from zero.
* **Cross-platform convenience:** Works natively on desktop computers, mobile devices, and even directly inside modern web browsers.
* **End-to-end security:** Protects every transferred byte with robust encryption and cryptographic file verification.
* **Nearby discovery:** Automatically detects other compatible devices on your local Wi-Fi network for instant sharing.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no file size limits, no user accounts required, and high-speed encrypted transfers.
* **Cons:** Both sender and receiver usually need to be online at the same time since files are not stored on persistent cloud servers.
