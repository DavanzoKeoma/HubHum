### What it does in plain English
This project enables an artificial intelligence to interact with a standard iPhone just like a human would, using a tiny, inexpensive micro-board as its eyes and hands. The board mirrors the phone screen and sends physical touch commands through Bluetooth or USB, meaning no complex computer software or jailbreaking is required. You simply give the system a plain-language goal, and it automatically taps, types, and navigates through mobile apps to complete the task.

### Who it's for
* **Everyday automation enthusiasts** who want to experiment with mobile task automation without dealing with developer accounts or complicated computer setups.
* **Accessibility experimenters** looking for innovative ways to interact with touch screens hands-free.
* **Makers and tinkerers** interested in combining microcontrollers with modern vision models for unique hardware projects.

### Key features
* **No computer required for operation:** The hardware board handles screen mirroring and input directly so your phone runs independently.
* **Supports any vision-capable AI model:** Connect your preferred OpenAI-compatible cloud model or local endpoint to power the intelligent agent.
* **Step-by-step visual replay:** Every automated run is recorded with actions drawn on top for easy review and debugging.
* **Dual input modes:** Switch between wired USB input and wireless Bluetooth control depending on your device setup.
* **Built-in safety features:** Easily stop the active automation at any time using the onscreen controls or physical triggers.
