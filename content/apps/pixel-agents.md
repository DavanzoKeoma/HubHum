### What it does in plain English
Pixel Agents brings your AI coding assistants to life by turning them into animated characters inside a digital office. As your AI works on tasks like writing or searching for files, your character will move around, sit at a desk, and perform actions to show you exactly what it is doing. It provides a fun, visual way to track your AI's progress and know immediately when it needs your attention.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use AI coding tools and want a more engaging way to monitor their progress.
* Tech enthusiasts who enjoy customizing their digital workspace with fun, retro-style pixel art.

### Key features
* **Live Activity Tracking:** Characters animate in real-time based on whether the AI is typing, reading, or running commands.
* **Customizable Office:** Design your own workspace with furniture, walls, and floors that you can save and export.
* **Personalized Characters:** Use the built-in wardrobe and pixel editor to create and dress your own unique AI agents.
* **Visual Notifications:** Get clear alerts through speech bubbles and chimes when your AI needs input or has finished a task.
* **Flexible Setup:** Works directly inside your code editor or as a standalone browser-based dashboard.

### How to install (Download)
* **For VS Code users:** [Download the extension (.vsix)](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents/releases/download/v1.6.0/pixel-agents-1.6.0.vsix) and install it via your editor's extension menu.
* **For standalone use:** Ensure you have Node.js installed, then run `npx pixel-agents` in your terminal to launch the office in your browser.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Adds a fun, visual layer to technical tasks; highly customizable; works in both code editors and browsers.
* **Cons:** Requires an existing AI coding tool (like Claude Code) to function; the visual office features may be distracting for some users.
