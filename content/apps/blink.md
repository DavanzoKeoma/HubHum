### What it does in plain English
Blink is a professional-grade tool that lets you connect to and manage your remote computers directly from your iPad or iPhone. It ensures your connection stays active even when you switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data, preventing annoying disconnections. The app is designed to feel just like a desktop computer, supporting custom fonts, themes, and external keyboards for a comfortable experience.

### Who it's for
* Professionals who need to manage servers or remote systems while on the move.
* Tech enthusiasts who want a reliable, desktop-quality command interface on their mobile devices.

### Key features
* **Always-on connectivity:** Uses specialized technology to keep your sessions alive even if your internet connection drops or changes.
* **Desktop-grade performance:** Fast rendering ensures that text and commands appear instantly without lag.
* **Full keyboard support:** Works perfectly with external Bluetooth keyboards, including custom key mapping for common shortcuts.
* **Customizable interface:** Personalize your workspace with your own fonts, color themes, and settings.
* **Touch-friendly controls:** Use simple gestures like swiping to switch between connections or pinching to zoom in on text.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely stable connection, highly customizable, and optimized specifically for iOS touch and keyboard inputs.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of remote server management to set up and use effectively.
