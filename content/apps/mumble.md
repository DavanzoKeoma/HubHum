### What it does in plain English
Mumble is a voice communication tool designed to help people talk to each other clearly and instantly. It focuses on high audio quality and low delay, making it perfect for group conversations. Because it is open-source, you can host your own private server to ensure your chats remain completely confidential.

### Who it's for
* Gaming groups and online communities who need reliable, lag-free voice coordination.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to host their own communication server without relying on big tech companies.

### Key features
* **High Audio Quality:** Uses advanced technology to ensure voices sound natural and clear.
* **Low Latency:** Designed to minimize the time between speaking and hearing, ensuring real-time conversation.
* **Self-Hosting:** You have the option to run your own server, keeping your communication history and data private.
* **Customizable:** Includes support for plugins and various settings to tailor the experience to your needs.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly across different computer operating systems.
