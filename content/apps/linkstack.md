### What it does in plain English
LinkStack provides a simple way to create a personalized profile page that gathers all your important links into one easy-to-access location. Unlike social media platforms that limit you to a single link, this tool allows you to share as many as you need. You can host this page on your own server to ensure your data remains private and under your complete control.

### Who it's for
* **Content Creators:** Individuals who want to direct followers to multiple social channels, shops, or portfolios from one professional page.
* **Small Businesses:** Organizations that need a central hub to manage and share various resources, contact methods, or project links with their audience.

### Key features
* **Total Ownership:** Host the platform on your own server so you never have to worry about third-party data tracking.
* **Easy Customization:** Change the look and feel of your page using a variety of themes that can be installed with just a few clicks.
* **Simple Management:** Use the intuitive dashboard to add, remove, or reorder your links without needing any technical knowledge.
* **Multi-User Support:** Allow others to register and create their own link pages on your instance if you choose to share your setup.
* **Automatic Updates:** Keep your site secure and up-to-date with a one-click update feature built directly into the dashboard.

### How to install (Download)
* **Standard Installation:** [Download the latest version from GitHub](https://github.com/linkstackorg/linkstack/releases) and upload the contents to your web server.
* **Docker Setup:** For those who prefer containerized environments, you can [learn more about the Docker version here](https://github.com/LinkStackOrg/linkstack-docker).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, no subscription fees, complete privacy, and very easy to set up.
* **Cons:** Requires your own web hosting or server space to run independently.
