### What it does in plain English
ShadowsocksX-NG is a convenient tool that lives in your Mac's menu bar to help you manage secure network connections and proxy servers. It allows you to import configuration settings, organize your servers into groups, and switch between different traffic routing modes effortlessly. You can test server speeds and route specific website traffic automatically while keeping your personal data securely encrypted on your device.

### Who it's for
- Mac users who need an efficient way to manage multiple proxy servers and subscription links.
- Remote workers and travelers looking to route specific application traffic securely on Apple Silicon Macs.
- Power users who want granular control over local network proxies and routing rule presets.

### Key features
- Manage servers, subscriptions, and connection modes directly from a clean menu bar interface.
- Switch easily between global mode, direct mode, and rule-based presets to control traffic routing.
- Test server latency and balance traffic loads across multiple upstream servers.
- Keep your server library and configurations encrypted locally on your Mac using advanced security tools.
- Compatible with popular extension plugins like v2ray-plugin and Cloak for enhanced transport options.
