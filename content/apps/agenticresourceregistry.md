### What it does in plain English
Agentic Resource Registry provides a single, organized home for all the custom tools, prompts, and settings your team uses for AI-assisted coding. It solves the problem of scattered files by creating a searchable library where team members can find and reuse approved resources. Additionally, it tracks how these tools are actually used, helping you identify what works best and fix issues before they become major problems.

### Who it's for
* **Software Development Teams** who want to standardize how their developers use AI coding assistants.
* **Team Leads and Managers** who need to oversee which AI tools are being used and ensure they are reliable and helpful.

### Key features
* **Centralized Library:** Store all your prompts, skills, and configuration files in one searchable place.
* **Team Governance:** Review and approve resources to ensure everyone on the team is using trusted, high-quality tools.
* **Usage Insights:** See real data on which agents and tools are actually helping your team and which ones need improvement.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Works seamlessly with popular coding assistants and command-line tools.
* **Session Replay:** Review past interactions to debug issues and understand how your agents are performing.
