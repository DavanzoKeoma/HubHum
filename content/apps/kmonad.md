### What it does in plain English
KMonad allows you to completely redefine how your keyboard works by changing what happens when you press, hold, or tap specific keys. You can create custom layouts, add new layers of functionality, and turn simple keys into powerful shortcuts. It works in the background to make your typing experience faster and more comfortable on any standard keyboard.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want to improve their typing efficiency and ergonomics.
* People who use small keyboards and need to access extra functions through custom layers.

### Key features
* **Custom Layers:** Create multiple keyboard layouts that you can switch between instantly for different tasks.
* **Tap and Hold:** Make a single key perform different actions depending on whether you tap it quickly or hold it down.
* **Multi-Tap:** Assign unique commands to a key based on how many times you press it in a row.
* **Universal Compatibility:** Works with almost any keyboard, bringing advanced features usually found only on expensive custom hardware to your desk.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, works with any keyboard, and offers infinite customization possibilities.
* **Cons:** Requires creating a text-based configuration file to set up your preferences, which may take some time to learn.
