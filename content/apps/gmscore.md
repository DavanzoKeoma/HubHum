### What it does in plain English
GmsCore lets Android phones run apps that normally require Google’s services, even when those services aren’t installed.  
It gives you the ability to use popular apps without needing Google’s proprietary software.  
The result is a more independent and privacy‑friendly device.

### Who it's for
- People who use Android phones but prefer not to install Google’s proprietary apps.  
- Users who run custom Android versions that lack Google services.  
- Anyone who wants to keep personal data private while still using popular apps.

### Key features
- Runs apps that depend on Google Play Services without the official Google package.  
- Works on devices where Google apps are removed or not available.  
- Updates can be applied by downloading a newer file from the official page.  
- Keeps personal data private by avoiding Google’s tracking.  
- Compatible with most Android versions.
