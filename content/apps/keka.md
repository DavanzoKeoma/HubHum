### What it does in plain English
Keka turns big files into smaller, easier‑to‑share bundles.  
It works with many common formats like zip and 7z.  
You can lock those bundles with a password for extra privacy.

### Who it's for
* Anyone who needs to send large documents, photos, or videos.  
* Students and teachers who want to keep homework or lecture notes tidy.  
* Home users who wish to protect personal files before storing them online.

### Key features
- Compress files into zip, 7z, tar, and other popular formats.  
- Add a password to keep compressed bundles private.  
- Drag‑and‑drop interface that feels natural on a Mac.  
- Supports splitting large archives into smaller pieces.  
- Works on both macOS and iOS devices.
