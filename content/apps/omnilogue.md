### What it does in plain English
Omnilogue is a desktop application that gathers and organizes your past conversation history from dozens of different AI coding assistants stored right on your machine. It provides an intuitive interface to search through old sessions, track conversation branches, inspect file edits, and review usage metrics without uploading any personal data. You can easily find past solutions and understand how your AI tools performed across different projects.

### Who it's for
* Developers and power users who work with multiple AI coding assistants and need a unified search archive.
* Privacy-conscious creators who want to review past AI logs completely offline without sharing data with cloud services.
* Anyone looking to audit token usage and explore past prompt branches across different coding projects.

### Key features
* **Universal Search:** Quickly browse and search through conversation histories from dozens of popular AI coding assistants in one place.
* **Complete Privacy:** Operates 100% offline directly from local disk files without requiring an external account or data upload.
* **Detailed Branch Inspection:** Follow conversation steps, view tool calls, and examine exact file edits made during sessions.
* **Usage & Cost Tracking:** Review token consumption and estimated costs where log data is available.
* **Optional WebUI:** Run a local server component if you prefer accessing your session archives through a web browser.
