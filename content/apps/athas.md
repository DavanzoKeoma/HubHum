### What it does in plain English
Athas is a modern tool designed to help you write and organize your text files and programming projects with ease. It uses your computer's graphics hardware to ensure that everything you type appears instantly without any lag. The software includes helpful smart assistants and built-in tools to keep your work organized and connected to your project history.

### Who it's for
*   Software developers and students who need a fast, reliable environment for writing code.
*   Power users who prefer keyboard-based shortcuts to navigate and edit their files quickly.

### Key features
*   **Ultra-responsive:** Built to run smoothly by utilizing your computer's graphics card.
*   **Smart Assistance:** Includes integrated AI agents to help you write and debug your work faster.
*   **Version Tracking:** Built-in support for managing changes to your files over time.
*   **Customizable Controls:** Supports classic keyboard shortcuts for those who prefer not to use a mouse.
*   **All-in-one Workspace:** Features a built-in terminal and database viewers so you can do more without switching apps.
