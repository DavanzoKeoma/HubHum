### What it does in plain English
Yazi is a lightning-fast file navigator that runs directly inside your command line window. It lets you preview images, videos, and documents instantly as you move through your folders.

### Who it's for
* Command-line power users who want a visual and snappy way to manage local files.
* Developers and writers looking to speed up their daily workflow without leaving the keyboard.

### Key features
* Lightning-fast navigation powered by modern systems engineering for instant response times.
* Built-in previews for photos, videos, code, and compressed archives without opening separate apps.
* Easy-to-use plugin system to add custom themes and extra tools with single commands.
