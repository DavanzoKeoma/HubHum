### What it does in plain English
Weylus turns your tablet or smartphone into a fully functional graphic tablet and secondary touch screen for your computer. It streams your computer display directly to your mobile device while capturing touch gestures, pen pressure, and stylus tilts. You can seamlessly control your desktop operating system and draw in art software using just a standard web browser on your tablet.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and illustrators looking for an affordable drawing tablet setup using existing mobile hardware.
* Students and note-takers who want to write or annotate documents on their computer using a tablet pen.
* Professionals needing an easy way to extend their desktop workspace onto a portable secondary screen.

### Key features
* Control your computer mouse cursor directly using touch input on your mobile device.
* Mirror your computer screen instantly to a tablet web browser with low latency.
* Support for advanced stylus features including pressure sensitivity and tilt on Linux systems.
* Multi-touch support for compatible creative applications like digital painting software.
* Hardware-accelerated video streaming options for smoother performance.
