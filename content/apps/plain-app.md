### What it does in plain English
PlainApp lets you view and manage your phone's files, photos, and messages directly from your computer's web browser. It works entirely over your local Wi-Fi, meaning your private data never leaves your home or office network. Because it uses your own connection, you don't need to create accounts or pay for subscriptions to access your own information.

### Who it's for
* People who want to transfer files or photos between their phone and computer without uploading them to the internet.
* Users who want to read and reply to text messages or view phone notifications while working on their desktop.

### Key features
* **Browser-based dashboard:** Access your phone's storage, contacts, and messages through a simple web page on your computer.
* **Total privacy:** Your data stays on your local network and is protected with strong encryption.
* **Built-in tools:** Includes handy extras like a note-taking app, an RSS reader, and a media player.
* **Screen mirroring:** View your phone's screen live on your computer monitor for easier navigation.
* **No accounts required:** The app is completely free, ad-free, and does not require you to sign up for any services.
