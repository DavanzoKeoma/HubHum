### What it does in plain English
TorrentPlayer lets you watch videos or listen to music directly from torrent files or links, similar to a regular streaming service. Instead of downloading the whole file first, its custom engine fetches the exact moment you want to watch right away. It includes a built-in background queue, a clean playback window, and network privacy controls without requiring accounts or subscriptions.

### Who it's for
* People who want to stream open-source media, lectures, podcasts, or films from public torrent networks without waiting for complete downloads.
* Privacy-conscious users looking for an ad-free media player with built-in tunneling options and zero telemetry.
* Anyone tired of rigid streaming service limits who wants a lightweight desktop application for local media playback.

### Key features
* **Instant seeking:** Skips to any timestamp in a video almost immediately by prioritizing the exact requested data chunks from the network.
* **Intelligent background queue:** Downloads subsequent items in the background while prioritizing the active video's bandwidth needs.
* **Built-in privacy tools:** Includes support for secure tunneling, proxy configurations, and a network kill switch to protect your connection.
* **Audio and video versatility:** Plays standard media formats and converts advanced multichannel audio codecs on the fly for seamless playback.
* **Zero telemetry:** Completely free, open-source software with no user tracking, accounts, or forced advertisements.
