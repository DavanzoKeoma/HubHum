### What it does in plain English
PureMac is a free, transparent application that helps you find and remove clutter from your computer's storage drive. It scans for leftover files from deleted apps, temporary system caches, and forgotten developer files that slow down everyday use. Best of all, it operates entirely offline with zero tracking or paid walls.

### Who it's for
* Owners of smaller entry-level Mac computers who frequently run out of storage space.
* Software developers and programmers who accumulate heavy development caches over time.
* Anyone looking for a trustworthy alternative to commercial cleaning tools without subscriptions.

### Key features
* **App Uninstaller:** Thoroughly removes applications along with their hidden configuration files and support folders.
* **Orphan Finder:** Locates and clears out leftover files from programs you uninstalled long ago.
* **Developer Cache Cleanup:** Safely purges heavy storage buildup from tools like Docker, Homebrew, and software building environments.
* **Duplicate & Similar File Finder:** Helps identify clutter and unnecessary duplicate items to free up disk space.
* **Zero Telemetry:** Operates locally on your device with no data sharing or hidden analytics.

### How to install (Download)
* [Download for Mac (.dmg)](https://github.com/momenbasel/PureMac/releases/download/v3.3.0/PureMac-3.3.0.dmg)
* Alternatively, install via Homebrew using your terminal: `brew install --cask puremac`

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscriptions, zero data tracking, native performance.
* **Cons:** Requires Full Disk Access permissions to scan hidden system areas completely.
