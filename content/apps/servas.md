### What it does in plain English
Servas lets you keep all your favorite web links in one place, organized the way you like.  
You can add tags, group them, and even let the app suggest groups based on tags.  
It works on any device, so your collection is always handy.

### Who it's for
- People who collect many articles, videos, or recipes online.  
- Students and researchers who need to organize reference links.  
- Families or small teams that want to share useful sites together.

### Key features
- Add and manage tags to describe each bookmark.  
- Create folders (groups) and nest them for deeper organization.  
- Smart groups that automatically collect items with matching tags.  
- Browser buttons for Chrome and Firefox to save pages instantly.  
- Dark and light themes plus the option to protect your account with extra security.
