### What it does in plain English
Harbor acts as a central hub that automatically connects various AI tools, such as chat interfaces and language models, so they work together instantly. Instead of spending hours manually linking different services, you simply type one command to get your entire AI environment running. It handles all the background connections and settings, allowing you to focus on using the tools rather than fixing them.

### Who it's for
*   **AI Enthusiasts:** People who want to experiment with the latest language models and AI agents on their own computer without needing advanced technical skills.
*   **Developers:** Professionals who need a quick, reliable way to spin up local AI environments for testing or coding tasks without reconfiguring their system every time.

### Key features
*   **One-Command Setup:** Launch complex AI stacks, including web search, voice chat, and image generation, with a single instruction.
*   **Pre-Wired Connections:** All services are automatically linked to work together, so you don't have to manually configure how they talk to each other.
*   **Versatile Tool Support:** Includes a wide catalog of popular AI interfaces, backends, and specialized agent tools.
*   **Easy Management:** Use the companion app or simple text commands to start, stop, or update your AI services.
*   **Portable Configurations:** Easily save your setup profiles or move your environment to a standard format if you decide to manage it yourself later.
