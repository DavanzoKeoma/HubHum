### What it does in plain English
YouPlot allows you to quickly create visual charts from text-based data without needing to open heavy spreadsheet software. It works by reading your data files or streams and instantly drawing bar charts, histograms, or scatter plots in your terminal window. It is designed to help you understand trends and patterns in your information at a glance while you work.

### Who it's for
* Data analysts or researchers who want to verify data patterns quickly without leaving their workspace.
* System administrators or power users who need to visualize logs or process counts on the fly.

### Key features
* **Versatile Plotting:** Supports various chart types including bar plots, histograms, line charts, scatter plots, and box plots.
* **Stream-Friendly:** Easily integrates into your existing workflow by accepting data directly from other system tools.
* **Lightweight:** Runs entirely within your terminal, making it extremely fast and efficient for quick data checks.
* **Customizable:** Offers options to adjust labels, titles, and colors to make your charts easier to read.
* **Configuration Support:** Allows you to save your preferred settings in a simple file for consistent results every time.

### How to install (Download)
YouPlot is available through several standard package managers. Choose the one that matches your system:

* **macOS (Homebrew):** `brew install youplot`
* **Ruby environment:** `gem install youplot`
* **Nix:** `nix shell nixpkgs#youplot`
* **Guix:** `guix install youplot`
* **Portable Version:** For a standalone experience, see [YouPlot2](https://github.com/red-data-tools/YouPlot2).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, very fast, and integrates perfectly with other command-line tools.
* **Cons:** Requires basic comfort with command-line interfaces, and some advanced features may require a short learning curve.
