### What it does in plain English
Gokapi allows you to share files with others while keeping full control over your data. You can set files to automatically delete themselves after a certain number of downloads or a specific amount of time. Because it is self-hosted, your files remain on your own hardware or private cloud storage rather than on a public company server.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want a private way to send sensitive documents to friends or colleagues.
* Small teams or home-lab enthusiasts who need a secure, temporary file-transfer system.

### Key features
* **Automatic Expiration:** Files are removed automatically after a set number of downloads or days to keep your storage clean.
* **End-to-End Encryption:** Keeps your files protected during the transfer process.
* **File Requests:** Create a special link that allows others to upload files directly to you, keeping your inbox uncluttered.
* **Flexible Storage:** Use your own local storage or connect to professional cloud storage services.
* **User Permissions:** Manage who is allowed to upload files with built-in user roles.

* **Pros:** Complete privacy, no subscription fees, and highly customizable.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of setting up a server or using Docker to get started.
