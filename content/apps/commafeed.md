### What it does in plain English
CommaFeed acts as a central hub for all your favorite websites, blogs, and news sources. It automatically checks for new content so you can read everything in one clean, organized feed. Because it is self-hosted, you maintain full control over your data and reading habits without any outside interference.

### Who it's for
*   **News enthusiasts** who want to keep up with dozens of blogs and websites without visiting each one individually.
*   **Privacy-conscious users** who want a reading experience free from tracking, data mining, or intrusive advertisements.

### Key features
*   **Customizable Layouts:** Choose from four different viewing styles to suit your reading preference.
*   **Full Responsiveness:** The interface works smoothly on both your computer and your mobile phone.
*   **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Navigate through your articles quickly and efficiently using simple key commands.
*   **Smart Automation:** Set up rules to automatically mark articles as read based on your specific interests.
*   **Total Privacy:** Since you host it yourself, your reading history and personal data stay entirely on your own equipment.
