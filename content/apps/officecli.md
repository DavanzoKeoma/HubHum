### What it does in plain English
OfficeCLI gives artificial intelligence tools full control over Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, and PowerPoint presentations through simple text commands. It runs as a self-contained program that renders documents visually, allowing automated assistants to spot layout errors and fix them in real time.

### Who it's for
* **Software Developers** who want to generate reports and documents directly from backend systems or automated test pipelines.
* **AI Tool Builders** who need a reliable, structured way for chat assistants to manipulate office files without human intervention.
* **Content Creators** looking to batch-produce documents, presentations, or spreadsheets without repetitive manual typing.

### Key features
* **Complete Format Support:** Read, modify, and create modern Word, Excel, and PowerPoint documents from scratch.
* **Visual Rendering Loop:** Built-in rendering engine allows assistants to preview document layouts as images and correct alignment automatically.
* **Formula & Data Engine:** Evaluates hundreds of spreadsheet formulas and builds complex tables instantly.
* **Template Merging:** Populate placeholder tags across multiple files efficiently using structured data sets.
