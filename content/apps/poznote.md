### What it does in plain English
Poznote is a flexible digital notebook that you can run on your own hardware or server to keep your thoughts and documents private. It gives you a clean, distraction-free environment to write notes, link information together, and manage daily projects. Because it is completely self-hosted, you retain total ownership of all your data without relying on external cloud providers.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want absolute privacy and control over their personal notes and journal entries.
* Freelancers and small teams seeking a lightweight, self-hosted alternative to commercial document workspaces.
* Knowledge workers who prefer managing information using Markdown and open formats.

### Key features
* Complete data ownership with a self-hosted architecture that runs smoothly on your own server or home hardware.
* Clean and intuitive writing interface supporting rich formatting and structured note organization.
* Built-in tools for synchronization, backup, and file attachment storage.
* Multi-user support with sharing options to collaborate with family members or teammates.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, complete data privacy, self-hosted control, lightweight and fast performance.
* **Cons:** Requires a server or basic technical setup knowledge to run independently, manual updates needed for self-hosted instances.
