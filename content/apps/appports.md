### What it does in plain English
AppPorts is a helpful tool designed to rescue your Mac from "disk almost full" warnings by safely relocating large programs and files to an external storage drive. Instead of completely breaking your workflow, it leaves a tiny, smart shortcut on your computer so you can still open and run your favorite apps with a single click. It handles complex background plumbing automatically, keeping your personal folders tidy and your local hard drive breathing easy.

### Who it's for
* Photographers, video editors, and music producers whose creative software libraries quickly swallow up internal computer storage.
* Everyday Mac owners with standard solid-state drives who want to keep local space free for system updates and documents.
* Developers and power users juggling hefty coding environments, developer tool directories, and extensive chat message archives.

### Key features
* **Arrow-Free Launchers:** Relocate massive software packages to an external drive while leaving behind a clean, familiar icon without the annoying shortcut arrow.
* **Smart Update Protection:** Safely manages self-updating programs so in-app upgrades continue normally without breaking your custom file paths.
* **Signature & Data Safety:** Checks app code signatures before moving files and creates dependable local backups to ensure everything boots up properly.
* **Split Messaging Data:** Easily divide bulky chat histories and media attachments so only the necessary files travel to your external drive.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source; frees up gigabytes of local storage; maintains normal app behavior and updates.
* **Cons:** Requires an external drive to be connected while using relocated apps; advanced folder configurations take a brief moment to learn.
