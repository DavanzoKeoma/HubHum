### What it does in plain English
Rowy turns your complex cloud database into a simple, organized spreadsheet that anyone can understand. It allows you to view, edit, and manage your data directly in your web browser without needing to write technical queries. You can also set up automated workflows to handle repetitive tasks, making your data work for you automatically.

### Who it's for
* **App Developers:** Who want to manage their project data quickly without switching between different technical tools.
* **Small Business Owners:** Who need a custom way to organize information and automate business processes without hiring a software engineer.

### Key features
* **Spreadsheet Interface:** View and edit your database records just like you would in a familiar office spreadsheet program.
* **Automated Workflows:** Create simple rules that trigger actions whenever your data changes, such as sending emails or updating other services.
* **Rich Data Types:** Support for over 30 different field types, including images, files, and interactive pickers.
* **Team Collaboration:** Set specific permissions so team members can only access or edit the data they need to see.
* **Secure Hosting:** Your data stays within your own cloud account, ensuring you keep full control over your information.

### How to install (Download)
Rowy is a web-based platform. You can get started immediately by using the official setup tool:
[Launch Rowy on your Cloud Project](https://rowy.app/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely intuitive interface, powerful automation capabilities, and keeps your data under your own control.
* **Cons:** Requires a cloud account to host the platform, and some advanced automation features may require basic knowledge of logic flows.
