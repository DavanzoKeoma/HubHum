### What it does in plain English
Ice is a lightweight application designed to clean up cluttered computer screens by managing small icons sitting at the top of your display. It lets you hide icons you rarely use, reveal them instantly with a simple hover or click, and keep your workspace looking neat. You can also customize the background appearance, add borders, or arrange items using a simple drag-and-drop layout.

### Who it's for
- Mac owners who have too many small icons crowding the top of their screens and want a minimal workspace.
- Professionals and students who use smaller laptops and need extra space for active app names.
- Anyone who loves customizing the visual layout and appearance of their operating system.

### Key features
- **Smart Hiding:** Keep essential icons visible while tucking away the rest until you hover, click, or scroll over the area.
- **Custom Appearance:** Add stylish borders, shadows, solid background tints, or gradients to your menu bar.
- **Search Panel:** Quickly type to find and open any hidden icon without digging through menus.
- **Notch Support:** Display hidden items neatly in a separate drop-down bar tailored for modern laptops.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, visit the official website at [icemenubar.app](https://icemenubar.app) or grab the latest package directly from the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/releases/latest). Unpack the downloaded file and drag it straight into your standard Applications folder. If you prefer command-line tools, you can also install it via Homebrew using `brew install --cask jordanbaird-ice`.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly customizable layout options, sleek design matching modern operating systems.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS 14 or newer to run, advanced styling settings might take a moment to learn.
