### What it does in plain English
Bark lets you send custom alerts and notifications directly to your iPhone using simple web requests. You can trigger alerts from your scripts, servers, or automation workflows without needing to set up complex notification systems. It keeps your device battery safe while supporting advanced iOS features like custom sounds, grouping, and critical alerts.

### Who it's for
* Developers and system administrators who want to receive instant server alerts on their iPhones.
* Automation enthusiasts looking to trigger phone notifications from home automation scripts or webhooks.
* Anyone who needs a private, self-hostable way to push custom messages to their iOS device.

### Key features
* Send push notifications instantly using simple web requests.
* Support for custom notification titles, subtitles, and body text.
* Attach custom icons, sounds, and direct opening links to alerts.
* Advanced iOS options including time-sensitive notifications and critical alerts.
* Privacy-focused with support for self-hosted servers and encrypted messages.

* **Pros:** Free and secure, lightweight on battery life, extremely simple to integrate via web requests.
* **Cons:** Requires an iOS device to receive notifications, advanced self-hosting requires basic server knowledge.
