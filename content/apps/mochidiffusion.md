### What it does in plain English
MochiDiffusion turns your Mac into a powerful, private art studio that creates images from text descriptions. It uses your computer's built-in hardware to process these images quickly without needing an internet connection. This means you can experiment with AI art freely, knowing your data never leaves your device.

### Who it's for
*   **Creative Professionals:** Designers and artists who need a fast, local tool for brainstorming concepts or creating unique visuals without uploading sensitive work to the cloud.
*   **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Individuals who want to enjoy AI image generation but are uncomfortable with sending personal data or prompts to external servers.
*   **Mac Enthusiasts:** Users with Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer) who want to leverage their device's specific capabilities for high-performance local computing.

### Key features
*   **Fully Offline Operation:** All image generation happens locally on your machine, ensuring complete privacy and no dependency on internet speed.
*   **Native Mac Performance:** Optimized to use your Mac's specific hardware for faster image creation and lower memory usage compared to generic tools.
*   **Multiple AI Models:** Supports various advanced image generation styles, including Stable Diffusion and FLUX.2 Klein, allowing for diverse artistic outputs.
*   **Built-in Gallery:** Includes a simple library to view, organize, and manage the images you have created directly within the app.
