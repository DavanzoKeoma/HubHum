### What it does in plain English
Open Cowork is a friendly desktop application that brings powerful artificial intelligence right to your computer. It allows you to chat with advanced intelligence models, organize your local files, and generate professional documents like spreadsheets and slide decks automatically. Everything runs within a secure folder or isolated environment on your machine, giving you full control over your data and projects.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users looking to automate repetitive file organization and document creation tasks.
* Writers and professionals who want to quickly generate presentation slides, text documents, or spreadsheets using AI.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want a local workspace where AI models help with everyday desktop tasks.

### Key features
* **One-Click Installation:** Ready-to-use installers for Windows and macOS that require no coding or complex environment setup.
* **Flexible Model Choice:** Connect your own keys from popular AI providers to power your daily tasks.
* **Built-In Document Skills:** Automated workflows for creating and editing presentations, word documents, spreadsheets, and PDF files.
* **Secure Workspace Isolation:** Keeps file changes confined to your designated folders and supports virtual machine isolation for safety.
* **Multimodal Interaction:** Simply drag and drop files and images directly into the conversation to get instant help.
