### What it does in plain English
LessPass is a smart tool that generates unique, secure passwords for every website you visit based on a single master password you choose. Because it calculates these passwords mathematically rather than saving them in a database, you never have to worry about syncing or losing your password vault. It provides a consistent way to access your accounts across all your devices without ever storing your private information.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want a secure way to manage passwords without relying on cloud-based storage services.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer an open-source solution that works offline and across multiple platforms.

### Key features
* **Stateless Generation:** Passwords are created on demand, meaning there is no vault file to sync or back up.
* **Cross-Platform Access:** Use it via web browser extensions, mobile apps, or directly through the official website.
* **Privacy Focused:** Your master password and login details are never sent to a server, keeping your information under your control.
* **Open Source:** The code is transparent and available for anyone to review, ensuring high security standards.

### How to install (Download)
* **Web Browsers:** Install the official extension for [Chrome](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/lesspass/lcmbpoclaodbgkbjafnkbbinogcbnjih) or [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/lesspass/).
* **Mobile:** Download the app for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1531215924), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lesspass.android), or via [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/com.lesspass.android/).
* **Web Interface:** Access the generator directly at [https://www.lesspass.com](https://www.lesspass.com).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, no account synchronization required, and works entirely offline.
* **Cons:** Requires you to remember your master password perfectly, as there is no "forgot password" recovery option.
