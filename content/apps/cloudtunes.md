### What it does in plain English
CloudTunes acts as a central hub that connects your music files stored in cloud storage with your favorite music videos from the web. It organizes everything into a clean, searchable library that looks and feels like a professional music player. You can create custom playlists that mix your personal files and online content, allowing you to listen to your entire collection from any device.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who have files scattered across different cloud storage services.
* People who want to combine their personal music collection with online video content in one single interface.

### Key features
* **Unified Library:** Combines your personal cloud-stored music and online video content into one searchable collection.
* **Smart Playlists:** Easily drag and drop tracks from different sources to create your own custom listening experience.
* **Social Integration:** Connects with music discovery services to help you find new recommendations based on your taste.
* **Keyboard Control:** Includes handy keyboard shortcuts to manage your playback without needing to click around.
* **Fast Syncing:** Automatically keeps your library updated as you add new files to your cloud storage.

### How to install (Download)
CloudTunes is a self-hosted web application. Because it is designed to be run on your own server or local computer, you can find the source code and setup instructions at the [Official CloudTunes GitHub official project page](https://github.com/jkbrzt/cloudtunes).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no monthly subscription fees, and gives you full control over your data.
* **Cons:** Requires some technical knowledge to set up on your own server or computer, and it is no longer actively updated by the original creator.
