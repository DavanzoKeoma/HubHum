### What it does in plain English
Reactive Resume helps you design professional resumes and cover letters while keeping complete ownership of your personal data. You can watch your document update live on screen as you type, choose from multiple clean design templates, and check your work against job descriptions. When you are done, export your clean PDF or share it instantly with a secure public link. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Job seekers wanting a professional, modern resume without paying recurring subscription fees.
* Privacy-conscious professionals who want to host their own career documents without corporate tracking.
* Students and career changers looking for clear templates and helpful feedback tools to land their next role.

### Key features
* Live page preview that shows your finished resume layout instantly as you write.
* Multiple templates with customizable fonts, colors, and layout sections to match your personal style.
* Built-in job application tracker to manage interviews, notes, and stages from saved to offer.
* Readability checks and keyword matching to help your resume pass automated hiring software.
* Ready-to-use digital planners, printable checklists, and clean document templates for daily organization via [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
