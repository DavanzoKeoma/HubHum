### What it does in plain English
Goku Image to SVG Tool turns standard photos and graphics into clean, scalable vector files that you can edit. It provides a user-friendly interface where you can upload an image and instantly see the conversion results. You can fine-tune details like color palettes and styles, then compare the original and the new version side-by-side before saving your file.

### Who it's for
* **Graphic Designers:** Professionals who need a quick way to trace logos or sketches into vector format.
* **Content Creators:** Anyone looking to convert simple icons or illustrations for use in high-quality digital projects.

### Key features
* **Instant Conversion:** Upload your image and see the vector result immediately without needing to type complex commands.
* **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Use a handy slider to view the original image and the new vector version simultaneously.
* **Customizable Settings:** Adjust color counts, detail levels, and styles to get the exact look you need.
* **Flexible Exports:** Download your results as an SVG file or render them as a PNG at different zoom levels.
* **No Installation Required:** Access the tool directly through your web browser for immediate use.
