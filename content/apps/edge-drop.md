### What it does in plain English
Edge-Drop is a clean, modern clipboard tool that tucks neatly against the side of your monitor so it is never in your way. Whenever you copy text, links, screenshots, or files, they automatically appear on this subtle side shelf ready for use. You can simply hover your mouse pointer over the edge to open the shelf, then click to paste or drag items directly into any other window on your computer.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and researchers who constantly juggle multiple screenshots, web links, and reference notes across windows.
* Everyday computer users who want an easier, faster alternative to standard system menus for finding previously copied items.
* Professionals and students looking to organize files, text snippets, and images without cluttering their main desktop space.

### Key features
* **Screen Edge Access:** Move your mouse pointer to the edge of your monitor to instantly reveal your clipboard history.
* **Universal Drag and Drop:** Drag files, links, or images directly from the shelf into any open app or document window.
* **Smart Cards & Stacks:** Group related files together into convenient stacks and add personal notes to important items so they never get auto-deleted.
* **Privacy Focused:** All your history remains strictly local on your computer with robust encryption and zero data collection.
* **Customizable Settings:** Adjust dock positions, trigger zones, open delays, and choose from 31 different language options.
