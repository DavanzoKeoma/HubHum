### What it does in plain English
OpenTabletDriver acts as a universal bridge between your computer and your drawing tablet, regardless of the brand. It allows you to customize your pen pressure, button shortcuts, and screen mapping in one unified window. By running as a lightweight background process, it ensures your tablet works exactly how you want it to without needing heavy, proprietary software.

### Who it's for
*   Digital artists and illustrators who use tablets from different manufacturers and want a consistent experience.
*   Osu! players or hobbyists who need precise control over their pen sensitivity and screen area mapping.

### Key features
*   **Universal Compatibility:** Works with a vast range of tablet models and manufacturers in one single program.
*   **Customizable Mapping:** Easily adjust your active drawing area, rotation, and screen offsets to match your personal workflow.
*   **Advanced Pen Settings:** Configure pressure sensitivity, tilt, and button bindings to suit your specific drawing style.
*   **Lightweight Performance:** Designed to be efficient and unobtrusive, perfect for systems where you want to avoid heavy background software.
*   **Plugin Support:** Expand functionality with community-made plugins for specialized needs.
