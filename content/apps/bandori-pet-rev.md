### What it does in plain English
Bandori-Pet-Rev places charming, fully animated virtual characters directly onto your computer screen. These friendly desktop companions can react to your system audio, converse using advanced artificial intelligence, and display hundreds of different costumes. You can customize your workspace with multiple characters at once, transforming a plain desktop into a lively rehearsal room.

### Who it's for
* Music and character design fans who want familiar virtual companions hanging out on their computer screens.
* Anyone looking to add a cheerful, interactive touch to their everyday desktop workspace.
* Users interested in experimenting with AI-driven character conversations and desktop customization.

### Key features
* Features dozens of well-known animated musical characters with extensive clothing options.
* Supports responsive character movements, expressions, and physical hair or clothing dynamics.
* Includes smart conversation memory where characters adapt and respond based on your interactions.
* Reacts dynamically to your default system audio playback by bouncing or stretching along to the beat.
* Runs lightweight tools in your system tray for quick character switching or hiding.
