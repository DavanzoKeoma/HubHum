### What it does in plain English
Snow Shot is a versatile screen capture and recording utility designed to help you grab exactly what you see on your monitor. It lets you snap scrolling web pages, select specific regions, and record video clips complete with audio. You can quickly point out important details using built-in drawing tools, pin reference windows to stay visible, and pull readable text straight out of any image using offline text recognition.

### Who it's for
* **Writers and support staff:** Quick capture of application windows with highlighted pointers and step numbers for clear instructional guides.
* **Students and researchers:** Pinning reference snippets side-by-side and instantly pulling typed text out of saved diagrams or lecture slides.
* **Content creators:** Recording smooth video walkthroughs or animated GIFs with system audio for tutorials and social posts.

### Key features
* **Flexible capture options:** Grab a custom region, the focused application window, an entire monitor, or take a scrolling long screenshot.
* **Handy drawing tools:** Mark up your captures with arrows, text boxes, geometric shapes, step numbers, and pixelating blur effects.
* **Floating reference pins:** Pin screenshots and copied snippets above other active windows so you never lose sight of important notes.
* **Built-in text recognition:** Extract readable text from any graphic or decode QR codes right on your device without sending data online.
* **Screen recording:** Record your desktop activity with microphone or system audio and export your work as video or animated image files.
