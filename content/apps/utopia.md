### What it does in plain English
Utopia acts as a central brain for your organization, organizing documents and data into a structured map that understands how facts change over time. Unlike standard search tools, it remembers the history of your information, allowing you to see exactly what was known and believed at any specific date in the past. It keeps your data private and offline, ensuring that your decision-making core remains under your complete control.

### Who it's for
* **Legal and Compliance Teams:** Professionals who need to track how contract terms, amendments, and obligations have evolved over years of business.
* **Operations and Research Managers:** Teams that need to maintain an accurate, historical record of project leadership, resource allocation, and organizational changes.

### Key features
* **Historical Accuracy:** Uses a dual-timeline system to record both when an event happened and when the system learned about it.
* **Smart Document Ingestion:** Automatically processes various file types like PDFs, spreadsheets, and presentations, while syncing with common business tools.
* **Evidence-Based Chat:** Provides answers with direct citations to the original documents, ensuring you can always verify the source of a claim.
* **Self-Governing Ontology:** Learns your business vocabulary as you add information, allowing the system to categorize and link facts automatically.
* **Private and Secure:** Designed to run entirely on your own hardware, keeping sensitive business intelligence off public cloud platforms.

### How to install (Download)
Utopia is designed to be run via Docker. You can access the project and setup instructions at the official official project page:

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Keeps a complete audit trail of knowledge, runs entirely offline for maximum privacy, and provides verifiable evidence for every answer.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with container software to set up, and the system is still in early development (v0.1), meaning it is best suited for testing and controlled environments.
