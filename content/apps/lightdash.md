### What it does in plain English
Lightdash connects to your company's data storage to turn raw numbers into easy-to-read charts and dashboards. It allows your team to define important business metrics once so that everyone sees the same accurate information. You can even use simple language to ask questions and get instant insights without needing to write complex computer code.

### Who it's for
* **Data Analysts** who want to spend less time building repetitive reports and more time finding insights.
* **Business Managers** who need to check key performance indicators and create custom reports without waiting for technical support.

### Key features
* **Unified Metrics:** Define your business logic in one place so every chart and report stays consistent across the entire company.
* **Conversational Analytics:** Ask questions in plain English and get immediate answers powered by your own trusted data.
* **Self-Service Reporting:** Create custom dashboards and data apps that allow non-technical team members to explore information independently.
* **Developer-Friendly Workflow:** Manage your analytics projects using standard professional tools, allowing for easy reviews and updates.
* **Flexible Embedding:** Display your charts and data tools directly inside your own company software or websites.

### How to install (Download)
The easiest way to get started is to use the [Lightdash Cloud service](https://www.lightdash.com/start), which requires no setup. If you prefer to host it on your own hardware, you can follow the [Official Self-Hosting Guide](https://docs.lightdash.com/self-host/self-host-lightdash).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Keeps data consistent across teams, supports natural language questions, and integrates well with professional data workflows.
* **Cons:** Setting up a self-hosted version requires some technical knowledge of server management, and the platform is best suited for teams already using a data warehouse.
