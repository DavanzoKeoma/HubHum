### What it does in plain English
When you are building a website, the standard status screens are often filled with scrolling text that is difficult to read. This tool transforms that messy information into a clean, professional dashboard that looks like a high-tech control center. It helps you monitor your project's progress and identify errors instantly without digging through endless lines of text.

### Who it's for
* Web developers who want a clearer way to monitor their project's status while they work.
* Teams who want to quickly spot errors or performance issues during the website creation process.

### Key features
* **Visual Status Overview:** Replaces scrolling text with organized, easy-to-read charts and tables.
* **Instant Error Detection:** Highlights problems clearly so you can fix them before they become major issues.
* **Customizable Layout:** Allows you to change colors and display settings to fit your personal workflow.
* **Wide Compatibility:** Works across major operating systems including macOS, Windows, and Linux.

### How to install (Download)
This tool is installed via the standard Node.js package manager. You can add it to your project by running the following command in your terminal:

`npm install --save-dev webpack-dashboard`

For detailed setup instructions and configuration options, please visit the [Official Releases and Documentation page](https://github.com/FormidableLabs/webpack-dashboard/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, significantly improves readability of development logs, and highly customizable.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of command-line tools to set up, and some advanced mouse-scrolling features may vary depending on your specific terminal software.
