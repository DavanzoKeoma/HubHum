### What it does in plain English
CloudStream is a free media player for your phone or TV that plays videos and audio without showing any advertisements. It starts empty, so you choose which sources to add, such as YouTube, Twitch, or public internet radio channels. This gives you full control over what you watch and how you access it, without any data tracking.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-focused viewers:** People who want to watch videos and listen to audio without their viewing habits being tracked or sold to advertisers.
*   **Tinkerers and customizers:** Users who like to set up their own media library by adding specific sources or channels that fit their personal interests.
*   **Android TV owners:** Individuals looking for a lightweight, free alternative to commercial streaming apps on their living room screen.

### Key features
*   **Completely ad-free:** The app does not display any commercial interruptions or pop-ups during playback.
*   **No data tracking:** Your viewing history and personal information are not collected or shared with third parties.
*   **Flexible source system:** You can add different "extensions" to connect to various media sources like YouTube, Twitch, or public IPTV lists.
*   **Multi-device support:** Works on standard Android phones, tablets, and Android TV boxes.
*   **Chromecast support:** You can easily cast your content from your phone to a compatible TV or speaker.
