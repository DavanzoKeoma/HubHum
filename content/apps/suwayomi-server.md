### What it does in plain English
Suwayomi-Server acts as a central hub that organizes your digital manga collection and keeps track of your reading progress. It connects to various online sources to automatically fetch new chapters and updates your library without manual effort. You can then access your organized collection from any web browser or compatible mobile app.

### Who it's for
* Dedicated manga readers who want a single, organized library for all their digital comics.
* Users who want to read their collection across multiple devices while keeping their progress perfectly synced.

### Key features
* **Centralized Library:** Keep all your manga in one place with automated organization and sorting.
* **Automatic Updates:** Automatically checks for and downloads new chapters as they are released.
* **Offline Reading:** Download your favorite series to read them even when you are not connected to the internet.
* **Cross-Platform Sync:** Track your reading progress across different devices and apps.
* **Flexible Access:** Use the built-in web interface or connect your preferred mobile reader app to the server.
