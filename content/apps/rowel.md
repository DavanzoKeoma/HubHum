### What it does in plain English
Rowel keeps all your passwords, digital keys, payment cards, and confidential configuration files safely locked inside an encrypted vault on your own hardware. It operates entirely offline without forcing you to sign up for accounts or monthly subscription fees. You can optionally sync your encrypted vault files across your personal cloud storage accounts so your data stays updated on all your devices.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who want total control over their personal passwords and sensitive login credentials without renting cloud storage.
* Software developers and IT professionals who need a secure place to store API keys, SSH credentials, and environment configuration files.
* Privacy-focused individuals looking for an open-source security tool that never stores unencrypted data on external servers.

### Key features
* **Local-first vault storage:** Your data remains encrypted on your own hard drive and works completely offline.
* **Easy data migration:** Import existing credentials and secure notes seamlessly from popular apps or browser exports.
* **Developer-friendly items:** Dedicated categories for SSH keys, environment variables, and secure application secrets.
* **Optional private sync:** Connect your own Google Drive or Dropbox storage to synchronize encrypted data between devices.
* **Built-in security audits:** Scan your vault for weak passwords, leaked credentials, and security health issues.
