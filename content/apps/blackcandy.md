### What it does in plain English
Black Candy is a personal music server that allows you to store your audio files in one place and listen to them from anywhere. It organizes your library and provides a clean interface for playback on your computer or mobile phone. By hosting it yourself, you keep full control over your music collection without relying on third-party subscription services.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who have large local collections of audio files and want to access them on the go.
* Privacy-conscious users who prefer to keep their media files on their own hardware rather than in the cloud.

### Key features
* **Self-hosted control:** You own the server and the data, ensuring your music library stays private.
* **Cross-platform access:** Listen to your music through a web browser or via dedicated mobile apps.
* **Flexible storage:** Easily map your existing music folders from your computer to the server.
* **Automatic metadata:** Optionally connect to music databases to pull in artist and album artwork automatically.
* **Simple setup:** Uses container technology to get your music server running in minutes.

### How to install (Download)
Black Candy is designed to be run using Docker. You can find the official images and setup instructions on the [Black Candy GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/blackcandy-org/blackcandy/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, complete ownership of your music, and works on both desktop and mobile.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of how to run containerized software, and you must provide your own storage hardware.
