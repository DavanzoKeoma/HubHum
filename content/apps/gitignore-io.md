### What it does in plain English
When you work on files and want to share only the important ones, you need a list that tells the system which files to ignore.  
This tool creates that list for you in seconds, based on the language, software foundation, or editor you use.  
You can copy the result and paste it into your project, keeping unwanted files out of your version history.

### Who it's for
- People who keep track of changes to their files and need a quick way to exclude temporary or build files.  
- Students or hobbyists working on coding projects who want a ready‑made ignore list.  
- Small teams starting a new project and looking for an instant solution.

### Key features
- Generates .gitignore content for many programming languages and tools.  
- Works directly in your browser, no installation required.  
- Provides ready‑to‑copy text you can paste into your project.  
- Free to use and the generated files are placed in the public domain.  
- Allows you to combine multiple technologies in a single list.

* **Pros:** free forever, no signup needed, instant results in the browser.
