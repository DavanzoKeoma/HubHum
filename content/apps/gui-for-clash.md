### What it does in plain English
It gives you a clear window to set up Clash the way you like.  
You can turn connections on or off with a click.  
Everything updates instantly so you always know what’s active.  

### Who it's for
People who want to tweak their internet settings without learning command lines.  
Travelers who need to switch between different network rules quickly.  
Home users who want a tidy way to manage their connection tools.  

### Key features
- Easy point‑and‑click setup for all your Clash rules.  
- Live view shows which rules are currently active.  
- Quick switches let you enable or disable profiles in seconds.  
- Looks clean on Windows, macOS and Linux.  
- Saves your settings automatically.  

### How to install (Download)
[Visit the official website to download the app](https://gui-for-cores.github.io/)  

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, works on all major desktop systems, no need to type commands.  
* **Cons:** Requires a working Clash installation first, advanced settings may need a brief read of the guide.
