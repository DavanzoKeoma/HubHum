### What it does in plain English
This project provides specialized software packages that bridge the gap between standard Linux operating systems and Microsoft Surface hardware. It enables proper functioning of touchscreens, trackpads, styluses, power management, and specialized internal chips that standard operating systems do not recognize out-of-the-box. By applying these custom updates, users can experience reliable day-to-day performance on laptops and tablets originally designed exclusively for commercial operating systems.

### Who it's for
* Owners of Microsoft Surface laptops or tablets who want to switch to or use Linux.
* Advanced computer users looking to fix missing touchscreen, pen, or keyboard inputs on Surface devices.
* Developers and enthusiasts testing open-source software on specialized portable hardware.

### Key features
* Adds support for the specialized internal controllers found in Microsoft Surface devices.
* Enables responsive touch, gesture, and digital pen processing on supported screens.
* Manages device power efficiency to preserve battery life during everyday tasks.
* Delivers regular updates tailored specifically to address unique Surface hardware quirks.
