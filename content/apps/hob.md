### What it does in plain English
Hob gives you a private, easy-to-use assistant right on your desktop that works entirely offline. It automatically configures top open-source models to fit your computer hardware specifications seamlessly. You can ask questions, analyze documents, and complete everyday writing tasks without relying on external internet servers.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to chat with AI without sharing personal data with third-party companies.
* Freelancers and professionals who need an offline assistant to help sort through documents and spreadsheets.
* Everyday computer users looking for a free, subscription-free alternative to mainstream AI chatbots.

### Key features
* Runs completely offline to keep all your personal documents and conversations strictly private.
* Automatically selects and sets up the best AI model suited for your specific computer hardware.
* Handles complex tasks like reviewing sales spreadsheets, filling out PDFs, and summarizing text files.
* Free and open-source software with no hidden fees, subscriptions, or login walls.
