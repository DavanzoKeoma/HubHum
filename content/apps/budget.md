### What it does in plain English
Budget is an open-source financial tracking application designed to help you understand where your money goes. You can log transactions, attach digital copies of your receipts, and organize everything using custom tags. It automatically generates visual charts and weekly email summaries so you can review your financial health at a glance.

### Who it's for
* Individuals wanting a private, self-hosted way to track personal spending without sharing data with third-party corporations.
* Households looking to manage shared expenses and monitor multiple currencies in one organized place.
* Anyone who prefers importing bank statements via CSV and reviewing clear weekly financial summaries.

### Key features
* Log, edit, and organize financial transactions effortlessly.
* Group spending habits using custom tags and categories.
* Upload and attach receipts directly to your transactions.
* Import existing financial records quickly using standard CSV spreadsheets.
* Visualize weekly balances and highest-expense categories through clean charts.
* Multi-currency and multi-language support for international use.

### How to install (Download)
You can use the ready-to-use hosted version directly on the [Official Budget Website](https://usebudget.com). Alternatively, if you prefer to host it on your own server, you can follow the setup instructions and download the files from the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/range-of-motion/budget).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, supports multiple currencies, lets you keep full ownership of your financial data.
* **Cons:** Requires technical setup and server knowledge if you choose to self-host rather than use the web service.
