### What it does in plain English
Piik is a simple way to share your computer screen, video games, or movies with up to 20 friends at once. You simply start a session, send a link to your friends, and they can watch directly in their web browser. It is designed for privacy, keeping your media streams between you and your guests.

### Who it's for
* Friends who want to watch movies or play games together remotely.
* People who need a quick, private way to show their screen to others without setting up complicated streaming accounts.

### Key features
* **No installation required:** Friends can join your stream instantly using just their web browser.
* **Direct connections:** Media is sent directly between participants whenever possible to keep things fast and private.
* **Interactive tools:** Includes built-in voice chat, text messaging, and reactions to keep everyone connected.
* **Remote control:** You can choose to let friends take control of your mouse or keyboard to play games together.
* **Flexible hosting:** Use the online version for quick sessions or host it on your own server for full control.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no account creation needed, and supports remote control for collaborative gaming.
* **Cons:** Requires a stable internet connection for smooth video, and some advanced features may require a bit of setup if you choose to host it yourself.
