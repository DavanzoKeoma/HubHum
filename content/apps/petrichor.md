### What it does in plain English
Petrichor is a dedicated offline music player built specifically for Apple computers. It scans your chosen folders to organize your entire song library, song details, and album artwork into a clean, easy-to-browse interface. Whether you enjoy high-resolution audio files, internet radio, or customized smart playlists, it handles your personal collection without needing an internet connection.

### Who it's for
* Audiophiles with large collections of local music files stored on their computer.
* Mac users looking for a native, fast, and distraction-free alternative to bloated streaming software.
* Listeners who want advanced audio features like gapless playback, crossfade, and equalizer controls.

### Key features
* Supports over 40 popular and high-resolution audio file formats.
* Organizes music by folders, artists, albums, and custom smart playlists.
* Streams internet radio and allows you to download synchronized lyrics automatically.
* Integrates smoothly with native macOS controls, dark mode, miniplayer, and AirPlay 2.
* Keeps your library completely private and offline with no forced user accounts or tracking.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, completely offline privacy, supports an extensive range of audio formats, native macOS integration.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS 14 or later, relies on well-organized file metadata for the best library experience.
