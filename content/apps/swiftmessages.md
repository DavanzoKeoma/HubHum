### What it does in plain English
It shows a small banner or popup inside your app. You can place it at the top, bottom or middle of the screen. You can change its colors, text and icons to match your design.

### Who it's for
iOS developers who want to add quick alerts, designers who need a ready‑made notification look, and product teams that need a simple way to inform users.

### Key features
- Show messages at the top, bottom or center of the screen.  
- Easy to change text, colors, icons, and add shadows.  
- Supports swipe‑to‑dismiss, tap‑to‑hide and other interactive gestures.  
- Works with both UIKit and SwiftUI.  
- Comes with ready‑made layouts you can copy and edit.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, open‑source, works with UIKit and SwiftUI, highly customizable, includes ready‑made designs.  
* **Cons:** requires adding the library to your Xcode project, some initial setup needed, no visual editor for the UI.
