### What it does in plain English
SkyEye is an all-in-one business management platform built for modern organizations, factories, and offices. It handles daily administrative tasks, customer relationships, warehouse inventory, and production workflows in a single workspace. With built-in artificial intelligence, teams can quickly generate documents, automate testing, and build custom forms without writing complex code.

### Who it's for
* Growing manufacturing companies looking to digitize their workshop and supply chain operations.
* Small to medium businesses needing unified human resources, inventory, and office administration tools.
* Teams and administrators wanting to build custom workflows and forms through drag-and-drop visuals.

### Key features
* Combines office administration, CRM customer tracking, and manufacturing execution in one system.
* Features drag-and-drop design tools to create custom business forms and approval workflows without coding.
* Includes intelligent document assistants for automatic summaries, meeting notes, and form autofill.
* Supports multi-device access across computer web browsers, smartphones, and tablets seamlessly.
