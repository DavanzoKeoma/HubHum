### What it does in plain English
jev-chat-jarvis acts as a smart decision assistant that reads incoming conversations to analyze the other person's true intent and risk level before you reply. It then instantly drafts ranked response suggestions that you can insert into your chat box with a single tap. You always remain in complete control because the app never sends messages on its own and requires your manual confirmation to hit send.

### Who it's for
* Busy professionals who want to navigate complex everyday digital conversations and negotiations with greater confidence.
* Mobile users looking for a thoughtful overlay assistant to help manage difficult or high-stakes social chat moments.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want AI chat assistance without handing their data over to unknown central servers.

### Key features
* **Deep Intent Analysis:** Evaluates conversations to determine the other person's exact mood, hidden meaning, and situational risk.
* **Ranked Drafts:** Automatically generates multiple smart reply options, checks them against the context, and ranks the best choices first.
* **One-Tap Insertion:** Fills your chosen response directly into your messaging app's input box so you can review and press send.
* **Bring Your Own Keys:** Connects securely directly to your chosen AI model provider using your personal private API key.
* **Multi-Platform Design:** Available for Android overlay use, desktop companion apps, and custom keyboard setups.
