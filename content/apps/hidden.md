### What it does in plain English
Hidden is a simple helper application for Mac computers that gets rid of visual clutter at the top of your screen. It allows you to tuck away rarely used status icons behind a neat little arrow button. You can show or hide them instantly with a single click whenever you need them.

### Who it's for
* Mac users who have too many tiny status icons taking up space across the top of their screen.
* Minimalists who want a clean, distraction-free desktop environment during presentations or focused work.
* Anyone who prefers organized system controls without complicated software configurations.

### Key features
* Hide or reveal extra status items instantly using a clean toggle arrow.
* Hold a simple keyboard shortcut and drag items to organize them effortlessly.
* Keep deeply hidden items tucked away in a secondary group for rare use.
* Choose from convenient reveal methods like mouse hover, scrolling, or shaking.
* Completely lightweight and designed specifically to run smoothly on modern macOS versions.
