### What it does in plain English
It builds the special boot files that let a regular PC start macOS.  
The program gathers information about your hardware and adds the right settings automatically.  
This saves you hours of manual tweaking and reduces mistakes.

### Who it's for
* Anyone who wants to try macOS on their own computer.  
* Users who have built a PC and need a simple way to prepare it for macOS.  
* Hobbyists who prefer a guided setup rather than digging through complex guides.

### Key features
* Detects your CPU, GPU, and other parts and picks compatible settings.  
* Adds necessary patches and drivers automatically.  
* Checks for the latest versions of the boot loader and required files before creating the package.  
* Lets you review and adjust settings before the final build.  
* Produces a ready‑to‑copy boot folder that can be placed on a USB drive.

### How to install (Download)
* **Download the program:** [Download ZIP (OpCore‑Simplify)](https://github.com/lzhoang2801/OpCore-Simplify/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)  
* Extract the ZIP file to a folder of your choice.  
* **Run the tool:**  
  * On Windows, double‑click `OpCore-Simplify.bat`.  
  * On macOS, double‑click `OpCore-Simplify.command`.  
  * On Linux, run `OpCore-Simplify.py` with your Python interpreter.  

Follow the on‑screen menus to select your hardware report, choose a macOS version, customize options if you wish, and finally build the boot folder.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** completely free, works on Windows/macOS/Linux, automates most of the hard work, creates a ready‑to‑use boot folder.  
* **Cons:** still requires basic understanding of macOS installation, occasional manual tweaks may be needed, relies on external tools for creating the USB installer.
