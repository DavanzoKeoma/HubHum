### What it does in plain English
CloudCLI gives you a user-friendly graphical interface for advanced coding assistants like Claude Code, Cursor CLI, and Codex. It lets you view active projects, browse files, track changes with Git, and chat with your assistants from your phone or tablet while away from your computer.

### Who it's for
* Developers who want to monitor or prompt their coding agents while away from their main workstations.
* Mobile-first creators looking to review code snippets, manage file trees, and handle quick project updates on the go.
* Teams and individuals who want a clean visual window into their command-line artificial intelligence sessions without typing terminal commands constantly.

### Key features
* **Responsive Design** - Clean interface built to work smoothly on desktop screens, tablets, and smartphones.
* **Integrated Shell Terminal** - Direct access to your coding assistants through a built-in terminal window.
* **File Explorer** - Interactive file tree featuring syntax highlighting and live editing capabilities.
* **Git Explorer** - Easily view, stage, update changes, and switch branches right from your screen.

* **Pros:** Free and open source, works across mobile and desktop devices, auto-discovers existing local sessions.
* **Cons:** Requires a separate subscription for your chosen coding assistant, advanced options require familiarity with command-line tools.
