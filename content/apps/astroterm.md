### What it does in plain English
Astroterm turns your computer's command window into a digital planetarium that displays the night sky. It calculates the exact positions of stars, planets, and constellations based on any date, time, or location you choose. You can watch the sky move in real-time or look back at historical celestial events without needing any specialized equipment.

### Who it's for
* Amateur astronomers who want a quick way to check what is visible in the sky tonight.
* Students or hobbyists interested in space and celestial mechanics who enjoy working in a text-based environment.

### Key features
* **Time Travel:** View the sky as it appeared in the past or how it will look in the future by setting custom dates and times.
* **Global Coverage:** See the stars from any location on Earth by entering a city name or specific coordinates.
* **Visual Details:** Displays moon phases, constellation stick figures, and star brightness levels using simple text characters.
* **Lightweight Performance:** Built to be fast and efficient, providing a smooth experience directly in your terminal window.
