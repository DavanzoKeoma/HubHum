### What it does in plain English
yutto is a straightforward tool designed to help you download videos, series, and educational content from Bilibili. It allows you to save individual clips or entire collections to your device so you can watch them whenever you want without needing an internet connection. The tool handles the technical details behind the scenes, ensuring you get the best quality available for your saved files.

### Who it's for
*   Students or researchers who want to save educational courses for offline study.
*   Casual viewers who want to keep a personal library of their favorite Bilibili series or videos.

### Key features
*   **Flexible Downloads:** Save single videos or entire series with a simple command.
*   **Quality Selection:** Automatically detects and offers various video and audio quality options.
*   **Subtitle Support:** Generates and saves subtitles alongside your video files.
*   **Batch Processing:** Easily download multiple episodes or entire playlists at once.
*   **Lightweight:** Runs efficiently on your computer without requiring heavy background processes.

### How to install (Download)
yutto is primarily a command-line tool. For most users, the easiest way to install it is via a package manager or Python's tool installer:

*   **Homebrew (macOS):** `brew tap siguremo/tap` followed by `brew install yutto`.
*   **Arch Linux:** Install via AUR using `yay -S yutto` or `paru -S yutto`.
*   **Python (pipx/uv):** If you have Python 3.11+ installed, use `pipx install yutto` or `uv tool install yutto`.

*Note: You must have [FFmpeg](https://yutto.nyakku.moe/guide/quick-start#ffmpeg-下载与配置) installed on your system for the tool to process video files correctly.*

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, supports batch downloading, and handles high-quality streams.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with typing commands in a terminal window to operate.
