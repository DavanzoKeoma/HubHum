### What it does in plain English
Open CoDesign is a local desktop application that lets you type a simple description and instantly generates a fully interactive design prototype, slide deck, or marketing mockup. Instead of relying on rigid cloud-only services, it runs directly on your own computer and lets you connect to whichever intelligent AI model you already use. You can easily view your design history, click directly on any element to request specific changes, and export clean, editable files without ever worrying about subscription lock-in.

### Who it's for
* **Product Designers & Creators:** Quickly spin up landing page concepts, UI layouts, and presentation decks from simple text ideas.
* **Freelancers & Developers:** Build interactive app prototypes for client pitches and export real code files without needing expensive cloud tools.
* **Privacy-Conscious Users:** Maintain full ownership of your creative files and design sessions locally on your own laptop.

### Key features
* **Local-first workspace:** Keeps all your project files, history, and workspace assets saved securely right on your own machine.
* **Multi-model flexibility:** Bring your own keys or sign in easily to use top AI models like Claude, GPT, Gemini, and local offline setups.
* **Interactive feedback:** Click directly on any part of your generated preview to drop a pin and instruct the model to rewrite just that region.
* **Multi-format exports:** Save your completed work as standard HTML packages, PowerPoint presentations, PDF documents, ZIP archives, or Markdown files.
* **Built-in demos:** Jumpstart your workflow instantly using fifteen included professional layout starters and design system tools.
