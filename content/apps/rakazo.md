### What it does in plain English
Rakazo is a private, customizable assistant platform that lets you run your own capable helpers with dedicated memory, automated routines, and secure sandbox computers. It connects to popular cloud models while keeping your data under your complete control across web, desktop, and mobile devices. You can organize multiple assistants into collaborative team chats where they solve complex tasks together and share live dashboard reports.

### Who it's for
* Power users and developers who want private, controllable artificial intelligence assistants that run locally or on their own servers.
* Remote teams looking for collaborative virtual teammates to automate routine workflows and share live progress reports.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to choose their own models and secure storage without relying on third-party cloud platforms.

### Key features
* **Personal Virtual Computers:** Every assistant gets its own browser, terminal, file system, and workspace environment to complete multi-step tasks.
* **Collaborative Group Chats:** Put multiple assistants into a single chat room where they divide labor, cross-check work, and report back.
* **Automated Routines:** Set assistants to run on a predictable schedule, trigger from incoming emails, or respond to custom webhooks.
* **Live Dashboards:** Assistants can generate fully formatted web pages and documents that you can open, share, and query directly.
* **Cross-Device Access:** Seamlessly synchronize your conversations and assistant team across web browsers, desktop apps, and mobile phones.
