### What it does in plain English
mirotalkwebrtc gives you full control over your online video meetings by letting you run your own secure conferencing platform. You can schedule virtual rooms, send email invitations, and manage user accounts from a clean dashboard. Because you host it yourself, your meeting data stays entirely under your control without relying on third-party corporations.

### Who it's for
* Remote teams who want an independent, private space for daily video syncs and discussions.
* Community organizers and educators looking to host structured virtual events with secure guest access.
* Privacy-conscious organizations that need complete ownership of their communication infrastructure.

### Key features
* Personal user dashboard for scheduling and managing virtual meeting rooms.
* Shareable web links and email invitation system for easy participant access.
* Optional subscription gating powered by Stripe for running paid membership platforms.
* Complete self-hosting support via Docker and local database configuration.
* Open-source flexibility allowing full custom branding and configuration.
