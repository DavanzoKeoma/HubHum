### What it does in plain English
Enchanted provides a clean, familiar chat interface on your Apple devices that connects directly to your own self-hosted smart models. It keeps your conversations completely private and offline-capable without sending your personal data to third-party servers. You can easily switch models, attach images, and search through past conversations from anywhere in your workflow.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to chat with smart models locally without sharing personal prompts.
* Apple ecosystem users looking for a native, polished chat companion across their phone, computer, and headset.
* Developers and tech enthusiasts running their own local smart model servers.

### Key features
* Seamless connection to locally hosted private smart models.
* Clean design that feels right at home on Apple devices.
* Full conversation history stored safely on your own device.
* Support for dark mode, voice prompts, and image attachments.
* Quick access panel on your computer to start chatting instantly.

### How to install (Download)
Get the app directly from the official Apple ecosystem catalog:
* [Download from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/enchanted-llm/id6474268307)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete privacy, works offline, clean user interface across all Apple devices, free open-source code.
* **Cons:** Requires a separate local smart model server setup to function, advanced networking configuration may take a few minutes if not using public access.
