### What it does in plain English
FeelUOwn is a music player designed to give you full control over your listening experience. It gathers music from various sources into one clean interface, allowing you to manage your library and playlists efficiently. Because it is highly flexible, you can tweak how it looks and behaves to perfectly match your personal preferences.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who want a single, unified place to manage local files and online streaming sources.
* Users who enjoy customizing their software and want a player that can be tailored to their specific workflow.

### Key features
* **Unified Library:** Easily combine your local music files with various online streaming services.
* **Highly Customizable:** Use configuration files to change how the player works, similar to how you would set up a professional text editor.
* **Smart Features:** Includes handy tools like desktop lyrics, automatic resource replacement, and multiple audio quality options.
* **AI Integration:** Explore new music through AI-powered radio stations and natural language playlist generation.
* **Programmatic Control:** Advanced users can control the player through external scripts or programs for a truly automated experience.

### How to install (Download)
You can find detailed installation instructions for Windows, macOS, and various Linux distributions on the [Official Documentation Page](https://feeluown.readthedocs.io/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly modular with plugin support, and very lightweight.
* **Cons:** The advanced customization features may take some time to learn for non-technical users.
