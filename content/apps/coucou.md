### What it does in plain English
Coucou acts as a friendly little virtual character that lives in your computer screen's notch or top corner, alerting you whenever background software needs your attention. It lets you review tasks, approve security permissions, and chat with helpful digital models without ever pulling you away from your current window. Furthermore, it brings voice dictation, meeting summaries, and remote mobile notifications right to your fingertips.

### Who it's for
* Software developers running background automated coding tools who want quick approval prompts without switching windows.
* Busy professionals who need reliable voice-to-text dictation and automatic meeting notes without installing intrusive meeting bots.
* Everyday computer users who enjoy a delightful desktop companion that organizes everyday tasks and system integrations.

### Key features
* Real-time monitoring of popular programming agents directly from your screen's top edge.
* Quick one-click permission approvals so you can authorize operations instantly.
* Offline voice dictation that pastes your spoken words into any application seamlessly.
* Call recording and transcription that generates summaries and to-do lists locally.
* Mobile device synchronization to receive alerts and manage tasks from your phone.
