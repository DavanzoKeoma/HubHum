### What it does in plain English
TimeOff.Management helps small and medium-sized teams keep track of who is in the office and who is away. It replaces messy spreadsheets with a clear calendar where employees can request time off and managers can approve it quickly. The system automatically updates everyone's schedule so the whole team always knows who is available.

### Who it's for
* **Small Business Owners:** Who need a low-cost way to manage staff holidays without hiring expensive HR software.
* **Team Managers:** Who want to see at a glance which team members are on leave without checking individual emails.
* **HR Administrators:** Who need to track vacation balances and ensure company policies are followed consistently.

### Key features
* **Flexible Views:** See absences on a calendar, by team, or in a simple list to fit your workflow.
* **Custom Leave Types:** Create specific categories like sick days, maternity leave, or working from home with their own rules.
* **Approval Workflow:** Employees request time off, managers get an email notification to approve or deny, and the calendar updates automatically.
* **Calendar Sync:** Connects with Google Calendar, Outlook, and other calendar apps so your team's availability is visible everywhere.
* **Mobile Friendly:** Both employees and managers can handle requests and approvals directly from their phones.

* **Pros:** Free to use, easy to set up, works on mobile devices, and allows for detailed customization of company policies.
* **Cons:** The self-hosted version requires some technical knowledge to install and maintain, and advanced features may require reading documentation.
