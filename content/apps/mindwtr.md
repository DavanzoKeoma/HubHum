### What it does in plain English
Mindwtr helps you clear your head by providing a simple place to store every task, idea, or project you need to remember. It guides you through a proven method for breaking down big goals into small, actionable steps so you can focus on what matters right now. Because it works entirely on your own device, your personal plans stay private and accessible even when you are offline.

### Who it's for
*   Busy professionals who need a reliable system to manage projects without the distraction of social features or productivity scores.
*   Privacy-conscious individuals who want to keep their to-do lists stored locally on their own devices rather than in a cloud-based service.

### Key features
*   **Capture Anywhere:** Quickly save tasks from your desktop or phone using simple shortcuts and widgets.
*   **Guided Workflow:** Follows a structured approach to help you sort, prioritize, and review your tasks weekly.
*   **Complete Privacy:** No account is required, and all your data remains on your device by default.
*   **Flexible Sync:** You choose how to sync your data, whether through a private folder, your own server, or standard cloud storage services.
*   **Import Support:** Easily bring in your existing lists from other popular task management tools.
