### What it does in plain English
Seal is a simple app for your Android phone that saves videos and music from the internet. You can paste a link from a website, and the app will download the file for you to watch or listen to offline. It handles both full videos and just the audio tracks, making it easy to keep your favorite content on your device.

### Who it's for
*   **Students and Learners:** People who want to save educational videos or lectures to watch later without using data.
*   **Offline Travelers:** Users who need to download movies or music before a trip to enjoy them without an internet connection.
*   **Privacy-Focused Users:** Individuals who prefer free, open-source tools that do not track their activity or show intrusive ads.

### Key features
*   **Wide Website Support:** Works with hundreds of popular video and audio platforms, including major streaming sites.
*   **Audio Extraction:** You can choose to download only the sound from a video, which is great for saving music or podcasts.
*   **Playlist Downloading:** Save entire lists of videos with a single click instead of downloading them one by one.
*   **Subtitle Integration:** Automatically adds subtitles to your downloaded videos if they are available on the original site.
*   **Custom Commands:** Allows advanced users to create specific download rules for different types of content.
