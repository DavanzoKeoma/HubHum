### What it does in plain English
Berty is a private messaging application that works completely independently of traditional telecommunication networks. It connects your devices directly to others nearby using Bluetooth and local network technologies rather than central servers. This ensures your conversations remain entirely confidential and resilient even during severe network outages or government censorship.

### Who it's for
* Travelers and explorers needing reliable communication in remote areas with weak or absent cellular signals.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to chat without sharing phone numbers, email addresses, or personal metadata.
* Communities living under strict internet censorship or experiencing local network shutdowns.

### Key features
* **End-to-End Encryption:** Protects your messages by default so only you and your intended recipient can read them.
* **Offline Functionality:** Continues sending and receiving messages locally via Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi without any internet access.
* **No Sign-Up Required:** Lets you start chatting instantly without creating an account using a phone number or email.
* **Serverless Architecture:** Operates on a decentralized peer-to-peer model where no third-party company stores your data.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, requires no personal phone number or email, and works entirely without an internet connection.
* **Cons:** Still under active development so advanced features may evolve, and heavy peer-to-peer usage can consume additional battery power.
