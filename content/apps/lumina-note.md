### What it does in plain English
Lumina-Note is a clean, modern workspace for writing and organizing your thoughts using everyday Markdown files stored securely on your computer. It features a built-in intelligent assistant that can read through your entire collection of notes to draft new pages, summarize complex documents, and automatically connect related ideas. You keep complete ownership of your data while taking advantage of flexible AI options, ranging from local privacy-first models to popular cloud services.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers managing extensive literature notes, reference materials, and study flashcards.
* Writers, bloggers, and creators looking to organize research and draft articles in a distraction-free environment.
* Knowledge workers who want an intelligent co-pilot to help structure and retrieve information across a growing digital notebook.

### Key features
* **Human-first file control:** Keeps all your notes as plain text files on your local drive so you never get locked into a proprietary format.
* **Intelligent vault assistant:** Operates securely across your entire notebook to reorganize folders, find connections, and answer questions about your content.
* **Connected thinking tools:** Supports direct links between notes, visual graph maps, and side-by-side live previews for effortless navigation.
* **Flexible AI model choices:** Connect your preferred intelligence provider, use local models privately via Ollama, or bring your own API keys.
* **Extensible workspace:** Customize your editing experience with themes, plugins, and custom commands tailored to your workflow.
