### What it does in plain English
Perspec fixes slanted, uneven photos of flat surfaces like documents, book pages, receipts, and building facades so they look completely flat and professional. When you drop an image into the app, it automatically finds the corners of your document and squares them off with computer vision. You can easily adjust the borders manually, save the corrected result in color or crisp black and white, or combine multiple photos into a searchable PDF.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers digitizing book pages or lecture whiteboards into clean digital files
* Office workers and administrators turning phone snapshots of paper forms and receipts into neat digital scans
* Photographers and architects straightening building facades and rectangular planar objects

### Key features
* Automatic corner detection that instantly finds the edges of documents and flat objects
* Drag-and-drop batch processing to fix dozens of photos in a row quickly
* Multiple saving modes including high-contrast black and white, grayscale, color, and smooth document filtering
* Built-in text recognition that turns your photos into searchable PDF documents
* Zoom and manual fine-tuning controls to position crop corners precisely where you want them
