### What it does in plain English
When an AI assistant makes a mistake and ruins your entire coding session, you usually have to restart and explain everything all over again. This application lets you directly open, find, and edit the exact past message where the AI went off track. The assistant then picks up your corrected history as if the mistake never happened, saving you valuable time.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and programmers who rely on local command-line and desktop AI assistants.
* Power users who want to clean up irrelevant chat noise and inject missing context into past discussions.

### Key features
* **Direct History Editing:** Modify any past message in your AI chat history to correct false assumptions immediately.
* **Safe Write-Backs:** Rewrites only the modified message line while keeping the rest of the session file untouched, complete with automatic backups.
* **Multi-Platform Support:** Works seamlessly across a wide variety of local coding assistant tools and prompt utilities in a single interface.
* **Audit Trail:** Tracks every single modification with a clear before-and-after comparison log for total peace of mind.
* **100% Offline Privacy:** Runs completely on your personal device with zero cloud dependency or external network traffic.
