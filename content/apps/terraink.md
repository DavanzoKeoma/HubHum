### What it does in plain English
Terraink is an interactive design tool that lets you generate stunning visual posters of any city or neighborhood on Earth. It uses real geographic data to map out roads, parks, rivers, and buildings into clean, professional graphic layouts. You can tweak color palettes, adjust custom fonts, and instantly export high-resolution art files ready for printing.

### Who it's for
* Graphic designers and home decor enthusiasts wanting custom wall art of their favorite cities.
* Travelers looking to commemorate memorable trips with personalized geographic keepsakes.
* Gift creators designing unique, meaningful art pieces for friends and family.

### Key features
* **Global map coverage:** Generate map layouts for any location worldwide using live geographic data sources.
* **Flexible color themes:** Choose from professionally curated palettes or build your own custom color scheme.
* **Layer customization:** Fine-tune individual styles for roads, water bodies, parks, and building footprints.
* **Typography controls:** Add city and country titles using a selection of clean, built-in display typefaces.
* **High-resolution export:** Save your final design in multiple file formats tailored for physical printing.
