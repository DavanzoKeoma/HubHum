### What it does in plain English
Serve turns any folder on your computer into a mini-website that others on your local network can view. It provides a clean, organized list of your files so you can easily browse or download them from another device. It is a simple way to preview your own projects or share documents without needing to upload them to a public server.

### Who it's for
* Web designers who need to preview their work on different devices like phones or tablets.
* Students or office workers who need to quickly share a folder of documents with someone else on the same Wi-Fi network.

### Key features
* **Instant Setup:** Start sharing a folder with a single command.
* **Clean Interface:** Automatically generates a user-friendly list view for your files.
* **Network Ready:** Makes your local content accessible to any device connected to your home or office network.
* **Customizable:** Allows you to adjust settings using a simple configuration file if you need specific behavior.

### How to install (Download)
To use this tool, you must have Node.js installed on your computer. Once installed, you can run the following command in your terminal:

`npm install --global serve`

After installation, navigate to the folder you want to share in your terminal and type `serve`.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, lightweight, and requires no complex setup or account registration.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with using a terminal or command prompt to start the service.
