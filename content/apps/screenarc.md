### What it does in plain English
ScreenArc is a smart video recording and editing utility that automatically follows your mouse movements and clicks to create smooth cinematic zoom effects. It removes the need for tedious manual keyframing by keeping viewers focused on the most important action on your screen. You can record your desktop, window, or webcam, customize backgrounds and frames, and instantly export high-definition videos tailored for any platform.

### Who it's for
* Software developers creating technical tutorials or feature walkthroughs
* Educators and online instructors preparing engaging course material
* Content creators and marketers producing product demos and social media clips

### Key features
* **Automatic Cinematic Zoom:** Smoothly follows your mouse cursor and clicks to keep viewers engaged without manual editing.
* **Flexible Capture:** Record your entire screen, a single application window, or a custom area with multi-monitor support.
* **Webcam Overlay:** Add a personal, professional touch by embedding your live webcam feed into the recording.
* **Visual Timeline Editor:** Easily trim clips, adjust frames, modify background colors, gradients, wallpapers, and drop shadows.
* **Instant Aspect Ratios:** Switch between standard widescreen (16:9), vertical formats (9:16), or square (1:1) with one click.
* **High-Quality Export:** Save your final projects as clean MP4 files or animated GIFs in resolutions up to 2K.
