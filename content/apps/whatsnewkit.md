### What it does in plain English
It helps you show a short, attractive page that highlights what’s new in your app.  
You can pick the look, the words and the icons to match your style.  
It works on iPhone, Mac and Vision Pro apps without extra hassle.

### Who it's for
* Developers who want to inform users about each update.  
* Designers looking for a quick way to present new features.  
* Product managers who need a simple way to communicate version changes.

### Key features
- Simple way to display new features.  
- Works with SwiftUI, UIKit and AppKit.  
- Supports iPhone, Mac and Vision Pro.  
- Choose automatic or manual showing.  
- Layout can be adjusted to fit your design.

* **Pros:** free and open source, works across all Apple platforms, fully customizable, no extra runtime cost.  
* **Cons:** limited to Apple ecosystems, requires some Swift coding, no pre‑built installer.
