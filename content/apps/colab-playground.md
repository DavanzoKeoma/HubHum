### What it does in plain English
It lets you start and stop Google Colab compute sessions right from your computer.  
It shows you how much you are spending and can stop sessions automatically when you set limits.  
It also lets you forward a runtime’s network ports to your machine and add extra tools called plugins.

### Who it's for
Students who run experiments on Colab, freelancers who need occasional GPU power, and hobbyists who want to keep cloud costs under control.

### Key features
- Simple setup that finds or installs the needed command‑line tool for you.  
- Manage multiple Google accounts and see each balance at a glance.  
- Choose CPU, GPU or TPU and set a minimum GPU memory to stay within budget.  
- Automatic stop and spending caps protect you from unexpected charges.  
- Forward ports from the cloud session to your local computer and run community plugins.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, open‑source, cost‑guard features, multi‑account support, plugin system.  
* **Cons:** only a Windows installer is provided, requires the Google Colab command‑line tool, advanced settings need a short learning curve.
