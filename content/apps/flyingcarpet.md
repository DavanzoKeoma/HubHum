### What it does in plain English
FlyingCarpet allows you to move photos, documents, and large files between your phone and computer without needing a cloud service or a data plan. It works by creating a direct, private connection between two devices that are near each other. Whether you are at home or on the go, you can share files instantly without relying on external servers.

### Who it's for
* People who need to move large files between their mobile devices and computers without using a USB cable.
* Travelers or remote workers who need to share data in places where there is no reliable internet or public network access.

### Key features
* **Universal Compatibility:** Works across Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.
* **No Internet Required:** Uses direct wireless connections so your data never leaves your local environment.
* **Flexible Modes:** Choose between creating a temporary direct hotspot or using an existing local network.
* **Secure Transfers:** Every file is encrypted during the transfer process to keep your personal data private.
* **Simple Setup:** Uses QR codes to quickly pair devices and start sending files immediately.
