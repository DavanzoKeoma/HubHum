### What it does in plain English
Kirby is a modern website publishing tool that stores your text, images, and pages directly as plain files instead of using a complex database. This file-based approach makes your website exceptionally fast, simple to back up, and entirely secure against common database attacks. Creators can easily design their own editing screens and organize content exactly how they want it to look.

### Who it's for
* Independent web designers and developers who want total control over website structure.
* Content creators and editors looking for a clean, distraction-free writing interface.
* Small business owners needing a fast, secure website that is easy to update.

### Key features
* Stores all content in simple text files rather than heavy database tables.
* Highly customizable control panel tailored to your specific project needs.
* Built without a rigid template structure so you control every single line of code.
* Excellent documentation and community support for quick troubleshooting.
* Lightweight core that loads pages instantly for all visitors.
