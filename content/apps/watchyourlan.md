### What it does in plain English
WatchYourLAN acts as a digital sentry for your home or office network by constantly scanning for connected devices. It automatically notifies you the moment a new device joins, so you always know exactly who is on your Wi-Fi. You can also view the history of when specific devices were online or offline to better understand your network activity.

### Who it's for
* Home users who want to ensure no unauthorized devices are using their internet connection.
* Tech-savvy individuals who want to keep a detailed log of their smart home devices and network traffic.

### Key features
* **Instant Alerts:** Get notified immediately when a new device appears on your network.
* **Activity History:** Keep a clear record of when devices connect or disconnect over time.
* **Simple Web Interface:** Manage and view your network status through an easy-to-use browser dashboard.
* **Data Export:** Send your network data to external visualization tools to create custom performance charts.
* **Lightweight Design:** Runs efficiently on low-power hardware without slowing down your connection.
