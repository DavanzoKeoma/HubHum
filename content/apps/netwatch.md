### What it does in plain English
netwatch acts like a live dashboard for your computer's internet connection, showing you exactly which programs are using data and how fast they are going. It automatically checks for common problems, like slow servers or blocked connections, and suggests simple fixes to get things running smoothly again. You can see this information in a clear, easy-to-read window that updates in real-time as you use your computer.

### Who it's for
*   **Tech-curious users:** People who want to understand why their internet feels slow or why certain apps are using so much data.
*   **Home network managers:** Individuals who manage their home Wi-Fi and want to spot devices or connections that are misbehaving.
*   **Remote workers:** Professionals who need to ensure their video calls and file transfers are stable and identify network hiccups before they become a problem.

### Key features
*   **Live Connection View:** See every active internet connection, the program using it, and the speed of each one instantly.
*   **Automatic Problem Detection:** The tool learns what "normal" looks like for your network and alerts you when something goes wrong, such as a slow DNS server.
*   **Speed and Latency Graphs:** Visualize your download and upload speeds and the time it takes for data to travel to different servers.
*   **Simple Fix Suggestions:** When an issue is detected, netwatch ranks the likely causes and provides clear steps to resolve them.
*   **Cross-Platform Support:** Works on Windows, macOS, Linux, and FreeBSD, so you can use it on almost any computer you own.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscription required, provides clear visualizations of complex network data, and automatically identifies common connection issues.
*   **Cons:** Requires a command-line interface to launch (though the interface itself is graphical), and some advanced features like deep packet inspection require elevated system permissions.
