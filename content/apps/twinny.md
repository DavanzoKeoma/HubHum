### What it does in plain English
Twinny acts as a smart companion inside your code editor that suggests lines of code as you type and answers questions about your project. It keeps all your data private by letting you choose exactly where the AI runs, whether on your own computer or a private company server. Because it works locally, you don't need to worry about your code being sent to third-party companies or stored in the cloud.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want AI assistance without sharing their proprietary code with external companies.
* Teams that need a secure, self-hosted AI environment to manage coding tasks and reviews.

### Key features
* **Real-time suggestions:** Get helpful code completions that appear as you type, tailored to your current project.
* **Interactive chat:** Ask questions about your files, search your workspace, or get help with complex coding problems.
* **Private and secure:** Run everything on your own hardware or a private network gateway, ensuring your data never leaves your control.
* **Flexible setup:** Connect to local models on your machine or use hosted services depending on your hardware needs.
* **Team collaboration:** Use a shared gateway to manage AI access, usage, and policies for your entire development team.

* **Pros:** Completely private with no telemetry, supports local models, free for individual use, and highly customizable.
* **Cons:** Requires some initial setup to connect your preferred AI models, and performance depends on your local hardware or server capacity.
