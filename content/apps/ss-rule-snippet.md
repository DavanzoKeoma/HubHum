### What it does in plain English
SS-Rule-Snippet provides clean, optimized, and lightweight rule sets for popular network routing and traffic management software across multiple devices. Instead of bogging down your device with bloated configurations, it keeps things minimal so your system runs faster and more reliably. By keeping your routing instructions streamlined, it reduces battery drain, prevents crashes, and improves overall connection efficiency.

### Who it's for
* Power users and tech enthusiasts who want to optimize their network routing tools on mobile and desktop devices.
* Anyone looking for clean, pre-tested configuration snippets instead of writing complex routing logic from scratch.

### Key features
* Carefully curated, lightweight rule lists designed to avoid heavy memory usage and system slowdowns.
* Comprehensive support for popular traffic management platforms across iOS, macOS, Windows, and other operating systems.
* Modular organization splitting rules into basic routing, specific application optimization, and functional enhancements.
* Regular community-driven updates to keep pace with changing network requirements and new application domains.
