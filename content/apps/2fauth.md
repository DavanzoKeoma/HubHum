### What it does in plain English
2FAuth acts as a digital vault for your two-factor authentication accounts, allowing you to generate security codes directly from your browser. It removes the need to constantly reach for your smartphone when logging into websites from a computer. By hosting it yourself, you keep full control over your security data and can easily back it up to prevent losing access to your accounts.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want to own their security data rather than relying on big tech cloud services.
* Desktop users who are tired of picking up their phone every time they need to enter a login code.

### Key features
* **Organized Access:** Group your various accounts to keep your dashboard clean and easy to navigate.
* **Quick Setup:** Add new accounts instantly by scanning QR codes or using a simple manual entry form.
* **Universal Compatibility:** Generates standard security codes used by almost all modern websites and services.
* **Enhanced Privacy:** Offers optional data encryption and auto-logout features to keep your information safe from prying eyes.
* **Flexible Hosting:** Designed to run on your own server, giving you complete ownership of your authentication database.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, puts you in full control of your data, works on both desktop and mobile browsers.
* **Cons:** Requires basic technical knowledge to set up on your own server, as it is not a simple "one-click" desktop program.
