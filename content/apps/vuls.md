### What it does in plain English
Vuls looks through the software on your Linux, FreeBSD, container or WordPress sites and tells you which known security problems exist.  
It does this without installing any extra programs on the machines you are checking.  
You get a clear report that shows exactly which items need attention.

### Who it's for
* System administrators who manage many servers and need a reliable way to stay on top of security alerts.  
* Developers who want to verify that their containers, libraries or WordPress sites are free from known flaws.  
* Small‑business IT staff who prefer a free, command‑line tool that works across many operating systems.

### Key features
- Scans Linux, FreeBSD, macOS, Windows, Docker containers and WordPress sites without needing an agent.  
- Pulls data from major vulnerability databases such as NVD, JVN, Red Hat OVAL and Microsoft CVRF.  
- Generates regular reports that can be sent by email or posted to Slack.  
- Offers fast scan modes that run with little load on the target machines, even when offline.  
- Can also check programming‑language libraries and network devices for known issues.
