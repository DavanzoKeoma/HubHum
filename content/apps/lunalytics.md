### What it does in plain English
Lunalytics is a modern web-based tool that keeps a close eye on your websites, servers, and online services around the clock. If something goes offline or encounters an error, it immediately triggers notifications through your favorite communication apps. It features a clean, collaborative interface that allows multiple team members to manage status pages and monitor system health together.

### Who it's for
* System administrators who want a collaborative status dashboard for their team and colleagues.
* Small business owners who need reliable uptime tracking without expensive subscription fees.
* Self-hosting enthusiasts looking for a clean, modern alternative to traditional monitoring tools.

### Key features
* Tracks HTTP(s), TCP connections, pings, Docker containers, and custom JSON queries.
* Built-in support for multiple users with advanced role-based access control.
* Fully customizable status pages and user themes to match your brand.
* Flexible notification routing including Discord, Telegram, Slack, and email.
* Simple self-hosting options via container tools or direct installation.

### How to install (Download)
To set up Lunalytics, visit the [Official Website](https://lunalytics.xyz) or explore the community discussion on [Discord](https://discord.gg/HkpDnUdf4u). You can also run it instantly using container tools or by cloning the source from the official official project page.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Clean modern user interface, multi-user access control, free and open source.
* **Cons:** Requires a server or hosting environment to keep running 24/7.
