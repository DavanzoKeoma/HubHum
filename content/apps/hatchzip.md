### What it does in plain English
HatchZip is a clever utility that unpacks large compressed folders by deleting pieces of the archive as it goes. Traditional extractors require double the space because they keep the original archive while creating new files right next to it. By shrinking the archive during extraction, this tool lets you unpack massive downloads even when your hard drive is nearly full.

### Who it's for
* Gamers dealing with massive multi-gigabyte game files on nearly full storage drives.
* Power users and everyday enthusiasts who regularly handle large compressed file collections.
* Anyone who has ever run out of disk space halfway through unpacking a giant archive.

### Key features
* Deletes extracted archive segments on the fly to reclaim valuable disk storage immediately.
* Supports popular archive formats including ZIP, 7z, RAR, tar, and split volume sets.
* Performs safety checks before modifying anything to ensure the operation will fit on your drive.
* Resumes interrupted extractions seamlessly after a system crash, power loss, or pause.
* Works smoothly across Windows, macOS, and Linux with both desktop and command line interfaces.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Saves massive amounts of temporary disk space, free and open source, supports pause and resume functionality.
* **Cons:** Consumes and removes the source archive by default unless changed in settings, not all legacy encrypted formats support in-place handling.
