### What it does in plain English
ODS installs and connects all the complex software required to run artificial intelligence directly on your own computer. It handles the heavy lifting of setting up chat interfaces, image generators, and automation tools so you don't have to do it manually. Because everything runs on your own hardware, your data stays private and you never have to pay for a subscription.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious users** who want to use AI tools without sending their personal files or conversations to big tech companies.
*   **Home lab enthusiasts and creators** who want a professional-grade AI setup for research, coding, or image generation without spending hours on technical configuration.

### Key features
*   **One-Command Setup:** Automatically detects your hardware and installs a complete, pre-wired AI stack.
*   **All-in-One Dashboard:** Manage your models, check your system's performance, and control services from a single, easy-to-use interface.
*   **Local Privacy:** Your chat history, documents, and generated images remain on your machine, with no telemetry or tracking.
*   **Extensible Design:** Easily add new features or services by enabling them through the built-in extension system.
*   **Hardware Aware:** Intelligently selects the best-performing AI models based on your specific computer's memory and graphics power.

### How to install (Download)
ODS provides simple, automated installers for all major operating systems. Please visit the official community page to get started:

*   [Join the ODS Community & Get Started](https://discord.gg/qGVygYada3)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to use, keeps your data private on your own hardware, and automates complex technical setups.
*   **Cons:** Requires a computer with decent hardware (especially a good graphics card) for the best experience, and the initial setup requires a significant amount of free disk space.
