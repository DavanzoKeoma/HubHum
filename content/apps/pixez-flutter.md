### What it does in plain English
PixEz Flutter is a free app that lets you browse and view images from the popular art community Pixiv. It works directly on your device, so you do not need to use a special internet tool to access the content. You can also view animated images smoothly, making it a simple way to enjoy digital art on any screen.

### Who it's for
* **Art enthusiasts** who want to easily browse and save images from Pixiv on their mobile devices or computers.
* **Casual users** who find the official website difficult to access or navigate and prefer a simpler, app-based experience.
* **Students and professionals** who need a lightweight, free tool to view reference images without paying for subscriptions.

### Key features
* **Direct Access:** Connects to Pixiv content without requiring additional internet configuration tools.
* **Animated Image Support:** Plays animated images smoothly, which is often difficult in other viewers.
* **Multi-Platform:** Available for Android, Windows, Linux, and iOS, so you can use it on almost any device.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** Designed with a clean layout that makes it easy to find and view images quickly.
