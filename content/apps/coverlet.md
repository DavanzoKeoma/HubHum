### What it does in plain English
Coverlet is a tool that tracks how much of your software project is covered by automated tests. It highlights exactly which lines, branches, and methods of your code are being checked during the testing process. By providing this visibility, it helps you ensure your software is reliable and that no critical parts of your work are left untested.

### Who it's for
* Software developers working on .NET projects who want to improve the quality of their code.
* Teams looking to maintain high standards by ensuring their automated tests are actually checking the intended parts of their application.

### Key features
* **Comprehensive Tracking:** Monitors line, branch, and method coverage to give you a complete picture of your testing status.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on Windows, macOS, and Linux environments.
* **Flexible Integration:** Offers multiple ways to run tests, including direct integration with standard testing tools and build systems.
* **Detailed Reporting:** Generates clear reports in various formats so you can easily review your testing progress.
