### What it does in plain English
MiMo-Code acts as a smart partner that lives inside your computer's terminal to help you write and organize software projects. It remembers your past work across different sessions, so it always understands the context of your files and goals. You can ask it to perform tasks like managing project files, running commands, or searching for information, all without leaving your coding environment.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who prefer working within the terminal rather than using graphical interfaces.
* Students or hobbyists who want an intelligent assistant to help them learn and maintain their coding projects.

### Key features
* **Persistent Memory:** Automatically remembers your project history and decisions so it doesn't have to relearn your setup every time you start.
* **Smart Task Handling:** Can break down complex requests into smaller, manageable steps and work on them in the background.
* **Flexible Model Support:** Connects to a wide range of popular AI models, allowing you to choose the one that fits your needs best.
* **Built-in Research Tools:** Includes specialized skills for searching the web, reading academic papers, and analyzing data.
* **Voice Control:** Allows you to interact with your coding assistant using your voice for faster input.

### How to install (Download)
* **macOS / Linux:** Open your terminal and run: `curl -fsSL https://mimo.xiaomi.com/install | bash`
* **Windows (PowerShell):** Run: `powershell -ep Bypass -c "irm https://mimo.xiaomi.com/install.ps1 | iex"`
* **Desktop Version:** For a visual interface, download the [Xiaomi MiMo Desktop](https://mimo-ai.xiaomimimo.com/desktop/) (Global version).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Highly efficient for terminal users, remembers project context across sessions, and supports many AI providers.
* **Cons:** Requires basic comfort with terminal commands and may need specific setup for certain features like voice input or web search.
