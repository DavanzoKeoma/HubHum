### What it does in plain English
itlwm adds native wireless networking capabilities to computers running macOS that utilize Intel Wi-Fi hardware. It acts as a translator between the computer operating system and the physical wireless card installed inside the machine. This allows custom hardware setups or non-standard Apple computers to connect to wireless internet networks reliably.

### Who it's for
* Enthusiasts building custom macOS computer systems using standard PC parts.
* People trying to replace incompatible wireless cards in existing portable computers.
* Advanced users looking to maintain network connectivity after operating system updates.

### Key features
* Connects Intel wireless adapters directly to the macOS network stack.
* Based on proven network architectures from the open-source community.
* Supports a wide variety of popular Intel wireless chip generations.
* Maintained actively with documentation and community support channels.

### How to install (Download)
* Read the introductory guidelines and compatibility list on the [itlwm Documentation Page](https://OpenIntelWireless.github.io/itlwm).
* Download the latest driver packages directly from the [itlwm Releases Page](https://github.com/OpenIntelWireless/itlwm/releases).
* Review troubleshooting steps and common questions on the [itlwm FAQ Page](https://openintelwireless.github.io/itlwm/FAQ).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, brings internet connectivity to unsupported hardware combinations, active developer community.
* **Cons:** Requires technical understanding of custom computer configurations, setup steps involve manual installation procedures.
