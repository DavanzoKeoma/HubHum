### What it does in plain English
AppManager helps you inspect, clean up, and organize every app installed on your phone or tablet. It reveals hidden background activities, scans for privacy trackers, and lets you back up your files securely. You can also clean out unwanted bloatware and take charge of what your apps are allowed to do behind the scenes.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious smartphone users who want to stop hidden background trackers and data collection
* Power users looking for a single tool to manage backups, permissions, and app installations
* People wanting to clean up pre-installed system clutter and free up storage space

### Key features
* Inspect all internal components, background services, and permissions of any app
* Scan applications to uncover hidden trackers and data collection code
* Install, share, and back up your apps with encrypted archives
* Clean out unwanted system bloatware safely
* View real-time error logs and manage background tasks smoothly
