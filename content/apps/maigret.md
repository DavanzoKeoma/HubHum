### What it does in plain English
Maigret automates the process of finding where a specific username exists across thousands of popular online platforms and forums. It parses public profile pages to gather available details, links, and associated accounts into a comprehensive report. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad).

### Who it's for
- Journalists and researchers investigating public digital footprints and online accounts.
- Security professionals assessing exposure and digital identity reuse risks.
- Individuals looking to audit their own public profile visibility across the web.

### Key features
- Scans thousands of websites and community platforms for matching usernames instantly.
- Extracts profile details, bios, and links to secondary accounts automatically.
- Exports clean investigation reports in HTML, PDF, Markdown, and visual graph formats.
- Optional AI-powered summary generation to synthesize findings into plain English.
- Ready-to-use digital planners, printable checklists, and clean document templates for daily organization via [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
