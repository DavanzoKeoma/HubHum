### What it does in plain English
TwinDesk merges all your computers, tablets, and phones into a single cohesive workspace without needing any cloud accounts or shared networks. You can push your mouse cursor off the edge of your laptop screen so it glides right onto your desktop monitor, complete with shared clipboard copying and file dragging. It also lets you answer phone calls, view mobile notifications, and control devices remotely while keeping your data entirely encrypted and private.

### Who it's for
* Professionals and students who juggle a work laptop, personal computer, and tablet on the same desk.
* Power users looking for an open-source alternative to ecosystem-locked cross-device utilities without paying subscriptions.
* Anyone wanting a secure, local-only way to share files, text, and screens between Mac, Windows, Linux, and mobile phones.

### Key features
* **Seamless Multi-Device Navigation:** Move your mouse cursor and type across different operating systems as if they were a single computer setup.
* **Shared Clipboard and Drag-and-Drop:** Copy text or images on one machine and instantly paste them onto another, or drag files across screen edges.
* **Phone Integration:** Mirror mobile notifications, answer calls, and access device photos directly from your computer workspace.
* **Local and Secure:** Operates entirely peer-to-peer over your local network, USB, or Bluetooth with end-to-end encryption and zero cloud storage.
* **Universal Compatibility:** Works across macOS, Windows, Linux, Android, and iOS devices with lightweight installers and web browser access options.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source forever, requires no cloud accounts or subscriptions, works completely offline via local Wi-Fi, USB, or Bluetooth.
* **Cons:** Unsigned first-party builds require manual security permission overrides on initial launch, and advanced multi-device layouts take a few moments to configure.
