### What it does in plain English
Sulu provides the foundation for building professional websites that need to handle complex content across multiple languages. It offers a clean, organized dashboard that allows teams to manage their site's structure and media without getting lost in technical details. By focusing on a smooth editing experience, it helps organizations keep their online presence updated and consistent.

### Who it's for
* Web developers looking for a robust, flexible base to build custom professional websites.
* Content managers and editors who need a reliable, user-friendly interface to update site information daily.

### Key features
* **Multi-lingual Support:** Easily manage content versions for different languages and regions from one place.
* **Intuitive Interface:** A clean, modern dashboard designed to make content updates simple and efficient.
* **Flexible Structure:** Allows for highly customized website layouts and content types to fit specific business needs.
* **Media Management:** Built-in tools to organize, upload, and manage images and files used across your site.

### How to install (Download)
To get started with your own project, visit the official [Sulu website](https://sulu.io/) for setup guides or use the [Sulu Project Template](https://github.com/sulu/skeleton) to begin your installation.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Highly flexible, excellent for complex multi-language sites, and completely free to use.
* **Cons:** Requires some technical knowledge to set up initially compared to simple drag-and-drop website builders.
