### What it does in plain English
Kaku is a high-performance command-line window designed specifically for Mac users who want a faster and more organized way to work. It comes pre-configured with helpful settings, so you can start working immediately without needing to spend hours on setup. The app also includes built-in support for AI assistants, allowing you to get coding suggestions and chat directly within your workspace.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and engineers who spend a significant amount of time working in a terminal.
* Power users who want a highly customizable, fast, and modern alternative to standard system tools.

### Key features
* **Ready to use:** Comes pre-configured with professional fonts, color themes, and standard Mac keyboard shortcuts.
* **Smart workspace:** Easily split your screen into multiple sections or tabs and automatically restore your setup when you reopen the app.
* **AI integration:** Use built-in tools to get command suggestions, chat with an AI assistant, or have code explained directly in your terminal.
* **Easy navigation:** Use your mouse to click links, open files, or manage tabs and windows without needing to memorize complex commands.
* **Remote access:** Share your terminal session via a web link to continue your work from another computer.
