### What it does in plain English
Kromacut is an open-source application that turns flat images into multi-colored, layered physical models for your 3D printer. It lets you upload artwork, automatically calculate the best sequence for your colored spools, and preview the final physical result from every angle. You can easily adjust colors, fine-tune layer heights, and export ready-to-print files directly to your slicer.

### Who it's for
* 3D printing enthusiasts looking to create vibrant, multi-color art pieces without owning an expensive multi-material nozzle system.
* Makers and hobbyists who want to turn personal photos, posters, or logos into eye-catching physical badges, coasters, and wall displays.
* Designers seeking a streamlined way to preview color blending and manage filament hiding distances for stacked printing.

### Key features
* **Smart Auto-Painting:** Automatically map your image colors to match the exact filaments you currently own using physics-based blending rules.
* **Comprehensive 3D Previews:** Inspect your project layer by layer in realistic 3D, wireframe, or transparent views before sending it to print.
* **Detailed Filament Calibration:** Measure and test how your specific spools blend together so you can predict exact color outcomes.
* **Flexible Image Editing:** Crop, resize, remove unwanted dithering, and retouch your artwork with built-in painting and text tools.
* **Universal Export Options:** Export standard STL files or color-aware 3MF files complete with manual layer-swap instructions.
