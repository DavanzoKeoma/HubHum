### What it does in plain English
Papra acts as a digital filing cabinet where you can store receipts, warranties, and important paperwork. It automatically scans your uploaded files so you can find exactly what you need later using simple keywords. You can organize your files with tags and folders, or even set up automatic rules to keep your digital space tidy without extra effort.

### Who it's for
*   **Home users** who want to digitize paper receipts and household warranties to clear out physical clutter.
*   **Small teams or families** who need a shared, organized space to keep important documents accessible to everyone.

### Key features
*   **Smart Search:** Quickly find any document using full-text search and custom filters.
*   **Automatic Organization:** Use tagging rules to automatically sort documents as you add them.
*   **Collaborative Spaces:** Create shared areas to manage documents with family members or colleagues.
*   **Secure Sharing:** Send documents to others with optional password protection and expiration dates.
*   **Flexible Access:** Use the web interface on any device, from your computer to your smartphone.

### How to install (Download)
Papra is a web-based platform. You can try it immediately without any setup by visiting the [Official Live Demo](https://demo.papra.app). If you prefer to host it on your own server for total privacy, follow the [Official Self-Hosting Guide](https://docs.papra.app/self-hosting/using-docker).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, highly customizable, and includes powerful text-extraction features.
*   **Cons:** Requires some technical knowledge to set up your own private server, though the web demo is ready to use instantly.
