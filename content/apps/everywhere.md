### What it does in plain English
Everywhere is a smart desktop helper that instantly understands whatever is visible on your computer screen. Instead of taking screenshots, copying text, or switching back and forth between windows, you simply press a shortcut key right where you are. It connects with your favorite artificial intelligence engines to answer questions, fix errors, and draft messages directly inside your active workflow.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who want to quickly summarize lengthy web pages or translate foreign words without leaving their text.
* Software developers and IT professionals looking to instantly analyze error messages and find troubleshooting solutions.
* Writers, marketers, and professionals who need fast help refining email drafts and checking factual accuracy on the fly.

### Key features
* Instantly perceives your active screen content using built-in system awareness without manual copying.
* Works smoothly with major artificial intelligence providers as well as private local models.
* Executes tasks across your computer through modern browser tools, file navigation, and helper scripts.
* Features a clean, frosted glass interface that blends naturally into your modern desktop environment.
* Supports global keyboard shortcuts so you can summon help at any moment without breaking your concentration.
