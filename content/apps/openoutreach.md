### What it does in plain English
OpenOutreach acts as a personal assistant that searches for people who are a perfect match for your business based on your own description. It explains exactly why each person is a good fit, so you always know why they were chosen. Finally, it sends personalized emails from your own account to start conversations without needing to upload lists or use external platforms.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners looking to find new clients without paying for expensive marketing databases.
* Freelancers and consultants who want to automate their outreach while keeping full control over their messaging.

### Key features
* **No list required:** Simply describe your product and target market, and the tool handles the discovery process automatically.
* **Transparent reasoning:** Every lead comes with a written explanation of why they fit your criteria, allowing you to refine your search by simply updating your description.
* **Privacy-focused:** Everything runs on your machine or your own server, ensuring you don't have to worry about social media account bans or platform restrictions.
* **Direct integration:** It works with your existing email account, making your outreach feel personal and authentic.
* **Pay-as-you-go:** Searching is free, and you only pay for verified contact information when you are ready to reach out.

### How to install (Download)
To get started, you can use the official command-line tool. Ensure you have the necessary environment set up and run:
`uv tool install openoutreach`

For those who prefer not to manage the installation themselves, you can use the hosted service at [OpenOutreach.app](https://openoutreach.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=openoutreach).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use for discovery, no monthly subscription for the software, and complete control over your data.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of how to run commands on your computer and requires your own API keys for the AI and email-finding services.
