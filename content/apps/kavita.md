### What it does in plain English
Kavita acts as your personal digital library center, letting you upload and organize your entire collection of comics, manga, webtoons, and books in one place. It hosts a private streaming server that formats your reading material cleanly for any phone, tablet, or computer screen. You can securely share your custom library with friends and family members while managing individual viewing permissions.

### Who it's for
* Comic book and manga collectors who want a gorgeous, centralized way to read their digital library anywhere.
* Families and groups of friends looking to pool and share their digital reading collections privately.
* Dedicated readers who want to sync their progress and notes directly with dedicated e-reader devices.

### Key features
* Supports a wide variety of file formats including standard comic archives, webtoons, electronic books, and raw images.
* Responsive built-in readers designed to look and feel natural on mobile touchscreens, tablets, and desktop displays.
* Advanced user management with role controls, age ratings, and secure authentication options.
* Smart filtering, automatic metadata organization, and custom reading lists to keep massive collections tidy.
* Dedicated e-reader synchronization to seamlessly transfer files and sync reading progress on supported devices.

* **Pros:** Fast performance, handles multiple formats effortlessly, works cleanly on any screen size, and respects user privacy through self-hosting.
