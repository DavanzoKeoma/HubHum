### What it does in plain English
Builder acts as a visual bridge that lets you design website pages by dragging and dropping elements exactly where you want them. It connects to your existing website setup, allowing you to turn static design files into functional, live web pages. You can make updates to your site's appearance instantly without needing to manually rewrite the underlying technical instructions.

### Who it's for
*   **Web Designers:** Professionals who want to turn their visual mockups into live pages without waiting for manual coding.
*   **Content Creators:** Marketing teams who need to update landing pages or site layouts quickly without relying on a technical team.

### Key features
*   **Drag-and-Drop Editor:** Move buttons, images, and text blocks around your screen to build pages intuitively.
*   **Design Conversion:** Automatically convert your existing design files into functional web components.
*   **Flexible Integration:** Works with many popular web technologies so you can keep your current site structure.
*   **Instant Publishing:** Push your design changes live to your website with a single click.

### How to install (Download)
Builder is a web-based platform. You do not need to download any software to your computer.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Significantly speeds up the design-to-live process, works with your existing site, and offers a user-friendly visual interface.
*   **Cons:** Requires an existing web project to connect to, and complex custom designs may still require some technical knowledge to fully integrate.
