### What it does in plain English
Flashpoint Launcher serves as your personal portal to a massive digital library of classic web games, animations, and interactive experiences. It organizes extensive collections into an easy-to-search interface so you can quickly find and launch nostalgic entertainment. The software handles everything behind the scenes, making it effortless to dive straight into vintage digital media without complicated setups.

### Who it's for
* Nostalgic gamers and digital culture enthusiasts wanting to replay classic web browser games.
* Researchers and historians working to preserve early internet animations and interactive media.
* Anyone looking to rediscover vintage web content that would otherwise be lost to time.

### Key features
* Organized library view for sorting through thousands of preserved games and animations.
* Fast search and filter tools to find specific titles, genres, or creators instantly.
* Built-in support for launching various vintage web formats securely on modern computers.
* Customizable configuration settings to manage your game storage folder and preferences.
* Active community updates and translation options for global accessibility.
