### What it does in plain English
Colanode provides a private, all-in-one digital workspace where teams can chat, write documents, and organize projects. Unlike many popular online tools, it uses a "local-first" approach, meaning your information is saved directly on your device before syncing with your team. This ensures you can keep working even without an internet connection while maintaining full ownership of your data.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious teams** who want to manage their own data without relying on big tech companies.
*   **Remote or hybrid workers** who need a reliable, offline-capable tool to stay organized and connected.

### Key features
*   **Real-Time Messaging:** Instant communication for teams and private conversations.
*   **Flexible Document Editor:** Create wikis, notes, and documents with an easy-to-use interface.
*   **Dynamic Databases:** Organize your work using tables, calendars, or project boards.
*   **Offline Access:** Work on your projects without an internet connection; your changes sync automatically once you are back online.
*   **Self-Hosting Options:** Advanced users can host their own server to ensure complete data sovereignty.
