### What it does in plain English
Graphic design software often leaves behind hidden metadata, comments, and extra code inside image files that you do not need. This utility reads your vector graphics and safely removes all that invisible clutter without changing how the image looks. As a result, your files become much smaller, helping websites and digital projects load much faster.

### Who it's for
* Web designers looking to optimize site graphics before publishing.
* Front-end developers aiming to speed up page load times.
* Digital artists wanting to clean up exported design assets.

### Key features
* Safely strips invisible editor metadata and comments from image files.
* Handles single files or processes entire folders recursively in seconds.
* Flexible plugin system to customize optimization rules and presets.
* Easy integration into your daily workflow and automated pipelines.

* **Cons:** Requires a basic command-line environment to operate.
