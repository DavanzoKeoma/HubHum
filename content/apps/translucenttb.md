### What it does in plain English
TranslucentTB is a small background program that transforms the look of your Windows desktop by making your taskbar see-through. You can choose to blur the background, give it a frosted glass effect, or tint it with any color you like. It automatically adapts when you open windows or maximize apps, giving your screen a clean and modern aesthetic.

### Who it's for
* Desktop personalization enthusiasts who want a sleek, modern look for Windows.
* Users who want to maximize their visible screen space with a minimalist taskbar style.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight system enhancement that runs quietly without draining battery or memory.

### Key features
* Choose from clear, blurred, frosted glass, or tinted taskbar styles.
* Dynamic modes that change taskbar appearance when windows open or maximize.
* Advanced color picker with live preview and transparency controls.
* Extremely lightweight, using only a few megabytes of memory.
* Compatible with popular desktop customization tools like RoundedTB and ExplorerPatcher.
