### What it does in plain English
This platform allows creators and developers to publish websites, documentation, or blogs directly from simple text files. By writing content locally and running a quick update command, your pages appear instantly across the live site and become accessible to both human readers and AI assistants. It eliminates complex content management systems in favor of clean, version-controlled text publishing.

### Who it's for
* **Writers and Bloggers:** People who prefer writing in simple text editors without wrestling with heavy publishing dashboards.
* **Developers and Documentation Teams:** Technical authors who want to manage project guides directly inside their existing code management workflows.
* **AI Builders:** Creators looking to make their knowledge bases instantly readable by both web browsers and automated language models.

### Key features
* **File-Based Publishing:** Write local text files and sync them across your live site without any complicated rebuild steps.
* **AI and Search Discovery:** Automatically generates helper files so search engines and AI agents can understand and index your content effortlessly.
* **Knowledge Graphs and Wikis:** Organizes your connected articles and documentation into searchable knowledge bases with visual connection maps.
* **Built-In Search and Chat:** Gives visitors the ability to search your content instantly or chat with an assistant to get answers about your site.
* **Multiple Themes:** Switch between clean light, dark, and neutral themes with adjustable typography to suit your reading style.

### How to install (Download)
To set up your own version of the platform, visit the [Official Setup Guide](https://www.markdown.fast/setup-guide) or follow the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/waynesutton/markdown-site) to clone and cloud setup your own instance.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, complete ownership of your files through version control, lightning-fast instant publishing.
* **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with text editing and command-line tools to set up your initial site.
