### Why this curated guide
Keep your everyday documents, receipts, study notes, and reference libraries fast, searchable, and portable.

### Featured highlights
* **[Joplin](/HubHum/apps/joplin/)** (*Desktop App*): Organize private notes, checklists, and web clips across your devices with end-to-end encryption and offline access.
* **[Stirling-PDF](/HubHum/apps/stirling-pdf/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Edit, convert, and secure your PDF documents locally without uploading your sensitive files to third-party web services.
* **[SiYuan](/HubHum/apps/siyuan/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): SiYuan is a secure, self-hosted tool for organizing your notes, documents, and ideas with powerful linking and AI assistance.
* **[Trilium](/HubHum/apps/trilium/)** (*Self-Hosted Web App*): Trilium is a hierarchical note-taking tool that helps you organize massive amounts of information into a searchable, connected library.
* **[CC Switch](/HubHum/apps/cc-switch/)** (*Desktop App*): Switch between different AI service providers with a single click and organize your prompts, plugins, and helper tools in one unified dashboard.
* **[orca](/HubHum/apps/orca/)** (*Desktop App*): Orca lets you run multiple AI coding assistants at the same time, giving each helper its own dedicated workspace so they never interfere with each other.
