### What it does in plain English
Aether gives you a place to talk, post, and build a community that isn’t run by a big company.  
Everything you share stays private and belongs to the members who create it.  
You can join or start groups that follow their own rules without any outside interference.

### Who it's for
* People who want a safe space to discuss topics without corporate oversight.  
* Hobby groups, clubs, or friends looking for a private online hangout.  
* Anyone who values control over their own data and conversations.

### Key features
- Simple chat and posting tools for everyday conversation.  
- Ability to create and manage your own independent community.  
- No central authority can read or control your content.  
- Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux computers.  

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free to use, keeps your conversations private, runs on all major desktop systems.  
* **Cons:** still in developer preview, may be unstable, collects usage metrics for debugging.
