### What it does in plain English
Startup‑Landing gives you a collection of ready‑made landing pages that are free to use. Each template is mobile‑friendly and ready to be customized. You can download, edit, and host them on any web server.

### Who it's for
- Small business owners looking for a quick online presence.  
- Freelancers wanting a professional portfolio.  
- Startups needing a polished product page.

### Key features
- Free to download and use.  
- Mobile‑optimized designs.  
- Weekly updates with new niches.  
- Easy to edit with simple HTML/CSS.  
- No coding required for basic changes.
