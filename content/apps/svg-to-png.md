### What it does in plain English
This tool converts vector graphics into standard image files by using your web browser's built-in rendering engine. Because browsers are designed to display complex web graphics, this tool captures your images exactly as they were intended to look. It handles advanced features like transparency and high-resolution scaling that often break in other conversion programs.

### Who it's for
* Graphic designers who need to export complex vector illustrations for web or print.
* Developers who create custom graphics by hand and need a reliable way to preview and save them as standard images.

### Key features
* **Browser-Native Accuracy:** Uses the same engine that displays websites to ensure your graphics look perfect.
* **High-Resolution Support:** Easily save your images at larger sizes without needing to stitch together screenshots.
* **Transparency Preservation:** Keeps your image backgrounds clear, which is essential for professional design work.
* **Batch Processing:** Save time by converting multiple files at once.
* **Custom Adjustments:** Includes built-in tools to trim edges and colorize your images before saving.

* **Pros:** Completely free to use, works directly in your browser, no software installation required, and handles complex SVG features better than many desktop programs.
* **Cons:** Requires the fonts used in your SVG to be installed on your computer for text to render correctly.
