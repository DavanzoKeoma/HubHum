### What it does in plain English
NodeWarden runs a password manager on Cloudflare Workers.  
It works with all Bitwarden clients and keeps your data in the cloud.  
You can use it offline and sync across devices.  

### Who it's for
- People who want a private password vault without a paid server.  
- Developers who want to host their own manager.  
- Anyone who uses Bitwarden clients and wants a free, offline‑ready alternative.  

### Key features
- Bitwarden‑compatible API and clients.  
- Offline‑ready web vault that can be installed as a PWA.  
- Built‑in TOTP and passkey support.  
- Cloud backup via WebDAV or S3.  
- Multi‑user with invite‑code registration.
