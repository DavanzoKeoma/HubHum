### What it does in plain English
IceCubesApp lets you use the Mastodon social network on your Apple device.  
It shows your home, local, federated, and trending timelines in a clean layout.  
You can post, reply, and manage multiple accounts all from one app.

### Who it's for
- Social media users who want a lightweight Mastodon client.  
- Developers who want to learn how an app is built for Apple devices.  
- Anyone who likes AI‑assisted writing and push notifications.

### Key features
- Browse and filter timelines with a sidebar.  
- Post threads up to five posts and add images.  
- AI tools for text correction and hashtag suggestions.  
- Push notifications and in‑app notification grouping.  
- Support for unlimited accounts and iCloud sync.
