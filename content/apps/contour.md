### What it does in plain English
Contour is a modern, high-speed window for your computer's command line that keeps your work safe even if the window closes. It lets you organize multiple shells using tabs, split screens, and color-coded status badges that follow your system theme. Plus, it supports advanced graphics, text ligatures, and background sessions that survive reboots or dropped network connections.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and system administrators who spend hours in the command line and need a snappy, reliable terminal.
* Power users who want split panes, tab color-coding, and background session recovery after a reboot.
* Anyone looking for a modern, highly customizable terminal that feels right at home on Windows 11, macOS, or Linux.

### Key features
* **Persistent Sessions:** Background background daemon keeps your shells running even if you close the window or lose your network link.
* **Tabs & Split Panes:** Easily drag and drop panes or tabs anywhere on your workspace with color-coded status badges.
* **GPU-Accelerated Speed:** Fast rendering pipeline designed for minimal key-press latency under heavy load.
* **Rich Graphics & Text:** Full support for font ligatures, emojis, Unicode grapheme clusters, Sixel images, and kitty graphics protocols.
* **Keyboard-Driven Navigation:** Vi-like input modes, quick-search hint mode for URLs and paths, and a global quick-terminal hotkey.
