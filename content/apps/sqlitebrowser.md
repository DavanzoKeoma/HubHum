### What it does in plain English
DB Browser for SQLite is a friendly, visual tool that lets you create, design, and edit database files. It uses a familiar spreadsheet-like layout, so you can view and change your data without needing to write complicated technical code. It includes helpful assistants to guide you through tasks like importing data from files or creating new tables.

### Who it's for
* Data enthusiasts who need to view or edit local database files without becoming professional programmers.
* Students or hobbyists who want to organize information in a structured way using a simple, visual interface.

### Key features
* **Spreadsheet-style editing:** View and modify your data in a grid that feels just like a standard office spreadsheet.
* **Helpful Wizards:** Step-by-step tools make it easy to create new databases, tables, and indexes.
* **Data Import/Export:** Easily move information in and out of your database using common file formats like CSV or text files.
* **Visual Graphing:** Create simple charts and graphs directly from your data to better understand your information.
* **Query Inspector:** A dedicated space to run custom searches and view a log of all actions performed on your database.

* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, very user-friendly for non-experts, and works across all major operating systems.
