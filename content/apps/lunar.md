### What it does in plain English
Lunar gives you simple, direct control over the brightness and volume of your external monitors, which Macs often make hard to adjust. It can automatically dim your screens based on the light in your room or the time of day to reduce eye strain. You can also quickly switch between different monitor inputs or rotate the screen orientation without digging through system settings.

### Who it's for
*   **Mac users with external monitors** who find it frustrating that they cannot use the standard brightness keys on their keyboard to control their external displays.
*   **Late-night workers** who need to dim their screens below the normal minimum level to avoid disturbing others or straining their eyes in dark rooms.
*   **Multitaskers** who frequently switch between different devices connected to their monitor, such as a laptop, a game console, or a streaming box.

### Key features
*   **Hardware Brightness Control:** Adjusts the actual brightness of your monitor using its internal settings, rather than just darkening the image on your Mac.
*   **Adaptive Brightness:** Automatically adjusts screen brightness and contrast based on your location's sunrise/sunset times or the light sensor in your MacBook.
*   **Input Switching:** Quickly change which device is displayed on your monitor using a simple dropdown menu or custom keyboard shortcuts.
*   **Sub-Zero Dimming:** Allows you to lower the brightness below 0% for comfortable viewing in very dark environments.
*   **Screen Rotation:** Rotate your external monitor to portrait or landscape mode instantly using keyboard shortcuts or the menu bar.
