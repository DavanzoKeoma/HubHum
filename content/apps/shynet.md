### What it does in plain English
Shynet helps you understand how people use your website by tracking visits and page views. Unlike many other tools, it does not use cookies or collect personal information that identifies your visitors. Because you host the software yourself, you remain the sole owner of all your traffic data.

### Who it's for
* Website owners who want to respect their visitors' privacy.
* Developers or small business owners who prefer to keep their data off third-party servers.

### Key features
* **Cookie-free tracking:** Monitors site activity without needing intrusive cookie consent banners.
* **Privacy-first:** Collects only the essential data needed to understand your audience.
* **Flexible setup:** Can run on a single small server or scale up for larger projects.
* **No JavaScript required:** Uses a simple, lightweight tracking method that works even when scripts are disabled.
* **Self-hosted control:** You own your data entirely, with no third-party companies involved.

### How to install (Download)
Shynet is designed to be self-hosted. You can find the complete setup instructions and cloud setup guides in the [Official Usage Guide](https://github.com/milesmcc/shynet/blob/master/GUIDE.md#installation).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no third-party data sharing, and highly respectful of user privacy.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up and maintain on your own server.
