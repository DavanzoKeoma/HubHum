### What it does in plain English
ArchiveBox collects bookmarks, browser history, and links, saving offline copies of web pages, documents, and media automatically. It stores standard files like readable text, screenshots, and PDFs in organized folders on your own system. Protect remote administrator dashboards when managing your site on the go with [PureVPN](https://billing.purevpn.com/aff.php?aff=49386762&subid=hubhum_01_MER047) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Researchers and journalists who need permanent offline copies of web references and source documents.
* Digital archivists and librarians wanting to maintain independent backups of digital content.
* Everyday internet users who hate broken links and want to keep a personal library of favorite articles.

### Key features
* Saves standard, durable formats including HTML, screenshots, PDFs, and media files that remain readable for decades.
* Automatically captures content from social media platforms, video sharing sites, and code project pages.
* Supports scheduled imports from browser history, RSS feeds, and bookmark export files.

* Offers a self-hosted web dashboard alongside a command-line interface and optional desktop app.
