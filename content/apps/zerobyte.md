### What it does in plain English
Zerobyte is a tool that helps you automatically copy your important files to a safe location. It uses a modern web dashboard to let you schedule these copies, monitor their progress, and ensure your data is encrypted so only you can read it. You can store your backups on your own computer, on a network drive, or in various cloud storage services.

### Who it's for
* Home users who want a reliable, set-it-and-forget-it way to protect their personal photos and documents.
* Small business owners or hobbyists managing their own servers who need a clear, visual way to keep their data safe.

### Key features
* **Automated Scheduling:** Set your backups to run automatically so you never have to remember to do them manually.
* **Strong Encryption:** Your data is scrambled before it leaves your device, keeping it private and secure from unauthorized access.
* **Flexible Storage:** Choose where to save your files, including local folders, network shares, or popular cloud storage providers.
* **Clear Monitoring:** Use the simple web dashboard to check the status of your backups and restore files whenever you need them.
* **Smart Retention:** Automatically clean up old backup versions to save space while keeping the ones you actually need.
