### What it does in plain English
Tio provides a straightforward way to connect to serial hardware devices without complicated configuration menus. It automatically detects, connects, and reconnects to your hardware ports even if they get unplugged and plugged back in. You can also automate interactions using built-in scripting, log data to files, and stream serial output over network sockets.

### Who it's for
* Embedded hardware developers testing microcontrollers and sensors.
* Hobbyists and makers tinkering with electronics and DIY boards.
* Systems engineers monitoring equipment connected via serial cables.

### Key features
* Automatically finds and reconnects to serial ports when devices are unplugged or reinserted.
* Supports custom baud rates, data bits, parity settings, and flow control.
* Built-in file upload capabilities using X-modem and Y-modem protocols.
* Automation support using simple scripting to interact with connected devices.
* Logging options to save serial output sessions directly to plain text files.
