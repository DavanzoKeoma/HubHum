### What it does in plain English
This application transforms web-based music streaming services into a dedicated desktop program, freeing up your browser memory. You can control your playback effortlessly using your keyboard's media keys or desktop shortcuts without hunting through open browser tabs. It integrates seamlessly into your operating system to deliver smooth background audio playback and instant song notifications.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want a dedicated desktop application for their streaming services instead of keeping browser tabs open.
* Power users looking for global keyboard shortcuts to skip tracks, pause, and adjust volume while working in other programs.
* Listeners who want customizable themes, built-in lyrics support, and system tray integration for a cleaner workspace.

### Key features
* Runs streaming audio as an independent desktop program without requiring an active browser window.
* Supports standard media keyboard keys for play, pause, stop, and track skipping.
* Includes a compact mini-player mode and customizable dark themes with accent colors.
* Displays desktop notifications whenever a new song starts playing.
* Offers options to route audio to specific output devices directly from the app interface.
