### What it does in plain English
JobOps acts as a central command center for your job search by gathering listings from over ten different websites into one view. It uses smart technology to score how well your background fits a specific role and helps you rewrite your resume to match each job description. Finally, it monitors your email inbox to automatically update the status of your applications as you receive responses from recruiters.

### Who it's for
*   Job seekers who want to stop juggling dozens of browser tabs and spreadsheets.
*   Professionals looking to save time on tailoring resumes for multiple similar roles.

### Key features
*   **Unified Search:** Browse listings from major job boards and niche platforms in one place.
*   **Smart Scoring:** Get an instant rating of how well your profile matches a specific job posting.
*   **Resume Tailoring:** Generate customized versions of your resume for each specific job application.
*   **Automated Tracking:** Connect your email to automatically detect interview invites, rejections, and status updates.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** Keep your data local and under your control by hosting the tool yourself.

### How to install (Download)
JobOps is a web-based tool designed to be hosted on your own computer or server.
*   **Self-Hosting:** Follow the official [Self-Hosting Guide](https://jobops.dakheera47.com/docs/getting-started/self-hosting) to set it up using Docker.
*   **Cloud Version:** If you prefer not to host it yourself, you can use the [JobOps Cloud service](https://jobops.app/).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Keeps your entire job search history in one place, saves significant time on manual resume editing, and works with your existing email account.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with setting up software on a computer, and the initial setup may take a few minutes to configure.
