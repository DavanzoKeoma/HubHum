### What it does in plain English
ImageToolbox is a versatile mobile application designed to help you edit pictures without any hassle or hidden costs. It provides hundreds of creative filters, drawing tools, background erasers, and conversion options right in your pocket. Whether you want to resize a batch of photos, scan documents, or apply artistic effects, this tool handles it smoothly offline.

### Who it's for
* Mobile photographers looking for a free, feature-packed editing suite without ads or subscriptions.
* Everyday users who need a quick way to crop, resize, or remove backgrounds from photos on their phone.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who want a powerful picture editor that works completely offline without tracking.

### Key features
* Packed with hundreds of customizable filters and artistic effects.
* Batch processing to edit or convert multiple images at once.
* Built-in background remover and document scanner.
* EXIF data editor and PDF creation tools.
* Completely free and open-source with no advertisements or hidden tracking.
