### What it does in plain English
Kaneo shows all your team’s work on easy‑to‑read boards, calendars or timelines.  
You can add details, files, comments and track time right where the task lives.  
It keeps everything together so you spend less time searching and more time getting things done.

### Who it's for
- Small teams that need a clear view of who is doing what.  
- Project groups that want a simple way to organize tasks without learning complex software.  
- Remote collaborators who need live updates and shared workspaces.

### Key features
- Boards, lists, calendar and timeline views.  
- Attach files, write notes, add comments and track time on each task.  
- Share workspaces, invite teammates and set custom roles.  
- Automatic updates and email or notification alerts.  
- Connects with GitHub, Gitea and popular chat tools.
