### What it does in plain English
DigUp is a smart desktop search tool that lets you find files by describing their actual contents rather than just remembering file names. It scans your photos, PDFs, documents, audio clips, and videos locally on your device, matching concepts, spoken words, or objects automatically. Everything runs completely on your computer to keep your private data safe and secure without sending anything to the cloud.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and writers who need to quickly locate specific quotes, paragraphs, or pages across hundreds of documents.
* Photographers and media collectors looking for specific visual elements in large image and video archives.
* Everyday computer users who struggle to remember where they saved important files, receipts, or notes.

### Key features
* Search files across more than 100 languages using everyday descriptive phrases or exact terms.
* Jump directly to the exact page of a PDF, the specific minute of a recording, or the exact video frame.
* Runs 100% on-device for total privacy without requiring an internet connection or cloud accounts.
* Supports code projects to find specific functions, code snippets, or notebook cells instantly.
* Menu bar and system tray integration for quick access via convenient global keyboard shortcuts.
