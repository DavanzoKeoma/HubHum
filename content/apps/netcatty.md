### What it does in plain English
Netcatty acts as a central hub for connecting to and managing multiple remote computers. It provides a visual way to browse files, run commands, and monitor server health without needing to juggle different programs. With its built-in assistant, you can even use simple language to perform complex tasks across your servers.

### Who it's for
* **System Administrators:** Professionals who need to keep an eye on many servers simultaneously.
* **Web Developers:** Individuals who frequently upload files and run commands on remote web hosting environments.

### Key features
* **Unified Workspace:** Manage all your remote connections, file transfers, and terminal windows in one window with split-screen support.
* **Smart File Management:** Easily move files between your computer and your servers using a simple drag-and-drop interface.
* **AI Assistant:** Use natural language to ask for server status updates or to help run complicated multi-step commands.
* **Visual Monitoring:** Keep track of your server’s performance, including memory and processor usage, through clear, real-time charts.
* **Customizable Interface:** Personalize your workspace with themes and highlight important text in your terminal to stay focused.
