### What it does in plain English
This tool helps you build accurate 3D maps of physical spaces as you move around with a camera or sensor. It connects visual images and depth measurements to stitch together a complete, navigable digital model of your surroundings. Whether you are mapping a single room or guiding an autonomous device, it handles the complex math behind the scenes.

### Who it's for
* Robotics hobbyists building autonomous navigation devices
* Researchers studying computer vision and spatial mapping
* Technology enthusiasts exploring 3D scanning and environment modeling

### Key features
* Real-time loop closure detection to correct mapping drift as you explore
* Standalone application interface for viewing and exporting 3D models easily
* Cross-platform support for major desktop operating systems and mobile hardware
* Integration options with popular robot control platforms and tools
* Extensible C++ library for developers looking to add spatial awareness to custom programs
