### What it does in plain English
Ikaros acts as a centralized content manager built specifically for digital media collections like anime, comics, games, music, and novels. It brings all your scattered media files into a single unified platform with rich metadata support. You can perform lightning-fast searches across hundreds of thousands of files to find what you need instantly.

### Who it's for
* Media enthusiasts and collectors who have massive libraries of anime, manga, or books spread across multiple folders.
* Individuals looking for a lightweight, self-hosted personal file organizer to catalog their entertainment media.

### Key features
* Unified resource management that gathers all your media into one clean platform.
* Rich metadata support so you can easily understand and organize foreign or alternative titles.
* Instant search capabilities that respond in milliseconds even when sorting through massive libraries.
* Plugin-friendly architecture that allows you to add extra functionality beyond the core features.
* Cross-platform client support including apps for Windows and Android devices.
