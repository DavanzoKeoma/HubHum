### What it does in plain English
Telegram Media Downloader allows you to save media files like photos, videos, and documents from your Telegram chats directly to your local storage. It works by connecting to your account, letting you choose specific channels or groups to monitor for new content. You can manage your downloads through a simple web interface or by sending commands to a personal bot.

### Who it's for
* People who want to back up important media files from their Telegram groups or channels.
* Users who prefer an automated way to collect content without manually saving every single file.

### Key features
* **Automated Downloads:** Set it up once and let it monitor your selected chats for new files.
* **Flexible Filtering:** Choose exactly which types of media (like videos or PDFs) you want to save.
* **Web Interface:** View your download progress and manage settings through a clean browser-based dashboard.
* **Cloud Integration:** Optionally upload your downloaded files directly to cloud storage services.
* **Bot Control:** Use simple chat commands to trigger downloads or manage the tool remotely.
