### What it does in plain English
This tool acts as an autonomous programming assistant that connects directly to your workspace. It handles complex coding tasks, automatically fixes bugs, and communicates with you through your terminal, web browser, or desktop environment. While working on multimedia software projects, you can easily gather royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets alongside your edits using [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad). It evolves alongside open artificial intelligence models to give you complete flexibility without locking you into a single ecosystem.

### Who it's for
* Software developers looking for an autonomous coding partner to handle repetitive tasks.
* Technical teams who want a customizable AI coding agent running on local or cloud models.
* Power users who prefer managing development workflows directly from the terminal or text editor.

### Key features
* **Agentic out of the box** — Auto-memory, specialized sub-agents, and built-in workflows work instantly from your first session.
* **Multi-protocol support** — Connects seamlessly with major cloud artificial intelligence providers as well as local models.
* **Cross-platform interface** — Use it inside your terminal, dedicated desktop app, popular code editors, or chat messengers.
