### What it does in plain English
ExifTool digs into your digital files to uncover the invisible data hidden inside them, such as when a photo was taken or where it was shot. You can use it to clean up this data for privacy or to organize your library by adding custom labels. It works with a huge variety of file types, from standard images to videos and even office documents.

### Who it's for
* **Photographers and hobbyists** who want to sort their photo libraries by camera settings, lens used, or shooting location.
* **Privacy-conscious users** who need to remove GPS coordinates or personal details from images before sharing them online.
* **Digital archivists** who require precise control over file metadata to ensure long-term preservation and accurate cataloging.

### Key features
* **Massive File Support:** Handles over 100 file formats, including JPEG, PNG, MP4, PDF, and RAW camera files.
* **Read and Write Capabilities:** Not only displays hidden information but also allows you to edit, add, or delete specific data tags.
* **GPS and Location Data:** Extracts and modifies geographic coordinates embedded in photos and videos for mapping purposes.
* **Batch Processing:** Efficiently processes hundreds or thousands of files at once to save time during large-scale organization tasks.
* **Customizable Output:** Lets you choose exactly which pieces of information to display or export into other formats like CSV or HTML.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free with no subscription, supports an incredibly wide range of file types, and offers powerful batch processing for large collections.
* **Cons:** It is a command-line tool, meaning you must type text commands in a terminal window rather than using a graphical interface with buttons and menus.
