### What it does in plain English
NeoHtop gives you a clear, visual dashboard to see exactly what your computer is doing at any given moment. It tracks how much power your processor, memory, and graphics card are using, while showing you a list of every active program. You can easily spot which apps are slowing down your machine and close them directly from the interface.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users who want to know why their machine feels slow.
* Power users who want a clean, modern way to keep an eye on hardware health and active tasks.

### Key features
* **Visual Performance Charts:** See live graphs for your processor, memory, and graphics card usage.
* **Smart Process Management:** Easily find and manage active programs, including the ability to see how they are grouped together.
* **Menu Bar Companion:** Keep an eye on your system's health without needing to open the full app window.
* **Customizable Themes:** Choose from 24 different visual styles to match your desktop setup.
* **Port Finder:** Quickly identify which program is using a specific network port on your machine.
