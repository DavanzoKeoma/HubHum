### What it does in plain English
LinkLiar is a friendly menu bar tool for macOS that helps you disguise the unique hardware address of your Wi-Fi and Ethernet connections. By changing this digital fingerprint, you can prevent trackers from recognizing your device when you connect to public Wi-Fi networks. It runs quietly in the background and gives you complete control over your network identity with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-conscious laptop owners who frequently use public Wi-Fi hotspots in cafes, airports, or hotels.
* Network administrators and testers who need a straightforward way to change connection addresses on macOS.

### Key features
* Simple status menu access directly from your Mac's top bar.
* Ability to disguise both Wi-Fi and Ethernet network hardware identifiers.
* Free and open-source tool with transparent code.
* Quick installation via standard package managers or manual setup.

### How to install (Download)
You can install LinkLiar by visiting the [Official Documentation and Site](https://halo.github.io/LinkLiar) or via Homebrew by running `brew install --cask linkliar` in your terminal.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, intuitive menu bar interface, no hidden subscription costs.
* **Cons:** Requires a one-time administrator password to function, and certain newer hardware models have system restrictions that limit address changes.
