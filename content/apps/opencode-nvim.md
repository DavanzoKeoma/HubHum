### What it does in plain English
This tool connects your text editor to an intelligent assistant that helps you write and improve your code. It allows you to send parts of your work to the assistant for explanations, bug fixes, or performance suggestions. You can then review and apply these changes directly within your familiar workspace without needing to leave your current task.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want to use AI tools without leaving their preferred text editor.
* Programmers who value a distraction-free workflow and want to keep their coding environment consistent.

### Key features
* **Seamless Integration:** Works directly inside your editor, keeping your existing setup and habits intact.
* **Context Awareness:** Automatically shares your current file, selected text, or project notes with the assistant to provide relevant help.
* **Interactive Editing:** Allows you to review, accept, or reject AI-suggested changes side-by-side with your original work.
* **Customizable Prompts:** Includes built-in shortcuts for common tasks like explaining code, writing tests, or optimizing performance.
* **Real-time Updates:** Automatically refreshes your files when the assistant makes changes, ensuring you are always looking at the latest version.

### How to install (Download)
This tool is a plugin for the Neovim text editor. You can install it using standard plugin managers like `vim.pack` or `lazy.nvim` by pointing your configuration to the [Official Source official project page](https://github.com/nickjvandyke/opencode.nvim).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, maintains your existing workflow, and provides deep integration with your editor's features.
* **Cons:** Requires a basic understanding of text editor configuration and relies on an external AI service to function.
