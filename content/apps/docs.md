### What it does in plain English
Docs is a web‑based text editor that lets people write together at the same time.  
It keeps documents organized in a clear, nested structure.  
Your data stays on your own servers or in the cloud you choose.

### Who it's for
Team leaders who need shared notes, teachers who want collaborative lesson plans, and anyone who wants to keep their writing private.

### Key features
- Real‑time collaboration with live cursors.  
- Structured documents with sub‑pages and a searchable index.  
- Offline editing and optional AI helpers.  
- Export to PDF, DOCX, or ODT.  
- Granular access control for shared documents.

### How to install (Download)
Use the online version: [Open Docs](https://docs.la-suite.eu/).  
If you prefer to host it yourself, follow the installation guide: [Self‑hosting guide](https://github.com/suitenumerique/docs/blob/main/documentation/installation/README.md).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, no subscription, data stays under your control.  
* **Cons:** Advanced export features need extra packages; self‑hosting requires Docker and some setup.
