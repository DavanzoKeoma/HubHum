### What it does in plain English
Screenlite is an open-source tool that helps you control and manage digital screens from a single location. You can upload media, schedule what plays, and monitor multiple displays without relying on expensive commercial software. It acts as a central hub where all your content organization takes place before being sent out to your screens.

### Who it's for
* Business owners managing lobby displays or promotional monitors
* Store managers updating digital menus and promotional signage
* IT administrators looking for a flexible, self-hosted presentation tool

### Key features
* Centralized control panel to manage all connected screens from one place
* Schedule content playlists to display specific media at exact times
* Flexible media storage integration for your images, videos, and slides
* Open-source code giving you total control and data privacy

### How to install (Download)
To set up and run the system, visit the [Official Website](https://screenlite.org) for documentation and setup guides.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, centralized management, no costly subscriptions
* **Cons:** Still in active development, requires technical setup knowledge
