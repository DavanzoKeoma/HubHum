### What it does in plain English
Organic Maps is a completely free, privacy-first navigation tool that downloads detailed maps directly to your device so you can explore without internet access. It provides turn-by-turn directions for walking, cycling, and driving while refusing to collect your personal data or track your location. Because it relies on community-edited map information, you get accurate trails, local landmarks, and hidden gems that commercial mapping apps often overlook.

### Who it's for
* Hikers and cyclists seeking reliable trail maps and elevation profiles in remote areas without cell service.
* Travelers and tourists wanting to navigate foreign cities without incurring expensive mobile data roaming charges.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer an everyday utility that contains zero advertisements, trackers, or data collection tools.

### Key features
* Fully functional offline maps that load quickly and save your device's battery life.
* Turn-by-turn voice navigation for walking, biking, and driving routes.
* Comprehensive hiking trails, cycling paths, contour lines, and mountain peaks.
* Fast offline search for restaurants, hotels, public transit, and points of interest.
* Ability to import and export your favorite saved places and travel tracks in standard file formats.
