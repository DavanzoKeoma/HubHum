### What it does in plain English
OpenRCT2 lets you design and run your own amusement park, from building thrilling coasters to managing daily finances. It improves upon the classic RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 experience with better graphics, smarter guest behavior, and new tools for park builders. You can play solo to complete challenges or team up with friends in a shared park.

### Who it's for
* **Theme park enthusiasts** who love planning layouts and optimizing guest happiness.
* **Casual gamers** looking for a relaxing management simulation without a subscription fee.
* **Multiplayer groups** who want to collaborate on building a massive virtual park together.

### Key features
* **Modernized Interface:** A cleaner, more intuitive menu system that makes managing your park easier.
* **Cooperative Multiplayer:** Invite friends to join your park and build together in real-time.
* **Enhanced Guest AI:** Visitors now react more realistically to attractions, weather, and park conditions.
* **Expanded Tools:** Access new building options and editing features that were not in the original game.
* **Cross-Platform Support:** Play on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android devices.
