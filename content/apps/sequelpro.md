### What it does in plain English
Sequel Pro is a user-friendly application for macOS that lets you connect to and manage your database servers. It provides a clear, graphical layout for viewing tables, running queries, and updating records without needing to remember complex commands. Whether you are building a website or analyzing server data, it helps you organize your information directly from your desktop.

### Who it's for
* **Web Developers:** Individuals building local or remote websites who need a reliable tool to inspect and modify database content.
* **Database Administrators:** Professionals managing structured information on server environments who want a responsive desktop interface.
* **Students and Learners:** People studying web development or data science who need an accessible environment to practice query writing.

### Key features
* **Clean Graphical Interface:** Browse databases, tables, and rows visually without typing raw commands for every action.
* **Query Editor:** Write, test, and save custom database queries with syntax highlighting and helper tools.
* **Import and Export Tools:** Easily back up your data or move tables between different servers using common file formats.
* **User Permission Management:** Review and adjust account access privileges directly within your connected database servers.
