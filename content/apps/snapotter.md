### What it does in plain English
SnapOtter is a comprehensive collection of tools that lets you edit, convert, and organize your digital files without ever uploading them to a third-party website. It runs directly on your computer or server, ensuring your private data stays under your control at all times. With over 200 features, it handles everything from basic image resizing and video trimming to advanced tasks like removing backgrounds and transcribing audio using local intelligence.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious users** who want to edit sensitive documents or personal photos without sending them to cloud-based services.
*   **Content creators and professionals** who need a fast, reliable way to batch-process media files and manage documents without recurring subscription fees.

### Key features
*   **Massive Toolset:** Access over 200 utilities for images, videos, audio, PDFs, and general file management.
*   **Local Intelligence:** Perform advanced tasks like background removal, photo restoration, and audio transcription entirely offline.
*   **Built-in Editor:** Use a browser-based, layer-driven image editor for quick touch-ups, filters, and design adjustments.
*   **Workflow Automation:** Chain multiple tools together into custom pipelines to automate repetitive file-processing tasks.
*   **Total Privacy:** Since the software runs on your own hardware, your files never leave your network or reach external servers.

### How to install (Download)
SnapOtter is designed to run as a containerized service. You can get started by downloading the configuration files:

*   [Download Standard Setup (docker-compose.yml)](https://github.com/snapotter-hq/SnapOtter/releases/download/v2.3.0/docker-compose.yml)
*   [Download GPU-Accelerated Setup (docker-compose-gpu.yml)](https://github.com/snapotter-hq/SnapOtter/releases/download/v2.3.0/docker-compose-gpu.yml)

*Note: To run these, ensure you have Docker installed on your system. Simply run `docker compose up -d` in the folder where you saved the file.*

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely private, no subscription fees, massive variety of tools, and works offline.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic familiarity with container software to set up, and some advanced AI features perform best with a dedicated graphics card.
