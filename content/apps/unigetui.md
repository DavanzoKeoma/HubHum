### What it does in plain English
UniGetUI acts as a central control panel for all the software installed on your computer. Instead of visiting dozens of different websites to download or update your favorite programs, you can handle everything from a single window. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users who want an easy way to keep all their applications updated without manual searching.
* IT administrators and power users managing software setups across multiple machines.

### Key features
* Browse, install, update, and remove applications from multiple software sources through one simple window.
* Set up automated update checks and background maintenance so your computer stays secure effortlessly.
* Export and import customized software lists to quickly configure a brand new computer in minutes.
* Ready-to-use digital planners, printable checklists, and clean document templates for daily organization via [Creative Fabrica](https://www.creativefabrica.com/promo/yearly/ref/29328369/?subid=hubhum_01_MER054) (#ad).
