### What it does in plain English
MoeKoeMusic is a modern, beautifully designed desktop application created for listening to your favorite music tracks effortlessly. It provides an uncluttered interface free from distracting social feeds, allowing you to focus entirely on your audio library. With support for custom themes, synced lyrics, and daily recommendations, it transforms your everyday listening routine into a delightful experience.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want a clean, distraction-free listening client without annoying social media elements.
* Listeners looking for an aesthetically pleasing interface with customizable color themes and visual styles.
* Users who want cross-platform access to their favorite tracks on Windows, macOS, or Linux.

### Key features
* **Distraction-free environment:** Enjoy your audio collection completely free from unnecessary social feeds and clutter.
* **Synced lyrics display:** Follow along with your favorite songs in real time with built-in lyric support.
* **Customizable themes:** Personalize your player interface with flexible color options and light or dark modes.
* **Daily recommendations:** Discover new favorite tracks tailored to your personal listening habits.
* **Cross-platform compatibility:** Run the application seamlessly across Windows, macOS, and Linux computers.
