### What it does in plain English
QuantDinger is a complete platform that helps you design, test, and run automated trading strategies using artificial intelligence. It connects market research, strategy coding in Python, historical backtesting, and live execution into a single self-hosted workspace. You maintain full ownership of your data, broker keys, and server infrastructure while leveraging modern automated tools.

### Who it's for
* Independent financial researchers and Python developers building custom trading algorithms.
* Small trading teams looking for a self-hosted workspace to manage risk and monitor multiple assets.
* Enthusiasts wanting to experiment with artificial intelligence and quantitative market analysis.

### Key features
* Multi-provider artificial intelligence market research and analytical tools.
* Python-based indicators and Strategy API V2 development environment.
* Comprehensive historical backtesting and parameter optimization workflows.
* Paper trading and live execution support for various crypto exchanges and traditional brokers.
* Secure self-hosted architecture with robust audit logs and monitoring overlays.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete open-source self-hosted stack, full control over private data and broker credentials, powerful automated research tools.
* **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to configure and maintain server environments, financial trading involves inherent market risks.
