### What it does in plain English
Single is an elegant template designed for self-hosted blogging platforms to give your writing a modern look. It removes unnecessary visual clutter so your visitors can focus purely on your articles and photography. Built-in dark mode ensures comfortable reading during late-night browsing sessions.

### Who it's for
* Blogners and writers who want a sophisticated, distraction-free reading layout.
* Personal journal keepers looking for an attractive dark mode option.
* Creators seeking a lightweight website template that loads quickly.

### Key features
* Modern minimalist layout with generous whitespace for great readability.
* Integrated dark mode switch for comfortable evening reading.
* Built-in image lightbox to showcase photos cleanly.
* Code highlighting support for technical tutorials and snippets.
* Lightweight structure without heavy external interface scripts.

### How to install (Download)
To set up this theme, visit the official [Official Website & Documentation](https://paugram.com) or read the setup steps on the [GitHub Source Page](https://github.com/Dreamer-Paul/Single). You can download the theme package directly from the [ZIP Download Page](https://github.com/Dreamer-Paul/Single/archive/master.zip) and place it inside your website's theme folder.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, elegant minimalist design, lightweight performance.
* **Cons:** Requires a compatible blogging engine installed on your server to function.
