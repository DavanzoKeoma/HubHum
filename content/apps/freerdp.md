### What it does in plain English
FreeRDP gives you a free and open way to connect to other computers over a network as if you were sitting right in front of them. It acts as a bridge between different operating systems so you can view screens, run programs, and manage files seamlessly. Because it is built on open standards, anyone can use it without hidden licensing fees or restrictions.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers and IT professionals who need reliable access to office workstations from home.
* System administrators managing multiple servers across different network environments.
* Everyday users looking for a transparent and trustworthy remote connection tool.

### Key features
* Connects smoothly to remote computers running standard sharing protocols.
* Open-source design ensures privacy, transparency, and no surprise subscription fees.
* Works seamlessly across multiple operating systems and hardware platforms.
* Highly customizable connection settings for display scaling, audio, and local device sharing.
* Backed by active community support and regular updates for improved safety and speed.
