### What it does in plain English
Saber is a digital notebook that lets you write by hand, just like you would on paper. It keeps your notes organized with folders and syncs them automatically so you can access your work from any device. Because it is open-source and uses strong security, you can be sure that your private thoughts and study materials stay completely under your control.

### Who it's for
* Students who need to take handwritten notes or highlight complex equations during class.
* Anyone who prefers the feel of handwriting but wants the convenience of digital organization and cloud syncing.

### Key features
* **Natural Writing:** Designed specifically for handwriting, ensuring your notes look and feel like they were written on paper.
* **Smart Dark Mode:** Automatically inverts colors for notes, images, and PDFs so you can write in white ink on a black background to reduce eye strain.
* **Advanced Highlighting:** Uses special rendering to ensure overlapping highlights remain clear and consistent without fading your text.
* **Total Privacy:** Features a dual-password system to keep your notes encrypted and secure, even from the people running the servers.
* **Flexible Organization:** Create as many folders as you need to keep your projects and classes perfectly sorted.
