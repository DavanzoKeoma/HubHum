### What it does in plain English
Vorta is a user-friendly application that safeguards your personal files, photos, and documents from accidental loss, computer failure, or cyber threats. It takes your important folders and safely stores them in a compressed, hidden format on an external drive or cloud storage provider. You can easily travel back in time to recover older versions of files or restore an entire folder with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users on macOS or Linux who want a dependable, set-and-forget safeguard for personal documents and photos.
* Privacy-conscious individuals who prefer complete ownership of their backed-up data without relying on mandatory commercial cloud subscriptions.
* Freelancers and remote workers who need a reliable way to protect sensitive client files against hardware crashes or accidental deletion.

### Key features
* **Encrypted Storage:** Locks your backups with strong passwords so nobody else can read your personal data.
* **Space-Saving Deduplication:** Automatically avoids storing duplicate copies of identical files to save precious disk space.
* **Flexible Schedules:** Set your backups to run automatically every hour, day, or week without manual intervention.
* **Complete File Recovery:** Browse through previous points in time to recover specific deleted files or entire directory trees easily.
* **No Vendor Lock-In:** Send your archives to local hard drives, private network servers, or any compatible cloud storage provider.
