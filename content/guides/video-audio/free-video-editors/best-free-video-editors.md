## Quick Answer: yt-dlp vs LosslessCut

Comparing **yt-dlp** and **LosslessCut** comes down to how you prefer to work. **yt-dlp** stands out as a top community favorite (209,259 GitHub stars) and runs as a **CLI Tool**: A reliable tool to save audio and video from thousands of online websites directly to your computer. Meanwhile, **LosslessCut** (46,153 GitHub stars) gives you another trusted free option: Trim, split, and merge large videos in seconds with zero quality loss and no slow re-encoding wait times.
