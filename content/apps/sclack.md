### What it does in plain English
Sclack is a lightweight communication tool that lets you send and receive messages straight from your terminal screen. It cuts away heavy graphical bloat so you can stay in touch with multiple workspaces without leaving your favorite command-line environment. You can manage channels, view rich text, and quickly switch conversations using simple keyboard shortcuts.

### Who it's for
* Developers and system administrators who live inside terminal windows all day.
* Power users looking for a distraction-free, lightning-fast alternative to traditional chat apps.
* Anyone managing multiple communication workspaces who wants to save system memory.

### Key features
* **Terminal Native:** Runs smoothly inside your favorite terminal emulator with minimal resource usage.
* **Multiple Workspaces:** Switch easily between up to nine different workspaces using hotkeys or mouse clicks.
* **Quick Switcher:** Instantly jump to any direct message or channel with a handy keyboard shortcut.
* **Custom Shortcuts:** Tailor every keybinding to fit your exact typing and navigation habits.
* **Media Rendering:** Optional support for viewing inline pictures and emojis directly inside text interfaces.
