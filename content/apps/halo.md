### What it does in plain English
Halo is a flexible website creation tool designed to help you build everything from simple personal blogs to professional online stores. It provides a central hub where you can manage your content, organize your knowledge, and showcase your work without needing deep technical knowledge. With a wide variety of themes and add-ons available, you can customize your site to look and function exactly how you want.

### Who it's for
* **Personal Bloggers:** Individuals who want a clean, professional space to share their thoughts or creative projects.
* **Small Business Owners:** Entrepreneurs looking for an all-in-one solution to manage their brand website and online shop.

### Key features
* **Versatile Content Management:** Easily switch between running a blog, a knowledge base, or a full e-commerce storefront.
* **Extensive Customization:** Access over 100 free themes and plugins to change the look and functionality of your site.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** Manage your entire website through a clear, intuitive dashboard.
* **Self-Hosted Control:** Keep full ownership of your data by hosting the platform on your own infrastructure.
