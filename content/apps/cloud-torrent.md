### What it does in plain English
Cloud Torrent acts as a personal control center for your file downloads, allowing you to add and monitor them from any device with a web browser. Once your files are downloaded, you can organize them or watch and listen to your media directly through the app's built-in player. It runs as a single, lightweight program that keeps your files accessible and organized in one central location.

### Who it's for
* Home media enthusiasts who want to manage their file library from different devices.
* Users who prefer a clean, browser-based interface to handle their downloads without needing complex software.

### Key features
* **Remote Control:** Add or manage your files from any web browser on your phone, tablet, or computer.
* **Built-in Media Player:** Preview and watch your video or audio files instantly without needing to download them to your local device first.
* **Drag-and-Drop:** Easily add new files to your queue by simply dragging them into the browser window.
* **Real-time Updates:** See your download progress immediately without needing to refresh your page.
* **Lightweight Design:** The entire application is contained in one small file, making it easy to set up and run.
