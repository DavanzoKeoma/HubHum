## Quick Answer: yt-dlp vs LosslessCut

Comparing **yt-dlp** and **LosslessCut** highlights two distinct approaches in **Video Cutters, Converters & Downloaders**. **yt-dlp** runs as a **CLI Tool** on **Windows** (209,259 GitHub stars): A reliable tool to save audio and video from thousands of online websites directly to your computer. By contrast, **LosslessCut** runs as a **Desktop App** on **Windows, macOS, Linux** (46,153 GitHub stars): Trim, split, and merge large videos in seconds with zero quality loss and no slow re-encoding wait times.

### When to Pick Which Option
- **Choose [yt-dlp](/HubHum/apps/yt-dlp/) (CLI Tool)** if you need what it specializes in: A reliable tool to save audio and video from thousands of online websites directly to your computer — it runs as fast terminal commands suited for automation and batch scripts (no graphical buttons) (209,259 GitHub stars, vs LosslessCut which runs as a Desktop App).
- **Choose [LosslessCut](/HubHum/apps/losslesscut/) (Desktop App)** if you need what it specializes in: Trim, split, and merge large videos in seconds with zero quality loss and no slow re-encoding wait times — it provides a visual desktop window with point-and-click controls instead of terminal commands (46,153 GitHub stars, vs yt-dlp which runs as a CLI Tool).
