### What it does in plain English
Foundation Sunshine is a specialized tool that lets you stream your Windows computer's screen and games to other devices like phones, tablets, or laptops. It is designed to make remote gaming feel just like sitting in front of your own monitor by reducing lag and supporting high-quality visuals. The software includes a user-friendly control panel to manage your game library, display settings, and connection preferences without needing complex technical knowledge.

### Who it's for
*   PC gamers who want to play their high-end games on a mobile device or a laptop while away from their main desk.
*   Users looking for a reliable, free way to access their Windows desktop remotely with support for advanced features like HDR and surround sound.

### Key features
*   **Smooth Visuals:** Supports high-quality streaming including HDR and vivid color profiles for a lifelike gaming experience.
*   **Optimized Performance:** Uses your computer's graphics card to handle video encoding, ensuring low latency and fluid motion.
*   **Centralized Control:** Includes a dedicated desktop interface to easily manage your games, display settings, and connected devices.
*   **Full Input Support:** Allows for native controller feedback, including advanced features like adaptive triggers, and supports mouse and stylus input.
*   **Versatile Sharing:** Enables file sharing, clipboard syncing, and microphone pass-through between your host PC and your remote device.
