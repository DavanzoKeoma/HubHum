### What it does in plain English
CompactGUI provides a simple way to shrink the size of large files, games, and programs on your computer. It uses advanced built-in Windows technology to compress your data so it takes up less room on your hard drive. You can continue to use your programs and play your games exactly as you always have, as the compression happens invisibly in the background.

### Who it's for
* Gamers who want to fit more titles on their hard drive without buying extra storage.
* Users with smaller solid-state drives (SSDs) who need to reclaim space from large software installations.

### Key features
* **Transparent Compression:** Files stay compressed on your disk but expand automatically when you open them, so you never notice a difference.
* **Visual Progress:** See exactly how much space you are saving with clear statistics and progress bars.
* **Automatic Monitoring:** A background tool keeps your folders compressed even if you update your games or add new files.
* **Customizable Settings:** Choose between different compression levels to balance speed and file size reduction.
* **Easy Integration:** Right-click directly on any folder in your file explorer to compress it instantly.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no subscriptions, and offers significant space savings on modern Windows systems.
* **Cons:** Not suitable for games that use advanced high-speed loading technology (DirectStorage), as it may slow down performance.
