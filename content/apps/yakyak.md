### What it does in plain English
YakYak provides a dedicated window on your computer for managing your Google Hangouts conversations. It allows you to send and receive messages, share images, and see when your contacts are typing. The app runs independently of your web browser, keeping your chats organized in one place.

### Who it's for
* People who prefer using a standalone desktop app rather than keeping a browser tab open for messaging.
* Users who want to keep their Google Hangouts history and notifications easily accessible on their computer.

### Key features
* Send and receive text messages, images, and emojis.
* Receive native desktop notifications for new messages.
* Manage your conversations by renaming them or adding new participants.
* Choose from different color themes to customize the look of your chat window.
* Syncs your chat history so you can scroll back through past conversations.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, lightweight, and provides a native desktop experience.
* **Cons:** It is an unofficial client, so Google may identify it as an "iOS device" when you sign in; video and audio calls require opening a browser window.
