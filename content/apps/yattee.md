### What it does in plain English
Yattee is a dedicated video player designed for Apple devices that lets you watch content without intrusive tracking or advertisements. It automatically skips sponsored segments inside videos and keeps track of your viewing queues and history seamlessly. Built natively for Apple systems, it ensures smooth high-definition playback while putting your personal privacy first.

### Who it's for
* Apple ecosystem users who want an ad-free and tracking-free video viewing experience.
* Privacy-conscious viewers who prefer open-source apps without corporate data collection.
* Power users looking to automate video playback using shortcuts and custom URL schemes.

### Key features
* Built natively using modern Apple interface software foundation for a smooth and responsive design.
* Automatically skips sponsored segments and promotional messages using community blocklists.
* Supports picture-in-picture mode, background audio playback, and ultra-high-definition 4K streams.
* Includes custom gesture controls, playback queues, and viewing history management.
* Integrates with system automation tools like Siri Shortcuts and custom URL actions.
