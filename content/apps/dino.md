### What it does in plain English
Dino lets you chat instantly with friends and colleagues.  
You can also make voice calls, share files, and join group conversations.  
All of this works on Linux and keeps your messages private.

### Who it's for
Anyone who uses a Linux computer and wants a simple, private way to talk online.  
People who need voice calls and file sharing without relying on large commercial services.  
Small teams or families looking for a secure group‑chat solution.

### Key features
- Send and receive instant text messages.  
- Make clear voice calls directly from the app.  
- Share photos, documents, and other files with a click.  
- Create and join group chats for projects or family talks.  
- Built‑in encryption protects your conversations automatically.
