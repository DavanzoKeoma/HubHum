### What it does in plain English
MiniSearch is a private search engine that pairs web results with a helpful artificial intelligence assistant. It gathers information from across the internet and runs the assistant right inside your browser window or local machine. Because everything stays local, your search history and personal queries are never sent to third-party companies or tracked.

### Who it's for
* Privacy-focused web users who want AI-generated summaries without sharing their personal data.
* People looking for a self-hosted search engine that operates smoothly without paid subscription keys.
* Power users who want local search history, quick browser integration, and customizable AI backends.

### Key features
* **Complete Privacy:** Keeps all search history, cached results, and chats stored locally in your browser.
* **In-Browser Intelligence:** Uses your computer's graphics card or processor to run curated AI models directly.
* **Verified Sources:** Generates clear answers complete with citations and source links for easy fact-checking.
* **Flexible Backends:** Connects easily to popular local AI servers or external tools if you prefer remote processing.
* **Integrated History:** Offers fuzzy-searchable past sessions, conversation memory, and usage analytics.
