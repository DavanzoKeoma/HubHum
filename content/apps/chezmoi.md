### What it does in plain English
chezmoi helps you keep your personal computer settings and preferences consistent when you use multiple computers. It securely stores your configuration files in one place and lets you update them everywhere with a single command. You can safely manage private passwords or tokens without accidentally sharing them publicly.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use multiple computers and need their custom terminal, editor, and system settings synchronized.
* System administrators who maintain identical working environments across various remote servers and personal machines.
* Power users who want a reliable backup and version control system for their computer preferences.

### Key features
* Synchronize your personal configuration files across different operating systems effortlessly.
* Keep sensitive information secure by encrypting passwords and private tokens automatically.
* Preview all proposed changes before applying them to your system to prevent mistakes.
* Manage machine-specific variations so your setup adapts correctly to each computer you use.
* Integrate seamlessly with popular version control platforms to track your file history.
