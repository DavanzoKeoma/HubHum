### What it does in plain English
OpenBudgeteer is a personal finance tool that helps you manage your money by breaking it down into distinct categories or buckets. It allows you to connect your bank accounts, track everyday purchases, and allocate every single coin towards specific life goals. By following a structured budgeting method, you can easily see what you can afford to spend right now versus what you need to save for later.

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a self-hosted alternative to commercial subscription budget planners
* Households wanting to organize shared expenses and savings goals in one private dashboard
* People who prefer the envelope method of money management adapted for the digital age

### Key features
* Digital bucket system to distribute income into custom spending and saving categories
* Transaction management views to review and categorize your bank activity
* Visual reports that give clear insights into your monthly cash flow and spending habits
* Account tracking to keep all your financial balances synchronized in one place
* Settings panel to customize currencies and preferences to fit your personal workflow

### How to install (Download)
You can set up OpenBudgeteer on your own server or computer using container technology alongside a database. For step-by-step instructions and setup guides, visit the [OpenBudgeteer Quick Start Guide](https://theaxelander.github.io/latest/quick-start/) or explore the main [Official Documentation Website](https://theaxelander.github.io).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete data privacy, free to use, highly structured budgeting philosophy
* **Cons:** Requires technical setup with server containers and a database, manual initial configuration
