### What it does in plain English
Dashboard Icons provides a massive, organized collection of over 3,400 high-quality logos and icons for your favorite software and web services. It ensures your digital dashboards and personal portals look clean and consistent with matching visual styles. You can even connect these icons to your AI assistants so they can automatically identify and label your tools for you.

### Who it's for
*   **Home Server Enthusiasts:** People who manage personal dashboards and want a uniform, professional look for all their services.
*   **AI Power Users:** Individuals who use coding assistants or automation agents that need to recognize and categorize various software tools.

### Key features
*   **Massive Library:** Over 3,400 icons covering popular apps, tools, and services.
*   **Multiple Formats:** Download icons in SVG, PNG, or WEBP to fit any project requirement.
*   **Theme Ready:** Includes both light and dark variants to match your specific background settings.
*   **AI Integration:** Connects directly to AI agents to automatically match and label your software configurations.
*   **Easy Access:** Simple search and download interface with direct links for quick use in your own projects.

### How to install (Download)
This is a web-based service. You do not need to install anything on your computer to use it.
*   **Browse and search icons:** Visit [dashboardicons.com](https://dashboardicons.com).
*   **For AI Agents:** Use the built-in connection link `https://dashboardicons.com/api/mcp` within your AI assistant's settings to give it access to the full library.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free to use, massive variety of icons, and includes helpful automation features for AI.
*   **Cons:** Requires an internet connection to fetch the latest icons, and some very niche software might not have an icon yet (though you can request them).
