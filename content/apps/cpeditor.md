### What it does in plain English
CP Editor provides a dedicated workspace for people who participate in coding competitions. It simplifies the process by allowing you to download problem details, write your solution, and test it against sample inputs all in one window. Once you are ready, it helps you submit your work directly to the competition platform.

### Who it's for
* Students and hobbyists who participate in online coding contests.
* Developers looking for a lightweight, focused tool to practice algorithmic problem-solving.

### Key features
* **Problem Fetching:** Automatically pull problem descriptions and sample test cases from supported platforms.
* **Integrated Testing:** Run your code against sample inputs with a single click to verify your logic.
* **Fast Compilation:** Built-in support for multiple programming languages to ensure your code runs quickly.
* **Submission Support:** Streamlined workflow to send your finished solutions to online judges.
* **Customizable Interface:** A clean, distraction-free layout that you can adjust to fit your personal coding style.
