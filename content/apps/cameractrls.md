### What it does in plain English
This application gives you full control over your Linux webcam, allowing you to adjust settings that are often hidden or difficult to change. You can fine-tune brightness, focus, and even pan or tilt your camera using a friendly visual window or simple text commands. It also supports specific features for popular camera brands, such as LED modes and preset positions, making it easier to get the perfect shot for video calls or streaming.

### Who it's for
* **Linux enthusiasts** who want more control over their webcam than the default system settings provide.
* **Streamers and content creators** using Linux who need to adjust camera angles, zoom, and lighting without switching to another operating system.
* **Developers and tech-savvy users** who prefer using command-line tools to automate camera settings for specific projects.

### Key features
* **Visual Control Panel:** A user-friendly window to adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, and focus with simple sliders.
* **Pan, Tilt, and Zoom (PTZ) Support:** Move your camera physically using your keyboard, mouse, or even a game controller for precise positioning.
* **Brand-Specific Extensions:** Special controls for popular cameras like Logitech, Kiyo Pro, and Dell, including LED modes and field-of-view adjustments.
* **Preset Saving and Restoring:** Save your favorite camera settings and have them automatically apply whenever you plug in your camera.
* **Live Camera Viewer:** A built-in window to see exactly what your camera is capturing while you adjust settings in real-time.
