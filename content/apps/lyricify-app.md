### What it does in plain English
Lyricify-App brings synchronized, real-time scrolling lyrics to your favorite desktop and mobile music services. It displays matching song words right on your screen with dynamic animations, custom layouts, and live album artwork. Whether you enjoy following line-by-line verses or want floating desktop lyrics while multitasking, the software seamlessly integrates with your music player.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who love singing along and following precise song lyrics in real-time.
* Desktop users who want customizable floating or full-screen lyric displays while working or relaxing.
* Listeners seeking an immersive audio companion that matches the visual style of their favorite streaming apps.

### Key features
* Real-time synchronized scrolling lyrics that follow your music precisely.
* Interactive desktop and taskbar lyric views for multitasking convenience.
* Dynamic album art and customized visual themes for your playback screen.
* Broad compatibility with major streaming services and media applications.
* Lightweight design with minimal impact on your system resources.
