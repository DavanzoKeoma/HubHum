### What it does in plain English
TextMate brings a clean and fast workspace for writing code, notes, and plain text documents on your Mac. It organizes large projects effortlessly while offering customizable themes and handy keyboard shortcuts. You can easily extend its capabilities with bundles to fit your exact writing or coding workflow.

### Who it's for
* Software developers looking for a fast, customizable text editor on macOS.
* Writers and bloggers who want a distraction-free environment for markdown and plain text.
* Anyone needing a reliable tool to view, edit, and organize code files.

### Key features
* Fast and responsive graphical interface tailored specifically for macOS.
* Advanced search and replace tools across multiple files.
* Highly customizable through community bundles and snippets.
* Built-in support for multiple programming languages and markup formats.
* Seamless session restore so you never lose your work.
