### What it does in plain English
E-TKT is a creative project that combines classic embossing tape with modern technology to help you organize your home or office. It allows you to print custom labels using a simple, reliable mechanism that you build yourself. By moving away from store-bought label makers, you eliminate the need for expensive, single-use plastic cartridges.

### Who it's for
*   DIY enthusiasts and makers who enjoy building their own hardware projects.
*   Environmentally conscious organizers looking to reduce plastic waste from office supplies.

### Key features
*   **Open Hardware:** Full documentation is provided so you can source parts and assemble the device yourself.
*   **Modern Connectivity:** Uses a built-in web interface that works on any device connected to your local network.
*   **No Internet Required:** The system operates entirely offline, keeping your data private and local.
*   **Sustainable:** Uses standard, widely available embossing tape instead of proprietary, expensive cartridges.

### How to install (Download)
E-TKT is a hardware project that requires assembly. You can find the complete, step-by-step instructions, parts list, and software setup guide on the official project website:
[Visit the E-TKT DIY Guide](https://andreisperid.github.io/E-TKT/diy/diy.html)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Environmentally friendly, no subscription fees, fully customizable, and great for learning electronics.
*   **Cons:** Requires manual assembly and sourcing of electronic components; not a pre-built consumer product.
