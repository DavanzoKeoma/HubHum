### What it does in plain English
Stream Tube lets you watch YouTube videos on your computer without any ads, pop‑ups or tracking.  
It works even when you’re offline and remembers where you left off.  
You can skip sponsor segments, download videos or audio, and enjoy smooth playback.

### Who it's for
* Anyone who watches YouTube regularly and wants a distraction‑free experience.  
* Users with limited bandwidth who need an audio‑only mode or lower‑quality playback.  
* People who prefer a lightweight desktop app over a full web browser.

### Key features
- No ads, no promoted videos, and built‑in SponsorBlock to skip sponsor reads.  
- Full YouTube navigation: Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, search, playlists, and live chat.  
- Hardware‑accelerated playback with support for 360p – 4K, captions, chapters, and picture‑in‑picture.  
- Audio‑only mode and data‑saving options for metered connections.  
- Easy video or audio downloads with optional yt‑dlp integration.
