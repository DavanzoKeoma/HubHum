### What it does in plain English
Microfeed is a simple way to manage and share your own podcasts, blog posts, photos, and videos online. It runs entirely on your own Cloudflare account, which keeps your costs low and your data under your control. You can manage your content through a friendly dashboard or use automated tools to handle updates for you.

### Who it's for
* Individuals who want to host their own blog or podcast without paying for expensive monthly subscriptions.
* Creators who want a simple, reliable way to share content while keeping full ownership of their data.

### Key features
* **Versatile Publishing:** Share audio, video, images, documents, and links from a single, organized feed.
* **Multiple Formats:** Automatically generates web pages, RSS feeds, and JSON feeds so your content is accessible everywhere.
* **Easy Management:** Includes a user-friendly dashboard for creating posts and customizing your site's look.
* **Automated Tools:** Use the official command-line tool to let AI agents help you manage or update your content.
* **Cost-Effective:** Leverages free usage quotas from Cloudflare to keep your site running for virtually no cost.

### How to install (Download)
Microfeed is designed to be set up directly on your Cloudflare account. You can get started by following the official guide:
[Get Started with Microfeed](https://www.microfeed.org/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to host using Cloudflare's generous quotas, supports many media types, and provides full ownership of your data.
* **Cons:** Requires a Cloudflare account and basic familiarity with setting up web services, though the automated setup tool makes this much easier.
