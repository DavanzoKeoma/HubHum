### What it does in plain English
PasswordPusher helps you share passwords, notes, or files without leaving permanent records in your email or chat history. You simply create a secure link, set how many times it can be opened or how long it should last, and send it to your recipient. Once the link is viewed or the time runs out, the information is permanently deleted from the system.

### Who it's for
* Professionals who need to share temporary access codes or sensitive documents with colleagues.
* Everyday users who want to avoid sending passwords through insecure messaging apps or email threads.

### Key features
* **Self-Destructing Links:** Data is automatically wiped once it reaches its view limit or expiration time.
* **Audit Logging:** Keep track of exactly when your shared information was accessed.
* **Optional Passphrases:** Add an extra layer of security by requiring a password to open the link.
* **Customizable Branding:** If you host it yourself, you can match the look and feel to your own organization.
* **Global Access:** The interface is available in over 30 languages to support teams around the world.

### How to install (Download)
* **Use the hosted service:** You can start sharing immediately at [pwpush.com](https://pwpush.com).
* **Self-hosting:** If you prefer to run it on your own server, follow the setup instructions at [docs.pwpush.com](https://docs.pwpush.com/docs/installation/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, highly secure with automatic deletion, and offers full audit logs.
* **Cons:** Self-hosting requires basic knowledge of server management or container software.
