### What it does in plain English
Pastel is a handy utility that lets you generate, inspect, and convert colors right from your terminal. It supports numerous color formats like RGB, HSL, and standard hex codes, making it simple to pick, mix, or match shades. Whether you are building color palettes or looking up specific design values, it streamlines color management for your projects.

### Who it's for
* Web designers and frontend developers picking color schemes
* Terminal enthusiasts and scripters working with colorized output
* Digital artists exploring color harmonies and conversions

### Key features
* Convert colors instantly between formats like hex, RGB, HSL, and CIELAB
* Generate sets of maximally distinct colors automatically for charts or palettes
* Pick color values directly from anywhere on your screen
* Analyze and name unknown color codes using standard CSS and X11 names
* Style terminal text output with custom background and foreground colors
