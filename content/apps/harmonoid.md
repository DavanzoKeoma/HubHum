### What it does in plain English
Harmonoid is a beautiful music player designed to bring your offline music collection to life with modern styling and fluid animations. It organizes your songs, reads file tags, and displays synchronized lyrics as your music plays. Working smoothly across computers and phones, it ensures your personal audio library always looks and sounds its best.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want a clean, modern interface for their local audio files.
* People who appreciate sleek design, smooth animations, and customizable themes.
* Listeners looking for a lightweight media player that supports all major audio formats without resource bloat.

### Key features
* Modern visual styling with light and dark themes.
* Built-in lyrics display including synchronized text and translations.
* Comprehensive audio controls like gapless playback, volume boost, and crossfade.
* Deep integration with system media controls on various operating systems.
* Lightweight installation with low memory usage and broad file format support.
