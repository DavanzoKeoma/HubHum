### What it does in plain English
ytplaylist-len is a handy online tool that calculates the exact running time of YouTube playlists and individual videos. It helps you figure out how long it will take to watch multiple videos back-to-back or at faster playback speeds like 1.5x or 2x. You can also map out a custom daily watch schedule to see your exact finish date.

### Who it's for
* Students and online learners organizing long educational video courses or lecture series.
* Content binge-watchers and researchers planning their daily media consumption and free time.
* Content creators and curators managing large video collections who need accurate timing estimates.

### Key features
* Analyze up to five playlists or videos simultaneously in a single request.
* Calculate viewing times instantly across speeds ranging from 1.25x to 2.00x.
* Set custom daily watch schedules to automatically estimate your completion date.
* Download high-resolution video thumbnails and generate precise timestamp links.
* Fully translated into 27 languages for global accessibility.
