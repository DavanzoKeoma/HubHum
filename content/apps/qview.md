### What it does in plain English
qView is a lightweight tool designed to display your photos without unnecessary clutter or complicated menus. It focuses on providing a clean, distraction-free window for viewing your image files. The software is built to be fast and responsive, ensuring your photos open instantly without slowing down your computer.

### Who it's for
* Casual users who want a simple way to view photos without the bloat of heavy editing software.
* Photographers or designers who need a quick, reliable tool to preview images while working on other tasks.

### Key features
* **Minimalist Interface:** A clean design that puts your images front and center without distracting toolbars.
* **Fast Performance:** Optimized to open images quickly, even on older or slower hardware.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Navigate through your folders and images easily using simple key commands.
* **Format Support:** Handles a wide variety of common image formats right out of the box.
* **Lightweight:** Uses very little memory, keeping your system running smoothly while you browse.
