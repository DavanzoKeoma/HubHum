### What it does in plain English
Beets is a powerful tool designed to bring order to messy digital music collections. It automatically scans your music files, identifies the songs, and corrects missing or inaccurate information like artist names and album titles. Once organized, it provides a simple way to manage, search, and browse your entire music library.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who have large, disorganized collections of digital audio files.
* People who want their music files to have consistent, professional-looking labels and organized folder structures.

### Key features
* **Automatic Tagging:** Corrects song metadata by matching your files against massive online music databases.
* **Smart Organization:** Automatically moves and renames your music files into a clean, consistent folder structure.
* **Duplicate Detection:** Scans your library to find and help you remove duplicate tracks or albums.
* **Extensible Plugins:** Offers a wide range of optional add-ons to fetch lyrics, album art, or even convert audio formats.
* **Library Browsing:** Includes a simple way to view your collection and play music directly through a web browser.

* **Pros:** Extremely powerful and customizable, free to use, and excellent at fixing messy metadata.
* **Cons:** Primarily controlled through text-based commands, which may require a short learning period for those used to visual interfaces.
