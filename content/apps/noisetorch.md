### What it does in plain English
NoiseTorch is a lightweight Linux utility that cleans up your microphone audio on the fly. It creates a virtual input device that filters out annoying background sounds like typing, heavy breathing, or computer fans. You can then select this filtered microphone in any voice chat or video meeting application with a single click.

### Who it's for
* Linux users who want crystal-clear voice calls without background distractions.
* Remote workers and gamers participating in frequent voice chats or video meetings.
* Content creators looking for an easy way to clean up live microphone audio.

### Key features
* Creates a virtual microphone that suppresses background noise in real time.
* Works seamlessly with popular communication and streaming apps.
* Features a simple two-click setup to load and unload your filtered audio.
* Includes adjustable voice activation thresholds to keep your natural voice untouched.
