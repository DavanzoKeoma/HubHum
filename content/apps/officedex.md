### What it does in plain English
OfficeDex acts as an intelligent assistant that writes and formats professional office files from simple sentences. Instead of manually adjusting styles, you describe what you need, and the application generates complete, ready-to-use documents, presentation slides, and spreadsheets. You can preview everything right inside the app without needing traditional software installed on your computer.

### Who it's for
* Professionals who regularly create lengthy reports, pitch decks, and financial analysis tables
* Busy founders and team leaders who want to skip tedious formatting tasks and focus purely on content
* Anyone looking to generate polished documents using natural conversation and local file privacy

### Key features
* **Conversational generation:** Type what you want in plain language and get properly structured sections, charts, and illustrations.
* **Instant live previews:** View your Word documents, presentations, and spreadsheets directly inside the app window.
* **Memory and style consistency:** The tool remembers your preferred layouts and formatting habits across different tasks.
* **Flexible intelligence options:** Connect your own preferred provider keys or run models entirely on your own machine.
* **Direct file saving:** Outputs real, usable files straight to your local folders for immediate sharing.
