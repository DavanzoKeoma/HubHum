### What it does in plain English
Ghost Downloader saves videos, music, torrents and other files you find online.  
It works wherever you watch – on a computer or on your phone.  
You never need a separate tool for each website.

### Who it's for
* People who watch YouTube, Bilibili or other video sites and want a local copy.  
* Users who need to grab large files from FTP, torrent or cloud links without hassle.  
* Anyone who prefers a single program instead of juggling many small utilities.

### Key features
- Handles video sites, torrent files, FTP, M3U8 streams and more, all from one place.  
- Adds a download button directly on the web page so you can click and save instantly.  
- Works on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android with a native look and feel.  
- Lets you pause, resume or change settings while a download is running.  
- Offers a simple command‑line helper for power users who like scripts.
