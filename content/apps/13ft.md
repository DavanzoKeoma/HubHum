### What it does in plain English
13ft is a personal tool that helps you read online articles that are blocked by subscription prompts or excessive advertisements. It works by mimicking a search engine crawler, which allows it to see the full content of a page just as a search engine would. You can host this tool on your own computer to gain a cleaner, more accessible reading experience whenever you need to view a single article.

### Who it's for
* Researchers or students who need to quickly access information from various news sites.
* Privacy-conscious readers who want to avoid cluttered websites filled with tracking ads.

### Key features
* **Self-Hosted Privacy:** You control the tool entirely on your own hardware, ensuring your reading habits remain private.
* **Smart Bypassing:** Automatically attempts multiple methods to retrieve content if the primary approach is blocked.
* **Simple Interface:** Paste a link into the tool to instantly strip away paywalls and distracting page elements.
* **Bookmark Integration:** Create a browser bookmarklet to "13ft-ize" any page you are currently viewing with a single click.
* **Customizable:** Easily change the language or host the service on your own local network for easy access from any device.

### How to install (Download)
To use 13ft, you will need Python installed on your computer. You can download the primary script here:
* [Download (portable.py)](https://github.com/wasi-master/13ft/releases/download/v0.5.0/portable.py)

Once downloaded, run the script from your terminal to start the service, then open the provided link in your web browser to begin using the tool.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, no subscription required, and gives you full control over your data.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of how to run a script in a terminal; some highly secure websites may still be difficult to access.
