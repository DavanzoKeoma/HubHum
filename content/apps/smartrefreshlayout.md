### What it does in plain English
SmartRefreshLayout is a powerful tool for mobile developers that adds smooth pull-to-refresh and infinite scrolling to Android application screens. It lets users pull down from the top of a list to fetch new information or scroll to the bottom to load more items automatically. The layout is designed to work seamlessly with almost any type of scrollable view and includes dozens of stylish visual animations and themes out of the box.

### Who it's for
* Android mobile application developers looking to enhance user navigation.
* UI/UX designers who want custom bouncy scroll physics and stylish loading indicators.
* Creators building modern news feeds, social timelines, or e-commerce catalogs on mobile.

### Key features
* Works smoothly with standard lists, grids, web pages, and complex nested view hierarchies.
* Supports advanced gestures including pull-to-refresh, load-more, multi-touch, and overscroll bouncing.
* Offers dozens of built-in and customizable visual headers and footers ranging from classic styles to animated designs.
* Modular library structure lets you include only the visual headers and footers your project actually needs.
* Fully compatible with modern AndroidX libraries and smooth coordinate layout scrolling.
