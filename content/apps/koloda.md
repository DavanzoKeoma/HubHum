### What it does in plain English
Koloda gives iOS apps a smooth, swipe‑able stack of cards that users can flick left or right.  
It works just like the familiar card‑swiping you see in popular dating apps.  
You can customize the look and behavior to fit any kind of content.

### Who it's for
* iOS app creators who want interactive, swipe‑able lists.  
* Designers looking for a ready‑made card animation that feels modern.  
* Hobbyists building simple demo projects with fun gestures.

### Key features
- Ready‑to‑use card stack that slides and bounces naturally.  
- Simple overlay that changes color when a card is dragged left or right.  
- Easy way to load and unload card views as the user swipes.  
- Built‑in support for undoing a swipe or resetting the stack.  
- Works on iOS 11 and later.

* **Pros:** free to use, no subscription, works with Swift 5, highly customizable.  
* **Cons:** requires a basic understanding of iOS view handling, advanced tweaks need some coding.
