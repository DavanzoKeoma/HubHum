### What it does in plain English
When you open a mobile application, there is often a brief moment where the screen is blank while the app prepares itself. This tool solves that problem by letting developers show a welcoming picture or logo immediately when the app opens. Once the app finishes loading its background tasks, the tool smoothly hides the picture to reveal your main screen.

### Who it's for
* Mobile application creators looking to polish their app startup experience.
* Developers building cross-platform apps for both Apple and Android devices.
* Programmers who want a simple, reliable way to manage app loading transitions.

### Key features
* Works smoothly on both Apple iOS and Google Android mobile devices.
* Gives you full control to show or hide the loading screen using simple code.
* Supports custom images, animations, and background colors to match your brand.
* Handles screen scaling automatically across different phone and tablet sizes.

### How to install (Download)
You can find the project files and release history on the [Official Releases Page](https://github.com/crazycodeboy/react-native-splash-screen/releases). To add this tool to your project, use your terminal to run:

`npm i react-native-splash-screen --save`

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, highly customizable, provides a much more polished user experience during startup.
* **Cons:** Requires a small amount of setup code in both Apple and Android project files.
