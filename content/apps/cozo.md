### What it does in plain English
Cozo is a smart storage system designed to handle information that is deeply interconnected, like social networks or complex research notes. Unlike traditional systems, it allows you to ask questions about how different pieces of data relate to each other using simple, human-readable logic. It is built to be fast and efficient, whether you are running it on a small personal device or a large server.

### Who it's for
* **Data enthusiasts and researchers** who need to map out complex relationships between different pieces of information.
* **Software creators** who want a reliable, easy-to-use database that can grow with their projects without needing complicated setup.

### Key features
* **Logical Querying:** Uses a clear, rule-based language that makes finding connections between data points straightforward.
* **Graph Intelligence:** Specifically designed to understand and navigate "networks" of data, making it perfect for finding hidden patterns.
* **Time Travel:** Allows you to view your data as it existed at any point in the past, ensuring you never lose track of changes.
* **Highly Portable:** Can be embedded directly into your own programs or run as a standalone service on almost any computer.
* **Efficient Performance:** Optimized to handle millions of records quickly while using very little memory.
