### What it does in plain English
ani-cli is a lightweight tool that allows you to search for and watch anime shows without needing a web browser. It connects to online sources to fetch video streams and plays them using your preferred media player. This provides a clean, distraction-free way to enjoy your favorite series directly from your desktop.

### Who it's for
* Anime fans who prefer a minimalist, keyboard-driven interface over cluttered websites.
* Users who want to watch shows on older or low-power hardware where web browsers might run slowly.

### Key features
* **Fast Searching:** Quickly find any show by typing its name directly into your terminal.
* **Streamlined Playback:** Automatically opens your chosen video player for a smooth viewing experience.
* **Download Support:** Easily save episodes to your device for offline viewing later.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works on Linux, macOS, Windows, and even mobile devices like Android.
* **Community Driven:** Regularly updated by a dedicated group of volunteers to ensure it keeps working smoothly.
