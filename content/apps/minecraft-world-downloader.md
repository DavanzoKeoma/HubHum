### What it does in plain English
This tool acts as a clever bridge between your game and an online multiplayer server. As you walk around and explore, it quietly records and saves all the surrounding map pieces directly to your computer. You can also use it to push previously visited chunks back to your game to see much further into the distance than normal.

### Who it's for
* Minecraft players who want to preserve their favorite multiplayer structures and builds.
* Explorers looking to keep a local offline copy of server maps they visit.
* Users wanting an expanded view distance on servers that normally restrict it.

### Key features
* Works smoothly with any game version without needing special client modifications.
* Automatically combines new discoveries with your existing saved world files.
* Captures container and storage inventories simply by opening them in game.
* Provides a clear map overview showing all your saved chunks in real time.
