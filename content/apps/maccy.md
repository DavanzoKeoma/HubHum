### What it does in plain English
Maccy silently records everything you copy on your Mac so you never lose an important text snippet or link again. When you need something you copied earlier, a simple keyboard shortcut opens a neat menu where you can instantly search through your history. You can pin frequently used items to the top, clear sensitive data with a keystroke, or paste items directly without formatting.

### Who it's for
* Writers and researchers who frequently copy multiple text snippets, quotes, and web links throughout the day.
* Developers and designers who need to manage snippets of code or text without cluttering their workflow.
* Anyone looking for a fast, minimalist tool to organize their computer clipboard history.

### Key features
* Lightweight and fast performance designed natively for macOS.
* Keyboard-first navigation lets you find and paste items without touching the mouse.
* Secure handling that automatically ignores sensitive data from password managers.
* Pinned items stay at the top of your history list for instant access.
* Clean, native user interface that blends seamlessly with your system.

### How to install (Download)
Download the application directly from the [Official Maccy Website](https://maccy.app).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely open source and free, extremely lightweight, keyboard-friendly.
* **Cons:** Requires macOS Sonoma 14 or higher, does not handle rich image previews like some heavyweight commercial alternatives.
