### What it does in plain English
inKino provides a clean, user-friendly interface to browse current movies and showtimes specifically for Finnkino cinema locations. It allows you to quickly check what is playing, view event details, and plan your theater visits. The app is designed to work seamlessly across your phone, tablet, and web browser.

### Who it's for
* Movie enthusiasts who frequently visit Finnkino cinemas.
* Users who want a fast, reliable way to check showtimes without navigating complex websites.

### Key features
* **Real-time Schedules:** Access up-to-date showtimes for all Finnkino cinema locations.
* **Movie Details:** View comprehensive information about films currently in theaters.
* **Multiplatform Support:** Consistent experience whether you are using a mobile device or a web browser.
* **Clean Interface:** A streamlined design focused on getting you the information you need quickly.
