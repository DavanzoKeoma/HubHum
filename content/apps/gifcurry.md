### What it does in plain English
Gifcurry is a dedicated video editor designed specifically for creating high-quality GIFs and short video loops. You can import most common video files, select precise timestamps, crop unwanted edges, and style your creation with customized text. Whether you prefer a point-and-click visual window or advanced terminal commands, it gives you complete control over your final output.

### Who it's for
* Content creators looking to produce custom animated memes and reactions for social media or messaging apps.
* Developers and designers who need lightweight looping animations to showcase software features in project documentation.
* Presenters and educators wanting to extract specific video highlights into punchy, silent visual clips.

### Key features
* **Dual Interface:** Choose between an approachable visual window or a powerful command-line tool.
* **Flexible Output:** Save your creations as traditional looping image files or standard video formats.
* **Custom Text Overlays:** Add styled captions with full control over fonts, colors, rotation, outline size, and timing.
* **Precision Cropping:** Trim unwanted borders from any side of the video frame to focus on the action.
* **Lightweight Performance:** Built natively without heavy web-browser software foundation for fast operation.
