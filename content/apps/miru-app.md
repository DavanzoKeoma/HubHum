### What it does in plain English
Miru App is an all-in-one entertainment hub that lets you read comics and novels while streaming your favorite videos in a single unified interface. It connects to various community sources and custom extensions so you can easily organize and enjoy your media collection across different devices. Features like automatic metadata matching and progress tracking keep your library tidy without manual effort.

### Who it's for
* Entertainment enthusiasts who want a single app to manage videos, reading material, and digital books.
* Readers and viewers who love customizing their content sources through open extensions.
* Users looking for an ad-free, cross-platform media reader and player for desktop and mobile.

### Key features
* **Multi-Format Support:** Read online comics and novels alongside watching video streams in one place.
* **Custom Extensions:** Easily add and manage community extension sources to expand your library.
* **Automatic Metadata:** Automatically fetches detailed information and artwork for your media using online databases.
* **Progress Tracking:** Connects with popular tracking services to keep tabs on what you have read or watched.
* **Network Privacy:** Built-in support for proxy server protocols to route your connection securely.
