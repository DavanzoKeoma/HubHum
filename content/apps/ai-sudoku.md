### What it does in plain English
This application lets you take a picture of a Sudoku puzzle and automatically finds the correct answer. It uses smart image processing to clean up the photo and recognize the handwritten or printed numbers. Once it reads the board, it calculates the solution so you can check your work or learn the final steps.

### Who it's for
*   **Puzzle enthusiasts** who want a quick way to verify their progress or get unstuck on a difficult level.
*   **Students and learners** interested in seeing how computers can recognize shapes and solve logical problems.
*   **Casual players** who prefer a hands-free approach to solving puzzles without typing in every number manually.

### Key features
*   **Photo Recognition:** Automatically detects the grid and numbers from a standard camera or phone photo.
*   **Smart Correction:** Allows you to manually fix any numbers the software misread before solving.
*   **Two Solving Engines:** Offers a choice between two different recognition methods, with the default option providing high accuracy.
*   **Visual Step-by-Step:** Shows you the stages of image processing, such as cleaning the image and finding the grid lines.
*   **Free and Private:** Runs entirely on your own computer, so your photos and puzzles never leave your device.
