### What it does in plain English
Many modern online video and media platforms no longer provide simple ways to follow updates using standard feed readers. This handy tool acts as a bridge, taking data from popular sites and outputting clean update feeds. You can easily plug these custom feeds into your preferred news reader to stay updated on your own terms.

### Who it's for
* Dedicated news readers who want to aggregate all their favorite content streams in one unified application.
* Privacy-focused users who prefer independent feed readers over algorithmic platform timelines.

### Key features
* Transforms various major video and media platforms into standard news feeds.
* Lightweight web application that can be run on your own server or accessed via public community instances.
* Supports optional caching tools to speed up link resolution and performance.
* Works seamlessly with most standard news reading software.

### How to install (Download)
You can find public community-run instances and setup instructions directly on the [Public RSS Box Instances Discussion Page](https://github.com/stefansundin/rssbox/discussions/64). If you prefer running your own instance, source code and instructions are available on the official source page.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open source, brings order back to fragmented media sites.
* **Cons:** Requires obtaining your own platform developer keys for certain services to function properly.
