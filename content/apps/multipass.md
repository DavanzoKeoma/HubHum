### What it does in plain English
Multipass creates a small, separate Ubuntu computer inside your own computer.  
You can start, stop, and delete these Ubuntu instances whenever you need them.  
It lets you try out Linux programs or learn Linux without touching your regular setup.

### Who it's for
Students learning Linux, hobbyists experimenting with software, and developers who need a clean test environment.

### Key features
- One‑click creation of a fresh Ubuntu system.  
- Easy start, stop, and delete commands.  
- Automatic download and updates of Ubuntu images.  
- Works on Windows, macOS and Linux.  
- Supports cloud‑init scripts for advanced setup.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, works on all major desktop OSes, no separate VM software needed.  
* **Cons:** primarily command‑line driven, may require administrator rights, initial download size can be several hundred megabytes.
