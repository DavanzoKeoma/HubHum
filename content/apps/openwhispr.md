### What it does in plain English
OpenWhispr allows you to dictate text into any application on your computer using a simple keyboard shortcut. It offers the unique ability to process your speech entirely offline, ensuring your private conversations and notes never leave your device. You can also connect it to various smart AI assistants to help you organize your thoughts, summarize meetings, or draft documents quickly.

### Who it's for
*   Professionals who need to transcribe meetings or dictate notes while maintaining strict data privacy.
*   Writers and students who want to speed up their workflow by speaking their ideas instead of typing them.

### Key features
*   **Private Dictation:** Speak into any app and have your words appear instantly at your cursor.
*   **Offline Processing:** Choose to run everything locally on your own hardware for maximum security.
*   **Smart Meeting Tools:** Automatically detect and transcribe calls with speaker identification.
*   **Flexible AI Integration:** Use built-in AI tools or connect your own preferred services to help summarize or edit your text.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
