### What it does in plain English
CotEditor is a streamlined, lightning-fast text editor built exclusively for Mac users who want a clean space to write notes, code, or scripts. It opens instantly and stays completely out of your way, letting you focus entirely on your words. Because it is built directly into the Mac system architecture, it feels completely natural and familiar from the very first second you open it.

### Who it's for
* Writers and bloggers drafting notes, articles, or markdown documents without heavy formatting distractions.
* Programmers and web developers needing a fast utility to view or edit raw code files.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, reliable replacement for bulky office software when handling basic text files.

### Key features
* Built entirely for Mac with a native look and feel that respects system appearance settings.
* Extremely fast startup time so you can open large text files instantly.
* Advanced text encoding support to handle legacy or specialized character sets without errors.
* Customizable color themes and syntax highlighting for dozens of programming and markup languages.
* Distraction-free full-screen mode to keep your attention locked on your writing.
