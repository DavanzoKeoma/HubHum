### What it does in plain English
Cinetry acts as a central hub that connects to your various personal media storage services, allowing you to browse and play your content from one place. It does not host or store any files itself, ensuring your data remains private and under your control. You can easily switch between different servers and search across your entire collection with a single, consistent experience.

### Who it's for
*   Home media enthusiasts who use multiple platforms like Jellyfin, Emby, or cloud storage to organize their movies and music.
*   Users who want a clean, unified way to access their personal digital libraries on their phone, computer, or TV without managing multiple different apps.

### Key features
*   **Unified Search:** Find content across all your connected media servers simultaneously.
*   **Cross-Platform Support:** Works seamlessly on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and TV devices.
*   **Privacy-Focused:** Operates as a local client that does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data.
*   **Broad Compatibility:** Connects to a wide variety of services including Jellyfin, Emby, Webdav, Alist, and many more.
*   **No Library Scanning:** Fetches data directly from your servers on demand, keeping your device performance fast.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free, respects user privacy, and supports a massive range of media services.
*   **Cons:** Requires you to have your own existing media servers set up; the interface is strictly a controller and does not provide content itself.
