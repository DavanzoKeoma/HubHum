### What it does in plain English
AF-Media-Bar brings intuitive music controls and synchronized song lyrics directly to your Windows taskbar or desktop. It connects smoothly with whatever media player or streaming app is currently running on your computer. You can easily switch playback tracks, adjust sound output devices, and watch scrolling lyrics without opening full applications.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who want to view scrolling song lyrics on their desktop while working.
* Everyday computer users who want a cleaner, faster way to control background music and audio devices.
* Anyone looking to customize their Windows taskbar with useful, lightweight desktop widgets.

### Key features
* Real-time media session detection working automatically with major music and video players.
* Synchronized scrolling lyrics display fetched from online project pages.
* Quick audio output device switching directly from the desktop overlay.
* Clean, modern interface designed to match Windows 10 and 11 aesthetic standards.
* Lightweight performance with no background advertising or tracking accounts required.
