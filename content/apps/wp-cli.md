### What it does in plain English
WP-CLI is a tool that lets you control your WordPress website by typing short text commands instead of clicking through web menus. It helps you perform repetitive tasks like updating plugins, managing users, or cleaning up your database in seconds. By bypassing the traditional dashboard, it provides a faster and more efficient way to handle site maintenance.

### Who it's for
* **Website Developers:** Professionals who need to manage multiple sites quickly without navigating through browser-based menus.
* **Site Administrators:** Users comfortable with basic computer commands who want to automate routine maintenance and troubleshooting tasks.

### Key features
* **Bulk Management:** Update all your plugins or themes at once with a single command.
* **Database Control:** Easily perform complex search-and-replace operations across your entire site content.
* **Advanced Troubleshooting:** Access site functions that are not available in the standard web dashboard.
* **Automation Ready:** Create custom scripts to handle your specific site workflows automatically.
* **Resource Efficient:** Performs tasks directly on the server, saving time and avoiding browser timeouts.

### How to install (Download)
The recommended way to use this tool is by downloading the standalone file:
* [Download for Linux/macOS (wp-cli-2.12.0.phar)](https://github.com/wp-cli/wp-cli/releases/download/v2.12.0/wp-cli-2.12.0.phar)

*Note: After downloading, you will need to make the file executable and move it to your system's path to run it by typing `wp` in your terminal.*

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, significantly faster than manual dashboard clicking, and highly customizable for power users.
* **Cons:** Requires comfort with a text-based terminal interface and is primarily designed for Linux or macOS environments.
