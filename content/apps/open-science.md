### What it does in plain English
AIPOCH Open-Science is a local-first software workbench designed to help researchers organize literature, analyze data, and run AI models safely on their own computers. It tracks every step of your analytical workflow so you can trace how a conclusion was reached. When your work is complete, you can bundle your notes, code, and findings into a single shareable package that lets others verify your exact evidence.

### Who it's for
* Academic and independent researchers wanting a local assistant for literature reviews and data analysis.
* Data scientists and students running code workflows in Python or R alongside intelligent agents.
* Research groups aiming to make their experiments and study outcomes fully transparent and reproducible.

### Key features
* **Local-first privacy:** Keeps your research data, settings, and file history securely stored on your own device.
* **Reproducible packages:** Export self-contained research packages so colleagues can inspect your exact data, code, and evidence trail.
* **Integrated programming:** Run Python or R scripts directly within your interactive analysis notebooks.
* **Flexible model connections:** Connect your own choice of model providers or use supported subscriptions.
* **Detailed provenance:** Track the origin of generated figures and tables back to their original inputs and code execution history.
