### What it does in plain English
EarTrumpet replaces the basic Windows volume slider with a powerful, easy-to-use mixer that sits right in your taskbar. It allows you to adjust the sound levels for each open program separately, so you can keep your music loud while muting a noisy web browser. You can also quickly switch which speakers or headphones your apps use with just a few clicks.

### Who it's for
* **Multitaskers** who want to balance audio levels between games, video calls, and music players.
* **Power users** who find the standard Windows audio settings too limited for managing multiple playback devices.

### Key features
* **Individual App Control:** Adjust the volume of specific programs without affecting the entire system.
* **Quick Device Switching:** Easily move audio from one device to another, such as switching from speakers to headphones.
* **Visual Feedback:** See real-time audio activity for every app with clear, responsive volume bars.
* **Customizable Shortcuts:** Set your own keyboard combinations to control volume levels quickly.
* **System Integration:** Matches your Windows light or dark theme and feels like a native part of the operating system.

* **Pros:** Completely free, lightweight, and integrates perfectly with the Windows interface.
* **Cons:** Designed specifically for Windows 10 and 11, so it is not available for other operating systems.
