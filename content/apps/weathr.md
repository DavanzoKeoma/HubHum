### What it does in plain English
Weathr brings the outside world straight to your command-line workspace by showing current meteorological conditions with delightful character-art animations. It fetches live climate updates based on your current location or custom coordinates, showing realistic elements like falling rain, snowstorms, flying airplanes, and day-to-night cycles. You can easily test different weather scenarios, customize measurement units, or check conditions quickly without opening a web browser.

### Who it's for
* Developers and system administrators who spend all day inside command-line interfaces and want quick temperature checks.
* Terminal enthusiasts who love retro aesthetics and custom text-based visual displays.
* Anyone looking for a lightweight, fast way to monitor local atmospheric conditions without heavy weather apps.

### Key features
* Real-time weather updates powered by open meteorological data services.
* Engaging text-based animations including rain, thunderstorms, snow, and passing airplanes.
* Automatic location detection based on your network connection or manual coordinate setting.
* Fully customizable display settings for temperature units, wind speeds, and interface layouts.
* Sandbox simulation mode allowing you to preview any weather condition on demand.
