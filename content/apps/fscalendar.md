### What it does in plain English
FSCalendar is a specialized toolkit designed for mobile app creators who need to display dates and schedules. It provides a visual calendar interface that users can interact with, swipe through, and customize to match the look of their app. It is built to be reliable and smooth, ensuring that date-based features work perfectly on Apple devices.

### Who it's for
* Mobile app developers building scheduling, task management, or event-tracking tools.
* Designers looking for a polished, ready-to-use calendar component that fits seamlessly into an iOS interface.

### Key features
* **Full Customization:** Easily change colors, fonts, and layouts to match your app's unique design.
* **Interactive Gestures:** Supports smooth swiping and selection, making it easy for users to pick dates.
* **Flexible Views:** Switch between monthly and weekly views with simple animations.
* **Native Performance:** Built specifically for iOS to ensure it feels fast and responsive for end-users.
* **Easy Integration:** Works well with standard development tools used for creating iPhone and iPad software.
