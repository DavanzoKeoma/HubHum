### What it does in plain English
This tool automates the process of creating a personal virtual private network server on a cloud machine or virtual server. It encrypts all your internet data so that outsiders cannot intercept or monitor your online activity while you browse on public or unsecured networks. For full control over encrypted off-site backups and private syncing, pair your setup with cloud storage on [InterServer](https://www.interserver.net/r/1196747?id=hubhum_01_MER008) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Individuals looking for a secure, private tunnel to browse the web safely from coffee shops, airports, or hotels.
* Tech enthusiasts and system administrators who want a reliable personal VPN without relying on paid commercial subscription services.
