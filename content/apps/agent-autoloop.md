### What it does in plain English
Agent Autoloop turns your existing AI tools into self-sufficient workers that handle repetitive tasks without needing you to watch the screen. It runs quietly in the background, managing workflows and following up on progress automatically. You only get a notification when the AI hits a roadblock or needs your final approval to proceed.

### Who it's for
* **Developers and Power Users** who use AI coding assistants and want them to handle multi-step tasks without manual supervision.
* **Productivity Enthusiasts** who want to automate routine digital chores like monitoring issues, organizing notes, or checking system status.

### Key features
* **Zero-Interruption Workflow:** AI agents work independently and only pause to ask for your input when necessary.
* **Universal Compatibility:** Works with most command-line AI tools without requiring you to change their original code.
* **Flexible Notifications:** Sends alerts to your preferred messaging apps or desktop notifications when it needs your attention.
* **Lightweight Efficiency:** Runs locally on your own computer, meaning no need for expensive cloud servers or public internet exposure.
* **Easy Rule Setup:** Configure what tasks to run and when using simple, human-readable settings.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open-source, runs entirely on your local machine, and significantly reduces manual AI monitoring.
* **Cons:** Requires basic comfort with command-line tools and initial setup of rules to define how your AI agents should behave.
