### What it does in plain English
Revanced Manager lets you add extra features to Android apps.  
It downloads the apps you want and applies the chosen patches.  
You can also tweak settings and keep everything organized in one place.  

### Who it's for
Android users who want more control over their apps.  
People who like to customize video or social media apps.  
Anyone who prefers a single app to manage patches.  

### Key features
- Download apps automatically.  
- Apply patches to any supported app.  
- Manage themes, signing, and updates.  
- Customize settings for each app.  
- Keep a history of patched versions.
