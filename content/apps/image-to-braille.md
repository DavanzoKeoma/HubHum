### What it does in plain English
Image-to-Braille takes any ordinary photo or graphic and converts it into textured patterns made entirely of unicode braille dots. It processes your visual files right on your screen using advanced shading and dithering techniques. You can easily copy and paste the resulting text art directly into messages, documents, or social media posts.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and creators looking to experiment with retro text-based aesthetics.
* Retro computer enthusiasts who love terminal-style visuals and ASCII art variations.
* Anyone looking to surprise friends by sending unique, text-based picture messages.

### Key features
* Instant browser-based conversion with zero files to download or install.
* Multiple visual rendering modes including standard conversion and advanced dithering.
* Inverted shade options to switch between light and dark background contrast.
* One-click copying to quickly transfer your newly created braille art anywhere.

### How to install (Download)
This is a web-based tool that runs entirely in your internet browser. You do not need to download or set up any software on your device. Simply open the [Official Web Application](https://505e06b2.github.io/Image-to-Braille/) to start converting your images immediately.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, works instantly in any web browser, requires no account creation or setup.
* **Cons:** Complex images with high color detail may lose clarity when translated into monochrome dots.
