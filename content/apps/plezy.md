### What it does in plain English
Plezy acts as a central hub that connects to your existing media collections, whether they are stored on Plex, Jellyfin, or Emby. It provides a beautiful, consistent way to browse your library, watch your favorite shows, and listen to music across your phone, computer, or television. You can even sync your watch progress and download content to enjoy offline whenever you are on the go.

### Who it's for
*   **Media Enthusiasts:** People who self-host their own movie and music libraries and want a high-quality, unified way to access them.
*   **Multi-Device Users:** Anyone who wants a seamless viewing experience that works exactly the same way on a desktop computer, a mobile phone, or a living room TV.

### Key features
*   **Unified Library:** Browse and play content from multiple media servers simultaneously in one app.
*   **Smart Playback:** Supports a wide range of video formats, high-quality audio, and automatic subtitle handling.
*   **Offline Access:** Download your favorite movies, shows, or music tracks to watch or listen without an internet connection.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Designed to work perfectly on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS with full support for remote controls and gamepads.
*   **Watch Together:** Host or join virtual watch parties with friends, allowing everyone to sync their playback perfectly.
