### What it does in plain English
Text Grab lets you pull text out of images, videos, PDFs, and locked application screens where normal copying fails. You simply take a snapshot or open a file, run it through the built-in scanner, and paste the clean result wherever you need it. Everything happens completely on your device without sending your personal data to external cloud servers.

### Who it's for
* Students and researchers extracting quotes or data tables from lecture slides and scanned books
* Office workers and data entry professionals moving invoice details and reference numbers into spreadsheets
* Everyday computer users who want to grab text from error messages, videos, or un-selectable web pages

### Key features
* **Full-Screen and Frame Capture:** Select any region of your display to instantly copy the recognized words to your clipboard
* **Built-In Text Cleanup:** Easily trim spacing, remove duplicate lines, convert cases, and format text into clean tables
* **Smart Calculations:** Evaluate numbers, quantities, and dates immediately using a built-in calculation pane alongside your text
* **Quick Lookup Memory:** Store frequently used phrases, web links, or identification numbers and paste them instantly with a shortcut
* **Bulk Folder Processing:** Scan entire folders of receipts, invoices, or screenshots in one batch to extract all text at once
