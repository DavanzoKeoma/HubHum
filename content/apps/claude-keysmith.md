### What it does in plain English
This utility helps you manage custom instructions for your local AI coding assistant so it performs better on your specific projects. It lets you preview any changes before applying them, ensuring you always know what instructions are being added. If you ever want to go back to the original settings, you can completely reverse the changes with a simple command.

### Who it's for
* Developers and power users who want more reliable and accurate responses from their local AI coding assistant.
* Anyone who prefers to preview configuration changes before letting software modify their project environments.
* Users looking for an easy way to switch custom helper instructions on and off across different coding projects.

### Key features
* **Safe preview mode:** Always reviews planned modifications before writing anything to your device.
* **Easy recovery:** Fully removes or rolls back custom instructions whenever you want.
* **Account privacy:** Works entirely locally without reading or modifying your private account keys.
* **Project scoping:** Targets specific project folders so instructions only apply where you need them.
