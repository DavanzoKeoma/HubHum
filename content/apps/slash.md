### What it does in plain English
Slash acts as a personal directory for your most frequently used websites and documents. Instead of hunting through bookmarks or old emails, you can create custom, human-readable shortcuts like "s/project" to jump directly to your destination. It keeps your digital workspace tidy and makes sharing important resources with your team effortless.

### Who it's for
*   **Remote Teams:** Professionals who need a central place to share internal company links and project dashboards.
*   **Power Users:** Individuals who want to stop wasting time searching for specific web pages and prefer a faster way to navigate their browser.

### Key features
*   **Custom Shortcuts:** Create easy-to-type, memorable shortcuts for any web address.
*   **Team Sharing:** Organize links into collections and share them securely with colleagues.
*   **Traffic Insights:** See how often your shortcuts are used to understand which resources are most valuable.
*   **Browser Integration:** Use the dedicated browser extension to trigger your shortcuts directly from the address bar.
*   **Self-Hosted Privacy:** Keep your data under your own control by hosting the platform on your own infrastructure.

### How to install (Download)
Slash is designed to be hosted on your own server. You can find the setup instructions and the browser extensions below:
*   [Self-hosting guide with Docker](https://github.com/yourselfhosted/slash/blob/main/docs/install.md)
*   [Browser Extension for Chrome, Edge, and Arc](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/slash/ebaiehmkammnacjadffpicipfckgeobg)
*   [Browser Extension for Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/your-slash/)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open source, highly customizable, and significantly improves daily navigation speed.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup to host it yourself, and it is not a "plug-and-play" service for non-technical users.
