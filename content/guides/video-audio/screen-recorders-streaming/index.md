### All Qualifying Software in This Topic

- **[Streamlink](/HubHum/apps/streamlink/)** — Streamlink lets you watch live video content directly in your favorite media player instead of using resource-heavy web browsers. *(CLI Tool · License: Open Source (BSD))*
- **[OpenScreen](/HubHum/apps/openscreen/)** — OpenScreen is a free, open-source tool that lets you record your screen and turn your captures into polished product demos and walkthroughs. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[eSearch](/HubHum/apps/esearch/)** — eSearch is a versatile utility that combines screen recording, text recognition, and instant translation into one seamless experience. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (GPL v3))*
- **[StreamCap](/HubHum/apps/streamcap/)** — StreamCap automatically monitors and records live broadcasts from dozens of websites so you never miss your favorite content. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (Apache 2.0))*
- **[Seanime](/HubHum/apps/seanime/)** — Seanime organizes your local media library and provides a unified interface for streaming anime and reading manga on any device. *(Self-Hosted Web App · License: Open Source (GPL v3))*
- **[streamlink-twitch-gui](/HubHum/apps/streamlink-twitch-gui/)** — A lightweight desktop application that lets you watch Twitch streams in your favorite media player instead of a heavy web browser. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[DownVid](/HubHum/apps/downvid/)** — DownVid is a modern, cross-platform video downloader that helps you save high-definition media from popular websites without watermarks. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[MarkerOn](/HubHum/apps/markeron/)** — Draw, highlight, and annotate directly on your computer screen using simple keyboard shortcuts. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[Kiri](/HubHum/apps/kiri/)** — Capture screenshots, record desktop video, and extract text from images with a fast local tool that keeps all your files completely private. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[CineFlow](/HubHum/apps/cineflow/)** — CineFlow is a modern desktop app that brings all your favorite movies and shows together with custom recommendations and smooth playback. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (MIT))*
- **[HotClip](/HubHum/apps/hotclip/)** — Transform long videos and live streams into engaging vertical short clips locally without watermarks or subscriptions. *(Desktop App · License: Open Source (AGPL v3))*
