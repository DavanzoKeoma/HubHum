### What it does in plain English
Axiom takes complex multimedia processing tools and puts them inside a clean, simple window. Instead of typing complicated text commands, you can click through options to change video formats, trim timelines, and adjust quality settings. It also helps you download media clips from online platforms and automatically generates the background processing code for you.

### Who it's for
* Video creators who want to convert media formats without learning complex command-line syntax.
* Content editors looking for a quick way to trim clips and batch-process files.
* Everyday users who need a portable tool to handle video and audio adjustments on Windows.

### Key features
* Simple visual interface that removes the need for text-based commands.
* Convert media files across a wide variety of popular formats.
* Built-in tools to trim timelines and resize video dimensions.
* Support for lossless and custom quality settings.
* Integrated helper for downloading video and music clips from the web.
