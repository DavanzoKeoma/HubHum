### What it does in plain English
Yuxi acts as a private command center where teams can store their documents and create custom digital assistants to handle complex work. It allows you to organize information, link related concepts, and run automated tasks while keeping all your data safely under your own control. By using this platform, teams can turn their internal knowledge into actionable results without relying on external cloud services.

### Who it's for
*   **Business Teams:** Organizations that need to manage internal documentation and automate repetitive research or reporting tasks.
*   **Data-Conscious Professionals:** Individuals or teams who want the power of modern AI tools but require full ownership and privacy of their data.

### Key features
*   **Unified Workspace:** A single interface to chat with your data, manage tasks, and view generated files.
*   **Smart Document Handling:** Automatically processes and indexes your files so you can ask questions and get accurate, context-aware answers.
*   **Customizable Assistants:** Build and configure your own agents to perform specific roles, use custom tools, or follow unique workflows.
*   **Visual Knowledge Mapping:** Automatically connects information across your documents to help you see how different topics and entities relate to each other.
*   **Secure Collaboration:** Manage team access and permissions to ensure that sensitive information remains visible only to the right people.

### How to install (Download)
Yuxi is designed to be self-hosted. You can find the installation instructions and the latest setup files on the [Official Yuxi Project Page](https://xerrors.github.io/Yuxi/).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Complete control over your data, supports private hosting, highly customizable for team workflows, and includes built-in tools for file management.
*   **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up and maintain a server, and you must provide your own connection to AI model services.
