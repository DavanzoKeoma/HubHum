### What it does in plain English
MacPass acts as a secure digital safe for all your website logins, Wi-Fi passwords, and other sensitive information. It uses strong encryption to keep your data private, ensuring that only you can access your credentials. The app is designed specifically for Mac users, offering a familiar interface that feels natural and easy to navigate.

### Who it's for
* **Mac users who value privacy:** Individuals who prefer keeping their personal data offline and encrypted rather than relying on cloud-based services.
* **Tech-savvy professionals:** People who need a reliable, local tool to manage numerous complex passwords without internet connectivity.
* **Open-source advocates:** Users who want full transparency into how their security software works and prefer community-driven development.

### Key features
* **Local Encryption:** Your password database is stored on your computer and protected by a master password, keeping your data offline.
* **KeePass Compatibility:** It can open and edit standard KeePass database files, making it easy to switch from other similar tools.
* **Native Mac Design:** Built specifically for macOS, it integrates smoothly with the system’s look and feel, including support for Touch ID.
* **Plugin Support:** You can extend the app’s capabilities with additional plugins, such as password generators or browser integrations.
* **Open Source:** The entire code is publicly available for review, ensuring no hidden tracking or backdoors exist in the software.
