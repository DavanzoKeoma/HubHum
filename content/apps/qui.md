### What it does in plain English
qui provides a clean, modern control panel that replaces the default interface for your file-sharing software. It allows you to connect and manage several different accounts or instances from one single window. The app also includes smart tools to automatically organize your files and keep your collection updated across different sources.

### Who it's for
* Power users who manage multiple file-sharing instances and want a unified view.
* People looking for a faster, more responsive way to organize and automate their downloads.

### Key features
* **Unified Control:** Manage all your file-sharing instances from one single, lightweight application.
* **Smart Automation:** Set up custom rules to automatically handle, sort, and organize your files.
* **High Performance:** Designed to stay fast and responsive even when managing very large collections.
* **Cross-Source Syncing:** Automatically find and add matching files across different trackers to keep your library complete.
* **Backup Tools:** Create scheduled snapshots of your settings to ensure your data is always safe and recoverable.
