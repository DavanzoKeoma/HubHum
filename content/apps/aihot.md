### What it does in plain English
This open-source web application automatically collects updates from multiple news sources and uses artificial intelligence to pick out the most relevant stories. It groups similar reports together, calculates what people are currently discussing, and generates clean daily, weekly, or monthly digests. You can customize the sources and selection criteria to fit any specific industry, turning the engine into your own personalized publication website.

### Who it's for
* Industry professionals and subject matter experts looking to curate automated newsletters for their niche.
* Community leaders and researchers wanting to track and summarize fast-moving discussions online.
* Website creators who want an out-of-the-box publishing engine powered by modern artificial intelligence.

### Key features
* **Automated Curation:** Scans feeds, web pages, and social channels, then uses independent scoring to select truly noteworthy content.
* **Smart Grouping:** Combines multiple reports about the same story into a single organized event with a clear chronological timeline.
* **Automated Digests:** Compiles daily, weekly, and monthly summaries automatically without extra manual work.
* **Flexible Customization:** Easily adjust categories, topics, and editorial prompts to match your unique industry requirements.
* **Ready-to-Use APIs:** Exposes content through standard web feeds and assistant-friendly endpoints for seamless sharing.

### How to install (Download)
To run your own independent website, visit the [Official Source / Credits](https://github.com/KKKKhazix/AIHOT) and clone the codebase to your server. You can also view the live demonstration and preview the project directly on the [Official Website](https://aihot.news).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely self-hosted, highly customizable editorial rules, and automated summary generation.
* **Cons:** Requires an external artificial intelligence model access key and basic technical familiarity to set up.
