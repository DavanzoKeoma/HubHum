### What it does in plain English
Kooboo lets you build, edit, and run complete websites and online stores from a single, easy-to-use tool. It includes a built-in AI assistant that can help you create pages, fix issues, and manage content without needing to hire a developer. You keep full control of your website files and can move them to any computer or server you choose.

### Who it's for
*   **Small business owners** who need a professional online store or brochure site but do not have a technical team.
*   **Freelancers and agencies** looking for a fast way to build and deliver client websites with full ownership.
*   **Tech-savvy individuals** who want to learn web development using a modern, all-in-one environment with AI support.

### Key features
*   **Built-in AI Assistant:** Helps you build, test, and repair your website by understanding your pages and content directly.
*   **All-in-One Tools:** Includes content management, online store features, email marketing, and analytics in one package.
*   **Portable and Private:** Your website data stays with you; you can download, back up, or move your site to any server.
*   **Visual and Code Editing:** Edit pages by clicking on elements or write custom code for more advanced functionality.
*   **Marketplace:** Access ready-made website templates and app packages that you can install and customize.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Free to use without time limits, includes powerful AI tools, and gives you full ownership of your website files.
*   **Cons:** Not open-source (you cannot view the internal code), and advanced features like TypeScript development require a paid plan.
