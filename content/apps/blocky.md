### What it does in plain English
Blocky acts as a digital gatekeeper for your home internet connection by filtering out advertisements and trackers before they load. It works across your entire network, meaning every device—from your phone to your smart fridge—benefits from a cleaner and faster browsing experience. Because it runs locally, it keeps your browsing habits private and does not share your data with any outside companies.

### Who it's for
*   **Privacy-conscious families** who want to protect their household from online tracking and malicious websites.
*   **Home network enthusiasts** who want a fast, lightweight way to manage internet traffic without needing a complex setup.

### Key features
*   **Network-wide filtering:** Blocks ads and trackers for every device connected to your home Wi-Fi or router.
*   **Custom control:** Allows you to create specific rules for different family members or devices, such as restricting certain content for children.
*   **Improved speed:** By stopping ads from loading, your web pages open faster and consume less data.
*   **Total privacy:** The software does not collect, store, or sell any of your personal data or browsing statistics.
*   **Flexible setup:** Designed to run efficiently on small home devices like a Raspberry Pi or standard home routers.

### How to install (Download)

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no hidden data collection, very low impact on system performance.
*   **Cons:** Requires a small amount of initial setup to configure your router or devices to use it.
