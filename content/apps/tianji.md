### What it does in plain English
Tianji is a comprehensive tool that helps you keep track of how your website is performing and whether your servers are running smoothly. Instead of juggling multiple separate services for traffic stats and system health, it brings everything into one organized view. It is designed to be lightweight and easy to manage for anyone who wants to monitor their digital projects without unnecessary complexity.

### Who it's for
*   **Website Owners:** Individuals who want to see visitor counts and page views without using complex, privacy-heavy tracking tools.
*   **System Administrators:** Small team leads who need to know immediately if their server goes offline or if performance is slowing down.

### Key features
*   **Unified Dashboard:** View website traffic, server status, and uptime alerts in a single, clean interface.
*   **Lightweight Tracking:** Get clear insights into your visitors and page performance without heavy overhead.
*   **Proactive Alerts:** Receive notifications if your website or server encounters connectivity issues.
*   **Team Collaboration:** Share monitoring data and reports easily with your colleagues.
*   **Flexible cloud setup:** Can be hosted on your own infrastructure to keep full control over your data.

*   **Pros:** Combines multiple essential tools into one, lightweight, open-source, and gives you full ownership of your data.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server setup (like Docker) to get the main dashboard running.
