### What it does in plain English
Realm is a specialized storage system designed for mobile devices like phones and tablets. It allows applications to keep track of user data locally, ensuring that information is saved instantly and remains accessible even when the device is offline. Because it is built specifically for mobile hardware, it helps apps run faster and use less battery power.

### Who it's for
* Mobile app creators looking for a reliable way to save user information.
* Developers who want their apps to remain fully functional when the user has no internet connection.

### Key features
* **Offline Access:** Keeps your data available on your device at all times, regardless of your internet connection status.
* **High Efficiency:** Designed to be lightweight, ensuring it doesn't drain your battery or take up unnecessary storage space.
* **Automatic Updates:** Changes made in one part of an app are instantly reflected everywhere else, keeping your data consistent.
* **Strong Security:** Offers built-in encryption to keep sensitive user information safe and private.

### How to install (Download)
Realm is designed to be integrated into mobile projects by developers. You can find the official setup instructions and integration guides at the [Realm Quick Start page](https://realm.io).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly efficient for mobile hardware, and works perfectly without an internet connection.
* **Cons:** Requires some programming knowledge to set up and integrate into an application.
