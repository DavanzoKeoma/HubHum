### What it does in plain English
QymCAD is a desktop application for designing mechanical parts, housings, and assemblies from scratch. Users can draw 2D sketches and turn them into solid 3D objects using extrusion, revolution, and other standard modeling tools. It automatically updates all related components and assemblies whenever a single dimension is modified.

### Who it's for
* Mechanical engineers and product designers seeking an offline 3D modeling environment.
* Makers and hobbyists creating custom parts for 3D printing and milling.
* Students learning parametric design and computer-aided drafting fundamentals.

### Key features
* Precise solid geometry powered by an industry-standard open modeling kernel.
* Parametric timeline where modifying any dimension automatically updates dependent features.
* Comprehensive assembly tools supporting joints, degrees of freedom, and interference checks.
* Broad file exchange capabilities supporting standard industry formats like STEP and STL.
* Entirely local operation with no mandatory cloud connection or subscription fees.
