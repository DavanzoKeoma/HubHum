### What it does in plain English
Baserow allows you to build powerful, custom databases and internal tools without needing to write a single line of code. It combines the ease of a familiar spreadsheet with the strength of a professional data management system. You can use it to track projects, manage customer information, or automate repetitive tasks, all while keeping your data private and secure.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who need to organize customer data or inventory without hiring a developer.
* Team leads looking to build custom internal dashboards and automated workflows to save time.

### Key features
* **Spreadsheet-like interface:** Manage your information using a familiar grid that is easy to learn and use.
* **Custom applications:** Build your own portals and dashboards to display your data exactly how you want.
* **Smart automation:** Set up rules to handle repetitive tasks automatically, reducing manual data entry.
* **AI Assistant:** Use built-in artificial intelligence to help you design your database structure and workflows through simple conversation.
* **Full data control:** Choose to use the cloud version or host it on your own server to ensure your data stays under your complete control.

### How to install (Download)
* **Get Started Online:** You can sign up and start using the platform immediately at [Baserow.io](https://baserow.io).
* **Self-Hosting:** For those who prefer to host it on their own infrastructure, detailed guides for [Docker](https://baserow.io/docs/installation/install-with-docker), [Helm](https://baserow.io/docs/installation/install-with-helm), and various cloud providers are available in the [Official Documentation](https://baserow.io/docs/index).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Extremely user-friendly interface, high level of data privacy, and powerful automation capabilities without needing technical skills.
* **Cons:** Advanced self-hosting setups may require some basic knowledge of server management.
