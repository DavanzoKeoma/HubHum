### What it does in plain English
Store-POS turns your computer into a professional cash register that works without an internet connection. You can scan products, manage your stock levels, and print receipts directly from the software. It also allows you to connect multiple computers in your store so they all share the same sales data in real time.

### Who it's for
*   **Small shop owners** who need a reliable, affordable alternative to expensive cloud-based register systems.
*   **Retail staff** who need a fast, simple interface for scanning items and processing payments.
*   **Multi-terminal businesses** that want to link several cash registers to a single central database on their local network.

### Key features
*   **Barcode & Search:** Quickly add items to the cart by scanning barcodes or typing product names.
*   **Inventory Management:** Track stock levels, add product photos, and organize items by category.
*   **Multi-Terminal Support:** Connect multiple computers on your local network to share a single database.
*   **Payment Flexibility:** Handle cash payments with quick-change calculations or process card transactions.
*   **Sales History:** Review past transactions by date, cashier, or status to keep your books accurate.

*   **Pros:** Free to use, works offline, supports multiple registers on one network, and includes built-in inventory tracking.
*   **Cons:** Primarily designed for Windows, and setting up multiple connected terminals requires basic network knowledge.
