### What it does in plain English
NETworkManager helps you manage and fix your home or office network connections from a single, organized dashboard. It includes built-in tools for connecting to remote computers, scanning for devices, and checking your internet speed. By gathering these features in one place, it removes the need to switch between multiple separate programs to troubleshoot your infrastructure.

### Who it's for
*   **System Administrators:** Professionals who need to manage multiple servers and network environments efficiently.
*   **IT Enthusiasts:** Home lab users who want a powerful, free way to monitor their local devices and troubleshoot connectivity issues.

### Key features
*   **Unified Dashboard:** Access remote desktop, file transfer, and command-line tools from one central window.
*   **Network Diagnostics:** Quickly scan for devices, check open ports, monitor connection quality, and trace network paths.
*   **Secure Profiles:** Organize your saved connections into encrypted profiles to keep sensitive login information safe.
*   **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Use the "Run Command" feature to launch any tool instantly by typing its name.
*   **Customizable Interface:** Choose between light and dark themes and select your preferred accent colors to match your workflow.
