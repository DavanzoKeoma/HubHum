### What it does in plain English
Radar acts as a visual map for your software environment, showing you exactly what is running and where things might be going wrong. It automatically highlights errors and tracks changes over time so you don't have to search through complex text files. You can also connect your favorite AI assistant to the tool to help you investigate and resolve technical incidents faster.

### Who it's for
* **System Administrators** who need a quick, visual overview of cluster health without digging through command-line logs.
* **Software Developers** who want to understand how their applications are performing and why they might be failing in a live environment.

### Key features
* **Visual Topology:** See how your services connect to each other in a clear, interactive map.
* **Issue Tracking:** A ranked list of what is currently broken, allowing you to jump straight to the source of a problem.
* **Timeline View:** A history of changes and events, making it easy to see exactly what happened before an error occurred.
* **AI Integration:** Built-in support for AI agents to help you diagnose and troubleshoot complex issues automatically.
* **Private and Secure:** Data stays on your machine, ensuring your cluster information remains private.
