### What it does in plain English
OpenHaystack lets you turn everyday Bluetooth devices into personal trackers that work with Apple's global device-finding network. By installing this software on your Mac, you can create custom tags to keep tabs on your belongings like keys, bags, or other gadgets. It uses the same technology that helps people find lost iPhones, allowing you to see the location of your tagged items on a map.

### Who it's for
* Hobbyists and makers who want to experiment with Bluetooth hardware and tracking technology.
* People who want to add tracking capabilities to their own custom devices or projects without buying commercial trackers.

### Key features
* **Custom Tag Creation:** Turn devices like the BBC micro:bit or ESP32 into functional tracking beacons.
* **Global Network Access:** Leverages the massive network of nearby iPhones to report the location of your items.
* **Privacy-Focused:** Uses public-key encryption so that only you can see the location of your specific devices.
* **Visual Mapping:** View the last known location of your tagged items directly within the desktop application.
