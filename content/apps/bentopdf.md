### What it does in plain English
BentoPDF gives you a complete suite of document tools that run entirely on your own device. Because everything happens locally in your web browser, your sensitive files remain completely private and secure. You can easily combine pages, convert documents, add signatures, or adjust layouts without installing complex software or creating an account.

### Who it's for
* Everyday users who need to quickly sign, merge, or convert personal documents without using shady online web tools.
* Privacy-conscious professionals who handle confidential contracts and cannot risk uploading files to third-party cloud servers.
* Students and researchers looking for a free, reliable way to extract text, manage pages, or convert e-books.

### Key features
* Complete local execution ensuring 100% data privacy with zero server uploads.
* Dozens of built-in utilities to merge, split, rotate, and rearrange document pages.
* Support for converting documents, images, and e-books into standard formats.
* Integrated digital signature and form creation capabilities.
* Completely free and open-source project with optional self-hosting support.

### How to install (Download)
You can use the official web application directly online, or host your own private instance using Docker or static web hosting.
* [Open Official Web Application](https://bentopdf.com/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely private client-side processing, no file upload limits, free and open-source.
* **Cons:** Heavy document conversions may require a modern web browser and adequate device memory.
