### What it does in plain English
This application gives you a friendly desktop window to download movies, series, regular videos, and courses from Bilibili. It automatically gathers subtitles, covers, and details so your downloaded files look clean and organized. You can also fetch community comments and format them nicely for your personal video collection.

### Who it's for
* Media enthusiasts who want to archive their favorite online videos and shows locally.
* People who organize their personal entertainment libraries using home server software.
* Viewers who want to save offline videos along with high-definition subtitles and covers.

### Key features
* Modern and clean desktop interface that runs smoothly on multiple operating systems.
* High-speed segment downloads that maximize your internet connection.
* Automatic subtitle downloading and conversion of comments into readable text formats.
* Metadata organization files that help home media software recognize your videos instantly.
* Built-in tools for managing download queues, pausing tasks, and custom file naming.
