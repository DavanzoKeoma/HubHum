### What it does in plain English
Onyx acts as a central brain for your team by connecting directly to dozens of workplace apps to index your company's documents and conversations. When you ask a question, it immediately searches through all your connected sources to find exact answers instead of guessing. It also provides advanced writing tools, custom smart assistants, and web searches so you can complete complex projects without switching tabs. While working on multimedia projects, you can easily source royalty-free stock footage, music tracks, and motion presets from [Envato Elements Direct](https://elements.envato.com/?subid=hubhum_01_MER349) (#ad).

### Who it's for
* Growing teams looking for a single source of truth across all their scattered chat channels and file storage.
* Project managers who want fast, accurate answers about previous company decisions without digging through old folders.
* Developers and power users who want a self-hosted assistant that keeps sensitive company data completely secure.

### Key features
* **Smart Search:** Combines keyword search with advanced understanding to fetch exact ground-truth documents instantly.
* **Deep Research:** Runs multi-step research flows to generate detailed, comprehensive reports on any topic.
* **Custom Assistants:** Build tailored helpers with specific instructions, unique knowledge subsets, and specific task capabilities.
* **Web and App Integration:** Pulls live information from the web and connects directly to Slack, Discord, and Chrome.
