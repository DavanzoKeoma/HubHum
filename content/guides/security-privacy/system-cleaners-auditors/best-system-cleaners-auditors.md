## Quick Answer: Sherlock vs Mole

Comparing **Sherlock** and **Mole** comes down to how you prefer to work. **Sherlock** stands out as a top community favorite (114,779 GitHub stars) and runs as a **Desktop App**: Easily discover where a specific username is registered across hundreds of online platforms and social networks in seconds. Meanwhile, **Mole** (75,661 GitHub stars) gives you another trusted free option: Mole is an open-source system cleaner that removes junk files, uninstalls applications completely, and monitors system performance.
