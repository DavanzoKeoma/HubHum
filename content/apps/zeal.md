### What it does in plain English
Zeal acts as a personal, searchable library for technical manuals and documentation. Because it works entirely offline, you can look up information instantly without waiting for web pages to load. It is designed to keep your reference materials organized and accessible whenever you need them, regardless of your internet status.

### Who it's for
* Software developers and students who need quick access to coding references while working offline.
* Technical writers or hobbyists who want to keep large documentation sets organized in one lightweight, searchable place.

### Key features
* **Offline Access:** Keep your entire library of documentation on your computer so you can work anywhere.
* **Instant Search:** Find exactly what you need across multiple documentation sets with a single, fast search bar.
* **Customizable Library:** Easily download and manage only the specific documentation sets that are relevant to your projects.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works consistently across different operating systems, providing a unified experience for all your devices.
