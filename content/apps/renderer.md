### What it does in plain English
This application is a specialized viewer designed to display 3D objects with advanced visual effects. It uses realistic lighting techniques to show how surfaces look under different conditions, such as metallic or rough textures. You can rotate, zoom, and inspect these models to see fine details and smooth animations.

### Who it's for
* Digital artists and 3D designers who need to preview their models.
* Students or enthusiasts interested in learning how 3D graphics are rendered on a computer.

### Key features
* **Realistic Lighting:** Supports advanced light reflection models to make materials look authentic.
* **Interactive Controls:** Easily rotate, pan, and zoom around objects using your mouse.
* **Material Inspection:** Includes a built-in tool to examine the specific properties of 3D materials.
* **Smooth Animation:** Handles complex skeletal movements for lifelike character motion.
* **Cross-Platform:** Works reliably on Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.

### How to install (Download)
You can download the pre-built versions for your operating system directly from the official [Releases page](https://github.com/zauonlok/renderer/releases).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free, lightweight, and does not require a subscription or heavy software installation.
* **Cons:** Primarily controlled via command line for specific scenes, which may feel unfamiliar to some users.
