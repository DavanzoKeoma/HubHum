### What it does in plain English
Nebula helps television and radio stations organize their video and audio files in one central library. It automatically prepares these files for broadcast by fixing technical issues like volume levels and screen sizes. It also schedules and controls the actual airing of programs, ensuring your content plays out on time without manual intervention.

### Who it's for
*   **Broadcast Engineers:** Professionals who need a reliable system to manage daily TV or radio schedules and ensure smooth playback.
*   **Media Producers:** Teams that need to catalog large libraries of video and audio assets, making them easy to search and reuse.
*   **Content Managers:** Individuals responsible for publishing finished programs to websites or social media channels after broadcast.

### Key features
*   **Smart Media Library:** A fast search engine that organizes your video and audio files with detailed notes on rights, genre, and technical specs.
*   **Automatic File Prep:** Automatically fixes audio loudness and video resolution issues so your content meets broadcast standards before it airs.
*   **Drag-and-Drop Scheduling:** Create daily or weekly broadcast schedules easily by dragging and dropping clips into a timeline, including live events.
*   **Live Broadcast Control:** Allows operators to make last-minute changes to the program order or trigger graphics during a live broadcast.
*   **Automatic Publishing:** Automatically uploads finished programs to your website or social media accounts once the broadcast is complete.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, proven reliability in 24/7 broadcast environments since 2012, and includes powerful tools for both media management and live scheduling.
*   **Cons:** Requires technical knowledge to set up on a server, and the interface is designed for professional broadcast workflows rather than casual home use.
