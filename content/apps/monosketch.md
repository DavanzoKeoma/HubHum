### What it does in plain English
MonoSketch lets you draw technical diagrams, circuit layouts, and flowcharts using simple text characters and symbols. Everything happens right inside your web browser without sending any of your personal creations to external cloud servers. Your work remains completely private on your device while you easily design, adjust, and copy your text creations.

### Who it's for
* Software developers sketching quick architecture notes for documentation files.
* System engineers mapping out network connections and electronic circuits using text.
* Creative writers and hobbyists designing text-based art and structured notes.

### Key features
* **Browser-based drawing:** Works immediately inside your web browser without requiring complex software setups.
* **Infinite canvas:** Provides unlimited scrolling space in all directions so your large diagrams never run out of room.
* **Smart alignment tools:** Connects lines automatically to shapes and handles borders, fills, and rounded corners smoothly.
* **Instant text export:** Copy your finished diagrams directly to your clipboard using handy keyboard shortcuts.
* **Private and secure:** Operates entirely on your client device so your data stays local.

### How to install (Download)
MonoSketch runs completely online as a web application. No installation is required.
* **Launch the web application:** [Try MonoSketch Online](https://app.monosketch.io/)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use, requires no installation, keeps all data private on your device.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern web browser and active internet connection to load initially.
