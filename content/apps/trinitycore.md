### What it does in plain English
TrinityCore lets you run your own private version of a popular online role‑playing game.  
You decide what the world looks like, what rules apply, and who gets to join.  
It gives hobbyists a way to experiment with game ideas without needing a big company.

### Who it's for
* Friends who want a private game space just for them.  
* Hobby developers who want to test new game ideas.  
* Teachers or clubs that need a sandbox for learning about online worlds.

### Key features
* Create custom maps and quests.  
* Set your own rules for character levels and items.  
* Invite friends or open the server to the public.  
* Monitor player activity with simple tools.  
* Receive free updates and improvements from the community.
