### What it does in plain English
SpleeterGui is a user-friendly tool that takes a standard music file and automatically separates it into individual components like vocals, drums, and bass. It removes the need for complex technical knowledge by providing a simple window where you can just drop your audio files to process them. This makes it perfect for anyone wanting to create backing tracks, remix songs, or isolate specific instruments from a recording.

### Who it's for
* Musicians and DJs who need to create custom backing tracks or isolate vocals for remixes.
* Music enthusiasts who want to study specific instruments or vocal performances within their favorite songs.

### Key features
* **Drag-and-Drop Interface:** Simply pull your music files into the app to start the separation process instantly.
* **All-in-One Package:** Includes everything you need to run the tool without having to install extra programming software.
* **Batch Processing:** Save time by processing multiple audio files at once rather than one by one.
* **High-Quality Output:** Uses advanced technology to ensure the separated audio tracks remain clear and usable for your projects.
