### What it does in plain English
optimizerDuck is a lightweight utility that cleans up background clutter and fine-tunes hidden settings inside your operating system. Instead of forcing you to navigate complex system menus or edit system files manually, it organizes powerful performance tweaks into a single friendly dashboard. It also includes handy tools to remove pre-installed apps, clean up leftover junk files, and automatically back up your changes so you can always undo them if needed.

### Who it's for
* PC gamers wanting to reduce system latency and free up valuable memory resources
* Users setting up a fresh computer who want to remove unwanted manufacturer apps
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to disable unnecessary background telemetry and data sharing

### Key features
* Apply dozens of carefully researched tweaks to boost gaming responsiveness and overall system speed
* Stop unwanted background tracking, advertising identifiers, and unnecessary telemetry data collection
* Clean up system junk, temporary cache files, and forgotten update leftovers to reclaim disk space
* Manage programs that launch automatically when you turn on your computer to speed up boot times
* Built-in automatic backup system that lets you undo any applied change with a single click
