### What it does in plain English
PI-Desktop gives artificial intelligence coding assistants a permanent home on your computer instead of trapping them inside a browser tab or code editor. You can connect your preferred cloud or local intelligence models, organize different projects into persistent sessions, and add custom plugins to fit your workflow. It keeps all your data safely stored on your machine while letting you delegate complex coding tasks to multiple helper agents at once.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want to manage multiple AI coding assistants across different local projects.
* Technical hobbyists looking to experiment with both cloud and local artificial intelligence models.
* Creators and engineers who need a customizable desktop workspace to handle automated coding workflows.

### Key features
* **Model freedom:** Switch easily between cloud intelligence services and private local models without losing your project setup.
* **Persistent sessions:** Keep track of past work across app restarts instead of dealing with disposable chat windows.
* **Task delegation:** Break large projects down by assigning subtasks to background helper agents.
* **Plugin marketplace:** Install custom tools, user interfaces, and background services to tailor the environment to your needs.
* **Local-first privacy:** Keep all your project files, history, and settings safely stored right on your own computer.
