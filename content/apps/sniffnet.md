### What it does in plain English
Sniffnet helps you keep a close eye on everything happening across your internet connection with zero hassle. It displays clear, live charts and details showing exactly which programs, websites, and devices are using your bandwidth. You can even set up custom alerts to notify you of unexpected network activity or potential security risks.

### Who it's for
* Everyday internet users curious about which apps secretly connect to the web in the background.
* Remote workers and gamers wanting to track bandwidth usage and spot unexpected lag sources.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking to discover the geographical location and domain names of remote hosts they connect with.

### Key features
* Inspect network adapter activity and filter observed traffic effortlessly.
* View real-time charts of internet traffic intensity and overall bandwidth statistics.
* Discover the geographical location, domain name, and corporate owner of remote hosts.
* Set custom notifications for specific network events and import dangerous connection blocklists.
