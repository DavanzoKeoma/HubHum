### What it does in plain English
Shattered Pixel Dungeon is a captivating strategy game where you explore ever-changing underground maps filled with tricky monsters and hidden treasures. Every time you play, the layout, items, and enemies are completely randomized, ensuring a fresh challenge on every run. You must carefully manage your inventory, choose your character upgrades wisely, and outsmart dangerous foes to survive.

### Who it's for
* Strategy fans who love challenging tactical gameplay and deep resource management.
* Casual gamers looking for a portable, highly replayable adventure experience.
* Anyone who enjoys classic dungeon-crawling adventures with modern quality-of-life updates.

### Key features
* Procedurally generated levels ensure no two dungeon runs are ever alike.
* Hundreds of unique weapons, armor pieces, potions, and scrolls to discover and utilize.
* Multiple distinct character classes with unique playstyles and abilities.
* Balanced difficulty progression that rewards tactical thinking and careful planning.
* Completely open-source design focused on fair gameplay without aggressive monetization.
