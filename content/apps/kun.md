### What it does in plain English
Kun is a private desktop application that brings together coding, document editing, and research tools in a single workspace. It connects directly to your preferred intelligence models while keeping your conversations and personal settings safely on your computer. You can chat with specialized assistants, organize files, and run multi-step tasks without sending your private information to the cloud.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who want a local coding environment integrated with capable AI assistants.
* Researchers and writers who need to organize documents, papers, and notes alongside active chat threads.
* Privacy-conscious users looking for an all-in-one desktop assistant that runs securely on local hardware.

### Key features
* Combines a full code editor, terminal, and design workspace into one cohesive view.
* Manages dedicated libraries for reading, note-taking, and analyzing documents or academic papers.
* Supports private chats with multiple specialized digital assistants that collaborate on complex tasks.
* Operates locally on your device, ensuring your logs, history, and preferences stay on your machine.
* Compatible with major cloud intelligence providers as well as completely offline local models.
