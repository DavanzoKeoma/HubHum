### What it does in plain English
Cockpit acts as a bridge between your website and your content, letting you manage text and images through a simple dashboard. It is designed to be lightweight, meaning it doesn't force a specific look or structure on your site. You can plug it into your existing project to start organizing your data immediately.

### Who it's for
* Web designers who want to give their clients an easy way to update site content.
* Developers building custom websites who need a simple way to store and retrieve information.

### Key features
* **Lightweight Design:** Keeps your website fast by only including the tools you actually need.
* **Flexible Integration:** Works with any website design, whether it is a simple landing page or a complex site.
* **User-Friendly Dashboard:** Provides a clean interface for non-technical users to edit text and media.
* **Data Portability:** Keeps your content organized in a way that is easy to move or back up.

### How to install (Download)
You can download the latest version and find setup instructions on the [Official Cockpit Website](https://getcockpit.com).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, highly flexible for custom designs, and very lightweight compared to traditional publishing platforms.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of how to upload files to a web server and ensuring your server meets the minimum requirements.
