### What it does in plain English
Karabiner-Elements is a versatile utility designed for Mac users who want total control over their typing experience. It allows you to change what individual keys do, swap key functions around, and set up custom shortcuts for repetitive typing tasks. The application runs quietly in the background, applying your personalized rules instantly across all apps.

### Who it's for
* Power users who want to optimize their workflow and speed up daily typing tasks.
* Users switching between different operating systems who want their Mac keyboard shortcuts to match familiar habits.
* Anyone looking to fix broken physical keys or adapt specialized keyboards for Mac usage.

### Key features
* Complete control to remap any key or mouse button on your keyboard.
* Support for creating complex, multi-step macros and custom shortcuts.
* Profile management to switch between different keyboard setups depending on what you are doing.
* Lightweight background service that runs smoothly without slowing down your computer.
* Compatible with both modern Apple Silicon chips and older Intel-based Mac computers.
