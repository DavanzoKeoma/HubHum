### What it does in plain English
This open educational resource provides a complete guide to handling data tasks directly inside your computer terminal. It shows you how to chain simple, powerful text-processing commands together to process information faster without opening heavy spreadsheet or analysis software. You get free access to the full book text, practical scripts, and real-world datasets to sharpen your everyday analytical skills.

### Who it's for
* Students and professionals wanting to speed up everyday data chores.
* Developers looking to leverage command-line tools for quick data analysis.
* Anyone curious about efficient data processing using standard computer terminals.

### Key features
* Complete online access to the second edition of the book.
* Practical examples and scripts for everyday data workflows.
* Real-world datasets to practice cleaning, exploring, and modeling.
* Creative Commons license allowing free reading and sharing.

### How to install (Download)
You can read the complete book online and access all accompanying scripts and materials directly at the [Official Website](https://datascienceatthecommandline.com).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free forever, highly practical, teaches reusable command-line skills.
* **Cons:** Requires familiarity with basic terminal usage.
