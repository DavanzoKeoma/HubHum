### What it does in plain English
Skales is a personal artificial intelligence assistant that runs directly on your computer rather than relying entirely on remote cloud servers. It connects safely to your files, calendar, email, and web browser to perform multi-step tasks automatically on your behalf. You can chat with it naturally, have it write or edit code, or let it run background tasks while you focus on other work.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and small business owners looking for a private AI helper to manage routine workflows.
* Everyday computer users who want an easy way to organize files, draft documents, and automate tasks.
* Developers and power users who want an integrated coding assistant with local privacy controls.

### Key features
* **Local-First Privacy:** Runs natively on your machine with flexible support for local intelligence models or your own provider keys.
* **Background Tasks:** Hand over a goal and let the assistant work through multi-step processes autonomously.
* **Coding Assistant:** Connects directly to local project folders for inline edits, reviews, and git tracking.
* **Cross-Device Support:** Companion mobile apps and syncing options let you take your assistant on the go.
* **No Complex Setup:** Installs just like a normal desktop application without requiring technical tools or containers.
