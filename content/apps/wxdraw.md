### What it does in plain English
wxDraw solves the problem of making moving graphics in WeChat mini programs, which are usually difficult to build from scratch. It lets you draw shapes like circles, lines, and text, then make them move, rotate, or change color smoothly. You can also let users tap or drag these shapes to interact with them directly on the screen.

### Who it's for
*   Developers building interactive games or visual tools inside WeChat mini programs.
*   Designers who want to add simple, smooth animations to their mobile apps without learning complex graphics code.
*   Teams looking for a lightweight way to handle user interactions with on-screen elements in a mobile environment.

### Key features
*   **Simple Shape Creation:** Easily draw rectangles, circles, polygons, lines, and text with just a few lines of code.
*   **Smooth Animations:** Move, rotate, and change the color of shapes with built-in timing controls that feel natural.
*   **Touch Interaction:** Detect when users tap, touch, or drag specific shapes on the screen to trigger actions.
*   **Lightweight Design:** The tool is small and fast, ensuring it does not slow down your mini program.
*   **Flexible Styling:** Add shadows, gradients, and custom colors to make your graphics look polished.

*   **Cons:** Specifically designed for WeChat mini programs, so it cannot be used in standard web browsers or other mobile platforms.
