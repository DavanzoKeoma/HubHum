### What it does in plain English
SparkyFitness is a private home for all your health data, from daily meals and workouts to sleep patterns and body measurements. Unlike popular apps that sell your information or lock features behind expensive subscriptions, this tool keeps your data on your own equipment. It allows families to track their progress together in one simple, organized dashboard.

### Who it's for
*   Individuals who want to take full ownership of their personal health and fitness data.
*   Families looking for a shared, ad-free way to encourage healthy habits together.

### Key features
*   **All-in-One Tracking:** Log nutrition, exercise, hydration, sleep, and body stats in a single interface.
*   **Privacy First:** You own your data, and it stays on your own server rather than being sent to third-party companies.
*   **Family Access:** Create multiple user profiles so everyone in the household can manage their own health goals.
*   **Smart Syncing:** Connects with common wearable devices and health platforms to automatically pull in your activity data.
*   **AI Assistant:** Use the optional chat feature to log your meals or ask questions about your progress.
