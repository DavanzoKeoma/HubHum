### What it does in plain English
CheckCle acts as a digital watchdog for your online projects, constantly checking if your websites and servers are running smoothly. It alerts you immediately if something goes offline or slows down, helping you fix issues before they affect your visitors. You can manage everything from a single dashboard, giving you full control over the health of your technology stack.

### Who it's for
*   **Small Business Owners:** People who need to know instantly if their online shop or company website stops working.
*   **System Administrators:** Professionals who want a simple way to keep an eye on multiple servers and services in one place.

### Key features
*   **Uptime Tracking:** Automatically checks your websites and services to ensure they are always reachable.
*   **Performance Metrics:** Monitors key health indicators like processor usage, memory, and storage space on your servers.
*   **Instant Alerts:** Sends notifications through popular messaging apps and email the moment a problem is detected.
*   **Public Status Pages:** Allows you to share the current health of your services with your users or team.
*   **SSL Monitoring:** Keeps track of your security certificates to ensure they never expire unexpectedly.

### How to install (Download)
CheckCle is designed to be run using Docker. You can find the full setup instructions and the quick-start guide at the official documentation page: [CheckCle Quick Start Guide](https://docs.checkcle.io).

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, provides a comprehensive view of your infrastructure, and supports many notification channels.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of container software to set up, and you must host it on your own server or computer.
