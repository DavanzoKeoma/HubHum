### What it does in plain English
ArcReel helps you manage the entire process of creating short-form videos from written stories or scripts. It uses smart assistants to keep your characters, scenes, and props looking the same across every shot. You can track your production costs and export your final work into professional editing software for the finishing touches.

### Who it's for
* **Content Creators:** Individuals looking to produce consistent, episodic video series or short films without manual animation.
* **Marketing Teams:** Professionals who need to quickly generate video advertisements while maintaining strict brand and character consistency.

### Key features
* **Visual Consistency:** Keeps characters, props, and settings identical across different video clips.
* **Project Management:** Organizes your storyboards, assets, and video units in one central dashboard.
* **Cost Tracking:** Monitors your usage of various AI services so you can stay within your production budget.
* **Flexible Export:** Create finished videos or export your project as a draft for further refinement in popular video editing tools.

### How to install (Download)
ArcReel is designed to be run on your own hardware using Docker. To get started, visit the [Official Setup Guide](https://docs.arc-reel.com/guide/getting-started) for detailed instructions on how to initialize the workspace on your system.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete control over your data, maintains visual consistency across scenes, and provides clear cost tracking for AI services.
* **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of server software to set up and maintain, and relies on external AI providers for the actual video generation.
