### What it does in plain English
OpenEmu transforms your Mac into a universal home for classic video games from dozens of legendary retro systems. It organizes your entire digital collection into a neat, visual library that feels right at home on macOS. You can simply drag and drop your game files, plug in almost any standard game controller, and start playing instantly.

### Who it's for
* Nostalgic gamers wanting to replay childhood favorites from older home consoles and handhelds.
* Digital archivists and game preservationists organizing classic software libraries.
* Mac users looking for a clean, polished, and hassle-free way to enjoy retro gaming without complex setup menus.

### Key features
* Unified, beautiful game library manager that organizes your collection by console platform.
* Automatic box art lookup and game details for your entire downloaded library.
* Out-of-the-box support for dozens of popular USB and Bluetooth game controllers.
* Save states, screen capture, and customizable control mappings for every single system.
* Modular plugin architecture that integrates proven game engines under the hood.

### How to install (Download)
* [Download OpenEmu Official Website](https://openemu.org)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Gorgeous native macOS interface, supports dozens of different consoles, free and open source.
* **Cons:** Requires a modern Mac running macOS Mojave or newer, game files (ROMs) must be supplied separately by the user.
