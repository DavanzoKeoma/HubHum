### What it does in plain English
SpotX‑Bash stops any ads that try to play while you use Spotify on your computer.  
It can also hide podcasts, audiobooks and other non‑music items so the home screen stays clean.  
The tool works on macOS and most Linux versions without needing extra software.

### Who it's for
- Anyone who listens to Spotify on a Mac or Linux PC and wants an ad‑free experience.  
- Users who prefer not to see podcasts or audiobooks on the home screen.  
- People looking for a simple way to keep Spotify up‑to‑date without interruptions.

### Key features
- Stops all audio, banner and video ads in Spotify.  
- Hides podcasts, audiobooks and episodes from the home view.  
- One‑click command to install or update the Spotify client.  
- Option to block automatic updates on macOS.  
- Optional flags for premium‑account mode and other advanced controls.

### How to install (Download)
- Run the installer script directly from your browser: [Run SpotX‑Bash script](https://spotx-official.github.io/run.sh)  
- Browse the official release page for more information: [SpotX‑Bash releases](https://github.com/SpotX-Official/SpotX-Bash/releases)

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** free forever, works on macOS and Linux, no extra programs required, open‑source community support.  
* **Cons:** you need to run a command in the terminal, must re‑apply after Spotify updates, advanced options may need a quick read of the help text.
