### What it does in plain English
Haven turns any spare Android phone into a sophisticated monitoring device for your home or office. It uses the camera, microphone, and motion sensors to detect if someone enters your space or touches your belongings. When an event is triggered, it instantly sends you a secure notification so you know exactly what is happening while you are away.

### Who it's for
* People who want to keep an eye on their personal belongings or hotel rooms while traveling.
* Individuals who need a simple, private way to monitor their home environment without complex equipment.

### Key features
* **Multi-Sensor Detection:** Uses your phone's camera, microphone, light sensor, and motion detectors to spot intruders.
* **Private Storage:** All recorded logs and media are stored locally on your device, not on a remote server.
* **Secure Alerts:** Sends instant notifications directly to you via encrypted messaging services.
* **Remote Access:** Allows you to check your event logs from anywhere using secure, private network connections.
* **Power Monitoring:** Detects if your device is unplugged or if there is a sudden loss of power.
