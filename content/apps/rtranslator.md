### What it does in plain English
RTranslator is a mobile application that allows you to have smooth, real-time translated conversations with people speaking different languages. It runs entirely on your phone without requiring an active internet connection, protecting your personal privacy. You can connect two phones together or use a quick speaker mode to handle everyday chats effortlessly.

### Who it's for
* Travelers visiting foreign countries who need reliable offline translation.
* International professionals and students conversing with peers locally.
* Anyone seeking a privacy-focused translation tool that works without cloud tracking.

### Key features
* **Offline Conversation Mode:** Connect two devices to translate speech instantly back and forth without internet.
* **Walkie-Talkie Mode:** Use a single phone to listen to two languages simultaneously for quick questions.
* **Complete Privacy:** Processes all speech recognition and translation directly on your device hardware.
* **Bluetooth Headset Support:** Listen to translated audio directly through wireless earpieces discreetly.
* **Wide Language Support:** Compatible with dozens of popular languages plus low-quality fallback options.
