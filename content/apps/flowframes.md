### What it does in plain English
Flowframes is a friendly Windows application that takes choppy or low-framerate videos and makes them wonderfully smooth. It uses artificial intelligence to invent and insert brand-new frames in between your existing footage. Whether you are working with old home movies, animated cartoons, or gaming clips, it handles the complex math behind the scenes so you get professional-looking results.

### Who it's for
* Content creators looking to upscale their video framerates for a cinematic look.
* 2D animators and editors wanting to smooth out frame rates without manual redrawing.
* Everyday users who want to make their personal video recordings look fluid and natural.

### Key features
* Supports multiple artificial intelligence engines for maximum hardware compatibility.
* Works seamlessly with modern graphics cards from both major hardware manufacturers.
* Smart duplicate frame removal specifically designed for clean 2D animations.
* Preserves your original audio tracks, subtitles, and metadata automatically.
* Encodes your final video while processing to save valuable time and disk space.
