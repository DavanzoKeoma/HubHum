### What it does in plain English
This tool gives you a clean visual task board to organize your daily projects and work items right on your computer. Instead of packing massive background engines inside, it uses your operating system's built-in viewing windows. This means it opens instantly, runs smoothly, and barely touches your computer's memory or battery.

### Who it's for
* Freelancers and remote workers who want a distraction-free task tracker without slowing down their computers.
* Privacy-minded individuals looking for lean, lightweight desktop tools that respect system resources.

### Key features
* Extremely small download size compared to typical web-based desktop applications.
* Uses almost zero computer processing power while running in the background.
* Clean visual task layout for organizing projects into customizable columns.
* Native look and feel that blends naturally into your operating system environment.
