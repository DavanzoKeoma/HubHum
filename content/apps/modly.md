### What it does in plain English
Modly is a friendly desktop application that lets you convert ordinary photographs into fully realized 3D objects. It runs entirely on your own computer hardware using your graphics card, keeping your personal creations completely private. You can easily connect different creative steps together in a visual workspace to generate, refine, and smooth your 3D models without needing an internet connection.

### Who it's for
* 3D artists and game designers looking to quickly turn concept sketches or photos into starting meshes.
* Hobbyists and makers eager to experiment with local artificial intelligence tools without complicated setup steps.
* Content creators who want privacy-friendly 3D generation running securely on their own machine.

### Key features
* Generates detailed 3D models straight from single photographs or text prompts using your graphics card.
* Runs completely offline to protect your privacy and ensure your work stays on your device.
* Features a flexible visual workflow builder to connect different processing and generation steps.
* Includes built-in mesh tools to smooth and optimize your finished creations right inside the app.
* Supports community extensions and downloadable model packs to expand your creative options.
