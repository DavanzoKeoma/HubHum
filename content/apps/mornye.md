### What it does in plain English
Mornye is a mobile music utility that brings your personal song collection, downloaded tracks, and network storage together into a single streamlined app. It features built-in high-resolution audio playback, synced lyrics, and customizable themes while remaining completely free and open source. You can organize your music folders, fine-tune your listening experience with an equalizer, and enjoy your tunes without any interruptions from advertisements or paid subscriptions.

### Who it's for
* Audiophiles looking for high-resolution bit-perfect music playback on mobile devices.
* Music lovers who want to organize local files, downloaded tracks, and home server music libraries in one place.
* Privacy-conscious listeners who prefer an open-source player with zero tracking, ads, or hidden subscriptions.

### Key features
* **High-Resolution Output:** Supports direct USB DAC connections and specialized audio modes for pristine sound quality.
* **Local and Network Library:** Scan device storage or stream directly from home servers using various network protocols.
* **Synced Lyrics:** View word-by-word timed lyrics with translation support and online search options.
* **Multiband Equalizer:** Customize your sound profile with built-in presets and loudness normalization controls.
* **Completely Free:** No subscription fees, no locked features, and zero annoying advertisements.
