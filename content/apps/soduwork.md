### What it does in plain English
SoduWork acts as a central hub where people and AI agents work together to complete tasks. You define exactly what needs to be done, and the system uses AI to perform the work and verify that it meets your specific requirements. Once a task is completed successfully, you can save the process as a reusable template so your entire team can benefit from it in the future.

### Who it's for
* **Team Leads** who want to standardize how their staff uses AI to ensure consistent, high-quality results.
* **Individual Professionals** who want to create a personal library of reliable AI workflows that they can reuse for recurring projects.

### Key features
* **Verified Delivery:** Every task includes clear success criteria, and a secondary AI review ensures the work is accurate before you sign off.
* **Shared Knowledge:** Successful AI interactions can be saved as plugins, allowing team members to share and improve upon proven methods.
* **Flexible AI Integration:** You can switch between different AI models and tools mid-conversation to find the best fit for each specific step of your project.
* **Full Accountability:** The system maintains a complete record of who requested a task, which agent performed the work, and who provided the final approval.
