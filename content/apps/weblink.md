### What it does in plain English
Weblink lets you connect your devices directly to chat, share files, make video calls, and control your computer remotely. Everything flows peer-to-peer over your network for maximum speed and privacy. You can easily access an authorized desktop right from a standard web browser or desktop app.

### Who it's for
* Remote workers who need secure, direct file transfer and desktop access between different computers.
* Privacy-conscious individuals looking for a self-hosted alternative to mainstream messaging and remote control tools.
* Teams and individuals wanting a quick way to share screens and collaborate without corporate tracking.

### Key features
* **Direct peer-to-peer connection:** Exchange messages, media, and files directly between devices without intermediary cloud storage.
* **Remote desktop control:** Operate an authorized computer safely using any phone or computer browser.
* **Meetings and recording:** Voice calls, video chats, screen sharing, and local source recording.
* **Resumable file transfers:** Send large files with local library management and shared file lists.
* **Self-hosting ready:** Connect clients to your own signaling backend for complete data independence.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source under the MIT license, direct peer-to-peer privacy, multiple communication tools in one app.
* **Cons:** Remote control requires manual device pairing and explicit authorization permissions.
