### What it does in plain English
The Most Dangerous Writing App is designed to cure writer's block by removing your safety net entirely. If you pause your typing for longer than five seconds, the screen fades to blur and all your progress vanishes. By forcing you to push past self-doubt and continuous editing, it helps you reach a pure state of creative flow.

### Who it's for
* Creative writers and novelists looking to break through severe writer's block
* Bloggers and students needing to push through rough drafts without overthinking
* Anyone wanting a high-stakes, adrenaline-fueled challenge for their daily journaling

### Key features
* Unforgiving five-second pause timer that instantly wipes unsaved text
* Distraction-free minimalist interface to keep your eyes glued to the words
* Customizable session lengths and word count goals to fit your schedule
* Web-based access that requires zero installation or complicated configuration

### How to install (Download)
You can use this application directly in your web browser without downloading any files. Visit the [The Most Dangerous Writing App Web Page](https://maebert.github.io/themostdangerouswritingapp) to start writing right away.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free to use, no account required, highly effective at stopping procrastination
* **Cons:** Terrifying if you lose your train of thought, zero recovery options once text is deleted
