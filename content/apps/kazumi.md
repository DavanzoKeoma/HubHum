### What it does in plain English
Kazumi is a flexible media companion that helps you gather and stream your favorite animated shows using customized rule sets. It connects to multiple video sources so you can watch episodes smoothly while enjoying synchronized community comments. The app also features picture enhancement tools that upscale low-resolution videos directly on your device.

### Who it's for
* Anime enthusiasts who want to organize and watch their favorite series across multiple devices.
* Viewers looking for an ad-free interface with community comment integration.
* Users who want advanced visual upscaling to improve older or low-resolution video feeds.

### Key features
* Customizable rule importer to gather video sources and episode directories.
* Integrated video player with variable playback speed and hardware acceleration.
* On-screen community comments and chronological show schedules.
* Real-time video quality enhancement for clearer playback.
* Synchronized history and watch lists across supported devices.
