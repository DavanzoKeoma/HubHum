### What it does in plain English
Komi Store acts as a centralized hub for finding and downloading free applications hosted on developer platforms like GitHub and Codeberg. It lets you search through active projects, check recent releases, and manage your software installations without visiting multiple websites. The app handles downloads smoothly, providing a streamlined way to keep your favorite tools up to date.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users looking for free, privacy-friendly alternative applications
* Open-source enthusiasts who want an easier way to discover new software projects
* People who manage multiple tools across different development platforms

### Key features
* Smart discovery system to find popular and trending community projects
* Direct release browsing and one-click software installation
* Built-in download mirrors for reliable file retrieval
* Custom collections and personalized app management tools
* Multi-language support across fourteen different languages
