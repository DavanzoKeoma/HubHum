### What it does in plain English
Timber is a clean and lightweight music player designed for your Android device that organizes your personal audio files effortlessly. It lets you browse your song library by albums, artists, or folders, while offering custom dark themes and multiple player layouts to match your aesthetic. You can easily manage playlists, view synced lyrics, and control your listening experience using home screen widgets or handy gesture shortcuts.

### Who it's for
* Music enthusiasts who keep local audio files stored directly on their mobile device.
* Android users looking for a clean, distraction-free interface without crowded streaming menus.
* Anyone wanting a lightweight player that supports desktop widgets and car dashboard integrations.

### Key features
* Organizes your entire library by songs, albums, artists, and device folders.
* Features six different playback screen styles to customize your look.
* Includes a built-in dark theme and custom accent colors.
* Supports swipe gestures for quick track switching.
* Integrates with Android Auto, smartwatches, and Chromecast devices.
