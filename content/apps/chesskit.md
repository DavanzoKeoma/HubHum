### What it does in plain English
Chesskit is a comprehensive web-based platform designed for chess enthusiasts to play, review, and analyze their games. It allows you to import matches from popular chess sites and provides professional-grade tools like engine evaluations and move classification. You can practice against a computer at various skill levels or solve puzzles to improve your game.

### Who it's for
* Casual players who want a clean, ad-free space to play and review their matches.
* Students and competitive players looking to analyze their performance with engine feedback.

### Key features
* **Game Analysis:** Review your moves with live engine evaluation and visual graphs.
* **Broad Compatibility:** Easily import and analyze games from major chess platforms.
* **Smart Classification:** Automatically labels your moves as brilliant, great, mistakes, or blunders.
* **Versatile Play:** Includes support for Chess960, puzzles, and matches against a computer.
* **Local Storage:** Keeps your game history saved directly within your browser.

### How to install (Download)
Chesskit is a web-based application and does not require installation. You can access it directly in your browser by visiting: [https://chesskit.org](https://chesskit.org).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, works on any device, no account setup required for basic features.
* **Cons:** Requires an internet connection to access the web interface, advanced features are best viewed on larger screens.
