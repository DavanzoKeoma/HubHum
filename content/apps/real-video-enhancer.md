### What it does in plain English
REAL Video Enhancer is a user-friendly application designed to upgrade your video collection. It lets you increase video smoothness by generating missing frames, enlarge resolution without losing clarity, and clean up visual noise or compression artifacts. Whether you want to revive old home movies or make modern clips look sharper, this tool handles the complex processing behind the scenes.

### Who it's for
* Video enthusiasts looking to modernize older or low-resolution footage.
* Content creators who want to increase frame rates for smoother playback.
* Everyday users who want a straightforward way to clean up compressed or grainy videos.

### Key features
* **Frame Interpolation:** Add extra frames to make choppy videos play back smoothly.
* **Smart Upscaling:** Increase video resolution while preserving fine details and sharpness.
* **Denoising and Decompression:** Clean up visual grain, compression artifacts, and digital noise.
* **Scene Change Detection:** Automatically recognize cuts to ensure transitions stay sharp and accurate.
* **Hardware Acceleration:** Leverage your computer's graphics card for fast, efficient rendering.
