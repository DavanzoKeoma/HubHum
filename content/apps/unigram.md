### What it does in plain English
Unigram is a dedicated messaging application built specifically for Windows 10 and 11. It allows you to chat, send files, and join groups while feeling like a natural part of your computer's interface. Because it is built for the system, it runs smoothly and integrates perfectly with your desktop notifications and settings.

### Who it's for
* Windows users who want a high-performance messaging app that doesn't rely on a web browser.
* People who manage multiple Telegram accounts and need a reliable, native way to switch between them.

### Key features
* **Native Windows Design:** Matches the look and feel of Windows 10 and 11 with support for light and dark themes.
* **High Performance:** Runs as a standalone program, ensuring it is faster and more responsive than web-based alternatives.
* **Full Feature Set:** Supports all standard messaging tools, including secret chats, voice and video calls, and file sharing.
* **Multi-Account Support:** Easily add and manage several different Telegram accounts within one window.
* **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Includes a wide range of hotkeys to help you navigate your messages and settings quickly.

* **Pros:** Completely free, native performance, no web browser overhead, and excellent system integration.
* **Cons:** Requires the Media Feature Pack if you are using the "N" edition of Windows 10.
