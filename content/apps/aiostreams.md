### What it does in plain English
AIOStreams acts as a central manager for your various streaming services, combining them into one clean, easy-to-navigate library. Instead of checking multiple apps to find what you want to watch, you set your viewing rules once and let the app handle the rest. It automatically removes duplicates, filters out low-quality results, and presents your media exactly how you like it across all your devices.

### Who it's for
*   **Media enthusiasts** who use multiple streaming addons and services and want a single, unified interface.
*   **Home theater users** who want a professional, customized viewing experience with advanced sorting and filtering options.

### Key features
*   **Unified Library:** Merges content from dozens of community addons, Usenet indexers, and debrid services into one list.
*   **Custom Rules:** Define your own filters for resolution, quality, and language so you only see the streams you actually want.
*   **Smart Sorting:** Automatically ranks results based on your preferences, such as prioritizing cached files or specific file sizes.
*   **Cross-Device Support:** Watch on your computer, phone, or TV using the dedicated app, or integrate it directly into your existing media player setup.
*   **Advanced Formatting:** Rename and organize your streams with a live preview tool to ensure your library looks exactly as you intend.

### How to install (Download)
*   **Get the App:** Visit the [Official AIOStreams Download Page](https://aiostreams.org/download) to find installers for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and Smart TVs.
*   **Public Instance:** If you prefer not to host it yourself, you can use a [Public Instance](https://aiostreams.org/getting-started/public-instances) to get started immediately.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely consolidates fragmented streaming sources, highly customizable rules, and works on almost any screen.
*   **Cons:** Advanced configuration options may require a short learning curve for new users, and self-hosting requires basic setup steps.
