### What it does in plain English
Mouzi is a silent background application that watches your computer's folders and organizes incoming files according to custom rules. It automatically moves photos, documents, and installers into designated subfolders the moment they arrive. You can manage everything through a discreet system tray menu and easily undo any file movement if needed.

### Who it's for
* Everyday computer users who are tired of a cluttered Downloads folder full of random files.
* Professionals and students who want their documents, images, and archives automatically separated into clean categories.
* Power users looking for a lightweight, private tool that operates locally without uploading personal data.

### Key features
* **Silent Background Operation:** Runs continuously with minimal memory usage while handling your files instantly.
* **Smart Sorting Rules:** Automatically routes files to specific folders based on their extension or filename patterns.
* **History and Quick Undo:** Keeps a local record of all organized actions so you can revert any move with a single click.
* **Privacy Focused:** Operates completely offline without cloud storage or tracking.
* **Portable Option Available:** Can be run directly without installation or left in your system tray on startup.
