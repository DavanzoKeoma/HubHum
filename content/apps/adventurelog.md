### What it does in plain English
AdventureLog is a personal travel companion that lets you pin every place you have visited on an interactive world map. You can create detailed trip itineraries, attach photos, and log your outdoor activities like hiking trails. Because you control the software, your travel history and personal data remain completely private and under your own ownership.

### Who it's for
*   **Frequent travelers** who want a central place to track visited countries, cities, and regions.
*   **Outdoor enthusiasts** who need to organize hiking routes, elevation data, and travel checklists in one organized dashboard.

### Key features
*   **Interactive Mapping:** Visualize your journeys with 2D and 3D map views that show exactly where you have been.
*   **Trip Planner:** Build day-by-day itineraries including flights, lodging, and activity notes.
*   **Travel Statistics:** View milestones, progress tracking, and geographic breakdowns of your adventures.
*   **Data Ownership:** Keep your travel history and photos on your own hardware, ensuring complete privacy.
*   **Collaboration:** Share your travel plans or invite friends to edit itineraries together.

### How to install (Download)
AdventureLog is designed to be hosted on your own computer or server.

### Pros and Cons
*   **Pros:** Completely free and open-source, no subscription fees, and total privacy for your personal location data.
*   **Cons:** Requires basic knowledge of setting up software on a computer or server; not a "click-and-go" mobile app.
