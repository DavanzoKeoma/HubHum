### What it does in plain English
DocKit brings all your heavy NoSQL databases together into a single, clean desktop application. Instead of wrestling with complex browser consoles or writing tedious commands from scratch, you can simply type what you want in plain language. The built-in smart assistant translates your requests into runnable queries while keeping all your sensitive keys and records safely stored on your own machine.

### Who it's for
* **Software Developers** who frequently jump between Elasticsearch, MongoDB, and DynamoDB during daily coding and troubleshooting.
* **Data Analysts** who want to inspect records, run searches, and export reports without memorizing complex database query syntax.
* **Privacy-Conscious Teams** who need a reliable local database client that never shares credentials or logs with external tracking servers.

### Key features
* **Natural Language Queries:** Type requests in everyday words to automatically generate accurate database commands.
* **Unified Workspace:** Access Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, DynamoDB, MongoDB, and EasySearch from one native window.
* **Secure Local Storage:** Keep your connection keys protected inside your operating system's native encrypted keychain.
* **Built-In Query History:** Automatically save and search through past queries to quickly repeat helpful tasks.
* **Flexible Data Export:** Move your query results and datasets easily into CSV, JSON, or Markdown formats.
