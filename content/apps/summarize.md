### What it does in plain English
Summarize takes web pages, online videos, podcasts, and files, then pulls out the most important information for you. It handles everything from extracting clean reading text to generating smart summaries using modern language models. You can use it directly from your command terminal or right inside your favorite web browser via an extension.

### Who it's for
* Researchers and students who need to process long articles, academic papers, and recorded lectures quickly.
* Content creators and journalists who regularly review hours of video or audio material and require quick transcripts.
* Busy professionals looking to cut through web clutter and get straight to the core message of any online content.

### Key features
* Extracts clean readable text from cluttered web pages while ignoring ads and sidebars.
* Summarizes video content and podcasts by leveraging built-in transcripts or automatic voice translation.
* Captures key presentation slides and video frames with optional text recognition.
* Runs locally on your machine or integrates with your preferred online intelligence providers.
* Includes a convenient side panel extension for popular web browsers to summarize tabs on the fly.
