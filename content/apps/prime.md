### What it does in plain English
Prime is a tool that allows you to store and organize your website's text, images, and data on your own private server. Instead of relying on a third-party service to hold your information, you keep full control over your content. It provides a clean, user-friendly interface to create, edit, and schedule your updates before they go live on your site.

### Who it's for
* Website owners who want to keep their data on their own servers rather than using external services.
* Content creators who need a flexible way to manage multiple languages and scheduled updates for their projects.

### Key features
* **Full Control:** Host your content management system on your own hardware or private cloud.
* **Flexible Organization:** Easily group and categorize your information using custom fields.
* **Multilingual Support:** Manage your content in several different languages from one dashboard.
* **Preview and Schedule:** See how your work looks before it is published and plan your updates for the future.
* **Access Management:** Control exactly who can view or edit specific parts of your website content.

### How to install (Download)
Prime is a web-based tool. You can get started by visiting the [Official Documentation and Setup Guide](https://docs.primecms.app).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete ownership of your data, no monthly subscription fees, and highly customizable.
* **Cons:** Requires some technical knowledge to set up and maintain on your own server.
