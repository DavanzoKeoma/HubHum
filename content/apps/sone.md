### What it does in plain English
Sone is a dedicated desktop application built specifically to bring the TIDAL music streaming service to Linux systems. It streams high-quality lossless audio directly to your digital-to-analog converter without unnecessary system alterations. The software gives users a polished interface complete with custom color themes, synced lyrics, and full library management.

### Who it's for
* Audiophiles using Linux who demand bit-perfect hardware audio streaming without browser resampling.
* Everyday desktop listeners looking for a fast, native application to manage their music streaming library.
* Broadcasters and content creators who want to display their currently playing tracks on streaming overlays.

### Key features
* High-resolution lossless audio streaming with automatic quality fallback options.
* Direct hardware audio access that bypasses system mixers for unaltered signal delivery.
* Flexible user interface featuring customizable color themes, light and dark modes, and floating miniplayers.
* Comprehensive listening history tracking and scrobbling support for Last.fm, Libre.fm, and ListenBrainz.
* Seamless music video playback integrated directly into your regular queues and playlists.
