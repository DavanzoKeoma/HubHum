### What it does in plain English
OpenShorts takes lengthy recordings like podcasts or webinars and automatically slices them into engaging vertical clips tailored for social media. It analyzes the content to find the strongest moments, adds professional animated captions, and crops the video dynamically to keep the speaker in frame. You can host it yourself for free using Docker or use the online cloud service to get your clips ready in seconds.

### Who it's for
- **Content creators** looking to repurpose long video streams into engaging short-form highlights without manual editing work.
- **Marketing agencies** needing to scale out promotional videos and actor-driven marketing clips for multiple clients.
- **Developers and tech enthusiasts** who want a customizable, self-hosted video pipeline that integrates with local tools or AI agents.

### Key features
- **Viral Moment Detection:** Automatically scans transcripts and scene changes to pick out the most engaging 15-to-60-second segments.
- **Smart Vertical Framing:** Intentionally tracks speakers, stacks multi-person conversations, and keeps action centered in vertical formats.
- **Auto Captions & Dubbing:** Generates burned-in word-level subtitles and supports voice translation in over 30 languages.
- **AI Shorts & UGC Creator:** Generates marketing videos featuring realistic virtual actors, scripts, and voiceovers from simple product text or URLs.
- **Social Auto-Publishing:** Connects directly with major social networks to schedule and post your finished clips automatically.

### How to install (Download)
To run OpenShorts on your own machine, make sure you have Docker and Docker Compose installed. Clone the official source official project page and start the containers using the instructions on the [Official OpenShorts official project page](https://github.com/mutonby/openshorts). If you prefer a ready-to-use setup without managing local hardware or configuration keys, you can access the hosted service directly at the [OpenShorts Website](https://www.openshorts.app/).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Completely free and open-source when self-hosted with no mandatory watermarks, powerful AI moment detection, and multi-platform scheduling.
* **Cons:** Requires your own API keys for advanced AI services when self-hosting, and local CPU rendering takes longer than managed cloud GPUs.
