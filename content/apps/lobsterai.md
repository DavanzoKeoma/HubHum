### What it does in plain English
LobsterAI is a smart desktop application that handles complex computer chores on your behalf. You simply describe what you need in everyday language, and it opens folders, reads spreadsheets, edits documents, and browses websites. It even lets you send commands remotely from popular chat apps on your phone so work keeps moving when you step away.

### Who it's for
* **Office professionals:** Automate routine spreadsheet organization, presentation design, and document formatting.
* **Researchers and marketers:** Gather web data, monitor online dashboards, and compile comprehensive daily reports effortlessly.
* **Busy organizers:** Manage scheduled tasks and keep your digital workspace tidy without manual clicking.

### Key features
* **Local file handling:** Reads and writes directly to your local folders, spreadsheets, and office presentation files.
* **Remote chat control:** Connects to mobile messaging apps so you can send instructions to your computer from anywhere.
* **Live artifact previews:** Shows generated web pages, images, videos, and documents instantly in a handy side panel.
* **Scheduled recurring jobs:** Sets up automated daily summaries, morning briefings, and regular status check-ups automatically.
* **Flexible model support:** Works with built-in models or your personal subscription keys from major artificial intelligence providers.
