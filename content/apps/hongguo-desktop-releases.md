### What it does in plain English
Hongguo Desktop is a friendly third-party player application designed to let you search, select, and watch serial video content right on your Windows desktop. It saves your viewing history automatically so you can pick up right where you left off without digging through menus. You can also adjust playback speeds, use compact window modes, and let episodes play continuously while you work or relax.

### Who it's for
* Everyday entertainment fans who prefer watching short-form series on a large desktop monitor instead of a tiny mobile phone screen.
* Viewers who want an uncluttered player with convenient keyboard shortcuts and automated viewing progress tracking.

### Key features
* **Direct Desktop Streaming:** Search, browse categories, and play episodes smoothly without requiring any mobile device emulators.
* **Smart Progress Memory:** Automatically saves your spot so you can resume viewing exactly where you stopped earlier.
* **Flexible Window Modes:** Easily switch to compact mini-players or use custom window sizes while multitasking.
* **Continuous Playback:** Enjoy automatic next-episode loading and adjustable playback speeds for a customized viewing experience.
* **Quick Boss Key:** Instantly hide or pause your viewing window using handy shortcut combinations.
