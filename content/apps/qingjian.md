### What it does in plain English
Qingjian is a typing tool that helps you learn a new language during your daily computer tasks. As you type in Chinese, it displays a subtle translation for each word suggestion in your chosen target language. It functions like a standard typing assistant while turning every email or document into a small learning opportunity.

### Who it's for
* Language learners who want to build vocabulary without needing extra study time.
* People who prefer a clean, privacy-focused typing experience that keeps all data on their own computer.

### Key features
* **Integrated Learning:** See translations for English, Japanese, German, Spanish, or Vietnamese directly in your suggestion list.
* **Privacy First:** All your typing habits and vocabulary statistics are stored locally on your device and never uploaded.
* **Smart Typing:** Supports advanced features like full-sentence input, spelling correction, and various keyboard layouts.
* **Progress Tracking:** View your daily and weekly typing volume along with a record of the new words you have encountered.
