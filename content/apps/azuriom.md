### What it does in plain English
Azuriom is a dedicated website platform designed to help game server owners build a professional online presence. It allows you to easily manage your player base, set up an online store for server items, and post important community updates. With its flexible design, you can connect your game server to a modern website that keeps your players engaged and informed.

### Who it's for
* Game server administrators looking for a centralized way to manage their community.
* Players who want a professional portal to check server status, news, and shop for in-game items.

### Key features
* **Unified Dashboard:** Manage your server's website, player accounts, and settings from one simple interface.
* **Extensive Add-ons:** Choose from dozens of extra features to customize your site exactly how you want it.
* **Game Compatibility:** Works seamlessly with popular titles like Minecraft, Rust, ARK, and many others.
* **Community Tools:** Built-in support for news, forums, and shop systems to keep your players active.
* **Open and Secure:** A transparent, community-trusted platform that prioritizes security and reliability.
