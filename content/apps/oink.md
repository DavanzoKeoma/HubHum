### What it does in plain English
Oink connects your nearby Windows and Mac computers so they function as a single, unified system. You can move your mouse cursor across screens, type on any device, and even drag and drop files between them instantly. It also allows you to share audio and clipboard content across all your connected machines automatically.

### Who it's for
* Professionals who use multiple computers simultaneously and want to reduce desk clutter.
* Creative workers who need to move files and text between a Mac and a Windows PC without using external storage drives.

### Key features
* **Unified Control:** Use one keyboard and mouse to navigate across all your connected computers.
* **Drag and Drop:** Easily move files, images, and text between different operating systems.
* **Shared Clipboard:** Copy text on one machine and paste it directly onto another.
* **Audio Sharing:** Play sound from multiple devices through a single set of speakers or headphones.
* **Secure Connection:** All traffic between your devices is encrypted by default for your privacy.
