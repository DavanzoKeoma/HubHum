### What it does in plain English
This application acts as a helpful artificial intelligence companion living right inside your computer command line. It can safely read your files, execute scripts, look up information online, and help you solve programming or everyday data tasks. By connecting to major artificial intelligence providers or running models directly on your hardware, it assists you with complex workflows without leaving your preferred environment.

### Who it's for
* Software developers looking for a fast, terminal-native assistant to automate routine coding and testing tasks.
* Power users and system administrators who want to translate natural language requests into correct command-line operations.
* Researchers and students who need an interactive tool to analyze local files, data sheets, and online sources quickly.

### Key features
* **Terminal integration:** Operates smoothly inside your command-line environment, text editors, or background servers.
* **Local file management:** Reads, writes, and edits files incrementally to apply requested changes safely.
* **Web browsing capabilities:** Searches and reads web pages to retrieve up-to-date answers for your questions.
* **Flexible model support:** Works with popular cloud artificial intelligence providers as well as completely offline local models.
* **Extensible architecture:** Supports custom plugins, reusable skills, and shared memory systems to adapt to your specific workflow.
