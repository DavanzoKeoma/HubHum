### What it does in plain English
Microweber is a visual tool that allows you to build professional websites and online stores without needing to write any code. You can design your pages by simply dragging and dropping text, images, and videos exactly where you want them to appear. It also includes built-in features to manage products, orders, and blog posts, making it a complete solution for getting your business online.

### Who it's for
* Small business owners who want to launch an online shop quickly without hiring a professional designer.
* Bloggers and content creators who want a simple, visual way to manage their articles and site layout.

### Key features
* **Drag-and-Drop Editor:** Move elements around your page in real-time to see exactly how your site will look before you publish.
* **Live Text Editing:** Click directly on any text on your screen to change it, just like you would in a word processor.
* **E-commerce Ready:** Includes built-in tools to manage your product catalog, shopping cart, and customer orders.
* **Pre-designed Layouts:** Comes with a variety of professional templates to help you get started with a polished look immediately.
* **Flexible Content Management:** Easily organize your site into pages, categories, and posts to keep your information tidy and accessible.
