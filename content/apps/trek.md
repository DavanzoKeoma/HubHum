### What it does in plain English
TREK brings every part of your journey into a single, private web application that you control completely. You can collaborate with friends or family on live maps, organize detailed day plans, and keep track of bookings and shared costs without relying on third-party services. It also works offline, synchronizes your data seamlessly across devices, and even lets you build custom photo books from your trip journals.

### Who it's for
* **Group travelers and families** who want to coordinate itineraries, packing lists, and shared expenses in real time.
* **Outdoor enthusiasts and hikers** looking for detailed topographic terrain maps, route planning, and GPS track comparisons.
* **Privacy-conscious explorers** who want complete ownership of their travel data on a self-hosted server without corporate tracking.

### Key features
* **Real-time collaboration:** Edit itineraries, chat, vote on activities, and view changes live with your travel companions.
* **Comprehensive bookings:** Store flights, trains, and accommodations with automated confirmation imports and currency conversion.
* **Advanced route mapping:** Plan hikes, road trips, and city walks with interactive maps, elevation profiles, and offline tile support.
* **Shared budgeting:** Split expenses in any currency, settle up balances, and export detailed financial reports.
* **AI assistant and offline sync:** Automate planning tasks with integrated AI tools and access your complete trip data even without an internet connection.
