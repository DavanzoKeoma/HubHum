### What it does in plain English
MovieBox-Tui gives you a clean, keyboard-friendly interface right inside your terminal to search for movies, television episodes, and live channels. It lets you pick your preferred video resolution, choose subtitles, and launch your favorite media player instantly. You can also pause and resume downloads for full seasons or individual files without needing complex setup routines.

### Who it's for
* Command-line enthusiasts who prefer managing and watching entertainment without leaving their terminal setup.
* Users looking for a lightweight, distraction-free way to organize watch history and stream video content.
* People who want a central hub to connect community video addons and local playlists.

### Key features
* **Terminal Interface:** Navigate smoothly using keyboard shortcuts, mouse clicks, or command palettes with multiple built-in color themes.
* **Resolution and Subtitle Pickers:** Choose your preferred video quality and select subtitle tracks before starting playback.
* **External Player Support:** Launch streams seamlessly using established media players like `mpv`, `VLC`, or `IINA`.
* **Batch Downloading:** Download episodes or complete seasons concurrently with built-in pause and resume support.
* **Live TV Support:** Import M3U playlists to watch channels with electronic program guide search options.
