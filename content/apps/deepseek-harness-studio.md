### What it does in plain English
DeepSeek Harness Studio brings a friendly, no-code desktop experience to advanced artificial intelligence tools and assistants. It lets you organize local projects, launch ready-made workflows, and discover useful extensions without ever touching a command-line terminal. You can connect your preferred local or cloud reasoning engines, chat with visual models, and manage your everyday tasks in one clean window.

### Who it's for
* Creators and writers looking to generate structured content, presentations, and reports using guided workflows.
* Developers and power users who want a graphical workspace to manage local intelligence models and custom assistant tools.
* Teams that need an organized, private environment to run local projects and document knowledge bases.

### Key features
* **No-Code Plugin & Extension Discovery:** Browse, install, and manage helpful add-ons directly from an online directory.
* **Ready-Made Workflow Presets:** Jumpstart projects with built-in templates for web applications, presentations, video planning, and reports.
* **Local Model Support:** Connect easily with local reasoning services like Ollama, vLLM, and SGLang.
* **Visual Enhancement:** Send images and screenshots directly to vision-capable models for analysis and design feedback.
* **Workspace & Session Management:** Organize conversations, reference local files, and keep track of background tasks effortlessly.
