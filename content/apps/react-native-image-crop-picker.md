### What it does in plain English
This tool helps mobile application creators add a smooth photo and video selection screen to their apps. Users can snap fresh pictures with their camera or pick multiple existing files from their photo library. It also includes built-in trimming, sizing, and cropping tools so people can focus on the exact part of the image they want to share.

### Who it's for
* Mobile app creators looking for reliable media selection tools.
* Developers building profile photo upload or social media sharing screens.
* Anyone needing a straightforward camera and gallery bridge for cross-platform apps.

### Key features
* Pick single or multiple images and videos effortlessly.
* Built-in editing screen for cropping, rotating, and resizing photos.
* Supports both front and rear camera capture directly inside your app.
* Adjustable compression settings to keep file sizes small and fast.
* Clean temporary file management to keep device storage clutter-free.
