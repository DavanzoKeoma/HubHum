### What it does in plain English
CodeBurn scans the activity logs already stored on your computer to calculate exactly how much your AI coding tools are costing you. It breaks down these expenses by specific projects, tasks, and the AI models used, giving you a clear view of where your money goes. The tool also provides actionable advice to help you stop wasting tokens on unnecessary tasks or repetitive processes.

### Who it's for
* Software developers who use AI-assisted coding tools and want to manage their monthly expenses.
* Freelancers or team leads who need to track which projects are consuming the most AI resources.

### Key features
* **Automatic Tracking:** Detects and reads session files from over 40 popular AI coding tools automatically.
* **Detailed Breakdown:** View costs categorized by project, git branch, specific task, or AI model.
* **Optimization Advice:** Identifies inefficient configurations or repetitive tasks and suggests fixes to save money.
* **Privacy-Focused:** Operates entirely locally on your machine with no accounts, proxies, or data uploads required.
* **Budget Monitoring:** Allows you to set your subscription plan and track usage against your limits in real time.
