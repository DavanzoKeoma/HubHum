### What it does in plain English
Plone is a complete system for building and managing websites that are secure and easy to use. It allows non-technical staff to update content easily while giving developers the tools to customize the site's look and behavior. It works on many different computer systems and can be hosted in the cloud or on your own servers.

### Who it's for
* **Non-profit organizations and community groups** that need a reliable, free platform to share news and manage members.
* **Small to medium businesses** looking for a professional website solution without paying for expensive monthly software licenses.
* **Technical teams** who want a flexible foundation to build custom web applications with specific security requirements.

### Key features
* **Built-in Security:** Offers detailed control over who can view, edit, or delete specific parts of the website.
* **Easy Content Management:** Provides a simple interface for adding text, images, and documents without needing to write code.
* **Multilingual Support:** Includes tools to manage content in multiple languages and has an interface translated into over 35 languages.
* **Flexible Design:** Can be customized to look like any modern website, with options for both traditional and modern user interfaces.
* **Accessibility Focused:** Designed to meet high standards for usability, ensuring the site is easy to use for people with disabilities.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free to use forever, highly secure, supports multiple languages, and has a long history of stability.
* **Cons:** The initial setup can be complex for beginners, and it requires a server environment to run, which may need technical assistance.
