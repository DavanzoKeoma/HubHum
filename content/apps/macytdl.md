### What it does in plain English
It lets you grab any video or whole playlist from the web and save it to your Mac. The program takes care of downloading, converting and organizing the files for you. You can also pause, resume or cancel a download whenever you like.

### Who it's for
People who want to keep a copy of favorite YouTube videos for offline watching. Teachers or students who need lecture videos saved for later study. Anyone who prefers a simple click‑and‑save experience on a Mac.

### Key features
- Save single videos or entire playlists from hundreds of sites.  
- Choose to download video, audio only, or both with subtitles and thumbnails.  
- Pause, resume or cancel each download at any time.  
- Run several downloads at once or one after another.  
- Adjust where files go, what format they use, and other settings through an easy dialog.
