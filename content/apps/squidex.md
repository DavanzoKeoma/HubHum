### What it does in plain English
Squidex acts as a central library for all your text, images, and data, keeping them organized in one place. Instead of being tied to a specific website design, it provides your content to any app, website, or digital service that needs it. This allows you to update your information once and have it automatically update everywhere it is displayed.

### Who it's for
* **Content Managers:** People who need to organize large amounts of information without worrying about the technical layout of a website.
* **App Developers:** Professionals who need a reliable, flexible way to store and retrieve data for their custom software projects.

### Key features
* **Centralized Storage:** Keep all your digital assets and text in one secure, organized hub.
* **Universal Access:** Easily share your content with websites, mobile apps, or other software services.
* **Flexible Organization:** Create custom structures for your data to match exactly what your project requires.
* **Ready for Growth:** Built to handle large amounts of information while remaining fast and responsive.

### How to install (Download)
Squidex is a web-based service. You can use the official hosted version or set it up on your own server:
* **Use the Cloud Version:** Start immediately at [https://cloud.squidex.io](https://cloud.squidex.io).
* **Self-Hosting:** Follow the official setup guides for [Azure](https://docs.squidex.io/01-getting-started/installation/platforms/install-azure), [AWS](https://docs.squidex.io/01-getting-started/installation/platforms/install-on-aws), [Docker](https://docs.squidex.io/01-getting-started/installation/platforms/install-docker), or [Render](https://docs.squidex.io/01-getting-started/installation/platforms/install-on-render).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Highly flexible for different types of projects, free to self-host, and separates content from design.
* **Cons:** Requires some technical knowledge to set up and manage on your own server.
