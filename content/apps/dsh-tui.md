### What it does in plain English
dsh-TUI transforms your standard text-based terminal into a colorful, interactive workspace for managing AI tasks. It adds helpful visual elements like a progress bar, status updates, and a pixel-art whale to make your workflow more engaging. You can easily track your projects, manage conversation history, and view AI responses without ever leaving your terminal window.

### Who it's for
* Developers and power users who prefer working within a terminal environment.
* People who want a lightweight, efficient way to use AI tools without the clutter of a full web browser.

### Key features
* **Visual Interface:** Adds a clean, organized layout to your terminal, including a helpful pixel-art whale mascot.
* **Live Status Tracking:** Keeps you updated on your AI's progress with real-time speed and task completion indicators.
* **Easy Navigation:** Includes a simple side panel and session manager to quickly switch between different projects and conversations.
* **Rich Formatting:** Supports clear display of diagrams, math formulas, and images directly within your terminal window.
* **Customizable Settings:** Allows you to adjust notifications, shortcuts, and display preferences to match your personal working style.
