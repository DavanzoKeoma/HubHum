### What it does in plain English
SD Maid 2/SE is an advanced utility tool designed to help you clean and organize your Android storage with ease. It scans your device to locate leftover files from uninstalled apps, hidden caches, and unwanted duplicates taking up precious space. With helpful scheduling options and built-in automation helpers, it keeps your phone running efficiently without manual hassle.

### Who it's for
* Android users whose devices are running low on storage space due to accumulated junk files.
* People who frequently test new apps and want to ensure uninstalled programs leave no digital clutter behind.
* Users looking for an open-source, ad-free maintenance manager that respects their privacy.

### Key features
* **CorpseFinder:** Automatically detects and removes data left behind by apps you no longer have installed.
* **AppCleaner:** Clears out expendable cache and temporary files to instantly recover valuable storage space.
* **Deduplicator:** Scans your internal folders to find and remove duplicate files, photos, and documents.
* **StorageAnalyzer:** Provides a clear visual breakdown of what is taking up space on your phone.
* **Scheduler:** Automates regular cleaning routines so your device stays tidy with zero effort.
