### What it does in plain English
WhisperSubTranslate takes any video file and automatically generates written subtitles from the spoken dialogue. It processes everything directly on your computer so your personal video files never get uploaded to the cloud. You can also translate the generated subtitles into dozens of different languages using powerful offline translation engines or your own online accounts.

### Who it's for
* Content creators and video editors who need fast, reliable subtitle files for their projects.
* Language learners and educators looking to watch foreign videos with accurate translated captions.
* Privacy-conscious users who want speech recognition and translation without depending on online subscription tools.

### Key features
* 100% private speech recognition that keeps your video files safely on your own machine.
* Offline translation support that lets you translate text without an active internet connection.
* Automatic model downloader that sets everything up without requiring separate software tools.
* Built-in sync repair models to keep dialogue perfectly timed even in long video clips.
* Convenient queue manager with live progress tracking and local history logs.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source, complete offline privacy, no recurring subscription fees, supports multiple languages.
* **Cons:** Requires a decent amount of storage space for high-quality speech and translation models, initial model downloads can take time depending on your internet speed.
