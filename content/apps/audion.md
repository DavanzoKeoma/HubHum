### What it does in plain English
Audion is a privacy-friendly music player that organizes and plays your personal audio collection without needing an internet connection. It features a modern interface inspired by popular streaming apps, complete with smooth animations, smart playlist management, and automatic metadata scanning. You can even view real-time synchronized karaoke lyrics that highlight word-by-word as your favorite songs play.

### Who it's for
* Music lovers who prefer keeping and owning their local library of audio files instead of paying for streaming subscriptions.
* Privacy-conscious listeners who want a clean listening experience without data tracking or online ads.
* Users looking for a sleek, customizable media hub that runs smoothly on desktop, mobile devices, and in the car.

### Key features
* **Offline Local Library:** Scan, organize, and play major audio file types like MP3, FLAC, AAC, and ALAC without tracking.
* **Synced Karaoke Lyrics:** Enjoy real-time word-by-word lyrics powered by online databases and cached locally for offline use.
* **Android Auto Support:** Easily browse your library, manage queues, and control playback right from your vehicle's dashboard display.
* **Custom Themes & Plugins:** Tailor the look with light or dark styling and extend functionality using custom plugins.
* **Self-Hosted Streaming Option:** Set up your own music streaming server using Docker to access your library from anywhere.
