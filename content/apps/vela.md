### What it does in plain English
Vela is a specialized editing workspace designed to help fiction and web novel writers brainstorm, outline, and draft long stories. It connects your choice of major AI language models directly to your computer while keeping all your documents and research notes stored locally on your device. The software includes built-in search features that pull details from your reference files instantly, helping you maintain consistent plots and character backgrounds across every chapter.

### Who it's for
* Web fiction and indie authors writing long-form stories who want a dedicated writing interface.
* Creative writers looking for an assistant to help outline scenes, develop character arcs, and refine text.
* Privacy-conscious creators who prefer keeping all personal notes and book drafts stored entirely on their own computer.

### Key features
* **Structured Worldbuilding:** Keep track of complex character profiles, settings, and main plot points in one organized sidebar.
* **Automated Story Outlines:** Generate narrative structures, chapter breakdowns, and scene goals using artificial intelligence.
* **Local Reference Search:** Import background documents and use vector search to recall important details while you write.
* **Flexible Model Choice:** Bring your own API keys to connect commercial intelligence providers or run models completely offline.
* **Customizable Workspace:** Arrange your editing panes, file tree, and writing assistants side by side in a multi-window layout.
