### What it does in plain English
Evolver acts as an intelligent coach for your AI-powered coding tools, continuously reviewing past project logs and error patterns. Instead of letting your assistant make the same mistakes repeatedly, it packages successful fixes and smart strategies into compact rules. These rules guide your coding sessions to become cleaner, faster, and much more accurate over time.

### Who it's for
- Software developers who use AI coding assistants and want them to learn from past project errors.
- Technical project managers looking to maintain consistent coding standards across multiple contributor environments.
- Open-source creators who need a structured way to capture and share proven problem-solving patterns.

### Key features
- **Automated Log Analysis:** Scans your project logs and error histories to spot recurring roadblocks.
- **Reusable Knowledge Capsules:** Converts successful fixes into structured assets so your assistant remembers them.
- **Customizable Strategy Presets:** Easily switch between balance, aggressive innovation, stability hardening, or emergency repair modes.
- **Safety-First Design:** Operates safely within your project workspace without executing unauthorized system changes.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open architecture, drastically reduces repetitive AI coding mistakes, highly customizable behavior.
* **Cons:** Requires a Git-initialized project directory to function properly, advanced strategy configurations take some time to master.
