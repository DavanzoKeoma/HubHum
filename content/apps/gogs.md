### What it does in plain English
Gogs provides a straightforward way to store, organize, and review your source code on your own hardware or server. It acts as a private hub where teams can collaborate on programming projects, review changes, and track issues without relying on external corporate platforms. * Independent software developers who want complete privacy and control over their project files.
* Small development teams looking for a fast, lightweight code collaboration workspace.
* Hobbyists and system administrators running personal servers on tiny hardware like a Raspberry Pi.
