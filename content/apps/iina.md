### What it does in plain English
IINA is a beautifully designed video and music player created specifically for modern versions of macOS. It handles nearly any media file you throw at it while fitting right in with the look and feel of your Apple computer. From watching high-definition movies with automatic subtitle matching to listening to albums in standalone music mode, it provides a smooth, clutter-free viewing and listening experience.

### Who it's for
* Mac users looking for a fast, gorgeous alternative to built-in or clunky media players.
* Film fans who want hassle-free subtitle searching and picture-in-picture playback.
* Music lovers who want a dedicated, lightweight window to enjoy their audio files.

### Key features
* Built on powerful playback technology for the best possible video decoding on Mac.
* Modern interface supporting dark mode, customizable color schemes, and floating controllers.
* Automatic online subtitle searching and intelligent matching for local subtitle files.
* Dedicated music mode that turns the player into a clean, minimal audio player.
* Advanced controls for touch gestures, shortcuts, plugins, and picture-in-picture.
