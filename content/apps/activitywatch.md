### What it does in plain English
ActivityWatch automatically tracks which applications you use, which websites you visit, and how long you are active at your keyboard. It stores all this information locally on your computer, ensuring that your personal habits are never shared with third parties. You can then view clear, visual summaries of your daily activity to help you understand and improve your productivity.

### Who it's for
*   **Freelancers and remote workers** who need an accurate record of their work hours for billing or personal review.
*   **Students and researchers** who want to analyze their study habits or screen time without sacrificing their digital privacy.

### Key features
*   **Automatic Tracking:** Records your active windows, browser tabs, and keyboard/mouse activity without manual input.
*   **Complete Privacy:** Your data stays on your machine, so you are the only one who can see your activity history.
*   **Visual Dashboards:** Provides easy-to-read charts and timelines to help you visualize how your day was spent.
*   **Extensible:** Supports various plugins and "watchers" to track specific activities like coding or specialized software usage.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Works seamlessly across Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android devices.
