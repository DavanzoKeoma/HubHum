### What it does in plain English
Vexa uses an automated bot to join your Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom calls and transcribe the conversation in real time. Unlike other services, it keeps your data private by allowing you to store transcripts and meeting summaries on your own servers. It then uses intelligent agents to turn those notes into an organized, searchable library of your team's decisions and projects.

### Who it's for
* **Privacy-conscious teams** who need to keep meeting data within their own secure network.
* **Project managers and researchers** who want to automatically turn hours of verbal discussions into a structured, searchable knowledge base.

### Key features
* **Multi-platform support:** Bots join Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Jitsi calls automatically.
* **Full data ownership:** Keep your transcripts and knowledge base on your own hardware, ensuring nothing leaves your control.
* **Smart knowledge compilation:** Agents process meeting transcripts to create interlinked notes, making it easy to find past decisions.
* **Self-hosted or cloud:** Choose between a simple hosted service or a fully self-hosted setup for maximum security.
* **Safe automation:** Use agents to prepare meeting briefs or triage follow-up tasks without risking your sensitive information.

### How to install (Download)
* **For a quick start without setup:** Sign in at [vexa.ai/signin](https://vexa.ai/signin) to use the hosted service.
* **For full self-hosting:** Follow the instructions in the [Vexa GitHub official project page](https://github.com/Vexa-ai/vexa) to run the stack on your own infrastructure using Docker.

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Complete control over your data, supports multiple major meeting platforms, and creates a searchable "second brain" for your team.
* **Cons:** Self-hosting requires some familiarity with basic server management, and advanced features like custom agent workflows have a learning curve.
