### What it does in plain English
Kodi turns your computer or TV box into a gorgeous entertainment center that plays all your movies, shows, and music. It automatically organizes your personal media files by fetching cover art, descriptions, and actor details. You can stream content from anywhere in your home network or install extra extensions for even more streaming choices.

### Who it's for
* Movie enthusiasts with large digital collections who want a gorgeous, couch-friendly viewing experience.
* Cord-cutters looking to consolidate all their personal home videos, music, and streaming sources into a single app.
* Families wanting a simple, remote-control-friendly interface on their living room TV.

### Key features
* Plays almost every popular video, music, and photo format available.
* Automatically organizes your media files with artwork, plot summaries, and cast lists.
* Network-ready streaming so you can watch files stored on other computers or network drives.
* Highly customizable appearance with downloadable visual themes and skins.
* Extensible ecosystem supporting community-built add-ons and extra features.

### How to install (Download)
* Head over to the official website and download the software for your operating system: [Kodi Download Page](https://kodi.tv/download).
* Android users can also install the application directly from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.xbmc.kodi).

### Pros and Cons
* **Pros:** Free and open source forever with no subscriptions, highly customizable interface, runs on a wide variety of hardware.
* **Cons:** Initial setup of media libraries takes a little time, advanced features can feel overwhelming for absolute beginners.
